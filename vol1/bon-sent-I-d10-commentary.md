## Distinctio X.

### Commentarius in Distinctionem X.

*De aeterna processione Spiritus sancti quantum ad personam quae procedit.*

> *«Nunc post Filii aeternitatem de Spiritu sancto, quantum Deo donante,»* etc.

## English

## Distinction X.

### Commentary on Distinction X.

*On the eternal procession of the Holy Spirit with respect to the person who proceeds.*

> *«Now, after [treating of] the eternity of the Son, [we shall treat] of the Holy Spirit, insofar as God grants it,»* etc.

The chunk holds only Bonaventure's prefatory matter for d.10: the running
heading (DISTINCTIO X.), the section heading (COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM

of the Lombard incipit lemma which Bonaventure is about to comment on. The
printed lemma terminates with `etc.` after `donante,` — Quaracchi truncates
