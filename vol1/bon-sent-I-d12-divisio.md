# bon-sent-I-d12-divisio
## *Commentarius in distinctionem XII: Divisio textus et tractatio quaestionum*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 219 -->

### COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XII.

*De aeterna processione Spiritus sancti, qualiter a Patre et Filio procedat.*

> *Item quaeritur, cum Spiritus sanctus procedat a Patre et Filio.*

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Haec est secunda pars, in qua Magister determinat, qualiter Spiritus sanctus procedit[^1] a Patre et Filio; et haec pars habet quatuor capitula secundum quatuor, quae determinat in quatuor capitulis[^2].

Nam *primo* quaerit et determinat, utrum Spiritus sanctus prius procedat a Patre, et determinat, quod *non*.

<!-- page 220 -->

*Secundo*, utrum *principalius et plenius*, ibi: *Nunc tractandum est quod secundo quaerebatur.*

*Tertio* vero determinat, utrum *proprie* a Patre procedat, et dicit quod *sic*, ibi: *Ex eodem sensu dicitur etiam proprie procedere de Patre.*

*Quarto* determinat, utrum Spiritus sanctus *proprie procedat a Patre per Filium*, ibi: *Forte etiam iuxta hanc intelligentiam*, ubi confirmat auctoritate Hilarii, quod Spiritus sanctus mittitur a Patre per Filium et procedit.

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Supposito ex praecedentibus, quod Spiritus sanctus procedat a Patre et Filio, ad intelligentiam huius partis quatuor quaeruntur de processione Spiritus sancti in comparatione ad utrumque.

*Primo* quaeritur, utrum Spiritus sanctus *prius* procedat a Patre quam a Filio.

*Secundo*, utrum *plenius* a Patre quam a Filio.

*Tertio*, utrum procedat a Patre *mediante Filio*.

*Quarto* et ultimo, utrum processio Filii *prior sit secundum ordinem intelligendi* processione Spiritus sancti, vel e converso.

### ARTICULUS UNICUS.

*De processione Spiritus sancti in comparatione ad Patrem et Filium.*

## English

### COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XII.

*On the eternal procession of the Holy Spirit — in what manner He proceeds from the Father and the Son.*

> *Likewise it is asked, since the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son.*

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

This is the second part, in which the Master determines in what manner the Holy Spirit proceeds[^1] from the Father and the Son; and this part has four chapters according to the four [points] which he determines in four chapters[^2].

For *first*, he asks and determines whether the Holy Spirit proceeds prior from the Father, and he determines that He does *not*.

*Secondly*, [he asks] whether [He proceeds] *more principally and more fully* [from the Father], at: *Now must be treated what was asked second.*

*Thirdly* he determines whether [the Spirit] proceeds *properly* from the Father, and he says *yes*, at: *In the same sense He is also said properly to proceed from the Father.*

*Fourthly*, he determines whether the Holy Spirit *properly proceeds from the Father through the Son*, at: *Perhaps also according to this same understanding*, where he confirms by the authority of Hilary that the Holy Spirit is sent and proceeds from the Father through the Son.

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

Supposing from what precedes, that the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son, for the understanding of this part four [questions] are asked concerning the procession of the Holy Spirit in comparison to both.

*Firstly* it is asked, whether the Holy Spirit proceeds *prior* from the Father than from the Son.

*Secondly*, whether [He proceeds] *more fully* from the Father than from the Son.

*Thirdly*, whether He proceeds from the Father *with the Son as medium*.

*Fourthly* and last, whether the procession of the Son *is prior in the order of understanding* to the procession of the Holy Spirit, or conversely.

### SOLE ARTICLE.

*On the procession of the Holy Spirit in comparison to the Father and the Son.*

## Notes

Bonaventure's commentary on d. 12 is structured as a single article (*articulus unicus*) with four questions, paralleling the four sub-points he reads out of Lombard's two chapters:

| Lombard's text (cap.) | Bonaventure's question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Cap. 1 (*prius*?) | Q. 1: *prius?* | No |
| Cap. 2 *principaliter / plenius* | Q. 2: *plenius?* | No |
| Cap. 2 *proprie* | (folded into the framing) | — |
| Cap. 2 *per Filium* (Hilary) | Q. 3: *mediante Filio?* | — |
| (added by Bonaventure) | Q. 4: *ordo intelligendi?* | — |

Bonaventure adds the fourth question (the order of intelligibility between the procession of the Son and that of the Holy Spirit) as a metaphysical follow-up not directly raised by Lombard's chapters but native to the scholastic apparatus on emanation in God.
