# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XVIII, Vers. 33-38
## *Ponitur hic eius examinatio facta a Pilato*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 488 -->

61. *Introivit ergo iterum* etc. Posita traditione Christi, qua Iudaei ipsum tradiderunt Pilato, ponitur hic eius *examinatio facta a Pilato*[^p488-4], in qua quatuor innuuntur: primo, *inquisitio;* deinde, *inquisitionis origo;* tertio, *ad inquisitionem responsio;* quarto, *responsionis discussio.*

(Vers. 33.). Primo ergo notatur *ipsa inquisitio;* propter quod dicit: *Introivit ergo iterum in praetorium Pilatus;* quia egressus fuerat ad quaerendum causam a Iudaeis et non invenerat, revertebatur ad inquirendum eam a reo; *et vocavit Iesum et dixit ei: Tu es rex Iudaeorum?* Vere ipse rex, quia ipse, de quo Ieremiae vigesimo tertio[^p488-5]: « Suscitabo David germen iustum, et regnabit rex ». Ideo de hoc maxime quaerit, quia hoc dicendo videbatur contradicere caesari, qui tunc dominabatur Iudaeis.

62. (Vers. 34.). *Respondit Iesus* etc. Tangitur secundum, scilicet *inquisitionis origo;* et ut sciatur origo, quaerit Dominus a Pilato: *A temetipso hoc dicis, an alii tibi dixerunt de me?* Non quaerit, quia dubitet; sed ut per eius responsionem malitiam detegat Iudaeorum[^p488-6]; et hoc quidem fit in Pilati responsione; unde:

63. (Vers. 35.). *Respondit Pilatus: Nunquid ego Iudaeus sum?* quasi dicat: hoc ego non impono tibi; *gens tua et pontifices tui*[^p488-7] *tradiderunt te mihi;* quasi dicat: hoc ab eis accepi: *Quid ergo fecisti,* propter quod ita te accusant?

64. (Vers. 36.). *Respondit Iesus.* Hic ponitur tertium, scilicet *Domini responsio,* in qua Dominus ipse confutat pravam intentionem Iudaeorum. Intendebant enim Iudaei per hoc Pilatum et caesarem contra ipsum provocare, quasi nollet in regno habere collegam; ideo Dominus hoc excludit dicens: *Regnum meum non est de hoc mundo.* Augustinus[^p488-8]: « Audite, Iudaei et gentes: *Regnum meum non est de hoc mundo;* non impedio dominationem vestram in hoc mundo. Quid vultis amplius »? — Et rationem reddit, quod regnum eius non est de hoc mundo; et est ratio sua talis: pro rege terrae ministri certant[^p488-9], ne tradatur ad mortem; pro me vero ministri mei non decertant: ergo ego non sum rex terrenus. Et ponit *maiorem* huius rationis: *Si ex hoc mundo esset regnum meum, ministri mei utique decertarent, ut non traderer Iudaeis; nunc autem;* apparet, supple; haec est conclusio; quod *regnum meum non est hinc,* quia nullus me defendit. Et ideo regnum eius non est de hoc mundo — quia, sicut dicitur primae Ioannis ultimo[^p488-10], « mundus totus in maligno positus est »; ideo petimus: « Adveniat regnum tuum » — sed in caelis est; unde Matthaei quinto: « Qui fecerit et docuerit, hic magnus vocabitur in regno caelorum » etc.

65. (Vers. 37.). *Dixit itaque ei Pilatus.* Hic ponitur quartum, scilicet *responsionis discussio.* Et quia Dominus dixerat, se regnum habere; arguit Pilatus ex responsione Domini, quasi admirans: *Ergo,* inquit, *rex es tu?* quasi dicat: ex quo regem te esse fateris, videtur ex verbis tuis, te velle regnare. Et Dominus quidem *respondet,* conclusionem[^p488-11] Pilati concedendo et intentioni satisfaciendo. Concedit conclusionem Pilati, cum dicit: *Tu dicis, quia rex sum ego;* quasi dicat: ego non nego quod tu dicis; sed tamen non ad regnandum temporaliter veni, ut credis; unde subdit: *Ego in hoc natus sum et ad hoc veni in mundum, ut testimonium perhibeam veritati. Natus,* scilicet nativitate temporali, non ad dominandum, sed ad veritatem docendum; Isaiae quinquagesimo quinto[^p488-12]: « Ecce, testem populis dedi eum, ducem ac praeceptorem gentibus ». Et subdit huius signum: *Omnis, qui est ex veritate, audit vocem meam;* supra octavo: « Qui ex Deo est verba Dei audit ». Et quia Pilatus non adhuc plene intelligebat, ideo adhuc quaerit; unde sequitur:

66. (Vers. 38.). *Dicit ei Pilatus: Quid est veritas?* Huius quaestionis non audivit solutionem, quia non exspectavit. Ad hoc solvit Dominus supra decimo quarto[^p488-13]: « Ego sum via, veritas et vita ».

<!-- page 489 -->

### Quaestiones.

67. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic primo de hoc quod dicit: *Regnum meum non est de hoc mundo.* — Ex hoc arguunt haeretici[^p489-1], quod mundus iste non pertineat ad Deum lucis, sed tenebrarum.

**Contra:** Supra primo[^p489-2]: *In propria venit.* — *Si dicas,* quod *mundus* non dicitur natura, sed homines mundane viventes; *contra:* Matthaei decimo tertio[^p489-3] dicitur, quod *Angeli colligent de regno eius omnia scandala;* sed constat, quod scandala non sunt nisi in hoc mundo.

**Respondeo:** Dicit Chrysostomus[^p489-4] breviter, quod idem est, ac si dicat, regnum suum non esse mundanum ac corporale.

Aliter potest dici, quod *regnum Christi* dicuntur illi, in quibus ipse regnat — et ei obediunt — non per *potentiam,* sed per *obedientiam.* Tales autem solum sunt Sancti, et isti non sunt de mundo; supra decimo septimo[^p489-5]: *Non sunt de mundo, sicut et ego non sum de mundo.* Tamen sunt *in mundo;* unde ibidem: *Non rogo, ut eos tollas de mundo, sed ut custodias eos a malo.*

68. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Omnis, qui est ex veritate, audit vocem meam.* — Aut *ex* dicit *materiam,* et tunc *nullus* ex veritate; aut *efficiens*[^p489-6], et tunc *omnes;* sed tamen non omnes audiunt.

Respondet Augustinus[^p489-7], quod in hoc verbo « gratiam commendavit, qua *secundum propositum sunt* praedestinati et *vocati Sancti.* Nam si naturam cogitemus, in qua creati sumus; cum omnes Veritas creaverit, quis non est *ex veritate?* Sed non omnibus praestatur *ex veritate,* ut audiant veritatem; sed quibus datum est, illi audiunt », supra sexto.

69. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de hoc, quod Pilatus non exspectavit Domini responsionem in quaestione sua.

Respondet Augustinus[^p489-8], quod « subito venit illi in mentem consuetudo Iudaeorum, qua solebat unus dimitti illis in pascha »; unde quia volebat eum liberare, statim exiit.

Aliter respondet Chrysostomus[^p489-9], quod « sciebat, quod quaestio illa indigebat tempore, et ipse properabat ad liberationem Christi; ideo statim exiit ».

Aliter potest dici, quod quia Pilatus iudicium suum a veritate inchoavit, sed in veritate non permansit; ideo de veritate quaestionem meruit inchoare, sed non solutionem audire[^p489-10].

## English
<!-- page 488 -->

61. *He went in therefore again* etc. With the delivering up of Christ set down, by which the Jews delivered him up to Pilate, there is set down here his *examination made by Pilate*[^p488-4], in which four things are hinted: first, *the inquiry;* then, *the origin of the inquiry;* third, *the response to the inquiry;* fourth, *the discussion of the response.*

(Vers. 33.). First, then, is noted *the inquiry itself;* on account of which he says: *Pilate therefore went into the praetorium again;* because he had gone out to seek the cause from the Jews and had not found it, he was returning to inquire it of the guilty one; *and he called Jesus and said to him: Art thou the king of the Jews?* Truly he is the king, because he is the one of whom Jeremias, the twenty-third chapter[^p488-5]: « I will raise up to David a just branch, and a king shall reign ». Therefore he asks about this most of all, because by saying this he seemed to contradict Caesar, who then had dominion over the Jews.

62. (Vers. 34.). *Jesus answered* etc. The second thing is touched, namely *the origin of the inquiry;* and that the origin may be known, the Lord asks of Pilate: *Sayest thou this thing of thyself, or have others told it thee of me?* He does not ask because he doubts; but that through his response he may uncover the malice of the Jews[^p488-6]; and this indeed comes about in Pilate's response; hence:

63. (Vers. 35.). *Pilate answered: Am I a Jew?* as if he said: I do not impose this on thee; *thy own nation and thy high priests*[^p488-7] *have delivered thee up to me;* as if he said: I have received this from them: *What then hast thou done,* on account of which they so accuse thee?

64. (Vers. 36.). *Jesus answered.* Here is set down the third thing, namely *the response of the Lord,* in which the Lord himself confutes the depraved intention of the Jews. For the Jews intended by this to provoke Pilate and Caesar against him, as though he would not have a colleague in the kingdom; therefore the Lord excludes this, saying: *My kingdom is not of this world.* Augustine[^p488-8]: « Hear, Jews and Gentiles: *My kingdom is not of this world;* I do not hinder your dominion in this world. What more do you want »? — And he renders the reason why his kingdom is not of this world; and his reason is this: for a king of the earth the ministers contend[^p488-9], lest he be delivered to death; but for me my ministers do not strive: therefore I am not an earthly king. And he sets down the *major* of this reasoning: *If my kingdom were of this world, my ministers would certainly strive that I should not be delivered to the Jews; but now;* it appears, supply; this is the conclusion; that *my kingdom is not from hence,* because no one defends me. And therefore his kingdom is not of this world — because, as is said in the first of John, the last chapter[^p488-10], « the whole world is seated in wickedness »; therefore we ask: « Thy kingdom come » — but it is in the heavens; hence Matthew, the fifth chapter: « He that shall do and teach, he shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven » etc.

65. (Vers. 37.). *Pilate therefore said to him.* Here is set down the fourth thing, namely *the discussion of the response.* And because the Lord had said that he has a kingdom, Pilate argues from the Lord's response, as if wondering: *Then,* he says, *art thou a king?* as if he said: since thou confessest that thou art a king, it seems from thy words that thou wishest to reign. And the Lord indeed *answers,* conceding Pilate's conclusion[^p488-11] and satisfying his intention. He concedes Pilate's conclusion when he says: *Thou sayest that I am a king;* as if he said: I do not deny what thou sayest; but yet I did not come to reign temporally, as thou believest; hence he adds: *For this was I born, and for this came I into the world, that I should give testimony to the truth. Born,* namely by temporal nativity, not to have dominion, but to teach the truth; Isaias, the fifty-fifth chapter[^p488-12]: « Behold, I have given him for a witness to the peoples, for a leader and a master to the Gentiles ». And he adds the sign of this: *Every one that is of the truth heareth my voice;* above, the eighth chapter: « He that is of God heareth the words of God ». And because Pilate did not yet fully understand, therefore he still asks; hence there follows:

66. (Vers. 38.). *Pilate saith to him: What is truth?* He did not hear the solution of this question, because he did not wait. To this the Lord gives the solution above, the fourteenth chapter[^p488-13]: « I am the way, the truth and the life ».

<!-- page 489 -->

### Questions.

67. Quaest. I. But it is asked here, first, about this that he says: *My kingdom is not of this world.* — From this the heretics argue[^p489-1] that this world does not pertain to the God of light, but of darkness.

**On the contrary:** Above, the first chapter[^p489-2]: *He came unto his own.* — *If thou say* that *the world* is not said of the nature, but of men living worldly; *on the contrary:* in Matthew, the thirteenth chapter[^p489-3], it is said that *the Angels shall gather out of his kingdom all scandals;* but it is established that scandals are nowhere but in this world.

**I respond:** Chrysostom says[^p489-4] briefly that it is the same as if he said that his kingdom is not worldly and bodily.

Otherwise it can be said that *the kingdom of Christ* is the name for those in whom he reigns — and they obey him — not through *power,* but through *obedience.* But such are the Saints alone, and these are not of the world; above, the seventeenth chapter[^p489-5]: *They are not of the world, as I also am not of the world.* Yet they are *in the world;* hence in the same place: *I pray not that thou take them out of the world, but that thou guard them from evil.*

68. Quaest. II. Likewise it is asked about this that he says: *Every one that is of the truth heareth my voice.* — Either *of* signifies *the matter,* and then *no one* is of the truth; or *the efficient*[^p489-6], and then *all;* but yet not all hear.

Augustine responds[^p489-7] that in this word « he commended grace, by which *according to purpose* the Saints *are* predestined and *called.* For if we consider the nature in which we were created; since the Truth created all, who is not *of the truth*? But it is not granted to all *of the truth* that they hear the truth; but those to whom it has been given, they hear », above, the sixth chapter.

69. Quaest. III. Likewise it is asked about this, that Pilate did not wait for the Lord's response to his question.

Augustine responds[^p489-8] that « suddenly there came into his mind the custom of the Jews, by which one was wont to be released to them at the pasch »; hence, because he wished to free him, he went out straightway.

Otherwise Chrysostom responds[^p489-9] that « he knew that that question needed time, and he was hastening to the liberation of Christ; therefore he went out straightway ».

Otherwise it can be said that, because Pilate began his judgement from the truth, but did not remain in the truth, therefore he merited to begin a question about the truth, but not to hear the solution[^p489-10].
