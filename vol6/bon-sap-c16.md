# Commentarius in librum Sapientiae — Cap. XVI, Vers. 1-29
## *De poena sensus duplici*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 209 -->

### Capitulum XVI.

#### *Secundo determinatur de supplicio poenae culpabilium.*

*Propter haec et his similia* etc. Postquam a decimo tertio capitulo egit de merito culpae quoad poenas graviores ordinatas ad condemnationem impiorum, hic agit de *supplicio poenae,* et primo de poenis *antecedentibus mortem,* scilicet in isto capitulo et decimo septimo; secundo, de poenis *inferentibus mortem,* scilicet capitulo decimo octavo et decimo nono.

#### Primo agitur de duabus poenis antecedentibus mortem.

In prima primo agit de poena *sensus,* scilicet de plagarum immissione, scilicet capitulo isto; secundo, de poena *damni,* scilicet de lucis subtractione, capitulo decimo septimo.

#### De poena sensus duplici.

In prima primo de poena inflicta per res *animatas;* secundo, de poena inflicta per res *inanimatas: Negantes enim te.*

#### Prima poena sensus, inflicta per res animatas.

In prima primo tangitur Aegyptiorum per vilia animalia et nociva *punitio;* secundo, econtra Hebraeorum per delectabilia animalia *consolatio: Pro quibus* etc.; tertio, eorum ab animalibus nocivis *liberatio: Etenim;* quarto, praedictae punitionis et liberationis *ratio: In hoc autem.*

(Vers. 1.). *Propter haec,* scilicet quia miserrimi colebant idola[^p209-6], sive propter idololatriae peccata, *et his similia,* id est propter alia peccata idololatriae praedictis similia, *passi sunt digne tormenta;* ad Ephesios quinto: « Propter haec venit ira Dei in filios diffidentiae »; Deuteronomii vigesimo quinto: « Pro mensura peccati erit et plagarum modus ». Non enim debet esse dedecus culpae sine decore iustitiae vel vindictae, secundum Augustinum[^p209-7]. *Et per multitudinem bestiarum,* terram scilicet eorum vastantium, ut muscarum, locustarum et ranarum et huiusmodi; *exterminati sunt,* id est graviter afflicti, et forte aliqui eorum occisi; supra duodecimo: « Per haec quae coluerunt, dedisti eis summa tormenta »; Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo: « Dentes bestiarum immittam in eos ».

<!-- page 210 -->

*Pro quibus tormentis* etc. Hic tangitur *Israelitarum consolatio,* et primo ponitur consolationis *modus;* secundo, *causa: Ut illi;* tertio, *celeritas: Hi autem;* quarto, *accelerationis opportunitas: Oportebat* etc.

(Vers. 2.). *Pro quibus tormentis,* id est loco quorum tormentorum, quasi e contrario; *bene disposuisti populum tuum,* scilicet Israeliticum, qui erat eius « populus peculiaris », ut habetur Deuteronomii septimo[^p210-1]. *Quibus,* id est cui populo, *dedisti concupiscentiam delectamenti sui,* id est concupitum delectabile; « desiderium enim, si compleatur, delectat animam », ut habetur Proverbiorum decimo tertio. *Novum saporem,* in cibum novi saporis, *escam parans eis ortygometram,* et dicitur ille avis, scilicet coturnix, ortygometra, ut dicunt ab Ortygia terra, in qua primo visa est et inde abducta[^p210-2]; vel *escas parans* etc.; et tunc secundum aliquos ponitur hic plurale pro singulari, cum dicit: *escas parans eis ortygometram,* id est coturnicem, secundum illud Psalmi[^p210-3]: « Petierunt, et venit coturnix » etc.; Exodi decimo sexto: « Factum est vespere, et ascendens coturnix cooperuit castra »; item Numerorum undecimo: « Ventus autem Domini egrediens coturnices arreptas trans mare detulit » etc. Sed non oportet, quod pro singulari ponatur plurale, quia potest glossari sic: *escas,* id est loco escarum.

(Vers. 3.). *Ut illi quidem,* scilicet filii Israel, *concupiscentes escam,* scilicet carnium Aegypti, Exodi decimo sexto[^p210-4], *propter ea,* scilicet coturnices, *quae illis ostensa sunt,* scilicet ad desiderandum, *et missa,* scilicet ad comedendum, *etiam a necessaria concupiscentia,* id est valde urgenti, *averterentur,* id est, ne amplius carnes Aegypti et reditum in Aegyptum desiderarent; in Psalmo[^p210-5]: « Concupierunt concupiscentiam in deserto »; item Numerorum undecimo: « Populus flagravit desiderio carnium », cum tamen dicatur Ecclesiastici decimo octavo: « Post concupiscentias tuas non eas, et a voluntate tua avertere ». *Hi autem,* scilicet filii Israel, *in brevi inopes facti,* id est modico tempore, quia eorum inopia parum duravit; Deus enim cito subvenit, secundum illud Isaiae sexagesimo quinto[^p210-6]: « Antequam clament, ego exaudiam »; *inopes,* id est cibo indigentes; *novam gustaverant escam,* id est insolitam vel insolitarum avium.

(Vers. 4.). *Oportebat enim* etc., quasi dicat: et recte sic factum; *oportebat enim,* id est opportunum erat, *illis,* scilicet Aegyptiis, *sine excusatione quidem,* scilicet culpae, pro qua puniebantur; ad Romanos primo[^p210-7]: « Ita, ut sint inexcusabiles »; item ibidem secundo: « Propter quod inexcusabilis es, o homo »; *supervenire interitum,* scilicet poenae interimentis supplicium a summo iudice ad eorum oppressionem; Iob decimo octavo: « Avellatur de tabernaculo suo fiducia eius, et calcet super eum quasi rex interitus »; item Proverbiorum vigesimo nono: « Viro, qui corripientem dura cervice contempserit, repentinus ei superveniet interitus ». *Illis,* dico Aegyptiis, *exercentibus tyrannidem,* id est crudele imperium in filios Israel, opprimendo eos duris operibus, ut patet Exodi primo[^p210-8] et quinto, et in flumine pueros eorum submergendo, ut patet Exodi primo. *His autem,* scilicet Israeliticis, *tantum ostendere,* supple: oportebat; *quemadmodum inimici eorum exterminabantur,* ut sic, visa eorum poena, corrigerentur, secundum illud Proverbiorum decimo nono[^p210-9]: « Pestilente flagellato, stultus sapientior erit »; item ibidem vigesimo primo: « Mulctato pestilente, sapientior erit parvulus ».

*Etenim* etc. Hic primo ostenditur *periculi gravitas;* secundo, *liberationis celeritas: Sed non in perpetuum;* tertio, *liberantis virtus* sive potestas: *Qui enim.*

(Vers. 5.). Dicit ergo: *Etenim,* quasi: bene dixi: *his autem tantum ostendere* etc.; *etenim,* pro quia; *cum illis,* scilicet filiis Israel, *supervenit saeva bestiarum ira,* scilicet ab offensa superni iudicis, quem offenderant murmurando contra ipsum, ut patet Numerorum vigesimo primo[^p210-10]: « Misit Dominus in populum serpentes ignitos ». *Morsibus perversorum colubrorum,* quia efficaces erant ad nocendum, *exterminabantur,* scilicet aliqui eorum; unde primae ad Corinthios decimo: « Murmuraveritis, sicut quidam eorum murmuraverunt et a serpentibus perierunt ».

(Vers. 6.). *Sed non in perpetuum,* id est sine fine, ita quod poena aeterna temporali continuaretur, sicut in Herode, Actuum duodecimo[^p210-11]. *Ira tua,*
<!-- page 211 -->
id est irae tuae effectus, non animi tui affectus, secundum illud: « Ego Dominus, et non irascor »; *permansit,* scilicet in filiis Israel; Habacuc tertio: « Cum iratus fueris, misericordiae recordaberis ». *Sed ad correptionem,* non ad condemnationem, *in brevi,* id est modico tempore, *turbati sunt,* id est afflicti, scilicet exterius et inde *turbati,* interius; Isaiae quinquagesimo quarto[^p211-1]: « Ad punctum in modico dereliqui te etc., et in momento indignationis abscondi faciem meam parumper a te, et in misericordia sempiterna misertus sum tui »; Ieremiae trigesimo: « Te autem non faciam in consummationem, sed castigabo te in iudicio, ut non videaris tibi innoxius ». *Signum habentes salutis;* Glossa[^p211-2]: « Serpentem aeneum pro signo positum, cuius aspectu sanabantur », ut patet Numerorum vigesimo primo. *Ad commemorationem mandati legis tuae,* scilicet custodiendae, secundum illud Psalmi: « Et memores sunt mandatorum ipsius ad faciendum ea », sicut puer negligens flagellatur, ut lectionem suam repetat et confirmet.

(Vers. 7.). Et bene dixi: *signum habentes,* non causam; *qui enim conversus est,* scilicet vel interius per poenitentiam ad Deum, vel exterius ad serpentem aeneum ad aspiciendum; *non per id quod videbatur*[^p211-3], scilicet oculo corporali, *sanabatur,* id est non per serpentem aeneum; *sed per te, Salvatorem omnium,* per dictum serpentem significatum; Ioannis tertio: « Sicut Moyses exaltavit serpentem in deserto, ita exaltari oportet Filium hominis »; sicut etiam mortuus non potuit suscitari per Elisei baculum, cui non in hoc obediebat, sed per ipsum, ut habetur quarti Regum quarto.

*In hoc autem ostendisti* etc. Hic subiungitur praedictae *punitionis et liberationis ratio,* scilicet ostensio divinae iustitiae et misericordiae, et primo ex parte Aegyptiorum punitorum ostensio *divinae iustitiae;* secundo, ex parte Hebraeorum liberatorum ostensio *divinae misericordiae* et quaedam causae aliae: *Filios autem tuos.*

(Vers. 8.). *In hoc autem,* scilicet in Aegyptiorum afflictione et Israelitarum facili liberatione, *ostendisti,* id est, ostendere voluisti, quamvis illi non viderint; unde non possunt conqueri nisi de caecitate oculorum suorum, sicut si alicui lippo ostendatur sol lucens, et ille non videat, secundum Augustinum[^p211-4]; *inimicis nostris,* scilicet Aegyptiis; mali enim sunt inimici iustorum, scilicet active, non passive, quia scilicet odiunt iustos, quamvis diligantur ab eis; *ostendisti,* inquam, *quia tu es,* scilicet solus, *qui liberas ab omni malo;* in Psalmo[^p211-5]: « Multae tribulationes iustorum, et de omnibus his liberabit eos Dominus ».

**Contra:** Cur multi non liberantur a malo?

Dicendum, quod sic est intelligendum: liberat omnes, qui liberantur, sicut illud Ioannis primo[^p211-6]: « Illuminat omnem hominem » etc., scilicet qui illuminatur.

(Vers. 9.). *Illos enim,* scilicet Aegyptios te non colentes, *locustarum et muscarum occiderunt morsus; locustarum,* Exodi decimo[^p211-7]; et *muscarum,* Exodi octavo; *occiderunt,* ad litteram aliquem eorum, quamvis non legatur ibi expresse. *Et non est inventa sanitas animae illorum,* Aegyptiorum scilicet, sicut filiis Israel a serpentibus; in Psalmo: « Non est sanitas in carne mea a facie irae tuae » etc.; Ieremiae trigesimo: « Insanabilis fractura tua, pessima plaga tua ». *Quia digni erant,* scilicet exigentibus eorum meritis, *ab huiusmodi,* scilicet vilibus animalibus, *exterminari;* unde Augustinus super Ioannem[^p211-8]: « Populum superbum Pharaonis Dominus domare potuit ursis et leonibus, sed muscas et res vilissimas immisit, ut eorum superbia domaretur ». Eadem ratione et homines pulicibus affliguntur; unde Augustinus: « Quid te inflas, humana superbia? Pulicibus resiste, ut dormias ».

*Filios autem tuos* etc. Hic ostenditur multiplex *ratio liberationis* Hebraeorum; sed ut ordinate procedat, primo repetit eorum *liberationem;* secundo, *liberationis multiplicem rationem: Misericordia enim tua.*

(Vers. 10.). *Filios autem tuos,* id est Israelitas; Exodi quarto[^p211-9]: « Primogenitus meus Israel »,
<!-- page 212 -->
*filios,* inquam, *tuos,* per fidem, secundum illud Ioannis primo: « Dedit eis potestatem filios Dei fieri, his qui credunt in nomine eius »; *nec draconum venenatorum vicerunt dentes,* id est serpentum ignitorum; Deuteronomii octavo: « Ductor tuus fuit in solitudine magna atque terribili, in qua erat serpens flatu adurens et scorpio ac dipsas ». Loquitur hic de bonis, qui filii Dei sunt, non de malis.

*Misericordia enim tua;* ostenditur autem hic *ratio multiplex liberationis Israelitarum,* quarum prima fuit *pietas Dei;* secunda, *periculum desperationis: In memoria enim;* tertia, *virtus divini sermonis: Etenim neque herba;* quarta, *ostensio divinae pietatis: Tu es enim, Domine;* quinta, *humana fragilitas: Homo autem* etc.

Bene dixi: *filios autem tuos,* id est Israelitas, *nec draconum venenatorum dentes,* id est morsus serpentum ignitorum, *vicerunt; misericordia enim tua,* id est effectus misericordiae tuae, *adveniens sanabat illos;* in Psalmo[^p212-1]: « Si dicebam: Motus est pes meus; misericordia tua, Domine, adiuvabat me »; Ieremiae trigesimo tertio: « Obducam cicatricem tuam et dabo tibi sanitatem »; « ipse enim vulnerat et medetur », Iob quinto.

(Vers. 11.). *In memoria enim sermonum tuorum,* id est per remunerationem mandatorum tuorum, *exterminabantur,* aliqui scilicet illorum, *et velociter sanabantur,* vel *salvabantur*[^p212-2], scilicet *alii* poenitentes, visa morte vel poena aliorum; iuxta illud Psalmi: « Cum occiderent eos », id est aliquos ex eis, « quaerebant eum » id est alii poenitentes. Vel: iidem *exterminabantur,* id est graviter puniebantur, *et velociter sanabantur, ne in altam,* id est profundam, *incidentes,* id est intus cadentes, *oblivionem,* scilicet desperationis, scilicet propter adiutorii et remedii dilationem; *non possent tuo uti adiutorio,* id est gratiae tuae liberantis, ipsi, dico, facti indigni misericordia tua propter desperationem; Proverbiorum decimo octavo[^p212-3]: « Impius, cum in profundum peccatorum venerit, contemnit »; in Psalmo: « Obliti sunt Deum, qui salvavit eos » etc.

(Vers. 12.). Et bene dixi: *in memoria sermonum tuorum; etenim,* pro quia; *neque herba,* id est potio ex herbis confecta, quae bibendo intra sumitur; *neque malagma,* id est emplastrum, quod extra loco doloris superponitur; *sanavit eos,* scilicet a morsibus serpentum. *Sed sermo tuus, Domine,* id est mandatum de serpente aeneo faciendo et aspiciendo, Numerorum vigesimo primo[^p212-4]; *qui,* scilicet sermo, *sanat omnia,* scilicet quae sanantur; unde hic est *distributio accommoda;* in Psalmo: « Misit verbum suum et sanavit eos ». Vel: *Sermo,* increatus, id est Filius, de quo Ioannis primo: « In principio erat Verbum »; iste Sermo, secundum Glossam, « curat corpora et a spiritualibus bestiis animas sanat »; Matthaei quarto: « Sanans omnem languorem et omnem infirmitatem ».

(Vers. 13.). *Tu enim es, Domine.* Bene dixi, quod *sermo tuus sanat omnia; tu enim es, Domine,* scilicet Creator omnium, *qui vitae et mortis habes potestatem,* id est vivificandi et mortificandi, secundum illud primi Regum secundo[^p212-5]: « Dominus mortificat et vivificat » etc.; item Ecclesiastici undecimo: « Mors et vita a Deo sunt ».

**Sed contra:** Quia dicitur supra primo[^p212-6]: « Deus mortem non fecit ».

Respondeo: dicendum, quod quamvis mortis non sit Deus causa meritoria nec efficiens; quantum tamen ad ipsam privationem habet potestatem mortis, quia, licet non sit *causa malorum,* habet tamen *posse super mala,* quia ex eis scit et potest elicere bona.

Ad illud quod obiicitur, quod mors non est a Deo; dicendum, quod in morte est *privatio* vitae, quae nihil est, et ideo causam *efficientem* non habet. sed magis *deficientem;* item, est ibi *poenalis afflictio,* quae aliquid est et a Deo est, iuste infligente eam peccatoribus merito peccatorum suorum.

*Et deducis ad portas mortis,* id est ad extremum vitae articulum, *et reducis,* scilicet sanando, ut patet in Ezechia, Isaiae trigesimo octavo[^p212-7].

(Vers. 14.). *Homo autem* etc., quasi dicat: sic potes mortificare et vivificare; *autem,* pro sed; *homo* potest occidere se, sed non vivificare, et hoc est quod dicit: *homo autem,* scilicet peccator, dictus *homo* ab humo; *occidit quidem,* id est certe, *per malitiam,* culpae, *animam suam*[^p212-8], propter quod dicitur in Psalmo: « Qui diligit iniquitatem odit animam suam »; est enim homicida sui ipsius; peccatum enim est gladius bis acutus, occidit enim corpus et animam; Ecclesiastici vigesimo primo: « Quasi romphaea bis acuta omnis iniquitas ». *Et cum exierit spiritus,* scilicet a corpore per mortem, *non*
<!-- page 213 -->
*revertetur,* scilicet in corpus in praesenti saeculo; Iob decimo[^p213-1]: « Antequam vadam, et non revertar, ad terram tenebrosam » etc.; item secundi Regum decimo quarto: « Omnes morimur et quasi aquae dilabimur in terram, quae non revertuntur ».

**Sed contra:** Quia dicitur in Psalmo[^p213-2]: « Exibit spiritus eius et revertetur in terram suam ».

Dicendum, quod *hoc*[^p213-3] intelligitur de reversione ad terram suae naturalis habitationis et corporis per *naturam;* sed *illud Psalmi* de reversione in terram suam, secundum *iustitiam retributionis,* et hoc per *virtutem divinam.*

*Nec revocabit,* scilicet homo, *animam,* a corpore separatam, *quae recepta est,* in loco suo, scilicet in gloria, vel in poena; Ecclesiastici undecimo[^p213-4]: « Ubicumque ceciderit lignum, sive ad austrum, sive ad aquilonem, ibi erit ».

(Vers. 15.). *Sed tuam manum,* id est potestatem, tam in praesenti quam in futuro, tam in vita quam in morte; *effugere impossibile est;* Iob decimo[^p213-5]: « Non est qui de manu tua possit eruere »; ideo dicitur in Psalmo: « Quo ibo a spiritu tuo, et quo a facie tua fugiam »? « Non enim est abbreviata manus Domini », immo longissima et totum continens, secundum illud Psalmi: « In manu tua, Domine, omnes fines terrae ».

#### Secunda poena sensus, inflicta per res inanimatas.

*Negantes enim* etc. Hic agit de poenis inflictis Aegyptiis per *res inanimatas;* et primo tangitur *Aegyptiorum punitio;* secundo, e contrario *Israelitarum consolatio: Pro quibus Angelorum;* tertio, *punitionis Aegyptiorum ratio: Nix autem* etc.; quarto, *ratio consolationis Israelitarum: Propter hoc et tunc.*

In prima ponit punitionis Aegyptiorum *causam meritoriam;* secundo, *modum et formam: Quod enim mirabile* etc.; tertio, *diversitatem* secundum plus et minus: *Quodam enim tempore.*

(Vers. 16.). *Negantes enim* etc. Bene dixi: *impossibile est effugere manum tuam; negantes enim te nosse impii,* id est Aegyptii, scilicet Pharao cum servis suis; *negantes,* inquam, *verbo,* unde Exodi quinto[^p213-6]: « Nescio Dominum et Israel non dimittam »; et *facto* illi, de quibus ad Titum primo: « Confitentur, se nosse Deum, factis autem negant ». *Per fortitudinem brachii tui,* id est per fortem potentiam tuam; Exodi decimo quinto: « Quis similis tui in fortibus, Domine » etc.; *flagellati sunt;* Exodi sexto: « Educam eos de ergastulo Aegyptiorum in brachio excelso »; Exodi decimo quinto: « Dextera tua percussit inimicum »; *novis aquis; et,* pro id est; *grandinibus,* grando enim est quasi aqua congelata, *et pluviis,* quae sunt quasi aquae fluidae, *persecutionem passi* sunt; Exodi nono[^p213-7]: « Videns Pharao, quod cessasset pluvia et grando »; *et per ignem,* id est simul cadentem, *consumti;* Exodi nono: « Grando et ignis mixta pariter ferebantur »; item in Psalmo: « Posuit pluvias eorum grandinem, ignem comburentem in terra ipsorum ».

(Vers. 17.). Bene dixi: *per ignem* cum *grandinibus; quod enim mirabile erat;* scilicet contrarium a contrario non destrui, cum scriptum sit Ecclesiastici tertio[^p213-8]: « Ignem ardentem exstinguit aqua »; unde sequitur: *In aqua, quae omnia exstinguit,* scilicet ignita, *ignis plus valebat,* id est, fortius agebat cum aqua. *Vindex enim est orbis,* id est vindictae instrumentum; *vindex,* inquam, *orbis iustorum,* id est vindicans iustos; unde supra quinto: « Pugnabit pro eo orbis terrarum contra insensatos ». Per praedictum textum innuit, quod punitio Aegyptiorum facta fuit non secundum ordinem naturae, sed secundum ordinem divinae iustitiae vindicantis Israelitas de Aegyptiis, qui eos oppresserant, Exodi primo[^p213-9]; Glossa: « Non mirum, quod grando et ignis mixta feruntur, quia ad Creatoris voluntatem omnia consentiunt ».

(Vers. 18.). *Quodam enim tempore,* quasi dicat: non semper uniformiter agebat; *enim,* pro sed; *quodam tempore mansuetabatur ignis,* scilicet quousque animalia timentium sermones Moysi revocarentur vel reducerentur de agris; Exodi nono[^p213-10]: « Mitte et congrega iumenta tua et omnia, quae habes in agris », vel agro; et post: « Qui timuit verbum Domini de servis Pharaonis fecit confugere servos suos et iumenta in domos ». *Mansuetabatur,* inquam, *ne comburerentur animalia, quae missa erant ad impios,* id est iumenta divinitus eis data. Nec solum propter hoc, *sed* etiam, *ut ipsi videntes,* praedictam interpolationem, *scirent,* scilicet per ipsam, *quoniam Dei iudicio,* non eventu naturali,
<!-- page 214 -->
*patiuntur persecutionem.* Item, secundum Glossam[^p214-1], « ut cognoscerent, quoniam omnis creatura parata est ulcisci iniuriam Creatoris »; in Psalmo: « Cognoscetur Dominus iudicia faciens », qui, sicut dicit Bernardus, non est cognitus iniurias patiens. Et notandum, quod Dominus punivit Sodomitas per ignem, Genesis decimo nono[^p214-2], sine mansuetudine, et hoc, quia nulli erant boni inter eos; sed Aegyptios per ignem cum mansuetudine, quia filii Israel erant in Aegypto, qui boni erant.

(Vers. 19.). *Et quodam tempore,* scilicet revocatis animalibus, *in aqua supra virtutem,* naturalem scilicet, quia per miraculum, *ignis exardescebat undique,* scilicet per totam terram Aegypti, *ut iniquam*[^p214-3] *terrae nationem exterminaret,* id est Aegyptios, qui erant terrestris natio, non caelestis; contra quod primae ad Corinthios decimo quinto: « Sicut portavimus imaginem terreni, portemus imaginem caelestis ».

*Pro quibus* etc. Hic circa consolationem Israelitarum primo tangitur *exhibitio beneficii;* secundo, *ratio exhibendi: Substantiam*[^p214-4] *enim.*

(Vers. 20.). *Pro quibus,* scilicet malis, id est loco quorum malorum e contrario, *Angelorum esca,* id est manna, quod per Angelos erat praeparatum; *nutrivisti populum tuum* etc.; unde in Psalmo[^p214-5]: « Panem Angelorum manducavit homo »; vel quia praefigurabat escam Angelorum, scilicet « panem vivum, qui de caelo descendit », Ioannis sexto. *Nutrivisti,* inquam, *populum tuum,* scilicet filios Israel; hoc enim cibo nutrivit eos, « quousque tangerent fines terrae promissionis », ut habetur Exodi decimo sexto[^p214-6]. *Et paratum panem,* id est refectionem, *de caelo,* scilicet aëreo descendentem, secundum illud Psalmi: « Pluit illis manna ad manducandum, et panem caeli dedit eis »; *praestitisti illis sine labore.*

**Contra:** Quia dicitur Numerorum undecimo[^p214-7]: « Circuibat populus et colligens illud frangebat mola, sive terebat in mortario, coquens in olla et faciens ex eo tortulas quasi saporis panis oleati ».

Respondeo: *sine labore* acquirendi per agriculturam, sed non sine labore parandi. Nos vero laborem habemus in acquirendo et parando; Genesis tertio[^p214-8]: « In sudore vultus tui ».

*Omne delectamentum in se habentem,* id est effectum omnis cibi delectantis, faciendo similem delectationem in comedente; *et omnis saporis suavitatem,* id est omnem suavem saporem; taliter enim unicuique sapiebat, sicut volebat, ut dicit Gregorius in sexto Moralium[^p214-9].

**Sed contra:** Dicitur Numerorum undecimo[^p214-10], quod habebat saporem « quasi panis oleati ».

Dicendum, quod de natura sua habebat determinatum saporem dulcissimum, scilicet sicut sapor « similae cum melle[^p214-11] »; sed per gratiam divinam habebat sapores diversos, scilicet secundum desideriorum diversitatem, ut tangit Glossa super illud primae ad Corinthios decimo[^p214-12]: « Omnes eandem escam spiritualem manducaverunt ».

**Sed contra:** Quia, si habebat saporem a quolibet desideratum, ergo non desiderassent carnes Aegypti, nec nauseasset anima eorum super manna tanquam super cibo levissimo, cum tamen oppositum legatur Numerorum vigesimo primo[^p214-13].

Dicendum secundum Glossam[^p214-14]: Boni saporem optimum ibi inveniebant, sed mali fastidiebant. Vel dicendum, quod requirebant in cibo non solum delectationem saporis quoad gustum, sed etiam coloris quoad aspectum, et odoris quoad odoratum. Hieronymus[^p214-15]: Aves coram divitibus in suis vaporibus decoquuntur, ut visu et nidore reficiantur, sicut deliciosi in vino, cum tamen dicatur Proverbiorum
<!-- page 215 -->
vigesimo tertio[^p215-1]: « Ne intuearis vinum, quando flavescit, cum splenduerit in vitro » etc.

(Vers. 21.). *Substantiam enim tuam,* id est tuam virtutem sustentandi, *et dulcedinem tuam*[^p215-2], id est potestatem delectandi, *quam habes in filios,* scilicet eos sustentando per cibi substantiam et delectando per eiusdem cibi saporem, *ostendebas* etc.; in Psalmo: « Parasti in dulcedine tua pauperi, Deus »; *ostendebas,* inquam, conferendo eis praedictam escam sustentantem et delectantem. *Et deserviens,* scilicet cibus vel panis praedictus, *uniuscuiusque voluntati,* scilicet comedentium, *ad quod quisque volebat convertebatur,* id est ad qualem saporem; habebat enim, ut dictum est[^p215-3], unum saporem determinatum per naturam, sed varios per divinam gratiam.

*Mystice* per manna significabatur congrue Christus sub Sacramento altaris, primo quia ipse est panis angelorum, scilicet sacerdotum, secundum illud Malachiae secundo[^p215-4]: « Angelus Domini exercituum est ». — Secundo, quia caelitus paratur, id est, quia virtute caelesti conficitur; Ioannis sexto: « Ego sum panis vivus, qui de caelo descendi »; in Psalmo: « Parasti cibum illorum, quoniam ita est praeparatio eius ». — Tertio, quia comedentes ex eo pro suo desiderio, id est devotione, reficiuntur, secundum illud Psalmi[^p215-5]: « Desiderium animae eius tribuisti ei »; item in Psalmo: « Desiderium eorum attulit eis, non sunt fraudati a desiderio suo ». — Quarto, quia cum ipso ros gratiae descendit et datur, sicut et ros cum manna descendebat, ut habetur Numerorum undecimo[^p215-6]. Et ideo panis iste *eucharistia* dicitur; eucharistia *bona gratia* interpretatur. — Quinto, quia, sicut apparebat manna ut « semen coriandri album[^p215-7] », sic et Christus sub nebula candida sumitur; Apocalypsis decimo quarto: « Ecce, nubem candidam, et supra nubem sedentem similem Filio hominis ». — Sexto, quia sex diebus, et non in Sabbato colligebatur[^p215-8], sic Christus in praesenti sub Sacramento sumitur, sed in futuro Sabbato, de quo Isaiae sexagesimo sexto: « Erit Sabbatum ex Sabbato », aperte videbitur; primae ad Corinthios decimo tertio: « Tunc autem facie ad faciem »; item primae Ioannis tertio: « Videbimus eum, sicuti est ». — Septimo, quia usque ad ingressum terrae promissionis in deserto per quadraginta annos cibo illo nutriti sunt[^p215-9], sic usque ad finem mundi in deserto huius mundi fideles Ecclesiae nutriuntur, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo octavo: « Ecce, ego vobiscum sum omnibus diebus usque ad consummationem saeculi ».

*Nix autem* etc. Hic tangitur *ratio afflictionis* Aegyptiorum: primo ex parte *punitorum;* secundo, ex parte *eorum, pro quibus puniebantur: Hoc*[^p215-10] *autem;* tertio, ex parte *Dei punientis: Creatura.*

(Vers. 22.). Dicit ergo: *Nix autem* etc. Ita dictum est[^p215-11], quod *ignis in aqua plus valebat; nix autem et glacies,* id est grando, *sustinebant vim ignis,* id est calorem, qui est eius qualitas activae virtutis. *Et non tabescebant,* id est, non liquefiebant; unde Exodi nono[^p215-12]: « Pluit Dominus grandinem super terram Aegypti; et grando et ignis mixta pariter ferebantur »; *ut scirent,* scilicet Israelitae, *quoniam fructus inimicorum,* id est Aegyptiorum, *exterminabat ignis ardens,* scilicet per calorem; *in grandine et pluvia coruscans,* scilicet per splendorem. « Linum et hordeum laesa sunt, eo quod hordeum virens esset, et linum iam folliculos germinaret », Exodi nono.

(Vers. 23.). *Hoc autem iterum* etc. Sciendum, quod ly[^p215-13] *hoc* potest esse ablativi casus, ut sic exponatur: *hoc,* pro hôc; vel nominativi, ita quod sic legatur: *hoc autem iterum,* supple: factum est, *ut nutrirentur iusti,* scilicet filii Israel. *Ignis*[^p215-14] *etiam suae virtutis oblitus est,* scilicet non exterminando fructus terrae iustorum, ut patet Exodi nono, ubi legitur « tantum in terra Gessen, ubi erant filii Israel, grando non cecidisse ». Sic et vim virtutis suae oblitus est ignis, quando tres pueros iustos in fornace non laesit, ut patet Danielis tertio.

(Vers. 24.). *Creatura enim tua*[^p215-15], scilicet quaecumque, *tibi factori deserviens,* id est tuae voluntati, qua omnia fecisti, secundum illud Psalmi: « Omnia, quaecumque voluit, fecit »; *excandescit,* id est, vehementer movetur et concitatur, *in tormentum,* punitionis, *adversus iniustos,* id est inimicos
<!-- page 216 -->
tuos. *Et lenior fit,* id est innociva vel innoxia, *ad benefaciendum pro his qui in te confidunt,* id est pro iustis; obedientibus enim Deo creaturae obediunt, — ut patet Iosue decimo[^p216-1], ubi legitur, quomodo sol stetit ad imperium Iosue — et rebellantibus rebellant; unde supra quinto: « Pugnabit pro eo orbis terrarum contra insensatos ».

*Propter hoc* etc. Hic tangitur *ratio consolationis* Israelitarum, primo, *recognitio divini beneficii;* secundo, *exhibitio cultus divini: Quod enim ab igne;* tertio, *vitatio ingratitudinis: Ingrati enim spes* etc.

(Vers. 25.). *Propter hoc,* scilicet quia creatura deservit Creatori, *et tunc,* scilicet quando filii Israel in deserto manna pascebantur, *in omnia,* id est in omnium rerum desideratarum saporem, *transfigurata,* scilicet ipsa creatura, scilicet manna; *omnium nutrici gratiae tuae,* id est gratuitae bonae voluntati tuae, ita quod intelligatur de gratia *increata;* Baruch quarto[^p216-2]: « Contristastis nutricem vestram Ierusalem »; *deserviebant*[^p216-3], id est obediebant; Glossa: « Confundantur hic peccatores, quia cum ceterae creaturae Factori suo deserviant, ipsi repugnant. Unde creaturarum omnium concordia flagellantur ». *Deserviebant,* inquam, *ad voluntatem,* complendam scilicet, *eorum qui a te desiderati sunt,* scilicet speciali amore dilecti, ut filii Israel; Deuteronomii septimo: « Te elegit Dominus Deus tuus, ut sis ei populus peculiaris de cunctis populis, qui sunt super terram ».

(Vers. 26.). *Ut scirent filii tui,* scilicet Israelitae, secundum illud Exodi quarto[^p216-4]: « Primogenitus meus Israel »; Isaiae primo: « Filios enutrivi »; *quos dilexisti, Domine,* scilicet aeterna dilectione et temporali; Malachiae primo: « Dilexi vos, dicit Dominus ». *Scirent,* inquam, *quoniam non nativitatis fructus,* id est non naturales fructus, de terra nati, secundum illud Genesis primo: « Germinet terra » etc.; *pascunt homines,* scilicet in quantum sunt homines, id est, secundum illud quo differunt a bestiis, scilicet secundum rationalem animae potentiam vel partem. *Sed sermo tuus,* id est creatus, id est mandatum tuum; Matthaei quarto[^p216-5]: « Non in solo pane vivit homo » etc. Vel: *Sermo,* increatus, id est Filius, de quo Ioannis decimo septimo: « Sermo tuus veritas est »; ibidem decimo quarto: « Ego sum via et veritas et vita »; *hos qui in te crediderunt, conservat;* Deuteronomii octavo: « Dedit tibi Dominus cibum manna, quod ignorabas tu et patres tui, ut ostenderet tibi, quod non in solo pane vivit homo, sed in omni verbo, quod procedit ex ore Dei ».

(Vers. 27.) *Quod enim ab igne non poterat exterminari,* scilicet manna, quia ad ignem durescebat, ita quod exinde fierent tortulae, ut patet Numerorum undecimo[^p216-6]; *statim ab exiguo radio solis calefactum tabescebat,* id est liquefiebat, ut patet Exodi decimo sexto. Unde oportebat, quod mane colligeretur.

(Vers. 28.). *Ut notum omnibus esset,* scilicet per hoc signum, *quoniam oportet praevenire solem,* id est solis ortum, *ad tuam benedictionem,* supple: obtinendam, hoc est ad recipiendum et colligendum manna, ne post ortum solis liquefieret calore solis; sic ad benedictionem gratiae obtinendam oportet praevenire solem mane vigilando, secundum illud Proverbiorum octavo[^p216-7]: « Qui mane vigilaverint ad me invenient me »; in Psalmo: « Mane astabo tibi et videbo » etc. *Et ad ortum lucis,* id est, statim ut oritur lux, vel versus orientem, ubi lux oritur, *te adorare,* scilicet pro receptione ipsius mannae; versus orientem, inquam, quia, sicut dicit Damascenus[^p216-8], omne melius est Deo tribuendum; unde quia oriens est melior pars caeli, ideo ad orientem est adorandum, secundum illud Psalmi: « Qui ascendit super caelum caeli ad orientem[^p216-9] ».

<!-- page 217 -->

Notandum autem, quod in hoc, quod manna in ortu solis liquefiebat, significabatur secundum Glossam[^p217-1], in ortu Christi sacramentum vetus cessaturum, quia « finis Legis Christus », ad Romanos decimo.

(Vers. 29.). *Ingrati enim spes* etc. Ita dixi, quod *tabescebat* calore *solis,* et hoc in signum huius, quia *spes ingrati tabescet* etc. Vel sic: bene dixi, quod oportet *adorare ad ortum lucis* etc., pro receptione praedicti beneficii, et hoc ad vitandam ingratitudinem; quae valde damnosa est. *Ingrati enim spes,* scilicet cuiuscumque impii, vel specialiter « Iudaici populi », secundum Glossam[^p217-2], *tanquam hibernalis glacies;* Glossa: « Constricta frigore infidelitatis », secundum illud Ecclesiastici quadragesimo tertio: « Frigidus ventus aquilo flavit, et gelavit crystallus ab aqua »; *tabescet,* id est liquefiet, id est, calore solis iustitiae dissolvetur in die iudicii vel particularis, vel universalis; in Psalmo: « Sicut fluit cera a facie ignis, sic pereant peccatores a facie Dei ». *Tabescet,* inquam, quoad poenam *damni,* scilicet amittendo omnia bona, quaecumque habuit vel speravit, scilicet bona gratiae et gloriae; *et disperiet,* id est, dupliciter peribit, id est, in corpore et anima patietur tormentum, et hoc quantum ad poenam *sensus. Disperiet,* inquam, *tanquam aqua supervacua,* id est inutilis, quae in terram effunditur vel proiicitur; sic et ingratus tanquam inutilis in infernum proiicietur, iuxta illud Matthaei vigesimo quinto[^p217-3]: « Servum inutilem eiicite in tenebras exteriores ». *Ingrati* ergo *spes tabescet et disperiet* etc.; unde supra quinto: « Spes impii tanquam lanugo, quae a vento tollitur »; item Bernardus[^p217-4]: « Ingratitudo est ventus urens, fontem pietatis exsiccans, rorem misericordiae, fluenta gratiae ».

### Chapter XVI.

#### *Secondly is determined the penalty of the punishment of the culpable.*

*For these things and the like to these* etc. After he has treated, from the thirteenth chapter, of the merit of fault as regards the heavier punishments ordained to the condemnation of the impious, here he treats of the *penalty of punishment,* and first of the punishments *preceding death,* namely in this chapter and the seventeenth; secondly, of the punishments *inflicting death,* namely in the eighteenth and nineteenth chapter.

#### First are treated the two punishments preceding death.

In the first, first he treats of the punishment *of sense,* namely of the sending of plagues, namely in this chapter; secondly, of the punishment *of loss,* namely of the withdrawal of light, in the seventeenth chapter.

#### On the twofold punishment of sense.

In the first, first of the punishment inflicted through *animate* things; secondly, of the punishment inflicted through *inanimate* things: *For denying thee.*

#### The first punishment of sense, inflicted through animate things.

In the first, first is touched the *punishing* of the Egyptians through vile and noxious animals; secondly, by contrast, the *consolation* of the Hebrews through delightful animals: *Instead of which* etc.; thirdly, their *deliverance* from the noxious animals: *For indeed;* fourthly, the *reason* of the aforesaid punishing and deliverance: *But in this.*

(Vers. 1.). *For these things,* namely because most miserable they worshipped idols[^p209-6], or for the sins of idolatry, *and the like to these,* that is, for other sins of idolatry like to the aforesaid, *they suffered torments worthily;* to the Ephesians, the fifth: « For these things the wrath of God cometh upon the children of unbelief »; Deuteronomy, the twenty-fifth: « According to the measure of the sin shall the measure also of the stripes be ». For there ought not to be the disgrace of fault without the comeliness of justice or of vengeance, according to Augustine[^p209-7]. *And by a multitude of beasts,* namely laying waste their land, as of flies, locusts and frogs and such; *they were destroyed,* that is, grievously afflicted, and perhaps some of them slain; above, the twelfth: « By these things which they worshipped, thou gavest them the greatest torments »; Deuteronomy, the thirty-second: « I will send the teeth of beasts upon them ».

<!-- page 210 -->

*Instead of which torments* etc. Here is touched the *consolation of the Israelites,* and first is set down the *manner* of the consolation; secondly, the *cause: That they;* thirdly, the *swiftness: But these;* fourthly, the *opportuneness of the hastening: For it behoved* etc.

(Vers. 2.). *Instead of which torments,* that is, in place of which torments, as if by contrast; *thou didst well dispose thy people,* namely the Israelite people, which was his « peculiar people », as is had in Deuteronomy, the seventh[^p210-1]. *To whom,* that is, to which people, *thou gavest the concupiscence of their delight,* that is, the coveted delightful thing; « for desire, if it be fulfilled, delighteth the soul », as is had in Proverbs, the thirteenth. *A new taste,* for food of a new taste, *preparing for them as food the ortygometra,* and that bird, namely the quail, is called ortygometra, as they say, from the land of Ortygia, in which it was first seen and from there brought away[^p210-2]; or *preparing as foods* etc.; and then, according to some, the plural is set here for the singular, when he says: *preparing as foods for them the ortygometra,* that is, the quail, according to that of the Psalm[^p210-3]: « They asked, and the quail came » etc.; Exodus, the sixteenth: « It came to pass in the evening, and the quail coming up covered the camp »; likewise Numbers, the eleventh: « And the wind of the Lord going out, taking quails up beyond the sea, brought them » etc. But it is not necessary that the plural be set for the singular, because it can be glossed thus: *as foods,* that is, in place of foods.

(Vers. 3.). *That they indeed,* namely the children of Israel, *coveting food,* namely of the flesh of Egypt, Exodus, the sixteenth[^p210-4], *by means of those things,* namely the quails, *which were shown to them,* namely for desiring, *and sent,* namely for eating, *might be turned away even from necessary concupiscence,* that is, very urgent, that is, so that they might no longer desire the flesh of Egypt and the return into Egypt; in the Psalm[^p210-5]: « They coveted concupiscence in the desert »; likewise Numbers, the eleventh: « The people burned with the desire of flesh », although it is said in Ecclesiasticus, the eighteenth: « Go not after thy concupiscences, and turn away from thy will ». *But these,* namely the children of Israel, *being made needy in a short time,* that is, in a little time, because their neediness lasted but little; for God came quickly to their aid, according to that of Isaias, the sixty-fifth[^p210-6]: « Before they call, I will hear »; *needy,* that is, lacking food; *had tasted a new food,* that is, an unaccustomed one, or one of unaccustomed birds.

(Vers. 4.). *For it behoved* etc., as if to say: and rightly was it so done; *for it behoved,* that is, it was opportune, *upon them,* namely the Egyptians, *without excuse indeed,* namely of the fault for which they were being punished; to the Romans, the first[^p210-7]: « So that they are inexcusable »; likewise in the same, the second: « Wherefore thou art inexcusable, O man »; *destruction to come,* namely the penalty of slaying punishment from the supreme judge, to their oppression; Job, the eighteenth: « Let his confidence be rooted out of his tabernacle, and let destruction tread upon him like a king »; likewise Proverbs, the twenty-ninth: « The man that with a stiff neck despiseth him that reproveth him, sudden destruction shall come upon him ». *Upon them,* I say the Egyptians, *exercising tyranny,* that is, cruel rule over the children of Israel, by oppressing them with hard works, as is clear from Exodus, the first[^p210-8] and the fifth, and by drowning their boys in the river, as is clear from Exodus, the first. *But to these,* namely the Israelites, *only to show,* supply: it behoved; *how their enemies were destroyed,* so that thus, having seen their punishment, they might be corrected, according to that of Proverbs, the nineteenth[^p210-9]: « The pestilent man being scourged, the fool shall be wiser »; likewise in the same, the twenty-first: « When a pestilent man is punished, the little one will be wiser ».

*For indeed* etc. Here first is shown the *gravity of the danger;* secondly, the *swiftness of the deliverance: But not for ever;* thirdly, the *strength* or power *of the deliverer: For he that.*

(Vers. 5.). He says, then: *For indeed,* as if: I said well: *but to these only to show* etc.; *for indeed* (*etenim*), for *quia* ("because"); *when upon these,* namely the children of Israel, *the fierce rage of beasts came,* namely from the offence of the supreme judge, whom they had offended by murmuring against him, as is clear from Numbers, the twenty-first[^p210-10]: « The Lord sent among the people fiery serpents ». *By the bitings of perverse serpents,* because they were efficacious for harming, *they were destroyed,* namely some of them; whence the first to the Corinthians, the tenth: « Murmur ye, as some of them murmured and perished by the serpents ».

(Vers. 6.). *But not for ever,* that is, without end, so that eternal punishment would be continued with the temporal, as in Herod, Acts, the twelfth[^p210-11]. *Thy wrath*
<!-- page 211 -->
that is, the effect of thy wrath, not the affection of thy mind, according to that: « I am the Lord, and I am not angry »; *endured,* namely in the children of Israel; Habacuc, the third: « When thou art angry, thou wilt remember mercy ». *But for correction,* not for condemnation, *in a short time,* that is, in a little time, *they were troubled,* that is, afflicted, namely outwardly, and thence *troubled* inwardly; Isaias, the fifty-fourth[^p211-1]: « For a small moment have I forsaken thee etc., in a moment of indignation have I hid my face a little while from thee, and with everlasting mercy have I had mercy on thee »; Jeremias, the thirtieth: « But thee I will not make an utter end of, but I will chastise thee in judgment, that thou mayest not seem to thyself innocent ». *Having a sign of salvation;* the Gloss[^p211-2]: « The brazen serpent set up for a sign, by the sight of which they were healed », as is clear from Numbers, the twenty-first. *For the remembrance of the commandment of thy law,* namely of keeping it, according to that of the Psalm: « And are mindful of his commandments to do them », as a negligent boy is beaten, that he may repeat his lesson and make it firm.

(Vers. 7.). And I said well: *having a sign,* not a cause; *for he that was turned,* namely either inwardly by penance to God, or outwardly to the brazen serpent for looking on it; *not by that which was seen*[^p211-3], namely with the bodily eye, *was he healed,* that is, not by the brazen serpent; *but by thee, the Saviour of all,* signified by the said serpent; John, the third: « As Moses lifted up the serpent in the desert, so must the Son of man be lifted up »; as also the dead child could not be raised by the staff of Eliseus, which he did not obey in this, but by Eliseus himself, as is had in the fourth of Kings, the fourth.

*But in this thou didst show* etc. Here is subjoined the *reason of the aforesaid punishing and deliverance,* namely the showing of divine justice and mercy, and first, on the part of the punished Egyptians, the showing of *divine justice;* secondly, on the part of the delivered Hebrews, the showing of *divine mercy* and certain other causes: *But thy children.*

(Vers. 8.). *But in this,* namely in the affliction of the Egyptians and the easy deliverance of the Israelites, *thou didst show,* that is, thou didst will to show, although they did not see; whence they cannot complain except of the blindness of their own eyes, as if the shining sun were shown to someone blear-eyed, and he did not see it, according to Augustine[^p211-4]; *to our enemies,* namely the Egyptians; for the wicked are enemies of the just, namely actively, not passively, because, namely, they hate the just, although they are loved by them; *thou didst show,* I say, *that thou art he,* namely alone, *who deliverest from all evil;* in the Psalm[^p211-5]: « Many are the afflictions of the just; but out of them all will the Lord deliver them ».

**On the contrary:** Why are many not delivered from evil?

It must be said that it is to be understood thus: he delivers all who are delivered, as that of John, the first[^p211-6]: « Enlighteneth every man » etc., namely who is enlightened.

(Vers. 9.). *For them,* namely the Egyptians who did not worship thee, *the bitings of locusts and of flies killed; of locusts,* Exodus, the tenth[^p211-7]; and *of flies,* Exodus, the eighth; *killed,* literally some of them, although it is not read there expressly. *And there was found no healing for their soul,* namely of the Egyptians, as there was for the children of Israel from the serpents; in the Psalm: « There is no health in my flesh, because of thy wrath » etc.; Jeremias, the thirtieth: « Thy bruise is incurable, thy wound is very grievous ». *Because they were worthy,* namely their merits requiring it, *by such things,* namely by vile animals, *to be destroyed;* whence Augustine on John[^p211-8]: « The Lord could have tamed the proud people of Pharao with bears and lions, but he sent flies and the vilest things, that their pride might be tamed ». For the same reason men too are afflicted with fleas; whence Augustine: « Why dost thou puff thyself up, human pride? Resist the fleas, that thou mayest sleep ».

*But thy children* etc. Here is shown the manifold *reason of the deliverance* of the Hebrews; but that he may proceed in order, first he repeats their *deliverance;* secondly, the *manifold reason of the deliverance: For thy mercy.*

(Vers. 10.). *But thy children,* that is, the Israelites; Exodus, the fourth[^p211-9]: « Israel is my firstborn »,
<!-- page 212 -->
*thy children,* I say, by faith, according to that of John, the first: « He gave them power to be made the sons of God, to them that believe in his name »; *not even the teeth of venomous dragons overcame,* that is, of fiery serpents; Deuteronomy, the eighth: « Who was thy leader in the great and terrible wilderness, wherein there was the serpent burning with his breath, and the scorpion and the dipsas ». He speaks here of the good, who are sons of God, not of the wicked.

*For thy mercy;* now here is shown the *manifold reason of the deliverance of the Israelites,* of which the first was the *piety of God;* the second, the *danger of despair: For in the remembrance;* the third, the *power of the divine word: For neither herb;* the fourth, the *showing of the divine piety: For it is thou, O Lord;* the fifth, *human frailty: But a man* etc.

I said well: *but thy children,* that is, the Israelites, *not even the teeth of venomous dragons,* that is, the bitings of fiery serpents, *overcame; for thy mercy,* that is, the effect of thy mercy, *coming healed them;* in the Psalm[^p212-1]: « If I said: My foot is moved: thy mercy, O Lord, assisted me »; Jeremias, the thirty-third: « I will close thy scar and give thee health »; « for he woundeth and cureth », Job, the fifth.

(Vers. 11.). *For in the remembrance of thy words,* that is, through the remuneration of thy commandments, *they were destroyed,* namely some of them, *and were quickly healed,* or *saved*[^p212-2], namely *others* doing penance, having seen the death or punishment of the others; according to that of the Psalm: « When he slew them », that is, some of them, « then they sought him », that is, the others doing penance. Or: the same *were destroyed,* that is, were grievously punished, *and were quickly healed, lest into deep,* that is, profound, *falling,* that is, falling within, *forgetfulness,* namely of despair, namely on account of the delay of help and remedy, *they should not be able to use thy help,* that is, of thy delivering grace, they themselves, I say, having become unworthy of thy mercy on account of despair; Proverbs, the eighteenth[^p212-3]: « The impious man, when he is come into the depth of sins, contemneth »; in the Psalm: « They forgot God, who saved them » etc.

(Vers. 12.). And I said well: *in the remembrance of thy words; for indeed* (*etenim*), for *quia* ("because"); *neither herb,* that is, a potion made from herbs, which is taken within by drinking; *nor mollifying plaster,* that is, a plaster which is laid on outside at the place of the pain; *healed them,* namely from the bitings of the serpents. *But thy word, O Lord,* that is, the commandment about making and looking at the brazen serpent, Numbers, the twenty-first[^p212-4]; *which,* namely the word, *healeth all things,* namely which are healed; whence here there is an *accommodated distribution;* in the Psalm: « He sent his word and healed them ». Or: *the Word,* uncreated, that is, the Son, of whom John, the first: « In the beginning was the Word »; this Word, according to the Gloss, « cures bodies and heals souls from the spiritual beasts »; Matthew, the fourth: « Healing every disease and every infirmity ».

(Vers. 13.). *For it is thou, O Lord.* I said well that *thy word healeth all things; for it is thou, O Lord,* namely the Creator of all, *who hast power of life and death,* that is, of quickening and of killing, according to that of the first of Kings, the second[^p212-5]: « The Lord killeth and maketh alive » etc.; likewise Ecclesiasticus, the eleventh: « Death and life are from God ».

**But on the contrary:** Because it is said above, the first[^p212-6]: « God made not death ».

I respond: it must be said that, although God is not the meritorious nor the efficient cause of death; yet as regards the privation itself he has power over death, because, although he is not the *cause of evils,* yet he has *power over evils,* because out of them he knows how, and is able, to draw forth goods.

To that which is objected, that death is not from God; it must be said that in death there is the *privation* of life, which is nothing, and therefore it has no *efficient* cause, but rather a *deficient* one; likewise, there is in it a *penal affliction,* which is something and is from God, who justly inflicts it on sinners by the merit of their sins.

*And leadest down to the gates of death,* that is, to the last moment of life, *and bringest back again,* namely by healing, as is clear in Ezechias, Isaias, the thirty-eighth[^p212-7].

(Vers. 14.). *But a man* etc., as if to say: thus thou canst kill and make alive; *but* (*autem*), for *sed;* *a man* can kill himself, but not make alive, and this is what he says: *but a man,* namely the sinner, called *homo* from *humus* ("earth"); *killeth indeed,* that is, certainly, *through malice,* of fault, *his own soul*[^p212-8], on account of which it is said in the Psalm: « He that loveth iniquity hateth his own soul »; for he is the murderer of himself; for sin is a two-edged sword, for it kills body and soul; Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-first: « All iniquity is like a two-edged sword ». *And when the spirit is gone forth,* namely from the body by death, *it shall not*
<!-- page 213 -->
*return,* namely into the body in the present age; Job, the tenth[^p213-1]: « Before I go, and return no more, to a land that is dark » etc.; likewise the second of Kings, the fourteenth: « We all die, and like waters that return no more, we fall down into the earth ».

**But on the contrary:** Because it is said in the Psalm[^p213-2]: « His spirit shall go forth, and he shall return into his earth ».

It must be said that *this*[^p213-3] is understood of the return to the land of its natural dwelling and of the body by *nature;* but *that of the Psalm,* of the return into his earth according to the *justice of retribution,* and this by *divine power.*

*Neither shall he call back,* namely the man, *the soul,* separated from the body, *that is received,* in its place, namely in glory, or in punishment; Ecclesiasticus [so printed; the passage is Eccles. 11:3], the eleventh[^p213-4]: « Wheresoever the tree shall fall, whether to the south or to the north, there shall it be ».

(Vers. 15.). *But thy hand,* that is, thy power, as much in the present as in the future, as much in life as in death; *it is impossible to escape;* Job, the tenth[^p213-5]: « There is none that can deliver out of thy hand »; therefore it is said in the Psalm: « Whither shall I go from thy spirit, and whither shall I flee from thy face »? « For the hand of the Lord is not shortened », nay, it is most long and contains the whole, according to that of the Psalm: « In thy hand, O Lord, are all the ends of the earth ».

#### The second punishment of sense, inflicted through inanimate things.

*For denying* etc. Here he treats of the punishments inflicted on the Egyptians through *inanimate things;* and first is touched the *punishing of the Egyptians;* secondly, by contrast, the *consolation of the Israelites: Instead of which… of Angels;* thirdly, the *reason of the punishing of the Egyptians: But snow* etc.; fourthly, the *reason of the consolation of the Israelites: Therefore even then.*

In the first he sets down the *meritorious cause* of the punishing of the Egyptians; secondly, the *manner and form: And which was wonderful* etc.; thirdly, the *diversity* according to more and less: *For at one time.*

(Vers. 16.). *For denying* etc. I said well: *it is impossible to escape thy hand; for the impious, denying to know thee,* that is, the Egyptians, namely Pharao with his servants; *denying,* I say, *by word,* whence Exodus, the fifth[^p213-6]: « I know not the Lord, neither will I let Israel go »; and *by deed,* those of whom it is said to Titus, the first: « They profess that they know God, but in their works they deny him ». *By the strength of thy arm,* that is, by thy strong power; Exodus, the fifteenth: « Who is like to thee, among the strong, O Lord » etc.; *were scourged;* Exodus, the sixth: « I will bring them out of the prison of the Egyptians with an outstretched arm »; Exodus, the fifteenth: « Thy right hand hath slain the enemy »; *by strange waters; and* (*et*), for *id est* ("that is"); *by hail,* for hail is as it were congealed water, *and by rains,* which are as it were flowing waters, *they suffered persecution;* Exodus, the ninth[^p213-7]: « Pharao, seeing that the rain and the hail had ceased »; *and by fire,* that is, falling at the same time, *consumed;* Exodus, the ninth: « The hail and fire mixed with it drove on together »; likewise in the Psalm: « He gave them hail for their rain, a burning fire in the land ».

(Vers. 17.). I said well: *by fire* with *hail; for that which was wonderful;* namely that a contrary was not destroyed by a contrary, since it is written in Ecclesiasticus, the third[^p213-8]: « Water quencheth a flaming fire »; whence there follows: *In water, which extinguisheth all things,* namely things on fire, *the fire had more force,* that is, it acted more strongly with the water. *For the world is an avenger,* that is, an instrument of vengeance; *an avenger,* I say, *the world, of the just,* that is, avenging the just; whence above, the fifth: « The whole world shall fight with him against the unwise ». By the aforesaid text he intimates that the punishing of the Egyptians was done not according to the order of nature, but according to the order of the divine justice avenging the Israelites on the Egyptians, who had oppressed them, Exodus, the first[^p213-9]; the Gloss: « It is no wonder that hail and fire are borne mixed together, because all things consent to the will of the Creator ».

(Vers. 18.). *For at one time,* as if to say: he did not always act uniformly; *enim* ("for"), for *sed* ("but"); *at one time the fire was mitigated,* namely until the animals of those who feared the words of Moses were called back or brought back from the fields; Exodus, the ninth[^p213-10]: « Send and gather together thy cattle and all that thou hast in the fields », or field; and after: « He that feared the word of the Lord among Pharao's servants made his servants and his cattle flee into houses ». *It was mitigated,* I say, *that the animals might not be burnt which were sent to the impious,* that is, the beasts of burden divinely given them. And not only on account of this, *but* also, *that they themselves seeing,* the aforesaid interruption, *might know,* namely through it, *that by the judgment of God,* not by a natural event,
<!-- page 214 -->
*they suffered persecution.* Likewise, according to the Gloss[^p214-1], « that they might know that every creature is ready to avenge the injury of the Creator »; in the Psalm: « The Lord shall be known when he executeth judgments », who, as Bernard says, is not known when he suffers injuries. And it is to be noted that the Lord punished the Sodomites by fire, Genesis, the nineteenth[^p214-2], without mitigation, and this because there were none good among them; but the Egyptians by fire with mitigation, because the children of Israel, who were good, were in Egypt.

(Vers. 19.). *And at another time,* namely when the animals had been called back, *in water above its power,* its natural power namely, because by miracle, *the fire burned on every side,* namely through the whole land of Egypt, *to destroy the wicked*[^p214-3] *nation of the land,* that is, the Egyptians, who were an earthly nation, not a heavenly one; against which the first to the Corinthians, the fifteenth: « As we have borne the image of the earthly, let us bear also the image of the heavenly ».

*Instead of which* etc. Here, concerning the consolation of the Israelites, first is touched the *showing of the benefit;* secondly, the *reason of the showing: For thy sustenance*[^p214-4].

(Vers. 20.). *Instead of which,* namely evils, that is, in place of which evils by contrast, *with the food of Angels,* that is, manna, which had been prepared by Angels; *thou didst nourish thy people* etc.; whence in the Psalm[^p214-5]: « Man ate the bread of Angels »; or because it prefigured the food of Angels, namely « the living bread, which came down from heaven », John, the sixth. *Thou didst nourish,* I say, *thy people,* namely the children of Israel; for with this food he nourished them, « until they reached the borders of the land of promise », as is had in Exodus, the sixteenth[^p214-6]. *And bread prepared,* that is, refreshment, *from heaven,* namely the aërial heaven, coming down, according to that of the Psalm: « He rained down manna upon them to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven »; *thou gavest them without labour.*

**On the contrary:** Because it is said in Numbers, the eleventh[^p214-7]: « The people went about, and gathering it, ground it in a mill, or beat it in a mortar, boiling it in a pot, and making cakes thereof of the taste as of bread tempered with oil ».

I respond: *without labour* of acquiring through agriculture, but not without labour of preparing. But we have labour in acquiring and preparing; Genesis, the third[^p214-8]: « In the sweat of thy face ».

*Having in it all delight,* that is, the effect of every delighting food, by making a like delight in the one eating; *and the sweetness of every taste,* that is, every sweet taste; for it so tasted to each one as he willed, as Gregory says in the sixth of the *Morals*[^p214-9].

**But on the contrary:** It is said in Numbers, the eleventh[^p214-10], that it had a taste « as of bread tempered with oil ».

It must be said that by its own nature it had a determinate taste, most sweet, namely like the taste « of flour with honey[^p214-11] »; but by divine grace it had diverse tastes, namely according to the diversity of desires, as the Gloss touches on that of the first to the Corinthians, the tenth[^p214-12]: « All did eat the same spiritual food ».

**But on the contrary:** Because, if it had the taste desired by anyone whatever, then they would not have desired the flesh of Egypt, nor would their soul have loathed the manna as a very light food, although the opposite is read in Numbers, the twenty-first[^p214-13].

It must be said, according to the Gloss[^p214-14]: The good found the best taste there, but the wicked loathed it. Or it must be said that they required in food not only the delight of taste as regards the palate, but also of colour as regards the sight, and of odour as regards the smell. Jerome[^p214-15]: Birds are cooked before the rich in their own vapours, that they may be refreshed by the sight and the savour, as the delicate are in wine, although it is said in Proverbs,
<!-- page 215 -->
the twenty-third[^p215-1]: « Look not upon the wine when it is yellow, when the colour thereof shineth in the glass » etc.

(Vers. 21.). *For thy sustenance,* that is, thy power of sustaining, *and thy sweetness*[^p215-2], that is, the power of delighting, *which thou hast towards thy children,* namely by sustaining them through the substance of the food and delighting them through the taste of the same food, *thou didst show* etc.; in the Psalm: « Thou hast provided in thy sweetness for the poor, O God »; *thou didst show,* I say, by bestowing on them the aforesaid food, sustaining and delighting. *And serving,* namely the aforesaid food or bread, *the will of every one,* namely of those eating, *it was turned to what each one willed,* that is, to what taste; for it had, as has been said[^p215-3], one taste determined by nature, but various ones by divine grace.

*Mystically* by the manna Christ under the Sacrament of the altar was fittingly signified, first because he is the bread of angels, namely of priests, according to that of Malachias, the second[^p215-4]: « He is the angel of the Lord of hosts ». — Secondly, because it is prepared from heaven, that is, because it is consecrated by heavenly power; John, the sixth: « I am the living bread, which came down from heaven »; in the Psalm: « Thou hast prepared their food, for so is its preparation ». — Thirdly, because those eating of it are refreshed according to their desire, that is, devotion, according to that of the Psalm[^p215-5]: « Thou hast given him his heart's desire »; likewise in the Psalm: « He brought them their desire, they were not defrauded of that which they craved ». — Fourthly, because with it the dew of grace comes down and is given, as the dew also came down with the manna, as is had in Numbers, the eleventh[^p215-6]. And therefore this bread is called *eucharistia;* eucharistia is interpreted *good grace.* — Fifthly, because, as the manna appeared like « white coriander seed[^p215-7] », so Christ too is received under a white cloud; Apocalypse, the fourteenth: « Behold a white cloud, and upon the cloud one sitting like to the Son of man ». — Sixthly, because it was gathered on six days, and not on the Sabbath[^p215-8], so Christ in the present is received under the Sacrament, but in the future Sabbath, of which Isaias, the sixty-sixth: « There shall be sabbath after sabbath », he will be seen openly; the first to the Corinthians, the thirteenth: « But then face to face »; likewise the first of John, the third: « We shall see him as he is ». — Seventhly, because until the entrance into the land of promise they were nourished in the desert with that food through forty years[^p215-9], so until the end of the world the faithful of the Church are nourished in the desert of this world, according to that of Matthew, the twenty-eighth: « Behold, I am with you all days, even to the consummation of the world ».

*But snow* etc. Here is touched the *reason of the affliction* of the Egyptians: first on the part *of the punished;* secondly, on the part *of those for whom they were punished: But this*[^p215-10]; thirdly, on the part *of God punishing: For the creature.*

(Vers. 22.). He says, then: *But snow* etc. So it was said[^p215-11] that *the fire had more force in water; but snow and ice,* that is, hail, *endured the force of fire,* that is, the heat, which is its quality of active power. *And melted not,* that is, were not liquefied; whence Exodus, the ninth[^p215-12]: « The Lord rained hail upon the land of Egypt; and the hail and fire mixed with it drove on together »; *that they might know,* namely the Israelites, *that the fruits of the enemies,* that is, of the Egyptians, *the burning fire destroyed,* namely through heat; *flashing in the hail and rain,* namely through brightness. « The flax and the barley were hurt, because the barley was green, and the flax was now bolled », Exodus, the ninth.

(Vers. 23.). *But this again* etc. It is to be known that the *ly*[^p215-13] *hoc* can be of the ablative case, so that it is expounded thus: *hoc* for *hôc* ("by this"); or of the nominative, so that it is read thus: *but this again,* supply: was done, *that the just might be nourished,* namely the children of Israel. *The fire*[^p215-14] *even forgot its own power,* namely by not destroying the fruits of the land of the just, as is clear from Exodus, the ninth, where it is read that « only in the land of Gessen, where the children of Israel were, the hail fell not ». So too the fire forgot the force of its power when it did not hurt the three just youths in the furnace, as is clear from Daniel, the third.

(Vers. 24.). *For thy creature*[^p215-15], namely whatever it be, *serving thee the maker,* that is, thy will, by which thou madest all things, according to that of the Psalm: « Whatsoever he would, he hath done »; *is enkindled,* that is, is vehemently moved and stirred up, *unto torment,* of punishing, *against the unjust,* that is, thy
<!-- page 216 -->
enemies. *And is made gentler,* that is, harmless or innocuous, *for doing good on behalf of those who trust in thee,* that is, on behalf of the just; for creatures obey those obedient to God — as is clear from Josue, the tenth[^p216-1], where it is read how the sun stood still at the command of Josue — and rebel against those who rebel; whence above, the fifth: « The whole world shall fight with him against the unwise ».

*Therefore* etc. Here is touched the *reason of the consolation* of the Israelites: first, the *recognition of the divine benefit;* secondly, the *showing of divine worship: For that which by fire;* thirdly, the *avoiding of ingratitude: For the hope of the ungrateful* etc.

(Vers. 25.). *Therefore,* namely because the creature serves the Creator, *even then,* namely when the children of Israel were fed with manna in the desert, *into all things,* that is, into the taste of all desired things, *transformed,* namely the creature itself, namely the manna; *to thy grace, the nurse of all,* that is, to thy gratuitous good will, so that it be understood of the *uncreated* grace; Baruch, the fourth[^p216-2]: « You have grieved Jerusalem that nursed you »; *they served*[^p216-3], that is, obeyed; the Gloss: « Let sinners here be confounded, because, while the other creatures serve their Maker, they resist him. Hence they are scourged by the concord of all creatures ». *They served,* I say, *the will,* to be fulfilled namely, *of those who were desired by thee,* namely loved with a special love, as the children of Israel; Deuteronomy, the seventh: « The Lord thy God hath chosen thee, to be his peculiar people of all peoples that are upon the earth ».

(Vers. 26.). *That thy children might know,* namely the Israelites, according to that of Exodus, the fourth[^p216-4]: « Israel is my firstborn »; Isaias, the first: « I have brought up children »; *whom thou lovedst, O Lord,* namely with eternal and temporal love; Malachias, the first: « I have loved you, saith the Lord ». *They might know,* I say, *that not the fruits of birth,* that is, not natural fruits, born of the earth, according to that of Genesis, the first: « Let the earth bring forth » etc.; *nourish men,* namely inasmuch as they are men, that is, according to that by which they differ from beasts, namely according to the rational power or part of the soul. *But thy word,* that is, created, that is, thy commandment; Matthew, the fourth[^p216-5]: « Not in bread alone doth man live » etc. Or: *the Word,* uncreated, that is, the Son, of whom John, the seventeenth: « Thy word is truth »; in the same, the fourteenth: « I am the way, and the truth, and the life »; *preserveth them that have believed in thee;* Deuteronomy, the eighth: « The Lord gave thee manna for thy food, which neither thou nor thy fathers knew, to show thee that not in bread alone doth man live, but in every word that proceedeth from the mouth of God ».

(Vers. 27.) *For that which could not be destroyed by fire,* namely the manna, because it hardened at the fire, so that cakes were made of it, as is clear from Numbers, the eleventh[^p216-6]; *being warmed by a little ray of the sun, presently melted,* that is, was liquefied, as is clear from Exodus, the sixteenth. Hence it was needful that it be gathered in the morning.

(Vers. 28.). *That it might be known to all,* namely through this sign, *that we ought to prevent the sun,* that is, the rising of the sun, *for thy blessing,* supply: to be obtained, that is, for receiving and gathering the manna, lest after the rising of the sun it should be liquefied by the heat of the sun; so, for obtaining the blessing of grace, one ought to prevent the sun by watching in the morning, according to that of Proverbs, the eighth[^p216-7]: « They that in the morning early watch for me shall find me »; in the Psalm: « In the morning I will stand before thee, and will see » etc. *And at the rising of the light,* that is, as soon as the light rises, or towards the east, where the light rises, *to adore thee,* namely for the reception of the manna itself; towards the east, I say, because, as Damascene says[^p216-8], everything better is to be ascribed to God; hence, because the east is the better part of the heaven, therefore one must adore towards the east, according to that of the Psalm: « Who mounteth above the heaven of heavens, to the east[^p216-9] ».

<!-- page 217 -->

And it is to be noted that in this, that the manna melted at the rising of the sun, it was signified, according to the Gloss[^p217-1], that at the rising of Christ the old sacrament would cease, because « the end of the Law is Christ », to the Romans, the tenth.

(Vers. 29.). *For the hope of the ungrateful* etc. So I said that it *melted* by the heat *of the sun,* and this in sign of this, that *the hope of the ungrateful shall melt* etc. Or thus: I said well that we ought *to adore at the rising of the light* etc., for the reception of the aforesaid benefit, and this to avoid ingratitude, which is very harmful. *For the hope of the ungrateful,* namely of any impious man whatever, or specially « of the Jewish people », according to the Gloss[^p217-2], *as the winter's ice;* the Gloss: « Bound by the cold of unbelief », according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the forty-third: « The cold north wind blew, and the water was congealed into crystal »; *shall melt,* that is, shall be liquefied, that is, shall be dissolved by the heat of the sun of justice in the day of judgment, either particular or universal; in the Psalm: « As wax melteth before the fire, so let the sinners perish at the presence of God ». *Shall melt,* I say, as regards the punishment *of loss,* namely by losing all goods, whatever it had or hoped for, namely the goods of grace and of glory; *and shall perish utterly,* that is, shall perish doubly, that is, shall suffer torment in body and soul, and this as regards the punishment *of sense. Shall perish utterly,* I say, *as unprofitable water,* that is, useless, which is poured out or thrown upon the earth; so the ungrateful man too, as useless, shall be thrown into hell, according to that of Matthew, the twenty-fifth[^p217-3]: « The unprofitable servant cast ye out into the exterior darkness ». *The hope of the ungrateful* therefore *shall melt and perish utterly* etc.; whence above, the fifth: « The hope of the impious is as dust, which is blown away with the wind »; likewise Bernard[^p217-4]: « Ingratitude is a burning wind, drying up the fountain of piety, the dew of mercy, the streams of grace ».
