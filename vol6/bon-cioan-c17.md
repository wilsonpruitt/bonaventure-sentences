### Capitulum XVII. *Haec locutus est Iesus*[^p609-2].

### Collatio LXI.

(Vers. 11.). Parum infra: *Pater sancte, serva eos quos dedisti mihi, ut sint unum in te, sicut et nos.*

1. Quia quasi per totum capitulum de superna unitate agitur, quam Dominus Iesus Christus discipulis suis imprecatur; ideo notandum de superna unitate, circa quam distingue, quod multiplex est unitas. Est enim unitas, cuius vinculum est *malignitas;* et est unitas, cuius vinculum est *carnalitas;* et est unitas, cuius vinculum est *caritas.*

2. De unitate *malignitatis;* Psalmus[^p609-3]: « Convenerunt in unum adversus Dominum et adversus Christum eius »; Iob quadragesimo primo de Behemoth: « Corpus illius est compactum squamis se prementibus; una uni coniungitur, et ne spiraculum quidem incedit per eas »; Proverbiorum primo: « Marsupium unum sit omnium nostrum », dixerunt impii. — De unitate *carnalitatis;* primae ad Corinthios sexto[^p609-4]: « Qui adhaeret meretrici unum corpus efficitur »; *meretrici,* ad *litteram* mulieri, vel *moraliter* carni; et « erunt, inquit, duo in carne una », id est in carnalitate una. Bernardus super illud Psalmi: « Ecce, quam bonum et quam iucundum habitare fratres in unum »: « Etsi in unum, inquit, non tamen in peccato ». — De unitate *caritatis;* ad Ephesios quarto[^p609-5]: « Solliciti servare unitatem spiritus in vinculo pacis », quam imprecatur Dominus discipulis, cum dicit: *Pater sancte, serva eos quos dedisti mihi, ut sint unum, sicut et nos.*

3. Et nota, quod unitas caritatis debet esse in homine triplex. Debet enim esse homo unus cum *Deo* per *conformitatem voluntatis;* debet esse unus cum *se ipso* per *integritatem mentis;* debet esse unus cum *proximo* per *concordiam caritatis.* — Debet ergo esse unus cum *Deo;* de qua unitate primae ad Corinthios sexto[^p609-6]: « Qui adhaeret Deo unus spiritus est ». De hac unitate Hugo: « Homo, aversus ab uno ad multa, evanuit; conversus a multis ad unum, vitam invenit ». — Debet esse unus cum *se ipso* per mentis integritatem; quam integritatem commendat in sponsa ipse Sponsus Canticorum sexto[^p609-7]: « Una est columba mea, perfecta mea »; *una,* id est integra, non divisa, quales sunt, de quibus Osee decimo: « Divisum est cor eorum; nunc interibunt ». — Debet etiam esse unus cum *proximo* per caritatis concordiam, quae commendatur in Psalmo, cum dicitur: « Ecce, quam bonum et quam iucundum habitare » etc.; et quam specialiter Dominus imprecatur, cum dicit: *Serva eos, ut sint unum.*

4. Et haec unitas cum proximo debet esse triplex. Debet esse unitas *voluntatum,* unitas *morum,* unitas *virtutum.* — Debet enim uniri fratrum *affectio* per *voluntatum indivisionem,* secundum quod dicitur Actuum quarto[^p609-8]: « Multitudinis credentium erat cor unum et anima una ». — Debet etiam uniri fratrum *conversatio* per *morum concordiam,* quia Deus ipse est, « qui habitare facit unius moris in domo », ut habetur in Psalmo; et iterum: « In domo Dei ambulavimus cum consensu ». — Debet etiam in unum convenire fratrum *operatio* per *mutuam subventionem,* sicut faciunt membra unius corporis ad invicem, quia, sicut dicitur ad Romanos duodecimo[^p609-9], « nos unum corpus sumus in Christo, singuli autem alter alterius membra »; et primae ad Corinthios duodecimo: « Sicut corpus unum est et membra habet multa; omnia autem membra corporis, cum sint multa, unum corpus sunt; ita et Christus. Etenim in uno Spiritu omnes nos in unum corpus baptizati sumus ».

5. *Serva,* inquit, *eos, ut sint unum, sicut et nos.* Notabile est, quod Dominus, in recessu suo pro discipulis suis orans, eisdem prae omnibus *imprecatur unitatem,* et eisdem prae omnibus *iniungit caritatem;* quod non est propter aliud nisi propter multiplicem fraternae unitatis[^p609-10] *utilitatem.* Est enim fraterna unitas *cadentibus* in remedium, *resurgentibus* in auxilium, *pugnantibus* in praesidium, *patientibus* in subsidium, *proficientibus* in ducatum, *postulantibus* in suffragium, *exspectantibus* in solatium, *perseverantibus* in tutelam, *pervenientibus* in gaudium.
<!-- page 610 -->

6. Est igitur fraterna unitas *cadentibus in remedium;* Ecclesiastae quarto[^p610-1]: « Melius est, esse simul duos quam unum; habent enim emolumenta societatis suae; si unus ceciderit, ab altero fulcietur. Vae soli! quia, si ceciderit, non habet sublevantem se ». Homo enim *ingratus* non potest sibi mereri gratiam; alius autem *gratus* potest illi mereri de congruo: « Melius est ergo » etc. — Est iterum *resurgentibus in auxilium,* ad impetrandam gratiam remissionis, per quam resurgant; saepe enim qui per se provocat aspectum regum ad puniendum per alios advocat ad miserendum. Unde Psalmus[^p610-2]: « Prospexit de excelso sancto suo; Dominus de caelo in terram aspexit, ut audiret gemitus compeditorum, ut solveret filios interemptorum ». Sed quando? « In conveniendo populos in unum et reges », scilicet subditos et praelatos, « ut serviant Domino ». — Est iterum *pugnantibus in praesidium.* « Si enim quispiam praevaluerit contra unum, duo resistent ei. Melius est ergo, duos esse simul quam unum », Ecclesiastae quarto[^p610-3]. Exemplum de milite, qui vocavit maiorem de filiis suis ad frangendum tres lanceas; unde et Israel, quandocumque legitur *congregatus* « quasi vir unus », legitur vicisse; cum vinci debet, ostensus est *dispersus;* unde Michaeas, tertii Regum ultimo: « Vidi Israel dispersum in montibus » etc. — Est iterum *patientibus in subsidium.* Levior enim efficitur quaecumque tribulatio, cum a pluribus supportatur; quod facit unitas fraterna; unde primae ad Corinthios duodecimo[^p610-4]: « Unum corpus sumus in Christo » etc.; et sequitur: « Si patitur unum membrum, compatiuntur omnia membra ».

7. Est nihilominus *proficientibus in ducatum et directionem operis;* unde primi Regum sexto[^p610-5] de vaccis reducentibus arcam Domini de terra Philisthiim legitur, quod « ibant vaccae in directum per viam, quae ducit Bethsames, et itinere uno gradiebantur, pergentes et mugientes, et non declinabant neque ad dexteram neque ad sinistram ». *Vaccae* sive iuvenculae, quae non noverant iugum, sunt noviter conversi ad poenitentiam; isti *referunt arcam,* id est animam de *Philisthiim,* id est de captivitate diaboli; qui *in directum incedunt,* quando incedunt itinere uno, quod est, quando sunt sub iugo uno, ut in religione. Quamdiu enim religiosus est alligatus iugo religionis et nondum fregit iugum nec rupit vincula; tunc necesse est, ut vadat in directum per iter, per quod incedit[^p610-6]. Alii autem vadunt *pergentes* per actionem et *mugientes* per clamorem orationis usque ad *domum Bethsames,* quae est domus solis, vel illuminationis; et non *declinant ad dexteram* praesumtionis neque ad *sinistram* remissionis. Cum ergo caritas fraterna alligat fratres ad iugum unum, etiam qui per se ire non potest trahitur ab aliis, ut proficiat. — Est iterum *postulantibus in suffragium.* Quod enim non valet unus, obtinet multitudo intercessorum; unde illud[^p610-7]: « Impossibile est, preces multorum non exaudiri ». Quod significatum est Actuum primo, ubi legitur, quod « erant omnes discipuli perseverantes unanimiter in oratione » etc. Etenim, cum essent congregati « in eodem loco, factus est repente de caelo sonus » etc.

8. Est praeterea *exspectantibus in solatium;* unde Psalmus[^p610-8]: « Ecce, quam bonum et quam iucundum habitare fratres in unum. Sicut unguentum in capite » id est Iesu Christo, « quod descendit » non nisi in membra capitis mediate, vel immediate continuata. Nota de eremitis, qui consolabantur se quotidie dicentes: cras migrabimus hinc. — Est praeter haec *perseverantibus in tutelam;* Ezechielis trigesimo septimo[^p610-9]: « Ecce, ego assumam filios Israel per poenitentiam de medio nationum, ad quas abierunt », per culpam, « et congregabo eos et faciam eos in gentem unam », per caritatem, « in montibus Israel » id est in perfectione iustitiae; et subiungit perseverantiam: « Et non erunt ultra duae gentes et non polluentur ultra in idolis suis, et salvos faciam eos de universis, in quibus peccaverunt ». Qui ergo in unum perseverant salvi erunt. Nam si separaverit se Dina a fratribus suis, prope est Hemor ad corrumpendum, Genesis trigesimo quarto[^p610-10]. — Est nihilominus *pervenientibus in gaudium.* Ibi enim est una vox laetantium et unus ardor cordium; ibi amor perfectus et unus omnium spiritus, ut dicit Augustinus[^p610-11]. Ibi erit illa felix consummatio
<!-- page 611 -->
unitatis, quam imprecabatur Dominus discipulis, Ioannis decimo septimo, cum rogabat, ut essent consummati in unum: « Rogo, inquit, ut sint unum, sicut et nos unum sumus: ego in eis, et tu in me, ut sint consummati in unum ».

### Annotatio 67.

(Cap. 16, 33.). *Confidite, quia ego vici mundum*[^p611-1].

Nota, quod Dominus dicit, se fecisse tria in mundo, scilicet *reliquisse* mundum, Ioannis decimo sexto[^p611-2]: « Relinquo mundum et vado a Patrem »; *vicisse* mundum; unde hic Ioannis decimo sexto: « Confidite, quia vici mundum »; *reprehendisse* mundum; Ioannis septimo: « Me odit mundus, quia testimonium perhibeo de eo, quia opera eius mala sunt »; per hoc ostendens fidelibus suis, quod relinquendae sunt mundanae *prosperitates,* sustinendae sive superandae mundanae *adversitates,* arguendae *perversitates* mundanae. — Sic mundum *conversatione* reliquit, *passione* superavit, *praedicatione* reprehendit.

### Collatio LXII.

(Vers. 5.). *Clarifica me, Pater, apud temetipsum claritate, quam habui, priusquam mundus fieret.*

1. Nota, quod Christus clarificatus fuit in *passione causaliter;* Ioannis duodecimo[^p611-3]: « Venit hora, ut clarificetur Filius hominis. Amen dico vobis: Nisi granum frumenti cadens in terram mortuum fuerit » etc. — Clarificatus fuit in *resurrectione actualiter;* Ioannis decimo tertio: « Deus clarificavit eum in semetipso », continua clarificatione. — Clarificatus fuit in *Apostolorum praedicatione evidenter;* Ioannis decimo septimo: « Clarificatus sum in eis » et ideo dicitur, Ioannis ultimo, de Petro: « Hoc dixit Iesus, significans, qua morte esset clarificaturus Deum ».

2. Nota autem, quod eius clarificatio manifestata est in tribus: in *miraculorum operatione;* Marci ultimo[^p611-4]: « Signa eos qui crediderint, haec sequentur »; in *linguarum divisione:* » Apparuerunt eis dispertitae linguae tanquam ignis », Actuum secundo; in *charismatum infusione;* primae ad Corinthios duodecimo: « Divisiones gratiarum sunt, idem autem spiritus ». — Et nota, quod operatio miraculorum clarificat eius *potentiam;* divisiones linguarum, eius *sapientiam;* infusio charismatum, eius *bonitatem.*

3. Nota postea, quod Christus fuit clarificatus a Deo *Patre;* unde hic: *Clarifica me, Pater* etc.; a *Spiritu sancto;* Ioannis decimo sexto[^p611-5]: « Ille me clarificabit, quia de meo accipiet »; a *discipulis;* Ioannis ultimo: « Hoc dixit, significans, qua morte Petrus esset clarificaturus Deum ». — Recollige ergo, quod clarificatus fuit Christus *tripliciter* et *per tria* et *a tribus: tripliciter,* quia in passione, in resurrectione et in Apostolorum praedicatione; *per tria,* quia per miraculorum operationem, per linguarum divisionem, per charismatum infusionem; et *a tribus,* scilicet a Deo Patre, a Spiritu sancto et a discipulis.

4. *Claritate, quam habui, priusquam mundus fieret.* Nota, quod multiplex claritas invenitur in Christo, quae etiam communicatur homini iusto. Est enim claritas *sapientiae;* Sapientiae sexto[^p611-6]: « Clara est et quae nunquam marcescit sapientia ». — Claritas *gratiae;* secundae ad Corinthios tertio: « Nos revelata facie gloriam Domini speculantes, in eandem imaginem transformamur a claritate in claritatem tanquam a Domini spiritu ». — Claritas *famae;* Sapientiae octavo[^p611-7]: « Habebo propter hanc » sapientiam « claritatem ad turbas et honorem apud seniores ». — Claritas *gloriae;* ad Philippenses tertio: « Salvatorem exspectamus, qui reformabit corpus humilitatis nostrae configuratum corpori claritatis suae ».

### Chapter XVII. *These things Jesus spoke*[^p609-2].

### Collation LXI.

(Vers. 11.). A little below: *Holy Father, keep them whom thou hast given me, that they may be one in thee, as we also are.*

1. Because almost through the whole chapter it treats of the supernal unity, which the Lord Jesus Christ prays down upon his disciples; therefore it is to be noted concerning the supernal unity, about which distinguish, that unity is manifold. For there is a unity whose bond is *malignity;* and there is a unity whose bond is *carnality;* and there is a unity whose bond is *charity.*

2. Of the unity *of malignity;* the Psalm[^p609-3]: « They met together in one against the Lord and against his Christ »; Job, the forty-first chapter, of Behemoth: « His body is shut close up with scales pressing upon one another; one is joined to another, and not so much as any air cometh between them »; Proverbs, the first chapter: « Let there be one purse of us all », said the wicked. — Of the unity *of carnality;* the first to the Corinthians, the sixth chapter[^p609-4]: « He who is joined to a harlot is made one body »; *to a harlot,* according to the *letter* to a woman, or *morally* to the flesh; and « they shall be, he says, two in one flesh », that is, in one carnality. Bernard on that of the Psalm: « Behold how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in one »: « Although in one, he says, yet not in sin ». — Of the unity *of charity;* to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter[^p609-5]: « Careful to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace », which the Lord prays down upon the disciples, when he says: *Holy Father, keep them whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we also are.*

3. And note that the unity of charity ought to be threefold in man. For man ought to be one with *God* through *conformity of will;* he ought to be one with *himself* through *integrity of mind;* he ought to be one with *his neighbour* through *concord of charity.* — He ought therefore to be one with *God;* of which unity the first to the Corinthians, the sixth chapter[^p609-6]: « He who is joined to God is one spirit ». Of this unity Hugh: « Man, turned away from the one to the many, vanished; turned from the many to the one, he found life ». — He ought to be one with *himself* through integrity of mind; which integrity the Bridegroom himself commends in the bride, the Canticle of Canticles, the sixth chapter[^p609-7]: « One is my dove, my perfect one »; *one,* that is, entire, not divided, such as are those of whom Osee, the tenth chapter: « Their heart is divided; now they shall perish ». — He ought also to be one with *his neighbour* through the concord of charity, which is commended in the Psalm, when it is said: « Behold how good and how pleasant it is to dwell » etc.; and which the Lord especially prays down, when he says: *Keep them, that they may be one.*

4. And this unity with the neighbour ought to be threefold. There ought to be unity *of wills,* unity *of manners,* unity *of virtues.* — For the *affection* of the brethren ought to be united through *the undividedness of wills,* according to what is said in Acts, the fourth chapter[^p609-8]: « Of the multitude of believers there was one heart and one soul ». — The *conversation* of the brethren ought also to be united through *concord of manners,* because God himself it is « who maketh men of one manner to dwell in a house », as is had in the Psalm; and again: « In the house of God we walked with consent ». — The *working* of the brethren ought also to come together in one through *mutual succour,* as the members of one body do toward one another, because, as is said to the Romans, the twelfth chapter[^p609-9], « we are one body in Christ, and each one members one of another »; and the first to the Corinthians, the twelfth chapter: « As the body is one, and hath many members; and all the members of the body, whereas they are many, are one body; so also is Christ. For in one Spirit were we all baptized into one body ».

5. *Keep,* he says, *them, that they may be one, as we also are.* It is notable that the Lord, praying for his disciples at his departure, prays down upon them before all things *unity,* and enjoins upon them before all things *charity;* which is for no other reason but for the manifold *usefulness* of fraternal unity[^p609-10]. For fraternal unity is to *the falling* for a remedy, to *the rising again* for help, to *the fighting* for a defence, to *the suffering* for a support, to *the advancing* for guidance, to *the asking* for suffrage, to *the waiting* for solace, to *the persevering* for a safeguard, to *those arriving* for joy.
<!-- page 610 -->

6. Fraternal unity is therefore to *the falling for a remedy;* Ecclesiastes, the fourth chapter[^p610-1]: « It is better that two should be together than one; for they have the advantage of their society; if one fall, he shall be supported by the other. Woe to him that is alone! for when he falleth, he hath none to lift him up ». For a man *not in grace* cannot merit grace for himself; but another, *in grace,* can merit it for him de congruo: « It is better therefore » etc. — It is again to *the rising again for help,* for obtaining the grace of remission, through which they may rise again; for often he who by himself provokes the regard of kings to punish, through others calls it to have mercy. Whence the Psalm[^p610-2]: « He hath looked forth from his high sanctuary; from heaven the Lord hath looked upon the earth, that he might hear the groans of them that are in fetters, that he might release the children of the slain ». But when? « When the peoples come together in one, and kings », namely subjects and prelates, « that they may serve the Lord ». — It is again to *the fighting for a defence.* « For if a man prevail against one, two shall withstand him. It is better therefore that two should be together than one », Ecclesiastes, the fourth chapter[^p610-3]. An example of the soldier who called the eldest of his sons to break three lances; whence also Israel, whenever it is read to have been *gathered together* « as one man », is read to have conquered; when it is to be conquered, it is shown *scattered;* whence Micheas, the third of Kings, the last chapter: « I saw Israel scattered upon the mountains » etc. — It is again to *the suffering for a support.* For any tribulation whatever is made lighter when it is borne by many; which fraternal unity does; whence the first to the Corinthians, the twelfth chapter[^p610-4]: « We are one body in Christ » etc.; and there follows: « If one member suffer anything, all the members suffer with it ».

7. It is nonetheless to *the advancing for guidance and direction of work;* whence in the first of Kings, the sixth chapter[^p610-5], it is read of the kine bringing back the ark of the Lord from the land of the Philistines, that « the kine went straight on by the way that leadeth to Bethsames, and walked on one road, going on and lowing, and turned not aside neither to the right hand nor to the left ». The *kine,* or heifers, which had not known the yoke, are those newly converted to penance; these *bring back the ark,* that is, the soul, from *the Philistines,* that is, from the captivity of the devil; who *go straight on* when they go on one road, that is, when they are under one yoke, as in religion. For as long as the religious is bound to the yoke of religion and has not yet broken the yoke nor burst the bonds; then it is necessary that he go straight on by the road by which he goes[^p610-6]. But others go *going on* through action and *lowing* through the cry of prayer as far as *the house of Bethsames,* which is the house of the sun, or of illumination; and they do not *turn aside to the right hand* of presumption nor to *the left* of slackness. Since therefore fraternal charity binds the brethren to one yoke, even he who cannot go by himself is drawn by the others, that he may advance. — It is again to *the asking for suffrage.* For what one cannot do, a multitude of intercessors obtains; whence that saying[^p610-7]: « It is impossible that the prayers of many should not be heard ». Which is signified in Acts, the first chapter, where it is read that « all the disciples were persevering with one mind in prayer » etc. For when they were gathered together « in the same place, there came suddenly a sound from heaven » etc.

8. It is moreover to *the waiting for solace;* whence the Psalm[^p610-8]: « Behold how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in one. As the ointment on the head », that is, on Jesus Christ, « which ran down » only into the members joined to the head, mediately or immediately. Note concerning the hermits, who consoled themselves daily saying: tomorrow we shall depart hence. — It is besides these to *the persevering for a safeguard;* Ezechiel, the thirty-seventh chapter[^p610-9]: « Behold, I will take the children of Israel through penance from the midst of the nations whither they are gone », through fault, « and I will gather them and make them one nation », through charity, « on the mountains of Israel », that is, in the perfection of justice; and he adds perseverance: « And they shall no more be two nations, and they shall not be defiled any more with their idols, and I will save them out of all the places wherein they have sinned ». They therefore who persevere in one shall be saved. For if Dina separate herself from her brethren, Hemor is near to corrupt her, Genesis, the thirty-fourth chapter[^p610-10]. — It is nonetheless to *those arriving for joy.* For there is one voice of those rejoicing and one ardour of hearts; there perfect love and one spirit of all, as Augustine says[^p610-11]. There shall be that happy consummation
<!-- page 611 -->
of unity, which the Lord prayed down upon the disciples, John, the seventeenth chapter, when he asked that they might be consummated in one: « I pray, he says, that they may be one, as we also are one: I in them, and thou in me, that they may be consummated in one ».

### Annotation 67.

(Cap. 16, 33.). *Have confidence, I have overcome the world*[^p611-1].

Note that the Lord says he did three things in the world, namely that he *left* the world, John, the sixteenth chapter[^p611-2]: « I leave the world and I go to the Father [so printed: *a Patrem* for *ad Patrem*] »; that he *overcame* the world; whence here, John, the sixteenth chapter: « Have confidence, I have overcome the world »; that he *reproved* the world; John, the seventh chapter: « The world hateth me, because I give testimony of it, that the works thereof are evil »; showing by this to his faithful that the world's *prosperities* are to be left, the world's *adversities* to be endured or overcome, the world's *perversities* to be convinced. — Thus he left the world by his *conversation,* overcame it by his *passion,* reproved it by his *preaching.*

### Collation LXII.

(Vers. 5.). *Glorify thou me, O Father, with thyself, with the clarity which I had, before the world was made.*

1. Note that Christ was glorified in his *passion causally;* John, the twelfth chapter[^p611-3]: « The hour is come, that the Son of man should be glorified. Amen I say to you: Unless the grain of wheat falling into the ground die » etc. — He was glorified in his *resurrection actually;* John, the thirteenth chapter: « God hath glorified him in himself », by a continual glorification. — He was glorified in *the preaching of the Apostles evidently;* John, the seventeenth chapter: « I am glorified in them »; and therefore it is said, John, the last chapter, of Peter: « This Jesus said, signifying by what death he should glorify God ».

2. But note that his glorification was manifested in three things: in *the working of miracles;* Mark, the last chapter[^p611-4]: « These signs shall follow them that shall believe »; in *the division of tongues:* » There appeared to them parted tongues as it were of fire », Acts, the second chapter; in *the infusion of charisms;* the first to the Corinthians, the twelfth chapter: « There are divisions of graces, but the same spirit ». — And note that the working of miracles glorifies his *power;* the divisions of tongues, his *wisdom;* the infusion of charisms, his *goodness.*

3. Note next that Christ was glorified by God *the Father;* whence here: *Glorify thou me, O Father* etc.; by *the Holy Spirit;* John, the sixteenth chapter[^p611-5]: « He shall glorify me, because he shall receive of mine »; by *the disciples;* John, the last chapter: « This he said, signifying by what death Peter should glorify God ». — Gather up therefore that Christ was glorified *threefoldly,* and *through three things,* and *by three: threefoldly,* because in the passion, in the resurrection and in the preaching of the Apostles; *through three things,* because through the working of miracles, through the division of tongues, through the infusion of charisms; and *by three,* namely by God the Father, by the Holy Spirit and by the disciples.

4. *With the clarity which I had, before the world was made.* Note that a manifold clarity is found in Christ, which is also communicated to the just man. For there is the clarity *of wisdom;* Wisdom, the sixth chapter[^p611-6]: « Wisdom is clear, and never fadeth away ». — The clarity *of grace;* the second to the Corinthians, the third chapter: « We, beholding the glory of the Lord with open face, are transformed into the same image from clarity to clarity, as by the spirit of the Lord ». — The clarity *of fame;* Wisdom, the eighth chapter[^p611-7]: « For her sake I shall have » wisdom « clarity among the multitude, and honour with the ancients ». — The clarity *of glory;* to the Philippians, the third chapter: « We wait for the Saviour, who will reform the body of our lowness, made like to the body of his clarity ».
