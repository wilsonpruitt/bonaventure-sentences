# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Scholion
## *Scholion*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 530 -->

### SCHOLION

Ultimarum divisionum membra, quae ibi omissa sunt, ubi posuimus *etc.*, videri possunt in notis marginalibus.

## English
<!-- page 530 -->

### SCHOLION

The members of the last divisions, which have been omitted there where we have put *etc.*, can be seen in the marginal notes.

**WHAT IT SAYS AND WHAT IT POINTS AT.** *ibi* is the Schemata that follow; *ubi posuimus etc.* refers to the branch-ends of the trees printed with *etc.* (Schema I's *facta Mariae Magd. etc.*, *de prima tria etc.*; Schema II's *de ipso praecursore ponuntur quatuor etc.* and the like). ⚠ **The *notae marginales* it sends the reader to are the commentary's MARGINALIA, which this corpus trims from the body into each chunk's `## Notes` Marginalia list** — so on this site the "omitted last divisions" are to be looked for in the pericope chunks' `## Notes`, not in the margin of the text. Recorded once, here. It is the same editorial claim the Sapientiae Scholion makes in long form (*Ob rationes typographicas pluries omisimus ultimas divisiones, quae facile ex notis marginalibus possunt suppleri*), so a schema branch that stops at *etc.* is the editors' stated omission, not a defect.

**PRINTED FORMS:** *etc.* is **italic** in the print (the editors quoting the schemata's own *etc.*), with a roman comma after it — kept italic in both languages. The raw has `etc,` and breaks the line after *possunt*; the plate's *possunt in notis marginalibus.* is one unbroken line. No hyphenation, no thin-space marks.
