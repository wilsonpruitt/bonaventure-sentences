# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XXI, Vers. 1-8
## *De tertia manifestatione notantur duo*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 519 -->

### Capitulum XXI.

#### De tertia manifestatione notantur duo.

1. *Postea manifestavit* etc. Hic agitur de tertia manifestatione[^p519-1], quae facta est ad *Petri confirmationem;* et dividitur haec pars in duas. In prima agitur de *manifestatione;* in secunda, de *Petri confirmatione,* ibi: *Cum ergo prandissent* etc.

Prima in duas, quia ad descriptionem manifestationis necesse est describere circumadiacentia; ideo primo tanguntur *illa;* secundo, *ipsa manifestatio,* ibi[^p519-2]: *Mane autem facto* etc.

*Circumadiacentia* manifestationem sunt *locus,* in quo manifestatur; et *personae,* quibus manifestatur; et *actus* sive negotium, ratione cuius manifestatur[^p519-3].

2. (Vers. 1.). Primo ergo describitur *locus,* in quo facta est haec manifestatio, cum dicitur: *Postea manifestavit se iterum Iesus*[^p519-4] *ad mare Tiberiadis;* ecce, locus. « Mare Tiberiadis idem est, quod stagnum Genesareth et quod mare Galilaeae »; dicitur autem *Tiberiadis* a Tiberiade civitate, quae iuxta illud stagnum sita est; dicitur autem *mare Galilaeae* a regione, in qua est; dicitur *stagnum Genesareth* a natura propria ipsius stagni, ut dicatur *Genesareth,* quasi auras generans[^p519-5]. — *Manifestavit autem sic.* Hic tanguntur *personae,* quibus manifestatur; unde dicit:

3. (Vers. 2.). *Erant simul Simon Petrus et Thomas, qui dicitur Didymus, et Nathanael, qui erat a Cana Galilaeae, et filii Zebedaei et alii ex discipulis eius duo.* Et ita erant septem, quia talis numerus est conveniens et sufficiens ad omne genus testimonii. Et commendatur in hoc septiformis gratia Spiritus sancti, quae debet esse in his qui vadunt *piscari,* id est populum Domino congregare; de quo septenario dicitur Isaiae quarto[^p519-6]: « Apprehendent septem mulieres virum unum » — sine his mulieribus non fit propagatio spiritualis — et Isaiae undecimo: « Requiescet super eum spiritus Domini, spiritus sapientiae et intellectus, spiritus consilii et fortitudinis, spiritus scientiae et pietatis, et replebit eum spiritus timoris Domini ».

4. (Vers. 3.) *Dicit eis Simon Petrus.* Hic tangitur *actus* sive officium, occasione cuius manifestatur; et hic quidem fuit actus piscationis, in quo omnes convenerant. Sed Petrus erat principalis, quia propter ipsum specialiter haec manifestatio facta est; unde dicit: *Dicit eis Simon Petrus: Vado piscari,* tanquam principalis, quia piscator fuerat; Matthaei quarto[^p519-7]: « Ambulans Iesus secus mare Galilaeae, vidit duos fratres, Simonem, qui vocatur Petrus, et Andream, fratrem eius, mittentes rete in mare; erant enim piscatores ». Et alii comitantur; unde dicit: *Dicunt ei: Venimus et nos tecum. Et* ideo, concordes in unum, *exierunt et ascenderunt navem,* ut piscarentur, *et in illa nocte*[^p519-8] *nihil prendiderunt,* et ita in vanum laboraverunt; Lucae quinto: « Praeceptor, per totam noctem laborantes, nihil cepi-
<!-- page 520 -->
mus »; Gregorius: « Facta est discipulis piscationis magna difficultas, ut, Magistro veniente, fieret admirationis magna sublimitas ».

5. *Mane autem facto.* Hic agitur de *Domini manifestatione*[^p520-1]; et dividitur haec pars in duas; quia primo describitur *ipsa manifestatio;* deinde ad praecedentes manifestationes *comparatur,* ibi: *Hoc iam tertio* etc.

In ipsa manifestatione quia dupliciter se manifestat: et quoad *divinam* naturam per miraculi operationem, et quoad *humanam* per comestionem; ideo sunt ibi duae particulae. In prima describitur manifestatio facta per *piscium comprehensionem;* in secunda, per *comestionem,* ibi[^p520-2]: *Ut ergo descenderunt in terram* etc.

Manifestatio ergo quoad mirabilem *piscium comprehensionem* hoc ordine describitur. Primo enim notatur *Christi apparitio;* secundo, *allocutio;* tertio, mirabilis *piscium comprehensio;* quarto, *Domini cognitio;* quinto, fervens *Petri dilectio.*

6. (Vers. 4.). *Domini* igitur *apparitio* tangitur, cum dicitur: *Mane autem facto, stetit Iesus in littore.* Ad litteram *mane stetit,* quia nocte ipsum videre non possent; secundum spiritualem intellectum, quia *mane* tempus gratiae significat, in quo Christus apparuit; ad Romanos decimo tertio[^p520-3]: « Nox praecessit, dies autem appropinquavit. Abiiciamus ergo opera tenebrarum et induamur arma lucis ». Et licet appareret, tamen discipuli non cognoscebant; ideo dicit: *Non tamen discipuli cognoverunt, quia Iesus est.* Chrysostomus[^p520-4]: « Non mox ostendit se ipsum, ut per miraculum, quod facturus erat, eum cognoscerent ».

7. (Vers. 5.). *Dicit*[^p520-5] *ergo eis Iesus.* Hic notatur secundum, scilicet *Domini allocutio;* et quaerit ab eis causam tanti laboris: *Pueri, nunquid pulmentarium habetis? Pulmentarium* vocat hic omne illud, quo panis conditur, sive piscis sit, sive aliud, quod nos communiter dicimus *pulmentum,* quasi quaerat ab eis Dominus, utrum pisces habeant vel ceperint, ex quibus cibum suum condire possint, ut panis illi[^p520-6] adiunctus sapiat. *Responderunt* ergo ei: *Non;* quasi dicant: adhuc laboramus, quia nondum habemus.

8. (Vers. 6.). *Dixit*[^p520-7] *eis: Mittite in dexteram navigii rete et invenietis.* Hic notatur tertium, scilicet *mirabilis piscium comprehensio.* Et fuit *mirabilis;* quia, cum nihil possent capere, ad divinum imperium tot ceperunt ipsi obtemperando; unde: *Miserunt ergo rete*[^p520-8], iuxta mandatum Domini, *et iam non valebant illud trahere prae multitudine piscium.* Simile accidit Lucae quinto: « Cum hoc fecissent, comprehenderunt multitudinem piscium copiosam »; ideo in magna copia, ut ostendatur divina largitas, quae, secundum illud Iacobi primo, « dat omnibus affluenter et non improperat ».

9. (Vers. 7.). *Dicit*[^p520-9] *ergo discipulus* etc. Hic notatur quartum, scilicet *Domini cognitio* a Ioanne; unde: *Dicit ergo discipulus ille, quem diligebat Iesus,* id est Ioannes, *Petro: Dominus est.* Ideo *Dominum* vocat, quia se potentem ostendit, et cui omnia obediunt, etiam pisces; et ideo timendus, secundum illud Malachiae primo[^p520-10]: « Si ego Dominus, ubi est timor meus »? Vocabant enim discipuli eum *Dominum;* supra decimo tertio: « Vos vocatis me *Magister* et *Domine* ».

10. *Simon ergo*[^p520-11] *Petrus.* Hic notatur quintum, scilicet *Petri fervens dilectio,* in hoc, quod non potuit se continere, quin statim curreret; ideo dicit: *Simon autem Petrus, cum audisset, quia Dominus est, tunica succinxit se,* ne nudus appareret coram Domino; *erat enim nudus; et misit se in mare,* ad natandum scilicet. Simile fecit Matthaei decimo quarto[^p520-12]: « Domine, si tu es, iube me venire ad te super aquas ».

11. (Vers. 8.). *Alii autem discipuli navigio venerunt;* quia non erant ita ferventes, non audentes mare intrare; Sapientiae decimo quarto[^p520-13]: « Exiguo ligno credunt homines animas suas » etc. Et alia ratio: *Non enim erant longe a terra,* et ideo cito venire poterant; *sed quasi cubitis ducentis, trahentes rete piscium.*
<!-- page 521 -->

12. Secundum *spiritualem* intelligentiam: piscatores nocte piscantes nihil prendiderunt; mane facto, apparuit Iesus, et retia impleverunt, et cubitis ducentis distantes a terra, retia traxerunt. — *Piscatores* sunt praedicatores; Ieremiae decimo sexto[^p521-1]: *Ecce, ego mittam piscatores multos, et piscabuntur eos;* et Matthaei quarto: *Venite post me; faciam vos fieri piscatores hominum.* — *Tempus noctis* est tempus Legis propter umbram litterae figurarum Legis; ad Hebraeos decimo[^p521-2]: *Umbram habens Lex futurorum bonorum, non ipsam imaginem rerum.* — Qui *nocte,* id est tempore Legis, *piscantur,* nihil accipiunt, quia, sicut dicitur ad Hebraeos septimo[^p521-3], *Lex neminem ad perfectum duxit.* — *Mane facto,* id est tempore gratiae, apparet Christus; secundae ad Corinthios tertio[^p521-4]: *Usque hodie velamen est super cor eorum. Nos autem revelata facie gloriam Domini speculantes, in eandem imaginem transformamur a claritate in claritatem, tanquam a Domini spiritu.* — Tunc *retia implentur,* quia post adventum Christi in carnem veritas manifestatur, et *pisces* in rete Ecclesiae comprehenduntur; propter quod Dominus dicebat supra duodecimo[^p521-5]: *Si exaltatus fuero a terra, omnia traham ad me ipsum.* — Hos *pisces,* scilicet fideles, praedicatores *trahunt,* quia per bona exempla ad vitam perducunt; de quo modo trahendi dicitur in Canticorum primo[^p521-6]: *Trahe me post te, in odorem curremus unguentorum tuorum.* — Et hoc *distantes ducentis cubitis,* in quo scilicet innuitur duplex perfectio, quae debet esse in praedicatore, scilicet *vitae* et *doctrinae;* de quibus Matthaei quinto[^p521-7]: *Qui fecerit et docuerit sic homines, hic magnus vocabitur in regno caelorum.*

### Quaestiones.

13. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic primo: cum stagnum Genesareth sit aqua dulcis et parva etiam in respectu maris[^p521-8], quare Evangelista appellat *mare?*

Dicendum, quod iuxta morem Iudaeorum hoc facit, qui magnam congregationem aquarum *mare* appellant, secundum illud Genesis primo[^p521-9]: *Congregationesque aquarum appellavit maria.*

14. Quaest. II. Item, cum scribatur Lucae nono[^p521-10]: *Nemo mittens manum ad aratrum et aspiciens retro aptus est regno Dei;* quid est, quod Petrus, qui retia reliquerat, rursus ad piscandum revertitur?

Respondet Gregorius[^p521-11], quod « negotium, quod ante conversionem sine peccato exstitit, hoc post conversionem repetere culpa non fuit ». Unde Augustinus: « Intelligendum est, discipulos non fuisse prohibitos arte sua licita et concessa victum necessarium quaerere, si aliud, unde viverent, non haberent ».

15. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Stetit Iesus in littore;* quaerit Gregorius[^p521-12], cum Dominus « ante passionem coram discipulis in *fluctibus maris* ambulaverit, supra sexto; quid est, quod post resurrectionem suam discipulis in mare laborantibus in *littore* stetit »?

Respondet Gregorius[^p521-13]: « Per *mare* praesens saeculum significatur, per *soliditatem littoris* perpetuitas aeternae quietis. Quia ergo Redemptor noster iam corruptionem carnis excesserat, post resurrectionem suam in littore stabat ».

16. Quaest. IV. Item, cum Dominus ante passionem iusserit, rete mitti in mare, Lucae quinto[^p521-14], non distinguens partem; quid est, quod post resurrectionem iubet, mitti in *dexteram navigii* partem?

Respondet Gregorius[^p521-15]: « Illa piscatio, in qua specialiter, in quam partem mitti rete debeat, non iubetur, praesentem Ecclesiam designavit, quae bonos simul ac malos colligit. Haec autem piscatio in solam *dexteram* missa est, quia ad videndum eius claritatis gloriam sola electorum Ecclesia pertingit, quae de sinistro opere nihil habebit ».

17. Quaest. V. Item quaeritur: quid est, quod Ioannes hic docet Petrum, cum dicit Petro: *Dominus est?* Nonne Petrus ita bene cognoscebat Dominum, ut Ioannes?
<!-- page 522 -->

Respondet Chrysostomus[^p522-1], quod « Petrus erat ferventior, Ioannes vero mente excellentior et perspicacior; ideo citius *cognovit,* sed non citius cucurrit ». — Vel potuit esse ratio, quia Petrus in piscando sollicitior[^p522-2] erat etc.

## English

### Chapter XXI.

#### On the third manifestation two things are noted.

1. *After this he manifested* etc. Here he treats of the third manifestation[^p519-1], which was made for the *confirmation of Peter;* and this part is divided in two. In the first he treats of the *manifestation;* in the second, of the *confirmation of Peter,* there: *When therefore they had dined* etc.

The first in two, because for the description of the manifestation it is necessary to describe the things lying round about it; therefore first *those* are touched; second, *the manifestation itself,* there[^p519-2]: *But when the morning was come* etc.

*The things lying round about* the manifestation are the *place,* in which he is manifested; and the *persons,* to whom he is manifested; and the *act* or business, by reason of which he is manifested[^p519-3].

2. (Vers. 1.). First, then, there is described the *place* in which this manifestation was made, when it is said: *After this Jesus manifested himself again*[^p519-4] *at the sea of Tiberias;* behold, the place. « The sea of Tiberias is the same as the lake of Genesareth and as the sea of Galilee »; but it is called *of Tiberias* from the city of Tiberias, which is situated beside that lake; and it is called *the sea of Galilee* from the region in which it is; it is called *the lake of Genesareth* from the proper nature of the lake itself, so that it is called *Genesareth* as if generating breezes[^p519-5]. — *And he manifested himself thus.* Here are touched the *persons* to whom he is manifested; whence he says:

3. (Vers. 2.). *There were together Simon Peter, and Thomas, who is called Didymus, and Nathanael, who was of Cana of Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two others of his disciples.* And so they were seven, because such a number is fitting and sufficient for every kind of testimony. And there is commended in this the sevenfold grace of the Holy Spirit, which ought to be in those who go *a fishing,* that is, to gather a people to the Lord; of which septenary it is said in Isaias, the fourth chapter[^p519-6]: « Seven women shall take hold of one man » — without these women there is no spiritual propagation — and in Isaias, the eleventh chapter: « The spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and of understanding, the spirit of counsel and of fortitude, the spirit of knowledge and of piety, and the spirit of the fear of the Lord shall fill him ».

4. (Vers. 3.) *Simon Peter saith to them.* Here is touched the *act* or office, on the occasion of which he is manifested; and this indeed was the act of fishing, in which all had come together. But Peter was the principal, because on his account especially this manifestation was made; whence he says: *Simon Peter saith to them: I go a fishing,* as the principal, because he had been a fisherman; Matthew, the fourth chapter[^p519-7]: « Jesus walking by the sea of Galilee saw two brethren, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishers ». And the others accompany him; whence he says: *They say to him: We also come with thee. And* so, agreeing in one, *they went forth and entered into the ship,* that they might fish, *and in that night*[^p519-8] *they caught nothing,* and so they laboured in vain; Luke, the fifth chapter: « Master, labouring all the night, we have taken noth-
<!-- page 520 -->
ing »; Gregory: « There came about for the disciples a great difficulty in fishing, that, the Teacher coming, there might come about a great sublimity of admiration ».

5. *But when the morning was come.* Here he treats of the *manifestation of the Lord*[^p520-1]; and this part is divided in two; because first there is described *the manifestation itself;* then it *is compared* to the preceding manifestations, there: *This is now the third time* etc.

In the manifestation itself, because he manifests himself in two ways: both as to the *divine* nature through the working of a miracle, and as to the *human* through eating; therefore there are two portions there. In the first there is described the manifestation made through the *taking of fishes;* in the second, through *eating,* there[^p520-2]: *As soon then as they came to land* etc.

The manifestation, then, as to the wonderful *taking of fishes* is described in this order. For first there is noted *the appearing of Christ;* second, *the address;* third, the wonderful *taking of fishes;* fourth, *the cognition of the Lord;* fifth, the fervent *love of Peter.*

6. (Vers. 4.). *The appearing of the Lord,* then, is touched when it is said: *But when the morning was come, Jesus stood on the shore.* According to the letter, *he stood in the morning,* because by night they could not see him; according to the spiritual understanding, because *morning* signifies the time of grace, in which Christ appeared; to the Romans, the thirteenth chapter[^p520-3]: « The night is passed, and the day is at hand. Let us therefore cast off the works of darkness and put on the armour of light ». And although he appeared, yet the disciples did not know him; therefore he says: *Yet the disciples knew not that it was Jesus.* Chrysostom[^p520-4]: « He does not straightway show himself, that through the miracle which he was about to do they might know him ».

7. (Vers. 5.). *Jesus therefore saith*[^p520-5] *to them.* Here is noted the second, namely *the address of the Lord;* and he asks of them the cause of so great a labour: *Children, have you any meat? Pulmentarium* ("meat") he here calls everything with which bread is seasoned, whether it be fish or something else, which we commonly call *pulmentum* ("relish"), as if the Lord were asking of them whether they had or had caught fishes, with which they could season their food, so that the bread, joined to it[^p520-6], might savour. *They answered* therefore him: *No;* as if they were saying: we are still labouring, because we do not yet have any.

8. (Vers. 6.). *He said*[^p520-7] *to them: Cast the net on the right side of the ship, and you shall find.* Here is noted the third, namely *the wonderful taking of fishes.* And it was *wonderful;* because, though they could take nothing, at the divine command they took so many, themselves obeying; whence: *They cast therefore the net*[^p520-8], according to the Lord's command, *and now they were not able to draw it for the multitude of fishes.* The like happened in Luke, the fifth chapter: « When they had done this, they took a copious multitude of fishes »; therefore in great abundance, that the divine largesse might be shown, which, according to that of James, the first chapter, « gives to all abundantly and upbraids not ».

9. (Vers. 7.). *That disciple therefore saith*[^p520-9] etc. Here is noted the fourth, namely *the cognition of the Lord* by John; whence: *That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved,* that is, John, *said to Peter: It is the Lord.* He calls him *Lord* for this reason, because he showed himself powerful, and one whom all things obey, even fishes; and therefore to be feared, according to that of Malachias, the first chapter[^p520-10]: « If I am the Lord, where is my fear »? For the disciples called him *Lord;* above, the thirteenth chapter: « You call me *Teacher* and *Lord* ».

10. *Simon therefore*[^p520-11] *Peter.* Here is noted the fifth, namely *the fervent love of Peter,* in this, that he could not contain himself from running at once; therefore he says: *But Simon Peter, when he heard that it was the Lord, girt his coat about him,* lest he should appear naked before the Lord; *for he was naked; and he cast himself into the sea,* namely to swim. He did the like in Matthew, the fourteenth chapter[^p520-12]: « Lord, if it is thou, bid me come to thee upon the waters ».

11. (Vers. 8.). *But the other disciples came in the ship;* because they were not so fervent, not daring to enter the sea; Wisdom, the fourteenth chapter[^p520-13]: « Men trust their lives even to a little wood » etc. And another reason: *For they were not far from the land,* and therefore they could come quickly; *but as it were two hundred cubits, dragging the net of fishes.*
<!-- page 521 -->

12. According to the *spiritual* intelligence: the fishermen fishing by night caught nothing; when morning was come, Jesus appeared, and they filled the nets, and, being two hundred cubits distant from the land, they drew the nets. — The *fishermen* are the preachers; Jeremias, the sixteenth chapter[^p521-1]: *Behold, I will send many fishers, and they shall fish them;* and Matthew, the fourth chapter: *Come after me; I will make you to be fishers of men.* — *The time of night* is the time of the Law, on account of the shadow of the letter of the figures of the Law; to the Hebrews, the tenth chapter[^p521-2]: *The Law having a shadow of the good things to come, not the very image of the things.* — Those who *fish by night,* that is, in the time of the Law, take nothing, because, as is said to the Hebrews, the seventh chapter[^p521-3], *the Law brought no one to perfection.* — *When morning was come,* that is, in the time of grace, Christ appears; the second to the Corinthians, the third chapter[^p521-4]: *Until this day the veil is upon their heart. But we, beholding the glory of the Lord with open face, are transformed into the same image from clarity to clarity, as by the Spirit of the Lord.* — Then *the nets are filled,* because after the coming of Christ in the flesh the truth is manifested, and *fishes* are taken in the net of the Church; on account of which the Lord said, above, the twelfth chapter[^p521-5]: *If I be lifted up from the earth, I will draw all things to myself.* — These *fishes,* namely the faithful, the preachers *draw,* because through good examples they lead them to life; of which manner of drawing it is said in the Canticle, the first chapter[^p521-6]: *Draw me after thee; we will run to the odour of thy ointments.* — And this *being two hundred cubits distant,* in which namely is intimated the twofold perfection which ought to be in the preacher, namely *of life* and *of teaching*[^tr-doctrina]; of which Matthew, the fifth chapter[^p521-7]: *He that shall do and shall so teach men, he shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven.*

### Questions.

13. Quaest. I. But it is asked here first: since the lake of Genesareth is sweet water and small even in respect of the sea[^p521-8], why does the Evangelist call it a *sea?*

It must be said that he does this according to the custom of the Jews, who call a great gathering of waters a *sea,* according to that of Genesis, the first chapter[^p521-9]: *And the gathering together of the waters he called seas.*

14. Quaest. II. Likewise, since it is written in Luke, the ninth chapter[^p521-10]: *No man putting his hand to the plough and looking back is fit for the kingdom of God;* what is it that Peter, who had left his nets, returns again to fishing?

Gregory responds[^p521-11] that « the business which before conversion existed without sin, to take this up again after conversion was no fault ». Whence Augustine: « It is to be understood that the disciples were not forbidden to seek the necessary sustenance by their licit and permitted craft, if they did not have anything else whence they might live ».

15. Quaest. III. Likewise it is asked about this, that he says: *Jesus stood on the shore;* Gregory asks[^p521-12], since the Lord « before the passion walked on the *waves of the sea* before the disciples, above, the sixth chapter; what is it that after his resurrection he stood on the *shore* while the disciples were labouring in the sea »?

Gregory responds[^p521-13]: « By the *sea* the present world is signified, by the *solidity of the shore* the perpetuity of eternal rest. Because, then, our Redeemer had already passed beyond the corruption of the flesh, after his resurrection he stood on the shore ».

16. Quaest. IV. Likewise, since the Lord before the passion commanded the net to be cast into the sea, Luke, the fifth chapter[^p521-14], not distinguishing a side; what is it that after the resurrection he commands it to be cast on the *right* side *of the ship?*

Gregory responds[^p521-15]: « That fishing, in which it is not specially commanded on which side the net ought to be cast, designated the present Church, which gathers the good together with the wicked. But this fishing was cast on the *right* side alone, because only the Church of the elect attains to seeing the glory of his clarity, which will have nothing of the left-hand work ».

17. Quaest. V. Likewise it is asked: what is it that John here teaches Peter, when he says to Peter: *It is the Lord?* Did not Peter know the Lord as well as John?
<!-- page 522 -->

Chrysostom responds[^p522-1] that « Peter was more fervent, but John more excellent and more perspicacious in mind; therefore he *knew* him sooner, but did not run sooner ». — Or the reason could have been that Peter was more solicitous[^p522-2] in fishing etc.
