# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XII, Vers. 36-50
## *Postquam descripta est futura gentium vocatio et passionis futurae praedictio et expositio; hic quarto innuitur futura Iudaeorum excaecatio*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 421 -->

55. *Haec locutus est Iesus.* Postquam descripta est futura gentium vocatio et passionis futurae praedictio et expositio; hic quarto innuitur futura *Iudaeorum excaecatio.* Et proceditur in huius rei declarationem[^p421-10] hoc ordine. Primo enim innuitur *ipsa excaecatio;* secundo vero, *aliquorum electio;* tertio vero *infirmorum electorum fit confortatio;* quarto vero ipsis *excaecatis infertur comminatio.*

(Vers. 36.). *Excaecatio Iudaeorum* innuitur in ipso facto Domini, quod abscondit se ab ipsis Iudaeis, qui ei contradicere coeperant; propter quod dicit: *Haec locutus est Iesus et abiit et abscondit se ab eis.* Augustinus[^p421-11]: « Non ab eis qui cum ramis palmarum obviam ei venerunt, sed ab his qui videbant et invidebant »; Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo: « Abscondam faciem meam ab eis et considerabo novissima eorum; generatio enim perversa est, et infi-
<!-- page 422 -->
deles filii ». Et ista absconsio corporalis signum erat absconsionis spiritualis[^p422-1]; et hoc innuit littera sequens:

56. (Vers. 37.). *Cum autem tanta signa fecisset coram eis, non credebant in eum;* sicut fuit signum de suscitatione Lazari; *non* tamen *credebant,* quia malitia eorum non sinebat; Sapientiae secundo[^p422-2]: « Excaecavit eos malitia eorum ». Nec hoc casu gestum est, sed a Domino praevisum et per Prophetam Isaiam praedictum; propter quod dicit:

57. (Vers. 38.). *Ut sermo Isaiae Prophetae impleretur, quem dixit*[^p422-3]*: Domine, quis credidit;* quasi dicat: rarus, vel nullus; *auditui nostro?* id est, ei quod audivimus a Domino; vel ei quod audit a nobis prophetantibus, quia « fides ex auditu », ad Romanos decimo. *Et brachium Domini cui revelatum est?* Brachium Domini, ut dicit Augustinus[^p422-4], est Filius Patris. « Sicut enim tuum brachium est, per quod operaris, ita Dei brachium dictum est Verbum, per quod operatus est mundum. Hoc brachium nec porrectum extenditur nec collectum contrahitur ». Nec solum fuit praevisum, sed etiam evenit iusto Dei iudicio; propter quod subdit:

58. (Vers. 39.). *Propterea non poterant credere,* in eum, quia scilicet excaecati erant, et hoc dixit Isaias; ideo subdit: *Quia iterum dixit Isaias:*

59. (Vers. 40.). *Excaecavit oculos eorum et induravit cor eorum;* Isaiae sexto[^p422-5]: « Excaeca cor populi huius et aures eius aggrava et oculos eius claude » etc.; et ad Romanos primo: « Tradidit eos Deus in reprobum sensum ». *Ut non videant oculis et non intelligant corde et,* intelligendo, *convertantur, et,* convertendo, *sanem eos;* Ieremiae tertio[^p422-6]: « Convertimini ad me, et sanabo aversiones vestras ». Et ne credas auctoritatem istam usurpatam, subdit:

60. (Vers. 41.). *Haec dixit Isaias, quando vidit gloriam eius et locutus est de eo;* Isaiae sexto[^p422-7], ubi dicitur, quod « vidit Dominum sedentem super solium excelsum et elevatum, et plena erat omnis terra maiestate eius ».

61. (Vers. 42.). *Verumtamen ex*[^p422-8] *principibus.* Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *quorundam electio;* ideo adversative dicit: licet dictum sit, quod non crediderunt, *verumtamen ex principibus multi crediderunt in eum;* ad Romanos undecimo: « In hoc tempore reliquiae secundum electionem gratiae salvae factae sunt ». Sed tamen non erant in fide perfecti; propter quod subdit: *Sed propter Pharisaeos non confitebantur, ut de*[^p422-9] *synagoga non eiicerentur;* supra nono: « Iam enim conspiraverant Iudaei, ut si quis eum confiteretur Christum, extra synagogam fieret ». Et hoc quidem timebant tanquam imperfecti; propter quod subdit rationem timoris:

62. (Vers. 43.). *Dilexerunt enim magis gloriam hominum quam gloriam Dei;* et stulte egerunt, secundum quod dicitur primi Machabaeorum secundo[^p422-10]: « A verbis viri peccatoris ne timueritis, quia gloria eius stercus et vermis est; hodie extollitur et cras non invenietur »; Psalmus: « Dissipavit ossa eorum qui hominibus placent » etc.; ad Galatas primo: « An quaero hominibus placere? Si adhuc hominibus placerem, Christi servus non essem ».

63. (Vers. 44.). *Iesus autem clamavit et dixit.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet *infirmorum in fide fortificatio,* quae fit affectuosa Christi exhortatione, qua sublevat mentes credentium ad dignitatem Divinitatis. Propter quod dicit: *Qui credit in me non credit in me,* secundum naturam visibilem, *sed in eum qui misit me*[^p422-11]*;* vel: credendo non honorat me tantum, sed eum qui misit me; in quo ostendit, quod credere in se non est credere in hominem, sed in Deum; et hoc manifestat:

64. (Vers. 45.). *Et qui videt me videt eum qui misit me;* ergo unum sunt in essentia mittens et missus, quia qui videt unum videt et alium. Unde infra decimo quarto[^p422-12]: « Philippe, qui videt me videt et Patrem meum ». Et ratio est *unitas essentiae,* « quia ego in Patre, et Pater in me est »; et ipse *Filius* est *ratio videndi Patrem,* sicut verbum, dicentem, sicut splendor, lucem. Propter quod dicit:

65. (Vers. 46.). *Ego lux veni in mundum, ut omnis, qui credit in me, in tenebris non maneat,* sed videat et ipse etiam lux fiat; ad Ephesios quin-
<!-- page 423 -->
to[^p423-1]: « Fuistis aliquando tenebrae, nunc autem lux in Domino »; et hoc credendo in eum, de quo dicitur ad Hebraeos primo: « Cum sit splendor gloriae et figura substantiae eius » etc.

66. (Vers. 47.). *Et si quis audierit verba mea.* Tangitur hic quartum, scilicet *contra non credentes facta comminatio;* et simul in comminatione ostendit *suam benignitatem* et *iudicii severitatem.* Propterea dicit: *Si*[^p423-2] *quis audierit verba mea et non custodierit ea, ego non iudico eum,* id est, non condemno, et hoc propter meam *benignitatem: Non enim veni,* in mundum, *ut iudicem mundum, sed ut salvificem mundum;* supra tertio[^p423-3]: « Non misit Deus Filium suum in mundum, ut iudicet mundum, sed ut salvetur mundus per ipsum »; Lucae nono: « Filius hominis non venit animas perdere, sed salvare ». Nec tamen effugient *severitatem iudicii;* propter quod dicit:

67. (Vers. 48.). *Qui me spernit et non accipit verba mea habet qui iudicet eum.* Et quis sit ille qui iudicat, ostendit: *Sermo, quem locutus sum, ille iudicabit eum in novissimo die;* Matthaei vigesimo quarto[^p423-4]: « Caelum et terra transibunt, verba autem mea non praeteribunt »; immo « stabunt, sicut dicit Chrysostomus, in ordine accusationis, redarguentia eos et omnem abscindentia excusationem »; Matthaei quinto: « Esto consentiens adversario tuo cito, dum es cum illo in via » etc., intelligitur de sermone Christi. Et quod sermo suus habeat virtutem iudicandi eos, manifestat: quia non est sermo humanus, sed divinus et verus; propterea dicit:

68. (Vers. 49.). *Quia ego a me*[^p423-5] *ipso non sum locutus,* sicut vane gloriosus, de quo supra: « Qui a semetipso loquitur gloriam propriam quaerit »; *sed qui misit me Pater, ille mihi mandatum dedit, quid dicam et quid loquar.* Et ideo ego loquor secundum mandatum eius.

69. (Vers. 50.). *Et scio, quia mandatum eius est vita aeterna;* unde Matthaei decimo nono[^p423-6]: « Si vis ad vitam ingredi, serva mandata »; Deuteronomii trigesimo: « Elige ergo vitam, ut et tu vivas, et semen tuum, et diligas Dominum Deum tuum atque obedias voci eius »; et ideo a mandato eius non declino. Propter quod dicit: *Quae ergo loquor ego, sicut dixit mihi Pater, sic loquor;* quia ipse loquens homo per omnia se divino mandato et voluntati conformabat; supra octavo[^p423-7]: « Quae audivi a Patre loquor in mundo ». Et ideo ex hoc vult habere, quod sermo suus non est spernendus, sed accipiendus ut sermo divinus; sicut de bonis dicitur primae ad Thessalonicenses secundo[^p423-8]: « Cum accepissetis verbum auditus Dei, accepistis illud non sicut verbum hominum, sed, sicut est vere, verbum Dei, qui operatur in vobis, qui credidistis ».

### Quaestiones.

70. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic primo de hoc quod dicit, quod *non poterant credere, quia Isaias dixit: Excaecavit* etc. — Videtur primo, quod male dicat, quia, si non poterant credere, ergo non erat eis imputandum in peccatum.
<!-- page 424 -->

Ad hoc respondet Chrysostomus[^p424-1]: « Non poterant, id est, non volebant ».

Sed illud non solvit[^p424-2]: 1. quia tunc facile esset omnia solvere; quare non dixit Evangelista: non volebant, cum ita bene haberet ad manum?

2. Item, esto quod is esset sensus: non volebant credere; quia Isaias dicit: *Excaecavit oculos eorum,* tunc videtur, quod Deus sit causa malae voluntatis eorum.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod *aliquid non posse* dicitur dupliciter: aut quia *nullo modo* potest, aut quia *cum magna difficultate* potest; sic dicitur, quod vir assuetus in malo non potest bene facere; Ieremiae decimo tertio[^p424-3]: « Si mutare potest Aethiops pellem suam, et pardus varietatem suam; sic vos potestis bene facere, cum didiceritis malum ». — Secundum istam viam dicitur, quod vir excaecatus non potest credere; et Evangelista hoc vult dicere, quod non poterant credere, quia *excaecati* erant, sicut praedixerat Isaias; non quod *praedictio* Isaiae fuerit causa, sed *propria malitia* et *divinum iudicium,* iudicium scilicet desertionis. Augustinus[^p424-4]: « Excaecat et obdurat Deus *deserendo, non adiuvando;* quod occulto iudicio fieri potest, non tamen iniusto ».

71. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Qui credit in me non credit in me* etc. — Videtur hic contradictio. — *Si tu dicas,* quod debet intelligi *tantum,* ut sit sensus: qui *credit* in me non credit in me tantum, sed etiam in eum qui misit me[^p424-5]; secundum istam viam deberet similiter dicere: qui *videt* me non videt me tantum, sed eum qui misit me.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod *fides* distinguit articulos, et quando quis cogitat de uno articulo, non cogitat de alio, immo aliqui credunt unum, qui non credunt alium; sed in *visione,* quia una est essentia, qui unam videt personam omnes videt. Ideo in *visione unit: qui videt me videt et Patrem*[^p424-6], quia impossibile est unum videre sine alio. Sed in *credulitate distinguit* et *solitudinem excludit:* qui credit in me non in me solum credit, sed in me et in eum qui misit me.

Aliter potest dici, quod est credere *auctoritati,* sed videre vel intelligere *rationi*[^p424-7]. Quia ergo *auctoritas* et potentia est in Patre per appropriationem, ratione cuius est credere; ideo Filius *credere* appropriat ad Patrem; et sic dictum est[^p424-8], quod *Pater trahit ad Filium;* sed in *videndo* et *revelando* Filius ducit ad Patrem ut verbum et lumen; et ideo *videre* non sic appropriat Patri, quemadmodum *credere,* cum dicit: *Qui credit in me* etc.

## English
<!-- page 421 -->

55. *These things Jesus spoke.* After the future calling of the Gentiles has been described, and the prediction and exposition of the future passion, here, fourth, the future *blinding of the Jews* is intimated. And one proceeds in the declaration[^p421-10] of this matter in this order. For first is intimated *the blinding itself;* second, *the election of some;* third, *the comforting of the infirm elect is made;* fourth, *a threat is brought upon* those *blinded.*

(V. 36.). *The blinding of the Jews* is intimated in the very deed of the Lord, in that he hid himself from those very Jews who had begun to contradict him; on account of which he says: *These things Jesus spoke; and he went away, and hid himself from them.* Augustine[^p421-11]: « Not from those who came to meet him with branches of palms, but from those who saw and envied »; Deuteronomy, the thirty-second chapter: « I will hide my face from them, and will consider their last things; for it is a perverse generation, and unfaithful
<!-- page 422 -->
children ». And this bodily hiding was a sign of the spiritual hiding[^p422-1]; and the following text intimates this:

56. (V. 37.). *And whereas he had done so many signs before them, they believed not in him;* such as was the sign concerning the raising of Lazarus; *they* nevertheless *believed not,* because their malice did not allow it; Wisdom, the second chapter[^p422-2]: « Their malice blinded them ». Nor was this done by chance, but it was foreseen by the Lord and foretold through the Prophet Isaias; on account of which he says:

57. (V. 38.). *That the word of Isaias the Prophet might be fulfilled, which he said*[^p422-3]*: Lord, who hath believed;* as if to say: rare, or none; *our hearing?* that is, that which we have heard from the Lord; or that which he hears from us prophesying, because « faith cometh by hearing », to the Romans, the tenth chapter. *And to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?* The arm of the Lord, as Augustine says[^p422-4], is the Son of the Father. « For as your arm is that through which you work, so the Word is called the arm of God, through which he wrought the world. This arm is neither extended by being stretched forth nor contracted by being drawn in ». Nor was it only foreseen, but it also came about by the just judgement of God; on account of which he subjoins:

58. (V. 39.). *Therefore they could not believe,* in him, because, namely, they had been blinded, and Isaias said this; therefore he subjoins: *Because Isaias said again:*

59. (V. 40.). *He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart;* Isaias, the sixth chapter[^p422-5]: « Blind the heart of this people, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes » etc.; and to the Romans, the first chapter: « God delivered them up to a reprobate sense ». *That they should not see with their eyes, and not understand with their heart, and,* by understanding, *be converted, and,* by being converted, *I should heal them;* Jeremias, the third chapter[^p422-6]: « Be converted to me, and I will heal your revoltings ». And lest you believe this authority to be usurped, he subjoins:

60. (V. 41.). *These things said Isaias, when he saw his glory, and spoke of him;* Isaias, the sixth chapter[^p422-7], where it is said that « he saw the Lord sitting upon a throne high and elevated, and all the earth was full of his majesty ».

61. (V. 42.). *However, of*[^p422-8] *the chief men.* Here is touched the second, namely, *the election of certain ones;* therefore he says adversatively: although it has been said that they did not believe, *however, of the chief men many believed in him;* to the Romans, the eleventh chapter: « In this time a remnant have been saved according to the election of grace ». But nevertheless they were not perfect in faith; on account of which he subjoins: *But because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, that they might not be cast out of*[^p422-9] *the synagogue;* above, the ninth chapter: « For the Jews had already agreed among themselves, that if any man should confess him to be Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue ». And this indeed they feared as imperfect men; on account of which he subjoins the reason of the fear:

62. (V. 43.). *For they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God;* and they acted foolishly, according to what is said in the first of Machabees, the second chapter[^p422-10]: « Fear not the words of a sinful man, for his glory is dung and worms; today he is lifted up, and tomorrow he shall not be found »; the Psalm: « He hath scattered the bones of them that please men » etc.; to the Galatians, the first chapter: « Do I seek to please men? If I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ ».

63. (V. 44.). *And Jesus cried, and said.* Here is touched the third, namely, *the fortifying of the infirm in faith,* which is made by the affectionate exhortation of Christ, by which he lifts up the minds of those believing to the dignity of the Divinity. On account of which he says: *He that believeth in me, doth not believe in me,* according to the visible nature, *but in him that sent me*[^p422-11]*;* or: by believing he does not honour me only, but him that sent me; in which he shows that to believe in him is not to believe in a man, but in God; and he manifests this:

64. (V. 45.). *And he that seeth me, seeth him that sent me;* therefore the one sending and the one sent are one in essence, because he who sees the one sees the other also. Whence below, the fourteenth chapter[^p422-12]: « Philip, he that seeth me seeth my Father also ». And the reason is *the unity of essence,* « because I am in the Father, and the Father is in me »; and the *Son* himself is *the reason of seeing the Father,* as the word is of the one speaking, as brightness is of the light. On account of which he says:

65. (V. 46.). *I am come a light into the world; that whosoever believeth in me, may not remain in darkness,* but may see and himself also become light; to the Ephesians, the fifth
<!-- page 423 -->
chapter[^p423-1]: « You were heretofore darkness, but now light in the Lord »; and this by believing in him, of whom it is said to the Hebrews, the first chapter: « Since he is the brightness of glory and the figure of his substance » etc.

66. (V. 47.). *And if any man hear my words.* Here is touched the fourth, namely, *the threat made against those not believing;* and at once in the threat he shows *his benignity* and *the severity of judgement.* Therefore he says: *If*[^p423-2] *any man hear my words, and keep them not, I do not judge him,* that is, I do not condemn him, and this on account of my *benignity: For I came not,* into the world, *to judge the world, but to save the world;* above, the third chapter[^p423-3]: « God sent not his Son into the world, to judge the world, but that the world may be saved by him »; Luke, the ninth chapter: « The Son of man came not to destroy souls, but to save ». Yet they will not escape *the severity of judgement;* on account of which he says:

67. (V. 48.). *He that despiseth me, and receiveth not my words, hath one that judgeth him.* And who that one is who judges, he shows: *The word that I have spoken, the same shall judge him in the last day;* Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p423-4]: « Heaven and earth shall pass, but my words shall not pass »; nay rather « they shall stand, as Chrysostom says, in the order of accusation, reproving them and cutting off every excuse »; Matthew, the fifth chapter: « Be at agreement with thy adversary betimes, whilst thou art in the way with him » etc., is understood of the word of Christ. And that his word has the power of judging them, he manifests: because it is not a human word, but divine and true; therefore he says:

68. (V. 49.). *For I have not spoken of*[^p423-5] *myself,* as one vainglorious, of whom above: « He that speaketh of himself seeketh his own glory »; *but the Father who sent me, he gave me commandment what I should say, and what I should speak.* And therefore I speak according to his commandment.

69. (V. 50.). *And I know that his commandment is life everlasting;* whence Matthew, the nineteenth chapter[^p423-6]: « If thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments »; Deuteronomy, the thirtieth chapter: « Choose therefore life, that both thou and thy seed may live, and that thou mayst love the Lord thy God, and obey his voice »; and therefore I do not decline from his commandment. On account of which he says: *The things therefore that I speak, even as the Father said unto me, so do I speak;* because he himself, speaking as a man, conformed himself in all things to the divine commandment and will; above, the eighth chapter[^p423-7]: « The things I have heard from the Father I speak in the world ». And therefore from this he wills to have it that his word is not to be despised, but to be received as a divine word; as is said of the good in the first to the Thessalonians, the second chapter[^p423-8]: « When you had received the word of the hearing of God, you received it not as the word of men, but, as it is indeed, the word of God, who worketh in you that have believed ».

### Questions.

70. Quaest. I. But here it is asked first concerning what he says, that *they could not believe, because Isaias said: He hath blinded* etc. — It seems first that he speaks badly, because, if they could not believe, then it was not to be imputed to them for sin.
<!-- page 424 -->

To this Chrysostom responds[^p424-1]: « They could not, that is, they would not ».

But that does not solve it[^p424-2]: 1. because then it would be easy to solve everything; why did the Evangelist not say: they would not, since he had it so well to hand?

2. Likewise, grant that this were the sense: they would not believe; because Isaias says: *He hath blinded their eyes,* then it seems that God is the cause of their bad will.

**I respond:** It must be said that *not to be able to do something* is said in two ways: either because one can *in no way,* or because one can *with great difficulty;* thus it is said that a man accustomed to evil cannot do well; Jeremias, the thirteenth chapter[^p424-3]: « If the Ethiopian can change his skin, and the leopard his spots; so you can do well, when you have learned evil ». — In this way it is said that a blinded man cannot believe; and the Evangelist wills to say this, that they could not believe, because they had been *blinded,* as Isaias had foretold; not that the *prediction* of Isaias was the cause, but *their own malice* and *the divine judgement,* namely the judgement of forsaking. Augustine[^p424-4]: « God blinds and hardens *by forsaking, not by helping;* which he can do by a hidden judgement, yet not by an unjust one ».

71. Quaest. II. Again, it is asked concerning what he says: *He that believeth in me, doth not believe in me* etc. — There seems to be a contradiction here. — *If you say* that one must understand *only,* so that the sense is: he that *believeth* in me doth not believe in me only, but also in him that sent me[^p424-5]; in this way he ought likewise to say: he that *seeth* me seeth not me only, but him that sent me.

**I respond:** It must be said that *faith* distinguishes the articles, and when someone thinks of one article, he does not think of another; nay rather some believe one who do not believe another; but in *vision,* because the essence is one, he who sees one person sees all. Therefore in *vision he unites: he that seeth me seeth the Father also*[^p424-6], because it is impossible to see one without the other. But in *belief he distinguishes* and *excludes solitude:* he that believeth in me doth not believe in me alone, but in me and in him that sent me.

In another way it can be said that to believe belongs to *authority,* but to see or understand to *reason*[^p424-7]. Since, then, *authority* and power are in the Father by appropriation, by reason of which there is believing; therefore the Son appropriates *to believe* to the Father; and thus it has been said[^p424-8] that *the Father draws to the Son;* but in *seeing* and *revealing* the Son leads to the Father as word and lumen[^tr-lumen]; and therefore he does not so appropriate *to see* to the Father, as he does *to believe,* when he says: *He that believeth in me* etc.
