# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. I, Vers. 19-23
## *Primo, de manifestatione per Ioannem*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 256 -->

#### Secundo, de manifestatione Verbi incarnati in speciali dupliciter.

44. *Et hoc est testimonium Ioannis.* Egit supra de manifestatione Verbi incarnati *in generali*, hic agit *in speciali;* et dividitur haec pars, quia ipsum est *sui* manifestativum et manifestatur *per vocem;* ideo habet haec pars duas partes. In prima agit de manifestatione facta per Ioannem, qui fuit *vox* praecurrens Verbum. In secunda de manifestatione facta per ipsum Verbum, infra eodem[^p256-4]: *In crastinum voluit exire.*

#### Primo, de manifestatione per Ioannem.

Pars prima habet tres partes secundum tria testimonia Ioannis, quae sic distinguuntur. Primum est de *naturarum duarum veritate;* secundum est de *baptizandi potestate;* tertium vero, de *sanctitate.* — Vel aliter etiam distinguuntur: quia primum fuit factum *Pharisaeis;* secundum, *turbis;* tertium vero, *Ioannis discipulis.* — Tertio modo sic: quia primum testimonium perhibitum est, Christo *absente;* secundum, Christo ad eum *veniente;* tertium, ante eum *deambulante.* Primum testimonium notatur hic: *Et hoc est testimonium;* secundum ibi[^p256-5]: *Altera die;* tertium ibi: *Altera iterum die.*

Prima pars, in qua testimonium ponit, Christo *absente*, habet duas, quia primo quaerentibus respondet veritatem de *se ipso;* secundo vero, de *Christo*, ibi[^p256-6]: *Et qui missi fuerant erant ex Pharisaeis.*

Veritatem ergo, qua de *se ipso* respondit quaerentibus, hoc ordine describit Evangelista: primo in quaerentibus insinuat *auctoritatem;* secundo, in Ioanne *stabilitatem;* tertio, in quaerentibus *importunitatem;* quarto vero, in Ioanne *humilem veritatem.*

45. (Vers. 19.). Quaerentium igitur *auctoritatem* insinuat: quia erant personae authenticae et auctorizabiliter missae, quibus Ioannes perhibet testimonium; propterea dicit: *Et hoc est testimonium Ioannis.* Ita dictum est supra[^p256-7], quod « Ioannes testimonium perhibet »; modo specificat: *Et hoc est testimonium Ioannis*, et demonstrat testimonium cum suis circumstantiis. *Hoc*, pronomen, *hoc, quando miserunt Iudaei ab Ierosolymis sacerdotes et levitas;* ecce, personarum auctoritas, quia missi erant et honorabiles erant; infra quinto[^p256-8]: « Vos misistis ad Ioannem, et ille testimonium perhibuit veritati »; *miserunt*, inquam, *ut interrogarent eum: Tu quis es?* in persona scilicet omnium. Simili modo misit Ioannes discipulos suos, ut quaererent in persona sua, ad Christum; Matthaei undecimo[^p256-9]: « Mittens Ioannes duos ex discipulis suis, ait illi: Tu es, qui venturus es, an alium exspectamus »? Hoc volebant quaerere Iudaei per levitas.

46. (Vers. 20.). *Et confessus est.* Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *Ioannis stabilitas*, qui non fuit eorum auctoritate perterritus, ut a vera confessione declinaret. Ideo dicit: *Et confessus est et non negavit*, id est, veram confessionem fecit et in ipsa permansit; secundae ad Corinthios primo[^p256-10]: « Fidelis autem Deus, quia sermo noster, qui fuit apud vos, non est in illo *est* et *non;* sed est in illo *est* ». *Et confessus est*, veritatem scilicet, *quia non sum ego Christus.* Respondit magis intentioni quam quaestioni. De hac fideli confessione Matthaei decimo[^p256-11]: « Omnis, qui confitebitur me coram hominibus, confitebor et ego eum coram Patre meo ». Ioannes maxime confessus est, quia cum posset reputari Christus, noluit; Gregorius: « Dum non vult appetere nomen Christi, factus est membrum Christi ».

47. (Vers. 21.). *Et interrogaverunt eum.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet *quaerentium importunitas*, quia non desistunt unica responsione, sed instanter quaerunt; ideo dicit: *Et interrogaverunt eum: Quid ergo?* id est, quid es, si non es Christus? *Elias es tu? Et dixit: Non sum.* Et adhuc instant: *Propheta es tu?* aliquis scilicet minor. *Et respondit: Non.*
<!-- page 257 -->
Ideo generaliter de aliis Prophetis quaerunt, de Elia specialiter, quia Eliam credebant praecedere Christum; Matthaei decimo septimo[^p257-1]: « Quid dicunt Scribae, quia Eliam oportet venire primum »? Sed adhuc instant:

48. (Vers. 22.). *Dixerunt ergo ei: Quis es?* Adhuc instant et rationem instantiae dicunt: *Ut responsum demus his qui miserunt nos. Quid dicis de te ipso?* Stultum videbatur eis redire ad mittentes sine certa responsione. Nam sapientis nuntii est responsa certa referre; Proverbiorum vigesimo secundo[^p257-2]: « Ecce, descripsi tibi sapientiam, ut eloquia veritatis responderes ex his illis qui miserunt te ».

49. (Vers. 23.). *Ait: Ego vox clamantis.* Tangitur hic quartum, scilicet in responsione *humilis veritas; humilis*, quia non sibi *dignitatem* attribuit, sed *officium.* Ideo ait: *Ego vox clamantis in deserto; clamantis*, inquam, hoc: *Dirigite viam Domini.* Ad litteram Ioannes in deserto clamabat; Matthaei tertio[^p257-3]: « Venit Ioannes praedicans in deserto Iudaeae et clamabat: Dirigite viam Domini »; quia praedicabat poenitentiam, per quam dirigitur via peccatoris ad Dominum, quae scilicet *obliqua* est, quamdiu peccat, *dirigitur* vero per mandatorum observantiam; unde infra decimo quarto[^p257-4]: « Si quis diligit me, sermonem meum servabit, et Pater meus diliget eum, et ad eum veniemus ». Sic dirigitur via Domini ad cor; et hoc *officium* confirmat testimonio Isaiae: *Sicut dicit Isaias propheta;* Isaiae quadragesimo: « Vox clamantis in deserto: Parate viam Domini ».

### Quaestiones.

50. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic primo de interrogatione Iudaeorum. Cum enim scirent originem Ioannis et vitam, ad quid quaerunt: *Tu quis es?*

Et respondendum est ad hoc, quod *quis* aliquando quaerit *substantiam*, ut cum dicitur: quis fecit hoc vel illud? Aliquando *propriam nominationem*, ut dicit Priscianus[^p257-5].

Quis, pater, ille virum qui sic comitatur euntem?

Aliquando specialiter inter alias conditiones quaerit *dignitatem;* nam personae dignae et eminentes *personae* ecclesiasticae vocantur[^p257-6]. Ideo hic dicendum, quod etsi scirent *originem, vitam* et *nomen*, tamen dubitabant de *dignitate.*

51. Quaest. II. Item de responsione Ioannis quaeritur, quia ipse respondet secundae interrogationi, quod non est Elias.

**Contra:** 1. Matthaei undecimo[^p257-7]: « Si vultis scire, ipse est Elias ». Ergo aut Dominus dicit falsum, aut ipse.

2. Item respondet, se non esse prophetam; *contra:* Lucae primo[^p257-8]: « Tu puer propheta Altissimi vocaberis ».

**Respondeo**, quod Ioannes respondebat magis ad intentionem quaerentium quam ad quaestionem. Intelligendum igitur, quod Iudaei quaerentes de Elia, corporaliter de ipso quaerebant, de illo eodem, qui fuit in veteri testamento; Ioannes vero respondet, quod non est Elias proprie; sed Dominus dicit eum Eliam figurative; quia hoc fuit Ioannes primo adventu, quod Elias secundo. Similiter dicendum, quod ipsi quaerebant de propheta et officio prophetandi praecise, sicut propheta praedicit *futura;* Ioannes vero propheta erat et plus quam propheta[^p257-9], quia demonstravit *praesentem.* Ideo secundum intentionem eorum, se non esse prophetam, respondet.

52. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de tertia responsione: 1. Quare scilicet Ioannes dictus est *vox*, et quare dixit, se esse *vocem?*

2. Item, quid est hoc quod dicit: *Dirigite viam Domini;* quia Ieremiae decimo[^p257-10]: « Scio, Domine, quia non est hominis via eius neque viri, ut dirigat gressus suos ». Ergo adhortatur ad impossibile.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod sicut Filius Dei rectissime dicitur *verbum;* ita praecursor eius rectissime dicitur *vox. Vox* quadruplicem proprietatem habet respectu verbi, ratione cuius recte dicitur. Vox *praeit* verbum; sic Ioannes Christum; Lucae primo[^p257-11]: « Ipse praeibit ante illum in spiritu et virtute Eliae ». *Manifestat* verbum; sic Ioannes Christum; supra eodem[^p257-12]: « Hic venit in testimonium, ut testimonium perhiberet de lumine ». *Transit* et relin-
<!-- page 258 -->
quit verbum manens; sic Ioannes Christum; infra tertio[^p258-1]: « Me oportet minui, illum autem crescere ». *Est inutilis* sine verbo; sic Ioannis praedicatio sine Christo; infra eodem[^p258-2]: « Ego baptizo vos in aqua, medius autem vestrum stetit quem vos nescitis ».

2. Quod obiicitur de directione, dicendum, quod *directio* viae est per gratiam et caritatem, quae a solo Deo est; sed *praeparatio* ad directionem est facienda, secundum quod in nobis est. Et hoc praecipitur hic; unde Gregorius[^p258-3]: « Via Domini ad cor dirigitur, cum ad praeceptum vita praeparatur ».

## English
<!-- page 256 -->

#### Second, on the manifestation of the incarnate Word in special, in two ways.

44. *And this is the testimony of John.* Above he treated of the manifestation of the incarnate Word *in general;* here he treats of it *in special;* and this part is divided, because it is itself manifestative *of itself*, and it is manifested *through the voice;* therefore this part has two parts. In the first he treats of the manifestation made through John, who was the *voice* running before the Word. In the second, of the manifestation made through the Word himself, below in the same[^p256-4]: *On the following day he would go forth.*

#### First, on the manifestation through John.

The first part has three parts according to the three testimonies of John, which are distinguished thus. The first is about the *truth of the two natures;* the second is about the *power of baptizing;* but the third, about *holiness.* — Or again they are distinguished otherwise: because the first was made *to the Pharisees;* the second, *to the crowds;* but the third, *to John's disciples.* — In a third way thus: because the first testimony was given with Christ *absent;* the second, with Christ *coming* to him; the third, with him *walking* before him. The first testimony is noted here: *And this is the testimony;* the second there[^p256-5]: *The next day;* the third there: *The next day again.*

The first part, in which he sets down the testimony with Christ *absent*, has two, because first he answers those asking the truth about *himself;* but second, about *Christ*, there[^p256-6]: *And they that were sent were of the Pharisees.*

The truth, then, which he answered those asking about *himself*, the Evangelist describes in this order: first he intimates *authority* in those asking; second, *steadfastness* in John; third, *importunity* in those asking; but fourth, *humble truth* in John.

45. (Vers. 19.). He intimates, then, the *authority* of those asking: because they were authentic persons and sent with authority, to whom John gives testimony; therefore he says: *And this is the testimony of John.* So it was said above[^p256-7] that « John giveth testimony »; now he specifies: *And this is the testimony of John*, and he points out the testimony with its circumstances. *This*, the pronoun, *this, when the Jews sent from Jerusalem priests and Levites;* behold the authority of the persons, because they had been sent and were honourable; below in the fifth[^p256-8]: « You sent to John, and he gave testimony to the truth »; *they sent*, I say, *to ask him: Who art thou?* namely in the person of all. In like manner John sent his disciples to Christ, that they might ask in his person; Matthew, the eleventh[^p256-9]: « John, sending two of his disciples, said to him: Art thou he that art to come, or look we for another »? This the Jews wished to ask through the Levites.

46. (Vers. 20.). *And he confessed.* Here is touched the second, namely *John's steadfastness*, who was not frightened by their authority so as to turn aside from the true confession. Therefore he says: *And he confessed, and did not deny*, that is, he made a true confession and remained in it; the second to the Corinthians, the first[^p256-10]: « But God is faithful, for our preaching, which was among you, there is not in it *It is* and *It is not;* but there is in it *It is* ». *And he confessed*, namely the truth, *that I am not the Christ.* He answered the intention rather than the question. Of this faithful confession, Matthew, the tenth[^p256-11]: « Every one that shall confess me before men, I will also confess him before my Father ». John confessed in the highest degree, because, though he could have been reputed the Christ, he would not; Gregory: « While he does not will to covet the name of Christ, he was made a member of Christ ».

47. (Vers. 21.). *And they asked him.* Here is touched the third, namely the *importunity of those asking*, because they do not leave off at a single answer, but ask insistently; therefore he says: *And they asked him: What then?* that is, what art thou, if thou art not the Christ? *Art thou Elias? And he said: I am not.* And still they insist: *Art thou a prophet?* namely some lesser one. *And he answered: No.*
<!-- page 257 -->
Therefore they ask about the other Prophets in general, about Elias in particular, because they believed that Elias was to precede Christ; Matthew, the seventeenth[^p257-1]: « Why do the Scribes say that Elias must come first »? But still they insist:

48. (Vers. 22.). *They said therefore unto him: Who art thou?* Still they insist, and they state the reason for their insistence: *That we may give an answer to them that sent us. What sayest thou of thyself?* It seemed foolish to them to return to those sending them without a certain answer. For it belongs to a wise messenger to bring back certain answers; Proverbs, the twenty-second[^p257-2]: « Behold, I have described wisdom to thee, that thou mightest answer the words of truth out of these to them that sent thee ».

49. (Vers. 23.). *He said: I am the voice of one crying.* Here is touched the fourth, namely *humble truth* in the answer; *humble*, because he does not attribute *dignity* to himself, but *office.* Therefore he says: *I am the voice of one crying in the desert; of one crying*, I say, this: *Make straight the way of the Lord.* To the letter John was crying in the desert; Matthew, the third[^p257-3]: « John came preaching in the desert of Judea, and he cried: Make straight the way of the Lord »; because he preached penance, through which the way of the sinner is made straight to the Lord, which namely is *crooked* as long as he sins, but is *made straight* through the observance of the commandments; whence below in the fourteenth[^p257-4]: « If any one love me, he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him ». Thus the way of the Lord is made straight to the heart; and this *office* he confirms by the testimony of Isaiah: *As said the prophet Isaias;* Isaiah, the fortieth: « The voice of one crying in the desert: Prepare ye the way of the Lord ».

### Questions.

50. Quaest. I. But it is asked here first about the questioning of the Jews. For since they knew John's origin and life, to what end do they ask: *Who art thou?*

And it must be answered to this that *who* sometimes asks for the *substance*, as when it is said: who did this or that? Sometimes for the *proper name*, as Priscian says[^p257-5]:

Who, father, is that one who thus accompanies the hero as he goes?

Sometimes, in particular, among other conditions it asks for the *dignity;* for persons of dignity and eminence are called ecclesiastical *persons*[^p257-6]. Therefore it must be said here that, although they knew his *origin, life* and *name*, yet they were in doubt about his *dignity.*

51. Quaest. II. Likewise it is asked about John's answer, because he answers to the second question that he is not Elias.

**On the contrary:** 1. Matthew, the eleventh[^p257-7]: « If you will know, he is Elias ». Therefore either the Lord says what is false, or he does.

2. Likewise he answers that he is not a prophet; *on the contrary:* Luke, the first[^p257-8]: « Thou, child, shalt be called the prophet of the Highest ».

**I respond** that John was answering the intention of those asking rather than the question. It is to be understood, then, that the Jews, asking about Elias, were asking about him bodily, about that same one who was in the old testament; but John answers that he is not Elias properly; but the Lord calls him Elias figuratively, because John was at the first coming what Elias will be at the second. Likewise it must be said that they were asking about a prophet and the office of prophesying strictly, as a prophet foretells *things to come;* but John was a prophet and more than a prophet[^p257-9], because he pointed out one *present.* Therefore, according to their intention, he answers that he is not a prophet.

52. Quaest. III. Likewise it is asked about the third answer: 1. Why namely John is called the *voice*, and why he said that he was the *voice?*

2. Likewise, what is this that he says: *Make straight the way of the Lord;* since Jeremiah, the tenth[^p257-10]: « I know, O Lord, that the way of a man is not his, nor of a man, that he should make straight his steps ». Therefore he exhorts to the impossible.

**I respond:** It must be said that, just as the Son of God is most rightly called *word*, so his forerunner is most rightly called *voice. The voice* has a fourfold property with respect to the word, by reason of which he is rightly so called. The voice *goes before* the word; so John before Christ; Luke, the first[^p257-11]: « He shall go before him in the spirit and power of Elias ». It *manifests* the word; so John manifests Christ; above in the same[^p257-12]: « This man came for testimony, to give testimony of the lumen[^tr-lumen] ». It *passes away* and
<!-- page 258 -->
leaves the word remaining; so John leaves Christ; below in the third[^p258-1]: « I must decrease, but he must increase ». It *is useless* without the word; so is John's preaching without Christ; below in the same[^p258-2]: « I baptize you in water; but there hath stood one in the midst of you, whom you know not ».

2. As to what is objected about the making straight, it must be said that the *making straight* of the way is through grace and charity, which is from God alone; but the *preparation* for the making straight is to be done according to what is in us. And this is what is commanded here; whence Gregory[^p258-3]: « The way of the Lord is made straight to the heart when the life is prepared for the precept ».
