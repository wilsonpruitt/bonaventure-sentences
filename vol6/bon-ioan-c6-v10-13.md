# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. VI, Vers. 10-13
## *Postquam determinata sunt antecedentia ad mirabilem refectionem, hic notantur concomitantia, quae sunt tria: primum, sessio discumbentium; secundum, multiplicatio panum; tertium, collectio fragmentorum*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 320 -->

15. *Dicit*[^p320-12] *ergo Iesus.* Postquam determinata sunt antecedentia ad mirabilem refectionem, hic notantur *concomitantia,* quae sunt tria: primum, *sessio discumbentium;* secundum, *multiplicatio panum;* tertium, *collectio fragmentorum.*

(Vers. 10.). Primo igitur notatur *sessio discumbentium* ad Christi imperium; unde ait: *Dicit ergo Iesus,* scilicet discipulis: *Facite homines discumbere,* hoc est, ut comedant, residere; *discumbere* enim vel *recumbere* proprie est comedentium; unde Lucae decimo quarto[^p320-13]: « Cum vocatus fueris ad nuptias, recumbe » etc. Et locus ad recumbendum erat aptus; unde dicit: *Erat autem foenum multum in loco.* Et in illo loco convenienti fecerunt discipuli eos recumbere; ideo dicit: *Discubuerunt ergo viri numero quasi quinque millia; quasi* dictum est, quia forte aliquis deerat vel supererat; sed quia Scriptura non facit vim in minutiis, alii dicunt, quod quinque millia; Matthaei decimo quarto[^p320-14]: « Manducantium autem fuit numerus quinque millia virorum, exceptis parvulis et mulieribus ».
<!-- page 321 -->

16. (Vers. 11.). *Accepit ergo Iesus* etc. Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *multiplicatio panum,* quae impetrata est in *gratiarum actione* et consummata in *panum distributione.* Ideo dicit: *Accepit ergo Iesus panes,* ad multiplicandum; manus enim potestativa est; unde Numerorum undecimo[^p321-1] dixit Dominus Moysi: « Nunquid invalida est manus Domini? Iam nunc videbis, utrum sermo meus compleatur ». *Et cum gratias egisset,* ad sanctificandum; primae ad Timotheum quarto[^p321-2]: « Nihil abiiciendum, quod cum gratiarum actione sumitur. Sanctificatur enim per verbum Dei et orationem ». *Distribuit discumbentibus,* scilicet ad comedendum, per Apostolos, ut scilicet diversis darent secundum suam voluntatem; Esther primo[^p321-3]: « Rex statuerat, praeponens mensis singulos de principibus suis, ut sumeret unusquisque quod vellet ». *Similiter ex piscibus, quantum volebant,* id est usque ad saturitatem; Exodi decimo sexto: « Vespere comedetis carnes, et mane saturabimini panibus ».

17. (Vers. 12.). *Ut autem impleti sunt.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet *collectio fragmentorum,* ne scilicet perirent; propter quod dicit: *Ut autem impleti sunt,* id est saturati, *dixit discipulis suis: Colligite quae superaverunt fragmenta, ne pereant.* Quia enim panis in usum hominum factus est, tunc fragmenta pereunt, quando in usum hominum non assumuntur; Psalmus[^p321-4]: « Panis cor hominis confirmet ». Et discipuli Domini imperium exsequuntur; unde dicit:

18. (Vers. 13.). *Collegerunt ergo et impleverunt duodecim cophinos;* ecce, mensura et plena mensura. Chrysostomus[^p321-5]: « Ego multiplicationem panum non solum admiror, sed certitudinem eius quod superfluit, et quod pares erant cophini numero Apostolorum, in quo doctrina Apostolorum per totum orbem erat distribuenda ex reliquiis doctrinae Christi ». *Collegerunt,* inquam, *et impleverunt duodecim cophinos fragmentorum ex quinque panibus hordeaceis, quae superfuerunt his qui manducaverant.* Has reliquias fecit colligi, ut appareret abundantia; unde in Psalmo[^p321-6]: « In reliquiis tuis praeparabis vultum eorum ».

19. *Allegorice.* Puer unus habebat quinque panes hordeaceos et duos pisces, qui in duodecim cophinos reliquiarum sunt multiplicati. *Puer* iste est Moyses, lator Legis, qui *puer* dicitur, quia famulus; ad Hebraeos tertio[^p321-7]: *Moyses in omni domo fidelissimus,* tanquam famulus. — *Panis* est eruditio verbi; Deuteronomii octavo[^p321-8]: *Non in solo pane vivit homo, sed ex omni verbo, quod procedit ab ore Dei.* — *Quinque* igitur *panes* sunt verba, de quibus Apostolus primae ad Corinthios decimo quarto[^p321-9]: *Malo loqui quinque verba in sensu meo* etc.; Hieronymus: « Quinque verba sunt quinque libri Moysi, de quibus Apostolus loquitur ». — Isti panes *multiplicantur,* dum multiplex eorum intelligentia a Domino aperitur; Lucae ultimo[^p321-10]: *Incipiens a Moyse, interpretabatur illis in omnibus Scripturis, quae de ipso erant.* — Ex horum multiplicatione remanent *duodecim cophini fragmentorum,* id est doctrina duodecim Apostolorum, qui dispersi per orbem, iuxta illud Psalmi[^p321-11]: *In omnem terram exivit sonus eorum;* et Marci ultimo: *Euntes in mundum universum, praedicate Evangelium omni creaturae.*

### Quaestiones.

20. Quaest. I. Quaeritur de multiplicatione panum. Si enim Dominus suam potentiam[^p321-12] manifestare volebat; et maioris potentiae est panes creare quam multiplicare, quia illud ex nihilo, hoc ex aliquo; et aeque facile erat Domino utrumque: ergo etc.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod utrumque est potentiae infinitae, tam multiplicatio ex tam parva materia in tantam quantitatem, quia hoc erat potentiae infinitae, quam creatio. Magis tamen multiplicavit, quam creavit, ut ora haereticorum concluderet, qui dicunt, haec visibilia ad Deum tenebrarum, non ad Deum lucis pertinere[^p321-13]. Ut ergo ostenderet, quod ipse esset, qui omnia visibilia creaverat, iam creata voluit multiplicare.
<!-- page 322 -->

Utrum autem Deus multiplicaverit panes, novam aliunde apponendo materiam, vel creando; vel illam extendendo; dubium esse videtur, nec hoc determino. Sine praeiudicio tamen dicendum, quod fuit ibi multiplicatio sine creatione et extrinseci appositione; nec fuit extensio materiae tantum, sicut est per virtutem agentis naturalis, sed simul fuit crementum in *materia et forma* per virtutem infinitam; et hoc non habet simile in natura, sicut nec creatio[^p322-1].

21. Quaest. II. Quaeritur hic: cum Dominus, supra secundo[^p322-2], optimum vinum fecerit, unde est hoc, quod panem hordeaceum et vilem turbis distribuit?

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod ratio fuit *litteralis* ex hoc, quod supra *transmutavit* aquam in[^p322-3] vinum et optimam formam dedit; hic *multiplicavit* et auxit; ideo, forma manente, perfectam quantitatem dedit, quia ultra necessitatem duodecim cophinos. — *Spiritualis* ratio est: quia per quinque panes quinque libri Moysi intelliguntur; doctrina autem Moysi aspera est et dura: et ideo panes *hordeaceos* multiplicavit[^p322-4].

22. Quaest. III. Item quaerit Chrysostomus[^p322-5]: propter quid in hoc miraculo faciendo gratias egit, cum in aliis non fecerit? — Videtur in se ostendisse impotentiam.

Responsio est, quod illud non fecit propter miraculum impetrandum, sed propter consuetudinem, quam habebat, in principio comestionis gratias agere et escas benedicere; in quo nos instruxit, quod cum gratiarum actione et oratione debemus sumere cibum[^p322-6].

23. Quaest. IV. Item quaestio est: cum omne superfluum sit evitandum; et Dominus omnia fecerit in certo *pondere et mensura*[^p322-7]: ergo videtur, quod non debuerit ultra sufficientiam panes multiplicare.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod etsi fuerint ultra *necessitatem,* non tamen ultra *utilitatem,* immo utilitas fuit *refectionis* et nostrae *eruditionis: refectionis,* quia postmodum discipuli manducaverunt; *eruditionis,* ut cognoscamus, quod Deus ultra nostram indigentiam potest et paratus est dare; cognoscamus etiam, miraculum fuisse verum; et ex certo numero superantium fragmentorum Apostolorum intelligamus doctrinam et eius fructum multum[^p322-8].

## English
<!-- page 320 -->

15. *Then Jesus saith*[^p320-12]. The antecedents to the wonderful refection having been determined, here are noted the *concomitants,* which are three: the first, *the sitting of those sitting down;* the second, *the multiplication of the loaves;* the third, *the collection of the fragments.*

(Verse 10.). First, then, is noted *the sitting of those sitting down* at Christ's command; whence he says: *Then Jesus saith,* namely to the disciples: *Make the men sit down,* that is, sit back so that they may eat; for *to sit down* or *to recline* properly belongs to those who eat; whence Luke, the fourteenth[^p320-13]: « When thou art invited to a wedding, recline » etc. And the place was apt for reclining; whence he says: *Now there was much grass in the place.* And in that convenient place the disciples made them recline; therefore he says: *The men therefore sat down, in number about five thousand; about* is said because perhaps someone was lacking or was over; but because Scripture does not lay stress on minutiae, others say that there were five thousand; Matthew, the fourteenth[^p320-14]: « And the number of them that did eat was five thousand men, besides children and women ».
<!-- page 321 -->

16. (Verse 11.). *And Jesus took* etc. Here the second is touched upon, namely *the multiplication of the loaves,* which was obtained in *the giving of thanks* and consummated in *the distribution of the loaves.* Therefore he says: *And Jesus took the loaves,* in order to multiply them; for the hand has power; whence in Numbers, the eleventh[^p321-1], the Lord said to Moses: « Is the hand of the Lord unable? Thou shalt presently see whether my word shall be fulfilled ». *And when he had given thanks,* in order to sanctify; in the first to Timothy, the fourth[^p321-2]: « Nothing is to be cast away that is taken with thanksgiving. For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer ». *He distributed to them that were set down,* namely for eating, through the Apostles, so that they might give to different ones according to their will; Esther, the first[^p321-3]: « The king had appointed, setting over the tables one each of his princes, that every man might take what he would ». *In like manner also of the fishes, as much as they would,* that is, to the point of being filled; Exodus, the sixteenth: « In the evening you shall eat flesh, and in the morning you shall have your fill of bread ».

17. (Verse 12.). *And when they were filled.* Here the third is touched upon, namely *the collection of the fragments,* that is, lest they should perish; on account of which he says: *And when they were filled,* that is, satisfied, *he said to his disciples: Gather up the fragments that remain, lest they be lost.* For since bread was made for the use of men, fragments perish then, when they are not taken up into the use of men; the Psalm[^p321-4]: « That bread may strengthen man's heart ». And the disciples carry out the Lord's command; whence he says:

18. (Verse 13.). *They gathered up therefore, and filled twelve baskets;* behold, measure and full measure. Chrysostom[^p321-5]: « I marvel not only at the multiplication of the loaves, but at the certitude of what was over, and that the baskets were equal to the number of the Apostles, in which the teaching[^tr-doctrina] of the Apostles was to be distributed through the whole world out of the remnants of the teaching of Christ ». *They gathered up,* I say, *and filled twelve baskets with the fragments of the five barley loaves, which remained over and above to them that had eaten.* He had these remnants gathered, so that the abundance might appear; whence in the Psalm[^p321-6]: « In thy remnants thou shalt prepare their face ».

19. *Allegorically.* One boy had five barley loaves and two fishes, which were multiplied into twelve baskets of remnants. This *boy* is Moses, the bearer of the Law, who is called *boy* because he was a servant; to the Hebrews, the third[^p321-7]: *Moses in all the house most faithful,* as a servant. — *Bread* is the erudition of the word; Deuteronomy, the eighth[^p321-8]: *Not in bread alone doth man live, but by every word that proceedeth from the mouth of God.* — The *five loaves,* then, are words, of which the Apostle speaks in the first to the Corinthians, the fourteenth[^p321-9]: *I had rather speak five words with my understanding* etc.; Jerome: « The five words are the five books of Moses, of which the Apostle speaks ». — These loaves *are multiplied* when their manifold intelligence is opened by the Lord; Luke, the last[^p321-10]: *Beginning at Moses, he expounded to them in all the scriptures the things that were concerning him.* — From the multiplication of these there remain *twelve baskets of fragments,* that is, the teaching of the twelve Apostles, who were dispersed through the world, according to that of the Psalm[^p321-11]: *Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth;* and Mark, the last: *Going into the whole world, preach the gospel to every creature.*

### Questions.

20. Question I. A question is asked about the multiplication of the loaves. For if the Lord wished to manifest his power[^p321-12]; and it belongs to greater power to create loaves than to multiply them, because the former is out of nothing, the latter out of something; and the one and the other were equally easy to the Lord: therefore etc.

**I respond:** It must be said that each belongs to infinite power, the multiplication out of so small a matter into so great a quantity, because this belonged to infinite power, as much as creation. Yet he multiplied rather than created, in order to shut the mouths of the heretics, who say that these visible things belong to the God of darkness, not to the God of light[^p321-13]. So, then, that he might show that he himself was the one who had created all visible things, he willed to multiply things already created.
<!-- page 322 -->

But whether God multiplied the loaves by adding new matter from elsewhere, or by creating; or by extending that matter, seems to be doubtful, nor do I determine this. Without prejudice, however, it must be said that there was there a multiplication without creation and without the addition of anything extrinsic; nor was there only an extension of the matter, as there is by the virtue of a natural agent, but there was at once an increase in *matter and form* by infinite virtue; and this has no likeness in nature, as neither does creation[^p322-1].

21. Question II. Here it is asked: since the Lord, above in the second[^p322-2], made the best wine, whence is it that he distributed barley bread, and mean, to the multitudes?

**I respond:** It must be said that the *literal* reason was from this, that above he *transmuted* water into[^p322-3] wine and gave it the best form; here he *multiplied* and increased; therefore, the form remaining, he gave a perfect quantity, because beyond necessity, twelve baskets. — The *spiritual* reason is: because through the five loaves the five books of Moses are understood; but the teaching of Moses is harsh and hard: and therefore he multiplied *barley* loaves[^p322-4].

22. Question III. Likewise Chrysostom asks[^p322-5]: why in working this miracle did he give thanks, when he did not do so in others? — He seems to have shown impotence in himself.

The response is that he did not do this for the sake of obtaining the miracle, but on account of the custom which he had of giving thanks and blessing the food at the beginning of a meal; in which he instructed us that we ought to take food with thanksgiving and prayer[^p322-6].

23. Question IV. Likewise there is a question: since everything superfluous is to be avoided; and the Lord made all things in certain *weight and measure*[^p322-7]: therefore it seems that he ought not to have multiplied the loaves beyond sufficiency.

**I respond:** It must be said that even if they were beyond *necessity,* yet they were not beyond *utility,* rather there was a utility *of refection* and of our *erudition: of refection,* because afterwards the disciples ate; *of erudition,* that we may know that God can and is ready to give beyond our need; that we may know also that the miracle was true; and that from the certain number of the fragments left over we may understand the teaching of the Apostles and its much fruit[^p322-8].
