# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. V, Vers. 40-47
## *Posita est testimoniorum certitudo, hic ponitur Iudaeorum non credentium reprehensio*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 316 -->

72. *Et non vultis venire ad me* etc. Posita est testimoniorum certitudo[^p316-13], hic ponitur *Iudaeorum non credentium reprehensio.* Reprehendit autem Dominus
<!-- page 317 -->
Iudaeos a quatuor: primo scilicet a *defectu amoris divini;* secundo, a *caecitate amoris*[^p317-1] *falsi;* tertio, ab *appetitu honoris vani;* quarto, a *defectu fidei.*

(Vers. 40.). Primo igitur reprehendit eos a *defectu amoris divini,* cuius signum est, quod ipsi ad eum non veniunt, quem Deus misit; unde dicit: *Et non vultis venire ad me, ut vitam habeatis.* Ita sufficientia testimonia habeo, tamen ad me venire non vultis, immo odientes fugitis; infra octavo[^p317-2]: « Quare loquelam meam non cognoscitis? Quia non potestis audire sermonem meum ». Nec ipsos arguit, quod non veniunt propter gloriam suam; unde dicit:

73. (Vers. 41.). *Claritatem ab hominibus non accipio,* sed a Patre; infra decimo septimo[^p317-3]: « Pater, clarifica Filium tuum »; ideo vos non arguo propter amorem gloriae propriae, sed propter defectum dilectionis divinae; unde dicit:

74. (Vers. 42.). *Sed cognovi vos, quia dilectionem Dei non habetis in vobis,* immo magis odium; infra decimo quinto[^p317-4]: « Nunc viderunt et oderunt et me et Patrem meum ». Et tales sunt digni reprehensione, quia carent veste nuptiali; Matthaei vigesimo secundo: « Amice, quomodo huc intrasti, non habens vestem nuptialem »?

75. (Vers. 43.). *Ego veni in nomine* etc. Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *caecitas amoris falsi:* quia non receperunt humilem, sed recipient superbum; unde dicit: *Ego veni in nomine Patris mei,* ipsius scilicet gloriam quaerens, non meam; infra octavo[^p317-5]: « Ego gloriam meam non quaero ». *Et non accepistis me; me* scilicet humilem; supra primo: « In propria venit, et sui eum non receperunt ». *Si alius venerit in nomine suo,* ut antichristus, qui quaeret gloriam propriam, quia dicitur secundae ad Thessalonicenses secundo[^p317-6]: « Extollitur supra omne, quod dicitur Deus, aut quod colitur, ita ut in templo Dei sedeat ». *Illum accipietis,* iusto scilicet Dei iudicio excaecati; secundae ad Thessalonicenses secundo: « Mittet eos Deus in operationem erroris, ut credant mendacio ». Et ratio huius est, quia noluerunt credere veritati.

76. (Vers. 44.). *Quomodo vos potestis credere* etc. Hic tertio reprehendit ab *appetitu honoris vani,* ratione cuius erant inhabiles ad credendum in veritatem, quia quaerebant vanitatem; propter quod dicit: *Quomodo vos potestis credere,* scilicet veritati, *qui gloriam ab invicem accipitis,* adhaerendo vanitati? Gloria enim humana vanitas est; Isaiae quadragesimo[^p317-7]: « Omnis caro foenum, et omnis gloria eius tanquam flos foeni »; et ex appetitu vanitatis redditur homo inhabilis ad fidem veritatis; unde in Psalmo: « Filii hominum, usquequo gravi corde? Ut quid diligitis vanitatem et quaeritis mendacium »? Sic quaeritis gloriam falsam, *et gloriam, quae a solo Deo est, non quaeritis,* gloriam scilicet veram. Haec a solo Deo est; Psalmus[^p317-8]: « Gratiam et gloriam dabit Dominus ». Propterea dicitur secundae ad Corinthios decimo: « Qui gloriatur in Domino glorietur; non enim qui se ipsum commendat, ille probatus est, sed quem Deus commendat ».

77. (Vers. 45.). *Nolite putare.* Tangitur hic quartum, scilicet *defectus fidei in Iudaeis,* non tantum ad credendum Christo, verum etiam Moysi; propter quod non tantum accusabantur a Christo, sed etiam a Moyse. Propterea dicit: *Nolite putare, quod*[^p317-9] *ego accusaturus sim vos apud Patrem;* quia non veni accusare, sed condonare. Unde et ipse *propitiatio* est, non *accusator;* primae Ioannis secundo: « Ipse est propitiatio pro peccatis nostris, non pro nostris autem tantum, sed etiam pro totius mundi ». Nec tamen carent accusatore; ideo dicit: *Est alius, qui accusat vos, Moyses, in quo speratis*[^p317-10], tanquam eius discipuli; infra nono: « Nos Moysi discipuli sumus; nos scimus, quia Moysi locutus est Deus ». Ille vos accusat, quia scilicet ei non creditis. Unde dicit:

78. (Vers. 46.). *Si enim crederetis Moysi, crederetis forsitan et mihi; forsitan* nota est libertatis; et vere *mihi; de me enim ille scripsit;* Deuteronomii decimo octavo[^p317-11]: « Prophetam suscitabit vobis Dominus de gente vestra; ipsum audietis, sicut et me ». Et ille vos accusabit, quia in eum non creditis. Unde dicit:

79. (Vers. 47.). *Si autem illius litteris non creditis, quomodo verbis meis credetis?* Quasi dicat: non; Lucae decimo sexto[^p317-12]: « Si Moysen et Prophetas non audiunt, neque si quis ex mortuis resurrexerit, credent ».

### Quaestiones.

80. Quaest. I. Quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Claritatem ab hominibus non accipio.*
<!-- page 318 -->

1. Quia infra ultimo[^p318-1] dicitur de Petro: *Significans, qua morte clarificaturus esset Deum.* Homines igitur clarificant Deum.

2. Item, ad Philippenses primo[^p318-2]: *Magnificabitur Christus in corpore meo sive per mortem, sive per vitam.*

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod differt dicere *clarificari in homine* et *per hominem* et *ab homine.* Clarificari *in homine* est suam claritatem homini revelare; clarificari *per hominem* est per illum aliis propalari[^p318-3]; clarificari *ab homine* est gloriam aliquam ab homine sibi dari et augeri. Primis duobus modis est verum, sed tertio falsum[^p318-4].

81. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Ego non accusabo vos apud Patrem.*

**Contra:** 1. Iustus est Christus et non potest non esse iustus: ergo sicut eius est laudare bonos, ita etiam accusare malos.

2. Item, Ieremiae vigesimo nono[^p318-5]: *Ego sum iudex et testis:* ergo si veri testis est accusare malos etc.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod Christus, quantum in se est, morte sua se obtulit; et quia pro omnibus, ideo in quantum est de se, neminem accusat; unde Glossa[^p318-6] dicit: « Sanguis Christi neminem accusat, sed clamat veniam, sanguis Abel vindictam », doctrina Moysi vindictam. Unde intelligendum, quod ipse non accusat *ex se,* sed *ex causa nostra*[^p318-7]. — Vel dicendum, quod non primo et principaliter accusat; sed magis principaliter Moyses, cui non credunt praedicenti Christum[^p318-8].

82. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de argumento Domini: *Si non creditis litteris Moysi, quomodo verbis meis credetis?* — Videtur esse locus *a minori*[^p318-9] negando.

**Respondeo:** Etsi sit minus *simpliciter,* non tamen *Iudaeis* minus, sed maius.

## English
<!-- page 316 -->

72. *And you will not come to me* etc. The certitude of the testimonies having been set down[^p316-13], here is set down *the reproof of the Jews who do not believe.* Now the Lord reproves
<!-- page 317 -->
the Jews from four things: first, namely, from *a defect of divine love;* second, from *the blindness of a false*[^p317-1] *love;* third, from *the appetite for vain honour;* fourth, from *a defect of faith.*

(Verse 40.). First, then, he reproves them from *a defect of divine love,* the sign of which is that they do not come to him whom God sent; whence he says: *And you will not come to me that you may have life.* So sufficient are the testimonies I have, yet you will not come to me; nay, hating me you flee; below, in the eighth[^p317-2]: « Why do you not know my speech? Because you cannot hear my word ». Nor does he reproach them because they do not come on account of his own glory; whence he says:

73. (Verse 41.). *Clarity from men I receive not,* but from the Father; below, in the seventeenth[^p317-3]: « Father, glorify thy Son »; therefore I do not reproach you on account of love of my own glory, but on account of a defect of divine dilection; whence he says:

74. (Verse 42.). *But I know you, that you have not the dilection of God in you,* nay rather hatred; below, in the fifteenth[^p317-4]: « But now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father ». And such are worthy of reproof, because they lack the wedding garment; Matthew, the twenty-second: « Friend, how camest thou in hither not having on a wedding garment »?

75. (Verse 43.). *I am come in the name* etc. Here is touched the second, namely *the blindness of a false love:* because they did not receive the humble one, but will receive the proud; whence he says: *I am come in the name of my Father,* seeking, namely, his glory, not mine; below, in the eighth[^p317-5]: « I seek not my own glory ». *And you received me not; me,* namely the humble one; above, in the first: « He came unto his own, and his own received him not ». *If another shall come in his own name,* as Antichrist, who will seek his own glory, for it is said in the second to the Thessalonians, the second[^p317-6]: « He is lifted up above all that is called God or that is worshipped, so that he sitteth in the temple of God ». *Him you will receive,* blinded, namely, by the just judgement of God; the second to the Thessalonians, the second: « God shall send them the operation of error, to believe lying ». And the reason of this is that they would not believe the truth.

76. (Verse 44.). *How can you believe* etc. Here, third, he reproves from *the appetite for vain honour,* by reason of which they were unfit for believing in the truth, because they sought vanity; on account of which he says: *How can you believe,* namely the truth, *who receive glory one from another,* cleaving to vanity? For human glory is vanity; Isaias, the fortieth[^p317-7]: « All flesh is grass, and all the glory thereof as the flower of the grass »; and from the appetite for vanity a man is rendered unfit for the faith of truth; whence in the Psalm: « O ye sons of men, how long will you be dull of heart? Why do you love vanity, and seek after lying »? So you seek a false glory, *and the glory which is from God alone you do not seek,* the true glory, namely. This is from God alone; the Psalm[^p317-8]: « The Lord will give grace and glory ». Therefore it is said in the second to the Corinthians, the tenth: « He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord; for not he who commendeth himself is approved, but he whom God commendeth ».

77. (Verse 45.). *Think not.* Here is touched the fourth, namely *the defect of faith in the Jews,* not only for believing Christ, but also Moses; on account of which they were accused not only by Christ, but also by Moses. Therefore he says: *Think not that*[^p317-9] *I will accuse you to the Father;* for I came not to accuse, but to pardon. Whence he himself too is *propitiation,* not *accuser;* the first of John, the second: « He is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours only, but also for those of the whole world ». Yet they do not lack an accuser; therefore he says: *There is one that accuseth you, Moses, in whom you trust*[^p317-10], as being his disciples; below, in the ninth: « We are the disciples of Moses; we know that God spoke to Moses ». He accuses you, namely because you do not believe him. Whence he says:

78. (Verse 46.). *For if you did believe Moses, you would perhaps believe me also; perhaps* is a note of liberty; and truly *me; for he wrote of me;* Deuteronomy, the eighteenth[^p317-11]: « The Lord will raise up to you a prophet of your nation; him you shall hear, as also me ». And he will accuse you, because you do not believe in him. Whence he says:

79. (Verse 47.). *But if you do not believe his writings, how will you believe my words?* As if he were to say: not at all; Luke, the sixteenth[^p317-12]: « If they hear not Moses and the Prophets, neither will they believe, if one rise again from the dead ».

### Questions.

80. Question I. It is asked about what he says: *Clarity from men I receive not.*
<!-- page 318 -->

1. Because below, in the last[^p318-1], it is said of Peter: *Signifying by what death he should glorify God.* Therefore men glorify God.

2. Likewise, to the Philippians, the first[^p318-2]: *Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether it be by death or by life.*

**I respond:** It must be said that there is a difference between saying *to be glorified in a man*, and *through a man*, and *by a man.* To be glorified *in a man* is to reveal one's clarity to a man; to be glorified *through a man* is to be made known through him to others[^p318-3]; to be glorified *by a man* is for some glory to be given and increased to oneself by a man. In the first two modes it is true, but in the third false[^p318-4].

81. Question II. Likewise it is asked about what he says: *I will not accuse you to the Father.*

**On the contrary:** 1. Christ is just and cannot not be just: therefore as it is his to praise the good, so too to accuse the wicked.

2. Likewise, Jeremias, the twenty-ninth[^p318-5]: *I am the judge and the witness:* therefore if it belongs to a true witness to accuse the wicked, etc.

**I respond:** It must be said that Christ, as far as is in him, offered himself by his death; and because for all, therefore, as far as is of himself, he accuses no one; whence the Gloss[^p318-6] says: « The blood of Christ accuses no one, but cries for pardon, the blood of Abel for vengeance », the teaching[^tr-doctrina] of Moses for vengeance. Whence it is to be understood that he does not accuse *of himself,* but *from our cause*[^p318-7]. — Or it must be said that he does not accuse first and principally; but rather Moses principally, whom they do not believe when he foretells Christ[^p318-8].

82. Question III. Likewise it is asked about the Lord's argument: *If you do not believe the writings of Moses, how will you believe my words?* — It seems to be the topic *from the lesser*[^p318-9], by denying.

**I respond:** Although it is lesser *simply,* yet *to the Jews* it is not lesser, but greater.
