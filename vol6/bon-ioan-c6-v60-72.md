# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. VI, Vers. 60-72
## *Supra posita est instructionis confirmatio propter Iudaeorum litigium; hic ponitur explanatio propter discipulorum scandalum; et proceditur hoc ordine*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 335 -->

95. *Haec dixit in Synagoga* etc. Supra posita est instructionis confirmatio propter Iudaeorum
<!-- page 336 -->
litigium; hic ponitur *explanatio* propter discipulorum scandalum; et proceditur hoc ordine. Primo enim innuitur *discipulorum murmuratio;* secundo, ob murmurationem *doctrinae explanatio;* tertio redditur *murmurandi ratio;* quarto, *non credentium retrocessio;* quinto, *credentium confirmatio;* sexto, *confirmatorum discretio.*

(Vers. 60.). *Discipulorum murmuratio* erat ex hoc, quod dixerat, carnem suam dari ad manducandum, et quod sine hoc non erat salus. Propter quod dicit: *Haec dixit in Synagoga docens, in Capharnaum,* id est in communi, et ideo multi saevierunt; infra decimo octavo[^p336-1]: « Ego semper docui in Synagoga ».

96. (Vers. 61.). *Multi ergo ex discipulis eius, audientes,* quod nunc scilicet dictum est de manducatione carnis, *dixerunt: Durus est hic sermo,* id est crudelis et inhumanus, humanam carnem manducare; *quis*[^p336-2] *potest eum audire?* Non dicunt *credere,* sed *audire,* propter horribilitatem; ideo non poterant audire, quia aures eorum aggravatae erant, iuxta illud Isaiae sexto: « Excaeca cor populi huius et aures eius aggrava » etc.

97. (Vers. 62.). *Sciens autem Iesus* etc. Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet propter murmurationem *doctrinae explanatio;* et licet murmuratio esset in occulto, tamen Dominum non latebat; unde dicit: *Sciens autem Iesus apud semetipsum, quia murmurarent de hoc discipuli eius;* non relatione hoc cognovit, sed *apud se;* infra decimo sexto[^p336-3]: « Dixerunt discipuli: Nunc scimus, quia scis omnia, et non est opus, ut quis te interroget ». Sciens ergo murmurationem et volens eam abscindere, explanat quod dixerat, hoc non esse intelligendum *carnaliter,* sed *spiritualiter.* Ostendit ergo, quod non est *carnaliter* intelligendum, tum quia *falsum,* tum quia *inutile.* Propterea *dixit eis: Hoc vos scandalizat*[^p336-4], quod scilicet dixi de manducatione meae carnis, quia carnaliter ipsam dividendam creditis; sicut Pharisaei, Matthaei decimo quinto: « Scis, quia Pharisaei, audito hoc verbo, scandalizati sunt »? Et hoc falsum; unde subdit:

98. (Vers. 63.). *Si ergo videritis Filium hominis ascendentem ubi erat prius*[^p336-5] — defectiva est constructio — tunc scietis, quia caro non est per frusta dividenda, quia totus Christus integer erit in caelo; Augustinus[^p336-6]: « Totus integer est in caelo; manet integer sub Sacramento, manet integer in corde tuo ». — Non solum intellectus eorum erat *falsus,* sed *inutilis;* unde:

99. (Vers. 64.). *Spiritus est qui vivificat, caro* autem, id est carnalis intelligentia, *non prodest quidquam;* secundae ad Corinthios tertio[^p336-7]: « Littera occidit, spiritus autem vivificat »; et ideo non sunt verba carnaliter intelligenda, quia non prosunt, sed spiritualiter, ut sic prosint. Unde *verba, quae ego locutus sum vobis, spiritus et vita sunt,* quia spiritualiter intellecta vivificant; unde ad Hebraeos quarto[^p336-8]: « Vivus est sermo Dei et efficax »; *vivus* est, quia vivificat; unde Deuteronomii octavo: « Non in solo pane vivit homo, sed in omni verbo, quod procedit ex ore Dei ». Sed non vivificant nisi spiritualiter intelligentem; unde Augustinus[^p336-9]: « Intellexisti spiritualiter? *Spiritus et vita sunt.* Intellexisti carnaliter? *Spiritus et vita sunt,* sed non *tibi* sunt ».

100. (Vers. 65.). *Sed sunt quidam.* Hic tangitur tertium, scilicet *murmurationis ratio,* quare scilicet discipuli murmurabant: quia fidem non habebant; sed hoc non omnes, sed quidam, qui a Deo electi non erant. Propterea dicit: *Sed sunt quidam ex vobis;* ita sunt verba spiritualiter intelligenda, sed quidam ex vobis non intelligunt; unde *quidam non credunt.* Et hoc non dicit ex coniectura, sed ex certa scientia. Propterea dicit Evangelista: *Sciebat enim Iesus ab initio, qui essent credentes*[^p336-10]*;* supra secundo: « Ipse enim sciebat, quid esset in homine »; Ecclesiastici vigesimo tertio: « Oculi Domini multo plus lucidiores sunt super solem, circumspicientes omnes vias hominum et profundum abyssi, et hominum corda intuentes in absconditas partes ». *Et quis traditurus esset eum;* unde non fuit traditus ut ignorans, quemadmodum alii homines. Ideo hoc dicit Evangelista, ne videatur Christus deceptus, admittendo discipulos instabiles et discipulos insidiantes, ut proditorem. Ratio autem, quare non credebant, erat, quia, etsi essent vocati exterius et secundum praesentem iustitiam, non tamen secundum electionem aeternam; et hanc supra[^p336-11] dixit et repetit.

<!-- page 337 -->
101. (Vers. 66.). *Et dicebat: Propterea dixi vobis: Nemo*[^p337-1] *potest venire ad me, nisi datum fuerit ei a Patre meo;* supra eodem: « Nemo venit ad me, nisi Pater traxerit eum ». *Venire* enim ad Filium « datum est optimum et donum perfectum », ideo « desursum », Iacobi primo.

102. (Vers. 67.). *Et*[^p337-2] *ex hoc multi discipulorum.* Hic tangitur quartum, scilicet *non credentium retrocessio;* unde dicit: *Ex hoc,* id est post hoc, vel *ex hoc,* id est occasione huius increpationis et instructionis, *multi discipulorum eius abierunt retrorsum,* non per *imitationem,* sicut dictum est Petro Matthaei decimo sexto[^p337-3]: « Vade retro me, satana », sed per *indignationem.* Unde non tantum corde recesserunt, sed etiam corpore; unde *et iam non cum illo ambulabant.* Ideo multi recesserunt, et pauci remanserunt, quia, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo[^p337-4], « multi sunt vocati, pauci vero electi »; et quia pauci electi ad regnum Dei, ideo pauci sunt, qui non retrocedant, secundum illud Lucae nono: « Nemo mittens manum ad aratrum et aspiciens retro aptus est regno Dei ».

103. (Vers. 68.). *Dixit ergo Iesus ad duodecim.* Tangitur hic quintum, scilicet *credentium et electorum confirmatio;* et haec quidem facta est in fideli confessione Petri; ideo ponitur *inquisitio Domini* et *responsio Petri.* Inquirit Dominus a duodecim, quos specialiter elegerat: *Nunquid et vos vultis abire?* me derelinquendo, secundum quod praecedentes faciunt; Ieremiae secundo[^p337-5]: « Me dereliquerunt, fontem aquae vivae ». Sed quaerit, non quia dubitet, sed ut fidem Petri in exemplum firmitatis adducat.

104. (Vers. 69.). *Respondit*[^p337-6] *ei Simon Petrus: Domine, ad quem ibimus?* Quasi dicat: non volumus abire, quia tibi similem non possumus invenire; Psalmus: « Non est similis tui in diis, Domine ». Nec propter haec verba debemus abire; unde dicit: *Verba vitae aeternae habes,* aliis non credentibus erant dura et mortifera, sed Petro et aliis credentibus erant vita. Et ratio subditur:

105. (Vers. 70.). *Et nos credimus*[^p337-7] *et cognovimus, quia tu es Christus, Filius Dei.* Similem confessionem fecit Matthaei decimo sexto: « Tu es Christus, Filius Dei vivi », « qui in hunc mundum venisti ». *Credimus,* dicit, et post *cognovimus,* quia fides est via in cognitionem. « Nisi enim credideritis, non intelligetis », Isaiae septimo.

106. (Vers. 71.). *Respondit eis Iesus.* Notatur hic sextum, scilicet *remanentium discretio.* Ostendit Dominus, quod etsi nullus de duodecim recederet, non tamen omnes fideles permanerent; propter quod dicit: *Respondit*[^p337-8] *Iesus,* confessioni Petri, quam pro omnibus dixerat: *Nonne ego duodecim vos elegi, et unus ex vobis diabolus est?* Iudas dicitur *diabolus,* quia ipsius suggestionibus consensit; Psalmus de Iuda: « Constitue super eum peccatorem, et diabolus stet a dextris eius » etc. Unum notat de malitia, ut ceteri timeant; et exponit Evangelista, de quo intelligebat, scilicet de Iuda; unde dicit:

107. (Vers. 72.). *Dicebat autem de Iuda Simonis Iscariotis*[^p337-9]*; hic enim erat traditurus eum, cum esset unus ex duodecim.* Hoc dictum est, ut timerent et ut non mirarentur de aliis, qui recesserant, quia non ita specialiter electi erant. De hac electione duodecim dicitur Matthaei decimo[^p337-10]: « Et convocatis duodecim, dedit eis potestatem » etc.; Lucae sexto: « Elegit duodecim ex ipsis » etc. Electio ista praefigurata est Iosue quarto: « Elige tibi duodecim viros singulos » etc.

### Quaestiones.

108. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic de hoc quod dicit: *Si videritis Filium hominis ascendentem, ubi erat prius.* — Hoc non potest dici secundum *humanam* naturam, quia secundum illam non erat in caelo prius; similiter nec secundum *divinam,* quia secundum illam adhuc erat in caelo, cum loquebatur; unde supra tertio[^p337-11]: *Nemo ascendit in caelum, nisi qui descendit de caelo, Filius hominis, qui est in caelo.*

**Respondeo,** quod illud dictum est, comparando Filium Dei secundum *divinam* naturam post carnis assumtionem, in qua videbatur esse humiliata, ad se ipsam ante assumtionem; quia tunc non poterat cogitari tanquam infirmus[^p337-12]. Idem est ergo, ac si diceret: si videritis Filium hominis ascendere, id est tantum clarificari, ut credatur Patri esse aequalis.

109. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Caro non prodest quidquam.* — Si enim nihil prodest: ergo non debet dari ad manducandum nec
<!-- page 338 -->
est vere cibus. Propter hoc dicunt haeretici, quod verba ista intelligenda sunt spiritualiter, ita quod non carnaliter; nec intelligendum, quod ad litteram sumamus carnem Christi sub Sacramento. Et dicunt, discipulos suos fuisse reprehensos, qui hoc cogitabant; unde dicunt, quod non est in Sacramento altaris nisi sicut in signo[^p338-1].

Sed pessime intelligunt, quia, dum renuunt carnem, fugiunt veritatem. Etsi enim verba Christi sint spiritualiter intelligenda, tamen nihilominus debent esse vera. Propter hoc dicendum, quod caro, quam traxit de Virgine, est verus cibus. — Quod autem dicitur, quod *non prodest quidquam,* intelligitur dupliciter: vel quia nihil prodest *sine spiritu;* sicut enim carbo non comburit nisi ignitus, tamen ignitus urit; sic caro Christi, si separetur a Verbo, non accendit, sed Verbo coniuncta est tanquam ignitus carbo[^p338-2]. — Aliter respondetur, quod *caro nihil prodest* secundum *carnalem intelligentiam,* sic intelligendo, quod Christus carnem suam daret dividendo sicut carnes in macello, et materialiter masticando sub forma carnis. Talis, inquam, intelligentia *non prodest;* sed quod vera caro sine sui sectione in Sacramento comeditur[^p338-3].

110. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de responsione Petri:

1. Quare Petrus solus respondit et magis quam alii?

2. Item, quare non est laudata eius confessio, sicut Matthaei decimo sexto[^p338-4]?

3. Item videtur, quod mentitus fuerit, saltem pro Iuda, quia ipse pro omnibus loquebatur.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod Petrus omnibus fuit ferventior, sicut patet in verbis suis, et audacter Domino loquebatur; alii non ita audebant; et ideo erat os Apostolorum et in quaerendo et in respondendo, et ideo pro omnibus respondit.

2. Ad aliud dicit Chrysostomus[^p338-5], quod respondit hic pro omnibus; et quia fides Iudae non erat laudabilis, ideo laudatus non est.

3. Nec tamen est mentitus, licet falsum dixerit, quia pio errore decipiebatur, bene iudicando de suis coapostolis.

111. Quaest. IV. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Unus ex vobis diabolus est.* — Quaerit Chrysostomus[^p338-6]: quare non sunt contristati, sicut infra decimo tertio, cum dixit: *Unus ex vobis me tradet?*

**Respondeo,** quod infra non tam sunt contristati pro culpa, quam pro Domini iniuria, quae erat sibi a suo discipulo inferenda; hic autem solum expressit malitiam[^p338-7].

112. Quaest. V. Item, quare non abiecit eum, ex quo diabolus erat, et sciebat, quod semper futurus esset malus?

Respondet Augustinus[^p338-8], quod « malis operibus bene utitur Deus, sicut homo male utitur bonis ». Ideo hominem reprobum elegit Iudam, ut per eum dispensatio divinae misericordiae in salutem mundi impleretur; et ostenderetur mirabilis patientia Dei, quod tam iniquum in tanto officio sustinebat; et ut nemo glorietur de praesenti vocatione.

## English
<!-- page 335 -->

95. *These things he said in the synagogue* etc. Above was set down the confirmation of the instruction on account of the
<!-- page 336 -->
striving of the Jews; here is set down the *explanation* on account of the scandal of the disciples; and it proceeds in this order. For first is indicated *the murmuring of the disciples;* second, on account of the murmuring, *the explanation of the teaching*[^tr-doctrina]*;* third is given *the reason for murmuring;* fourth, *the going back of those not believing;* fifth, *the confirmation of those believing;* sixth, *the discerning of those confirmed.*

(Verse 60.). *The murmuring of the disciples* arose from this, that he had said that his flesh was given to be eaten, and that without this there was no salvation. On account of which he says: *These things he said, teaching in the synagogue, in Capharnaum,* that is, in common, and therefore many raged; below, in the eighteenth[^p336-1]: « I have always taught in the synagogue ».

96. (Verse 61.). *Many therefore of his disciples, hearing* what, namely, had now been said about the eating of the flesh, *said: This saying is hard,* that is, cruel and inhuman, to eat human flesh; *who*[^p336-2] *can hear it?* They do not say *believe,* but *hear,* on account of its horribleness; therefore they could not hear, because their ears had been made heavy, according to that of Isaias, the sixth: « Blind the heart of this people, and make their ears heavy » etc.

97. (Verse 62.). *But Jesus knowing* etc. Here the second is touched upon, namely, on account of the murmuring, *the explanation of the teaching;* and although the murmuring was in secret, yet it did not escape the Lord; whence he says: *But Jesus knowing in himself, that his disciples murmured at this;* he did not know this by report, but *in himself;* below, in the sixteenth[^p336-3]: « The disciples said: Now we know that thou knowest all things, and there is no need that any should ask thee ». Knowing, then, the murmuring and willing to cut it off, he explains what he had said, that this is not to be understood *carnally,* but *spiritually.* He shows, then, that it is not to be understood *carnally,* both because it is *false* and because it is *useless.* Therefore *he said to them: This scandalizes you*[^p336-4], namely what I said about the eating of my flesh, because you believe it is to be divided carnally; as the Pharisees, Matthew, the fifteenth: « Dost thou know that the Pharisees, when they heard this word, were scandalized »? And this is false; whence he adds:

98. (Verse 63.). *If then you shall see the Son of man ascending where he was before*[^p336-5] — the construction is defective — then you shall know that the flesh is not to be divided into pieces, because the whole Christ will be entire in heaven; Augustine[^p336-6]: « He is whole and entire in heaven; he remains entire under the Sacrament, he remains entire in thy heart ». — Not only was their understanding *false,* but *useless;* whence:

99. (Verse 64.). *It is the spirit that quickeneth, the flesh* however, that is, carnal intelligence, *profiteth nothing;* the second to the Corinthians, the third[^p336-7]: « The letter killeth, but the spirit quickeneth »; and therefore the words are not to be understood carnally, because so they do not profit, but spiritually, that so they may profit. Whence *the words that I have spoken to you, are spirit and life,* because, understood spiritually, they quicken; whence to the Hebrews, the fourth[^p336-8]: « The word of God is living and effectual »; it is *living,* because it quickens; whence Deuteronomy, the eighth: « Not in bread alone doth man live, but in every word that proceedeth from the mouth of God ». But they do not quicken except the one who understands spiritually; whence Augustine[^p336-9]: « Hast thou understood spiritually? *They are spirit and life.* Hast thou understood carnally? *They are spirit and life,* but they are not so *to thee* ».

100. (Verse 65.). *But there are some.* Here the third is touched upon, namely *the reason for the murmuring,* that is, why the disciples murmured: because they did not have faith; but not all of them did this, only some, who were not chosen by God. Therefore he says: *But there are some of you;* thus the words are to be understood spiritually, but some of you do not understand; whence *some believe not.* And he does not say this from conjecture, but from certain knowledge. Therefore the Evangelist says: *For Jesus knew from the beginning, who they were that did believe*[^p336-10]*;* above, in the second: « For he knew what was in man »; Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-third: « The eyes of the Lord are far more bright than the sun, beholding round about all the ways of men and the bottom of the deep, and looking into the hearts of men into the hidden parts ». *And who he was that would betray him;* whence he was not betrayed as one unknowing, as other men are. Therefore the Evangelist says this, lest Christ should seem deceived in admitting unstable disciples and disciples lying in wait, such as the traitor. Now the reason why they did not believe was that, although they were called outwardly and according to present justice, yet not according to the eternal election; and this he said above[^p336-11] and repeats.

<!-- page 337 -->
101. (Verse 66.). *And he said: Therefore did I say to you: No man*[^p337-1] *can come to me, unless it be given him by my Father;* above, in the same: « No man cometh to me, unless the Father draw him ». For *to come* to the Son is « the best gift and the perfect gift », and therefore « from above », James, the first.

102. (Verse 67.). *And*[^p337-2] *from this many of the disciples.* Here the fourth is touched upon, namely *the going back of those not believing;* whence he says: *From this,* that is, after this, or *from this,* that is, on the occasion of this rebuke and instruction, *many of his disciples went backward,* not by *imitation,* as was said to Peter, Matthew, the sixteenth[^p337-3]: « Go behind me, Satan », but by *indignation.* Whence they withdrew not only in heart, but also in body; whence *and now they walked no more with him.* Therefore many withdrew, and few remained, because, according to that of Matthew, the twentieth[^p337-4], « many are called, but few chosen »; and because few are chosen to the kingdom of God, therefore few there are who do not go back, according to that of Luke, the ninth: « No man putting his hand to the plough and looking back is fit for the kingdom of God ».

103. (Verse 68.). *Then Jesus said to the twelve.* Here the fifth is touched upon, namely *the confirmation of those believing and chosen;* and this indeed was done in the faithful confession of Peter; therefore *the inquiry of the Lord* and *the response of Peter* are set down. The Lord inquires of the twelve, whom he had specially chosen: *Will you also go away?* by forsaking me, as those before do; Jeremias, the second[^p337-5]: « They have forsaken me, the fountain of living water ». But he asks, not because he doubts, but to bring forward Peter's faith as an example of firmness.

104. (Verse 69.). *And Simon Peter answered*[^p337-6] *him: Lord, to whom shall we go?* As if he said: we do not will to go away, because we can find none like to thee; the Psalm: « There is none among the gods like unto thee, O Lord ». Nor ought we to go away on account of these words; whence he says: *Thou hast the words of eternal life;* to the others, not believing, they were hard and deadly, but to Peter and the others believing they were life. And the reason is added:

105. (Verse 70.). *And we believe*[^p337-7] *and have known, that thou art the Christ, the Son of God.* He made a like confession in Matthew, the sixteenth: « Thou art Christ, the Son of the living God », « who art come into this world ». *We believe,* he says, and afterwards *we have known,* because faith is the way into knowledge. « For unless you believe, you shall not understand », Isaias, the seventh.

106. (Verse 71.). *Jesus answered them.* Here the sixth is noted, namely *the discerning of those remaining.* The Lord shows that, although none of the twelve should withdraw, yet not all would remain faithful; on account of which he says: *Jesus answered*[^p337-8] Peter's confession, which he had spoken for all: *Have not I chosen you twelve, and one of you is a devil?* Judas is called *a devil,* because he consented to his suggestions; the Psalm of Judas: « Set thou the sinner over him, and may the devil stand at his right hand » etc. He notes one for his malice, that the rest may fear; and the Evangelist explains of whom he meant, namely of Judas; whence he says:

107. (Verse 72.). *Now he spoke of Judas of Simon Iscariot*[^p337-9]*; for this one was to betray him, whereas he was one of the twelve.* This was said that they might fear and that they might not wonder at the others, who had withdrawn, because they were not so specially chosen. Of this election of the twelve it is said in Matthew, the tenth[^p337-10]: « And having called together the twelve, he gave them power » etc.; Luke, the sixth: « He chose twelve of them » etc. This election was prefigured in Josue, the fourth: « Choose thee twelve men, one by one » etc.

### Questions.

108. Question I. But it is asked here about what he says: *If you shall see the Son of man ascending, where he was before.* — This cannot be said according to the *human* nature, because according to it he was not in heaven before; likewise neither according to the *divine,* because according to it he was still in heaven while he was speaking; whence above, in the third[^p337-11]: *No man hath ascended into heaven, but he that descended from heaven, the Son of man, who is in heaven.*

**I respond** that this was said by comparing the Son of God according to the *divine* nature after the assumption of the flesh, in which it seemed to be humbled, to itself before the assumption; for then he could not be thought of as weak[^p337-12]. It is the same, then, as if he said: if you shall see the Son of man ascend, that is, be so far glorified that he is believed to be equal to the Father.

109. Question II. Likewise it is asked about what he says: *The flesh profiteth nothing.* — For if it profits nothing: then it ought not to be given to be eaten, nor
<!-- page 338 -->
is it truly food. On account of this heretics say that these words are to be understood spiritually, so that not carnally; nor is it to be understood that we take the flesh of Christ to the letter under the Sacrament. And they say that his disciples were reproved who thought this; whence they say that it is not in the Sacrament of the altar except as in a sign[^p338-1].

But they understand most wrongly, because, while they refuse the flesh, they flee the truth. For although the words of Christ are to be understood spiritually, yet nonetheless they must be true. On account of this it must be said that the flesh which he drew from the Virgin is true food. — But that it is said that it *profiteth nothing* is understood in two ways: either because it profits nothing *without the spirit;* for as a coal does not burn unless it be kindled, yet kindled it burns; so the flesh of Christ, if it were separated from the Word, does not inflame, but joined to the Word it is as a kindled coal[^p338-2]. — Otherwise it is answered that *the flesh profiteth nothing* according to *carnal intelligence,* understanding it thus, that Christ would give his flesh by dividing it as meats in the shambles, and that it would be chewed materially under the form of flesh. Such intelligence, I say, *profiteth nothing;* but that the true flesh is fed on in the Sacrament without being cut into parts[^p338-3].

110. Question III. Likewise it is asked about the response of Peter:

1. Why did Peter alone respond, and more than the others?

2. Likewise, why was his confession not praised, as in Matthew, the sixteenth[^p338-4]?

3. Likewise it seems that he lied, at least as regards Judas, because he was speaking for all.

**I respond:** It must be said that Peter was more fervent than all, as is clear in his words, and he spoke boldly to the Lord; the others did not so dare; and therefore he was the mouth of the Apostles both in asking and in responding, and therefore he responded for all.

2. To the other, Chrysostom says[^p338-5] that he responded here for all; and because the faith of Judas was not praiseworthy, therefore he was not praised.

3. Yet he did not lie, although he said what was false, because he was deceived by a pious error, judging well of his fellow apostles.

111. Question IV. Likewise it is asked about what he says: *One of you is a devil.* — Chrysostom asks[^p338-6]: why were they not made sorrowful, as below, in the thirteenth, when he said: *One of you shall betray me?*

**I respond** that below they were made sorrowful not so much for the fault as for the injury to the Lord, which was to be inflicted on him by his own disciple; but here he expressed only the malice[^p338-7].

112. Question V. Likewise, why did he not cast him off, since he was a devil, and he knew that he would always be wicked?

Augustine responds[^p338-8] that « God uses evil works well, as man uses good things evilly ». Therefore he chose Judas, a reprobate man, that through him the dispensation of the divine mercy for the salvation of the world might be fulfilled; and that the wonderful patience of God might be shown, in that he bore one so wicked in so great an office; and that no one might glory in his present calling.
