# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XVIII, Vers. 38-40
## *Hic ostenditur in hoc, quod volentem dimittere faciunt condemnare*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 489 -->

70. *Et cum hoc dixisset* etc. Supra ostensa est malitia Iudaeorum in hoc, quod Pilatum compulerunt Christum examinare; hic ostenditur in hoc, quod *volentem dimittere faciunt condemnare*[^p489-11]. Et dividitur haec pars in duas, in quarum prima Iudaei *resistunt iudicio veritatis,* ne liberetur; in secunda impetrant et *extorquent falsitatis iudicium,* ut condemnetur, ibi[^p489-12]: *Pilatus ergo, cum audisset hos sermones.*

Et quia triplex est modus iudicandi: secundum *benignitatem, severitatem* et *aequitatem;* hoc triplici iudicio nititur Pilatus liberare Christum, et Iudaei e contrario resistunt. Ideo sunt hic tres partes. In prima resistunt *iudicio benignitatis;* in secunda, *iudicio severitatis,* ibi[^p489-13]: *Tunc apprehendit eum Pilatus;* in tertia, *iudicio aequitatis,* ibi: *Dicit eis Pilatus* etc.

Quantum ergo ad primum innuuntur tria: *allegatio innocentiae* in Christo, *rememoratio clementiae* in iudice, *induratio nequitiae* in Iudaeis.

71. (Vers. 38.). *Allegatur* ergo *innocentia in Christo,* propter quam debet dimitti; unde dicit Evangelista: *Et cum hoc dixisset,* scilicet quaestionem praedictam, affectans Christum liberare; *iterum exivit ad Iudaeos,* qui scilicet erant extra praetorium, ut allegaret Christi innocentiam; unde: *Et dixit*[^p489-14] *eis: Ego nullam invenio in eo causam,* scilicet mortis. Et verum dixit; unde in Psalmo: « Neque iniquitas mea neque peccatum meum, Domine ».

72. (Vers. 39.). Et quia non sufficiebat allegare innocentiam, ideo secundo *rememoratur clementia:*
<!-- page 490 -->
*Est autem consuetudo vobis, ut unum dimittam vobis in pascha.* Victor[^p490-1]: « Quia in pascha a servitute liberati sunt, in pascha haec consuetudo dimittendi fuit illis ». *Vultis*[^p490-2]*, dimittam vobis regem Iudaeorum?* Augustinus: « Avelli ab eius corde non potuit, Christum esse regem Iudaeorum, tanquam hoc ibi, sicut in titulo, ipsa veritas fixerit, de qua, quid esset, interrogavit ».

73. (Vers. 40.). Deinde subditur *induratio malitiae in Iudaeis,* qui non fuerunt flexi propter Christi innocentiam nec ad iudicis clementiam, sed potius rei perstiterunt: unde dicit: *Clamaverunt rursus et dixerunt*[^p490-3]*: Non hunc, sed Barabbam,* scilicet non hunc innocentem, sed quem scimus esse nocentem; unde Evangelista addit: *Erat autem Barabbas latro;* in quo mira malitia exprimitur Iudaeorum, quod latronem et eius salutem Christo Domino praetulerunt. Unde Chrysostomus[^p490-4]: « O de polluta mente! Noxium petunt dimitti, innoxium vero cruciari iubent »; Actuum tertio: « Vos iustum et sanctum negastis et petistis, virum homicidam donari vobis; auctorem vero vitae interfecistis ».

## English
<!-- page 489 -->

70. *And when he had said this* etc. Above was shown the malice of the Jews in this, that they compelled Pilate to examine Christ; here it is shown in this, that *they make the one willing to release him condemn him*[^p489-11]. And this part is divided in two, in the first of which the Jews *resist the judgement of truth,* lest he be freed; in the second they obtain and *extort the judgement of falsity,* that he may be condemned, there[^p489-12]: *Pilate therefore, when he had heard these words.*

And because the mode of judging is threefold: according to *benignity, severity* and *equity;* by this threefold judgement Pilate strives to free Christ, and the Jews on the contrary resist. Therefore there are three parts here. In the first they resist *the judgement of benignity;* in the second, *the judgement of severity,* there[^p489-13]: *Then Pilate took him;* in the third, *the judgement of equity,* there: *Pilate saith to them* etc.

As to the first, then, three things are hinted: *the allegation of innocence* in Christ, *the recalling of clemency* in the judge, *the hardening of wickedness* in the Jews.

71. (Vers. 38.). *Innocence* therefore *in Christ is alleged,* on account of which he ought to be released; hence the Evangelist says: *And when he had said this,* namely the aforesaid question, eager to free Christ, *he went out again to the Jews,* who namely were outside the praetorium, that he might allege Christ's innocence; hence: *And he said*[^p489-14] *to them: I find no cause in him,* namely of death. And he spoke truly; hence in the Psalm: « Neither my iniquity nor my sin, O Lord ».

72. (Vers. 39.). And because it did not suffice to allege innocence, therefore, second, *clemency is recalled:*
<!-- page 490 -->
*But you have a custom that I should release one unto you at the pasch.* Victor[^p490-1]: « Because at the pasch they were freed from servitude, at the pasch this custom of releasing was theirs ». *Will you*[^p490-2]*, that I release unto you the king of the Jews?* Augustine: « It could not be torn from his heart that Christ is the king of the Jews, as though the truth itself, about which he asked what it was, had fixed this there, as in the title ».

73. (Vers. 40.). Then is subjoined *the hardening of malice in the Jews,* who were not bent on account of Christ's innocence nor to the judge's clemency, but rather persisted as guilty ones: hence he says: *They cried out again and said*[^p490-3]*: Not this man, but Barabbas,* namely not this innocent one, but him whom we know to be nocent; hence the Evangelist adds: *Now Barabbas was a robber;* in which a wondrous malice of the Jews is expressed, that they preferred a robber and his safety to Christ the Lord. Hence Chrysostom[^p490-4]: « O, of a polluted mind! They ask that the noxious be released, but the innoxious they order to be crucified »; Acts, the third chapter: « You denied the just and holy one, and asked that a man who was a murderer be granted unto you; but the author of life you killed ».
