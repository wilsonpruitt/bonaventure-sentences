# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. VIII, Vers. 37-50
## *Postquam ostensa est per doctrinam Christi liberatio, evacuatur hic Iudaeorum gloriatio, quia gloriabantur se liberos per naturam; et hoc quidem Dominus facit hoc ordine*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 365 -->

54. *Scio, quia filii Abrahae estis.* Postquam ostensa est per doctrinam Christi liberatio, *evacuatur* hic *Iudaeorum gloriatio,* quia[^p365-6] gloriabantur se liberos per naturam; et hoc quidem Dominus facit hoc ordine. Primo innuit *duplex genus filiationis:* secundum *naturam* et *imitationem;* secundo ostendit, quod per *imitationem non sunt filii Abrahae;* tertio, quod *non sunt filii Dei;* quarto, quod *sunt filii diaboli.*

(Vers. 37.). Primo igitur innuitur *duplex genus filiationis:* per *naturam* et per *imitationem;* per *naturalem generationem,* cum dicit: *Scio, quia filii Abrahae estis,* secundum carnem scilicet geniti; Isaiae quinquagesimo primo[^p365-7]: « Attendite ad Abraham, patrem vestrum, et ad Saram, quae peperit vos »; et hunc patrem habetis per *generationem,* sed alium habetis per *imitationem;* unde dicit: *Sed quaeritis me interficere, quia sermo meus non capit in vobis;* Ecclesiastici vigesimo primo[^p365-8]: « Cor fatui quasi vas confractum, omnem sapientiam non tenebit »; Proverbiorum nono. Et hoc quidem est opus iniquitatis, et secundum hoc alium patrem habetis, quem imitamini; unde:

55. (Vers. 38.). *Ego quod vidi apud Patrem meum, hoc*[^p365-9] *loquor,* ipsum imitando; ad Ephesios quinto: « Imitatores Dei estote sicut filii carissimi ». *Et vos quae vidistis apud patrem vestrum facitis;* unde patrem habetis per *imitationem.* Sicut hi habebant duplicem patrem: secundum *carnem* et *imitationem;* sic Abraham duo genera filiorum; ad
<!-- page 366 -->
Romanos nono[^p366-1]: « Non qui semen sunt Abrahae, hi omnes filii, sed qui filii sunt promissionis ».

56. (Vers. 39.). *Responderunt et dixerunt ei.* Hic tangitur secundum; ostenditur enim, quod *non sunt filii Abrahae per imitationem,* sicut ipsi gloriabantur; propter quod dixerunt ei: *Pater noster Abraham est.* Ideo hoc replicant, quia dixerat, quod faciebant opera patris; et ostendit Dominus, quod non intellexit de illo patre; ideo *dicit eis Iesus: Si filii Abrahae estis, opera Abrahae facite,* id est, si vultis esse filii per *imitationem,* sicut estis secundum *carnem;* opera Abrahae facite, alioquin frustra gloriabimini; Matthaei tertio[^p366-2]: « Et ne velitis dicere: Patrem habemus Abraham; dico enim vobis, quia potens est Deus de lapidibus istis suscitare filios Abrahae ». Et quia opera Abrahae non facitis, *a destructione consequentis,* nec *filii eius* dicendi estis. Ideo dicit:

57. (Vers. 40.). *Nunc autem quaeritis me interficere, hominem, qui veritatem locutus sum vobis, quam audivi a Deo,* et ita iustum et innocentem; *hoc* autem *Abraham non fecit,* quia iniquum est et Lege prohibetur; Exodi vigesimo tertio[^p366-3]: « Innocentem et iustum non interficies ». Ideo alius est pater, quem imitamini; propter quod subdit:

58. (Vers. 41.). *Vos autem*[^p366-4] *facitis opera patris vestri.* Chrysostomus: « Opera *Abrahae* fuerunt fides, obedientia, mansuetudo; *vestra,* infidelitas, impietas et crudelitas ».

*Dixerunt itaque ei.* Tangitur hic tertium; ostenditur enim, quod *non sunt filii Dei.* Quia enim iam intellexerant, quod loqueretur de patre spirituali; ideo commendant, se esse filios Dei tanquam cultores eius, non idololatriae deditos, quae *fornicatio* dicitur in Scriptura; unde dicit: *Dixerunt itaque ei: Nos ex fornicatione nati non sumus,* fornicantes cum idolis, secundum illud Ieremiae secundo[^p366-5]: « Sub omni ligno frondoso fornicata es »; et: « Polluisti terram fornicationibus tuis »; non sic nos, sed *unum patrem habemus Deum,* quem colimus iuxta imperium ipsius; Exodi vigesimo[^p366-6]: « Non habebis deos alienos coram me ». Hunc *non* habebant; Malachiae primo: « Si Pater ego sum, ubi est honor meus? Et si Dominus ego sum, ubi est timor meus »? Et quia inaniter gloriabantur, ostendit, quod non sunt filii Dei; unde subdit:

59. (Vers. 42.). *Dicit*[^p366-7] *eis Iesus: Si Deus pater vester esset, diligeretis utique me,* tanquam fratrem vestrum, quia spiritus adoptionis est spiritus amoris; ad Romanos octavo: « Accepistis spiritum adoptionis filiorum, in quo clamamus: Abba, Pater ». *Ego enim a Deo*[^p366-8] *processi,* ut Filius scilicet per generationem; *et veni,* in mundum per incarnationem, et sicut ab illo sum genitus, ita etiam et missus; unde: *neque*[^p366-9] *a me ipso veni, sed ille me misit.* Non propria auctoritate veni; ad Hebraeos quinto: « Christus non semetipsum clarificavit, ut pontifex fieret »; quocontra Ieremiae vigesimo tertio: « Currebant, et ego non mittebam eos »; item Exodi quarto: « Obsecro, Domine, mitte quem missurus es ». Et tamen me non diligitis; et hoc probat quaerens ab eis:

60. (Vers. 43.). *Quare,* inquit, *loquelam meam non cognoscitis?* scilicet credendo in me. Ratio huius erat odium, *quia non potestis audire sermonem meum,* propter odium et invidiam; Sapientiae secundo[^p366-10]: « Gravis est nobis ad videndum ».

61. (Vers. 44.). *Vos ex patre diabolo estis.* Tangitur hic quartum; ostenso enim, quod non sunt filii Dei, ostendit, quod sunt *filii diaboli,* ut sic omnis gloria eorum evacuetur. Et *proponit,* cum dicit: *Vos ex patre diabolo estis; ex* non dicit ibi *generationem,* sed *imitationem* exempli; Ezechielis decimo sexto[^p366-11]: « Pater tuus Amorrhaeus, et mater tua Cethaea »; et primae Ioannis tertio: « Qui facit peccatum ex diabolo est ». — *Probat* hoc, quia eum imitantur; unde: *desideria patris vestri vultis facere.* Haec sunt desideria, de quibus primae ad Timotheum ultimo[^p366-12]: « Desideria multa inutilia et nociva, quae mergunt homines in interitum et perditionem ». Ostendit, quod imitantur eum in duobus, scilicet in *homicidio* et *mendacio:* in *mendacio* est curvitas intellectus, in *homicidio* affectus. Ideo dicit: *Ille homicida erat ab initio,* id est post humani generis initium; quia primum hominem ad mortem perduxit promittendo vitam et negando mortem; « Nequaquam, inquit, moriemini », Genesis tertio[^p366-13]; et Sapientiae secundo: « Deus creavit hominem inexterminabilem,
<!-- page 367 -->
invidia autem diaboli mors intravit in orbem terrarum » etc. Et in hoc vos eum imitamini; sicut supra[^p367-1] dictum est, vultis me interficere. — Imitamini etiam eum in *mendacio;* unde dicit: *Et in veritate non stetit;* immo ad falsitatem et vanitatem cecidit; Isaiae decimo quarto[^p367-2]: « Quomodo cecidisti de caelo, lucifer, qui mane oriebaris »? *Quia veritas non est in eo.* Propter peccatum enim superbiae non recuperavit; Ezechielis vigesimo octavo: « Nihil factus es et non eris in perpetuum ». *Cum loquitur mendacium, ex propriis loquitur,* quia a se habet, non alio suggerente. *Ipse autem mendax est et pater eius,* id est mendacii; Iob penultimo[^p367-3]: « Ipse est rex super omnes filios superbiae ». Augustinus: « Pater mendacii est diabolus, pater veritatis est Deus. Recedite a patre mendacii et currite ad patrem veritatis, amplectimini veritatem, ut accipiatis libertatem ». Huius patris filii sunt omnes mendaces, qui oderunt veritatem. Et tales erant Iudaei; et hoc probat Dominus, quod odiant veritatem; unde dicit:

62. (Vers. 45.). *Ego autem quia*[^p367-4] *veritatem dico, non creditis mihi,* et ita veritatem oditis; Psalmus: « Filii hominum, usquequo gravi corde »? Et quia possent dicere: non discredimus propter odium veritatis, sed iniquitatis; ideo addit:

63. (Vers. 46.). *Quis ex vobis arguet me de peccato?* Quasi dicat: nullus potest. Propter hoc reprehenditur Iob, decimo quinto[^p367-5]: « Arguis verbis eum qui non est aequalis tibi ». Propter quid scilicet mihi non creditis? *Si ego veritatem dico, quare non creditis mihi?* Hoc certe non est propter culpam meam, sed vestram malitiam; et hoc probat:

64. (Vers. 47.). *Qui ex Deo est verba Dei audit,* primae Ioannis quarto[^p367-6]: « Qui novit Deum audit nos; qui non est ex Deo non audit nos »; sed vos non auditis, et quare? *Propterea vos non auditis, quia ex Deo non estis,* sed magis ex diabolo; Ecclesiastici tertio[^p367-7]: « Auris bona cum omni concupiscentia audiet sapientiam »; quocontra Iudaei non audiebant; Actuum septimo: « Dura cervice et incircumcisis cordibus et auribus, vos semper Spiritui sancto resistitis ».

65. *Responderunt ergo Iudaei et dixerunt*[^p367-8]. Postquam ostensa est liberatio, et Iudaeorum evacuata est gloriatio; exprimitur hic *ipsorum indignatio* ad Domini reprehensionem. Et indignationis signum est *illatio convicii.* Primo ponitur *convicii illatio;* secundo, *patiens responsio;* tertio, *patientis responsionis ratio.*

(Vers. 48.). Primo *conviciantur: Nonne bene dicimus nos, quia Samaritanus es tu et daemonium habes?* Quia Dominus dixerat, quod non erant filii Abrahae, sed diaboli; ideo duplex reddunt convicium: primum contra primum[^p367-9]: *Samaritanus es,* non filius Abrahae; secundum contra secundum: *Daemonium habes,* non imitaris Deum, sed diabolum, cuius instructione doces et adiutorio signa facis; unde Matthaei duodecimo[^p367-10]: « In Beelzebub principe daemoniorum eiicit daemonia ».

66. (Vers. 49.). *Respondit Iesus.* Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *patiens responsio,* quia non ad totum convicium respondet, sed ad partem. Unde dicit: *Ego daemonium non habeo,* cuius virtute opera faciam ad gloriam meam; *sed honorifico Patrem meum,* qui in caelis est; quia omnia in gloriam Patris faciebat, secundum illud Apostoli primae ad Corinthios decimo[^p367-11]: « Omnia in gloriam Dei facite ». *Et vos inhonorastis me,* inferendo contumeliam, et per consequens etiam inhonorastis Patrem; supra quinto: « Qui non honorificat Filium non honorificat Patrem, qui misit illum ».

67. (Vers. 50.). *Ego*[^p367-12] *gloriam meam non quaero.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet *patientis responsionis ratio.* Non enim respondebat ad se glorificandum; sed ideo sustinebat eorum convicium, non quia verum, sed quia de gloria non curabat; propter quod dicit: *Ego gloriam meam non quaero,* et ideo contumeliae vestrae non respondeo; Hieronymus[^p367-13]: « Qui non quaerit gloriam non sentit contumeliam ». Ideo de contumelia illata non curo, sed tamen non credatis vos immunes a culpa et retributione digna; unde dicit: *Est qui quaerat et iudicet,* scilicet Pater, qui quaerit gloriam Filii; infra duodecimo[^p367-14]: « Et clarificavi et iterum clarificabo »; *et iudicet;* quasi dicat: eius iudicio reservo. Gregorius: « Filius illatas contumelias Patris iudicio reservat, ut nobis profecto insinuet, quantum patientes esse debemus, dum se adhuc ulcisci non vult et ipse qui iudicat ».

### Quaestiones.

68. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic primo de hoc quod dicitur in littera: *Vos quae vidistis apud patrem vestrum, haec facitis.* — Nunquam enim diabolum viderant. — Et praeterea, nonne etiam per se ipsos poterant peccare?

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod etsi homo possit per se ipsum peccati habere notitiam, pauca tamen aut nulla sunt, in quibus diabolus non interponat partes suas suggerendo. Et *videre* dicuntur opera patris diaboli, dum suggestiones eius vident in corde et complent in opere[^p368-1].

69. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Vos ex patre diabolo estis.* — Ex hoc arguit Manichaeus, quod Dominus non sit factor et creator hominum[^p368-2] malorum.

Ad hoc respondet Augustinus[^p368-3], quod *pater* hic vocatur non qui generat per *originem,* sed per *imitationem.* Similiter haec praepositio *ex* non dicit hic originem in *esse primo,* sed *secundo.* Unde Augustinus[^p368-4]: « Hic *filii* dicuntur non nascendo, sed imitando ». Noluit Adam imitari Deum obediendo et imitatus est diabolum peccato consentiendo.

70. Item obiicitur de hoc quod dicit: *Ille homicida erat ab initio.* — Ex hoc opponit Manichaeus, quod malitia diaboli non habet initium; et sic videtur, quod duo sint principia: unum boni et alterum mali[^p368-5].

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod verbum illud quinque modis exponitur.

Uno modo sic: *homicida erat ab initio:* quia sicut Verbum erat in principio, quia non habuit principium. — Sed haec est haeretica et eliditur per litteram; non enim dicit *in initio*[^p368-6], sed *ab initio.*

Alia expositio est, quod a suo initio factus est malus, sicut videtur dicere Psalmus[^p368-7]: *Draco iste, quem formasti ad illudendum ei.* — Et haec est haeretica, quia, sicut impossibile est, quod summe calidum infrigidet, sic quod summe bonum malum faciat; et illud verbum Psalmi et consimilia intelligenda sunt ex post facto[^p368-8].

Tertia expositio est haec: *homicida erat ab initio,* id est statim post initium, quia parva fuit mora inter creationem et lapsum; et ideo subditur: *Ille in veritate non stetit,* quia non perseveravit.

Quarta expositio est haec: *homicida ab initio,* non suo, sed generis humani; unde Augustinus[^p368-9]: « Non posset homo occidi, nisi factus fuisset homo ».

Quinta haec, quod *homicida ab initio:* quia in eodem instanti coepit esse a Deo et malus a se, sicut anima nostra, quae corpori infunditur. — Primae duae haereticae; secundae duae catholicae; ultima dubia et declinans ad haereticas, licet non sit haeretica; et ideo communiter non tenetur.

71. Quaest. IV. Item obiicitur de hoc quod dicit, quod diabolus est *p[at]er mendacii.* — Ergo cum omne peccatum sit mendacium, videtur, quod sit unum principium peccati[^p368-10].

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod non est ponere *primum malum,* quod quidem sit primum in genere *entis,* quasi non sit ab alio; sed est ponere *primum malum,* ante quod non praecessit aliud malum; et hoc quidem est origo sequentium malorum, non semper *effective,* sed *occasionaliter* et *suggestive.* Ideo dicit: *Mendax est et pater eius,* scilicet mendacii[^p368-11].

72. Quaest. V. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Qui ex Deo est.* — Aliqui[^p368-12] sumserunt robur erroris, quod boni spiritus sunt ex Deo *materialiter.*
<!-- page 369 -->

Respondetur eis, quod hoc erroneum est dicere, quod Deus alicuius sit *materia,* cum sit simplicissimus.

Sed tunc *obiicitur;* quia, si *ex* dicit ibi originem et causam efficientem; et omnes sumus a Deo: omnes ergo audimus verbum Dei[^p369-1]. — Item, nonne ipsi audiebant?

**Respondeo:** Sicut praedictum est[^p369-2], *ex* dicit originem non quantum ad *esse primum,* sed quantum ad *esse secundum,* quod est *esse gratiae* et *culpae,* et hoc quidem secundum conformitatem et imitationem. Unde sensus est: *qui ex Deo est,* per conformitatem voluntatis, *verba Dei audit,* aure cordis, non corporis, quia sic[^p369-3] mali audiunt.

73. Quaest. VI. Item quaeritur de hoc, quod Iudaei ista duo obiecerunt Christo magis quam alia. — Aliquam[^p369-4] rationem habebant, quare haec convicia magis dicerent.

Dicendum, quod eis videbatur, quod Christus in observatione Legis quaedam faceret, quaedam transiret, ut Sabbatum et huiusmodi. Et quia Samaritani hoc faciebant, quia partim observabant, partim non[^p369-5]; ideo dixerunt eum *Samaritanum.* — Item, Dominus in doctrina sua quaedam dicebat valde sapientia et aliquando quaedam eis valde improbabilia, quia non intelligebant eum; ideo videbatur eis loqui tanquam homo alienatus: et ideo, quia daemonium inducit in mentis alienationem, dicebant, eum *daemonium* habere[^p369-6].

74. Quaest. VII. Item quaeritur: cum duo fuissent ei obiecta, quare Dominus tantum ad alterum respondit?

Respondet Gregorius[^p369-7], « quod alterum negavit, alterum tacendo consensit. *Samaritanus* enim interpretatur *custos,* et ipse custos erat, de quo Psalmus: *Nisi Dominus custodierit civitatem* » etc.

Aliter respondetur, quod certum erat, eum de Iudaeis natum; ideo non oportuit, eum respondere, quia evidenter erat falsum.

Tertio modo respondetur, quod illud de *Samaritano* tangebat *suum* vituperium, sed de *daemonio* tangebat vituperium *divinum,* et in Spiritum sanctum blasphemia erat, de qua dicitur Matthaei duodecimo[^p369-8], et derogabat gloriae Patris; ideo docuit nos Dominus sustinere *nostra* improperia, sed non *divina.* Et hoc patet ex responsione sua: *Ego daemonium non habeo, sed honorifico Patrem meum.*

## English
<!-- page 365 -->

54. *I know that you are the children of Abraham.* After liberation through the teaching[^tr-doctrina] of Christ has been shown, *the glorying of the Jews is* here *made void,* because[^p365-6] they gloried that they were free by nature; and the Lord does this in this order. First he intimates *a twofold kind of filiation:* according to *nature* and to *imitation;* second he shows that by *imitation they are not the children of Abraham;* third, that *they are not the children of God;* fourth, that *they are the children of the devil.*

(V. 37.). First, then, *a twofold kind of filiation* is intimated: by *nature* and by *imitation;* by *natural generation,* when he says: *I know that you are the children of Abraham,* namely begotten according to the flesh; Isaias, the fifty-first chapter[^p365-7]: « Look unto Abraham your father, and to Sara that bore you »; and this father you have by *generation,* but another you have by *imitation;* whence he says: *But you seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you;* Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-first chapter[^p365-8]: « The heart of a fool is like a broken vessel, and no wisdom at all shall it hold »; Proverbs, the ninth chapter. And this indeed is the work of iniquity, and according to this you have another father, whom you imitate; whence:

55. (V. 38.). *I speak that which I have seen with my Father,*[^p365-9] imitating him; to the Ephesians, the fifth chapter: « Be ye imitators of God, as most dear children ». *And you do the things that you have seen with your father;* whence you have a father by *imitation.* As these had a twofold father: according to *the flesh* and to *imitation;* so Abraham has two kinds of children; to the
<!-- page 366 -->
Romans, the ninth chapter[^p366-1]: « Not they that are the seed of Abraham are all children, but they that are the children of the promise ».

56. (V. 39.). *They answered and said to him.* Here the second is touched; for it is shown that *they are not the children of Abraham by imitation,* as they themselves gloried; on account of which they said to him: *Abraham is our father.* They reply this because he had said that they did the works of their father; and the Lord shows that he did not mean that father; therefore *Jesus saith to them: If you be the children of Abraham, do the works of Abraham,* that is, if you wish to be children by *imitation,* as you are according to *the flesh;* do the works of Abraham, otherwise you will glory in vain; Matthew, the third chapter[^p366-2]: « And do not wish to say: We have Abraham for our father; for I say to you that God is able of these stones to raise up children to Abraham ». And because you do not do the works of Abraham, *by the destruction of the consequent,* neither are you to be called *his children.* Therefore he says:

57. (V. 40.). *But now you seek to kill me, a man who have spoken the truth to you, which I have heard of God,* and so a just and innocent man; *this* however *Abraham did not,* because it is wicked and is forbidden by the Law; Exodus, the twenty-third chapter[^p366-3]: « The innocent and just thou shalt not kill ». Therefore it is another father whom you imitate; on account of which he subjoins:

58. (V. 41.). *But you*[^p366-4] *do the works of your father.* Chrysostom: « The works of *Abraham* were faith, obedience, meekness; *yours,* unfaithfulness, impiety and cruelty ».

*They said therefore to him.* Here the third is touched; for it is shown that *they are not the children of God.* For because they had already understood that he was speaking of a spiritual father; therefore they commend themselves as being children of God, as his worshippers, not given to idolatry, which is called *fornication* in Scripture; whence he says: *They said therefore to him: We are not born of fornication,* committing fornication with idols, according to that of Jeremias, the second chapter[^p366-5]: « Under every leafy tree thou hast committed fornication »; and: « Thou hast polluted the land with thy fornications »; not so we, but *we have one father, God,* whom we worship according to his command; Exodus, the twentieth chapter[^p366-6]: « Thou shalt not have strange gods before me ». Him they did *not* have; Malachias, the first chapter: « If I am the Father, where is my honour? And if I am the Lord, where is my fear »? And because they gloried emptily, he shows that they are not the children of God; whence he subjoins:

59. (V. 42.). *Jesus saith*[^p366-7] *to them: If God were your father, you would indeed love me,* as your brother, because the spirit of adoption is the spirit of love; to the Romans, the eighth chapter: « You have received the spirit of adoption of sons, in which we cry: Abba, Father ». *For from God*[^p366-8] *I proceeded,* namely as Son by generation; *and came,* into the world by incarnation, and as I am begotten of him, so too am I also sent; whence: *for*[^p366-9] *I came not of myself, but he sent me.* I did not come by my own authority; to the Hebrews, the fifth chapter: « Christ did not glorify himself, that he might be made a high priest »; against which, Jeremias, the twenty-third chapter: « They ran, and I did not send them »; likewise Exodus, the fourth chapter: « I beseech thee, Lord, send whom thou wilt send ». And yet you do not love me; and this he proves, asking them:

60. (V. 43.). *Why,* he says, *do you not know my speech?* namely by believing in me. The reason for this was hatred, *because you cannot hear my word,* on account of hatred and envy; Wisdom, the second chapter[^p366-10]: « He is grievous unto us to behold ».

61. (V. 44.). *You are of your father the devil.* Here the fourth is touched; for having shown that they are not the children of God, he shows that they are *the children of the devil,* that thus all their glory may be made void. And he *proposes* it when he says: *You are of your father the devil; of* does not state there *generation,* but *imitation* of example; Ezechiel, the sixteenth chapter[^p366-11]: « Thy father was an Amorrhite, and thy mother a Cethite »; and the third chapter of the first of John: « He that committeth sin is of the devil ». — He *proves* this, because they imitate him; whence: *the desires of your father you will do.* These are the desires of which the last chapter of the first to Timothy speaks[^p366-12]: « Many unprofitable and hurtful desires, which drown men into destruction and perdition ». He shows that they imitate him in two things, namely in *murder* and *lying:* in *lying* is the crookedness of the understanding, in *murder* that of the affection. Therefore he says: *He was a murderer from the beginning,* that is, after the beginning of the human race; because he led the first man to death by promising life and denying death; « By no means, he says, shall you die », Genesis, the third chapter[^p366-13]; and Wisdom, the second chapter: « God created man incorruptible,
<!-- page 367 -->
but by the envy of the devil death came into the world » etc. And in this you imitate him; as was said above[^p367-1], you wish to kill me. — You imitate him also in *lying;* whence he says: *And he stood not in the truth;* nay, he fell to falsity and vanity; Isaias, the fourteenth chapter[^p367-2]: « How art thou fallen from heaven, O lucifer, who didst rise in the morning »? *Because truth is not in him.* For because of the sin of pride he did not recover; Ezechiel, the twenty-eighth chapter: « Thou art made nothing, and thou shalt not be for ever ». *When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own,* because he has it from himself, with no other suggesting it. *But he is a liar, and the father thereof,* that is, of the lie; Job, the next to last chapter[^p367-3]: « He is king over all the children of pride ». Augustine: « The father of the lie is the devil, the father of truth is God. Withdraw from the father of the lie and run to the father of truth, embrace the truth, that you may receive liberty ». Of this father all liars are the children, who hate the truth. And such were the Jews; and the Lord proves this, that they hate the truth; whence he says:

62. (V. 45.). *But because*[^p367-4] *I say the truth, you believe me not,* and so you hate the truth; the Psalm: « O ye sons of men, how long dull of heart »? And because they could say: we disbelieve not out of hatred of the truth, but of iniquity; therefore he adds:

63. (V. 46.). *Which of you shall reprove me of sin?* As if to say: no one can. For this Job is reproved, the fifteenth chapter[^p367-5]: « Thou reprovest with words him who is not equal to thee ». Why, namely, do you not believe me? *If I say the truth, why do you not believe me?* This is certainly not because of my fault, but your malice; and this he proves:

64. (V. 47.). *He that is of God heareth the words of God,* the fourth chapter of the first of John[^p367-6]: « He that knoweth God heareth us; he that is not of God heareth us not »; but you do not hear, and why? *Therefore you hear them not, because you are not of God,* but rather of the devil; Ecclesiasticus, the third chapter[^p367-7]: « A good ear will hear wisdom with all desire »; against which, the Jews did not hear; Acts, the seventh chapter: « With stiff neck and uncircumcised hearts and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit ».

65. *The Jews therefore answered and said*[^p367-8]. After liberation has been shown, and the glorying of the Jews has been made void; here is expressed *their indignation* at the Lord's reproof. And the sign of indignation is *the bringing of abuse.* First is set down *the bringing of abuse;* second, *the patient response;* third, *the reason for the patient response.*

(V. 48.). First *they abuse him: Do not we say well that thou art a Samaritan, and hast a devil?* Because the Lord had said that they were not the children of Abraham, but of the devil; therefore they return a twofold abuse: the first against the first[^p367-9]: *Thou art a Samaritan,* not a son of Abraham; the second against the second: *Thou hast a devil,* thou dost not imitate God, but the devil, by whose instruction thou teachest and by whose help thou workest signs; whence Matthew, the twelfth chapter[^p367-10]: « By Beelzebub the prince of devils he casteth out devils ».

66. (V. 49.). *Jesus answered.* Here the second is touched, namely *the patient response,* because he does not respond to the whole abuse, but to a part. Whence he says: *I have not a devil,* by whose power I should do works for my own glory; *but I honour my Father,* who is in heaven; because he did all things for the glory of the Father, according to that of the Apostle, the first to the Corinthians, the tenth chapter[^p367-11]: « Do all things for the glory of God ». *And you have dishonoured me,* by inflicting contumely, and consequently you have also dishonoured the Father; above, in the fifth chapter: « He who honoureth not the Son, honoureth not the Father, who hath sent him ».

67. (V. 50.). *But I*[^p367-12] *seek not my own glory.* Here the third is touched, namely *the reason for the patient response.* For he did not respond in order to glorify himself; but he bore their abuse, not because it was true, but because he did not care about glory; on account of which he says: *I seek not my own glory,* and therefore I do not respond to your contumely; Jerome[^p367-13]: « He who does not seek glory does not feel contumely ». Therefore I do not care about the contumely inflicted, but yet do not believe yourselves immune from fault and from a fitting retribution; whence he says: *There is one that seeketh and judgeth,* namely the Father, who seeks the glory of the Son; below, in the twelfth chapter[^p367-14]: « I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again »; *and judgeth;* as if to say: I reserve it to his judgement. Gregory: « The Son reserves the contumelies inflicted on him to the judgement of the Father, that he may surely intimate to us how patient we ought to be, while even he who judges does not yet wish to avenge himself ».
<!-- page 368 -->

### Questions.

68. Quaest. I. But here it is asked, first, concerning this which is said in the letter: *You do the things that you have seen with your father.* — For they had never seen the devil. — And besides, could they not also sin of themselves?

**I respond:** It must be said that, although man can of himself have knowledge of sin, yet there are few things or none in which the devil does not interpose his part by suggesting. And they are said *to see* the works of their father the devil when they see his suggestions in the heart and fulfil them in deed[^p368-1].

69. Quaest. II. Likewise it is asked concerning this which he says: *You are of your father the devil.* — From this the Manichaean argues that the Lord is not the maker and creator of evil men[^p368-2].

To this Augustine responds[^p368-3] that *father* is called here not one who begets by *origin,* but by *imitation.* Likewise this preposition *of* does not state here origin in *first being,* but in *second.* Whence Augustine[^p368-4]: « Here they are called *children* not by being born, but by imitating ». Adam would not imitate God by obeying, and imitated the devil by consenting to sin.

70. Likewise it is objected concerning this which he says: *He was a murderer from the beginning.* — From this the Manichaean opposes that the malice of the devil has no beginning; and so it seems that there are two principles: one of good and the other of evil[^p368-5].

**I respond:** It must be said that that word is expounded in five ways.

In one way thus: *he was a murderer from the beginning:* because, as the Word was in the beginning, because he had no beginning. — But this is heretical and is struck down by the letter; for it does not say *in the beginning*[^p368-6], but *from the beginning.*

Another exposition is that from his own beginning he was made evil, as the Psalm seems to say[^p368-7]: *This dragon, which thou hast formed to play with him.* — And this is heretical, because, just as it is impossible that what is supremely hot should chill, so is it that the supremely good should make evil; and that word of the Psalm and the like are to be understood from after the fact[^p368-8].

The third exposition is this: *he was a murderer from the beginning,* that is, immediately after the beginning, because there was little delay between creation and the fall; and therefore it is subjoined: *He stood not in the truth,* because he did not persevere.

The fourth exposition is this: *a murderer from the beginning,* not his own, but that of the human race; whence Augustine[^p368-9]: « Man could not be killed unless man had been made ».

The fifth is this, that *a murderer from the beginning:* because in the same instant he began to be from God and evil from himself, as our soul, which is infused into the body. — The first two are heretical; the second two catholic; the last doubtful and declining towards the heretical ones, although it is not heretical; and therefore it is not commonly held.

71. Quaest. IV. Likewise it is objected concerning this which he says, that the devil is *the f[at]her of the lie.* — Therefore, since every sin is a lie, it seems that there is one principle of sin[^p368-10].

**I respond:** It must be said that one is not to posit *a first evil* which would be first in the genus *of being,* as though it were not from another; but one is to posit *a first evil* before which no other evil preceded; and this indeed is the origin of the evils that follow, not always *effectively,* but *occasionally* and *suggestively.* Therefore he says: *He is a liar, and the father thereof,* namely of the lie[^p368-11].

72. Quaest. V. Likewise it is asked concerning this which he says: *He that is of God.* — Some[^p368-12] have taken from it the strength of an error, that good spirits are of God *materially.*
<!-- page 369 -->

It is answered to them that it is erroneous to say that God is the *matter* of anything, since he is most simple.

But then *it is objected;* because, if *of* states there origin and efficient cause; and we all are from God: therefore we all hear the word of God[^p369-1]. — Likewise, did not they themselves hear?

**I respond:** As was said before[^p369-2], *of* states origin not as to *first being,* but as to *second being,* which is *the being of grace* and *of fault,* and this indeed according to conformity and imitation. Whence the sense is: *he that is of God,* by conformity of will, *heareth the words of God,* with the ear of the heart, not of the body, because thus[^p369-3] the wicked hear.

73. Quaest. VI. Likewise it is asked concerning this, that the Jews objected these two things to Christ rather than others. — Had they some[^p369-4] reason why they should say these abuses rather?

It must be said that it seemed to them that Christ did some things in the observance of the Law and passed over some, as the Sabbath and the like. And because the Samaritans did this, because they observed in part and in part not[^p369-5]; therefore they called him a *Samaritan.* — Likewise, the Lord in his teaching said some things very wise and sometimes some things very improbable to them, because they did not understand him; therefore he seemed to them to speak as a man alienated: and therefore, because a devil induces alienation of mind, they said that he had a *devil*[^p369-6].

74. Quaest. VII. Likewise it is asked: since two things had been objected to him, why did the Lord respond to only one?

Gregory responds[^p369-7] « that the one he denied, the other he consented to by keeping silent. For *Samaritan* is interpreted *keeper,* and he was the keeper, of whom the Psalm speaks: *Unless the Lord keep the city* » etc.

Otherwise it is responded that it was certain that he was born of the Jews; therefore it was not necessary for him to respond, because it was evidently false.

In the third way it is responded that that about the *Samaritan* touched *his own* vituperation, but that about the *devil* touched *divine* vituperation, and was blasphemy against the Holy Spirit, of which it is said in Matthew, the twelfth chapter[^p369-8], and derogated from the glory of the Father; therefore the Lord taught us to bear *our own* reproaches, but not *divine* ones. And this is plain from his response: *I have not a devil, but I honour my Father.*
