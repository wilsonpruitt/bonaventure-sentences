# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XVII, Vers. 20-23
## *In parte tertia Christus petit credituris conformitatem gratiae*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 474 -->

#### In parte tertia Christus petit credituris conformitatem gratiae.

35. *Non pro eis*[^p474-7] *rogo tantum.* Haec est tertia pars capituli, in qua Dominus credituris per Apostolos *petit conformitatem gratiae;* et hoc ordine procedit. Primo ponit *petitionem;* secundo, *exaudiendi rationem,* ibi[^p474-8]: *Et ego claritatem, quam dedisti.*

In petitione igitur petit credituris duplicem unitatem, scilicet *dilectionis* et *fidei.*

(Vers. 20.). Propter unitatem *dilectionis,* quam petit per Apostolos credituris, dicit: *Non pro eis,* scilicet pro Apostolis, *rogo tantum, sed et pro eis qui credituri sunt per verbum eorum in me;* pro illis, inquam, rogo:

36. (Vers. 21.). *Ut*[^p474-9] *sint unum,* per conformitatem dilectionis, *sicut tu Pater in me, et ego in te,* id est, sicut nos unum sumus, *ut et ipsi sint unum in nobis;* ad Galatas tertio: « Omnes vos unum estis in Christo Iesu ». Ista unitas est per adhaerentiam caritatis; primae ad Corinthios sexto: « Qui adhaeret Deo unus spiritus est ». Quando vero uniuntur sibi invicem in Deo, tunc sunt unum in Deo; unde primae ad Corinthios duodecimo: « In uno Spiritu in unum corpus baptizati sumus ».

37. Sic petit eis unitatem caritatis, petit etiam *unitatem fidei;* ideo ait: *Ut mundus credat, quia tu me misisti. Mundus,* id est universitas filiorum Dei dispersorum per mundum, quos debebat in unitatem fidei congregare. Supra undecimo[^p474-10] dictum est, quod moriturus erat, « ut filios Dei, qui erant dispersi, congregaret in unum », et hoc uniendo in fide nominis sui; quia, supra primo, « dedit potestatem filios Dei fieri his qui credunt in nomine eius »; et Actuum quarto: « Non est aliud nomen sub caelo datum hominibus, in quo oporteat nos salvos fieri ».

38. *Et ego claritatem.* Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *exaudiendi ratio,* quia ideo claritatem suam communicaverat Apostolis, ut mundus converteretur ad fidem. Triplicem autem claritatem communicaverat eis, scilicet *potentiae, sapientiae* et *bonitatis: potentiae* in faciendo miracula, propter quae convertebantur ad fidem *Iudaei,* qui[^p474-11] « quaerunt signa »;
<!-- page 475 -->
*sapientiae* in annuntiando documenta, propter quae *Graeci* converterentur, qui « quaerunt *sapientiam* ».

(Vers. 22.). Propter hanc *duplicem claritatem* dicit: *Et ego claritatem, quam dedisti mihi, dedi eis;* Chrysostomus[^p475-1]: « Eam quae per *signa,* eam quae per *dogmata* ». De datione claritatis *signorum,* Lucae nono[^p475-2]: « Convocatis duodecim, dedit eis virtutem et potestatem super omnia daemonia, et ut languores curarent ». — De datione claritatis *documentorum,* Lucae vigesimo primo: « Ego dabo vobis os et sapientiam, cui non poterunt resistere et contradicere omnes adversarii vestri ». — Dedit etiam *claritatem bonitatis;* propter quod subdit: *Ut sint unum, sicut et nos unum sumus,* per proportionem; et haec est proportio:

39. (Vers. 23.). *Ego in eis, et tu in me, ut sint consummati in unum,* id est perfecti in unitate *caritatis,* et per hoc efficiantur clari. Unde Chrysostomus[^p475-3]: « Haec est magna gloria, ut sint unum, et haec signis maior gloria est. Sicut enim Deum admiramur, quoniam non est contentio atque discordia apud naturam illam, et maxima est haec gloria; ita et hi hinc efficiantur clari ». Unde supra decimo tertio[^p475-4]: « In hoc cognoscent omnes, quia discipuli mei estis, si dilectionem ad invicem habueritis ». Et hanc claritatem Apostolis dedit. — *Et cognoscat mundus, quia tu me misisti,* propter claritatem scilicet, quam dedi eis in nomine meo; *et dilexisti eos, sicut et me dilexisti;* et hoc cognoscat per unitatem et concordiam, quam videbit in eis. Chrysostomus[^p475-5]: « Ostendens, signo pacem magis homines posse attrahere. Sicut enim contentio dissolutivum, ita et consonantia communicativum »; Actuum secundo[^p475-6]: « Erant perseverantes in doctrina Apostolorum et communicatione fractionis panis et orationibus; fiebatque autem omni animae timor ».

### Quaestiones.

40. Quaest. I. Quaeritur hic: quia, si nulli sunt salvati per Christum, nisi pro quibus oravit; et ipse solum oravit pro Apostolis et credituris per eos: ergo illi qui crediderunt per Christum, ut Nicodemus[^p475-7], caruerunt beneficio orationis, et ita salutis.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod *credituri per Apostolos* non tantum dicuntur qui tantum ad eorum praedicationem conversi sunt, sed etiam qui eorum vestigiis adhaeserunt. — Vel potest dici, quod Dominus orabat pro his qui iam crediderunt generaliter, non tantum pro Apostolis[^p475-8].

41. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc, quod Dominus oravit pro credituris per Apostolos, *ut essent unum.*

**Contra:** Multi crediderunt, qui fuerunt divulsi et retrocesserunt: ergo non fuit pro omnibus exauditus.

**Respondeo:** Quidam sunt de numero fidelium *nomine,* ut mali Christiani; quidam *merito praesentis iustitiae;* quidam *cognitione praevisionis aeternae,* de qua secundae ad Timotheum secundo[^p475-9]: *Novit Dominus qui sunt eius;* et pro talibus orat Dominus, pro aliis non, quia primae Ioannis secundo: *Ex nobis exierunt, sed ex nobis non erant.*

42. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Sint unum, sicut et nos.*

1. Quid est quod dicit, cum sint unum in *essentia,* homines autem esse non possunt unum in *essentia?*

2. Item, *sicut tu in me, et ego in eis*[^p475-10]; nulla omnino videtur similitudo.

Dixit Arius, quod erat *similitudo,* et sicut, cum dicitur, quod sumus unum, non propter hoc sequitur, quod sumus unum in essentia; sic nec, quando Filius dicit[^p475-11]: *Ego et Pater unum sumus.*

Et male dicit, quia, quando dicitur, quod aliqui sint unum, sicut dicit Augustinus[^p475-12], vel necesse
<!-- page 476 -->
est, *addi determinationem,* vel de necessitate dicit *conformitatem in natura,* non tantum *in voluntate;* unde de duobus hominibus vel pluribus, cum sese diligunt, *sunt unum* recte dicitur, sed de homine et Deo nequaquam. Unde necesse est, si Filius vere dicit Patri: *Ego et tu unum sumus,* quod habeant eandem naturam; si autem divina natura non est divisibilis, tunc *simpliciter* sunt unum.

Quod ergo quaerit, dicendum, quod similitudo est valde longinqua, sicut ibi[^p476-1]: *Estote misericordes, sicut et Pater vester misericors est.* Sicut misericordia divina excedit in infinitum misericordiam meam, ita unitas unitatem.

Sed quare ecclesia malorum non facit unum, sicut Ecclesia Christi?

Responsio est: quia, si mali conveniant in aliqua malitia exterius, differunt tamen in voluntate interius, differunt in fine, quia quilibet proprium quaerit commodum[^p476-2]; sed boni conveniunt in utrisque.

## English
<!-- page 474 -->

#### In the third part Christ asks for those who will believe the conformity of grace.

35. *Not for them*[^p474-7] *do I pray only.* This is the third part of the chapter, in which the Lord *asks* for those who will believe through the Apostles *the conformity of grace;* and he proceeds in this order. First he sets down *the petition;* second, *the reason for being heard,* there[^p474-8]: *And I, the clarity which thou hast given.*

In the petition, then, he asks for those who will believe a twofold unity, namely *of dilection* and *of faith.*

(Vers. 20.). On account of the unity *of dilection,* which he asks through the Apostles for those who will believe, he says: *Not for them,* namely for the Apostles, *do I pray only, but for them also who shall believe in me through their word;* for those, I say, I pray:

36. (Vers. 21.). *That*[^p474-9] *they may be one,* through the conformity of dilection, *as thou, Father, in me, and I in thee,* that is, as we are one, *that they also may be one in us;* to the Galatians, the third chapter: « You are all one in Christ Jesus ». This unity is through the adherence of charity; the first to the Corinthians, the sixth chapter: « He who adheres to God is one spirit ». But when they are united to one another in God, then they are one in God; hence the first to the Corinthians, the twelfth chapter: « In one Spirit we were baptized into one body ».

37. Thus he asks for them the unity of charity; he asks also *the unity of faith;* therefore he says: *That the world may believe that thou hast sent me. The world,* that is, the universality of the sons of God dispersed through the world, whom he was to gather into the unity of faith. Above, the eleventh chapter[^p474-10], it was said that he was to die, « that he might gather together in one the children of God, that were dispersed », and this by uniting them in the faith of his name; because, above, the first chapter, « he gave power to be made the sons of God to them that believe in his name »; and Acts, the fourth chapter: « There is no other name under heaven given to men, whereby we must be saved ».

38. *And I, the clarity.* Here is touched the second, namely *the reason for being heard,* because he had communicated his clarity to the Apostles for this, that the world might be converted to the faith. But he had communicated to them a threefold clarity, namely *of power, of wisdom* and *of goodness: of power* in working miracles, on account of which *the Jews* were converted to the faith, who[^p474-11] « seek signs »;
<!-- page 475 -->
*of wisdom* in announcing lessons, on account of which *the Greeks* would be converted, who « seek *wisdom* ».

(Vers. 22.). On account of this *twofold clarity* he says: *And I, the clarity which thou hast given me, have given them;* Chrysostom[^p475-1]: « That which is through *signs,* that which is through *dogmas* ». Of the giving of the clarity *of signs,* Luke, the ninth chapter[^p475-2]: « Calling together the twelve, he gave them power and authority over all devils, and to cure diseases ». — Of the giving of the clarity *of lessons,* Luke, the twenty-first chapter: « I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to resist and gainsay ». — He gave also *the clarity of goodness;* on account of which he subjoins: *That they may be one, as we also are one,* by proportion; and this is the proportion:

39. (Vers. 23.). *I in them, and thou in me, that they may be consummated in one,* that is, perfect in the unity *of charity,* and through this they are made clear. Hence Chrysostom[^p475-3]: « This is great glory, that they be one, and this glory is greater than signs. For as we wonder at God, because there is no contention nor discord in that nature, and this is the greatest glory; so these also are hence made clear ». Hence above, the thirteenth chapter[^p475-4]: « By this shall all men know that you are my disciples, if you have dilection one for another ». And this clarity he gave to the Apostles. — *And that the world may know that thou hast sent me,* namely on account of the clarity which I have given them in my name; *and hast loved them, as thou hast also loved me;* and this it may know through the unity and concord which it will see in them. Chrysostom[^p475-5]: « Showing that peace can draw men more than a sign. For as contention is a dissolving thing, so also consonance is a communicating thing »; Acts, the second chapter[^p475-6]: « They were persevering in the doctrine[^tr-doctrina] of the Apostles and in the communication of the breaking of bread and in prayers; and fear came upon every soul ».

### Questions.

40. Quaest. I. It is asked here: because, if none are saved through Christ except those for whom he prayed; and he prayed only for the Apostles and for those who would believe through them: therefore those who believed through Christ, as Nicodemus[^p475-7], lacked the benefit of the prayer, and so of salvation.

**I respond:** It must be said that *those who will believe through the Apostles* are said to be not only those who were converted at their preaching, but also those who adhered to their footsteps. — Or it can be said that the Lord was praying generally for those who had already believed, not only for the Apostles[^p475-8].

41. Quaest. II. Likewise it is asked concerning this, that the Lord prayed for those who would believe through the Apostles, *that they might be one.*

**On the contrary:** Many believed who were torn away and went back: therefore he was not heard for all.

**I respond:** Some are of the number of the faithful *in name,* as bad Christians; some *by the merit of present justice;* some *by the cognition of eternal prevision,* of which the second to Timothy, the second chapter[^p475-9]: *The Lord knoweth who are his;* and for such the Lord prays, for the others not, because the first of John, the second chapter: *They went out from us, but they were not of us.*

42. Quaest. III. Likewise it is asked concerning what he says: *That they may be one, as we also.*

1. What is it that he says, since they are one in *essence,* but men cannot be one in *essence?*

2. Likewise, *as thou in me, and I in them*[^p475-10]; there seems to be no similitude at all.

Arius said that it was *a similitude,* and as, when it is said that we are one, it does not follow on this account that we are one in essence; so neither when the Son says[^p475-11]: *I and the Father are one.*

And he speaks ill, because, when it is said that some are one, as Augustine says[^p475-12], it is either necessary
<!-- page 476 -->
*that a determination be added,* or it says of necessity *conformity in nature,* not only *in will;* hence of two men or more, when they love each other, it is rightly said *they are one,* but of man and God by no means. Hence it is necessary, if the Son truly says to the Father: *I and thou are one,* that they have the same nature; but if the divine nature is not divisible, then they are *simply* one.

As to what he asks, then, it must be said that the similitude is very remote, as there[^p476-1]: *Be ye merciful, as your Father also is merciful.* As the divine mercy exceeds infinitely my mercy, so unity exceeds unity.

But why does the church of the wicked not make one, as the Church of Christ does?

The response is: because, if the wicked agree in some malice exteriorly, yet they differ in will interiorly, they differ in the end, because each one seeks his own advantage[^p476-2]; but the good agree in both.
