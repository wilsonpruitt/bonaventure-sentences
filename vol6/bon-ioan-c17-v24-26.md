# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XVII, Vers. 24-26
## *In parte quarta Christus eisdem petit perpetuitatem gloriae*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 476 -->

#### In parte quarta Christus eisdem petit perpetuitatem gloriae.

43. *Pater, quos dedisti mihi.* Quarta pars capituli[^p476-3] est, in qua illis quibus petiit conformitatem gratiae, *petit perpetuitatem gloriae* hoc ordine. Primo ponitur *petitio;* secundo, *petitionis exaudiendae ratio.*

(Vers. 24.). *Petitio* est de gloria; et quia gloria est per visionem Divinitatis, quam videbimus in patria; ideo dicit: *Pater, quos dedisti mihi,* per praedestinationem scilicet, *volo, ut, ubi sum ego, et illi sint*[^p476-4], per gloriosam cohabitationem; supra duodecimo: « Si quis mihi ministrat, me sequatur; et ubi ego sum, ibi sit et minister meus ». *Ubi sum,* id est, statim futurus sum secundum humanitatem. — Vel *ubi sum* secundum Divinitatem, id est, sint mecum, qui ubique sum. Augustinus[^p476-5]: « Esse *cum* illo magnum bonum est; nam miseri esse possunt, *ubi* est ille qui ubique est. Beati vero sunt *cum* illo, quia semper fruendo illi adhaerent ». Et quod ita intelligendum, patet per hoc quod sequitur: *Ut videant claritatem meam, quam dedisti mihi.* Haec est excellentia Divinitatis; ideo dicit: *Quia dilexisti me ante mundi constitutionem;* supra quinto[^p476-6]: « Pater diligit Filium et omnia demonstrat illi ». Hanc claritatem videbunt soli boni; Isaiae vigesimo sexto: « Tollatur impius, ne videat claritatem Dei ». Ad hanc claritatem modo conformamur per gratiam; secundae ad Corinthios tertio: « Nos revelata facie gloriam Dei speculantes, in eandem imaginem transformamur a claritate in claritatem, tanquam a Domini spiritu ».

44. (Vers. 25.). *Pater iuste, mundus* etc. Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *exauditionis ratio,* quare scilicet debent hi videre per gloriam, quia modo cognoscunt per gratiam; mali vero modo non cognoscunt nec tunc cognoscent; ideo dicit: *Pater iuste,* quia « reddes unicuique secundum opera sua[^p476-7] ». *Mundus te non cognovit;* supra primo: « Erat lux vera, quae illuminat » etc.; ideo non rogo pro mundo. *Ego autem cognovi te, et hi cognoverunt, quia tu me misisti,* et hoc me revelante; ideo dicit:

45. (Vers. 26.). *Et notum feci eis nomen tuum;* Psalmus[^p476-8]: « Narrabo nomen tuum fratribus meis »; Matthaei undecimo: « Nemo novit Filium nisi Pater, neque Patrem quis novit nisi Filius, et cui voluerit Filius revelare ». Nec tantum fecit notum *per se,* sed amplius faciet notum *per Spiritum sanctum;* ideo dicit: *Et notum faciam,* et hoc per Spiritum sanctum, qui est amor Patris et Filii; ideo dicit: *Ut dilectio, qua dilexisti me, sit in ipsis,* per Spiritus sancti donationem; ad Romanos quinto[^p476-9]: « Caritas Dei diffusa est in cordibus nostris per Spiritum sanctum, qui datus est nobis ». *Et ego in ipsis,* quia, primae Ioannis quarto[^p476-10], « qui manet in caritate in Deo manet, et Deus in eo »; et ideo dicitur ibidem:
<!-- page 477 -->
« In hoc cognovimus, quoniam in ipso manemus, et ipse in nobis, quoniam de Spiritu suo dedit nobis ». Et qui hunc Spiritum habet et Filium securus est, ut habeat Patrem. Unde Augustinus[^p477-1] dicit, quod « Pater Filium suum dedit in pretium redemptionis, Spiritum sanctum in pignus adoptionis, se ipsum in hereditatem adoptatis ».

### Quaestiones.

46. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *ut, ubi ego sum, et illi sint mecum.* — *Contra:* Supra decimo quarto[^p477-2]: *In domo Patris mei mansiones multae sunt.*

**Respondeo:** « In dispari claritate par gaudium[^p477-3] »; ratione claritatis disparis est multitudo; ratione communis gaudii, ubi caput et membra.

47. Quaest. II. Item, *habitat lucem inaccessibilem,* primae ad Timotheum ultimo[^p477-4]

**Respondeo**, quod *inaccessibile*[^p477-5], vel quia nullo modo, vel quia difficulter, vel quia non humana virtute, vel quia acceditur sola divina gloria.

48. Quaest. III. Item, excellentiae corrumpunt[^p477-6]; sed claritas Dei est excellentissima.

**Respondeo:** Intelligendum: ubi apprehendens est *corporale,* apprehensum potest laedere.

49. Quaest. IV. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit, quod *mundus,* id est mundani, *non cognoverunt.* — *Contra:* Ad Romanos primo[^p477-7]: *Qui cum Deum cognovissent, non sicut Deum glorificaverunt* etc.

**Respondeo:** Est *notitia simplex* et *approbationis*[^p477-8]; *simplici notitia* noverunt, *approbationis* non; et ideo cognoscendo nihil meruerunt.

## English
<!-- page 476 -->

#### In the fourth part Christ asks for the same the perpetuity of glory.

43. *Father, those whom thou hast given me.* It is the fourth part of the chapter[^p476-3], in which, for those for whom he asked the conformity of grace, *he asks the perpetuity of glory,* in this order. First is set down *the petition;* second, *the reason for the petition's being heard.*

(Vers. 24.). *The petition* is concerning glory; and because glory is through the vision of the Divinity, which we shall see in the fatherland; therefore he says: *Father, those whom thou hast given me,* namely through predestination, *I will that where I am, they also may be*[^p476-4], through glorious cohabitation; above, the twelfth chapter: « If any man minister to me, let him follow me; and where I am, there let my minister also be ». *Where I am,* that is, where I am presently to be according to the humanity. — Or *where I am* according to the Divinity, that is, let them be with me, who am everywhere. Augustine[^p476-5]: « To be *with* him is a great good; for the wretched can be *where* he is who is everywhere. But the blessed are *with* him, because by always enjoying him they adhere to him ». And that it is to be so understood is plain through what follows: *That they may see my clarity, which thou hast given me.* This is the excellence of the Divinity; therefore he says: *Because thou hast loved me before the creation of the world;* above, the fifth chapter[^p476-6]: « The Father loveth the Son and sheweth him all things ». This clarity the good alone will see; Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter: « Let the wicked be taken away, lest he see the clarity of God ». To this clarity we are now conformed through grace; the second to the Corinthians, the third chapter: « We, beholding the glory of God with open face, are transformed into the same image from clarity to clarity, as by the spirit of the Lord ».

44. (Vers. 25.). *Just Father, the world* etc. Here is touched the second, namely *the reason for the hearing,* why, namely, these ought to see through glory: because they now know through grace; but the wicked do not know now, nor will they know then; therefore he says: *Just Father,* because « thou wilt render to every man according to his works[^p476-7] ». *The world hath not known thee;* above, the first chapter: « That was the true light, which enlighteneth » etc.; therefore I do not pray for the world. *But I have known thee, and these have known that thou hast sent me,* and this by my revealing it; therefore he says:

45. (Vers. 26.). *And I have made known thy name to them;* the Psalm[^p476-8]: « I will declare thy name to my brethren »; Matthew, the eleventh chapter: « No one knoweth the Son but the Father, neither doth any one know the Father but the Son, and he to whom the Son will reveal him ». Nor has he made it known only *through himself,* but he will make it known further *through the Holy Spirit;* therefore he says: *And I will make it known,* and this through the Holy Spirit, who is the love of the Father and of the Son; therefore he says: *That the dilection wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them,* through the donation of the Holy Spirit; to the Romans, the fifth chapter[^p476-9]: « The charity of God is poured forth in our hearts by the Holy Spirit, who is given to us ». *And I in them,* because, the first of John, the fourth chapter[^p476-10], « he that abideth in charity abideth in God, and God in him »; and therefore it is said in the same place:
<!-- page 477 -->
« In this we have known that we abide in him, and he in us, because he hath given us of his Spirit ». And he who has this Spirit and the Son is secure that he has the Father. Hence Augustine[^p477-1] says that « the Father gave his Son as the price of redemption, the Holy Spirit as the pledge of adoption, himself as the inheritance for the adopted ».

### Questions.

46. Quaest. I. But it is asked concerning what he says: *that where I am, they also may be with me.* — *On the contrary:* Above, the fourteenth chapter[^p477-2]: *In my Father's house there are many mansions.*

**I respond:** « In unequal clarity, equal joy[^p477-3] »; by reason of the unequal clarity there is multitude; by reason of the common joy, where are the head and the members.

47. Quaest. II. Likewise, *he inhabiteth light inaccessible,* the first to Timothy, the last chapter[^p477-4]

**I respond** that *inaccessible*[^p477-5] is said either because in no way, or because with difficulty, or because not by human power, or because it is approached by the divine glory alone.

48. Quaest. III. Likewise, excellences corrupt[^p477-6]; but the clarity of God is most excellent.

**I respond:** It is to be understood: where the apprehending is *corporeal,* the apprehended can harm it.

49. Quaest. IV. Likewise it is asked concerning what he says, that *the world,* that is, worldly men, *have not known.* — *On the contrary:* To the Romans, the first chapter[^p477-7]: *Who, when they had known God, did not glorify him as God* etc.

**I respond:** There is a *simple knowledge* and one *of approbation*[^p477-8]; by *simple knowledge* they knew, by that *of approbation* not; and therefore in knowing they merited nothing.
