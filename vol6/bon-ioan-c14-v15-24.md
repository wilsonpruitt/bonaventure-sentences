# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. XIV, Vers. 15-24
## *Supra hortatus est Dominus ad fidem, in hac secunda parte, quia « fides sine operibus mortua est », hortatur ad observationem mandatorum; et hoc quidem facit hoc ordine*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 440 -->

24. *Si diligitis me* etc. Supra hortatus est Dominus ad fidem, in hac secunda parte, quia « fides sine operibus mortua est[^p440-10] », hortatur *ad observationem mandatorum;* et hoc quidem facit hoc ordine. Primo hortatur promittendo *auxilium;* secundo, promittendo *solatium;* tertio, *praemium;* quarto exprimit, quod sit horum *meritum.*

(Vers. 15.). Primo ergo hortatur ad *observantiam mandatorum;* et quia contingit, mandata servari ex timore, ut in Lege, et hoc non quaerit; contingit etiam servari amore, et hoc quaerit: ideo dicit: *Si diligitis me, mandata mea servate,* scilicet amore mei; secunda Ioannis[^p440-11]: « Haec est caritas, ut ambulemus secundum mandata eius ». Talibus *promittit auxilium;* propter quod dicit:
<!-- page 441 -->

25. (Vers. 16.). *Ego rogabo Patrem, et alium Paracletum dabit vobis. Paracletus* dicitur consolator vel advocatus; *alium* dicit, in quo innuit se consolatorem et advocatum; primae Ioannis secundo[^p441-1]: « Advocatum habemus apud Patrem Iesum Christum iustum ». Alium advocatum promittit eis aeternum, quia ipse consolator in praesentia corporali non fuerat nisi ad tempus. *Ut maneat vobiscum in aeternum;* non sic in Saul, de quo primi Regum decimo sexto[^p441-2]: « Spiritus Domini recessit a Saul » etc. Et quis sit iste advocatus, subdit:

26. (Vers. 17.). *Spiritum veritatis,* qui a Patre procedit, dabit, scilicet alium Paracletum *Spiritum veritatis* etc. Hunc Spiritum describit quantum ad *effectum,* unde vocat *Spiritum veritatis,* quia docet vera, sicut spiritus erroris falsa; infra decimo sexto[^p441-3]: « Cum venerit, docebit vos omnem veritatem ». Describit etiam quantum ad *hospitium,* quia non in infidelibus, sed fidelibus, non in animalibus hominibus, sed spiritualibus; propter quod subdit: *Quem mundus non potest accipere.* Et ratio est, quia caecus est per infidelitatem; *quia non videt eum,* cognitione aperta, *nec scit eum,* quacumque intelligentia; Sapientiae primo[^p441-4]: « Spiritus sanctus disciplinae effugiet fictum et auferet se a cogitationibus, quae sunt absque intellectu »; et primae ad Corinthios secundo: « Animalis homo non percipit ea quae Dei sunt ». *Vos autem cognoscetis eum, quia apud vos manebit et in vobis erit;* Apocalypsis secundo[^p441-5]: « Vincenti dabo manna absconditum, et dabo illi calculum candidum, et in calculo nomen scriptum, quod nemo scit, nisi qui accipit ». De hac notitia Sapientiae decimo quinto: « Nosse te consummata iustitia est, et scire iustitiam et virtutem tuam radix est immortalitatis ». Et notandum quod dicit: *Apud vos manebit et in vobis erit,* significans duplicem gratiam Spiritus sancti: *praevenientem* et *subsequentem;* Psalmus[^p441-6]: « Misericordia eius praeveniet me »; et in alio Psalmo: « Et misericordia tua subsequetur me omnibus diebus vitae meae ».

27. (Vers. 18.). *Non relinquam vos orphanos.* Hic secundo discipulis mandata observantibus *promittit solatium* de sua visitatione; propter quod dicit: *Non relinquam vos orphanos,* id est desolatos; *veniam ad vos,* visitando scilicet; Marci ultimo[^p441-7] dixit Angelus: « Praecedet vos in Galilaeam; ibi eum videbitis, sicut dixit vobis ». Et sicut Spiritus sanctus non mittitur nisi ad bonos, sic et Christi visitatio tantum bonis; propterea subdit:

28. (Vers. 19.). *Adhuc modicum, et mundus me iam non videt,* quia post passionem non apparuit mundanis, sed Apostolis; Actuum decimo[^p441-8]: « Dedit eum manifestum fieri non omni populo, sed testibus praeordinatis a Deo ». *Vos autem videbitis*[^p441-9] *me; quia ego vivo,* per vitam gloriae, *et vos vivetis,* per vitam gratiae, quae inchoatur in fide; de qua Habacuc secundo: « Iustus autem meus ex fide vivit ». Et quod hanc vitam fidei perfectae tunc habeant, monstrat:

29. (Vers. 20.). *In illa die vos cognoscetis, quia ego sum in Patre meo;* in quo notatur personarum distinctio et unitas essentiae; quod spectat ad fidem Trinitatis[^p441-10]; *et vos in me, et ego in vobis,* per gratiam, et hoc spectat ad fidem redemptionis. *Vos in me,* sicut *palmites* in vite; infra decimo quinto: « Ego sum vitis, vos palmites; manete in me ». *Et ego in vobis,* sicut *inhabitator* in habitaculo: « Templum Dei sanctum est, quod estis vos », primae ad Corinthios tertio.

30. (Vers. 21.). *Qui habet mandata.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet quia observantibus mandata *promittitur praemium.* Et ratio huius est, quia talis, qui servat mandata, diligitur a Domino, et ideo beatificatur et glorificatur; propter quod dicit: *Qui habet mandata mea et servat ea, ille est, qui diligit me;* quia, sicut dicit Gregorius[^p441-11], « probatio dilectionis exhibitio est operis »; unde in Psalmo: « Iustitia illius in filios filiorum his qui servant testamentum eius et memores sunt mandatorum ipsius ad faciendum ea ». Qui sic servat, ille quidem diligit vere, non verbo tantum, sicut illi, de quibus dicitur
<!-- page 442 -->
primae Ioannis tertio: « Non diligamus verbo neque lingua, sed opere et veritate ». *Qui autem diligit me diligetur a Patre meo,* et etiam a me, *et ego diligam eum;* Proverbiorum octavo[^p442-1]: « Ego diligentes me diligo »; et dilectionem ostendam in praemio; unde: *Et manifestabo ei me ipsum,* et hoc est praemium; unde in Psalmo: « Ostendam illi salutare meum »; Glossa: « Visio est tota merces »; infra decimo septimo: « Haec est vita aeterna, ut cognoscant te solum verum Deum, et quem misisti Iesum Christum ».

31. (Vers. 22.). *Dicit ei Iudas* etc. Hic quarto loco exprimitur, *meritum esse in mandatorum observatione.* Quia enim dixerat, quod manifestare se debebat Apostolis et non mundo; coepit dubitare discipulus, propter quid diceret. Propter quod sequitur: *Dicit ei Iudas, non ille Iscariotes: Domine, quid factum est,* id est, quae causa est huius[^p442-2], *quia te ipsum nobis manifestaturus es et non mundo?* Et respondet ei Dominus, quod *meritum* est in causa, quia illi mandata non observant, in quibus est meritum videndi; propter quod dicit:

32. (Vers. 23.). *Respondit Iesus et dixit ei: Si quis diligit me, sermonem meum servabit;* Lucae sexto[^p442-3]: « Quid vocatis me Domine, Domine, et non facitis quae praecipio vobis »? « Amor Dei non est otiosus; operatur enim magna, si est; si autem operari renuit, amor non est ». Et ex hac observatione meretur Domini manifestationem; unde *et Pater meus diliget eum;* et ex hac dilectione sequitur manifestatio; ideo dicit: *Et ad eum veniemus.* Et si venit Dominus, nullum bonum deest; Sapientiae septimo[^p442-4]: « Venerunt mihi omnia bona pariter cum illa ». *Et mansionem apud eum faciemus,* et hoc merito observantiae mandatorum; unde Gregorius: « Qui Deum vere diligit et mandata eius perfecte custodit, in eo mansionem Dominus facit; quia sic eum Divinitatis amor penetrat, ut ab hoc amore tentationis tempore non recedat ». — Et ideo, supple, manifestabor vobis, qui mandata mea servatis, eo quod me diligitis, et ideo meremini.

33. (Vers. 24.). *Qui non diligit me sermones meos non servat;* et ideo talis non meretur me videre. Talis est mundus et mundani; infra decimo quinto[^p442-5]: « Si mundus vos odit, scitote, quia me priorem vobis odio habuit »; et ideo mandata eius non servat; et ideo se non manifestat mundo. Et ne reputes, quod sermones suos proprios dixerit et non Patris, subdit: *Et sermonem, quem audistis, non est meus,* id est sermo; antiptosis est, in qua ponitur casus pro casu; *sed eius qui misit me Patris;* supra duodecimo[^p442-6]: « Ex me ipso non sum locutus; sed qui misit me Pater, ipse mihi mandatum dedit, quid dicam et quid loquar ».

### Quaestiones.

34. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic primo de hoc quod dicit: *Si diligitis me, mandata mea servate;* et post: *Ego rogabo Patrem, et alium Paracletum dabit vobis.* — Ergo videtur, quod possent diligere et mandata servare, antequam daretur eis Spiritus sanctus. Vel si habent[^p442-7], *quare* dabit et *quomodo?*

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod Spiritum sanctum habebant ad diligendum et mandata servandum; sed eis promittebatur a Domino ad ampliores effectus et maius munus. Unde Augustinus[^p442-8]: « Restat, ut intelligamus, Spiritum sanctum habere qui diligit et habendo mereri, ut plus habeat et plus habendo plus diligat ».

35. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Ego rogabo Patrem.* — Augustinus dicit, de Natura et gratia[^p442-9], quod « nullus orat pro eo quod potest per se facere »: ergo si Filius orat pro datione Spiritus sancti, Filius non potest dare Spiritum sanctum.

Respondendum ad hoc, quod eo modo *rogat,* quo modo *meretur;* et hoc secundum humanam naturam, secundum quam passus et sublimatus sua passione et oratione meruit nobis Spiritus sancti donationem, et secundum illam naturam non poterat *dare,* sed *impetrare*[^p442-10].
<!-- page 443 -->

36. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Alium Paracletum dabit vobis.* — Si enim *Paracletus* dicitur advocatus, et advocatus est mediator; Spiritus vero sanctus non potest esse mediator: ergo nec Paracletus, ergo nec advocatus.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod Spiritus sanctus dicitur *Paracletus* in duplici significatione: et quia *consolator* spe veniae, unde datus est nobis *in pignus hereditatis aeternae*[^p443-1]*;* et etiam, quia *advocatus.* Sed *advocatus* dicitur dupliciter: aut quantum ad *personam,* aut quantum ad *officium. Filius* est advocatus quantum ad *personam* advocati, quia est mediator; sed *Spiritus sanctus* quantum ad *officium.* — Triplex autem advocati est officium, scilicet *postulare,* et hoc habet Spiritus sanctus; ad Romanos octavo[^p443-2]: *Ipse Spiritus postulat pro nobis gemitibus inenarrabilibus.* — *Respondere,* et hoc facit Spiritus sanctus; Matthaei decimo[^p443-3]: *Nolite cogitare, quomodo, aut quid loquamini; dabitur enim vobis in illa hora, quid loquamini; non enim vos estis, qui loquimini, sed Spiritus Patris vestri, qui loquitur in vobis.* — Tertium est adversam partem *arguere,* et hoc Spiritus sancti; infra decimo sexto: *Cum venerit ille, arguet mundum de peccato* etc.

37. Quaest. IV. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit, quod *mundus non cognoscit Spiritum sanctum, vos autem cognoscetis, quia in vobis erit.*

*Sed obiicitur:* quia Spiritus sanctus est in omnibus, tam in bonis quam in malis, cum sit Deus: ergo omnes cognoscunt. — *Si tu dicas,* quod non loquitur de *esse per praesentiam,* sed *per gratiam;* istud est falsum, quia[^p443-4] *nemo scit, utrum amore, an odio dignus sit:* ergo nemo scit, utrum habeat Spiritum sanctum quantum ad effectum gratiae.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod hic loquitur de cognitione Spiritus sancti in suo effectu *manifesto*[^p443-5]; unde loquitur de *esse* eius in nobis, non per *essentiam,* sed per *effectum,* et ideo patet responsio ad primum.

Sed notandum[^p443-6], quod duplex est effectus Spiritus sancti: quidam, qui est in nobis et *nos respicit,* sicut amare, credere et timere, et hos sumus certi nos habere, cum habemus; quidam, qui *respicit aliquid supra nos,* scilicet gratificare, quod est gratum Deo facere, et quantum ad hunc effectum non est nobis notus nisi per specialem revelationem; potest tamen esse notus probabiliter, licet non certitudinaliter[^p443-7].

38. Quaest. V. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicitur de Patre, quod *Pater veniet;* unde: *Ad eum veniemus.*

1. Propter quid potius dicit de se et Patre quam de Spiritu sancto?

2. Et videtur, quod non debeat de Patre dici, quia Pater *mittit* Filium. Quodsi Pater simul veniret cum Filio, qua ratione mitteret Filium, eadem ratione et se ipsum[^p443-8]. — Item, quomodo *venit* qui ubique est et creaturam non assumit[^p443-9]?

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod *venire Dei ad nos,* sicut dicit Augustinus, intelligitur secundum *effectum,* non per *essentiam;* unde Augustinus[^p443-10]: « Veniunt ad nos, dum venimus ad eos; veniunt subveniendo, venimus obediendo; veniunt illuminando, venimus intuendo; veniunt implendo, venimus capiendo ». Quoniam ergo opera Trinitatis sunt indivisa[^p443-11], ideo, quando venit Spiritus sanctus, secundum *veritatem* venit Pater et Filius; tamen secundum *appropriationem* attribuitur Patri, alteri personarum. — *Mittere* vero, praeter hoc quod dicit *effectum,* importat *personalem distinctionem* per auctoritatem et subauctoritatem; et ideo, etsi Pater *veniat* cum Filio, non tamen *mittit* se cum Filio. — De Spiritu sancto non facit mentionem, quia supra[^p443-12] dixerat.

## English
<!-- page 440 -->

24. *If you love me* etc. Above the Lord exhorted to faith; in this second part, because « faith without works is dead[^p440-10] », he exhorts *to the observance of the commandments;* and this indeed he does in this order. First he exhorts by promising *help;* second, by promising *solace;* third, a *reward;* fourth he expresses what is the *merit* of these things.

(Vers. 15.). First, then, he exhorts to the *observance of the commandments;* and because it happens that the commandments are kept out of fear, as in the Law, and this he does not seek; it happens also that they are kept out of love, and this he seeks: therefore he says: *If you love me, keep my commandments,* namely out of love of me; the second of John[^p440-11]: « This is charity, that we walk according to his commandments ». To such he *promises help;* on account of which he says:
<!-- page 441 -->

25. (Vers. 16.). *I will ask the Father, and he shall give you another Paraclete. Paraclete* is said for consoler or advocate; *another* he says, in which he intimates that he himself is a consoler and advocate; the first of John, the second[^p441-1]: « We have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the just ». He promises them another advocate, an eternal one, because he himself as consoler in bodily presence had been so only for a time. *That he may abide with you for ever;* not so in Saul, of whom the first of Kings, the sixteenth[^p441-2]: « The spirit of the Lord departed from Saul » etc. And who this advocate is, he adds:

26. (Vers. 17.). *The Spirit of truth,* who proceeds from the Father, he shall give, namely another Paraclete, *the Spirit of truth* etc. This Spirit he describes as to *effect,* whence he calls him *the Spirit of truth,* because he teaches true things, as the spirit of error false ones; below, the sixteenth[^p441-3]: « When he is come, he will teach you all truth ». He describes him also as to *lodging,* because not in unbelievers, but in the faithful, not in animal men, but in spiritual ones; on account of which he adds: *Whom the world cannot receive.* And the reason is, because it is blind through unbelief; *because it seeth him not,* by open cognition, *nor knoweth him,* by any intelligence whatever; Wisdom, the first[^p441-4]: « The Holy Spirit of discipline will flee from the deceitful, and will withdraw himself from thoughts that are without understanding »; and the first to the Corinthians, the second: « The animal man perceiveth not the things that are of God ». *But you shall know him, because he shall abide with you, and shall be in you;* the Apocalypse, the second[^p441-5]: « To him that overcometh, I will give the hidden manna, and will give him a white counter, and in the counter a name written, which no man knoweth, but he that receiveth it ». Of this knowledge Wisdom, the fifteenth: « To know thee is perfect justice, and to know thy justice and thy power is the root of immortality ». And it is to be noted that he says: *He shall abide with you, and shall be in you,* signifying a twofold grace of the Holy Spirit: *prevenient* and *subsequent;* the Psalm[^p441-6]: « His mercy shall prevent me »; and in another Psalm: « And thy mercy will follow me all the days of my life ».

27. (Vers. 18.). *I will not leave you orphans.* Here second he *promises solace* to the disciples who observe the commandments, from his visitation; on account of which he says: *I will not leave you orphans,* that is, desolate; *I will come to you,* namely by visiting; Mark, the last[^p441-7], the Angel said: « He shall go before you into Galilee; there you shall see him, as he told you ». And as the Holy Spirit is not sent except to the good, so also the visitation of Christ is only for the good; therefore he adds:

28. (Vers. 19.). *Yet a little while, and the world seeth me no more,* because after the passion he did not appear to worldly men, but to the Apostles; the Acts, the tenth[^p441-8]: « He gave him to be made manifest, not to all the people, but to witnesses preordained by God ». *But you shall see*[^p441-9] *me; because I live,* by the life of glory, *and you shall live,* by the life of grace, which is begun in faith; of which Habacuc, the second: « But my just man liveth by faith ». And that they then have this life of perfect faith, he shows:

29. (Vers. 20.). *In that day you shall know, that I am in my Father;* in which is noted the distinction of the persons and the unity of the essence; which pertains to the faith of the Trinity[^p441-10]; *and you in me, and I in you,* by grace, and this pertains to the faith of redemption. *You in me,* as *branches* in the vine; below, the fifteenth: « I am the vine, you the branches; abide in me ». *And I in you,* as an *inhabitant* in a habitation: « The temple of God is holy, which you are », the first to the Corinthians, the third.

30. (Vers. 21.). *He that hath the commandments.* Here the third is touched, namely that to those who observe the commandments a *reward is promised.* And the reason of this is, because such a one, who keeps the commandments, is loved by the Lord, and therefore is beatified and glorified; on account of which he says: *He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them; he it is that loveth me;* because, as Gregory says[^p441-11], « the proof of dilection is the showing of the work »; hence in the Psalm: « His justice unto children's children, to such as keep his covenant, and are mindful of his commandments to do them ». He who keeps them thus, he indeed loves truly, not in word only, as those of whom it is said
<!-- page 442 -->
in the first of John, the third: « Let us not love in word, nor in tongue, but in deed and in truth ». *And he that loveth me, shall be loved of my Father,* and also by me, *and I will love him;* Proverbs, the eighth[^p442-1]: « I love them that love me »; and the dilection I will show in the reward; hence: *And will manifest myself to him,* and this is the reward; hence in the Psalm: « I will show him my salvation »; the Gloss: « Vision is the whole reward »; below, the seventeenth: « This is eternal life, that they may know thee, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent ».

31. (Vers. 22.). *Judas saith to him* etc. Here in the fourth place it is expressed that *the merit is in the observance of the commandments.* For because he had said that he was to manifest himself to the Apostles and not to the world; the disciple began to doubt on account of what he said it. On account of which it follows: *Judas saith to him, not the Iscariot: Lord, what has come about,* that is, what is the cause of this[^p442-2], *that thou wilt manifest thyself to us, and not to the world?* And the Lord responds to him that *merit* is the cause, because those do not observe the commandments, in which is the merit of seeing; on account of which he says:

32. (Vers. 23.). *Jesus answered, and said to him: If any one love me, he will keep my word;* Luke, the sixth[^p442-3]: « Why call you me, Lord, Lord; and do not the things which I command you »? « The love of God is not idle; for it works great things, if it is; but if it refuses to work, it is not love ». And from this observance he merits the manifestation of the Lord; hence *and my Father will love him;* and from this dilection the manifestation follows; therefore he says: *And we will come to him.* And if the Lord comes, no good is lacking; Wisdom, the seventh[^p442-4]: « All good things came to me together with her ». *And will make our mansion with him,* and this by the merit of the observance of the commandments; hence Gregory: « He who truly loves God and perfectly keeps his commandments, in him the Lord makes a mansion; because the love of Divinity so penetrates him, that he does not withdraw from this love in the time of temptation ». — And therefore, supply: I will be manifested to you, who keep my commandments, for this reason, that you love me, and therefore you merit.

33. (Vers. 24.). *He that loveth me not, keepeth not my words;* and therefore such a one does not merit to see me. Such is the world and worldly men; below, the fifteenth[^p442-5]: « If the world hate you, know ye, that it hath hated me before you »; and therefore it does not keep his commandments; and therefore he does not manifest himself to the world. And lest thou count that he spoke his own words and not the Father's, he adds: *And the word* [*sermonem*]*, which you have heard, is not mine,* that is, word [*sermo*]; it is antiptosis, in which one case is put for another; *but the Father's who sent me;* above, the twelfth[^p442-6]: « I have not spoken of myself; but the Father who sent me, he gave me commandment what I should say, and what I should speak ».

### Questions.

34. Quaest. I. But it is asked here first concerning this which he says: *If you love me, keep my commandments;* and after: *I will ask the Father, and he shall give you another Paraclete.* — Therefore it seems that they could love and keep the commandments before the Holy Spirit was given to them. Or if they have him[^p442-7], *why* will he give him and *how?*

**I respond:** It must be said that they had the Holy Spirit for loving and keeping the commandments; but he was promised to them by the Lord for fuller effects and a greater gift. Hence Augustine[^p442-8]: « It remains that we understand that he who loves has the Holy Spirit, and by having him merits to have more, and by having more loves more ».

35. Quaest. II. Likewise it is asked concerning this which he says: *I will ask the Father.* — Augustine says, *On Nature and Grace*[^p442-9], that « no one prays for that which he can do by himself »: therefore if the Son prays for the giving of the Holy Spirit, the Son cannot give the Holy Spirit.

It must be responded to this that he *asks* in that mode in which he *merits;* and this according to the human nature, according to which, having suffered and been exalted, he merited for us by his passion and prayer the donation of the Holy Spirit, and according to that nature he could not *give,* but *obtain*[^p442-10].
<!-- page 443 -->

36. Quaest. III. Likewise it is asked concerning this which he says: *He shall give you another Paraclete.* — For if *Paraclete* is said for advocate, and an advocate is a mediator; but the Holy Spirit cannot be a mediator: therefore neither a Paraclete, therefore neither an advocate.

**I respond:** It must be said that the Holy Spirit is called *Paraclete* in a twofold signification: both because he is a *consoler* by the hope of pardon, whence he is given to us *for a pledge of the eternal inheritance*[^p443-1]*;* and also because he is an *advocate.* But *advocate* is said in two ways: either as to the *person,* or as to the *office. The Son* is an advocate as to the *person* of an advocate, because he is mediator; but *the Holy Spirit* as to the *office.* — Now the office of an advocate is threefold, namely *to ask,* and this the Holy Spirit has; to the Romans, the eighth[^p443-2]: *The Spirit himself asketh for us with unspeakable groanings.* — *To respond,* and this the Holy Spirit does; Matthew, the tenth[^p443-3]: *Take no thought how, or what to speak; for it shall be given you in that hour what to speak; for it is not you that speak, but the Spirit of your Father that speaketh in you.* — The third is *to reprove* the opposing party, and this is of the Holy Spirit; below, the sixteenth: *When he is come, he will reprove the world of sin* etc.

37. Quaest. IV. Likewise it is asked concerning this which he says, that *the world does not know the Holy Spirit, but you shall know, because he shall be in you.*

*But it is objected:* because the Holy Spirit is in all, in the good as well as in the wicked, since he is God: therefore all know him. — *If thou say* that he is not speaking of *being through presence,* but *through grace;* this is false, because[^p443-4] *no one knows whether he be worthy of love or hatred:* therefore no one knows whether he has the Holy Spirit as to the effect of grace.

**I respond:** It must be said that here he is speaking of the cognition of the Holy Spirit in his *manifest*[^p443-5] effect; hence he speaks of his *being* in us, not through *essence,* but through *effect,* and therefore the response to the first is evident.

But it is to be noted[^p443-6] that the effect of the Holy Spirit is twofold: one, which is in us and *regards us,* as to love, to believe and to fear, and these we are certain that we have, when we have them; another, which *regards something above us,* namely to gratify, which is to make pleasing to God, and as to this effect it is not known to us except by special revelation; it can, however, be known probably, although not with certitude[^p443-7].

38. Quaest. V. Likewise it is asked concerning this which is said of the Father, that *the Father will come;* hence: *We will come to him.*

1. On account of what does he speak of himself and the Father rather than of the Holy Spirit?

2. And it seems that it ought not to be said of the Father, because the Father *sends* the Son. But if the Father came together with the Son, by the same reason by which he would send the Son, he would also send himself[^p443-8]. — Likewise, how does he *come* who is everywhere and assumes no creature[^p443-9]?

**I respond:** It must be said that *the coming of God to us,* as Augustine says, is understood according to *effect,* not through *essence;* hence Augustine[^p443-10]: « They come to us, when we come to them; they come by succouring, we come by obeying; they come by enlightening, we come by gazing; they come by filling, we come by taking in ». Since, then, the works of the Trinity are undivided[^p443-11], therefore, when the Holy Spirit comes, according to *truth* the Father and the Son come; yet according to *appropriation* it is attributed to the Father, the other of the persons. — But *to send,* besides the fact that it says an *effect,* imports a *personal distinction* through authority and subauthority; and therefore, although the Father *comes* with the Son, yet he does not *send* himself with the Son. — He makes no mention of the Holy Spirit, because he had spoken of him above[^p443-12].
