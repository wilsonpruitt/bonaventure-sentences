# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. I, Vers. 24-28
## *Iam dixit Ioannes veritatem de se ipso; hic vero testimonium dat de Christo et describit hoc ordine*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 258 -->

53. *Et qui missi fuerant.* Iam dixit Ioannes veritatem de se ipso; hic vero testimonium dat de *Christo* et describit hoc ordine. Primo ponitur *quaerentium improbitas;* secundo, *testimonii veritas;* tertio, *testimonii celebritas.*

(Vers. 24.). Tangitur ergo primo *quaerentium improbitas:* quia, cum audissent veritatem, quaerunt calumniari veritatis doctorem. Et huius ratio erat, quia malitiosi erant; ideo dicit: *Qui missi fuerant erant ex Pharisaeis*, ideo malitiosi et observatores; Chrysostomus[^p258-4]: « Illi missi fuerunt, qui veritatem nescierunt quaerere, sed invidere »; ideo malitiose et improbe quaerunt. Ideo dicit:

54. (Vers. 25.). *Et interrogaverunt eum et dixerunt ei*, quasi ipsum redarguendo: *Quid ergo baptizas, si tu non es Christus neque Elias neque propheta?* Sine auctoritate facis et stulte. Unde Chrysostomus[^p258-5]: « Quia non valuerunt eum blanditiis supplantare, accusationes ei immittere tentant ». Unde exclamat Chrysostomus: « O de dementia! o de elatione! o de intemperanti investigatione! Missi estis ab eo edocendi: *Tu quis es?* Et crimen legis vultis imponere »? In hoc ergo innuitur quaerentium improbitas.

55. (Vers. 26.). *Respondit eis Ioannes.* Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *testimonii veritas.* Ioannes enim veritatem sui officii exprimit et ex hoc dignitatem Christi. Ideo dicit: *Ego baptizo vos in aqua;* id est, lavo exterius, non tanquam Christus interius; sed ad Christum praeparo; unde Actuum decimo nono[^p258-6]: « Ioannes baptizavit baptismo poenitentiae dicens: In eum, qui venturus erat, ut crederent ». Ideo subdit: *Medius autem vestrum stetit quem vos nescitis;* id est inter vos; *quem nescitis*, secundum quod Dominus infra octavo[^p258-7] dicit: « Neque me scitis neque Patrem meum scitis ». Ad illum ego praeparo. Ideo dicit:

56. (Vers. 27.). *Ipse est qui post me venturus est*, quia ego illum praecedo ut praecursor. *Qui ante me factus est*, id est dignior me; infra tertio[^p258-8]: « Me oportet minui, illum autem crescere ». Et quanto dignior sit, ostendit: *Cuius ego non sum dignus, ut solvam corrigiam calceamenti.* Ad *litteram* hoc verum est, quia nullus quantumcumque bonus debet se dignum reputare, ut etiam Christum discalceet; unde dicebat ille centurio Matthaei octavo[^p258-9]: « Domine, non sum dignus, ut intres sub tectum meum ». — *Allegorice* vero *calceamentum* est humanitas Christi; *pes calceatus*, Divinitas humanata; *corrigia calceamenti*, unio naturarum. *Solvere* ergo *corrigiam calceamenti* est explicare mysterium unionis, ad quod indignum se Ioannes dicit, quia Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio[^p258-10]: « Generationem eius quis enarrabit »? quasi dicat: nemo dignus.

57. (Vers. 28.). *Haec in Bethania*[^p258-11] *facta sunt.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet *testimonii celebritas*, quae notatur in hoc, quod in loco communi hoc testimonium dedit, ubi plures concurrebant. Ideo dicit: *Haec in Bethania facta sunt trans Iordanem, ubi
<!-- page 259 -->
erat Ioannes baptizans;* et ita multus populus concurrebat; infra tertio[^p259-1]: « Erat autem Ioannes baptizans in Aennon iuxta Salim, quia aquae multae erant illic ».

### Quaestiones.

58. Quaest. I. Quaestio parvula hic incidit de hoc quod dicit: *Ego baptizo in aqua.* — 1. Si enim baptismus Ioannis solum exterius carnem lavabat, videtur, quod frustra et inutiliter fiebat.

2. Item, Ioannes Lege perfectior est, ergo baptismus Ioannis Sacramentis Legis: si igitur circumcisio dat gratiam, multo[^p259-2] magis baptismus Ioannis.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod baptismus Ioannis non fuit in *remedium*, sed in *praeparationem ad remedium:* ideo non sequitur ex hoc, quod sit superfluum; nec sequitur, quod gratiam conferre debeat[^p259-3].

Baptismus autem ille *praeparabat* ad remedium tripliciter: propter *vitae emendationem;* unde dicitur Matthaei tertio[^p259-4]: « Baptizabantur ab eo confitentes peccata sua ». Propter *assuefactionem*, ut essent idonei et non respuerent baptismum Christi; unde Gregorius[^p259-5]: « Decens erat, ut qui nasciturum nascendo praevenerat, baptizaturum quoque Dominum baptizando praeveniret ». Tertia vero ratio, propter *Christi manifestationem;* infra eodem[^p259-6]: *Ut manifestaretur in Israel, propterea veni ego in aqua baptizans.*

59. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit: *Medius vestrum.* Unde est hoc, quod Dominus semper locum *medium* eligit, cum sit *caput?* — Et videtur, quod non esset in medio eorum; non enim est nisi in medio bonorum[^p259-7].

Dicendum, quod secundum *carnem* erat medius eorum, id est in medio tanquam homo mediocris. Dicitur autem Christus *stare in medio*, vel etiam locum medium specialiter eligere, quia *medium* est locus *humilitatis;* Lucae vigesimo secundo[^p259-8]: « Ego autem in medio vestrum sum, sicut qui ministrat », et Matthaei decimo octavo. — Locus *communitatis*, quia uniformiter se habet ad omnes; Actuum decimo[^p259-9]: « In veritate comperi, quia non est personarum acceptor Deus » etc. — Locus *unitatis*, quia extrema uniuntur in medio; ad Ephesios secundo[^p259-10]: « Ipse est pax nostra, qui fecit utraque unum ». — Locus *stabilitatis*, quia medium mundi est fixum; primae ad Corinthios tertio[^p259-11]: « Fundamentum aliud nemo potest ponere, praeter id quod est Christus Iesus ». — Locus *proximitatis*, quia omnibus partibus approximat; Actuum decimo septimo[^p259-12]: « Si forte attrectent eum aut inveniant, quamvis non sit longe ab unoquoque nostrum; in ipso enim vivimus et movemur et sumus ».

60. Quaest. III. Item quaeritur de illo verbo, quod dicit ipse: *Qui post me venturus est.*

**Contra:** Matthaei undecimo[^p259-13] dicitur, quod misit duos ex discipulis suis dicens ei: « Tu es, qui venturus es, an alium exspectamus »? Ibi dubitavit, quomodo hic asserit?

Ad hoc dicunt haeretici, quod Ioannes primo credidit, sed postmodum dubitavit. Sed mentiuntur, quia Dominus tunc commendavit Ioannem[^p259-14]. — Propter hoc dicendum, quod Ioannes certitudinaliter sciebat, ipsum esse Christum; nec tunc interrogavit per discipulos tanquam dubitans, sed ut dubitationi discipulorum suorum consuleret, qui in eum non credebant[^p259-15].

## English
<!-- page 258 -->

53. *And they that were sent.* John has already told the truth about himself; but here he gives testimony about *Christ*, and he describes it in this order. First is set down the *wickedness of those asking;* second, the *truth of the testimony;* third, the *renown of the testimony.*

(Vers. 24.). First, then, is touched the *wickedness of those asking:* because, when they had heard the truth, they seek to calumniate the doctor of truth. And the reason for this was that they were malicious; therefore he says: *They that were sent were of the Pharisees*, therefore malicious and watchers; Chrysostom[^p258-4]: « Those were sent who knew not how to seek the truth, but to envy it »; therefore they ask maliciously and wickedly. Therefore he says:

54. (Vers. 25.). *And they asked him, and said to him*, as if reproving him: *Why then dost thou baptize, if thou be not Christ, nor Elias, nor a prophet?* Thou doest it without authority, and foolishly. Whence Chrysostom[^p258-5]: « Because they could not supplant him with flatteries, they try to cast accusations upon him ». Whence Chrysostom exclaims: « O the madness! O the pride! O the intemperate inquisitiveness! You were sent to be taught by him: *Who art thou?* And you wish to lay a charge of the law upon him »? In this, then, the wickedness of those asking is intimated.

55. (Vers. 26.). *John answered them.* Here is touched the second, namely the *truth of the testimony.* For John expresses the truth of his own office, and from this the dignity of Christ. Therefore he says: *I baptize you in water;* that is, I wash outwardly, not, as Christ does, inwardly; but I prepare for Christ; whence Acts, the nineteenth[^p258-6]: « John baptized with the baptism of penance, saying: In him who was to come, that they should believe ». Therefore he adds: *But there hath stood one in the midst of you, whom you know not;* that is, among you; *whom you know not*, according as the Lord says below in the eighth[^p258-7]: « Neither me do you know, nor my Father do you know ». For him I prepare. Therefore he says:

56. (Vers. 27.). *The same is he that shall come after me*, because I go before him as forerunner. *Who was made before me*, that is, more worthy than I; below in the third[^p258-8]: « I must decrease, but he must increase ». And how much more worthy he is, he shows: *The latchet of whose shoe I am not worthy to loose.* To the *letter* this is true, because no one, however good, ought to repute himself worthy even to unshoe Christ; whence that centurion said, Matthew, the eighth[^p258-9]: « Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldst enter under my roof ». — But *allegorically* the *shoe* is the humanity of Christ; the *shod foot*, the humanized Divinity; the *latchet of the shoe*, the union of the natures. *To loose*, then, *the latchet of the shoe* is to unfold the mystery of the union, for which John says he is unworthy, because Isaiah, the fifty-third[^p258-10]: « Who shall declare his generation »? as if to say: no one is worthy.

57. (Vers. 28.). *These things were done in Bethania*[^p258-11]. Here is touched the third, namely the *renown of the testimony*, which is noted in this, that he gave this testimony in a common place, where many flocked together. Therefore he says: *These things were done in Bethania, beyond the Jordan,
<!-- page 259 -->
where John was baptizing;* and so a great multitude flocked together; below in the third[^p259-1]: « And John was baptizing in Ennon near Salim, because there was much water there ».

### Questions.

58. Quaest. I. A small question arises here about what he says: *I baptize in water.* — 1. For if John's baptism only washed the flesh outwardly, it seems that it was done in vain and uselessly.

2. Likewise, John is more perfect than the Law; therefore John's baptism is more perfect than the Sacraments of the Law: if then circumcision gives grace, much more[^p259-2] does John's baptism.

**I respond:** It must be said that John's baptism was not for a *remedy*, but for a *preparation for the remedy:* therefore it does not follow from this that it is superfluous; nor does it follow that it ought to confer grace[^p259-3].

Now that baptism *prepared* for the remedy in three ways: on account of the *amendment of life;* whence it is said, Matthew, the third[^p259-4]: « They were baptized by him, confessing their sins ». On account of *accustoming*, that they might be fit and might not reject the baptism of Christ; whence Gregory[^p259-5]: « It was fitting that he who by being born had gone before the one who was to be born should also, by baptizing, go before the Lord who was to baptize ». But the third reason, on account of the *manifestation of Christ;* below in the same[^p259-6]: *That he may be made manifest in Israel, therefore am I come baptizing in water.*

59. Quaest. II. Likewise it is asked about what he says: *In the midst of you.* Whence is it that the Lord always chooses the *middle* place, since he is the *head?* — And it seems that he was not in the midst of them; for he is only in the midst of the good[^p259-7].

It must be said that according to the *flesh* he was in the midst of them, that is, in the middle as a man of middling estate. But Christ is said to *stand in the midst*, or also to choose the middle place specially, because the *middle* is the place of *humility;* Luke, the twenty-second[^p259-8]: « But I am in the midst of you, as he that serveth », and Matthew, the eighteenth. — The place of *commonness*, because it stands uniformly toward all; Acts, the tenth[^p259-9]: « In very deed I perceive that God is not a respecter of persons » etc. — The place of *unity*, because the extremes are united in the middle; to the Ephesians, the second[^p259-10]: « He is our peace, who hath made both one ». — The place of *stability*, because the middle of the world is fixed; the first to the Corinthians, the third[^p259-11]: « Other foundation no man can lay, but that which is Christ Jesus ». — The place of *proximity*, because it draws near to all parts; Acts, the seventeenth[^p259-12]: « If happily they may feel after him or find him, although he be not far from every one of us; for in him we live, and move, and are ».

60. Quaest. III. Likewise it is asked about that word which he himself says: *He that shall come after me.*

**On the contrary:** In Matthew, the eleventh[^p259-13], it is said that he sent two of his disciples, saying to him: « Art thou he that art to come, or look we for another »? There he doubted; how does he assert it here?

To this the heretics say that John at first believed, but afterwards doubted. But they lie, because the Lord then commended John[^p259-14]. — On account of this it must be said that John knew with certainty that he was the Christ; nor did he then ask through his disciples as one doubting, but in order to provide for the doubt of his disciples, who did not believe in him[^p259-15].

[^tr-doctrina]: **En.** Translator's note: the Latin *doctrina* holds together what English divides among doctrine, teaching and learning. Here Gregory says of the Pharisees' envoys that they *doctrinam nesciunt quaerere, sed invidere*, set beside Chrysostom's *veritatem nescierunt quaerere* in the body; it names what John offered them to receive, so the English takes "teaching".
