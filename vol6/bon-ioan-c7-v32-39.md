# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. VII, Vers. 32-39
## *Confutavit Dominus malignantes in corde, mentientes in sermone; hic confutat persequentes in opere per potentiam*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 348 -->

47. *Audierunt autem*[^p348-8] *Pharisaei* etc. Confutavit Dominus malignantes in corde, mentientes in sermone; hic confutat *persequentes in opere* per potentiam. Et dividitur haec pars, quia primo ponitur *persequentium confutatio;* secundo, *Iudaicae machinationis destructio,* ibi[^p348-9]: *Ex illa ergo turba* etc.

*Confutationem persequentium* hoc ordine describit Evangelista. Nam primo ponitur *insidiarum praeparatio;* secundo, *ipsorum* per doctrinam *confutatio;* tertio, *confutatorum dubitatio;* quarto, non intelligentium *dubitantium*[^p348-10] *ad fidem vocatio.*

(Vers. 32.). Primo igitur ponitur *insidiarum praeparatio,* quae facta est a Pharisaeis et sacerdotibus propter invidiam, quia turba videbatur velle in eum credere. Propterea dicit: *Audierunt Pharisaei turbam murmurantem de illo haec,* quia palam loqui non audebat. *Et miserunt principes et Pharisaei ministros, ut apprehenderent Iesum*[^p348-11]. Pharisaei *audierunt,* quia ipsi erant observatores; sed ipsi et principes *miserunt,* quia erant potentiores. Simile dicitur Matthaei vigesimo secundo[^p348-12]: « Abeuntes Pharisaei consilium fecerunt, ut caperent Iesum in sermone; et mittunt ei discipulos suos cum Herodianis »; Lucae undecimo: « Coeperunt Pharisaei graviter insistere et os eius opprimere de multis, insidiantes ei ».

48. (Vers. 33.). *Dixit ergo eis Iesus: Adhuc modicum* etc. Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *ministrorum persequentium confutatio,* qui armata manu venerant capere eum; et confutat eos, ostendendo eorum impotentiam dupliciter: primo, quia eum modo non possent *capere;* secundo, quia eum post modicum tempus non possent *invenire;* propter hoc dicit: *Adhuc modicum tempus vobiscum sum,* scilicet vivens vita mortali; vel: *vobiscum sum,* per mortalis vitae conversationem; Lucae ultimo[^p348-13]: « Haec sunt verba, quae locutus sum vobis, cum adhuc essem vobiscum ». *Et vado ad Patrem*[^p348-14], *qui me misit,* scilicet *vado* per passionem, infra decimo sexto; ideo me modo capere non potestis, quia non est adhuc tempus; infra decimo: « Ego pono animam meam, ut iterum sumam eam; et nemo tollit eam a me »; et sic modo non potestis me *comprehendere* nec postmodum *invenire.* Ideo subdit:

49. (Vers. 34.). *Quaeretis me et non invenietis;* quaeretis meum subsidium in vastatione Romanorum facta sub Tito et Vespasiano[^p348-15]. — Vel: *Quaeretis me;* per compunctionem et dolorem, *et non invenietis,* quia multi inciderunt in desperationem; tamen aliqui poenitentes quaesierunt et non invenerunt corporaliter, sed spiritualiter; Actuum secundo[^p348-16]: « His auditis, compuncti sunt corde ». — Vel: *Quaeretis me et non invenietis,* in iudicio; Proverbiorum
<!-- page 349 -->
primo[^p349-1]: « Mane consurgent et non invenient me, eo quod exosam habuerunt sapientiam ». — Vel aliter: *Quaeretis me,* voluntate nocendi; *et non invenietis,* potestate offendendi; quia dicetur ei post resurrectionem: « Sede a dextris meis, donec ponam inimicos tuos scabellum pedum tuorum[^p349-2] ». Et ideo subdit, quod nocere non poterunt. *Et ubi sum ego, vos non potestis venire;* ideo non poteritis invenire, quia in caelo erat futurus; infra decimo quarto[^p349-3]: « Vado parare vobis locum, et iterum veniam et accipiam vos ad me ipsum, ut, ubi sum ego, et vos sitis ».

50. (Vers. 35.). *Dixerunt ergo Iudaei* etc. Hic tangitur tertium, scilicet consequens *Iudaeorum dubitatio* ex hoc, quod ipsum non intelligebant; unde dicunt: *Quo hic iturus est, quia non inveniemus eum?* Et colloquendo quaerunt de loco: *Nunquid in dispersionem gentium iturus est et docturus gentes?* « Hic dubitando, ut dicit Augustinus[^p349-4], prophetaverunt. Iturus enim erat ad gentes, non per praesentiam sui corporis, sed per Apostolos; Psalmus: Populus, quem non cognovi, servivit mihi, in auditu auris obedivit mihi ». Et quia multum dubitabant, ideo iterant dubitationem.

51. (Vers. 36.). *Quis est iste*[^p349-5] *sermo, quem dixit: Quaeretis me et non invenietis; et ubi sum ego, vos non potestis venire?* Ideo non merentur intelligere, quia noluerunt ad credendum se humiliare.

52. (Vers. 37.). *In novissimo autem die* etc. Tangitur quartum, scilicet *ad fidem invitatio,* cuius merito datur sapientia et intelligentia. Propterea dicit: *In novissimo autem die magni festi*[^p349-6]; hic erat octavus et ultimus, qui erat solemnis; *stabat Iesus et clamabat. Stabat,* per *immobilitatem;* Malachiae tertio[^p349-7]: « Ego Dominus et non mutor »; *clamabat,* per *caritatem;* Psalmus: « Laboravi clamans », ut excitaret ad veniendum: *Veniat et bibat,* secundum meam *largitatem. Si quis sitit, veniat et bibat;* Isaiae quinquagesimo quinto: « Omnes sitientes venite ad aquas; venite, emite absque argento et absque ulla commutatione vinum et lac ». Et modus adveniendi describitur, quia est per *fidem;* unde dicit:

53. (Vers. 38.). *Qui credit in me, sicut dicit Scriptura, flumina de ventre eius fluent aquae vivae.*

54. Quaerit Chrysostomus[^p349-8], ubi hoc dicat; et dicit, quod « nusquam hoc dicit », ad litteram, sed ad intellectum; Isaiae quinquagesimo octavo: « Eris quasi hortus irriguus et quasi fons aquarum, cuius non deficient aquae »; Proverbiorum quinto[^p349-9] secundum aliam translationem: « Deriventur fontes tui foras, et in plateis aquas tuas divide ». Et dicit *flumina,* quia *flumen* dicit abundantiam et impetum, et pluraliter dicit. Unde Chrysostomus[^p349-10]: « Videbis manifeste impletum quod dicitur, si ad Stephani sapientiam, si ad Petri linguam, si ad Pauli cursum inspexeris; nihil enim eis resistebat, nec plebis furor nec tyrannorum insurrectio nec daemonum insidiae nec mortes quotidianae; sed sicut flumina omnia secum trahentes abibant ». Et quia hoc spiritualiter, non carnaliter erat dictum, ideo exponit Evangelista:
<!-- page 350 -->

55. (Vers. 39.). *Hoc autem dixit de Spiritu, quem accepturi erant credentes in eum;* de quo Actuum secundo[^p350-1]: « Factus est repente de caelo sonus tanquam advenientis spiritus vehementis ». Hunc accepturi erant, sed nondum acceperant. Propterea dicit: *Nondum enim Spiritus erat datus, quia Iesus nondum erat glorificatus;* infra decimo sexto: « Si abiero, mittam vobis eum ».

### Quaestiones.

56. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur de hoc quod dicitur hic, quod *flumina de ventre* credentium *fluent aquae vivae.* Dicit[^p350-2] enim, quod hoc intelligitur de Spiritu sancto.

1. Sed Spiritus sanctus solum a Deo Patre Filioque procedit: non ergo fluit de ventre credentium.

2. Item, vir sanctus habens Spiritum non potest dare Spiritum sanctum, sed a Deo datur tantum[^p350-3]: non ergo fluit de ventre etc.

3. Item, quid nomine *ventris* intelligitur, propter quod dicit, quod *fluent de ventre?*

Respondet Chrysostomus[^p350-4], quod *flumina Spiritus sancti* dicuntur eius dona et gratiae. Gratia autem recte dicitur *flumen fluens,* non quia ab uno fluat in alterum, sed quia, cum mentem intraverit, omni fonte magis manat nec deficit nec stat, sed *fluit* et *refluit. Fluit* a Deo et facit hominem *refluere* in Deum, iuxta proprietatem aquae, quae tantum ascendit in fluxu, quantum descendit. Propterea scribitur supra quarto[^p350-5]: *Fiet in eo fons aquae salientis in vitam aeternam;* ideo Ecclesiastae primo: *Ad locum, unde exeunt flumina, revertuntur, ut iterum fluant.*

3. Quod quaeritur, quid per *ventrem* intelligatur; respondet Chrysostomus[^p350-6], quod *venter* cor dicitur, iuxta illud Psalmi: *Legem tuam in medio ventris mei.* Magis tamen dixit *ventrem,* ut significaret abundantiam gratiarum. — Glossa[^p350-7] vero exponit illud de fluxu doctrinae et consilii de ventre bonae conscientiae et piae, secundum illud: *Venter meus ad Moab quasi cithara sonabit.*

57. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de hoc quod dicit, quod *Spiritus sanctus nondum erat datus.*

**Contra:** 1. Prophetae prophetaverunt vera, sed non nisi per Spiritum sanctum: ergo habuerunt Spiritum sanctum; unde David[^p350-8]: *Spiritum sanctum tuum ne auferas a me.*

2. Item, Apostoli faciebant miracula; sed Hieronymus[^p350-9] dicit, quod « non potuerunt miracula facere sine Spiritus sancti gratia ».

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod datio Spiritus sancti triplex est: *manifesta, manifestior, manifestissima*[^p350-10]. *Manifesta* fuit in prophetantibus et miracula facientibus ante passionem; *manifestior* fuit in insufflatione post resurrectionem, quia in signo visibili, ut flatu, datus est; sed *manifestissima,* post ascensionem, quia in signo visibili et audibili; et secundum quod manifestius datus est, sic et abundantius.

Quod ergo dicitur, quod *nondum erat datus;* intelligendum est: in evidentia et abundantia[^p350-11].

## English
<!-- page 348 -->

47. *But the Pharisees heard*[^p348-8] etc. The Lord refuted the malicious in heart, those lying in speech; here he refutes *those persecuting in deed* through power. And this part is divided, because first is set down *the refutation of the persecutors;* second, *the destruction of the Jewish machination,* at the place[^p348-9]: *Of that multitude therefore* etc.

*The refutation of the persecutors* the Evangelist describes in this order. For first is set down *the preparation of snares;* second, *their refutation* through teaching[^tr-doctrina]; third, *the doubting of the refuted;* fourth, the *calling to faith* of those not understanding, *the doubting*[^p348-10].

(Verse 32.). First, then, is set down *the preparation of snares,* which was made by the Pharisees and priests on account of envy, because the multitude seemed to wish to believe in him. On account of which he says: *The Pharisees heard the people murmuring these things concerning him,* because it did not dare to speak openly. *And the rulers and Pharisees sent ministers to apprehend Jesus*[^p348-11]. The Pharisees *heard,* because they themselves were the watchers; but they and the rulers *sent,* because they were the more powerful. The like is said in Matthew, the twenty-second[^p348-12]: « The Pharisees going, took counsel how to ensnare Jesus in his speech; and they send to him their disciples with the Herodians »; in Luke, the eleventh: « The Pharisees began violently to urge him, and to oppress his mouth about many things, lying in wait for him ».

48. (Verse 33.). *Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little* etc. Here the second is touched upon, namely, *the refutation of the persecuting ministers,* who had come with armed hand to take him; and he refutes them, showing their powerlessness in two ways: first, because they could not *take* him now; second, because after a little time they could not *find* him; on account of this he says: *Yet a little time I am with you,* namely, living a mortal life; or: *I am with you,* by the converse of mortal life; Luke, the last[^p348-13]: « These are the words which I spoke to you, while I was yet with you ». *And I go to the Father*[^p348-14], *who sent me,* namely, *I go* through the passion, below, in the sixteenth; therefore you cannot take me now, because it is not yet the time; below, in the tenth: « I lay down my life, that I may take it again; and no man taketh it away from me »; and so you cannot *lay hold of* me now, nor *find* me afterwards. Therefore he adds:

49. (Verse 34.). *You shall seek me, and shall not find me;* you shall seek my help in the devastation by the Romans made under Titus and Vespasian[^p348-15]. — Or: *You shall seek me;* through compunction and sorrow, *and shall not find me,* because many fell into despair; yet some, repenting, sought and did not find him bodily, but spiritually; Acts, the second[^p348-16]: « When they had heard these things, they had compunction in heart ». — Or: *You shall seek me, and shall not find me,* in the judgment; Proverbs,
<!-- page 349 -->
the first[^p349-1]: « They shall rise in the morning, and shall not find me, because they have hated wisdom ». — Or otherwise: *You shall seek me,* by the will to harm; *and shall not find me,* by the power to injure; because it will be said to him after the resurrection: « Sit at my right hand, until I make thy enemies the footstool of thy feet[^p349-2] ». And therefore he adds that they would not be able to harm. *And where I am, you cannot come;* therefore you will not be able to find me, because he was to be in heaven; below, in the fourteenth[^p349-3]: « I go to prepare a place for you, and I will come again and will take you to myself, that where I am, you also may be ».

50. (Verse 35.). *The Jews therefore said* etc. Here the third is touched upon, namely, the consequent *doubting of the Jews,* from this, that they did not understand him; whence they say: *Whither will this man go, that we shall not find him?* And conferring together they ask about the place: *Will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?* « In doubting this, as Augustine says[^p349-4], they prophesied. For he was to go to the Gentiles, not by the presence of his body, but by the Apostles; the Psalm: A people, which I knew not, hath served me; at the hearing of the ear they have obeyed me ». And because they doubted much, therefore they repeat the doubt.

51. (Verse 36.). *What is this*[^p349-5] *saying that he hath said: You shall seek me, and shall not find me; and where I am, you cannot come?* Therefore they do not merit to understand, because they would not humble themselves to believing.

52. (Verse 37.). *And on the last day* etc. The fourth is touched upon, namely, *the invitation to faith,* by whose merit wisdom and intelligence are given. On account of which he says: *And on the last day of the great feast*[^p349-6]; this was the eighth and the last, which was solemn; *Jesus stood and cried. He stood,* through *immobility;* Malachias, the third[^p349-7]: « I am the Lord, and I change not »; *he cried,* through *charity;* the Psalm: « I have laboured with crying », so as to rouse them to coming: *Let him come and drink,* according to my *largess. If any man thirst, let him come and drink;* Isaias, the fifty-fifth: « All you that thirst, come to the waters; come, buy without money and without any exchange wine and milk ». And the manner of coming is described, because it is by *faith;* whence he says:

53. (Verse 38.). *He that believeth in me, as the Scripture saith, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.*

54. Chrysostom asks[^p349-8] where it says this; and he says that « it nowhere says this » to the letter, but to the understanding; Isaias, the fifty-eighth: « Thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a fountain of waters, whose waters shall not fail »; Proverbs, the fifth[^p349-9], according to another translation: « Let thy fountains be conveyed abroad, and in the streets divide thy waters ». And he says *rivers,* because *river* says abundance and impetus, and he says it in the plural. Whence Chrysostom[^p349-10]: « Thou shalt see what is said manifestly fulfilled, if thou look to the wisdom of Stephen, to the tongue of Peter, to the course of Paul; for nothing withstood them, neither the fury of the populace nor the insurrection of tyrants nor the snares of demons nor daily deaths; but like rivers they went away drawing all things with them ». And because this was said spiritually, not carnally, therefore the Evangelist expounds it:
<!-- page 350 -->

55. (Verse 39.). *Now this he said of the Spirit, which they who believed in him should receive;* of whom it is said in Acts, the second[^p350-1]: « There came suddenly from heaven a sound, as of a mighty wind coming ». This they were to receive, but they had not yet received. On account of which he says: *For as yet the Spirit was not given, because Jesus was not yet glorified;* below, in the sixteenth: « If I go, I will send him to you ».

### Questions.

56. Question I. But it is asked about this, that it is said here that *rivers of living water shall flow out of the belly* of believers. For he says[^p350-2] that this is understood of the Holy Spirit.

1. But the Holy Spirit proceeds only from God the Father and the Son: therefore he does not flow out of the belly of believers.

2. Likewise, a holy man having the Spirit cannot give the Holy Spirit, but he is given by God alone[^p350-3]: therefore he does not flow out of the belly etc.

3. Likewise, what is understood by the name of *belly,* on account of which he says that *they shall flow out of the belly?*

Chrysostom responds[^p350-4] that *the rivers of the Holy Spirit* are called his gifts and graces. And grace is rightly called *a flowing river,* not because it flows from one into another, but because, when it has entered the mind, it wells up more than any fountain, and neither fails nor stands still, but *flows* and *flows back. It flows* from God and makes man *flow back* into God, according to the property of water, which ascends as much in its flowing as it descends. On account of this it is written above, in the fourth[^p350-5]: *There shall be in him a fountain of water springing up into life everlasting;* therefore in Ecclesiastes, the first: *Unto the place from whence the rivers come, they return, to flow again.*

3. As to what is asked, what is understood by *belly:* Chrysostom responds[^p350-6] that the *belly* is called the heart, according to that of the Psalm: *Thy law in the midst of my belly.* Yet he said *belly* rather, to signify the abundance of graces. — The Gloss[^p350-7], however, expounds it of the flowing of teaching and counsel from the belly of a good and pious conscience, according to that: *My belly shall sound like a harp for Moab.*

57. Question II. Likewise it is asked about this, that he says that *the Holy Spirit was not yet given.*

**On the contrary:** 1. The Prophets prophesied true things, but not except through the Holy Spirit: therefore they had the Holy Spirit; whence David[^p350-8]: *Take not thy Holy Spirit from me.*

2. Likewise, the Apostles worked miracles; but Jerome[^p350-9] says that « they could not work miracles without the grace of the Holy Spirit ».

**I respond:** It must be said that the giving of the Holy Spirit is threefold: *manifest, more manifest, most manifest*[^p350-10]. It was *manifest* in those prophesying and working miracles before the passion; *more manifest* in the breathing upon them after the resurrection, because he was given in a visible sign, as by breath; but *most manifest* after the ascension, because in a sign visible and audible; and according as he was given more manifestly, so also more abundantly.

What, then, is said, that *he was not yet given;* is to be understood: in evidence and abundance[^p350-11].
