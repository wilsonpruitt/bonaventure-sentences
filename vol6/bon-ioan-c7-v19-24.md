# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Ioannis — Cap. VII, Vers. 19-24
## *Pars secunda agit de doctrinae communicatione dupliciter*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 344 -->

#### Pars secunda agit de doctrinae communicatione dupliciter.

27. *Nonne Moyses dedit* etc. Haec est secunda pars, in qua agitur de *doctrinae communicatione.*
<!-- page 345 -->
Et quia duplex « est opus sapientis: manifestare mentientes et non mentiri de quibus novit[^p345-1] »; ideo primo confutat *Iudaeorum perversitatem;* secundo *doctrinae suae manifestat nobilitatem,* infra octavo[^p345-2]: *Iterum locutus est eis Iesus: Ego sum lux mundi.*

Prima pars habet quatuor partes. Primo enim confutat *malignantes in corde;* secundo vero, *obloquentes in ore,* ibi[^p345-3]: *Dicebant ergo quidam ex Ierosolymis;* tertio, *insurgentes per potentiam,* ibi: *Audierunt ergo Pharisaei;* quarto, *insidiantes per malitiam,* ibi: *Iesus autem perrexit,* in principio octavi.

*Malignantes* igitur confutat hoc ordine. Primo ponitur ipsa *redargutio;* secundo, *Iudaeorum recalcitratio;* tertio, *redargutionis explicatio;* quarto, *a malo revocatio.*

(Vers. 19.). *Redarguit* ergo eos Dominus de occulta cordis malitia, quod ipsum occidere volebant contra Legis mandatum; propterea dicit: *Nonne Moyses dedit vobis Legem?* quam observare tenemini per vestram promissionem, secundum illud Exodi vigesimo quarto[^p345-4]: « Cuncta, quae praecepit nobis Dominus, faciemus ». *Et nemo ex vobis facit Legem?* Psalmus: « Omnes declinaverunt, simul inutiles facti sunt; non est qui faciat bonum, non est usque ad unum »; quia iam *corde* eam praevaricati estis; quod ostendit revelans cordis malitiam.

28. (Vers. 20.). *Quid me quaeritis interficere?* cum Lex praecipiat in decalogo: « Non occides », Exodi vigesimo[^p345-5] et Deuteronomii quinto; et hoc innocentem. Propterea quaerit: *Quid me quaeritis interficere? Quid?* id est propter quam causam, quasi dicat: sine causa; Exodi vigesimo tertio[^p345-6]: « Innocentem et iustum non interficies ».

*Respondit turba.* Tangitur hic secundum, scilicet *Iudaeorum recalcitratio,* quia crimen non agnoscunt, sed arguenti conviciantur. Propterea dicitur: *Respondit turba,* non unus, sed tota multitudo, *et dixit: Daemonium habes.* Hoc ei obiecerunt Matthaei duodecimo[^p345-7] et infra octavo; ecce, convicium. Chrysostomus[^p345-8]: « Irae verbum est et furoris et animae inverecundantis ». *Quis te quaerit interficere?* Quasi dicant: nullus. Mentiuntur, quia ipsi habent daemonium et filii sunt diaboli, qui « mendax est et pater eius ».

29. (Vers. 21.). *Respondit Iesus et dixit eis.* Tangitur hic tertium, scilicet *redargutionis explicatio,* quia propter hoc, quod sanaverat in Sabbato, volebant eum occidere; unde dicit: *Unum opus feci, et omnes miramini.* Opus hoc fuit curationis paralytici; de hoc mirabantur, quod fecerat, et indignabantur, quod in Sabbato; supra quinto[^p345-9]: « Persequebantur Iudaei Iesum, quia hoc faciebat in Sabbato », quasi solveret Legem. Ideo ostendit Dominus, quod non solvit Legem, per *locum a maiori*[^p345-10], et facit tale argumentum: si circumcisio, quae non est ex Lege, sed ex Patribus, Sabbatum non solvit, quia opus nobile est: ergo cum salus totius hominis sit opus nobilius, multo minus solvet. Propter quod dicit:

30. (Vers. 22.). *Propterea Moyses dedit vobis circumcisionem, non quia ex Moyse est, sed ex Patribus,* id est, non quia fuit sibi praeceptum primo, sed Abrahae; Genesis decimo septimo[^p345-11]: « Circumcidetur ex vobis omne masculinum ». *Et in Sabbato circumciditis hominem,* propter illud quod ibidem scriptum est: « Infans octo dierum circumcidetur »; et tamen non creditis Legem solvere, sed observare. Ex hoc arguit per *locum a maiori,* quia magis videtur, quod circumcisio solvat quam perfecta salus.

31. (Vers. 23.). *Si circumcisionem accipit homo in Sabbato; ut non solvatur Lex Moysi; mihi indignamini, quia totum hominem sanum feci in Sabbato?* Quasi dicat: cum sit licitum et Sabbatum non solvat, non est iusta indignatio vestra, quia, secundum illud ad Romanos decimo[^p345-12], « aemulationem Dei habent, sed non secundum scientiam ».

32. (Vers. 24.). *Nolite iudicare* etc. Hic tangitur quartum, scilicet *a malo revocatio;* propter quod dicit: *Nolite iudicare secundum faciem,* accipiendo humanam faciem. De bono iudice Isaiae undecimo[^p345-13]: « Non secundum visionem oculorum iudicabit neque secundum auditum aurium arguet ». *Sed iustum iudicium iudicate,* secundum merita, non secundum faciem personarum. Augustinus[^p345-14]: « Ille non iudicat personaliter, qui diligit aequaliter ». Et quia inaequaliter diligebant, quia magis curabant de exterioribus; ideo iniuste iudicabant; unde dicitur Matthaei vigesimo tertio[^p345-15]: Vos Pharisaei « mundatis quod
<!-- page 346 -->
de foris est »; et iustificabant se coram hominibus; Lucae decimo sexto: « Vos estis, qui iustificatis vos coram hominibus; Deus autem novit corda vestra ».

### Quaestiones.

33. Quaest. I. Sed quaeritur hic, utrum circumcidendum esset in Sabbato. — Et quod sic, videtur hic dicere Dominus. Et ratio est, quia octavo die debebat fieri[^p346-1].

**Sed contra:** Praeceptum de Sabbato maximum erat inter omnia praecepta Legis et pluries repetitum[^p346-2]: ergo praeceptum minus debet cedere maiori. Debebant ergo cessare, ne circumciderent in Sabbato.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod praeceptum de Sabbato magnum est et circumcisioni non obviabat, quia non prohibebat opus salutis, sed servitutis: unde in Sabbato exponebatur Lex[^p346-3], quia doctrina opus est salutare; et ideo circumcisioni non cedebat, sed simul utrumque praeceptum currebat.

34. Quaest. II. Item quaeritur de argumento Domini, quod circumcisio non solvebat Sabbatum, multo minus nec paralytici curatio[^p346-4]. — Videtur argumentum istud nihil valere:

1. Quia circumcisio erat in praecepto nec poterat differri, sed curatio in alio die poterat fieri.

2. Praeterea, circumcisio opus erat *spirituale* quantum ad hoc, quod mundabat ab originali; sed curatio illa fuit opus *corporale,* quia aegritudo erat corporalis.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod Lex Moysi circumcisionem non praecepit, sed supposuit quasi a digniori; unde dicitur Exodi duodecimo[^p346-5]: *Si quis peregrinorum voluerit in vestram transire coloniam, circumcidetur.* Sed Sabbatum in Lege praeceptum est et non auferebat circumcisionem, quae a Patribus erat: ergo multo minus nec curationem a Deo, quamvis non esset praecepta.

2. Ad aliud dicendum, quod curatio illa paralytici non tantum erat vel fuit *corporalis,* sed etiam *spiritualis;* unde dictum est ei[^p346-6]: *Iam amplius noli peccare.* Et ideo dicitur in littera: *Totum hominem sanum feci,* scilicet interius et exterius; esto etiam, quod tantum exterius in corpore, tamen adhuc opus erat pietatis, et utique pietatis opera tanquam spiritualia Lex reputabat. Unde in Sabbato licebat eleemosynam dare. Si enim licebat asinum adaquare[^p346-7], magis eleemosynam dare, maxime infirmum liberare.

35. Quaest. III. Sed quaeritur: quare circumcisio fuit data ante Legem? propter hoc quod dicit: *Non ex Moyse* etc.

Dicendum, quod causa dationis circumcisionis una praecipua fuit distinctio populi, de quo nasciturus erat Christus, a nationibus; et quia primo vocatus est Abraham, cui facta est repromissio, ideo ab illo inchoavit[^p346-8]. — Sunt et aliae causae circumcisionis dandae, quare data fuerit populo Dei magis quam aliud signum, quae his versibus continentur:

« Est signum, meritum, distinguit, sive medetur,  
Circumcisa caro significatque, docet ».

Est *signum* fidei Abrahae, *meritum* obedientiae, *distinguit* populum, *medetur* contra originale, *significat* octavam resurrectionis, *docet* castitatem[^p346-9].

## English
<!-- page 344 -->

#### The second part treats of the communication of teaching in two ways.

27. *Did not Moses give* etc. This is the second part, in which *the communication of teaching*[^tr-doctrina] is treated.
<!-- page 345 -->
And because twofold « is the work of the wise man: to make manifest those who lie, and not to lie about the things he knows[^p345-1] »; therefore first he refutes *the perversity of the Jews;* second, *he manifests the nobility of his teaching,* below, in the eighth[^p345-2]: *Again Jesus spoke to them: I am the light of the world.*

The first part has four parts. For first he refutes *the malicious in heart;* second, *those who speak against him with the mouth,* at the place[^p345-3]: *Some therefore of Jerusalem said;* third, *those who rise up through power,* at the place: *The Pharisees therefore heard;* fourth, *those who lie in wait through malice,* at the place: *And Jesus went,* at the beginning of the eighth.

*The malicious,* then, he refutes in this order. First is set down the *reproof* itself; second, *the kicking back of the Jews;* third, *the explanation of the reproof;* fourth, *the recall from evil.*

(Verse 19.). The Lord, then, *reproves* them for the hidden malice of the heart, in that they wished to kill him against the commandment of the Law; on account of this he says: *Did not Moses give you the Law?* which you are bound to observe by your promise, according to that of Exodus, the twenty-fourth[^p345-4]: « All things that the Lord hath commanded us, we will do ». *And yet none of you keepeth the Law?* The Psalm: « They are all gone aside, they are become unprofitable together: there is none that doth good, no not one »; because you have already transgressed it in *heart;* which he shows by revealing the malice of the heart.

28. (Verse 20.). *Why seek you to kill me?* since the Law commands in the decalogue: « Thou shalt not kill », Exodus, the twentieth[^p345-5], and Deuteronomy, the fifth; and this one, an innocent man. Therefore he asks: *Why seek you to kill me? Why?* that is, for what cause, as if he were to say: without cause; Exodus, the twenty-third[^p345-6]: « The innocent and just person thou shalt not put to death ».

*The multitude answered.* Here the second is touched upon, namely, *the kicking back of the Jews,* because they do not acknowledge the crime, but revile the one who reproves them. Therefore it is said: *The multitude answered,* not one, but the whole multitude, *and said: Thou hast a devil.* This they cast at him in Matthew, the twelfth[^p345-7], and below, in the eighth; behold the reviling. Chrysostom[^p345-8]: « It is a word of anger and of fury and of a shameless soul ». *Who seeketh to kill thee?* As if they were to say: no one. They lie, because they themselves have a devil and are sons of the devil, who « is a liar, and the father thereof ».

29. (Verse 21.). *Jesus answered and said to them.* Here the third is touched upon, namely, *the explanation of the reproof,* because on account of this, that he had healed on the Sabbath, they wished to kill him; whence he says: *One work I have done, and you all wonder.* This work was the curing of the paralytic; they wondered at this, that he had done it, and were indignant, that he had done it on the Sabbath; above, in the fifth[^p345-9]: « The Jews persecuted Jesus, because he did this on the Sabbath », as though he were breaking the Law. Therefore the Lord shows that he does not break the Law, by *the topic from the greater*[^p345-10], and makes an argument of this kind: if circumcision, which is not from the Law but from the fathers, does not break the Sabbath, because it is a noble work: then, since the health of the whole man is a nobler work, much less will it break it. On account of which he says:

30. (Verse 22.). *Therefore Moses gave you circumcision, not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers,* that is, not because it was commanded first to him, but to Abraham; Genesis, the seventeenth[^p345-11]: « All the male kind of you shall be circumcised ». *And on the Sabbath you circumcise a man,* on account of that which is written in the same place: « An infant of eight days shall be circumcised »; and yet you do not believe that you break the Law, but that you observe it. From this he argues by *the topic from the greater,* because it seems more that circumcision breaks it than does perfect health.

31. (Verse 23.). *If a man receive circumcision on the Sabbath; that the Law of Moses may not be broken; are you indignant at me, because I have healed the whole man on the Sabbath?* As if he were to say: since it is lawful and does not break the Sabbath, your indignation is not just, because, according to that to the Romans, the tenth[^p345-12], « they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge ».

32. (Verse 24.). *Judge not* etc. Here the fourth is touched upon, namely, *the recall from evil;* on account of which he says: *Judge not according to the appearance,* taking it as the human appearance. Of the good judge, Isaias, the eleventh[^p345-13]: « He shall not judge according to the sight of the eyes, nor reprove according to the hearing of the ears ». *But judge just judgment,* according to merits, not according to the appearance of persons. Augustine[^p345-14]: « He does not judge personally who loves equally ». And because they loved unequally, because they cared more for exterior things; therefore they judged unjustly; whence it is said in Matthew, the twenty-third[^p345-15]: You Pharisees « make clean what is
<!-- page 346 -->
outside »; and they justified themselves before men; Luke, the sixteenth: « You are they who justify yourselves before men; but God knoweth your hearts ».

### Questions.

33. Question I. But it is asked here whether one ought to circumcise on the Sabbath. — And that one should, the Lord seems to say here. And the reason is, because it was to be done on the eighth day[^p346-1].

**On the contrary:** The precept about the Sabbath was the greatest among all the precepts of the Law and was repeated several times[^p346-2]: therefore the lesser precept ought to yield to the greater. They ought therefore to have ceased, lest they circumcise on the Sabbath.

**I respond:** It must be said that the precept about the Sabbath is great and did not stand in the way of circumcision, because it did not prohibit the work of salvation, but of servitude: whence on the Sabbath the Law was expounded[^p346-3], because teaching is a salutary work; and therefore it did not yield to circumcision, but both precepts ran together.

34. Question II. Likewise it is asked about the Lord's argument, that circumcision did not break the Sabbath, much less, then, the curing of the paralytic[^p346-4]. — This argument seems to be worth nothing:

1. Because circumcision was under precept and could not be put off, but the curing could be done on another day.

2. Moreover, circumcision was a *spiritual* work as regards this, that it cleansed from original sin; but that curing was a *bodily* work, because the sickness was bodily.

**I respond:** It must be said that the Law of Moses did not command circumcision, but presupposed it as from something worthier; whence it is said in Exodus, the twelfth[^p346-5]: *If any of the strangers be willing to pass into your colony, he shall be circumcised.* But the Sabbath is commanded in the Law and did not take away circumcision, which was from the fathers: therefore much less the curing from God, although it was not commanded.

2. To the other it must be said that that curing of the paralytic was not only *bodily,* but also *spiritual;* whence it was said to him[^p346-6]: *Now sin no more.* And therefore it is said in the letter: *I have healed the whole man,* namely, inwardly and outwardly; and granted even that it was only outwardly in the body, still it was a work of piety, and the Law certainly reckoned works of piety as spiritual. Whence on the Sabbath it was lawful to give alms. For if it was lawful to water an ass[^p346-7], it is more lawful to give alms, and most of all to set free one who is sick.

35. Question III. But it is asked: why was circumcision given before the Law? on account of this, that he says: *Not because it is of Moses* etc.

It must be said that one principal cause of the giving of circumcision was the distinguishing of the people, of whom Christ was to be born, from the nations; and because Abraham was called first, to whom the promise was made, therefore it began from him[^p346-8]. — There are also other causes of the giving of circumcision, why it was given to the people of God rather than another sign, which are contained in these verses:

« It is a sign, a merit, it distinguishes, or it heals,  
Circumcised flesh, and it signifies, it teaches ».

It is a *sign* of the faith of Abraham, a *merit* of obedience, it *distinguishes* the people, it *heals* against original sin, it *signifies* the octave of the resurrection, it *teaches* chastity[^p346-9].

[^tr-doctrina]: **En.** Translator's note: the Latin *doctrina* holds together what English divides among doctrine, teaching and learning. Here it names the second part of the Doctor's showing himself director *verbo doctrinae* — *doctrinae communicatio*, the teaching given out to the people at the feast — and the *nobilitas* of *his teaching* in 8:12; in Question I *doctrina opus est salutare* is the expounding of the Law on the Sabbath. It is a teaching given and heard, not a body of propositions, so "teaching" is the English throughout this chunk, as in the pericopes before it.
