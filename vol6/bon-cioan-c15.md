## *Surgite, eamus hinc · Annotationes 58–65*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 604 -->

### Capitulum XV. *Surgite, eamus hinc*[^p604-3].

### Annotatio 58.

(Vers. 1.). *Ego sum vitis vera.*

Nota, quod Christus fuit *vitis* in trunco *contemptibilis,* in palmitibus *multiplex,* in fructu *dulcis* sive suavis. — In *trunco* fuit *contemptibilis;* Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio[^p604-4]: « Ascendet sicut virgultum coram eo, et sicut radix de terra sitienti; non est ei species neque decor ». — In *palmitibus, multiplex;* Psalmus: « Extendit palmites suos usque ad mare et usque ad flumen propagines eius ». *Palmites* Christi fideles sui; Ioannis decimo quinto[^p604-5]: « Ego sum vitis, vos palmites ». — In *fructu, dulcis;* Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto: « Ego quasi vitis fructificavi suavitatem odoris » etc.; et Canticorum secundo: « Sub umbra illius quem desideraveram, sedi, et fructus eius dulcis gutturi meo ».

### Annotatio 59.

(Vers. 2.). *Omnem palmitem in me non ferentem fructum tollet eum.*

1. Nota, quod de vite Ecclesiae tollitur palmes inutilis aliquando per *divinam reprobationem,* sicut legitur de Saul primi Regum decimo sexto[^p604-6]: « Usquequo tu luges Saul, cum ego proiecerim eum »? dicitur Samueli. — Aliquando per *excommunicationem,* sicut legimus de adultero, primae ad Corinthios quinto: « Iudicavi huiusmodi tradere satanae in interitum carnis » etc. — Aliquando per *aeternam damnationem,* sicut dicitur de reprobis Matthaei decimo tertio: « Colligent eos qui faciunt iniquitatem, et mittent eos in caminum ignis »; et Matthaei vigesimo quinto: « Servum inutilem eiicite in tenebras exteriores ».

2. *Et omnem, qui fert fructum, purgabit eum.* Nota, quod palmes spiritualis purgatur tripliciter: per sacramentalem *confessionem;* primae Ioannis primo[^p604-7]: » Si confiteamur peccata nostra, fidelis et iustus est, ut remittat nobis peccata nostra »; *fidelis est,* ut « mundet nos ab omni iniquitate ». — Per temporalem *tribulationem;* Psalmus: « Igne nos examinasti, sicut examinatur argentum ». — Per extremam *excommunicationem;* Malachiae tertio: « Sedebit conflans et emundans argentum et purgabit filios Levi ».

### Annotatio 60.

(Vers. 3.). *Iam mundi estis propter sermonem, quem locutus sum vobis.* Require supra de munditia capitulo decimo tertio[^p604-8].

### Annotatio 61.

(Vers. 4.). *Manete in me, et ego in vobis.*

Nota, quod manendum est in Christo per *fidem,* per *caritatem* et per *operationem.* — In Deo enim manet qui in Christum *credit;* primae Ioannis quarto[^p604-9]: « Quisquis confessus fuerit, quoniam Iesus est Filius Dei, Deus in ipso manet, et ipse in Deo ». — Item, in Deo manet qui ipsum *diligit;* primae Ioannis quarto: « Qui manet in caritate in Deo manet, et Deus in eo ». — In Deo manet qui ipsi *obedit;* Ioannis decimo quinto: « Si praecepta mea servaveritis, manebitis in dilectione mea »; et primae Ioannis secundo: « Qui dicit, se in ipso manere, debet, sicut ille ambulavit, et ipse ambulare ».

### Annotatio 62.

(Vers. 7.). *Si manseritis in me, et verba mea in vobis manserint, quodcumque volueritis, petetis, et fiet vobis.*

Habitum est supra immediate, qualiter manendum est in Deo; describitur hic *fructus* manendi in eo, qui est petitionum nostrarum exauditio. Nota igitur, quod petitio solet exaudiri propter *gratiam petentis;* unde primae Ioannis tertio[^p604-10]: « Si non reprehenderit nos cor nostrum, fiduciam habemus ad
<!-- page 605 -->
Deum, ut quidquid petierimus accipiamus ». — Aliquando propter *meritum petentis;* unde hic dicitur: *Si manseritis in me* etc. — Aliquando propter *instantiam petentis;* propter quod dicitur Matthaei septimo[^p605-1]: « Petite, et accipietis; quaerite, et invenietis; pulsate, et aperietur vobis »; et Lucae undecimo: « Amen dico vobis, quia, si perseveraverit pulsans, et si non dabit illi surgens, eo quod amicus eius sit, propter improbitatem tamen eius surget et dabit illi ».

### Annotatio 63.

(Vers. 15.). *Iam non dicam vos servos.* Require supra octavo capitulo[^p605-2] de servitute mala.

### Annotatio 64.

(Vers. 16.). *Non vos me elegistis, sed ego elegi vos.*

Nota, quod *omnes fideles* electi sunt ad *gratiam;* ad Ephesios primo[^p605-3]: « Elegit nos ante mundi constitutionem ». — *Apostoli* in gratiae *praerogativam;* supra sexto: « Ego duodecim vos elegi ». — *Christus* in singularem *plenitudinem;* Canticorum quinto: « Dilectus meus electus ex millibus ».

### Annotatio 65.

(Vers. 18.). *Si mundus vos odit, scitote, quia me priorem vobis odio habuit.*

1. Nota, quod aliquando *mundus* dicitur *universitas creaturae;* Ioannis primo[^p605-4]: « Mundus per ipsum factus est ». — Aliquando, *congregatio Ecclesiae;* Ioannis tertio: « Sic Deus dilexit mundum » etc. — Aliquando, *amatores vanitatis* mundanae; et hic est, de quo hic dicitur: *Si mundus vos odit* etc. Hic est mundus, qui Christum *ignoravit* ut caecus, *odivit* ut malignus, *occidit* ut sacrilegus. Require de hoc supra septimo capitulo[^p605-5].

2. *Si mundus vos odit.*

Nota, quod est *odire* per *affectum;* primae Ioannis tertio[^p605-6]: « Qui odit fratrem suum homicida est ». — Est *odire* per *effectum;* Psalmus: « Qui diligit iniquitatem odit animam suam ». — Est *odire* per *negligentiam,* sicut dicitur de malis Proverbiorum primo secundum aliam litteram: « Quaerent me et non invenient, oderunt enim sapientiam ». — Est *odire* per *concomitantiam,* sicut dicitur, quod « qui odit legem odit regem »; secundum hoc dicitur Ioannis decimo quinto[^p605-7]: « Qui me odit et Patrem meum odit ».

## English

<!-- page 604 -->

### Chapter XV. *Arise, let us go hence*[^p604-3].

### Annotation 58.

(Vers. 1.). *I am the true vine.*

Note that Christ was a *vine* *contemptible* in the trunk, *manifold* in the branches, *sweet* or pleasant in the fruit. — In the *trunk* he was *contemptible;* Isaias, the fifty-third chapter[^p604-4]: « He shall grow up as a tender plant before him, and as a root out of a thirsty ground; there is no beauty in him, nor comeliness ». — In the *branches, manifold;* the Psalm: « It stretched forth its branches unto the sea, and its boughs unto the river ». The *branches* of Christ are his faithful; John, the fifteenth chapter[^p604-5]: « I am the vine, you the branches ». — In the *fruit, sweet;* Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth chapter: « As the vine I have brought forth a pleasant odour » etc.; and Canticles, the second chapter: « I sat under his shadow, whom I desired, and his fruit was sweet to my palate ».

### Annotation 59.

(Vers. 2.). *Every branch in me that beareth not fruit, he will take away.*

1. Note that from the vine of the Church the unprofitable branch is taken away sometimes through *divine reprobation,* as is read of Saul in the first of Kings, the sixteenth chapter[^p604-6]: « How long wilt thou mourn for Saul, since I have rejected him »? it is said to Samuel. — Sometimes through *excommunication,* as we read of the adulterer, the first to the Corinthians, the fifth chapter: « I have judged to deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh » etc. — Sometimes through *eternal damnation,* as is said of the reprobate, Matthew, the thirteenth chapter: « They shall gather them that work iniquity, and shall cast them into the furnace of fire »; and Matthew, the twenty-fifth chapter: « The unprofitable servant cast ye out into the exterior darkness ».

2. *And every one that beareth fruit, he will purge it.* Note that the spiritual branch is purged in three ways: through sacramental *confession;* the first of John, the first chapter[^p604-7]: » If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just, to forgive us our sins »; *he is faithful,* to « cleanse us from all iniquity ». — Through temporal *tribulation;* the Psalm: « Thou hast tried us by fire, as silver is tried ». — Through extreme *excommunication;* Malachias, the third chapter: « He shall sit refining and cleansing the silver, and shall purge the sons of Levi ».

### Annotation 60.

(Vers. 3.). *Now you are clean by reason of the word, which I have spoken to you.* Look above concerning cleanness, at the thirteenth chapter[^p604-8].

### Annotation 61.

(Vers. 4.). *Remain in me, and I in you.*

Note that one is to remain in Christ through *faith,* through *charity* and through *working.* — For he remains in God who *believes* in Christ; the first of John, the fourth chapter[^p604-9]: « Whosoever shall confess that Jesus is the Son of God, God remaineth in him, and he in God ». — Likewise, he remains in God who *loves* him; the first of John, the fourth chapter: « He that remaineth in charity remaineth in God, and God in him ». — He remains in God who *obeys* him; John, the fifteenth chapter: « If you keep my commandments, you shall remain in my dilection »; and the first of John, the second chapter: « He that saith he remaineth in him, ought himself also to walk, even as he walked ».

### Annotation 62.

(Vers. 7.). *If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, you shall ask whatever you will, and it shall be done unto you.*

It was had immediately above how one is to remain in God; here is described the *fruit* of remaining in him, which is the hearing of our petitions. Note therefore that a petition is wont to be heard on account of the *grace of the petitioner;* whence the first of John, the third chapter[^p604-10]: « If our heart do not reprehend us, we have confidence towards
<!-- page 605 -->
God, that whatsoever we shall ask, we may receive ». — Sometimes on account of the *merit of the petitioner;* whence it is said here: *If you remain in me* etc. — Sometimes on account of the *instance of the petitioner;* for which reason it is said in Matthew, the seventh chapter[^p605-1]: « Ask, and you shall receive; seek, and you shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you »; and Luke, the eleventh chapter: « Amen I say to you, that if he shall continue knocking, and although he will not rise and give him, because he is his friend, yet because of his importunity he will rise and give him ».

### Annotation 63.

(Vers. 15.). *I will not now call you servants.* Look above, at the eighth chapter[^p605-2], concerning evil servitude.

### Annotation 64.

(Vers. 16.). *You have not chosen me, but I have chosen you.*

Note that *all the faithful* are chosen unto *grace;* to the Ephesians, the first chapter[^p605-3]: « He chose us before the foundation of the world ». — The *Apostles* unto the *prerogative* of grace; above, in the sixth chapter: « I have chosen you twelve ». — *Christ* unto a singular *fullness;* Canticles, the fifth chapter: « My beloved, chosen out of thousands ».

### Annotation 65.

(Vers. 18.). *If the world hate you, know ye that it hath hated me before you.*

1. Note that sometimes the *world* is called *the universality of the creature;* John, the first chapter[^p605-4]: « The world was made by him ». — Sometimes, *the congregation of the Church;* John, the third chapter: « God so loved the world » etc. — Sometimes, *the lovers of worldly vanity;* and this is the one of which it is said here: *If the world hate you* etc. This is the world which *knew not* Christ as one blind, *hated* him as one malignant, *slew* him as one sacrilegious. Look concerning this above, at the seventh chapter[^p605-5].

2. *If the world hate you.*

Note that there is *hating* through *affection;* the first of John, the third chapter[^p605-6]: « Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer ». — There is *hating* through *effect;* the Psalm: « He that loveth iniquity hateth his own soul ». — There is *hating* through *negligence,* as is said of the wicked in Proverbs, the first chapter, according to another reading: « They shall seek me, and shall not find me, for they hated wisdom ». — There is *hating* through *concomitance,* as it is said that « he who hates the law hates the king »; according to this it is said in John, the fifteenth chapter[^p605-7]: « He that hateth me hateth my Father also ».
