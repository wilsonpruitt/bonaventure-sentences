### Capitulum XX. *Una autem Sabbati.*

### Annotatio 70.

(Vers. 2.). *Tulerunt Dominum de monumento, et nescimus, ubi posuerunt eum.*

1. Nota triplicem Christi *positionem.* Fuit enim positus in *praesepio;* Lucae secundo[^p619-6]: « Invenietis infantem pannis involutum et positum in praesepio ». — Fuit positus in *patibulo crucis;* Threnorum tertio: « Tetendit arcum suum et posuit me quasi signum ad sagittam ». — Positus etiam fuit in *monumento;* Ioannis decimo nono: « Ibi ergo, quia iuxta erat monumentum, posuerunt Iesum ».

2. Nota, quod Dominus in *praesepio* dat formam *innocentibus;* in *patibulo* dat formam *proficientibus;* in *monumento* dat formam *contemplantibus.* — In *praesepio* positus informat *innocentes* ad duo: ad relinquendam mundanam *possessionem* et mundanam *ambitionem.* Primum notat Evangelista[^p619-7], cum dicit: « Invenietis infantem pannis involutum »; secundum, cum dicit: « Positum in praesepio ». Primum enim est *paupertatis;* secundum, *humilitatis.* — In *patibulo* positus informat *proficientes* ad duo: ad *praecepta implenda* et *adversa sustinenda;* quae duo tanguntur ad Philippenses secundo[^p619-8], cum dicitur: « Factus obediens Patri usque ad mortem »; ecce, *impletio praecepti;* « mortem autem crucis »; ecce, *sustinentia supplicii.* — In *monumento* positus informat *contemplantes* ad duo: ad *quaerendam quietem spiritualem* et ad *fugiendam iactantiam mundanam.* Primum, quia iacuit in sepulcro *quietus;* secundum, quia fuit in sepulcro *absconditus;* quae duo tanguntur Matthaei vigesimo septimo[^p619-9], ubi dicitur, quod « accepto corpore, Ioseph posuit illud in monumento suo novo »; ecce, primum; « et advolvit saxum magnum ad ostium monumenti »; ecce, secundum.

### Collatio LXXII.

(Vers. 9.). *Oportebat eum a mortuis resurgere.*

1. Nota, quod multiplex fuit causa, quare oportuit, Christum a mortuis resurgere. Primo, ut *Scripturas adimpleret;*
<!-- page 620 -->
unde hic dicitur[^p620-1]: « Nesciebant Scripturam, quia oportebat, eum a mortuis resurgere »; et Actuum secundo: « Quem Deus suscitavit a mortuis, solutis doloribus inferni, iuxta quod impossibile erat, teneri eum ab illo. David enim dicit in eum: Providebam Dominum coram me semper etc.; quoniam non derelinques animam meam in inferno nec dabis Sanctum tuum videre corruptionem ». — Secundo, ut *captivos liberaret.* Ipso enim resurgente, resurrexerunt qui captivi tenebantur in limbo; primae ad Corinthios decimo quinto[^p620-2]: « Christus resurrexit a mortuis, primitiae dormientium »; unde Ecclesia:

> Triumphans pompa nobili,
> Victor surgit de funere.

Tertio, ut *discipulos confortaret.* Sicut enim tristes fuerunt ex eius passione, ita gavisi sunt resurrectione ex eius visione; Ioannis vigesimo[^p620-3]: « Gavisi sunt discipuli, viso Domino ». — Quarto, ut *fidem confirmaret;* unde primae ad Corinthios decimo quinto: « Si Christus non surrexit, inanis est nostra praedicatio et fides nostra »; et iterum: « Si autem Christus praedicatur, quia surrexit a mortuis, quomodo quidam in vobis dicunt, quod resurrectio mortuorum non est »? — Quintum est, ut *mores informaret.*

2. Et nota, quod *informat* nos resurrectio Christi ad *desertionem peccatorum,* ad *profectum meritorum,* ad *tolerantiam adversorum,* ad *contemptum mundanorum* et ad *desiderium aeternorum.* — Informat ergo nos resurrectio Christi ad *desertionem peccatorum.* Sicut ergo Christus mortalem vitam deseruit, quando resurrexit; unde ad Romanos sexto[^p620-4]: « Christus, resurgens ex mortuis, iam non moritur » etc. — et sequitur — « ita et vos existimate, vos quidem mortuos esse peccato, viventes autem Deo »; et iterum: « Sed neque exhibeatis membra vestra arma iniquitatis peccato, sed exhibete vos Deo tanquam ex mortuis viventes » etc. — Item ad *profectum meritorum.* Nam et Christus in resurrectione novitatem vitae assumsit; unde ad Romanos sexto[^p620-5]: « Quomodo Christus surrexit a mortuis per gloriam Patris, ita et nos in novitate vitae ambulemus ». — Item ad *tolerantiam adversorum.* Videntes enim, quod est post mortem vivere, non debemus mortem pro Christo refugere; et ideo, sicut habetur ad Hebraeos undecimo, « distenti sunt » Sancti « non suscipientes redemptionem, ut meliorem invenirent resurrectionem »; ad Romanos sexto: « Si complantati facti sumus similitudini mortis eius, simul et resurrectionis erimus ». — Item ad *contemptum mundanorum* et ad *desiderium aeternorum,* quemadmodum Christus in sua resurrectione mundum deseruit et ad Patrem ascendit. De his duobus ad Colossenses tertio[^p620-6]: « Si consurrexistis cum Christo, quae sursum sunt quaerite, ubi Christus est in dextera Dei sedens » etc.; et iterum: « Quae sursum sunt sapite, non quae super terram ».

### Collatio LXXIII.

(Vers. 17.). *Ascendo ad Patrem meum et Patrem vestrum.*

1. Nota, quod quadruplicem ascensum Christi legimus in Scriptura, scilicet ascensum Christi in *carnem,* ascensum Christi in *crucem,* ascensum Christi *ab inferis* et ascensum Christi *ad Patrem.* — De ascensu Christi in *carnem,* Isaiae decimo nono[^p620-7]: « Ecce, ascendet Dominus super nubem levem et ingredietur Aegyptum ». — De ascensu Christi in *crucem,* Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio: « Ascendet sicut virgultum coram eo et sicut radix de terra sitienti ». — De ascensu Christi *ab inferis,* in Psalmo: « Iter facite ei qui ascendit super occasum; Dominus nomen illi ». — De ascensu Christi *ad Patrem,* hic: *Ascendo ad Patrem meum* etc.

2. Et nota[^p620-8], quod ascensus iste ultimus, qui inter alios est principalis, *nobilissimus* fuit quantum ad primatum, *potentissimus* quantum ad modum, *iucundissimus* quantum ad pompam, *altissimus* quantum ad terminum. — *Nobilissimus* fuit quantum ad *primatum.* Nullus enim ascendit in caelum primo et per se nisi Christus. Nam quotquot praeter ipsum ascendunt, *per* ipsum et *post* ipsum; et ideo Ioannis tertio[^p620-9]: « Nemo ascendit in caelum, nisi qui descendit de caelo, Filius hominis, qui est in caelo ». — Fuit *potentissimus* quantum ad *modum.* Non enim ascendit translatus, ut Henoch, vel subvectus, ut Elias, sed propria virtute elevatus; unde comparatur eius ascensio volatui; Psalmus[^p620-10]: « Ascendit super Cherubim et volavit ». — *Iucundissimus* quantum ad *pompam;* unde in Psalmo: « Ascendit Deus in iubilo et Dominus in voce tubae ». — Fuit
<!-- page 621 -->
etiam *altissimus* quantum ad *terminum;* Psalmus: « Psallite Domino, qui ascendit super caelum caeli ad orientem »; et ad Ephesios quarto: « Ipse est, qui ascendit super omnes caelos, ut adimpleret omnia ».

3. *Ascendo,* inquit, *ad Patrem meum.* Iuxta quadruplicem Domini ascensionem nota quadruplicem generis humani *fructum.* Nota, quod aliquis solet ascendere ante alium, ut *viam ostendat,* ut *iter expediat,* ut *manum extendat* ad trahendum alium post se, ut *hospitium accipiat.* Per hunc modum ascendit Christus ante genus humanum. — In *incarnatione* ascendit ante nos, ut *viam vitae ostenderet;* Michaeae secundo[^p621-1]: « Ascendam ante eos, pandens iter ». Hoc figuratum est Exodi decimo tertio, ubi legitur, quod « ascenderunt filii Israel de terra Aegypti; Dominus autem praecedebat eos per diem in columna nubis et per noctem in columna ignis, ut esset dux itineris eorum in utroque tempore ». Per *columnam nubis* intellige in Christo exemplum *vitae;* per *columnam ignis* intellige in Christo exemplum vel eloquium *doctrinae;* quae duo portavit Christus ante nos quasi ceroferarius noster ad illuminandum nobis in sua *conversatione.*

3. In *passione* ascendit Christus ante nos, ut *iter* nobis *expediret.* Obstabat enim volentibus adire regnum impedimentum *poenae* et impedimentum *pugnae.* Sed Christus, ascendens in crucem, et poenam tulit et pugnam vicit, ita quod quilibet post ipsum ascendere possit. Quod figuratum est primi Regum decimo quarto[^p621-2], ubi legitur, quod « ascendit Ionathas contra stationem Philisthinorum, manibus et pedibus reptans, et armiger eius post eum »; et sequitur, quod cum vidissent faciem Ionathae, « alii cadebant ante Ionatham, alios armiger eius interficiebat, sequens eum ». Item figuratum est primi Machabaeorum quinto, dicente Timotheo de Iuda Machabaeo: « Cum appropinquaverit Iudas et exercitus eius ad torrentem aquae, si transierit ad nos prior, non poterimus sustinere eum, quia potens poterit adversus nos. Si vero timuerit transire et posuerit castra citra flumen, transfretemus ad eos et poterimus adversus illum »; et sequitur, quod « Iudas transfretavit ad illos prior et omnis populus post eum, et contritae sunt omnes gentes a facie eorum ». *Transitus torrentis* est sustinentia poenae, *contritio gentium* victoria pugnae; quae duo fecit Christus in sua passione; et sic expedivit nobis iter vitae.

4. In *resurrectione* ascendit ante nos, ut ad erigendum nos nobis *manum extenderet.* Omnes enim recludebamur in fossato mortis, quousque Christus, ascendens de morte, iacentibus extendit manum suam et extraxit eos ab inferis. Et pingi solet Christus egrediens ab inferis manu retro extensa ad trahendum post se alios. Ne timeas ergo, homo, submergi in profundo mortis, quia tibi tendit manum qui ascendit ab inferis; Deuteronomii trigesimo tertio[^p621-3]: « Ascensor caeli auxiliator tuus ». Ipse enim est, qui extendit manum Petro ad erigendum de mari, cum coepisset mergi, Matthaei decimo quarto.

5. In *ascensione* ascendit ante nos ad *hospitium praeparandum;* Ioannis decimo quarto[^p621-4]: « Vado vobis parare locum, et si abiero et praeparavero vobis locum, iterum venio et accipiam vos ad me ipsum »; et ad Ephesios quarto: « Ascendit super omnes caelos, ut adimpleret omnia ». Tunc autem adimplebuntur omnia, quando recipiet suos in aeterna tabernacula, « iudicabit nationes et implebit ruinas ». — Sic ergo Christus ascendit in *incarnatione* ad iter ostendendum, in *passione* ad iter expediendum, in *resurrectione* ad manum extendendum, in *ascensione* ad hospitium praeparandum.

### Collatio LXXIV.

(Vers. 19.) *Stetit etiam Iesus in medio discipulorum suorum et dixit eis: Pax vobis.*

1. Congruit hic duo de Christo notare, scilicet *statum* — et *verbum.* *Status* eius dat nobis *formam virtutis,* *verbum* vero *formam pacis.* Stetit enim *in medio;* unde dicitur: *Stetit Iesus in medio discipulorum suorum;* et protulit *verbum pacis;* unde sequitur: *Pax vobis.*

*Stetit,* inquit, *in medio.*

Require supra de statu Iesu in medio, capitulo primo[^p621-5].

2. *Et dixit eis: Pax vobis.* Nota, quod triplex pax distinguitur in Scriptura, scilicet pax *mundana,* pax *interna,* pax *aeterna;* sive pax *temporis,* pax *pectoris* et pax *aeternitatis;* vel aliter: pax *peccatorum,* pax *iustorum* et pax *Beatorum.* — De pace *peccatorum,* in Psalmo[^p621-6]: « Zelavi super iniquos, pacem peccatorum videns ». — De pace *iustorum,* Psalmus: « Pax multa diligentibus legem tuam »; et Isaiae quadragesimo octavo: « Utinam attendisses mandata mea! facta fuisset sicut flumen pax tua ». — De pace *Beatorum,* Sapientiae tertio: « Iustorum animae » etc.; et sequitur: « Visi sunt oculis insipientium mori, illi autem sunt in pace ».

3. Pax *peccatorum contemnenda est,* quia *ignominiosa;* quod figuratum est, primi Regum undecimo[^p621-7], in responsione Naas Ammonitae ad filios Israel: « In hoc, inquit, feriam vobiscum foedus, ut omnium
<!-- page 622 -->
vestrum oculos dexteros eruam ponamque vos opprobrium in universo Israel ». *Oculus dexter,* meditatio supernorum, quem amittunt qui pacem peccatorum ineunt. Exemplum de medico, qui loco oculi humani commutavit oculum caprae. — Item, quia *falsa* sive seditiosa; unde Matthaei vigesimo sexto[^p622-1]: « Quemcumque osculatus fuero, ipse est; tenete eum »; et secundi Regum vigesimo legitur de Ioab, quod ex una parte « tenebat mentum Amasae, quasi osculans eum », et ex alia parte infigebat ei gladium. — Item, quia *instabilis* et momentanea; primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto[^p622-2]: « Cum dixerint: Pax et securitas, tunc repentinus eis superveniet interitus, et dolor sicut parturientis, et non effugient ».

4. Pax *iustorum quaerenda* est, quae debet esse triplex. Debet enim homo habere pacem ad *Deum;* Proverbiorum duodecimo[^p622-3]: « Qui ineunt pacis consilia, sequitur eos gaudium ». Haec est pax, quam annuntiavit Angelus, Lucae secundo: « In terra pax hominibus bonae voluntatis ». — Debet etiam habere pacem ad *proximum;* ad Ephesios quarto: « Solliciti, servare unitatem spiritus in vinculo pacis ». — Debet etiam habere pacem ad *se ipsum;* de qua ad Romanos octavo: « Prudentia carnis mors est, prudentia vero spiritus vita et pax ».

5. Pax *Beatorum desideranda,* quia non patitur *incursum tentationis;* unde in Psalmo[^p622-4]: « Confortavit Dominus seras portarum tuarum, benedixit filiis tuis in te. Qui posuit fines tuos pacem » etc.; Isaiae trigesimo secundo: « Sedebit populus meus in pulcritudine pacis, in tabernaculis fiduciae, in requie opulenta ». — Non patitur *pressuram tribulationis;* Ioannis decimo sexto[^p622-5]: « In mundo pressuram habebitis », in me autem pacem; et in Psalmo: « In pace in id ipsum dormiam et requiescam ». — Non patitur *periculum occasus;* Isaiae trigesimo secundo: « Erit opus iustitiae pax, et cultus iustitiae silentium, et securitas usque in sempiternum ».

### Annotatio 71.

(Vers. 15.). *Illa existimans, quod hortulanus esset* etc.

Nota, quod Dominus post resurrectionem in triplici specie se ostendit: in specie *hortulani;* unde hic: *Illa existimans, quod hortulanus esset* etc. In specie *peregrini;* unde Lucae ultimo[^p622-6]: « Tu solus peregrinus es in Ierusalem »? In specie *crucifixi;* unde in eodem: « Videte manus meas et pedes meos, quia ego sum ». — Nota igitur, quod specialiter in specie *hortulani* se ostendit *incipientibus,* in quibus plantat germina virtutum; in specie *peregrini proficientibus,* quos deducit per viam mandatorum; in specie *crucifixi perfectis,* quos accendit ad imitationem passionis.

### Annotatio 72.

(Vers. 19.). *Stetit Iesus in medio eorum.*

Nota, quod amor solet formari aliquando ex *visu,* aliquando ex *auditu,* aliquando ex *tactu,* aliquando ex *convictu.* Diligimus enim illum, in quo bonum vidimus, vel a quo bonum audivimus, vel in quo bonum experti sumus, vel cui bene conviximus. — His omnibus modis reformat Salvator caritatem discipulorum in die resurrectionis, quae laesa fuerat in tempore passionis. Dedit enim se discipulis cognoscibilem ex *visu;* unde hic dicitur: *Stetit in medio eorum;* et paulo post[^p622-7]: « Ostendit eis manus et latus ». — Ex *auditu,* quando salutavit eos; unde hic dicitur: « Pax vobis ». — Ex *tactu,* cum vocavit eos ad palpandum; Lucae ultimo: « Palpate et videte, quia spiritus carnem et ossa non habet, sicut me videtis habere ». — Ex *convictu,* quando quaesivit aliquid ad comedendum; unde Lucae ultimo: « Habetis hic aliquid, quod manducetur »?

(Vers. 25.). *Vidimus Dominum.* Require exemplum in fine[^p622-8]

### Chapter XX. *And on the first day of the week.*

### Annotation 70.

(Vers. 2.). *They have taken away the Lord out of the monument, and we know not where they have laid him.*

1. Note the threefold *laying* of Christ. For he was laid in the *manger;* Luke, the second chapter[^p619-6]: « You shall find the infant wrapped in swaddling clothes, and laid in a manger ». — He was laid on the *gibbet of the cross;* Lamentations, the third chapter: « He hath bent his bow, and laid me as a mark for the arrow ». — He was laid also in the *monument;* John, the nineteenth chapter: « There therefore, because the monument was nigh at hand, they laid Jesus ».

2. Note that the Lord in the *manger* gives a form to the *innocent;* on the *gibbet* he gives a form to the *proficient;* in the *monument* he gives a form to the *contemplative.* — Laid in the *manger,* he instructs the *innocent* to two things: to relinquishing worldly *possession* and worldly *ambition.* The first the Evangelist[^p619-7] notes, when he says: « You shall find the infant wrapped in swaddling clothes »; the second, when he says: « Laid in a manger ». For the first is of *poverty;* the second, of *humility.* — Laid on the *gibbet,* he instructs the *proficient* to two things: to *fulfilling the precepts* and *sustaining adversities;* which two are touched upon to the Philippians, the second chapter[^p619-8], when it is said: « Becoming obedient to the Father unto death »; behold, *the fulfilment of the precept;* « even the death of the cross »; behold, *the sustaining of the penalty.* — Laid in the *monument,* he instructs the *contemplative* to two things: to *seeking spiritual rest* and to *fleeing worldly boasting.* The first, because he lay in the sepulchre *at rest;* the second, because he was in the sepulchre *hidden;* which two are touched upon in Matthew, the twenty-seventh chapter[^p619-9], where it is said that « Joseph, taking the body, laid it in his own new monument »; behold, the first; « and he rolled a great stone to the door of the monument »; behold, the second.

### Collation LXXII.

(Vers. 9.). *He must rise again from the dead.*

1. Note that the cause was manifold why it behoved Christ to rise again from the dead. First, that he might *fulfil the Scriptures;*
<!-- page 620 -->
whence it is said here[^p620-1]: « They knew not the Scripture, that he must rise again from the dead »; and the Acts, the second chapter: « Whom God hath raised up from the dead, having loosed the sorrows of hell, as it was impossible that he should be holden by it. For David saith concerning him: I foresaw the Lord before me always etc.; because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, nor suffer thy Holy One to see corruption ». — Secondly, that he might *set free the captives.* For when he rose again, they rose again who were held captive in limbo; the first to the Corinthians, the fifteenth chapter[^p620-2]: « Christ is risen from the dead, the firstfruits of them that sleep »; whence the Church:

> Triumphing in noble pomp,
> The victor rises from the grave.

Thirdly, that he might *comfort the disciples.* For as they were sad from his passion, so were they glad at the resurrection from the sight of him; John, the twentieth chapter[^p620-3]: « The disciples were glad, when they saw the Lord ». — Fourthly, that he might *confirm faith;* whence the first to the Corinthians, the fifteenth chapter: « If Christ be not risen, our preaching is vain, and our faith »; and again: « Now if Christ be preached, that he arose from the dead, how do some among you say, that there is no resurrection of the dead »? — The fifth is, that he might *form morals.*

2. And note that the resurrection of Christ *forms* us to the *deserting of sins,* to the *advancement of merits,* to the *toleration of adversities,* to the *contempt of worldly things* and to the *desire of eternal things.* — The resurrection of Christ therefore forms us to the *deserting of sins.* For as Christ deserted the mortal life when he rose again; whence to the Romans, the sixth chapter[^p620-4]: « Christ, rising again from the dead, dieth now no more » etc. — and it follows — « so do you also reckon that you indeed are dead to sin, but alive unto God »; and again: « But neither yield ye your members as instruments of iniquity unto sin, but present yourselves to God, as those that are alive from the dead » etc. — Likewise to the *advancement of merits.* For Christ too in the resurrection took up newness of life; whence to the Romans, the sixth chapter[^p620-5]: « As Christ is risen from the dead by the glory of the Father, so we also may walk in newness of life ». — Likewise to the *toleration of adversities.* For seeing that there is living after death, we ought not to flee death for Christ; and therefore, as is had to the Hebrews, the eleventh chapter, the Saints « were racked, not accepting redemption, that they might find a better resurrection »; to the Romans, the sixth chapter: « If we have been planted together in the likeness of his death, we shall be also of his resurrection ». — Likewise to the *contempt of worldly things* and to the *desire of eternal things,* even as Christ in his resurrection deserted the world and ascended to the Father. Of these two, to the Colossians, the third chapter[^p620-6]: « If you be risen with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is sitting at the right hand of God » etc.; and again: « Mind the things that are above, not the things that are upon the earth ».

### Collation LXXIII.

(Vers. 17.). *I ascend to my Father and to your Father.*

1. Note that we read in Scripture of a fourfold ascent of Christ, namely the ascent of Christ into *flesh,* the ascent of Christ onto the *cross,* the ascent of Christ *from hell* and the ascent of Christ *to the Father.* — Of the ascent of Christ into *flesh,* Isaias, the nineteenth chapter[^p620-7]: « Behold, the Lord will ascend upon a swift cloud, and will enter into Egypt ». — Of the ascent of Christ onto the *cross,* Isaias, the fifty-third chapter: « He shall ascend as a tender plant before him, and as a root out of a thirsty ground ». — Of the ascent of Christ *from hell,* in the Psalm: « Make a way for him who ascendeth upon the west; the Lord is his name ». — Of the ascent of Christ *to the Father,* here: *I ascend to my Father* etc.

2. And note[^p620-8] that this last ascent, which is the principal among the others, was *most noble* as to primacy, *most mighty* as to manner, *most joyful* as to pomp, *highest* as to its term. — It was *most noble* as to *primacy.* For no one ascends into heaven first and of himself but Christ. For as many as ascend besides him, ascend *through* him and *after* him; and therefore John, the third chapter[^p620-9]: « No man hath ascended into heaven, but he that descended from heaven, the Son of man who is in heaven ». — It was *most mighty* as to *manner.* For he did not ascend translated, as Henoch, or carried up, as Elias, but raised up by his own power; whence his ascension is compared to flight; the Psalm[^p620-10]: « He ascended upon the Cherubim, and he flew ». — *Most joyful* as to *pomp;* whence in the Psalm: « God is ascended with jubilee, and the Lord with the voice of the trumpet ». — It was
<!-- page 621 -->
also *highest* as to its *term;* the Psalm: « Sing ye to the Lord, who ascendeth above the heaven of heavens, to the east »; and to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter: « He it is who ascended above all the heavens, that he might fill all things ».

3. *I ascend,* he says, *to my Father.* According to the fourfold ascension of the Lord note a fourfold *fruit* for the human race. Note that one is wont to ascend before another, that he may *show the way,* that he may *clear the road,* that he may *stretch out a hand* to draw the other after him, that he may *receive a lodging.* In this manner Christ ascended before the human race. — In the *incarnation* he ascended before us, that he might *show the way of life;* Micheas, the second chapter[^p621-1]: « I will ascend before them, opening the road ». This was figured in Exodus, the thirteenth chapter, where it is read that « the children of Israel ascended out of the land of Egypt; and the Lord went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, and by night in a pillar of fire, that he might be the guide of their road at both times ». By the *pillar of a cloud* understand in Christ the example of *life;* by the *pillar of fire* understand in Christ the example or utterance of *teaching*[^tr-doctrina]; which two Christ carried before us as our taper-bearer, to give light to us in his *conversation.*

3. In the *passion* Christ ascended before us, that he might *clear the road* for us. For to those wishing to go to the kingdom the hindrance of *punishment* and the hindrance of *fighting* stood in the way. But Christ, ascending onto the cross, both bore the punishment and won the fight, so that anyone may ascend after him. Which was figured in the first of Kings, the fourteenth chapter[^p621-2], where it is read that « Jonathan ascended against the station of the Philistines, creeping on hands and feet, and his armourbearer after him »; and it follows that when they had seen the face of Jonathan, « some fell before Jonathan, others his armourbearer slew, following him ». Likewise it was figured in the first of Machabees, the fifth chapter, when Timotheus said of Judas Machabeus: « When Judas and his army shall draw near to the torrent of water, if he pass over to us first, we shall not be able to withstand him, for he will mightily prevail against us. But if he be afraid to pass over, and pitch his camp on this side of the river, let us pass over to them, and we shall prevail against him »; and it follows that « Judas passed over to them first, and all the people after him, and all the Gentiles were crushed before them ». *The passing of the torrent* is the sustaining of punishment, *the crushing of the Gentiles* the victory of the fight; which two Christ did in his passion; and so he cleared for us the road of life.

4. In the *resurrection* he ascended before us, that he might *stretch out his hand* to us to raise us up. For we were all shut up in the ditch of death, until Christ, ascending from death, stretched out his hand to those lying there and drew them out of hell. And Christ is wont to be painted coming forth from hell with his hand stretched backward to draw others after him. Fear not therefore, O man, to be sunk in the depth of death, for he stretches his hand to thee who ascended from hell; Deuteronomy, the thirty-third chapter[^p621-3]: « The ascender of heaven is thy helper ». For he it is who stretched out his hand to Peter to raise him up from the sea, when he began to sink, Matthew, the fourteenth chapter.

5. In the *ascension* he ascended before us to *prepare a lodging;* John, the fourteenth chapter[^p621-4]: « I go to prepare a place for you, and if I shall go and prepare a place for you, I come again, and will take you to myself »; and to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter: « He ascended above all the heavens, that he might fill all things ». And all things will be filled then, when he shall receive his own into the eternal tabernacles, « he shall judge the nations, and shall fill ruins ». — So therefore Christ ascended in the *incarnation* to show the road, in the *passion* to clear the road, in the *resurrection* to stretch out his hand, in the *ascension* to prepare a lodging.

### Collation LXXIV.

(Vers. 19.) *Jesus also stood in the midst of his disciples, and said to them: Peace be to you.*

1. It is fitting here to note two things of Christ, namely his *standing* — and his *word.* His *standing* gives us *the form of virtue,* his *word* the *form of peace.* For he stood *in the midst;* whence it is said: *Jesus stood in the midst of his disciples;* and he uttered *the word of peace;* whence it follows: *Peace be to you.*

*He stood,* he says, *in the midst.*

Look above, in the first chapter[^p621-5], concerning the standing of Jesus in the midst.

2. *And he said to them: Peace be to you.* Note that a threefold peace is distinguished in Scripture, namely *worldly* peace, *inward* peace, *eternal* peace; or the peace *of time,* the peace *of the breast* and the peace *of eternity;* or otherwise: the peace *of sinners,* the peace *of the just* and the peace *of the Blessed.* — Of the peace *of sinners,* in the Psalm[^p621-6]: « I had a zeal upon the wicked, seeing the peace of sinners ». — Of the peace *of the just,* the Psalm: « Much peace have they that love thy law »; and Isaias, the forty-eighth chapter: « O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments! thy peace had been as a river ». — Of the peace *of the Blessed,* Wisdom, the third chapter: « The souls of the just » etc.; and it follows: « In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die, but they are in peace ».

3. The peace *of sinners is to be despised,* because it is *ignominious;* which was figured in the first of Kings, the eleventh chapter[^p621-7], in the answer of Naas the Ammonite to the children of Israel: « On this condition, he says, will I make a covenant with you, that I may pluck out the right eyes of all
<!-- page 622 -->
of you, and make you a reproach in all Israel ». *The right eye* is the meditation of things above, which they lose who enter into the peace of sinners. The example of the physician who, in place of a human eye, substituted the eye of a goat. — Likewise, because it is *false* or seditious; whence Matthew, the twenty-sixth chapter[^p622-1]: « Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he; hold him »; and in the second of Kings, the twentieth chapter, it is read of Joab that on the one side he « held the chin of Amasa, as though kissing him », and on the other side drove a sword into him. — Likewise, because it is *unstable* and momentary; the first to the Thessalonians, the fifth chapter[^p622-2]: « When they shall say: Peace and security, then shall sudden destruction come upon them, and pains as upon her that is with child, and they shall not escape ».

4. The peace *of the just is to be sought,* which ought to be threefold. For a man ought to have peace towards *God;* Proverbs, the twelfth chapter[^p622-3]: « They who take counsels of peace, joy followeth them ». This is the peace which the Angel announced, Luke, the second chapter: « On earth peace to men of good will ». — He ought also to have peace towards his *neighbour;* to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter: « Careful to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace ». — He ought also to have peace towards *himself;* of which to the Romans, the eighth chapter: « The wisdom of the flesh is death, but the wisdom of the spirit is life and peace ».

5. The peace *of the Blessed is to be desired,* because it does not suffer the *onset of temptation;* whence in the Psalm[^p622-4]: « The Lord hath strengthened the bolts of thy gates, he hath blessed thy children within thee. Who hath placed peace in thy borders » etc.; Isaias, the thirty-second chapter: « My people shall sit in the beauty of peace, and in the tabernacles of confidence, and in wealthy rest ». — It does not suffer the *distress of tribulation;* John, the sixteenth chapter[^p622-5]: « In the world you shall have distress », but in me peace; and in the Psalm: « In peace in the selfsame I will sleep, and I will rest ». — It does not suffer the *danger of setting;* Isaias, the thirty-second chapter: « The work of justice shall be peace, and the service of justice silence, and security for ever ».

### Annotation 71.

(Vers. 15.). *She, thinking that it was the gardener* etc.

Note that the Lord after the resurrection showed himself in a threefold appearance: in the appearance *of a gardener;* whence here: *She, thinking that it was the gardener* etc. In the appearance *of a pilgrim;* whence Luke, the last chapter[^p622-6]: « Art thou only a pilgrim in Jerusalem »? In the appearance *of one crucified;* whence in the same place: « See my hands and my feet, that it is I ». — Note therefore that specially in the appearance *of a gardener* he shows himself to *beginners,* in whom he plants the seeds of the virtues; in the appearance *of a pilgrim* to the *proficient,* whom he leads along the way of the commandments; in the appearance *of one crucified* to the *perfect,* whom he kindles to the imitation of the passion.

### Annotation 72.

(Vers. 19.). *Jesus stood in the midst of them.*

Note that love is wont to be formed sometimes from *sight,* sometimes from *hearing,* sometimes from *touch,* sometimes from *living together.* For we love him in whom we have seen good, or from whom we have heard good, or in whom we have experienced good, or with whom we have lived well together. — In all these ways the Saviour re-forms the charity of the disciples on the day of the resurrection, which had been wounded in the time of the passion. For he gave himself to the disciples to be known from *sight;* whence it is said here: *He stood in the midst of them;* and a little after[^p622-7]: « He shewed them his hands and side ». — From *hearing,* when he greeted them; whence it is said here: « Peace be to you ». — From *touch,* when he called them to handle him; Luke, the last chapter: « Handle, and see, for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as you see me to have ». — From *living together,* when he asked for something to eat; whence Luke, the last chapter: « Have you here anything that may be eaten »?

(Vers. 25.). *We have seen the Lord.* Look for the example at the end[^p622-8]
