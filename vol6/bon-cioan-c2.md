### Capitulum II. *Nuptiae factae sunt.*

### Collatio VIII.

(Vers. 1.). *Nuptiae factae sunt in Cana Galilaeae, et erat Mater Iesu ibi.*

1. Volens Evangelista describere miraculum nuptiarum, convertit intentionem suam ad duo, scilicet ad describendum *nuptiarum convivium* — et ad describendum *coetum assistentium*, et hoc non incongrue. In *convivio* enim factum est miraculum, ab *assistentibus sancitum*[^p545-2]. *Convivium* nuptiarum incipit describere, cum dicit: *Nuptiae factae sunt; coetum assistentium*, cum dicit: *Et erat Mater Iesu ibi.*

2. *Nuptiae,* inquit, *factae sunt.* Praetermissis nuptiis *corporalibus*, quarum sanctitati attestatur, quod Deus eas auctoritate propria in *paradiso* instituit, sicut habetur in Genesis secundo[^p545-3]: « Relinquet homo patrem et matrem et adhaerebit uxori suae »; et easdem in *terra* sua praesentia consecravit et miraculorum suorum initiis insignivit, ut habetur in praesenti Evangelio; notandum, quod secundum *spiritualem intellectum* quatuor sunt genera nuptiarum. Sunt enim nuptiae *carnales* — nuptiae *spirituales* — nuptiae *sacramentales* — et nuptiae *aeternales;* sive nuptiae *culpae*, nuptiae *gratiae*, nuptiae *eucharistiae* et nuptiae *gloriae.*

3. Est enim matrimonium *hominis* et *carnalis concupiscentiae*[^p545-4], et huiusmodi habet nuptias *culpae.* Est matrimonium *Dei* et *animae*, et huiusmodi habet nuptias *gratiae.* Est matrimonium *Iesu Christi* et *Ecclesiae*, et huiusmodi habet in praesenti nuptias *eucharistiae* quasi prandium et in futuro quasi coenam nuptias *gloriae.* — Sic *triplex matrimonium* habet *quadruplex convivium* nuptiale. Primum fit in *prostibulo concupiscentiae;* secundum, in *thalamo conscientiae;* tertium, in *tabernaculo Ecclesiae;* quartum, in *palatio gloriae.*

4. Sunt ergo nuptiae *carnales*, in quibus est « animarum inquinatio, nativitatis immutatio, nuptiarum inconstantia, inordinatio moechiae et impudicitiae », secundum quod habetur Sapientiae decimo quarto[^p545-5]. — Nuptiarum huiusmodi matrimonium *initiatur* in mala delectatione, *ratificatur* in consensu, *consummatur* in opere.

5. Sunt nuptiae *spirituales Dei* et *animae;* et hae possunt intelligi convenienter per nuptias factas in Cana Galilaeae. *Cana* idem est quod zelus[^p545-6]; *Galilaea* transmigratio sive transmeans, aut rota sive volubilis. Istae enim nuptiae fiunt in conscientia animae habentis zelum dilectionis; quae quidem anima transmigrat in montem Domini per *devotionem mentis*, et est volubilis per *agilitatem operis.* In his nuptiis convertit Dominus aquam lacrymarum in vinum consolationis, maxime si sit *Mater Iesu ibi.* — Quoniam autem istae nuptiae sunt Religiosorum proprie, notandum, quod horum matrimonium *initiatur* in ingressu religionis, *ratificatur* in voto professionis, *consummatur* in perfecta impletione promissionis.

6. Sunt etiam nuptiae *sacramentales*, quae significantur Esther secundo[^p545-7], ubi Assuerus, repudiata Vasthi, « adamavit Esther super omnes mulieres et posuit diadema regni in capite eius et fecit eam regnare in loco Vasthi, et iussit, praeparari convivium permagnificum cunctis principibus et servis suis pro coniunctione et nuptiis Esther ». *Vasthi*, synagoga; *Esther*, Ecclesia; *Assuerus*, Christus; *fides*,
<!-- page 546 -->
coniunctio; *convivium permagnificum*, eucharistia. — Nuptiarum huiusmodi matrimonium *initiatum* fuit in Christi promissione facta sanctis Patribus[^p546-1], *ratificatum* fuit in incarnatione, *consummatum* in passione.

7. Sunt etiam nuptiae *aeternales*, de quibus Apocalypsis decimo nono[^p546-2] dicitur: « Beati, qui ad coenam nuptiarum Agni vocati sunt ». — Et illarum quidem nuptiarum solemnitas *initiatur* in praesenti in iustis per spem de futuris bonis; *ratificatur* in adeptione primae stolae, animae scilicet, et *consummatur* in adeptione secundae stolae, scilicet corporis.

8. Cum igitur quatuor sint genera nuptiarum, et omne coniugium faciat ex pluribus unum; notandum, quod primae faciunt duo in *una carne*, id est in una carnalitate; primae ad Corinthios sexto[^p546-3]: « An nescitis, quoniam qui adhaeret meretrici unum corpus efficitur? Erunt, inquit, duo in carne una ». — Secundae faciunt unum in *uno spiritu;* primae ad Corinthios sexto: « Qui adhaeret Deo unus spiritus est ». — Tertiae faciunt unum in *uno corpore mystico;* ad Romanos duodecimo: « Unum corpus sumus in Christo, singuli autem alter alterius membra ». — Quartae faciunt unum in *una beatitudine*, de qua Ioannis decimo septimo: « Pater, rogo, ut sint consummati in unum, sicut et nos unum sumus »; loquebatur de discipulis.

9. *Nuptiae factae sunt* etc. Cum sint quatuor genera nuptiarum, a nuptiis *culpae* revocamur et ad alias invitamur. Sunt autem nuptiae culpae fugiendae, quia habent in principio *ignominiam* et in fine *poenam.* — In principio sunt *ignominiosae;* non enim decet spiritum ad imaginem Dei creatum tam vilem uxorem, sicut est carnalis concupiscentia, ducere; quod figuratum est Genesis vigesimo septimo[^p546-4], dicente Rebecca: « Taedet animam meam vitae meae super filias Heth; si acceperit Iacob uxorem de stirpe huius terrae, nolo vivere ». *Rebecca*, conscientia; *Iacob* filius eius, humanus spiritus; *filiae Heth*, carnales concupiscentiae, quibus si se *coniunxerit* spiritus per consensum, machinatur in mortem conscientiae. — In fine sunt *exitiosae;* consummantur enim in poena, secundum quod dicit Veritas Lucae decimo septimo[^p546-5]: « Sicut factum est in diebus Noe, ita erit in diebus Filii hominis. Edebant et bibebant et ducebant uxores et dabantur ad nuptias usque in diem, qua intravit Noe in arcam; et venit diluvium et perdidit omnes ».

10. Ab istis ergo revocamur, ad alias autem invitamur. Invitamur ad nuptias *gratiae*, ut Deo nubamus; ad nuptias *eucharistiae*, ut ibi discumbamus; ad nuptias *gloriae*, ut ibi regnemus. — Ad nuptias *gratiae* invitatur anima[^p546-6], ut Deo *desponsetur;* ubi cavendum est, ne inveniatur digna *repudio;* quod accidit duabus de causis. Prima est, quando *abnegat debitum* per inobedientiam; secunda est, quando *ingreditur torum alienum* per impudicitiam. — Primum incurrit anima omittendo praecepta; secundum, committendo illicita. Propter primum repudiata est Vasthi, quia noluit obedire Assuero[^p546-7]; propter secundum « licet homini dimittere uxorem causa fornicationis », ut habetur Matthaei decimo nono.

11. Ad nuptias *eucharistiae* invitatur anima, ut ibi *recumbat* et epuletur; ubi cavendum est, ne inveniatur digna *repelli a convivio*, quod fit duabus de causis. Prima est, quando *se gerit nimis superbe;* secunda, quando *non est parata satis honeste.* — Prima causa est in hypocritis vel ambitiosis, in quibus est superbia intentionis; secunda est in lascivis et dissolutis, in quibus est inhonestas conversationis. Propter primum dicitur Lucae decimo quarto[^p546-8]: « Cum invitatus fueris ad nuptias, vide, ne recumbas in primo loco, ne forte honoratior te sit invitatus » etc. Nota totam parabolam. Propter secundum eiectus est de nuptiis ille quem invenit rex « non indutum veste nuptiali », quem iussit « proiici in tenebras exteriores », ut habetur Matthaei vigesimo secundo.

12. Ad nuptias *gloriae* invitatur anima, ut ibi *coronetur;* ubi cavendum est, ne inveniatur digna *repelli a caelesti palatio;* quod fit duabus de causis. Prima est, quando venire *contemnit;* secunda, quando venire *negligit.* — Venire *contempserunt* illi, de quibus Matthaei vigesimo secundo[^p546-9] dicitur, quia « misit rex hora coenae servos suos vocare invitatos ad nuptias », at illi nolebant venire; et causa fuit, ut Lucae decimo quarto tangitur, quia alius *emerat villam* mundanae vanitatis, alius *boves* mundanae
<!-- page 547 -->
curiositatis, alius desponsaverat *concupiscentiam* carnalis voluptatis. De quibus dixit rex: « Quia nemo eorum gustabit coenam meam ». — Venire *neglexerunt* illae *fatuae virgines*, quae tarde venientes, et ideo clausam ianuam invenientes, audierunt: « Nescio vos », sicut habetur Matthaei vigesimo quinto. Propter hoc commendatur vigilantia sponsae Apocalypsis decimo nono, de qua dicitur: « Venerunt nuptiae Agni, et uxor eius praeparavit se » etc.

### Collatio IX.

(Vers. 3.). *Vinum non habent.*

1. O quam bonum est, Matrem Iesu ad suum convivium invitare, quae pauperibus convivii adhibet *solatium* compassionis, *consilium* instructionis, *suffragium* orationis! *Compassionis indicium* est, quod dicit: *Vinum non habent. Instructionis consilium*, quod dixit[^p547-1]: *Quaecumque vobis dixerit, facite. Suffragium orationis* fuit, quod a Filio miraculum conversionis aquae in vinum impetravit.

2. *Vinum non habent.* Notandum, quod est vinum *naturae* — vinum *gratiae* — et vinum *culpae.*

Vinum *naturae* est *moderandum;* Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo[^p547-2]: « Ne comprimaris in convivio » vino. « Intellige, quae sunt proximi tui, ex te ipso ». Est autem moderandum propter tria: ut vivas in *iucunditate* quantum ad *animam;* Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo: « Exsultatio animae et cordis vinum moderate potatum ». Ut vivas in *sanitate* quantum ad *corpus;* Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo: « Aequa vita hominibus vinum in sobrietate »; et infra eodem: « Sanitas est animae et corpori sobrius potus ». Ut vivas in *prosperitate* quantum ad *possessionem*, quia, Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo, « vinum multum potatum irritationem et iram et ruinas multas facit »; et Proverbiorum vigesimo primo: « Qui amat vinum et pinguia non ditabitur ».

3. Vinum *culpae* est *abominabile;* Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo[^p547-3]: « Fel draconum vinum eorum et venenum aspidum insanabile ». Est autem triplex: *luxuriae* vel voluptatis; Amos sexto: « Bibentes vinum in phialis et optimo unguento delibuti »; Sapientiae secundo: « Vino pretioso et unguentis nos impleamus »; ad Ephesios quinto: « Nolite inebriari vino, in quo est luxuria »; Proverbiorum vigesimo: « Luxuriosa res vinum, et tumultuosa ebrietas ». — *Avaritiae* vel cupiditatis; Proverbiorum quarto[^p547-4]: « Comedunt panem impietatis et vinum iniquitatis bibunt »; Ezechielis vigesimo septimo: « Damascenus negotiator tuus in multitudine operum tuorum, in multitudine diversarum opum, in vino pingui, in lanis optimi coloris ». — *Superbiae* vel vanitatis; Habacuc secundo[^p547-5]: « Quomodo vinum decipit potantem, sic erit vir superbus et non decorabitur ». Hoc est vinum illius *meretricis magnae*, id est vanitatis mundanae, « cum qua fornicati sunt reges terrae et inebriati sunt qui inhabitant terram », scilicet « vino prostitutionis eius », Apocalypsis decimo septimo.

4. Vinum hoc sequitur vinum *poenae* triplex: vinum *remorsus conscientiae.* Haec est poena, quae infertur in praesenti; Proverbiorum vigesimo tertio[^p547-6]: « Ne intuearis vinum, quando flavescit, cum splenduerit in vitro color eius; ingreditur blande, sed in fine mordebit ut coluber et sicut regulus » etc. — Vinum *damnationis aeternae*, quae poena sustinenda est in inferno; Apocalypsis decimo quarto: « Si quis adoraverit bestiam et imaginem eius et acceperit characterem eius in fronte sua, aut in manu sua; hic bibet de vino irae Dei, quod mistum est mero in calice ipsius, et cruciabitur igne et sulphure in conspectu Angelorum sanctorum et ante conspectum Agni »; Ieremiae vigesimo quinto: « Sume calicem vini furoris huius de manu mea » etc. — *Temporales calamitates et miseriae;* tertii Esdrae tertio[^p547-7]: « Viri, quam praevalet vinum omnibus hominibus! seducit mentem » etc.; et Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo: « Amaritudo animae vinum multum potatum ».

5. Vinum *gratiae* quadruplex: *Raspetum compunctionis;* Psalmus[^p547-8]: « Ostendisti populo tuo dura; potasti nos vino compunctionis ». — *Rosetum compassionis;* Canticorum quinto: « Comedi favum cum melle meo, bibi vinum meum cum lacte meo », id est planctum compassionis cum laetitia redemptionis, quae inveniuntur in cruce Christi. — *Salmetum devotionis;* Canticorum octavo: « Dabo tibi poculum ex vino condito et mustum pomorum granatorum meorum ». — *Claretum* sive aromaticum *internae fruitionis;* Canticorum primo: « Meliora sunt ubera tua vino, fragrantia unguentis optimis ».
<!-- page 548 -->
6. Vinum *culpae fugiendum*, quia *infatuat;* Isaiae vigesimo octavo[^p548-1]: « Prae vino nescierunt et prae ebrietate erraverunt; absorpti sunt a vino, erraverunt in ebrietate, nescierunt videntem ». — Quia *depauperat;* Proverbiorum vigesimo primo: « Qui amat vinum et pinguia non ditabitur ». — Quia *exterminat;* Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo: « Multos exterminavit vinum »; Proverbiorum vigesimo tertio: « Cui vae? Cuius patri vae? etc. Nonne his qui commorantur in vino et student calicibus epotandis »?

7. Vinum *gratiae* est *appetendum*, quia *sanativum;* Lucae decimo[^p548-2] de Samaritano, qui iussit, plagae vulnerati infundi vinum et oleum. — Quia *confortativum;* tertii Esdrae tertio: « Omnem mentem convertit in securitatem »; primae ad Timotheum quinto: « Modico vino utere propter stomachum tuum et frequentes tuas infirmitates ». — Quia *iucundativum;* Psalmus: « Vinum laetificat cor hominis »; Canticorum primo: « Exsultabimus et laetabimur in te, memores uberum tuorum super vinum ».

### Collatio X.

(Vers. 19.). *Solvite templum hoc, et in tribus diebus excitabo illud.*

1. In verbis istis duo tanguntur. Primo praedicitur *passio* Salvatoris, cum dicitur: *Solvite templum hoc.* — Secundo *resurrectio* ipsius, cum dicitur: *Et in tribus diebus* etc. — *Solvite*, ait, *templum*, « id est, solvetis[^p548-3] ». Scriptura enim aliquando imperativo utitur *imperative*, ut Ecclesiastae ultimo: « Deum time et mandata eius observa ». Aliquando *consultive*, ut Matthaei decimo nono: « Vade et vende omnia, quae habes, et da pauperibus ». Aliquando *permissive*, ut Matthaei octavo: « Si eiicis nos hinc, mitte nos in porcos. Et dixit eis: Ite ». Aliquando *ironice;* Ioannis decimo tertio: « Quod facis fac citius », dixit Dominus Iudae de proditione. Aliquando *praedictive*, ut hic: *Solvite templum* « id est, solvetis ».

2. *Solvite templum.* Nota, quod multiplex est templum *spirituale*, scilicet templum *gloriae* — templum *Ecclesiae* — templum *fidelis animae* — templum *uteri virginalis* — et templum *humanitatis assumtae.* — De templo *gloriae*, Apocalypsis undecimo[^p548-4]: « Apertum est templum Dei in caelo, et visa est arca testamenti eius in templo eius »; et Psalmus: « In templo eius omnes dicent gloriam ». — De templo *Ecclesiae*, Malachiae tertio: « Ecce, ego mitto angelum meum »; et sequitur: « Et statim veniet ad templum suum Dominator, quem vos quaeritis, et Angelus testamenti, quem vos vultis ». *Mitto angelum*, id est Ioannem; *et veniet Dominator*, id est Christus; *ad templum*, id est ad Ecclesiam. — De templo *fidelis animae*, primae ad Corinthios tertio[^p548-5]: « Templum Dei sanctum est, quod estis vos ». — De templo *uteri virginalis*, Ezechielis quadragesimo tertio: « Maiestas Dei ingressa est templum per viam portae, quae respicit ad orientem ». *Maiestas Dei*, plenitudo Divinitatis; *templum*, uterus virginalis; *oriens*, caelestis curia; *porta illuc respiciens*, devotio assidua; *via* Deum adducens, humilitas vera, « quia respexit humilitatem ancillae suae ». De isto templo Augustinus[^p548-6]: « Templum Dei factus est uterus virginalis, nesciens virum ». — De templo *humanitatis assumtae*, hic: *Solvite templum hoc.* Templum hoc fuit aedificatum in incarnatione, solutum in passione et reaedificatum in resurrectione, secundum quod hic dicitur: *Solvite templum hoc, et excitabo illud.*

3. De templo *gloriae* nota, quod est *desiderabile* recordantibus; Ionae secundo[^p548-7]: « Cum angustiaretur anima mea in me, Domini recordatus sum; ut veniat ad te oratio mea ad templum sanctum tuum ». Est *mirabile* contemplantibus; Psalmus: « Sanctum est templum tuum, mirabile in aequitate ». *Delectabile* intrantibus; Psalmus: « Afferentur in laetitia et exsultatione, adducentur in templum regis ».

4. De templo *uteri virginalis* nota, quod fuit a Deo *dedicatum in sanctificatione*, quando scilicet « sanctificavit tabernaculum suum Altissimus », sicut dicitur in Psalmo[^p548-8]. Tunc enim potuit dici: « Sanctum est templum tuum, mirabile in aequitate ». Et re vera in hoc templo mirabilis fuit adaequatio, quando convenerunt fides et ratio, mater et virgo, Deus et homo: primum in *mente*, secundum in *carne*, tertium in *prole.*
<!-- page 549 -->
Fuit a Deo *ornatum in conversatione;* ornatum, inquam, *bonis cogitationibus*, quod figuratum est tertii Regum sexto[^p549-1] per *fenestras obliquas*, quas fecit Salomon in templo. *Fenestrae obliquae* sive vitreae, per quas ingreditur lux, sunt meditationes sanctae, per quas ingreditur divina irradiatio in animam. — Ornatum *sanctis affectionibus*, quod significatum est in eodem[^p549-2], ubi dicitur, quod omnes « cardines ostiorum ex auro erant ». Per *aurum* intellige caritatem; per *cardines* templi, Virginis *affectiones*, quae omnes caritate erant informatae. — Ornatum etiam *virtuosis operibus;* unde in eodem[^p549-3] dicitur, quod Salomon « omnes parietes templi sculpsit variis caelaturis et torno ». In *varietate* nota *multiplicitatem* virtutis; in *sculptura, eminentiam* perfectionis; in *torno, modestiam* discretionis.

Fuit etiam templum a Deo *inhabitatum in incarnatione;* unde Ezechielis quadragesimo tertio[^p549-4]: « Maiestas Dei ingressa est templum ». Nota et expone secundum quod supra. Bernardus: « Nec in terris locus dignior virginalis uteri templo, in quo Dei Filium Maria suscepit; nec in caelis regali solio, in quo Mariam Mariae Filius collocavit ».

5. De templo *humanitatis assumtae* nota, quod locus est *divinae habitationis;* ibi enim visus est temporaliter qui in caelo regnat aeternaliter; et ideo de hoc potest dici illud quod dicitur in Psalmo[^p549-5]: « Dominus in templo sancto suo; Dominus in caelo sedes eius ». In templo enim carnis apparet qui in abscondito caeli sedet. — Est etiam locus *divinae exauditionis;* Psalmus: « Exaudivit de templo sancto suo vocem meam ». De hoc templo exaudivit latronem in cruce pendentem, cui dictum est: « Hodie mecum eris in paradiso ». — Est etiam locus *divinae visitationis*, quia « visitavit nos oriens ex alto[^p549-6] ». Salus enim nostra processit de isto templo, quod figuratum est, Ezechielis quadragesimo septimo, « in aquis, quae descendebant in latus templi dexterum »; de quibus dicitur infra in eodem, quod omnes, ad quos « pervenerit torrens, vivent ». *Aquae*, quae *descendebant in latus templi* dexterum, sunt Sacramenta, quae effluxerunt de latere Christi.

6. De templo *Ecclesiae* nota, quod huiusmodi templum *fuit fundatum* in *Apostolis* et *Patriarchis, aedificatur* in *iustis, consummabitur* in *Beatis.* — *Fuit fundatum* in sanctis Patribus; quod figuratum est tertii Regum quinto[^p549-7], ubi praecepit Salomon, quod « tollerent lapides grandes, lapides pretiosos in fundamentum templi et quadrarent »; *grandes*, virtute; *pretiosos*, caritate; *quadratos*, perfectione. — *Aedificatur* in iustis; ad Ephesios secundo: « Fratres, iam non estis hospites et advenae etc., superaedificati super fundamentum Apostolorum et Prophetarum etc., in quo omnis aedificatio constructa crescit in templum sanctum in Domino ». — *Consummabitur* in Beatis; Ieremiae trigesimo: « Aedificabitur civitas in excelso, et templum iuxta ordinem suum ». *Civitas* haec est Ecclesia, quae dicitur *civitas* ratione *firmitatis*, et *templum* ratione *sanctitatis.*

7. Templum istud debent *praelati aedificare, ornare, custodire. Aedificare* sive instaurare debent verbo praedicationis; primo Esdrae quinto[^p549-8]: « Nos sumus servi Dei et aedificamus templum, quod erat exstructum ante hos annos ». — *Ornare*, exemplo conversationis; Ecclesiastici quadragesimo quinto: « Circumcinxit illum », scilicet Aaron, « tintinnabulis aureis, dare sonitum in incessu suo, auditum facere sonum in templo in memoriam gentis suae » etc. — *Custodire*, vigilantia sollicitudinis *pastoralis*, quod figuratum est primi Paralipomenon nono de levitis, de quibus dicitur, quod « per gyrum templi morabantur in custodiis suis, ut, cum tempus fuisset, aperirent fores ».

8. In isto templo debent *subditi adorare, laudare* et *offerre. Adorare* divinam potentiam; Psalmus[^p549-9]: « Adorabo ad templum sanctum tuum in timore tuo ». *Laudare* divinam sapientiam; Psalmus: « In templo eius omnes dicent gloriam ». *Offerre* confitendo divinam clementiam; Psalmus: « A templo tuo, quod est in Ierusalem, tibi offerent reges munera ». — Vel dic: adorare *corde*, laudare *ore*, offerre *opere.*

9. De templo *fidelis animae* nota, quod fidelis anima dicitur *templum*, quia ad modum templi locus est *sanctitatis, emunitatis* et *religionis.* — Locus est *sanctitatis* a Deo sanctificatus et inhabitatus; primae ad Corinthios tertio[^p549-10]: « Templum Dei
<!-- page 550 -->
sanctum est, quod estis vos »; et ibidem: « Nescitis, quia templum Dei estis vos, et Spiritus Dei habitat in vobis »? — Locus est *emunitatis* a Deo emunitus; unde primae ad Corinthios tertio: « Si quis templum Dei violaverit, disperdet illum Deus »; et tertii Regum octavo: « Si fugerit populus tuus hostes suos » etc. — Locus est *religionis* soli divino cultui dedicatus, propter quod idolorum omnis cultus, id est peccatorum servitus, interdicitur in hoc templo; secundae ad Corinthios sexto: « Quae conventio Christi ad Belial? Aut quae pars fidelis cum infideli? Qui autem consensus templo Dei cum idolis »? quasi dicat: nullus; et adiungitur: « Vos estis templum Dei vivi » etc. — Sic ergo anima fidelis est locus *sanctitatis, emunitatis* et *religionis*, propter quod dicitur *templum Dei.*

10. Sed ecce, peccatores templi huius *polluunt* sanctitatem, *violant* emunitatem, *immutant* religionem. — *Polluunt sanctitatem;* Psalmus[^p550-1]: « Deus, venerunt gentes in hereditatem tuam, polluerunt templum sanctum tuum ». — *Violant emunitatem;* unde Nahum secundo: « Portae fluviorum apertae sunt, et templum ad solum dirutum, et miles captivus abductus est » etc. — *Immutant religionem*, quia templum et vasa eius alienis usibus exponunt; primi Machabaeorum secundo: « Sancta in manus extraneorum facta sunt; templum Dei sicut homo ignobilis; vasa eius captiva abducta sunt ». — Templum illud esset *mundandum* per dolorem poenitentiae, esset *reaedificandum* per profectum iustitiae, esset *ornandum* per decorem scientiae.

### Annotatio 4.

(Vers. 23.). *Signa, quae faciebat, videntes, multi crediderunt in nomine eius.*

Nota, quod aliqui crediderunt in Christum propter *signa*, aliqui propter *verba*, aliqui propter *opera:* propter *signa*, ut hic: *Multi crediderunt* etc. — Propter *verba;* Ioannis quarto[^p550-2]: « Et multo plures crediderunt propter sermonem eius ». — Propter *opera;* Ioannis decimo: « Si non vultis mihi credere, operibus credite ».

### Annotatio 5.

(Vers. 24.). *Ipse autem Iesus non credebat semetipsum eis.*

Nota, quod Christus cavit sibi a *turbis*, a *Iudaeis*, ab *Herode* et a *discipulis.* — A *turbis*, ut hic: *Ipse Iesus non credebat semetipsum eis.* — A *Iudaeis*, Ioannis decimo[^p550-3]: « Quaerebant eum apprehendere, et exivit de manibus eorum ». — Cavit ab *Herode;* Lucae vigesimo tertio: « Interrogabat eum Herodes multis sermonibus. At ipse nihil ei respondit ». — Cavit sibi a *discipulis;* Lucae ultimo, quando « cum illis ibat », et tamen se illis non manifestabat. — A turbis cavit propter illorum *infirmitatem;* a Pharisaeis propter illorum *perversitatem;* ab Herode propter eius *curiositatem;* a discipulis propter eorum *imperfectionem;* docens nos cavere ab *infirmis* et *perversis* et *curiosis* et etiam aliquando ab *amicis.*

### Annotatio 6.

(Vers. 25.). *Ipse enim sciebat, quid esset in homine.*

Nota, quod *homo* accipitur tripliciter. Aliquando accipitur pro *fragilitate naturae*, ut in Psalmo[^p550-4]: « Sciant gentes, quoniam homines sunt ». — Aliquando pro *inordinatione concupiscentiae;* primae ad Corinthios tertio: « Cum sit inter vos zelus et contentio, nonne carnales estis et secundum hominem ambulatis »? — Aliquando pro *dignitate personae;* Sapientiae nono: « Constituisti hominem, ut dominaretur omni creaturae, quae a te facta est ».

### Chapter II. *There was a marriage.*

### Collation VIII.

(Vers. 1.). *There was a marriage in Cana of Galilee, and the Mother of Jesus was there.*

1. The Evangelist, wishing to describe the miracle of the marriage, turns his intention to two things, namely to describing *the banquet of the marriage* — and to describing *the company of those present*, and this not unfittingly. For the miracle was done *at the banquet*, *obtained by those present*[^p545-2]. He begins to describe the *banquet* of the marriage when he says: *There was a marriage; the company of those present*, when he says: *And the Mother of Jesus was there.*

2. *There was a marriage,* he says. Passing over *bodily* marriages — to whose holiness it bears witness that God instituted them by his own authority in *paradise*, as is had in the second chapter of Genesis[^p545-3]: « A man shall leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife »; and consecrated the same on *earth* by his presence and distinguished them with the beginnings of his miracles, as is had in the present Gospel — it is to be noted that according to the *spiritual understanding* there are four kinds of marriages. For there are *carnal* marriages — *spiritual* marriages — *sacramental* marriages — and *eternal* marriages; or the marriages *of fault*, the marriages *of grace*, the marriages *of the eucharist* and the marriages *of glory.*

3. For there is a matrimony *of man* and *carnal concupiscence*[^p545-4], and such has the marriages *of fault.* There is a matrimony *of God* and *the soul*, and such has the marriages *of grace.* There is a matrimony *of Jesus Christ* and *the Church*, and such has in the present the marriages *of the eucharist*, as it were a dinner, and in the future, as it were a supper, the marriages *of glory.* — Thus a *threefold matrimony* has a *fourfold* nuptial *banquet.* The first is held in *the brothel of concupiscence;* the second, in *the bridal chamber of conscience;* the third, in *the tabernacle of the Church;* the fourth, in *the palace of glory.*

4. There are therefore *carnal* marriages, in which there is « the defiling of souls, the changing of birth, the inconstancy of marriages, the disorder of adultery and of uncleanness », according to what is had in the fourteenth chapter of Wisdom[^p545-5]. — The matrimony of such marriages *is begun* in evil delight, *is ratified* in consent, *is consummated* in the deed.

5. There are the *spiritual* marriages *of God* and *the soul;* and these can fittingly be understood by the marriage made in Cana of Galilee. *Cana* is the same as zeal[^p545-6]; *Galilee*, a passing over or passing through, or a wheel or turning. For these marriages are made in the conscience of a soul having the zeal of dilection; which soul indeed passes over into the mountain of the Lord by *devotion of mind*, and is turning by *agility of work.* In these marriages the Lord turns the water of tears into the wine of consolation, most of all if *the Mother of Jesus* be *there.* — But since these marriages belong properly to Religious, it is to be noted that their matrimony *is begun* in the entrance into religion, *is ratified* in the vow of profession, *is consummated* in the perfect fulfilment of the promise.

6. There are also *sacramental* marriages, which are signified in the second chapter of Esther[^p545-7], where Assuerus, having put away Vasthi, « loved Esther more than all the women, and he set the royal crown on her head, and made her queen instead of Vasthi, and he commanded a magnificent feast to be prepared for all the princes and for his servants, for the joining and the marriage of Esther ». *Vasthi*, the synagogue; *Esther*, the Church; *Assuerus*, Christ; *faith*,
<!-- page 546 -->
the joining; *the magnificent feast*, the eucharist. — The matrimony of such marriages *was begun* in the promise of Christ made to the holy Fathers[^p546-1], *was ratified* in the incarnation, *was consummated* in the passion.

7. There are also *eternal* marriages, of which it is said in the nineteenth chapter of the Apocalypse[^p546-2]: « Blessed are they that are called to the marriage supper of the Lamb ». — And the solemnity of those marriages indeed *is begun* in the present in the just through hope of future goods; *is ratified* in the obtaining of the first stole, namely of the soul, and *is consummated* in the obtaining of the second stole, namely of the body.

8. Since therefore there are four kinds of marriages, and every wedlock makes one out of several, it is to be noted that the first make two in *one flesh*, that is, in one carnality; the sixth chapter of the first to the Corinthians[^p546-3]: « Know you not that he who cleaveth to a harlot is made one body? For they shall be, saith he, two in one flesh ». — The second make one in *one spirit;* the sixth chapter of the first to the Corinthians: « He who cleaveth to God is one spirit ». — The third make one in *one mystical body;* the twelfth chapter to the Romans: « We are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another ». — The fourth make one in *one beatitude*, of which the seventeenth chapter of John: « Father, I pray that they may be made perfect in one, as we also are one »; he was speaking of the disciples.

9. *There was a marriage* etc. Since there are four kinds of marriages, we are called back from the marriages *of fault* and invited to the others. Now the marriages of fault are to be fled, because they have *ignominy* in the beginning and *punishment* in the end. — In the beginning they are *ignominious;* for it does not become a spirit created to the image of God to take so vile a wife as carnal concupiscence is; which was figured in the twenty-seventh chapter of Genesis[^p546-4], where Rebecca says: « I am weary of my life because of the daughters of Heth; if Jacob take a wife of the stock of this land, I choose not to live ». *Rebecca*, conscience; *Jacob* her son, the human spirit; *the daughters of Heth*, carnal concupiscences, to which if the spirit *join* itself through consent, it plots the death of conscience. — In the end they are *deadly;* for they are consummated in punishment, according to what the Truth says in the seventeenth chapter of Luke[^p546-5]: « As it came to pass in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man. They did eat and drink, they married wives and were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark; and the flood came and destroyed them all ».

10. From these, then, we are called back, but to the others we are invited. We are invited to the marriages *of grace*, that we may wed God; to the marriages *of the eucharist*, that we may sit at table there; to the marriages *of glory*, that we may reign there. — The soul is invited to the marriages *of grace*[^p546-6], that it may *be espoused* to God; where it must take care lest it be found worthy *of repudiation;* which happens for two causes. The first is when it *denies the debt* through disobedience; the second is when it *enters another's bed* through unchastity. — The first the soul incurs by omitting the precepts; the second, by committing unlawful things. For the first, Vasthi was put away, because she would not obey Assuerus[^p546-7]; for the second « it is lawful for a man to put away his wife for the cause of fornication », as is had in the nineteenth chapter of Matthew.

11. The soul is invited to the marriages *of the eucharist*, that it may *recline* there and feast; where it must take care lest it be found worthy *to be driven from the banquet*, which happens for two causes. The first is when it *bears itself too proudly;* the second, when it *is not prepared honourably enough.* — The first cause is in hypocrites or the ambitious, in whom is pride of intention; the second is in the wanton and dissolute, in whom is dishonourableness of conversation. For the first it is said in the fourteenth chapter of Luke[^p546-8]: « When thou art invited to a wedding, see that thou sit not down in the first place, lest perhaps one more honourable than thou be invited » etc. Note the whole parable. For the second, he was cast out of the marriage whom the king found « not having on a wedding garment », whom he commanded « to be cast into the exterior darkness », as is had in the twenty-second chapter of Matthew.

12. The soul is invited to the marriages *of glory*, that it may *be crowned* there; where it must take care lest it be found worthy *to be driven from the heavenly palace;* which happens for two causes. The first is when it *scorns* to come; the second, when it *neglects* to come. — Those *scorned* to come of whom it is said in the twenty-second chapter of Matthew[^p546-9] that « the king sent his servants at the hour of supper to call them that were invited to the marriage », and they would not come; and the cause was, as is touched on in the fourteenth chapter of Luke, that one *had bought a farm* of worldly vanity, another *oxen* of worldly
<!-- page 547 -->
curiosity, another had espoused the *concupiscence* of carnal pleasure. Of whom the king said: « That none of them shall taste my supper ». — Those *foolish virgins neglected* to come who, coming late and therefore finding the door shut, heard: « I know you not », as is had in the twenty-fifth chapter of Matthew. For this reason the watchfulness of the bride is commended in the nineteenth chapter of the Apocalypse, of whom it is said: « The marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath prepared herself » etc.

### Collation IX.

(Vers. 3.). *They have no wine.*

1. O how good it is to invite the Mother of Jesus to one's banquet, who brings to the poor of the banquet the *solace* of compassion, the *counsel* of instruction, the *suffrage* of prayer! *The indication of compassion* is that she says: *They have no wine. The counsel of instruction*, that she said[^p547-1]: *Whatsoever he shall say to you, do ye. The suffrage of prayer* was that she obtained from the Son the miracle of the turning of water into wine.

2. *They have no wine.* It is to be noted that there is the wine *of nature* — the wine *of grace* — and the wine *of fault.*

The wine *of nature* is *to be moderated;* the thirty-first chapter of Ecclesiasticus[^p547-2]: « Be not straitened at a banquet » with wine. « Understand what belongs to thy neighbour by thyself ». Now it is to be moderated for three reasons: that thou mayest live in *gladness* as regards the *soul;* the thirty-first chapter of Ecclesiasticus: « Wine drunken with moderation is the joy of the soul and the heart ». That thou mayest live in *health* as regards the *body;* the thirty-first chapter of Ecclesiasticus: « Wine drunken with sobriety is equal life to men »; and below in the same: « Sober drinking is health to soul and body ». That thou mayest live in *prosperity* as regards *possession*, because, in the thirty-first chapter of Ecclesiasticus, « wine drunken much causeth provocation and anger and many ruins »; and in the twenty-first chapter of Proverbs: « He that loveth wine and fat things shall not be rich ».

3. The wine *of fault* is *abominable;* the thirty-second chapter of Deuteronomy[^p547-3]: « Their wine is the gall of dragons and the venom of asps, which is incurable ». Now it is threefold: of *lust* or pleasure; the sixth chapter of Amos: « That drink wine in bowls and anoint themselves with the best ointments »; the second chapter of Wisdom: « Let us fill ourselves with costly wine and ointments »; the fifth chapter to the Ephesians: « Be not drunk with wine, wherein is luxury »; the twentieth chapter of Proverbs: « Wine is a luxurious thing, and drunkenness riotous ». — Of *avarice* or cupidity; the fourth chapter of Proverbs[^p547-4]: « They eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of iniquity »; the twenty-seventh chapter of Ezechiel: « The Damascene was thy merchant in the multitude of thy works, in the multitude of divers riches, in rich wine, in wool of the best colour ». — Of *pride* or vanity; the second chapter of Habacuc[^p547-5]: « As wine deceiveth him that drinketh it, so shall the proud man be, and he shall not be honoured ». This is the wine of that *great harlot*, that is, of worldly vanity, « with whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and they who inhabit the earth have been made drunk », namely « with the wine of her whoredom », the seventeenth chapter of the Apocalypse.

4. This wine is followed by a threefold wine *of punishment:* the wine *of the remorse of conscience.* This is the punishment that is inflicted in the present; the twenty-third chapter of Proverbs[^p547-6]: « Look not upon the wine when it is yellow, when the colour thereof shineth in the glass; it goeth in pleasantly, but in the end it will bite like a snake and like a basilisk » etc. — The wine *of eternal damnation*, which punishment is to be borne in hell; the fourteenth chapter of the Apocalypse: « If any man shall adore the beast and his image, and receive his character in his forehead or in his hand; he also shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is mingled with pure wine in the cup of his wrath, and shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the sight of the holy Angels and in the sight of the Lamb »; the twenty-fifth chapter of Jeremias: « Take the cup of wine of this fury at my hand » etc. — *Temporal calamities and miseries;* the third chapter of the third of Esdras[^p547-7]: « O ye men, how strong is wine above all men! it deceiveth the mind » etc.; and the thirty-first chapter of Ecclesiasticus: « Wine drunken with excess is bitterness of the soul ».

5. The wine *of grace* is fourfold: *the raspetum of compunction;* the Psalm[^p547-8]: « Thou hast shewn thy people hard things; thou hast made us drink the wine of compunction ». — *The rosetum of compassion;* the fifth chapter of the Canticles: « I have eaten the honeycomb with my honey, I have drunk my wine with my milk », that is, the lament of compassion with the joy of redemption, which are found in the cross of Christ. — *The salmetum of devotion;* the eighth chapter of the Canticles: « I will give thee a cup of spiced wine and new wine of my pomegranates ». — *The claretum*, or spiced wine, *of inward fruition;* the first chapter of the Canticles: « Thy breasts are better than wine, smelling sweet of the best ointments ».
<!-- page 548 -->
6. The wine *of fault is to be fled*, because it *makes foolish;* the twenty-eighth chapter of Isaias[^p548-1]: « Through wine they knew not, and through drunkenness they erred; they are swallowed up with wine, they have gone astray in drunkenness, they have not known him that seeth ». — Because it *impoverishes;* the twenty-first chapter of Proverbs: « He that loveth wine and fat things shall not be rich ». — Because it *destroys;* the thirty-first chapter of Ecclesiasticus: « Wine hath destroyed very many »; the twenty-third chapter of Proverbs: « Who hath woe? To whose father woe? etc. Surely they that pass their time in wine, and study to drink off their cups »?

7. The wine *of grace is to be desired*, because it is *healing;* the tenth chapter of Luke[^p548-2], of the Samaritan, who had wine and oil poured into the wounds of the wounded man. — Because it is *strengthening;* the third chapter of the third of Esdras: « It turneth every mind into security »; the fifth chapter of the first to Timothy: « Use a little wine for thy stomach's sake and thy frequent infirmities ». — Because it is *gladdening;* the Psalm: « Wine maketh glad the heart of man »; the first chapter of the Canticles: « We will be glad and rejoice in thee, remembering thy breasts more than wine ».

### Collation X.

(Vers. 19.). *Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.*

1. In these words two things are touched. First the *passion* of the Saviour is foretold, when it is said: *Destroy this temple.* — Second his *resurrection*, when it is said: *And in three days* etc. — *Destroy*, he says, *the temple*, « that is, you will destroy[^p548-3] ». For Scripture sometimes uses the imperative *imperatively*, as in the last chapter of Ecclesiastes: « Fear God and keep his commandments ». Sometimes *by way of counsel*, as in the nineteenth chapter of Matthew: « Go, sell all that thou hast, and give to the poor ». Sometimes *by way of permission*, as in the eighth chapter of Matthew: « If thou cast us out hence, send us into the swine. And he said to them: Go ». Sometimes *ironically;* the thirteenth chapter of John: « That which thou dost, do quickly », the Lord said to Judas of the betrayal. Sometimes *by way of prediction*, as here: *Destroy the temple*, « that is, you will destroy ».

2. *Destroy the temple.* Note that the *spiritual* temple is manifold, namely the temple *of glory* — the temple *of the Church* — the temple *of the faithful soul* — the temple *of the virginal womb* — and the temple *of the assumed humanity.* — Of the temple *of glory*, the eleventh chapter of the Apocalypse[^p548-4]: « The temple of God was opened in heaven, and the ark of his testament was seen in his temple »; and the Psalm: « In his temple all shall speak his glory ». — Of the temple *of the Church*, the third chapter of Malachias: « Behold, I send my angel »; and there follows: « And presently the Ruler, whom you seek, shall come to his temple, and the Angel of the testament, whom you desire ». *I send my angel*, that is, John; *and the Ruler shall come*, that is, Christ; *to the temple*, that is, to the Church. — Of the temple *of the faithful soul*, the third chapter of the first to the Corinthians[^p548-5]: « The temple of God is holy, which you are ». — Of the temple *of the virginal womb*, the forty-third chapter of Ezechiel: « The majesty of God went into the temple by the way of the gate that looked to the east ». *The majesty of God*, the fulness of the Divinity; *the temple*, the virginal womb; *the east*, the heavenly court; *the gate looking thither*, assiduous devotion; *the way* leading God in, true humility, « because he hath regarded the humility of his handmaid ». Of this temple Augustine[^p548-6]: « The virginal womb, knowing not man, was made the temple of God ». — Of the temple *of the assumed humanity*, here: *Destroy this temple.* This temple was built in the incarnation, destroyed in the passion and rebuilt in the resurrection, according to what is said here: *Destroy this temple, and I will raise it up.*

3. Of the temple *of glory* note that it is *desirable* to those who remember; the second chapter of Jonas[^p548-7]: « When my soul was in distress within me, I remembered the Lord; that my prayer may come to thee, unto thy holy temple ». It is *wonderful* to those who contemplate; the Psalm: « Holy is thy temple, wonderful in justice ». *Delightful* to those who enter; the Psalm: « They shall be brought with gladness and rejoicing, they shall be brought into the temple of the king ».

4. Of the temple *of the virginal womb* note that it was *dedicated* by God *in sanctification*, namely when « the most High sanctified his own tabernacle », as is said in the Psalm[^p548-8]. For then it could be said: « Holy is thy temple, wonderful in justice ». And in very truth there was in this temple a wonderful equalling, when faith and reason, mother and virgin, God and man came together: the first in the *mind*, the second in the *flesh*, the third in the *offspring.*
<!-- page 549 -->
It was *adorned* by God *in conversation;* adorned, I say, *with good thoughts*, which was figured in the sixth chapter of the third of Kings[^p549-1] by the *slanting windows* which Solomon made in the temple. The *slanting* or glass *windows*, through which light enters, are holy meditations, through which the divine irradiation enters into the soul. — Adorned *with holy affections*, which is signified in the same[^p549-2], where it is said that all « the hinges of the doors were of gold ». By *gold* understand charity; by the *hinges* of the temple, the *affections* of the Virgin, which were all informed by charity. — Adorned also *with virtuous works;* whence in the same[^p549-3] it is said that Solomon « carved all the walls of the temple with divers figures and turning ». In the *variety* note the *multiplicity* of virtue; in the *carving*, the *eminence* of perfection; in the *turning*, the *modesty* of discretion.

The temple was also *inhabited* by God *in the incarnation;* whence the forty-third chapter of Ezechiel[^p549-4]: « The majesty of God went into the temple ». Note and expound according to what was said above. Bernard: « Nor is there on earth a worthier place than the temple of the virginal womb, in which Mary received the Son of God; nor in heaven than the royal throne, on which the Son of Mary placed Mary ».

5. Of the temple *of the assumed humanity* note that it is the place *of the divine dwelling;* for there he was seen in time who reigns in heaven eternally; and therefore of it can be said what is said in the Psalm[^p549-5]: « The Lord is in his holy temple; the Lord's throne is in heaven ». For in the temple of the flesh he appears who sits in the hidden place of heaven. — It is also the place *of the divine hearing;* the Psalm: « He heard my voice from his holy temple ». From this temple he heard the thief hanging on the cross, to whom it was said: « This day thou shalt be with me in paradise ». — It is also the place *of the divine visitation*, because « the Orient from on high hath visited us[^p549-6] ». For our salvation went forth from this temple, which was figured, in the forty-seventh chapter of Ezechiel, « in the waters that came down to the right side of the temple »; of which it is said below in the same that all to whom « the torrent shall come shall live ». *The waters* that *came down to the side of the temple* on the right are the Sacraments, which flowed out from the side of Christ.

6. Of the temple *of the Church* note that this temple *was founded* in the *Apostles* and *Patriarchs, is built* in the *just, will be consummated* in the *Blessed.* — *It was founded* in the holy Fathers; which was figured in the fifth chapter of the third of Kings[^p549-7], where Solomon commanded that « they should bring great stones, costly stones, for the foundation of the temple, and should square them »; *great*, in virtue; *costly*, in charity; *squared*, in perfection. — *It is built* in the just; the second chapter to the Ephesians: « Brethren, now you are no more strangers and foreigners etc., built upon the foundation of the Apostles and Prophets etc., in whom all the building, being framed together, groweth up into a holy temple in the Lord ». — *It will be consummated* in the Blessed; the thirtieth chapter of Jeremias: « The city shall be built in her height, and the temple according to the order thereof ». This *city* is the Church, which is called a *city* by reason of *firmness*, and a *temple* by reason of *holiness.*

7. This temple *prelates* ought *to build, to adorn, to guard.* They ought *to build* or restore it by the word of preaching; the fifth chapter of the first of Esdras[^p549-8]: « We are the servants of God, and we are building the temple that was built these many years ago ». — *To adorn* it, by the example of conversation; the forty-fifth chapter of Ecclesiasticus: « He compassed him », namely Aaron, « with golden bells, to give a sound as he went, to make a noise that might be heard in the temple, for a memorial to the children of his people » etc. — *To guard* it, by the watchfulness of *pastoral* solicitude, which was figured in the ninth chapter of the first of Paralipomenon concerning the levites, of whom it is said that they « remained round about the temple in their wards, that when the time was, they might open the doors ».

8. In this temple *subjects* ought *to adore, to praise* and *to offer. To adore* the divine might; the Psalm[^p549-9]: « I will worship towards thy holy temple in thy fear ». *To praise* the divine wisdom; the Psalm: « In his temple all shall speak his glory ». *To offer*, confessing the divine clemency; the Psalm: « From thy temple which is in Jerusalem, kings shall offer presents to thee ». — Or say: to adore *with the heart*, to praise *with the mouth*, to offer *with the work.*

9. Of the temple *of the faithful soul* note that the faithful soul is called a *temple* because, after the manner of a temple, it is a place *of holiness, of immunity* and *of religion.* — It is a place *of holiness*, sanctified and inhabited by God; the third chapter of the first to the Corinthians[^p549-10]: « The temple of God
<!-- page 550 -->
is holy, which you are »; and in the same place: « Know you not that you are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you »? — It is a place *of immunity*, made immune by God; whence the third chapter of the first to the Corinthians: « If any man violate the temple of God, him shall God destroy »; and the eighth chapter of the third of Kings: « If thy people flee before their enemies » etc. — It is a place *of religion*, dedicated to the divine worship alone, for which reason all worship of idols, that is, the servitude of sins, is forbidden in this temple; the sixth chapter of the second to the Corinthians: « What concord hath Christ with Belial? Or what part hath the faithful with the unbeliever? And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols »? as if to say: none; and there is added: « You are the temple of the living God » etc. — Thus, then, the faithful soul is a place *of holiness, of immunity* and *of religion*, for which reason it is called *the temple of God.*

10. But behold, sinners *pollute* the holiness of this temple, *violate* its immunity, *change* its religion. — *They pollute its holiness;* the Psalm[^p550-1]: « O God, the heathens are come into thy inheritance, they have defiled thy holy temple ». — *They violate its immunity;* whence the second chapter of Nahum: « The gates of the rivers are opened, and the temple is thrown down to the ground, and the soldier is led away captive » etc. — *They change its religion*, because they expose the temple and its vessels to strange uses; the second chapter of the first of Machabees: « The holy things are come into the hands of strangers; her temple is become as a man without honour; the vessels of her glory are carried away captive ». — That temple would need *to be cleansed* by the sorrow of penance, would need *to be rebuilt* by the advance of justice, would need *to be adorned* by the comeliness of knowledge.

### Annotation 4.

(Vers. 23.). *Seeing the signs which he did, many believed in his name.*

Note that some believed in Christ on account of *signs*, some on account of *words*, some on account of *works:* on account of *signs*, as here: *Many believed* etc. — On account of *words;* the fourth chapter of John[^p550-2]: « And many more believed because of his own word ». — On account of *works;* the tenth chapter of John: « If you will not believe me, believe the works ».

### Annotation 5.

(Vers. 24.). *But Jesus did not trust himself unto them.*

Note that Christ guarded himself from *the crowds*, from *the Jews*, from *Herod* and from *the disciples.* — From *the crowds*, as here: *Jesus did not trust himself unto them.* — From *the Jews*, the tenth chapter of John[^p550-3]: « They sought to take him, and he escaped out of their hands ». — He guarded himself from *Herod;* the twenty-third chapter of Luke: « Herod questioned him in many words. But he answered him nothing ». — He guarded himself from *the disciples;* the last chapter of Luke, when « he went with them », and yet did not make himself manifest to them. — He guarded himself from the crowds on account of their *infirmity;* from the Pharisees on account of their *perversity;* from Herod on account of his *curiosity;* from the disciples on account of their *imperfection;* teaching us to beware of the *infirm* and the *perverse* and the *curious*, and even sometimes of *friends.*

### Annotation 6.

(Vers. 25.). *For he knew what was in man.*

Note that *man* is taken in three ways. Sometimes it is taken for *the frailty of nature*, as in the Psalm[^p550-4]: « Let the Gentiles know themselves to be men ». — Sometimes for *the disorder of concupiscence;* the third chapter of the first to the Corinthians: « Whereas there is among you zeal and contention, are you not carnal, and walk according to man »? — Sometimes for *the dignity of the person;* the ninth chapter of Wisdom: « Thou hast appointed man, that he should have dominion over every creature that was made by thee ».
