## Apparatus

[^tr-doctrina]: **En.** Translator's note: the Latin *doctrina* holds together what English divides among doctrine, teaching and learning. Here it stands four times in Schema II, all within the branch that summarises Capitula VII–VIII and III: *doctrina Domini* (the Lord's teaching of Nicodemus), *verbo doctrinae*, *doctrinae communicatio* and *doctrinae nobilitas* — the Doctor's showing himself director by the word of teaching, the teaching given out to the people at the feast, and the nobility of his teaching. It is a teaching given and heard, not a body of propositions, so "teaching" is the English at all four, as `bon-ioan-c7-v1-13`, `bon-ioan-c7-v19-24` and `bon-ioan-c8-v12-20` render the passages these branches summarise.
