# d.33 littera — Tier-2 ambiguities (2026-05-07)

Source: `vol1/bon-sent-I-d33-littera.md`. Raw OCR pt2 lines 15062–15351; printed pp. 567–569; PDF pt2 pp. 157–159 (300/500 dpi reads).

## Inline `[?]` flags

1. **Latin body, p.567 Cap. I opening**: `an ita sint personis[?], ut non sint personae` — OCR shows fragmented column read; the printed PDF clearly reads "personis" (in [the] persons), but Latin grammar would expect ablative absolute or "in personis" / "personae". Tentatively rendered as "personis"; printed text may have an idiomatic dative or be a typo for "in personis". 500-dpi PDF re-read confirms "sint personis" — leaving as printed.

2. **Latin body, p.568, end of Hilary chain**: `quod proprietas Filii filius sit, sic et Deus[?]` — OCR garble "ftius/Deus"; the PDF reads `filius sit, sic et Deus`. Sense: the property of the Son is *son*, and likewise *God* — that is, the property is both the person and the divine essence. Reading retained; flag for verification at decade polish.

3. **Apparatus [^9]**: `Libr. II, de Trin. n. 8, sed nonnullis a Magistro [in]transpositis et mutatis. [?]` — OCR shows `i transpositis` (line 15224); reading either "in-" (= "ill-transposed") or "iis" (= "those") is possible. The 500-dpi PDF shows what appears to be "nonnullis ... transpositis"; the bracketed `[in]` is conjectural. Hilary *de Trinitate* II.8 is the cited source.

4. **Apparatus [^10]**: `De Fide orth. III, c. 6. Vide supra d. XXVII. p. [I]. [?]` — OCR truncates the part-numeral after `p.`. The cross-reference is to a previous distinction's part. Likely "p. II." since d.XXVII has a pars II (cf. apparatus [^18]). Left bracketed as `[I]` for verification.

5. **Apparatus [^15]**: `Post non substan­tiae codd. et plurimae edd. contra Vat., edd. 6, 8 et origini[s] omittunt nomen. [?]` — OCR ends `et origini` mid-word at line 15332; likely "originali" or "origini[s]". Left as `origini[s]`.

6. **Apparatus [^17]**: `Codd. et edd. 3, 1 substantiae, quod repetunt infra ar[...] id est. [?]` — OCR truncates `ar` (line 15338: `ar id est`); printed page edge cut the word. Likely `articulo` or short `articuli`. Left as `ar[...]`.

## Resolved silently (OCR garbles fixed against PDF eyes-on)

- `delerminantur` → `determinantur`; `personarwm` → `personarum`; `vobabula` → `vocabula` (etc.) — standard OCR letter-substitution corrections.
- `usia` retained as-is in the chapter title — Greek loanword (οὐσία) appearing in printed Quaracchi and standard in the Lombard tradition; not a typo for `essentia`.
- `Bethii` → `Boethii`; `Damascenus` (consistent across PDF and OCR).
- `mecum est et` and similar Vulgate-cross-reference apparatus retained verbatim from raw OCR.
