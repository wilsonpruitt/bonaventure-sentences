# Tier-2 ambiguities — bon-sent-I-d34-a1-q3

Source: OCR raw lines 17543–17680 of `raw/bonaventure_vol1_pt2_raw.txt`; PDF pp. 181–182 (printed pp. 591–592) of `raw/doctorisseraphic12bona.pdf`.

- **bon-sent-I-d34-a1-q3, fundam. 1, apparatus [^1] target text**: OCR has `et q. i, ;ic ilub. 3` (line 17623). Read as `et q. 1, sic dub. 3`, but `;ic` glyph could be `sic` or `hic`. Currently rendered `sic[?]` in apparatus. → Plausibly `hic` (a common Quaracchi cross-reference idiom: "et q. 1, hic dub. 3"). Re-verify on 600dpi PDF on next polish pass.

- **bon-sent-I-d34-a1-q3, respondeo, body anchor for [^7] (`In non paucis codd. desideratur tum`)**: OCR p.591 right column footnote 7 references a missing/extra particle *tum*, but the body text in OCR (lines 17559–17604, 17605–17621) does not show an unambiguous *tum* or footnote-marker glyph. Anchor placed at "et sic[^7] de aliis" / "and so[^7] of the others" as a plausible site for the variant correlative *tum sic*. → Resolve at next decade polish (d.31–d.40 polish blocker).

- **bon-sent-I-d34-a1-q3, ad 2, "intellectus appropriat[^8] propter intellectum"**: OCR has the prima facie reading "intellectus appropriat" with apparatus note that Vat. and cod. cc had wrongly *appropriatur* (passive). The Quaracchi editors' emendation is followed; flag retained to confirm marker position on PDF re-pass.
