## n. 10 (apparatus, p. 617 footer entry n. 10) — `abscondi a lumine [?] lumine`

**Context.** The note records a manuscript variant in the Augustine quotation about sins being hidden from the light of God's truth. Quaracchi cites: *Paulo ante codd. abscondi a lumine | lumine.*

**OCR raw (lines 20036–20040):**
```
omissis. Paulo ante codd. abscondi a lumine          |
...
tumine.
```

The `|` glyph is almost certainly a column-rule artifact, and the trailing `tumine` is OCR'd `lumine` (`t` mistaken for `l`). The Quaracchi editors are likely flagging that some codices read *abscondi a lumine* in place of (or paired with) a different *lumine* construction one or two lines earlier in the Master's text. Without the column-clean source the exact contrast is opaque.

**Currently rendered.** `abscondi a lumine [?] lumine` with a parenthetical reader-note explaining the OCR fragmentation.

**Resolution disposition.** Defer to the d.31–d.40 polish-blocker pass (every-decade `[?]` resolution gate); the variant does not affect the body text — it is a marginal apparatus note about a manuscript reading.
