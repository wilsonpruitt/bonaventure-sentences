## Flagged items

**bon-sent-I-d22-a1-q3, scholion §I (Richard of Mediavilla quotation), footnote [^27]**: OCR lacuna. The djvu OCR truncates Richard's text at `intelligimus di-` (line 68347) and resumes at `sed ratione huius, quod per nomen` (line 68350). Two short clauses are missing — likely "[intelligimus di-]stincte rem significatam in creatura prius existentem; sed ratione huius, quod per nomen [intendimus rem in Deo prius existentem]" or similar. The chunk renders these as `[?]` flags inside the Richard quotation. → Resolve by consulting Quaracchi printed p. 396 column 2 directly (PDF page 498) at 400 dpi, or by looking up Richard of Mediavilla, *Comm. in I Sent.* d. 22, q. 4 ad 1 in a critical edition.

**bon-sent-I-d22-a1-q3, footnote [^23] (Notandum)**: OCR has `Notanduni, quod : spicit terminum, enuntiare autem propositionem` (line 68388). The subject of `respicit` is dropped — almost certainly `dicere`. Rendered in the Latin apparatus as `*[dicere?]*` and translated as "*[to say]*". → Verify against Quaracchi printed p. 396 footnote 7.
