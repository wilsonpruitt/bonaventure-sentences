## Cap. VII heading — chapter numbering of Pars II

**Context.** In the OCR (raw lines 21452–21915) the explicit chapter labels found in the Pars II range are: `Cap. VI` (line ~21743), `Cap. VIII` (line ~21785), and `Cap. IX` (line ~21853). No `Cap. V` and no `Cap. VII` labels are visible — they are likely either OCR-dropped or hidden by column-rule artifacts. Context strongly suggests:

- `Cap. V` = the section beginning « Cum Deus sit ubique et semper, non tamen localis, nec loco nec tempore movetur. » at line 21728 (the rubric-style title that opens Pars II).
- `Cap. VII` = the section « Utrum spiritus creati sint locales et circumscriptibiles » at line ~21785.

I have rendered the chunk with Capp. V, VI, VII, VIII, and have flagged the heading « *Whether created spirits are local and circumscriptible.* » with `[?]` because the OCR explicit label at the same printed position is `Cap. VIII` rather than `Cap. VII`. Either Quaracchi has indeed numbered these chapters V–VI–VIII–IX (skipping VII), or the OCR has dropped one heading earlier in the column. Either is bibliographically possible (Lombard's Distinction XXXVII has a contested chapter division across editions).

**Resolution disposition.** Defer to the d.31–d.40 polish-blocker pass against the 600-dpi PDF; if Quaracchi truly skips VII, restore the OCR labels (V, VI, VIII, IX) with a brief editorial note.
