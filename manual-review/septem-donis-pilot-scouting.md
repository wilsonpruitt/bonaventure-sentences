## ⛔ The Hexaemeron's decisive test comes out the OTHER WAY here

The frozen Hexaemeron rule reads: *"THE CHUNK IS THE COLLATIO, and the frozen test decided it:
`COLLATIO I.` CARRIES APPARATUS ANCHOR ¹."* **It does not here.**

Read at 10× on the 450 dpi plate and compared side by side with p. 329:

- **p. 329 (Hexaemeron):** a well-formed superscript standing **after** the period — `COLLATIO I.` ¹ —
  clearly raised above cap height, and answered by a footer note documenting the work's title in
  the codices.
- **p. 457 (this work):** the only mark is a small comma-shaped speck **between** `COLLATIO` and
  `I.`, at cap height, a few pixels across and of no digit shape. **Nothing stands after the
  period.** p. 457's seven footer notes are all body-anchored (n. 1 = the `Hortamur vos` scripture
  note on ¶ 1); none documents the title or the codices.

**Why this is worth writing down rather than shrugging at.** The Hexaemeron's anchor is what
*proved* the collatio was the annotated unit there. Absent it, the collatio is still the right
chunk unit here — the volume index lists exactly these nine, the running head names them, and
Quaracchi cites them as `de donis coll. N. n. M` — **but it is settled by the index and the
citation practice, not by an anchor.** Do not go looking for an anchor that is not there, and do
not carry the Hexaemeron's sentence forward into this work's notes.

⚠ **The raw is not diagnostic either way.** Both headings render as `COLLATIO<double space><numeral>`
in the djvu OCR — p. 329's real anchor is swallowed exactly as p. 457's absent one is. This is the
standing Vol V fact (the OCR has no footnote numerals at all) applied to a heading; it means the
anchor question can only ever be answered on the plate.

## What DOES port from the Hexaemeron

- **Each collatio opens with a `SUMMARIUM`, and it is RENDERED, not trimmed.** Verified on p. 457:
  full-measure editorial synopsis in small italic with small-cap opener, keyed to the collatio's
  paragraph numbers with em-dash articulations (`Introductio, numero 1. 2. — Divisio argumenti, 3.
  — Pars I. De gratiae ortu. …`). **It carries no anchor.** Render as `### Summarium` at the head
  of each language block, references verbatim, body following.
- **The `SUMMARIUM` header garbles** — p. 457 reads `SuM.MARiiiM`, p. 507 `SuMMARiuM`. **Find it by
  content, never by header grep.**
- **★ COUNT THE BODY, EVERY TIME.** The c6 and c14 mechanism (the Summarium running short of its
  own body) is a property of the genre, not of the Hexaemeron. Collatio I's Summarium ends at
  `17. 18.` and the body's numbered paragraphs run 1–18 — agreement, this once.
- **Marginalia are present AS WELL as the Summarium** (p. 457 col. 1 alone carries *Introductio* ·
  *In Christo omnia benedicuntur* · *De Lege et gratia* · *Notandum* · *Pars I. De gratiae ortu* ·
  *Tria consideranda*) and are trimmed to the Marginalia list in `## Notes` per the standing Vol V
  convention.
- **Division titles go into the `WORKS` registry ONE AT A TIME**, each verified against the in-place
  printed subtitle. Collatio I's index text and in-place subtitle were compared and **agree**
  (*Praemittitur tractatio de gratia secundum eius ortum, usum et fructum.*).
- **Fix every span from the next real bare `COLLATIO N.` header on the band, never from a running
  head.** The running head here is `DE DONIS SPIRITUS S. COLLATIO N.` and garbles (`SPIRITLS`,
  `SI>1RITUS`, `SPIR[TUS`, `DOiMS`, `COLLATIO LX` for IX); the real header garbles too
  (`COLLATIO IL` = II, `in.` = III, `YL` = VI, `VIL` = VII, `VIIL` = VIII).

## Gutter — p. 457

`colcrop.py vol5 457` measures **1203 on a 51 px run** — under the frozen 60 px floor, so it was
escalated. The skew screen reports a **166 px drift** and names the outliers itself (slices 0.08,
0.17, 0.25) — **this is the work-opening case, not skew**: the full-width `COLLATIO I.` heading,
the subtitle and the Summarium stack across the gutter at the head of the leaf. Discounting them,
the direct per-column profile over rows 0.36–0.95 is stable: **band 1181–1229 (49 px), centre rule
1195–1215 (peak 269), midpoint → 1204.** Three windows (0.36 / 0.42 / 0.50 low) agree at 1202–1205.
**Adopt 1204.** The 51 px run is the centre rule inking heavily, the ordinary Vol V cause.

Expect the same at **every one of the nine collatio openings**, since each opens with a full-width
heading + Summarium: the default window will land on the stack and report nonsense. Profile below
the Summarium.

## Cadence

- **Chunks:** `bon-don-c{1..9}`, `work: septem-donis`, `division: 1..9`, `type: collatio`.
  **No suffix-less slug in this work** — the census blind-spot class does not apply here (there is
  no prologue and no scholion), the first Vol V work of which that is true.
- **~5.2 printed pp per chunk**, ~7 footer notes per page → expect ~35 apparatus entries per chunk.
- **ONE gate, at the work close** (49 pp, per the frozen table). The shakedown trigger does not
  fire separately: this is not a new register, it is the register the Hexaemeron just spent 128
  pages exercising, and the one convention that differs (the heading anchor) is settled above.
- **Deploy boundary = work close.**
- **Plates per collatio, never in bulk** (8 GB machine).
