# d.1 a.1 q.3 — Tier-2 ambiguities (2026-05-07)

**bon-sent-I-d1-a1-q3, Apparatus [^3] "ed. 4" [?]**: OCR raw line 14049 reads `obni-tentibus antiquioribus mss. et ed. 4, post unum omittit increatum`. The digit `4` is anomalous — every other footnote in this chunk and the surrounding distinction cites `ed. 1` (the editio princeps Romana 1588). Quaracchi practice is to compare codices against ed. 1; ed. 4 (= editio Vaticana 1596) is referred to as "Vat." not "ed. 4". Most likely an OCR misread of `1` as `4`. Rendered as `ed. 4` in apparatus per literal-from-OCR rule, with `[?]` flag — should be resolved to `ed. 1` once confirmed against 600dpi PDF in next polish pass.

**bon-sent-I-d1-a1-q3, Apparatus [^8] "Locus a fine" rendering**: OCR has `II. de Differentiis topicis. Locus a flne`. `flne` is `fine`. "Locus a fine" is a technical Boethian topical-locus heading ("the topic from the end"), not a positional indicator ("near the end"). The previous chunk version mistranslated as "near the end"; corrected in this rebuild to "the topic from the end."

**bon-sent-I-d1-a1-q3, Body fn7 anchor — "summa veritas, sic summa veritas" Vat. variant**: OCR fn7 (line 14076) cites the Vat. inversion `nempe sicut summa boniias, sic summa veritas` — the printed `boniias` = `bonitas`. Silently corrected. (Not a [?] flag — context unambiguous.)
