## [?] Flags

### 1. *insiaiu* / *stnmiai* → rendered as *statim* (OCR fn. 5 position)

**Location**: body, objection to the "per causam" reply, two occurrences (one in the body text, one in the replication).

**OCR text**: 
- First occurrence: `quod non praedicatur aliquid de Deo insiaiu / ex tempore praedicatione per essentiam vel inhae- / rentiam`
- Second occurrence: `hoc dicitur ex tempore, et non stnmiai / per causam`

**Currently rendered**: *statim* ("at once") in both positions. The word *statim* makes good contextual sense: the objector is saying that even if something is not predicated *at once/immediately* through essence or inherence, it is predicated through cause.

**Possible alternative**: *simpliciter* ("simply/absolutely") — this is a common scholastic formula in this context and would also make sense. However *statim* fits the OCR fragments *insiaiu* and *stnmiai* better phonetically (s-t-a-t-i-m → fragments plausibly garble to those forms).

**Resolution needed**: PDF eyes-on at printed pp. 521–522 to confirm the word.
