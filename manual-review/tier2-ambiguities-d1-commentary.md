# Tier-2 ambiguities — d.1 commentary preamble

Promotion date: 2026-05-08

The d.1 commentary chunk holds only Bonaventure's prefatory matter for d.1
(the COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM I heading, subtitle, and Lombard incipit
lemma) — the substantive divisio textus is in `bon-sent-I-d1-divisio.md` and
the question matter follows in the article chunks.

## Ambiguities

None. The OCR (raw lines 13215–13223 of `raw/bonaventure_vol1_raw.txt`) is
clean for this six-line block; no `[?]` flags were raised.

## Boundary decision

- Chunk Latin = raw lines 13215–13223 only (heading + subtitle + lemma).
- The next line, 13224 (`DIVISIO TEXTUS.`), opens `bon-sent-I-d1-divisio.md`;
  no overlap.
- The previous line block (≤ 13212) is the apparatus footer of the prologue's
  ultima pars and belongs to the prologue chunks, not d.1.
- No apparatus footnotes attach to this preamble.
