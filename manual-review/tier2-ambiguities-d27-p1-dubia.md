## DUB V truncated tail (`ergo acci-`)

**Location**: end of DUB V's Videtur paragraph, p. 480 col. B, raw_pt2 line 6270.

**Issue**: The IA djvu OCR shows `ergo acci-` and then the column flows directly into `Respondeo:` with no continuation of the truncated word. The page-bottom band of p. 480 col. B truncates the word.

**Best reading**: presumably *accidentales* ("accidental"), reading the argument as: properties that do not determine nature would be *accidentales* — which is impossible in divinis, hence the objection. This fits the argumentative shape (the Respondeo answers by distinguishing two senses of *ordo naturae*).

**Disposition**: flagged with `[?]` in body, `[^15]` editorial-note apparatus entry. Resolution requires 600-dpi PDF eyes-on at p. 480 col. B page bottom.
