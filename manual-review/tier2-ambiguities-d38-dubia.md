# Tier-2 ambiguities — d.38 Dubia (`bon-sent-I-d38-dubia.md`)

Logged 2026-05-07 during Tier-2 promotion. Source: pp. 680–682 (PDF pp. 270–272).

OCR for p.680 (left/right columns of the dubia opening) was substantially dropped from `bonaventure_vol1_pt2_raw.txt` (lines ~26266–26273 retained only fragmented scholion stubs and the page-header for p.681); the body of DUB. I and the start of DUB. III on p.681 right column had to be transcribed directly from a 600 dpi PDF render of pp. 270–271 of `doctorisseraphic12bona.pdf`.

## Inline `[?]` flags

- **DUB. I, ¶1**: `... nisi quod intelligam[?], quod res habet esse in se ...` — printed text faintly inked on right margin of p.680 r.col; the verb agreement (`intelligam` vs. an alternative passive `intelligatur`) is plausible but not certain. Resolve at 600 dpi if revisited.
- **DUB. I, ¶2**: `... Deus aliter cognoscit rem ut habet[?] esse, sicut Augustinus dicit ...` — apparatus note 2 reports `habuit` (codd. T cc `habuerit`), but the printed body word at the marker position appears compressed; reading uncertain between `habet`, `habuit`, `habuerit`. Variant pertains to the printed footnote crux.
- **DUB. I, ¶3 / line break across p.680→p.681**: `... vis cognitivae deflectatur supra[?] // rem ut est in proprio genere ...` — line-break preposition `supra` is typed at the very last line of p.680 right column with no terminal punctuation; reading is plausible but not 100% certain (could be `super`).
- **DUB. II, ¶1**: `... ergo connotat aliquod temporale[?], ergo videtur, quod cognoscatur ex tempore ...` — apparatus note 5 confirms cod. Y reads `quid temporale`, indicating the body's `aliquod temporale` is the editor's (Vatican) reading; flagged because the choice between adjectival and pronominal forms is editorially debated.
- **DUB. IV, ¶1**: `... cum dictum Prophetae fuerit verum certitudinaliter[?] ...` — adverb is unusual; spelling appears clearly `certitudinaliter` in the printed text but is a medieval scholastic neologism whose force ("certitudinarily") may merit a translator's note.
- **DUB. IV, ¶2 (p.682)**: `... sive quia dictum de[?] conditione intelligitur ...` — preposition `de` appears at line start; could plausibly be `sub` in some manuscripts (the parallel construction earlier in the doubt suggests `sub conditione`, "under a [conditional] condition"); flagged for review.
