## Structure (raw, with plate spot-checks)

- **Shape of a capitulum = Ecclesiastes' shape:** full-measure `Capitulum N.` → class-3 italic heading (`Secundo
  comparatur status iustorum statui iniustorum in ipso iudicio quoad tria.`) → divisio with marginale `Divisio.` →
  small-bold sub-headings (`Primo, de iustorum accusatione.`) → `(Vers. 1.)` addresses → expositio with marginalia.
  Sampled at Cap. V (L23479) and Cap. XIII (L31742). Rulings 4, 9, 12, 15–17 (headings, as-printed, flat depth, the
  heading-less opener) all port unchanged.
- ⭐ **A printed PART level the index does not list.** p. 110, after the Expositio prologi: full-measure
  `COMMENTARIUS / IN LIBRUM SAPIENTIAE.` → small-cap **`DIVISIO GENERALIS.`** (*Quoniam, sicut dictum est, principes et
  praelati … primo hortatur ad iustitiam, scilicet usque ad sextum capitulum; secundo, ad sapientiam, scilicet a
  capitulo sexto usque in finem*) → **`PARS I. EXHORTATIO AD IUSTITIAM.`** (L19382) → three nested subdivisiones
  (*Primo, de triplici iustitia hominis quoad Deum* · *De iustitia cordis agitur tripliciter* · *In prima primo ponit
  exhortationem…*) → **`Capitulum I.`** (L19411) → `(Vers. 1.)`. And **`PARS II. EXHORTATIO AD SAPIENTIAM.`** stands at
  L24606, before Capitulum VI (p. 144; the `Capitulum VI.` heading itself is dropped by the raw). Pars I = cc. 1–5,
  Pars II = cc. 6–19, which is also what the two Schemata draw.
- **Quaestiones exist only as MARGINAL-LEVEL paragraphs** — ~5 sites where the raw carries a marginale `Quaestio.`
  beside an ordinary paragraph (L20343 Cap. I, L27296 and L27910 Cap. VIII, L32876 Cap. XIV, L35800 Cap. XVII), no
  display heading, no index line. Also `Nota` ×18, `Divisio.` ×14, *moraliter* ×34, *allegor-* ×4 — the mystical
  expositions are inline, as in Ecclesiastes.
- **Scholion p. 233 is SIX LINES** (L37995, `SCHOLIOK` in the raw): *Duo schemata, quae sequuntur, exhibent sub uno
  conspectu principales divisiones duarum partium, in quas auctor hunc librum dividit … Ob rationes typographicas
  pluries omisimus ultimas divisiones, quae facile ex notis marginalibus possunt suppleri.* No numbered sections,
  unlike Ecclesiastes' two-section Scholion. **Schema I** L38038 (p. 234, Pars I) · **Schema II** L38231 (p. 235,
  Pars II). The Scholion ties the two schemata together in words → ruling 19's test is met.

# PROPOSED RULINGS 27–35 (for Wilson; recommendation first, alternatives with their cost)

**27. Chunk unit = the CAPITULUM, as the index lists it: NINETEEN chunks `bon-sap-c{1..19}`, `division: N`,
`verses: "1-N"` (the index label, verbatim), `type: pericope`, ~6.6 pp each (max 9).** This is ruling 1's own
principle (the index unit, with whatever Quaestiones it carries) applied to an index whose unit is the chapter;
Quaracchi's self-citation form (`supra v. N`, `c. 1, 16`) is chapter+verse and nothing finer. — *Alt (b): split at
the printed sub-headings (`Primo, de iustorum accusatione.`).* Cost: units with no index line and no citation form,
exactly what ruling 1 refused in the other direction.

**28. Prooemium = TWO chunks, division 0, mirroring ruling 2:** `bon-sap-prooem` (section 1, `type: prooemium`,
pp. 107–109: *Introductio generalis* ¶¶1–3 + *De quadruplici causa* ¶¶4–9) and `bon-sap-prol` (section 2,
`type: expositio`, pp. 109–110: *Prologus S. Hieronymi* + *Expositio prologi*). **The p. 110 `COMMENTARIUS / IN LIBRUM
SAPIENTIAE.` stack, the `DIVISIO GENERALIS`, the `PARS I` heading and the three subdivisiones all go to `bon-sap-c1`**
(the short-opener rule; the `bon-eccl-c1-v1` precedent, whose *Divisio generalissima* sits in c1). — *Alt: a third
division-0 chunk for the Divisio generalis.* Cost: no index line, nothing cites it, and Pars I's subdivisio runs
