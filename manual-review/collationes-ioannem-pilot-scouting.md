## What is on the leaves (plate, 450 dpi, pHYs checked = 449.99 on every leaf)

- **p. 533 half-title** `SERAPHICI DOCTORIS / SANCTI BONAVENTURAE / COLLATIONES / IN EVANGELIUM S. IOANNIS`
  (0.654 % dark). · **p. 534 BLANK** (0.009 %). · **p. 535 the body opens** on the full-measure display stack
  `COLLATIONES¹ / IN EVANGELIUM S. IOANNIS` + ornament. ⭐ **THE WORK HEADING IS ANCHORED** (the *Ioannem* p. 239
  and Hexaemeron shape). **n. 1 is an editorial dissertation on the nature of the work**, filling the whole left footer
  block and most of the right. It says:
  - the pieces are *schemata collationum sive sermonum*, and many are only *breves annotationes*;
  - Bonaventure himself set the *collationes* in verse order first, then went back to omitted verses with short notes
    that usually begin *nota* / *notandum est*;
  - ⭐⭐ ***congruum esse putavimus, breviora haec schemata distinguere a collationibus tum speciali inscriptione
    annotationum, tum distincta numeratione*** — **the Collatio / Annotatio numbering and the "Annotatio" heading are the
    EDITORS'.** In the codices the pieces are *not distinguished by number or by a new line*, only by a separation sign and
    painted initials, sometimes not even that;
  - ⭐ **the only division the CODICES mark is the Gospel CAPITULUM** (A by a new line + the chapter's first words;
    BC also write *capitulum primum… capitulum secundum*);
  - the work was composed *in usum praedicationis*, as a supplement to the Commentary (tropological and moral), and
    **the editors say they will NOT cross-refer to the Commentary** (the reader is to turn to the same verse there).
    ⚠ They do it anyway at ≥5 sites (`supra Comment. in Ioan. c. 5. n. 57`, raw L2650–2667 of the band, and others).
- Then `CAPITULUM I. In principio erat Verbum.` (full measure, small caps), then **`COLLATIO I.`** (in column,
  **unanchored**), then the verse address `(Vers. 5.). Ioannis primo: Lux in tenebris lucet…`, then ¶ `1.`, with marginalia
  (`Divisio.`, `Pars I.`, `Primo, Christus est lux dupliciter.`). **Nothing stands between the work heading and
  Capitulum I: no prologue, no Summarium.**

  Omnia per ipsum facta sunt. — Notandum…` → `ANNOTATIO 2.` → `ANNOTATIO 3.`. **An annotatio runs ~15–25 lines, has
  marginalia and its own apparatus anchors, and has no paragraph numbers.** The register numbers straight through the

- **p. 615:** Coll. LXVI ¶ 3 → **`CAPITULUM XVIII. et XIX. Tunc ergo apprehendit Pilatus Iesum⁵.`** (full measure,

  numberings.** n. 4 gives the editors' authenticity judgement. The Appendix pieces stand at the end of the codex they

## Structure (raw, with plate spot-checks)

- **Headings in the band:** `COLLATIO N.` survives 63× of 81 (garbles `VIM`=VIII, `xu`, `XIIL`, `LllL`, `L.XXVL`).
  `Annotatio N.` survives 29× of 81. `Capitulum` survives 21 of 22 lines (`Capitulu.m`, `C.^piTULUM`, `Capitchjm`).
  **Find annotationes by content (`(Vers. N.)` + *Nota*/*Notandum*) and by the plate, never by header grep.** ⚠ The
  band line `COLLATIO LIX COLLATIO LX.` is **two headings, one per column, merged by the OCR**; they are not one unit.
- **Running head `COLLATIONES IN IOANNEM CAP. N. COLL. N.`** (75 hits) sometimes carries two numbers (`COLL. VI. VII.`,
  `LIX. LX.`); that is the leaf's content, not a merged unit. On annotation leaves the head **still names the collatio**
  (p. 544 reads `CAP. I. COLL. VII.` over Annot. 1–3). Ownership comes from the in-column heading, never the head.
- **A collatio's shape:** `COLLATIO N.` → verse address `(Vers. N.)` + the lemma, Gospel locus spelled out (*Ioannis
  primo*) → ¶¶ **numbered per collatio** from `1.` → divisio by `Primo… Secundo…` with marginalia → tropological
  exposition, dense in scripture (it is a preacher's schema). Some addresses are not John at all (*Cap. VI, 27.*
  under Cap. IV; *Cap. 4, 13.* · *3, 5.* · *20, 25.* · *Luc. 3, 6.* in Cap. XXI; *Cant.*, *Eph.*, *Rom.* in the
  Appendix). **Transcribe the address as printed.**
- ⭐ **Quaracchi cites this work by COLLATIO + PARAGRAPH and by ANNOTATIO number**, inside the work:
  `Collat. XVIII. n. 3`, `Collat. XXV. n. 2`, `supra collat. XXII. n. 2. et 3.`, `Collat. XL. n. 5. seqq.`,
  `Collat. XLII. n. 12` (8 with `n.`, 18 `Collat. N` in all, raw). Annotationes: `Vide infra pag. 567, annotat. 22`,
  `Vide annotationem praecedentem`. Also `supra pag. N` (18), `supra nota N` (3). **Inbound from Vols I–V + *Ioannem*:
  zero ledger records** (`index/citations.tsv` has no `Collat… in Ioan.` key).

`bon-cioan-c{1..18}`, **`bon-cioan-c18-19`** (pp. 615–618, the joint `CAPITULUM XVIII. et XIX.` passion unit: its

(a) **the capitulum is the only division the codices transmit.** n. 1 says the collatio/annotatio numbering and the

(b) collationes average ~1 p. and annotationes ~¼ p., **5× smaller than the Vol V collatio chunks** (3.7–5.5 pp).

`c. N, n. M` addressable inside coarser pericope chunks.
