# Tier-2 ambiguities — d.18 littera

- **bon-sent-I-d18-littera, ^20 (Hilar. *de Trin.* V, n. 37)**: The Quaracchi note flags that the original Hilary reads *per desectionem* (a cutting-off), but all editions and manuscripts (and the commentators including Bonaventure here in dub. 1, and Aquinas) print *per defectionem*. The OCR of the apparatus collapses the two forms — they look identical because the editor is contrasting them. Rendered in body as *per defectionem* (per the printed text) with the apparatus note preserving both forms and adding a bracketed clarification. → If the printed apparatus is consulted on PDF p. 320 fn. 12, verify the *desectionem*/*defectionem* distinction and adjust the apparatus note accordingly.
- **bon-sent-I-d18-littera, Cap. V heading**: Heading row missing in OCR (`Cap. V.` line dropped); restored from the printed page (PDF p. 423) where the chapter title `An Filius, cum sit nobis datus, possit dici noster, ut Spiritus sanctus.` is preserved as the chapter rubric. → No further action.
