# Tier-2 ambiguities — d.35 a.1 q.2 (*Utrum in ideis ponenda sit pluralitas secundum rem*)

Translated 2026-05-07 from raw OCR lines 18858–19081 of `bonaventure_vol1_pt2_raw.txt`.
Confirmed against PDF pages 195–197 (printed pages 605–607).

No inline `[?]` flags remain — all initially uncertain readings were resolved against the printed PDF.

## Resolved during translation (logged for record)

- **Apparatus marker for fundam. 4 (`opposit-`/`-tio`/`Filamenta-lius`/`improbatur`/etc.)**: OCR shows scattered marginal labels (`opposi-`, `Filamenta-`, *improbatur*, *Sentenia (an)tiqua*, etc.). These are Quaracchi marginal glosses (left-margin labels for arguments and reply sections), NOT footnote markers. Excluded from body, as is the corpus convention since d.18.
- **Fundam. 1 OCR `incommutabiles»` followed by `'` then conclusio column**: the `'` is footnote marker 1; resolved at end of clause "incommutabiles»" before the period. PDF confirms.
- **Conclusio**: column-break OCR is heavily fragmented; reconstructed from PDF p. 605 right column: *«Ideae in Deo sunt ipsa divina veritas, et ideo secundum rem est una idea.»*
- **`unde sigillum magis et minus imprimitur`** (sed contra reply 2): OCR `sigillhim`; standard `sigillum`.
- **`niwido`** in resp. opin. 1: OCR garble for `mundo`; PDF confirms `mundo`.
- **`raateria`/`raateriam`/`raateriaiiter`/`distinctionera`/`compositionera`/`oppositionera`/`huraana`/`sumraam`/`quaravis`**: standard OCR `m`→`ra` substitution; silently corrected to `materia`/`materiam`/`materialiter`/`distinctionem`/`compositionem`/`oppositionem`/`humana`/`summam`/`quamvis`.
- **`distin- ctionera`**: line-break + `m`→`ra` artifact; resolved to `distinctionem`.
- **`niedio`/`mritas`** (resp. body): `mritas` for `veritas`; PDF confirms.
- **`secundum mritas`** in `secundum rem est ipsa divina veritas`: OCR garble; resolved.
- **`oranes`/`unum sunt`**: OCR `oranes` → `omnes`.
- **`Espiicat`** (margin marker, ad opin.): marginal label `Explicat`, omitted from body (marginal gloss, not Bonaventure's text).
- **`possitaptari`**: OCR run-together; resolved as `possit aptari`.
- **`pluribussecundum materiam'`**: OCR run-together with footnote marker; resolved as `pluribus secundum materiam[^14]`.
- **`muUas`/`muUa`/`mullis`/`mullis`/`mullis`**: standard OCR `lt`/`ll`→`U` artifacts; resolved to `multas`/`multa`/`multis`.
- **`onmino`/`oranimode`**: `omnino`/`omnimode`.
- **Apparatus footnote 5: OCR `et II. Metaph. text. 5. (I. brevior c. 2.)`** — `brevior` is the *minor* (shorter) recension of Aristotle's *Metaphysics* in medieval Latin; preserved as italic. PDF confirms.
- **Apparatus footnote 11: `et cod. I exhibens potuerunt`** — OCR has `cod. ` with no letter; PDF p. 606 footnote 5 confirms `cod. I`.
- **Apparatus footnote 14 long Scotus quote**: OCR fragmented across columns; re-stitched against PDF p. 606 footnote 9.
- **Scholion I `omniwm cognoscibilium`**: `omniwm` → `omnium`.
- **Scholion II `q. M.`**: OCR shows `q. M. m. 1` for one Alex. Hal. citation; the `M` is likely a Roman numeral garble (`Mn.`/`Min.` or similar) but appears consistently in the OCR and is preserved as printed; PDF p. 607 reads `q. M.` clearly.

No outstanding `[?]` flags. Chunk is full Tier-2.
