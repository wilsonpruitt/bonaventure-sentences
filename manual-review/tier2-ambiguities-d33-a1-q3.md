# Tier-2 ambiguities — d.33 a.1 q.3

Distinction 33, articulus unicus, quaestio III: *Utrum notio de notione praedicetur*.

Promoted to Tier 2 on 2026-05-07 from raw lines 16026–16289, printed pp. 576–578.

## Resolved silently during transcription

- OCR title `Utrum notio de notiom praedieetur` → `Utrum notio de notione praedicetur` (obvious *m/n* and *ie/i* OCR drift).
- `QUAESTIO lll.` → `QUAESTIO III.` (Roman numeral OCR garble).
- `S77` running-head → printed page 577.
- `arte Priorum^` (footnote anchor) tied to apparatus entry on Aristot., I. *Prior.* c. 6.
- `imporlatur` → `importatur`; `palernitas` → `paternitas`; `innascibililas` → `innascibilitas`; `innascibibtas`, `innascibibtatis` → `innascibilitas`/-tatis throughout (consistent OCR `bil`/`bib` confusion).
- `magnitudo est bonitas`: OCR rendered `magnitudo est cnm dicitur` mid-line — reflowed correctly to `cum dicitur, magnitudo est bonitas`.
- `^"` and stray `"` before fundamentum 2 (right column header carryover) suppressed as column-bleed artifact.
- `differeas non essentia`, `differens non essentia` from q2 right column drift (this is residual from q2's apparatus footnote, not q3 body — excluded from chunk).
- `iypostasi` → `hypostasi`; `liypostasi` → `hypostasi`; `liabet` → `habet`.
- Apparatus footnote 5: combined three closely-spaced raw entries (`Cod. W unitas.` / `Aliqui codd. ut H V Y...` / `Vat. cum pluribus codd. et ed. I proprietatis...`) into one critical-apparatus entry, since they all gloss the same site (`unio proprietatum… in una persona`), and OCR rendered them as inline sub-glosses at the bottom of p. 577 left col.
- Greek in scholion II: `o|j.wvuii.a, 5uvtivu[i.a, j:apt!)vu(j.a` → `ὁμώνυμα, συνώνυμα, παρώνυμα` (standard transliteration of Aristotle's *Cat.* 1).
- `ExeEoi;` → Ἔκθεσις (Greek "exposition").
- `S. p. I. q. 68. m. S. a. 2.` → `S. p. 1, q. 68, m. 5, a. 4. 5. et 6. § 6.` (combining the partial OCR fragments at lines 224–225 of the raw extract).
- `S. a. 7.5. q. 2.` → `S. a. 75. q. 2.` (Henr. Gand. citation).
