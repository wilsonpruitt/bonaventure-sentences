- **Scholion I, item 2**: OCR `lU illud qiiod ost contiiicns intra` — partially garbled by column-merge across page break. My transcription rendered `intimitatis exsistentiam[?], ut illud quod est continens intra`. The first noun is heavily garbled; might be `existentiam` or `inhaesionem` — needs PDF check.

- **Scholion I, item 2**: `analogo modo, sicui[?] lu\ csl in aerc` → my reading `analogo modo, sicut[?] lux est in aere`. The garble `sicui` for `sicut` plus broken `lux` is fairly safe but flagged.

- **Scholion II**: `est «iiosti-iiiin. iiini cssoiiiia` → my reading `est «nostrum»[?]`. Highly uncertain — could be `nostrum`, `notum`, or `verum`. The Quaracchi convention quotes a controversial term in the Scotus/Thomas dispute; PDF needed.

- **Scholion II, twice**: `in aliqna lo` and `in iliqiic liini` → my reading `in aliqua re[?]`. Possibly `in aliquo loco` (locale) — fits the Scotus claim about action at a distance. Either reading defensible; PDF needed.

- **Scholion II**: Trismegistus footnote ([^10]) closing — OCR garble `supponitur tum corundeni` rendered as `supponitur [esse] eorundem`. The bracketed `esse` is editorial supplement; `eorundem` from `corundeni` is unambiguous (`c→e`, `i→m` typical).
