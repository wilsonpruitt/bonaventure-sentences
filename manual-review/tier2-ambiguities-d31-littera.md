## `[?]` flags raised in this chunk (2026-05-06)

1. **Cap. III, Pars II — `ex innascibili[?] ac non nata substantia`** (Latin body, p. 531).
   OCR (line 11444): `ex innascibili ac non nata substantia perfecta nativitas`. Per Quaracchi p. 531 fn 2 (`Propositio 26. n. 39. 60... In originali ex impassibili pro ex innascibili`), the original Hilary reading is `ex impassibili`; Lombard apparently substituted `innascibili` to fit his theme of innascibility (as the surrounding paragraph hammers `innascibilis`). The `[?]` is left **in body** to flag that the reading is editorially debated, not because OCR is unclear. Disposition: **ACCEPT-AS-PRINTED** (Quaracchi prints `ex innascibili`; the variant is documented in apparatus [^13]). Flag may be removed at d.40 polish pass once consistency cadence is reviewed.
