# Tier-2 ambiguities: d39 a2 q3 (*Utrum Deus cognoscat universa praesenter*)

OCR raw lines 27615–27867 of `raw/bonaventure_vol1_pt2_raw.txt`. Resolutions to be settled in the next decade-polish (d.31–d.40) at 600 dpi.

- **Scholion I, end of Olivi clause**: OCR garbled — `Petrus ioan. Olivi Ord. Min. eo \n I tanquam haereticam censura- \n verit.` Currently rendered as: *"so [?] censured [it] as heretical [?]"* / Latin retained as `eo [?] tanquam haereticam censura[?] verit`. → Verify against PDF p. 696; likely `Petrus Ioan. Olivi Ord. Min. eam tanquam haereticam censurae subiecit` or similar; possibly `eo modo tanquam haereticam censuravit` (with line break splitting `censura-/verit` into a single word `censuravit`). Most likely reading: `eam tanquam haereticam censuravit` → "censured it as heretical".

Currently rendered `tb [?]`. → Likely a Quaracchi shorthand (perhaps OCR garble of `id`, `illud`, or the editorial sigil for *idem*). Verify against PDF p. 697.
