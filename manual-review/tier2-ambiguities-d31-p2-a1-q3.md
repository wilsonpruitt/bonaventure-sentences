Logged 2026-05-06 during Tier-2 promotion of d.31 p.II a.1 q.3 ("Quare aeternitas approprietur Patri, species Imagini, et usus Muneri").

All `[?]` flags below are localized to the Scholion II citation list — the printed page-side OCR (raw lines 12806–12818) is mangled by inter-column bleed where the two-column layout collapses. Body text is clean; apparatus and Scholion I are clean. None of these affect doctrinal content.

- **scholion II, "S. Thom., hic q. 2 [?]"**: OCR raw `hic q. 2 I` — final glyph either `1` (= "q. 2 a. 1") truncated or stray column-edge artifact. Likely intended `hic q. 2 a. 1` per parallel Quaracchi citation pattern.

- **scholion II, "Petr. a Tar., hic [?]"**: OCR `Petr. a Tar., hic` runs off into a line break with no quaestio/articulus number visible because of column drift. Resolve: 600 dpi.

- **scholion II, "Henr. Gand., [?] 71. q. 4."**: OCR shows `Henr. Gand . 7i.q. 4.—` with no `S. a.` (Summa articulus) prefix legible. Pattern in parallel chunks (cf. q.2 scholion II) is `Henr. Gand., S. a. NN. q. N`; here the `S. a.` is OCR-eaten.

- **scholion II, "Dionys. Carth., hic q. [?]"** and **"Biel, hic q. [?]"**: Final two citations bleed across column gutters into the next page-block (`ARTIGULUS II.`); quaestio numbers not recoverable from raw OCR.
