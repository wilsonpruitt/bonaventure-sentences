1. **Cap. I, end of paragraph (raw L12814)**: OCR reads `formam praescriptam in doctrina tenere advertet` (future). Quaracchi may print `advertit` (present); the prior chunk had `advertit`. The OCR could be a `e/i` confusion. Currently rendered `advertet[?]` in Latin and "perceives[?]" in English. → Resolve by eyes-on PDF p. 26 line ~10 from bottom of body block.

2. **Apparatus note 23 (Psalm reference)**: OCR raw L13069 reads `Psalm. 13, 2.` for the citation underlying Lombard's *Bonorum meorum non eges*. The text *Bonorum meorum non eges* is Vulgate Psalm 15:2 (Heb. 16:2). The OCR `13` is almost certainly a glyph misread of `15` (the printed Quaracchi roman/arabic glyph for `15` is commonly mis-OCR'd as `13` when the `5` digit's serifs blur). I have rendered the apparatus entry as `Psalm. 15, 2`, with the cross-reference noted in the English entry. Flag for verification at PDF p. 27 footer, footnote 11. → Resolve by eyes-on PDF.

3. **Apparatus note 24 (chapter/number for *De doctr.* citation)**: OCR raw L13072 reads `Ibid. c. 32. n. 33;`. The numbering `c. 32, n. 33` is plausible for *De doctrina christiana* I; however many critical editions cite the parallel as I.32.35. I have rendered as `c. 32, n. 35` based on the standard CCSL paragraphing — but flag for verification against the Quaracchi printed footer.
