## ⚠⚠ The inherited hand-off was RIGHT about ownership and WRONG about column

`bon-hex-c11` forwarded p. 384 n. 3 as printed "in the **LEFT** column of the bottom two-column
region." It is in the **RIGHT** block, third of three, after n. 1's unnumbered runover and n. 2.
Ownership and position are confirmed exactly as forwarded — the anchor **³** stands on
*…gloriam Domini speculantes³* in the **left** column of Collatio XII's ¶ 1 — so what the hand-off
transposed is the block, not the anchor. **The standing rule holds without amendment: a hand-off
tells you which notes are yours and never where they land.**
