# d.33 a.1 q.2 — Tier-2 ambiguities

Promoted 2026-05-07. Raw lines 15801–16025 (`raw/bonaventure_vol1_pt2_raw.txt`). Printed pp. 574–576.

## [?] flags raised

1. **Footnote 4 (Boeth.)** — body uses the formula *Deus est quo maius excogitari non potest*, which is Anselmian (*Proslogion* 2), not Boethian. The Quaracchi apparatus cites Boeth. III. de Consol. prosa 10 — presumably for the underlying axiom of the supreme good rather than the verbal formula. Flagged as `[?]` in apparatus English gloss; not corrected. Resolve later via 600 dpi PDF eyes-on if it survives the d.40 polish pass.

2. **Footnote 7 (Pictaviensis †1203 vs †1205)** — OCR shows `(f 1203)` for Peter of Poitiers' death year. Standard reference works give †1205 (chancellorship 1193–1205). The OCR character `f` is the obelus †; the digit may be a 5 OCR'd as 3. I rendered as †1205 since the historical date is clear. Confirm via 600dpi PDF if revisited.

3. **Footnote 11 — apparatus block reconstruction** — The OCR for this entry is heavily fragmented across columns ("Vat., omissis vocibus *non, quia nec* ... Nostra lectio est..."). I have re-assembled what the Vat. omits and what the common reading inserts, but the exact OCR of the omitted span (the `non particula in ante essentia` phrase) is jumbled. Reading retained as a best reconstruction; the practical sense (Vat. is shorter and changes the second half of the *quia nec refertur nec distinguitur* clause) is preserved.

4. **Body marker count** — 5 fn markers on p.574 + 8 fn markers on p.575 = 13 total apparatus entries. p.576 (scholion + ad-quaestionem-incidentem block) carries no separate body footnotes in this question; the in-scholion citations are inline. Marker parity verified: 13 in Latin body, 13 in English body, 13 apparatus definitions.

5. **Conclusio**: The OCR rendered the conclusio mid-column with a second sub-conclusion inline ("conclusio 1") in the margin, suggesting Quaracchi marks two sub-conclusions in the respondeo. I rendered only the explicit `CONCLUSIO.` block (lines 15891–15898) as the boxed conclusio; the marginal "conclusio 1" / "conclusio 2" tags throughout the respondeo were dropped per Tier-2 convention (they are editorial marginalia, not Bonaventure's text).

6. **Marginalia "Ad quaest. incidentem"** — p.575/576 has this marginal heading just before the *Unde notandum* block. Rendered as a bold inline heading rather than a separate `###` to keep the structural shape clean (it's a continuation of the respondeo, answering the embedded incidental question raised in fundamentum 5).
