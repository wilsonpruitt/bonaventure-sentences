## Ambiguities flagged with `[?]`

1. **Body, p. 556, "quod *se* [?] *ipso* potest teneri originaliter, vel formaliter"** — OCR has `*e ipso` (line 13923, with `*` glyph rendering an asterisk-like marker) which I have read as `se ipso` (the demonstrative discussed in the immediately preceding clause `utrum Filius sit sapiens per se ipsum vel se ipso`). The OCR's leading `*` may be a footnote marker rather than a glyph; not pursued via PDF since context is unambiguous.

2. **Apparatus [^2]: `Vat. cum pluribus edd. scilicet`.** — Attachment to body word "scilicet" (in "principalem quaestionem prius positam, scilicet utrum...") is inferred but the OCR note is truncated; the actual variant relation (whether Vat. *adds* or *substitutes* "scilicet") is not legible. Marker placed at first body occurrence of "scilicet" as best guess.

3. **Apparatus [^3]: `Vat. ampliat addendo post sapiens verba scilicet [...]; et subinde post genita verba vel sapientia [...]`** — This entry is reconstructed from the heavily OCR-garbled NOTAE AD COMMENTARIUM tail (raw lines 13900–13903): `addit ampliat addendo post sapiens verha sciticet, iilni <iiliii-iix. !( subinde post genita- verba vel sapieiitiii il. 11,100 iilUmn verba etiam aliquanto`. The general substance (additions after *sapiens* and after *genita*) is legible; the exact added words and citation tags `[...]` are not. Marker placed at the second occurrence of "sapiens" in the body (introducing the *sapientia genita* sub-question).

4. **Apparatus [^4]: `Vat. et ed. 2 tamen; paulo ante eadem omittit ita ante dictum`** — The body words "tamen", "ita", and "dictum" do not occur in the divisio text on p. 556 in this chunk — these are evidently variants on the *Lombard* littera printed on p. 555 above the commentary (Cap. III–V). Marker positioned at "affirmativam" only as a placeholder so the entry parses; the actual Lombard text it modifies belongs in the d.32 littera chunk if/when extracted. Flagged for re-attribution.

5. **Apparatus [^5]: `Codd. BE cognita. Vat. ... addit ipsa ante praemissa, et deinde hanc ante quaestionem`** — Words "cognita", "praemissa", and "quaestionem" do not appear at the proximate Bonaventure body position. The marker "una tantum sit sapientia" was the closest plausible attachment. Likely actually a Lombard littera variant; flagged for re-attribution if the d.32 littera chunk is built.

6. **Page-555 Lombard footnotes excluded.** Two footnotes from the page-555 footer were judged to belong to the *Lombard* text printed on the upper portion of the same page (Cap. I–V), not the Bonaventure commentary, and were therefore not folded into this divisio's apparatus:
   - `Hic c. I. locus Augustini est VI. de Trin. c. 5. n. 7.` (Augustine ref for Lombard's c. I)
   - `Num. 18.` (Sentences-internal cross-ref)
   - `Libr. XV. de Trin. c. 7. n. 12.` (Augustine ref)
   These should be picked up by the d.32 littera chunk when it is promoted.
