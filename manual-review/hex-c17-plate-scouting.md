## ★★★ THE SPAN WAS WRONG AGAIN, AND AGAIN THE BAND CAUGHT IT — IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION
The raw's page markers put `COLLATIO XVIII.` just after the p. 414 marker, which reads as
"XVIII opens at the head of p. 414" and gives **pp. 409–413**. **It does not.** p. 414 is a
**boundary leaf**: its top ~17 % carries the end of Collatio XVII (¶ 27's close in the left column,
¶ 28 — the Epilogus — in the right), and `COLLATIO XVIII.` prints **below that**, at ~40 % of the
leaf, with its own full-measure subtitle and Summarium.
**Printed span = pp. 409–414. Total apparatus owned = 41 entries** (409: 6 · 410: 7 · 411: 7 ·
412: 8 · 413: 10 · 414: 3).
⚠ Note the shape of the error: c15 and c16 were each handed a span that was **one leaf too long**;
c17's raw-based inference was **one leaf too short**. The lesson is not "the inference runs long" —
it is that **only the printed header on the band decides**, in either direction.
