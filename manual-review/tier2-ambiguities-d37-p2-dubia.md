# Tier-2 ambiguities — d.37 p.2 dubia

Source: `vol1/bon-sent-I-d37-p2-dubia.md` (raw lines 24645–24846 of pt2).

**bon-sent-I-d37-p2-dubia, Dub II Resp.**: phrase `eatenus qua ad situm est, nisi pariter sit ad formam`. OCR clearly preserves `eatenus qua` (rather than expected `eatenus quia` / `eatenus qua [scil. movetur]`); kept verbatim and flagged. → Resolve via 600dpi PDF eyes-on at d.30→d.40 polish pass; likely `eatenus qua [movetur]` with anaphoric pronoun.

**bon-sent-I-d37-p2-dubia, Dub IV opinio 1, body**: `sed prius ostendunt`. OCR's right-column footer note about *sed prius* / *sed primum* (cod. T) and *contingat* prefix-non vs no-non confirms there's a textual problem here. Kept the Quaracchi *sed prius* reading, flagged. Apparatus footnote 9 captures the variant.

**bon-sent-I-d37-p2-dubia, Dub IV opinio 1, body**: `et contingat aliquos duos omnino idem scire et cogitare`. The Vat./almost-all-codd. reading prefixed *non* before *contingat*, which Quaracchi rejects as corrupting the argument. Kept Quaracchi (*non*-less) reading; flagged for the editorial choice. Resolved at apparatus level (note 9).
