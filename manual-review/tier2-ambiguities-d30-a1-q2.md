## Resolved Ambiguity

**`.Egid. H.`** in Scholion II (OCR line 10723 approx.): The OCR reads `.Egid. H., hic 1. princ. q. 2.` The `.` before *Egid.* is a standard OCR artifact for a capital. `H.` is ambiguous — in parallel usage elsewhere in this volume the abbreviation is `Aegid. R.` for *Aegidius Romanus*. Rendered as `Aegid. R.` in the transcription. No `[?]` placed as this is consistent with the volume's citation practice.

**`to l<jom^lii;`** in footnote [^11] (OCR rendering of Greek `τὸ ἰσοσκελές`): OCR garbles the Greek entirely. The Greek was restored from context (the word *isosceles* is being etymologized) and standard Greek form. No `[?]` needed as the referent is certain.
