# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. II, Vers. 21-24
## *Qualiter factus sit sub lege sacramentali*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 54 -->

#### Secundo, de Christi nascentis humilitate, quatenus factus est sub triplici lege.

44. *Et postquam consummati* etc. Supra descripsit Evangelista veritatem nativitatis Christi, per quam ostenditur, qualiter Christus *factus est de muliere;* hic secundo describitur *humilitas*[^p54-5] Christi nascentis, per quam declaratur, qualiter *factus est sub Lege.* Quoniam autem Legis praecepta sunt in triplici differentia: quaedam sunt *sacramentalia,* quaedam sunt *caeremonialia* et quaedam *moralia;* ideo haec pars habet tres; in quarum prima ostenditur, qualiter Christus factus est sub lege *sacramentali* in circumcisione; in secunda vero, qualiter factus est sub *caeremoniali* in sui oblatione, ibi[^p54-6]: *Et postquam impleti sunt* etc.; in tertia, qualiter factus est sub lege *morali* in Dei honoratione, ibi: *Puer autem crescebat* etc.

#### Qualiter factus sit sub lege sacramentali.

45. (Vers. 21.). Quantum ad primum notandum, quod « omnis, qui se humiliat, exaltabitur[^p54-7] »; ideo *praemittitur humiliatio* secundum Legis praeceptum, cum dicitur: *Et postquam consummati sunt dies octo, ut circumcideretur puer* etc. Ita enim erat praeceptum Genesis decimo septimo[^p54-8]: « Infans octo dierum circumcidetur in vobis ». Et recte dicuntur isti dies *consummati* propter perfectionem humilitatis et inchoationem nostrae salutis; Ecclesiastici decimo octavo: « Cum consummaverit homo », scilicet octo dies, « tunc incipiet », nos scilicet liberare. Iam enim octava die incipiebat pro nobis sanguinem suum fundere. Bernardus[^p54-9]: « Audivimus congruam lectionem de *Verbo abbreviato:* abbreviatum est in carne, amplius abbreviabitur carnis suscepta circumcisione ». Et post: « Quem nemo potest arguere de peccato, ipse peccati remedium sustinet humile pariter et austerum ». Voluit autem Dominus circumcidi tum propter *Legis approbationem et impletionem,* de qua dicit Matthaei quinto[^p54-10]: « Non veni Legem solvere, sed adimplere ». Tum etiam propter *humilitatis ostensionem;* Psalmus: « Propter verba labiorum tuorum ego custodivi vias duras »; alia littera: « vias latronis ». Bernardus[^p54-11]: « Qui sine peccato est non dedignatur peccator reputari », immo « latronis cauterio est signatus ». Tum etiam propter *spiritualis*
<!-- page 55 -->
*circumcisionis introductionem;* Ieremiae quarto[^p55-1]: « Circumcidimini Domino et auferte praeputia cordium vestrorum, viri Iuda ». Tum etiam propter *conformitatis et concordiae commendationem;* ad Hebraeos secundo[^p55-2]: « Debuit per omnia fratribus similari ». Bernardus: « Amator communitatis et commendator Iesus, quomodo illam desereret »?

46. Deinde additur *clarificatio propter nomen impositum,* cum subditur: *Vocatum est nomen eius Iesus,* id est Salvator, ut scilicet ipso nomine scias, quod non accepit circumcisionem propter necessitatem suae curationis, sed propter dispensationem nostrae salutis; Iob decimo tertio[^p55-3]: « Ipse erit salvator meus ». Hoc autem est nomen dignum omni veneratione; ad Philippenses secundo: « Humiliavit semetipsum etc. Propter quod et Deus exaltavit illum et donavit illi nomen, quod est super omne nomen, ut in nomine Iesu omne genu flectatur ». Hoc autem nomen competit ipsi Christo *vere* et *proprie: vere,* secundum illud Matthaei primo[^p55-4]: « Vocabis nomen eius Iesum; ipse enim salvum faciet populum suum a peccatis eorum »; convenit etiam *proprie,* secundum illud Actuum quarto: « Non est in aliquo alio salus. Nec enim aliud nomen est sub caelo datum hominibus, in quo oporteat nos salvos fieri ».

47. Postremo subditur *impositi nominis approbatio* per praeconium angelicum, cum subditur: *Quod vocatum est ab Angelo, priusquam in utero conciperetur;* Isaiae sexagesimo secundo[^p55-5]: « Vocabitur tibi nomen novum, quod os Domini nominabit »; et Ecclesiastae sexto: « Qui futurus est, iam vocatum est nomen eius, et scitur, quod homo sit ». Et hoc quidem factum est nunc; supra primo: « Vocabis nomen eius Iesum ». — Hoc vero nomen divinum est et angelicum, quod desiderat sponsa Canticorum primo[^p55-6]: « Oleum effusum nomen tuum ». Bernardus: « Aridus est omnis cibus animae, si non isto oleo infundatur; insipidus est, si non hoc sale condiatur. Si scribas, non sapit mihi, nisi legero ibi Iesum. Si disputes aut conferas, non sapit, nisi sonuerit ibi Iesus. Iesus mel in ore, in aure melos, in corde iubilus ». Et ideo Isaiae vigesimo sexto[^p55-7] dicitur: « Nomen tuum et memoriale tuum in desiderio animae ».

48. *Spiritualiter* nota, quod per *octo dies* intelliguntur *octo aetates,* quarum sex sunt in decursu mundi secundum sex distinctiones notabiles[^p55-8], septima in quiete animarum, quae currit simul cum sexta et non habet vesperam, octava in resurrectione, in qua fit circumcisio ab omni culpa et miseria. Et ideo circumcisio fiebat in octavo die ad illius circumcisionis designationem[^p55-9].

49. Secundum *tropologiam* autem et *allegoriam* simul intelligitur *virtus Spiritus septiformis*[^p55-10], et octavus est *perseverantia finalis,* quae nunquam deficit. — Prima ergo pertinet ad *lucem fidei,* quia prima die facta est lux; Proverbiorum quarto[^p55-11]: « Iustorum semita quasi lux splendens procedit et crescit usque ad perfectam diem ». — Secunda vero ad *firmitatem spei,* in qua factum fuit firmamentum; ad Hebraeos sexto[^p55-12]: « Fortissimum habemus solatium, qui confugimus ad tenendam propositam spem ». — Tertia ad *viriditatem caritatis,* quando terra germinavit; Proverbiorum undecimo: « Iusti quasi virens folium germinabunt ». Gregorius: « Non habet aliquid viriditatis ramus boni operis, nisi manserit in radice caritatis ». — Quarta, in qua facta sunt luminaria, ad luminosam *discretionem prudentiae,* quae viget in virili aetate; Ecclesiastae duodecimo[^p55-13]: « Memento Creatoris tui, antequam veniat tempus afflictionis »; et ad Philippenses secundo: « Lucetis sicut luminaria in mundo ». — Quinta, ad *fortitudinem potentiae,* quae probatur in aquis tribulationis, quoniam quinta die factus est aquae ornatus; Psalmus[^p55-14]: « Salvum me fac, Deus, quoniam intraverunt aquae »; Psalmus: « Misit de summo et assumsit me de aquis multis ». — Sexta ad *rectitudinem iustitiae,* propter *severitatem* respectu bestialium motuum, et *pietatem* respectu hominum, quoniam sexta die sunt bruta producta cum homine; Iob quinto[^p55-15]: « Scias, quod pacem habeat tabernaculum tuum, et visitans speciem tuam, non peccabis ».
<!-- page 56 -->
— Septima ad *sanctificationem temperantiae,* quoniam diem septimam sanctificavit, in qua quievit; Exodi decimo nono[^p56-1]: « Vade ad populum, et sanctifica illos hodie et cras, et lavent vestimenta sua »; et vigesimo: « Memento, ut diem Sabbati sanctifices ». — Octava ad *consummationem finalis perseverantiae;* Levitici vigesimo tertio[^p56-2]: « Octavus dies erit celeberrimus atque sanctissimus »; unde et Psalmus, qui est de transitu ad gloriam, scilicet: « Domine, ne in furore tuo arguas me », intitulatur *Psalmus pro octava.*

#### Qualiter Christus factus sit sub lege caeremoniali.

50. *Et postquam impleti sunt* etc. Supra ostendit Evangelista, qualiter Christus factus fuit sub lege sacramentali, hic secundo ostendit, qualiter factus fuit sub lege *caeremoniali,* et habet haec pars iuxta praedictam tres partes. In prima praemittitur *Christi humiliatio* secundum Legis praeceptum; in secunda vero subditur *Christi clarificatio* per veritatis testimonium, ibi[^p56-3]: *Et ecce homo erat;* in tertia, *consummatio praedictorum,* ibi: *Et cum perfecissent omnia* etc.

Circa primum igitur, scilicet *Christi humiliationem,* quatuor sunt attendenda, quae tangit Evangelista. Describit enim Christi oblationem quantum ad *tempus statutum,* quantum ad *locum designatum,* quantum ad *mandatum scriptum,* quantum ad *pretium oblatum.*

51. (Vers. 22.). Primo igitur quantum ad *tempus statutum* dicitur: *Et postquam impleti sunt dies purgationis eius secundum legem Moysi;* Levitici duodecimo[^p56-4]: « Mulier, si, suscepto semine, pepererit masculum, immunda erit septem diebus. Ipsa vero triginta tribus diebus manebit in sanguine purificationis suae ». In hoc autem, quod dicit: « Si, suscepto semine, pepererit », ostenditur, quod beata Virgo non fuit illi legi necessitate subiecta, sed humilitate obediens. Bernardus[^p56-5]: « Putas, dicturus Moyses, mulierem, quae peperisset masculum, immundam esse, non timuerit super Matrem Dei blasphemiae crimen inducere, et idcirco praemisit: suscepto semine »? Et post: « Vere, o beata Virgo, non habes causam, nec tibi opus est purificatione. Sed nunquid filio tuo opus erat circumcisione »? Unde potest dici quod dixit Assuerus Esther, decimo quinto[^p56-6]: « Non propter te, sed pro omnibus lex ista posita est ».

52. Secundo, quantum ad *locum designatum,* subditur: *Tulerunt illum in Ierusalem, ut sisterent eum Domino.* Is enim erat locus electus ad divinum cultum; secundi Paralipomenon sexto[^p56-7]: « Elegi Ierusalem, ut sit nomen meum in ea ». Et in hoc loco partus debebat Deo praesentari; Deuteronomii decimo sexto: « Tribus vicibus in anno apparebit omne masculinum tuum in conspectu Domini Dei tui, in loco, quem elegerit Dominus ».

53. (Vers. 23.). Tertio, quantum ad *mandatum scriptum,* subditur: *Sicut scriptum est in lege Domini, quia omne masculinum, adaperiens vulvam, sanctum Domino vocabitur;* Exodi decimo tertio[^p56-8]: « Sanctifica mihi omne primogenitum, quod aperit vulvam in filiis Israel, tam de hominibus quam de iumentis ». Sed illud non videtur convenire virgini Mariae, quae est porta clausa ante partum et post partum et in partu. Et ideo potest dici, sicut supra[^p56-9] dictum est de lege purificationis. Vel certe adapertio vulvae intelligitur quantum ad fecundationem, non quantum ad claustri apertionem; Genesis vigesimo nono[^p56-10]: « Aperuit Dominus vulvam Liae, sorore eius sterili permanente »; et primi Regum primo: « Exprobrabat aemula Annae, quod conclusisset Dominus vulvam eius, quia sterilis erat ».

54. (Vers. 24.). Quarto, quantum ad *pretium oblatum* subinfertur: *Et ut darent* pro eo *hostiam, secundum quod dictum est in lege Domini, par turturum, aut duos pullos columbarum.* Haec enim erat oblatio pauperum; Levitici duodecimo[^p56-11]: « Quodsi non invenerit manus eius nec potuerit offerre agnum, sumet duos turtures, vel duos pullos columbarum,
<!-- page 57 -->
unum in holocaustum et alterum pro peccato; orabitque pro eo sacerdos, et sic mundabitur ». Ecce, mira paupertas in parentibus Christi, ut agnum non haberent! — Et nota, quod sub disiunctione ponit utramque oblationem, ut ostendat, utramque Domino convenire. Per *columbam* igitur intelligitur vita activa, sed per *turturem* solivagum contemplativa, quarum utraque hominem reddit Deo acceptabilem, sicut dicit Beda[^p57-1]. — Et nota tres oblationes circa hostiam nostram, quae est Dominus Iesus. Prima est ipsius oblatio a parentibus, quae invitat ad *humilitatem.* Secunda est oblatio pro ipso de avibus, quae nos invitat ad *paupertatem.* Tertia est oblatio in cruce pro omnibus, quae nos invitat ad *pietatem.* De primis duabus habetur hic, de tertia Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio[^p57-2]: « Oblatus est, quia ipse voluit ».

55. *Spiritualiter* autem notanda sunt tria, scilicet *mysterium purgationis Mariae, sacramentum delationis dominicae, significantia oblationis factae.* — Quantum ad primum notandum, quod *Maria,* quae interpretatur stella maris, sive amarum mare[^p57-3], animam designat, sive sit in lumine vitae *contemplativae,* sive in amaritudine vitae *activae;* et utrique necessaria est purgatio. Animae namque *contemplativae* necessaria est purgatio a *superbia,* quod quidem fit per timorem; Iob quadragesimo primo[^p57-4]: « Cum sublatus fuerit, timebunt Angeli, et territi purgabuntur ». Animae *activae* necessaria est purgatio a *negligentia,* quod fit per rigorem et laborem; Ecclesiastici septimo: « De negligentia purga te cum paucis ».

56. Quantum ad *sacramentum delationis dominicae* notandum, quod legimus[^p57-5] puerum Iesum delatum in *Ierusalem;* legimus etiam delatum in *Aegyptum,* Matthaei secundo. In quo aperitur nobis, quod proles mentis nostrae, quae est intelligentia, modo *elevanda* est ad *contemplationem aeternorum,* quae per Ierusalem designatur; *Ierusalem* enim interpretatur visio pacis[^p57-6]; modo *deprimenda* est ad *considerationem defectuum* nostrorum, quae per *Aegyptum* designatur; *Aegyptus* enim interpretatur tenebrae. Et hoc est quod dicitur in Psalmo[^p57-7]: « Quoniam tu illuminas lucernam meam, Domine; Deus meus, illumina tenebras meas »; et Iob ultimo: « Auditu auris audivi te, nunc autem oculus meus videt te » etc.

57. Quantum ad *significantiam oblationis* notandum, quod oblatio indeterminate intelligitur de *turture* et *columba.* Utraque enim avis gemitum habet pro cantu[^p57-8], sed gemitus turturis competit *contemplativis.* Et hic geminatur; unde dicitur: *par turturum.* Primus gemitus est *amoris;* ad Romanos octavo[^p57-9]: « Nos ipsi primitias spiritus habentes, et ipsi intra nos ingemiscimus, adoptionem filiorum Dei exspectantes ». Secundus est *devotionis;* ad Romanos octavo: « Quid oremus, sicut oportet, nescimus; sed Spiritus est, qui postulat pro nobis gemitibus inenarrabilibus »; Canticorum secundo: « Vox turturis audita est in terra nostra ». — Gemitus autem columbae competit *activis;* et hic etiam est duplex: unus pro *peccatis propriis;* Psalmus[^p57-10]: « Afflictus et humiliatus sum nimis, rugiebam a gemitu cordis mei »; Isaiae quinquagesimo nono: « Rugiemus quasi ursi omnes, et quasi columbae meditantes gememus ». Secundus gemitus est pro *peccatis alienis;* Threnorum primo[^p57-11]: « Omnes portae eius destructae, sacerdotes eius gementes »; Nahum secundo: « Miles captivus abductus est, et ancillae eius gementes minabantur ut columbae ».
<!-- page 58 -->

In hac *purgatione* ergo et *delatione* et *oblatione* significatur, quod qui purgatus est a superbia et a negligentia dispositus est, ut *ascendat* ad contemplationem Dei et *descendat* ad considerationem sui, et *sursum ascendens* gemat ex amore et devotione, *deorsum rediens* gemat ex contritione et compassione; et in his consistit perfectio animae sanctae et Deo devotae.

## English
<!-- page 54 -->

#### Secondly, on the humility of Christ being born, inasmuch as he was made under a threefold law.

44. *And after were consummated* etc. Above, the Evangelist described the truth of the nativity of Christ, by which it is shown how Christ *was made of a woman;* here, secondly, is described the *humility*[^p54-5] of Christ being born, by which it is declared how he *was made under the Law.* Now since the precepts of the Law are of a threefold difference: some are *sacramental,* some are *ceremonial* and some *moral;* therefore this part has three; in the first of which it is shown how Christ was made under the *sacramental* law in circumcision; but in the second, how he was made under the *ceremonial* in his offering, there[^p54-6]: *And after were accomplished* etc.; in the third, how he was made under the *moral* law in the honouring of God, there: *And the child grew* etc.

#### How he was made under the sacramental law.

45. (Vers. 21.). As to the first it is to be noted that « every one that humbleth himself shall be exalted[^p54-7] »; therefore *humiliation is put first* according to the precept of the Law, when it is said: *And after eight days were consummated, that the child should be circumcised* etc. For so was the precept of Genesis, the seventeenth chapter[^p54-8]: « An infant of eight days old shall be circumcised among you ». And rightly are these days said to be *consummated,* on account of the perfection of humility and the beginning of our salvation; Ecclesiasticus, the eighteenth chapter: « When a man hath consummated », namely the eight days, « then shall he begin », namely to free us. For already on the eighth day he was beginning to shed his blood for us. Bernard[^p54-9]: « We have heard a fitting reading concerning the *Word abridged:* abridged it is in the flesh, more abridged will it be by the circumcision of the flesh taken on ». And after: « He whom no one can convict of sin undergoes the remedy of sin, humble and austere alike ». Now the Lord willed to be circumcised, both on account of the *approval and fulfilment of the Law,* of which he says in Matthew, the fifth chapter[^p54-10]: « I am not come to destroy the Law, but to fulfil »; and also on account of the *showing of humility;* the Psalm: « For the sake of the words of thy lips, I have kept hard ways »; another reading: « the ways of the robber ». Bernard[^p54-11]: « He who is without sin does not disdain to be reckoned a sinner », nay rather « he is marked with the robber's brand ». And also on account of the *introduction of spiritual*
<!-- page 55 -->
*circumcision;* Jeremias, the fourth chapter[^p55-1]: « Be circumcised to the Lord, and take away the foreskins of your hearts, ye men of Juda ». And also on account of the *commendation of conformity and concord;* to the Hebrews, the second chapter[^p55-2]: « It behoved him in all things to be made like unto his brethren ». Bernard: « Jesus, the lover and commender of community, how should he desert it »?

46. Then is added the *glorification on account of the name imposed,* when it is subjoined: *His name was called Jesus,* that is, Saviour, so namely that by the very name thou mayest know that he received circumcision not on account of the need of his own healing, but on account of the dispensation of our salvation; Job, the thirteenth chapter[^p55-3]: « He shall be my saviour ». Now this is a name worthy of all veneration; to the Philippians, the second chapter: « He humbled himself etc. For which cause God also hath exalted him, and hath given him a name which is above all names, that in the name of Jesus every knee should bow ». Now this name belongs to Christ himself *truly* and *properly: truly,* according to that of Matthew, the first chapter[^p55-4]: « Thou shalt call his name Jesus; for he shall save his people from their sins »; it befits him also *properly,* according to that of the Acts, the fourth chapter: « Neither is there salvation in any other. For there is no other name under heaven given to men, whereby we must be saved ».

47. Lastly is subjoined the *approval of the name imposed* by the angelic proclamation, when it is subjoined: *Which was called by the Angel, before he was conceived in the womb;* Isaias, the sixty-second chapter[^p55-5]: « Thou shalt be called by a new name, which the mouth of the Lord shall name »; and Ecclesiastes, the sixth chapter: « He that shall be, his name is already called, and it is known that he is man ». And this indeed was done now; above, the first chapter: « Thou shalt call his name Jesus ». — But this name is divine and angelic, which the bride desires, the Canticles, the first chapter[^p55-6]: « Thy name is as oil poured out ». Bernard: « Dry is all food of the soul, if it be not steeped in this oil; insipid it is, if it be not seasoned with this salt. If thou write, it has no savour for me, unless I read Jesus there. If thou dispute or confer, it has no savour, unless Jesus sound there. Jesus is honey in the mouth, melody in the ear, jubilation in the heart ». And therefore in Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter[^p55-7], it is said: « Thy name, and thy memorial are in the desire of the soul ».

48. *Spiritually,* note that by the *eight days* are understood the *eight ages,* of which six are in the course of the world according to six notable distinctions[^p55-8], the seventh in the rest of souls, which runs together with the sixth and has no evening, the eighth in the resurrection, in which there comes about the circumcision from all guilt and misery. And therefore circumcision was done on the eighth day for the designation of that circumcision[^p55-9].

49. But according to *tropology* and *allegory* together is understood the *virtue of the sevenfold Spirit*[^p55-10], and the eighth is *final perseverance,* which never fails. — The first, then, pertains to the *light of faith,* because on the first day light was made; Proverbs, the fourth chapter[^p55-11]: « The path of the just, as a shining light, goeth forwards and increaseth even to perfect day ». — But the second to the *firmness of hope,* on which the firmament was made; to the Hebrews, the sixth chapter[^p55-12]: « We have the strongest comfort, who have fled for refuge to hold fast the hope set before us ». — The third to the *greenness of charity,* when the earth brought forth; Proverbs, the eleventh chapter: « The just shall spring up as a green leaf ». Gregory: « The branch of a good work has nothing of greenness, unless it abide in the root of charity ». — The fourth, on which the lights were made, to the luminous *discretion of prudence,* which flourishes in manly age; Ecclesiastes, the twelfth chapter[^p55-13]: « Remember thy Creator, before the time of affliction come »; and to the Philippians, the second chapter: « You shine as lights in the world ». — The fifth, to the *fortitude of power,* which is proved in the waters of tribulation, since on the fifth day the adornment of the water was made; the Psalm[^p55-14]: « Save me, O God, for the waters are come in »; the Psalm: « He sent from on high, and took me out of many waters ». — The sixth to the *rectitude of justice,* on account of *severity* with respect to bestial motions, and *piety* with respect to men, since on the sixth day the brutes were brought forth together with man; Job, the fifth chapter[^p55-15]: « Know thou that thy tabernacle hath peace, and visiting thy beauty, thou shalt not sin ».
<!-- page 56 -->
— The seventh to the *sanctification of temperance,* since he sanctified the seventh day, on which he rested; Exodus, the nineteenth chapter[^p56-1]: « Go to the people, and sanctify them to day, and to morrow, and let them wash their garments »; and the twentieth: « Remember that thou sanctify the Sabbath day ». — The eighth to the *consummation of final perseverance;* Leviticus, the twenty-third chapter[^p56-2]: « The eighth day shall be most solemn and most holy »; whence also the Psalm which is of the passage to glory, namely: « O Lord, rebuke me not in thy indignation », is entitled *A Psalm for the octave.*

#### How Christ was made under the ceremonial law.

50. *And after were accomplished* etc. Above, the Evangelist showed how Christ was made under the sacramental law; here, secondly, he shows how he was made under the *ceremonial* law, and this part has, according to what was said before, three parts. In the first *the humiliation of Christ* is put first, according to the precept of the Law; but in the second is subjoined *the glorification of Christ* by the testimony of truth, there[^p56-3]: *And behold there was a man;* in the third, *the consummation of the foregoing,* there: *And after they had performed all things* etc.

Concerning the first, then, namely *the humiliation of Christ,* four things are to be attended to, which the Evangelist touches. For he describes the offering of Christ as to the *time appointed,* as to the *place designated,* as to the *commandment written,* as to the *price offered.*

51. (Vers. 22.). First, then, as to the *time appointed* it is said: *And after the days of her purgation, according to the law of Moses, were accomplished;* Leviticus, the twelfth chapter[^p56-4]: « A woman, if, having received seed, she shall bear a man child, shall be unclean seven days. But she shall remain three and thirty days in the blood of her purification ». Now in this, that he says: « If, having received seed, she shall bear », it is shown that the blessed Virgin was not subject to that law by necessity, but obedient by humility. Bernard[^p56-5]: « Thinkest thou that Moses, being about to say that a woman who had borne a man child was unclean, did not fear to bring the charge of blasphemy upon the Mother of God, and for that reason put first: having received seed »? And after: « Truly, O blessed Virgin, thou hast no cause, nor hast thou need of purification. But had thy son any need of circumcision »? Whence there can be said what Assuerus said to Esther, the fifteenth chapter[^p56-6]: « Not for thee, but for all, is this law laid down ».

52. Secondly, as to the *place designated,* it is subjoined: *They carried him to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord.* For this was the place chosen for divine worship; the second of Paralipomenon, the sixth chapter[^p56-7]: « I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name may be in it ». And in this place the offspring was to be presented to God; Deuteronomy, the sixteenth chapter: « Three times in a year shall all thy males appear in the sight of the Lord thy God, in the place which the Lord shall choose ».

53. (Vers. 23.). Thirdly, as to the *commandment written,* it is subjoined: *As it is written in the law of the Lord: Every male opening the womb shall be called holy to the Lord;* Exodus, the thirteenth chapter[^p56-8]: « Sanctify unto me every firstborn that openeth the womb among the children of Israel, as well of men as of beasts ». But that does not seem to befit the virgin Mary, who is the gate shut before bringing forth and after bringing forth and in bringing forth. And therefore it can be said, as was said above[^p56-9] of the law of purification. Or surely the opening of the womb is understood as to fecundation, not as to the opening of the enclosure; Genesis, the twenty-ninth chapter[^p56-10]: « The Lord opened Lia's womb, her sister remaining barren »; and the first of Kings, the first chapter: « Anna's rival upbraided her, that the Lord had shut up her womb, because she was barren ».

54. (Vers. 24.). Fourthly, as to the *price offered,* it is inferred: *And that they might give* for him *a victim, according as it is said in the law of the Lord, a pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons.* For this was the offering of the poor; Leviticus, the twelfth chapter[^p56-11]: « And if her hand find not, nor be able to offer a lamb, she shall take two turtles, or two young pigeons,
<!-- page 57 -->
one for a holocaust, and another for sin; and the priest shall pray for him, and so he shall be cleansed ». Behold, the wondrous poverty in the parents of Christ, that they had not a lamb! — And note that he puts both offerings under a disjunction, to show that both befit the Lord. By the *dove,* then, is understood the active life, but by the solitary *turtle* the contemplative, each of which renders man acceptable to God, as Bede says[^p57-1]. — And note three offerings concerning our victim, who is the Lord Jesus. The first is the offering of himself by his parents, which invites to *humility.* The second is the offering for him of birds, which invites us to *poverty.* The third is the offering on the cross for all, which invites us to *piety.* Of the first two it is had here; of the third, Isaias, the fifty-third chapter[^p57-2]: « He was offered because it was his own will ».

55. *Spiritually,* moreover, three things are to be noted, namely *the mystery of Mary's purgation, the sacrament of the Lord's carrying, the significance of the offering made.* — As to the first it is to be noted that *Mary,* which is interpreted star of the sea, or bitter sea[^p57-3], designates the soul, whether it be in the lumen[^tr-lumen] of the *contemplative* life, or in the bitterness of the *active* life; and to each purgation is necessary. For to the *contemplative* soul purgation from *pride* is necessary, which indeed comes about through fear; Job, the forty-first chapter[^p57-4]: « When he shall be raised up, the Angels shall fear, and being affrighted shall be purged ». To the *active* soul purgation from *negligence* is necessary, which comes about through rigour and labour; Ecclesiasticus, the seventh chapter: « Purge thyself of negligence with a few ».

56. As to the *sacrament of the Lord's carrying* it is to be noted that we read[^p57-5] the child Jesus carried into *Jerusalem;* we read him also carried into *Egypt,* Matthew, the second chapter. In which it is opened to us that the offspring of our mind, which is intelligence, is now to be *raised up* to the *contemplation of eternal things,* which is designated by Jerusalem; for *Jerusalem* is interpreted vision of peace[^p57-6]; now to be *pressed down* to the *consideration of* our *defects,* which is designated by *Egypt;* for *Egypt* is interpreted darkness. And this is what is said in the Psalm[^p57-7]: « For thou lightest my lamp, O Lord; O my God, enlighten my darkness »; and Job, the last chapter: « With the hearing of the ear I have heard thee, but now my eye seeth thee » etc.

57. As to the *significance of the offering* it is to be noted that the offering is understood indeterminately of the *turtle* and the *dove.* For each bird has groaning for song[^p57-8], but the groaning of the turtle befits *contemplatives.* And this is doubled; whence it is said: *a pair of turtledoves.* The first groaning is of *love;* to the Romans, the eighth chapter[^p57-9]: « We ourselves, having the firstfruits of the spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption of the sons of God ». The second is of *devotion;* to the Romans, the eighth chapter: « What we should pray for as we ought, we know not; but it is the Spirit who asketh for us with unspeakable groanings »; the Canticles, the second chapter: « The voice of the turtle is heard in our land ». — But the groaning of the dove befits *actives;* and this also is twofold: one for *one's own sins;* the Psalm[^p57-10]: « I am afflicted and humbled exceedingly, I roared with the groaning of my heart »; Isaias, the fifty-ninth chapter: « We will all roar like bears, and like meditating doves we will groan ». The second groaning is for *the sins of others;* Lamentations, the first chapter[^p57-11]: « All her gates are broken down, her priests groaning »; Nahum, the second chapter: « The soldier is led away captive, and her bondwomen were led away groaning as doves ».
<!-- page 58 -->

In this *purgation,* then, and *carrying* and *offering* it is signified that he who has been purged from pride and from negligence is disposed to *ascend* to the contemplation of God and to *descend* to the consideration of himself, and *ascending on high* to groan from love and devotion, *returning below* to groan from contrition and compassion; and in these consists the perfection of the soul that is holy and devoted to God.
