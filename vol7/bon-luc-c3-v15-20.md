# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. III, Vers. 15-20
## *Praedicatio Ioannis commendatur secundo quoad excellentiam Christi tripliciter*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 78 -->

#### Praedicatio Ioannis commendatur secundo quoad excellentiam Christi tripliciter.

35. (Vers. 15.). *Existimante autem populo* etc. Commendata est praedicatio Ioannis in comparatione ad exigentiam populi; hic secundo commendatur in comparatione ad *excellentiam Christi* in hoc, quod gloriam Christo debitam non usurpat. Unde sicut supra[^p78-3] administrabat doctrinam sanam, ita hic vitat gloriam non suam. Ad quod explicandum tria introducuntur: primum est *falsitatis existimatio in plebe;* secundum est *veritatis confessio in Ioanne;* tertium est *sublimitatis commendatio in Salvatore.*

Quantum ad *existimationem falsitatis in plebe* dicitur: *Existimante autem populo, et cogitantibus omnibus in cordibus suis,* existimatione falsa et cogitatione erronea de Ioanne, ne forte esset Christus, in Lege scilicet promissus; nec tantum existimantibus, sed etiam quaerentibus exterius, Ioannis primo[^p78-4]: « Miserunt Iudaei ab Ierosolymis sacerdotes et levitas ad Ioannem, ut interrogarent eum: Tu quis es »? Et hoc, quia suspicabantur, eum esse Christum; unde supra primo: « Quis, putas, puer iste erit »? Non autem debebant eum existimare Christum, sed eius ministrum, secundum illud primae ad Corinthios quarto: « Sic nos existimet homo ut ministros Christi » etc. Et ideo in tali cogitatione errabant; unde Beda[^p78-5]: « Mira caecitas Iudaeorum, quod in Ioanne sponte credebant, hoc in Salvatore tantis signis et virtutibus approbato, et etiam ipso Ioanne attestante, non credunt ». Unde non immerito « caeci et duces caecorum » vocantur, Matthaei decimo quinto, qui « ponebant tenebras lucem et lucem tenebras »; unde Ioannis primo: « Non erat ille lux ».

36. (Vers. 16.). Quantum ad *veritatis confessionem in Ioanne* subditur: *Respondit Ioannes, dicens omnibus,* id est publice, quia omnes errabant: *Ego quidem aqua baptizo vos,* et « tantum, sicut dicit Ambrosius[^p78-6], corpora tingo »; ac per hoc non sum salvator animarum, quem vos me arbitramini esse. Unde ipse dixit Ioannis primo: « Super quem videris Spiritum sanctum descendentem et manentem, hic est, qui baptizat in Spiritu sancto ». Unde cum quaereretur a Iudaeis, quis esset, secundum quod dicitur Ioannis primo[^p78-7], « confessus est et non negavit, et confessus est: Quia non sum ego Christus ». Et ideo poterat dicere illud secundae ad Corinthios duodecimo: « Parco autem, ne quis me existimet supra id quod videt in me, aut aliquid audit ex me ».

37. Et nota, quod est baptismus *fluminis, flaminis* et *sanguinis.* Baptismus *fluminis* est duplex: quidam in *aqua tantum,* ut beati Ioannis; Actuum decimo nono[^p78-8]: « In quo baptizati estis? Qui dixerunt: In Ioannis baptismate »; quidam in *aqua et Spiritu,* ut baptismus Christi; Ioannis tertio: « Nisi quis renatus fuerit ex aqua et Spiritu sancto » etc. — Baptismus *flaminis:* quidam in *dono gratiae;* Actuum primo[^p78-9]: « Ioannes quidem baptizavit aqua, vos autem baptizabimini Spiritu sancto »; quidam in *verbo doctrinae;* Canticorum quarto: « Dentes tui sicut greges tonsarum, quae ascenderunt de lavacro », per quas *praedicatores* intelligimus; et Ioannis decimo quinto: « Vos mundi estis propter sermonem, quem locutus sum vobis ». — Baptismus *sanguinis:* quidam in *tribulatione;* Isaiae quarto[^p78-10]: « Si abluerit Dominus sordes filiarum Sion et sanguinem Ierusalem laverit de medio eius in spiritu iudicii et spiritu ardoris »; quidam in *morte;* Lucae duodecimo: « Baptismo habeo baptizari et quomodo coarctor, usque dum perficiatur ».

38. Quantum autem ad *commendationem sublimitatis in Salvatore* subditur: *Venit*[^p78-11] *autem fortior*
<!-- page 79 -->
*me* etc. Commendat autem Ioannes Christum quantum ad *mysterium incarnationis,* quantum ad *Sacramentum reparationis* et quantum ad *iudicium retributionis.* In primo est nostrae salutis initium; in secundo, incrementum; in tertio, complementum.

Quantum autem ad *mysterium incarnationis* dicit: *Venit autem fortior me*[^p79-1], fortior, dignior et excellentior; Isaiae nono: « Vocabitur nomen eius Admirabilis », quia, primae ad Corinthios primo, « quod infirmum est Dei fortius est hominibus »; et hoc quidem non modicum, sed incomparabiliter; propter quod subditur: *Cuius non sum dignus solvere corrigiam calceamentorum eius. Ad litteram* potest hoc esse, ut tantum se humiliet, quod non sit dignus servire ei in humillimo ministerio; unde etiam Matthaei tertio[^p79-2]: « Cuius non sum dignus calceamenta portare ». — Vel *spiritualiter* per *calceamentum* intelligitur humana natura assumta, secundum illud Psalmi[^p79-3]: « In Idumaeam extendam calceamentum meum ». Per *corrigiam* intelligo ipsam unionem. Est ergo sensus secundum Bedam: *Cuius non sum dignus* etc., « id est incarnationis mysterium explicare », quia dicitur Apocalypsis quinto, quod « nemo inventus est dignus aperire librum et solvere signacula eius nisi solus Agnus ». — Aliter intelligitur tertio modo sic: *Non sum dignus* etc., id est evangelicam praedicationem per mundum portare; unde Beda[^p79-4]: « Calceamentum nuptiale est evangelica praedicatio, qua calceati fuerunt Apostoli »; ad Ephesios sexto: « Calceati pedes in praeparatione Evangelii pacis ». — Aliter etiam exponitur quarto modo, ut sit sensus: *Non sum dignus solvere,* id est, ipsum secundum morem Legis discalceare et uxorem eius mihi copulare; unde Ioannis tertio[^p79-5]: « Qui habet sponsam sponsus est; amicus autem sponsi stat » etc. — Sed secundum primum sensum maius videtur habere testimonium, in quo mira exprimitur beati Ioannis humilitas, ut, cum reputetur a Deo maximus, ipse reputet se minimum, secundum illud Ecclesiastici tertio[^p79-6]: « Quanto magnus es, humilia te in omnibus, et coram Deo invenies gratiam ».

39. Quantum ad *Sacramentum reparationis* subditur: *Ipse vos baptizabit in Spiritu sancto;* Actuum primo[^p79-7]: « Ioannes quidem baptizavit aqua, vos autem baptizabimini Spiritu sancto »; alioqui, « nisi quis renatus fuerit ex aqua et Spiritu sancto, non potest introire in regnum Dei »; et hoc quidem erit *interius. Et igni,* hoc dicitur quantum ad *signum exterius;* de quo dicitur Actuum secundo[^p79-8], quod « apparuerunt illis dispertitae linguae tanquam ignis »; et hoc, quia Spiritus sancti *amor* recte per ignem designatur, secundum quod dicitur infra duodecimo: « Ignem veni mittere in terram » etc. — Vel *igni,* scilicet *tribulationis* purificantis a sequela peccati, secundum illud Sophoniae tertio[^p79-9]: « In igne zeli mei devorabitur omnis terra ». — Vel *igni,* scilicet *purgatorio,* in quo purgabitur scoria peccati; primae ad Corinthios tertio: « Ipse autem salvus erit, sic tamen quasi per ignem ». — Vel in *igne tentationis;* Psalmus: « Igne me examinasti, et non est inventa in me iniquitas ».

40. (Vers. 17.). Quantum ad *iustitiam retributionis* subdit: *Cuius ventilabrum in manu eius.* Hic autem *iustitia retributionis* commendatur quantum ad tria, scilicet quantum ad *iudicium discretivum meritorum,* quantum ad *praemiationem bonorum* et quantum ad *punitionem reproborum.*

Quantum igitur ad *iudicium discretivum* dicitur: *Cuius ventilabrum in manu eius.* Hoc dicit quantum ad potestatem iudiciariam, qua discernuntur boni a malis, sicut per ventilabrum purgantur frumenta a scoriis; Ieremiae decimo quinto[^p79-10]: « Dispergam
<!-- page 80 -->
eos ventilabro in portis terrae ». Et dicitur *in manu,* id est plenaria potestate, secundum illud Ioannis quinto[^p80-1]: « Pater omne iudicium dedit Filio »; et ideo dicitur primae Petri quinto: « Humiliamini sub potenti manu Dei, ut vos exaltet in tempore visitationis »; Malachiae tertio: « Ecce venit, dicit Dominus exercituum, et quis poterit cogitare diem adventus eius »? Et quoniam potestatis iudiciariae est sequestrare bonos a malis, ut bonos glorificet et malos affligat; ideo quantum ad *discretionem* et *sequestrationem* dicitur: *Et purgabit*[^p80-2] *aream suam;* Ioel ultimo: « Mundabo sanguinem eorum, quem non mundaveram, et Dominus commorabitur in Sion ». Quia boni permixti sunt modo malis occultis; ideo dicitur in Psalmo: « Ab occultis meis munda me », in persona Ecclesiae. Hoc fiet, quando implebitur illud Isaiae vigesimo sexto: « Tollatur impius, ne videat gloriam Dei »; et alibi: « Foris canes, venefici, impudici et idolis servientes ». — Et nota, quod Ecclesia militans intelligitur per *areolam* propter arctitudinem *humilitatis;* Canticorum sexto[^p80-3]: « Dilectus meus descendit in hortum suum ad areolam aromatum »; sed per *aream* propter latitudinem *caritatis;* Iudicum sexto: « Ponam hoc vellus in area » etc. Haec mundatur in fine, secundum illud Matthaei decimo tertio: « Sic erit in consummatione saeculi; exibunt Angeli et separabunt malos de medio iustorum ».

41. Quantum autem ad *praemiationem bonorum* dicitur: *Et congregabit triticum in horreum suum;* et hoc est, quando Sancti in gloria ad unum reducentur: Isaiae quinquagesimo sexto[^p80-4]: « Haec dicit Dominus, qui congregat dispersos Israel: Adhuc congregabo congregationes ». Et recte electi ratione fructuositatis comparantur tritico, secundum illud Ioannis duodecimo: « Nisi granum frumenti cadens in terram mortuum fuerit » etc.; Iob quinto: « Ingredieris in abundantia sepulcrum, sicut infertur acervus tritici in tempore suo ».

42. Quantum vero ad *punitionem reproborum* subditur: *Paleas autem comburet igni inexstinguibili. Paleas* reprobos vocat, quia sunt materia combustionis; Ieremiae vigesimo tertio[^p80-5]: « Quid paleis ad triticum? dicit Dominus ». Unde Iob vigesimo primo: « Erunt sicut paleae ante faciem venti et sicut favilla, quam turbo dispergit »; Psalmus: « Deus meus, pone illos ut rotam et sicut stipulam ante faciem venti ». Et ita paleae sunt in pabulum ignis sempiterni, qui nec exstinguitur nec exstinguit, secundum illud Isaiae ultimo[^p80-6]: « Vermis eorum non morietur, et ignis eorum non exstinguetur »; et Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo: « Ignis succensus est in furore meo et ardebit usque ad inferni novissima ». Haec autem palearum exustio longe erit a tritico congregato, quia isti erunt in inferno, boni autem in paradiso, in quo congregabuntur in gloria, quia hic congregati fuerunt in gratia.

43. Propter quod intelligendum, quod triplex est congregatio: ad *studium veritatis;* Ecclesiastici quinquagesimo primo[^p80-7]: « Appropiate ad me, indocti, et congregate vos in domum disciplinae »; ad *exercitium virtutis;* Ioel secundo: « Canite tuba in Sion, sanctificate ieiunium, vocate coetum, congregate populum, sanctificate ecclesiam » etc.; ad *praemium felicitatis;* Psalmus: « Principes populorum congregati sunt cum Deo Abraham ». — Ad *exercitium virtutis,* videlicet in *oratione;* Actuum quarto[^p80-8]: « Cum orassent, motus est locus, in quo erant congregati »; in *praedicatione;* Actuum primo: « Oportet ex his viris, qui nobiscum sunt congregati », et postea: « testem resurrectionis eius nobiscum fieri unum ex istis »; in *conversatione;* Matthaei decimo octavo: « Ubi sunt duo, vel tres congregati in nomine meo, ibi sum in medio eorum ». — Ad *studium veritatis* ad cognoscendum divina *praecepta;* Deuteronomii quarto[^p80-9]: « Congrega ad me universum populum, ut audiant sermones meos »; divina *Sacramenta;* Psalmus: « Congregate illi sanctos eius, qui ordinant testamentum eius super sacrificia »; divina *promissa;* Genesis penultimo: « Congregamini ad me, filii Iacob, ut annuntiem quae ventura sunt vobis ». — Ad *praemium felicitatis* per *cohaerentiam pacis;* Matthaei vigesimo quarto[^p80-10]:
<!-- page 81 -->
« Mittet Filius hominis Angelos suos »; et postea: « Et congregabunt electos eius a quatuor ventis » etc.; per *refulgentiam lucis;* Isaiae sexagesimo: « Omnes isti congregati sunt, venerunt tibi »; per *consonantiam laudis;* Psalmus: « Salvos nos fac, Domine Deus noster, et congrega nos de nationibus, ut confiteamur nomini sancto tuo ».

#### Praedicatio Ioannis commendatur tertio quoad perfidiam tyranni tripliciter.

44. (Vers. 18.). *Multa quidem et alia exhortans evangelizabat* etc. Supra commendavit praedicationem Ioannis in comparatione ad exigentiam populi et excellentiam Christi; in hac parte commendat in comparatione ad *perfidiam tyranni,* in qua laudatur patientia, quia, Proverbiorum decimo nono[^p81-1], « doctrina viri per patientiam noscitur ». In hac igitur parte primo introducitur Ioannes *exhortans populum obedientem;* secundo, *corripiens principem praevaricantem;* tertio, *sustinens adversarium persequentem.*

Quantum ergo ad *exhortationem populi obedientis* dicitur: *Multa quidem et alia exhortans evangelizabat populo;* sed tamen omnia erant ad bonum ordinata; secundae ad Corinthios sexto[^p81-2]: « Adiuvantes exhortamur, ne in vacuum gratiam Dei recipiatis » etc.; et primae ad Thessalonicenses secundo: « Exhortatio mea non de errore, aut immunditia nec in dolo, sed sicut probati sumus, ut crederetur nobis Evangelium ». Et ideo subditur: *Evangelizabat,* id est, bona annuntiabat; Isaiae quinquagesimo secundo[^p81-3]: « Quam pulcri super montes pedes annuntiantis et praedicantis pacem, annuntiantis bona, praedicantis salutem » etc.

45. (Vers. 19.). Quantum ad *correptionem principis praevaricantis* subditur: *Herodes autem tetrarcha,* de quo habitum est supra eodem[^p81-4], *cum corriperetur ab illo de Herodiade, uxore fratris sui;* Matthaei decimo quarto: « Dicebat Ioannes Herodi: Non licet tibi habere eam ». *Et de omnibus malis, quae fecit Herodes.* Et ita non parcebat ei; Ecclesiastici decimo[^p81-5]: « Gloria divitum, honoratorum et pauperum timor Dei est, non despicere hominem iustum pauperem, et non magnificare virum peccatorem divitem ». Unde sibi competit illud quod dicitur de Eliseo Ecclesiastici quadragesimo octavo: « In diebus suis non pertimuit principem, et potentia nemo vicit illum, nec superavit eum verbum aliquod ». Et quia publice peccabat, publice arguebat, secundum illud primae ad Timotheum quinto[^p81-6]: « Peccantem coram omnibus argue, ut ceteri timorem habeant », et hoc quidem audacter; ad Titum secundo: « Haec loquere et exhortare et argue cum omni imperio ».

46. (Vers. 20.). Quantum ad *supportationem adversarii persequentis* subditur de Herode: *Adiecit et hoc super omnia et inclusit Ioannem in carcere*[^p81-7]. Et sic verificatum est illud Ecclesiastici tertio: « Cor nequam gravabitur in doloribus, et peccator adiiciet ad peccandum »; et hoc iusto Dei iudicio; Apocalypsis ultimo: « Qui in sordibus est sordescat adhuc ». Et sic verificatum est illud Amos quinto[^p81-8]: « Odio habuerunt corripientem in porta »; sicut Achab Michaeam, tertii Regum ultimo; sicut Sedecias Ieremiam, trigesimo secundo; sicut alius Herodes Petrum, Actuum duodecimo. Propter quod ad Hebraeos undecimo: « Alii ludibria et verbera experti, insuper et vincula et carceres, secti sunt » etc.

## English
<!-- page 78 -->

#### The preaching of John is commended second as to the excellence of Christ, in three ways.

35. (Vers. 15.). *And as the people were of opinion* etc. The preaching of John has been commended in comparison with the requirement of the people; here, second, it is commended in comparison with the *excellence of Christ,* in this, that he does not usurp the glory due to Christ. Hence, just as above[^p78-3] he administered sound doctrine, so here he avoids glory not his own. For explaining which three things are introduced: the first is the *opinion of falsehood in the common people;* the second is the *confession of truth in John;* the third is the *commendation of sublimity in the Saviour.*

As to the *opinion of falsehood in the common people* it is said: *And as the people were of opinion, and all were thinking in their hearts,* with a false opinion and an erroneous thought, of John, that perhaps he might be the Christ, namely the one promised in the Law; and not only were they of opinion, but they also inquired outwardly, John, the first chapter[^p78-4]: « The Jews sent from Jerusalem priests and levites to John, to ask him: Who art thou »? And this, because they suspected that he was the Christ; hence above, in the first chapter: « What a one, thinkest thou, shall this child be »? But they ought not to have thought him the Christ, but his minister, according to that saying of the first to the Corinthians, the fourth chapter: « Let a man so think of us as of the ministers of Christ » etc. And therefore they erred in such a thought; hence Bede[^p78-5]: « Wonderful the blindness of the Jews, that what they believed of their own accord in John, this they do not believe in the Saviour, approved by such signs and powers, and with John himself also attesting ». Hence not undeservedly are they called « blind, and leaders of the blind », Matthew, the fifteenth chapter, who « put darkness for light, and light for darkness »; hence John, the first chapter: « He was not the light ».

36. (Vers. 16.). As to the *confession of truth in John* it is added: *John answered, saying unto all,* that is, publicly, because all were erring: *I indeed baptize you with water,* and « only, as Ambrose says[^p78-6], do I dip bodies »; and through this I am not the saviour of souls, whom you think me to be. Hence he himself said, John, the first chapter: « He upon whom thou shalt see the Holy Spirit descending and remaining, he it is that baptizeth in the Holy Spirit ». Hence when it was asked by the Jews who he was, according to what is said in John, the first chapter[^p78-7], « he confessed, and did not deny: and he confessed: I am not the Christ ». And therefore he could say that saying of the second to the Corinthians, the twelfth chapter: « But I forbear, lest any man should think of me above that which he seeth in me, or any thing he heareth from me ».

37. And note that there is a baptism of *water* [*fluminis*], of *the Spirit* [*flaminis*] and of *blood.* The baptism of *water* is twofold: one in *water only,* as that of blessed John; Acts, the nineteenth chapter[^p78-8]: « In what were you baptized? Who said: In John's baptism »; one in *water and the Spirit,* as the baptism of Christ; John, the third chapter: « Unless a man be born again of water and the Holy Spirit » etc. — The baptism of *the Spirit:* one in the *gift of grace;* Acts, the first chapter[^p78-9]: « John indeed baptized with water, but you shall be baptized with the Holy Spirit »; one in the *word of doctrine;* Canticles, the fourth chapter: « Thy teeth as flocks of sheep that are shorn, which came up from the washing », by which we understand *preachers;* and John, the fifteenth chapter: « You are clean by reason of the word which I have spoken to you ». — The baptism of *blood:* one in *tribulation;* Isaias, the fourth chapter[^p78-10]: « If the Lord shall wash away the filth of the daughters of Sion, and shall wash away the blood of Jerusalem out of the midst thereof, by the spirit of judgement and by the spirit of burning »; one in *death;* Luke, the twelfth chapter: « I have a baptism wherewith to be baptized: and how am I straitened until it be accomplished ».

38. But as to the *commendation of sublimity in the Saviour* it is added: *But there cometh*[^p78-11] *one mightier*
<!-- page 79 -->
*than I* etc. Now John commends Christ as to the *mystery of the incarnation,* as to the *Sacrament of reparation* and as to the *judgement of retribution.* In the first is the beginning of our salvation; in the second, its increase; in the third, its completion.

But as to the *mystery of the incarnation* he says: *But there cometh one mightier than I*[^p79-1], mightier, worthier and more excellent; Isaias, the ninth chapter: « His name shall be called Wonderful », because, the first to the Corinthians, the first chapter, « the weakness of God is mightier than men »; and this indeed not in a small measure, but incomparably; on account of which it is added: *the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to loose. According to the letter* this can be, that he so humbles himself that he is not worthy to serve him in the humblest ministry; hence also Matthew, the third chapter[^p79-2]: « Whose shoes I am not worthy to bear ». — Or *spiritually* by the *shoe* is understood the human nature assumed, according to that saying of the Psalm[^p79-3]: « Into Idumea will I stretch out my shoe ». By the *latchet* I understand the union itself. The sense, then, according to Bede, is: *I am not worthy* etc., « that is, to unfold the mystery of the incarnation », because it is said in the Apocalypse, the fifth chapter, that « no man was found worthy to open the book and to loose the seals thereof, but the Lamb alone ». — Otherwise it is understood in a third way thus: *I am not worthy* etc., that is, to carry the evangelical preaching through the world; hence Bede[^p79-4]: « The nuptial shoe is the evangelical preaching, with which the Apostles were shod »; to the Ephesians, the sixth chapter: « Your feet shod in the preparation of the gospel of peace ». — Otherwise again it is expounded in a fourth way, so that the sense is: *I am not worthy to loose,* that is, according to the custom of the Law to unshoe him and to join his wife to myself; hence John, the third chapter[^p79-5]: « He that hath the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom standeth » etc. — But the first sense seems to have the greater testimony, in which the wonderful humility of blessed John is expressed, that, while he is reputed by God the greatest, he himself reputes himself the least, according to that saying of Ecclesiasticus, the third chapter[^p79-6]: « The greater thou art, the more humble thyself in all things, and thou shalt find grace before God ».

39. As to the *Sacrament of reparation* it is added: *He shall baptize you in the Holy Spirit;* Acts, the first chapter[^p79-7]: « John indeed baptized with water, but you shall be baptized with the Holy Spirit »; otherwise, « unless a man be born again of water and the Holy Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God »; and this indeed will be *interior. And with fire,* this is said as to the *exterior sign;* of which it is said in Acts, the second chapter[^p79-8], that « there appeared to them parted tongues as it were of fire »; and this, because the *love* of the Holy Spirit is rightly designated by fire, according to what is said below, in the twelfth chapter: « I am come to cast fire on the earth » etc. — Or *with fire,* namely of *tribulation* purifying from the sequel of sin, according to that saying of Sophonias, the third chapter[^p79-9]: « With the fire of my zeal shall all the earth be devoured ». — Or *with fire,* namely *purgatorial,* in which the dross of sin will be purged; the first to the Corinthians, the third chapter: « But he himself shall be saved, yet so as by fire ». — Or in the *fire of temptation;* the Psalm: « Thou hast tried me by fire, and iniquity hath not been found in me ».

40. (Vers. 17.). As to the *justice of retribution* he adds: *Whose fan is in his hand.* Now here the *justice of retribution* is commended as to three things, namely as to the *discretive judgement of merits,* as to the *rewarding of the good* and as to the *punishment of the reprobate.*

As to the *discretive judgement,* then, it is said: *Whose fan is in his hand.* This he says as to the judiciary power, by which the good are discerned from the wicked, as by the fan the grains are purged of dross; Jeremias, the fifteenth chapter[^p79-10]: « I will scatter
<!-- page 80 -->
them with a fan in the gates of the land ». And it is said *in his hand,* that is, with plenary power, according to that saying of John, the fifth chapter[^p80-1]: « The Father hath given all judgement to the Son »; and therefore it is said in the first of Peter, the fifth chapter: « Be you humbled under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in the time of visitation »; Malachias, the third chapter: « Behold he cometh, saith the Lord of hosts, and who shall be able to think of the day of his coming »? And since it belongs to the judiciary power to sequester the good from the wicked, that he may glorify the good and afflict the wicked; therefore as to the *discretion* and *sequestration* it is said: *And he will purge*[^p80-2] *his floor;* Joel, the last chapter: « I will cleanse their blood which I had not cleansed, and the Lord will dwell in Sion ». Because the good are now mixed with the hidden wicked; therefore it is said in the Psalm: « From my secret ones cleanse me », in the person of the Church. This will come to pass when that saying of Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter, shall be fulfilled: « Let the wicked be taken away, that he may not see the glory of God »; and elsewhere: « Without are dogs, sorcerers, the unchaste and those that serve idols ». — And note that the Church militant is understood by the *little floor* on account of the narrowness of *humility;* Canticles, the sixth chapter[^p80-3]: « My beloved is gone down into his garden, to the little floor of aromatical spices »; but by the *floor* on account of the breadth of *charity;* Judges, the sixth chapter: « I will put this fleece on the floor » etc. This is cleansed at the end, according to that saying of Matthew, the thirteenth chapter: « So shall it be at the consummation of the world: the Angels shall go out, and shall separate the wicked from among the just ».

41. But as to the *rewarding of the good* it is said: *And he will gather the wheat into his barn;* and this is when the Saints shall be brought back to one in glory: Isaias, the fifty-sixth chapter[^p80-4]: « Thus saith the Lord, who gathereth the scattered of Israel: I will yet gather gatherings ». And rightly are the elect compared to wheat by reason of fruitfulness, according to that saying of John, the twelfth chapter: « Unless the grain of wheat falling into the ground die » etc.; Job, the fifth chapter: « Thou shalt enter into the grave in abundance, as a heap of wheat is brought in its season ».

42. But as to the *punishment of the reprobate* it is added: *But the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire.* He calls the reprobate *chaff,* because they are the matter of burning; Jeremias, the twenty-third chapter[^p80-5]: « What hath the chaff to do with the wheat, saith the Lord »? Hence Job, the twenty-first chapter: « They shall be as chaff before the face of the wind, and as ashes which the whirlwind scattereth »; the Psalm: « O my God, make them like a wheel, and as stubble before the face of the wind ». And so the chaff is for the food of the everlasting fire, which is neither quenched nor quenches, according to that saying of Isaias, the last chapter[^p80-6]: « Their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched »; and Deuteronomy, the thirty-second chapter: « A fire is kindled in my wrath, and shall burn even to the lowest hell ». But this burning of the chaff will be far from the gathered wheat, because these will be in hell, but the good in paradise, in which they will be gathered in glory, because here they were gathered in grace.

43. On account of which it is to be understood that the gathering is threefold: for the *study of truth;* Ecclesiasticus, the fifty-first chapter[^p80-7]: « Draw near to me, ye unlearned, and gather yourselves together into the house of discipline »; for the *exercise of virtue;* Joel, the second chapter: « Blow the trumpet in Sion, sanctify a fast, call a solemn assembly, gather together the people, sanctify the church » etc.; for the *reward of felicity;* the Psalm: « The princes of the people are gathered together with the God of Abraham ». — For the *exercise of virtue,* namely in *prayer;* Acts, the fourth chapter[^p80-8]: « When they had prayed, the place was moved wherein they were gathered together »; in *preaching;* Acts, the first chapter: « Of these men who are gathered together with us », and afterwards: « one of these must be made a witness with us of his resurrection »; in *conversation;* Matthew, the eighteenth chapter: « Where there are two or three gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them ». — For the *study of truth,* for knowing the divine *precepts;* Deuteronomy, the fourth chapter[^p80-9]: « Gather unto me all the people, that they may hear my words »; the divine *Sacraments;* the Psalm: « Gather ye together his saints to him, who set his covenant before sacrifices »; the divine *promises;* Genesis, the next to last chapter: « Gather yourselves together unto me, ye sons of Jacob, that I may tell you the things that are to come to you ». — For the *reward of felicity* through the *coherence of peace;* Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p80-10]:
<!-- page 81 -->
« The Son of man shall send his Angels »; and afterwards: « And they shall gather together his elect from the four winds » etc.; through the *refulgence of light;* Isaias, the sixtieth chapter: « All these are gathered together, they are come to thee »; through the *consonance of praise;* the Psalm: « Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the nations, that we may give thanks to thy holy name ».

#### The preaching of John is commended third as to the perfidy of the tyrant, in three ways.

44. (Vers. 18.). *And many other things exhorting, did he preach the gospel* etc. Above he commended the preaching of John in comparison with the requirement of the people and the excellence of Christ; in this part he commends it in comparison with the *perfidy of the tyrant,* in which patience is praised, because, Proverbs, the nineteenth chapter[^p81-1], « the learning of a man is known by patience ». In this part, then, John is introduced first *exhorting the obedient people;* second, *correcting the prevaricating prince;* third, *bearing the persecuting adversary.*

As to the *exhortation of the obedient people,* then, it is said: *And many other things exhorting, did he preach the gospel to the people;* but yet all were ordered to the good; the second to the Corinthians, the sixth chapter[^p81-2]: « Helping, we exhort you that you receive not the grace of God in vain » etc.; and the first to the Thessalonians, the second chapter: « My exhortation was not of error, nor of uncleanness, nor in deceit, but as we were approved, that the gospel should be committed to us ». And therefore it is added: *He preached the gospel,* that is, he announced good things; Isaias, the fifty-second chapter[^p81-3]: « How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that announceth and preacheth peace, that announceth good things, that preacheth salvation » etc.

45. (Vers. 19.). As to the *correction of the prevaricating prince* it is added: *But Herod the tetrarch,* of whom it was treated above in the same chapter[^p81-4], *when he was corrected by him for Herodias, his brother's wife;* Matthew, the fourteenth chapter: « John said to Herod: It is not lawful for thee to have her ». *And for all the evils which Herod had done.* And so he did not spare him; Ecclesiasticus, the tenth chapter[^p81-5]: « The glory of the rich, of the honourable and of the poor is the fear of God: not to despise a just man that is poor, and not to magnify a sinful man that is rich ». Hence there befits him that which is said of Eliseus in Ecclesiasticus, the forty-eighth chapter: « In his days he feared not the prince, and no man overcame him in power, nor did any word overcome him ». And because he sinned publicly, he reproved him publicly, according to that saying of the first to Timothy, the fifth chapter[^p81-6]: « Reprove him that sinneth before all, that the rest also may have fear », and this indeed boldly; to Titus, the second chapter: « These things speak, and exhort, and reprove with all authority ».

46. (Vers. 20.). As to the *bearing of the persecuting adversary* it is added of Herod: *He added this also above all, and shut up John in prison*[^p81-7]. And so was verified that saying of Ecclesiasticus, the third chapter: « A wicked heart shall be laden with sorrows, and the sinner will add to sinning »; and this by the just judgement of God; the Apocalypse, the last chapter: « He that is in filth, let him be filthy still ». And so was verified that saying of Amos, the fifth chapter[^p81-8]: « They have hated him that correcteth in the gate »; as Achab hated Micheas, the third of Kings, the last chapter; as Sedecias hated Jeremias, the thirty-second chapter; as the other Herod hated Peter, Acts, the twelfth chapter. On account of which, to the Hebrews, the eleventh chapter: « Others had trial of mockeries and stripes, moreover also of bands and prisons; they were cut asunder » etc.
