# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XXI, Vers. 20-24
## *Cum autem videritis circumdari ab exercitu etc.*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 530 -->

28. *Cum autem videritis circumdari ab exercitu* etc. Postquam descripsit quae ventura erant generaliter, et etiam persecutiones ipsius Ecclesiae; hic[^p530-7] describit *quae ventura sunt circa destructionem Ierusalem,* per quam intelligitur tribulatio, quam Ecclesia passura est tempore antichristi. Habet autem pars ista duas partes. In quarum prima praedicit *bellum praecedens;* in secunda vero, *excidium subsequens,* ibi[^p530-8]: *Vae autem praegnantibus et nutrientibus* etc.

*Bellum* autem praecedens describit quantum ad tria, scilicet quantum ad *periculum pugnae, remedium fugae* et *iudicium divinae vindictae.*

29. (Vers. 20.). Primo quantum ad *periculum pugnae* dicit: *Cum autem videritis, circumdari ab exercitu Ierusalem,* per obsidionem Romanorum, secundum illud Ezechielis quarto[^p530-9]: « Fili hominis, sume tibi laterem, et pones eum coram te et describes in eo civitatem Ierusalem et ordinabis adversus eam obsidionem et aedificabis munitiones et comportabis aggerem et dabis contra eam castra et pones arietes in gyro ». — *Tunc scitote, quia appropinquavit*[^p530-10] *desolatio eius,* per eversionem omnimodam; Danielis nono: « Civitatem et sanctuarium dissipabit populus cum duce venturo; et finis eius vastitas, et post finem belli statuta desolatio » etc.

30. *Spiritualiter* autem per *exercitum circumdantem Ierusalem* intelligitur insurrectio perversorum contra ecclesiasticam pacem procurata per antichristum et ministros ipsius, secundum illud Apocalypsis decimo sexto[^p530-11]: « Et vidi, de ore draconis et de ore bestiae et de ore pseudoprophetae exire tres spiritus immundos in modum ranarum. Sunt enim spiritus daemoniorum facientes signa, et procedunt ad reges totius terrae congregare eos in proelium ad diem magnum omnipotentis Dei »; et Apocalypsis decimo nono: « Vidi bestiam et reges terrae et exercitus eorum congregatos ad faciendum proelium cum illo qui sedebat in equo ». Sed quia mali incomparabiliter plures erunt, ideo dicuntur *circumdare.* In cuius figuram primi Regum vigesimo tertio[^p530-12]: « Saul et viri eius in modum coronae cingebant David et viros eius, ut caperent eos ». Unde Iob decimo nono: « Simul venerunt latrones eius et per me fecerunt sibi viam et obsederunt tabernaculum meum in gyro ».

31. (Vers. 21.). Secundo quantum ad *remedium fugae* subdit: *Tunc qui in Iudaea sunt fugiant ad*
<!-- page 531 -->
*montes,* ne inveniantur; *et qui in medio eius sunt*[^p531-1] *discedant,* ne includantur; *et qui in regionibus non intrent in eam,* ne ibi capiantur. Exercitus enim Romanorum primo per Iudaeam evertit multas civitates, antequam veniret Ierusalem. Et omnes pene Iudaei intraverant ibi, sicut dicit Iosephus[^p531-2], propter solemnitatem et propter securitatem; ideo comprehensi sunt omnes iusto iudicio Dei, secundum illud Ieremiae duodecimo: « Congrega eos sicut gregem ad victimam et sanctifica eos in die occisionis ». Sed Christiani per fugam liberati sunt; unde Glossa Bedae[^p531-3]: « Ecclesiastica historia narrat, Christianos, qui in Iudaea erant, imminente excidio, ab Angelo monitos fuisse et trans Iordanem in civitate Pella, donec desolatio Iudaeae impleretur, habitasse ».

32. *Spiritualiter* autem insinuatur hic remedium, quod erit infirmis in illa valida persecutione antichristi, scilicet *fugae* et *occultationis.* Licet enim *imperfectis* fugere propter cavenda pericula; unde Zachariae secundo[^p531-4]: « O, o, o! fugite de terra aquilonis, dicit Dominus ». Quibus Christus condescendens, abscondebat se a Iudaeis, secundum illud Ioannis septimo: « Post haec ambulabat Iesus in Galilaeam. Non enim volebat ambulare in Iudaea, quia quaerebant eum Iudaei interficere ». Licet etiam hoc viris *perfectis,* cum vident, quod magis expedit saluti populi, quod fugiant; unde dixit Dominus discipulis Matthaei decimo[^p531-5]: « Cum autem persequentur vos in una civitate, fugite in aliam »; et de Paulo dicitur Actuum nono, quod cum « Iudaei custodirent portas Damasci die ac nocte, ut Paulum interficerent, accipientes eum discipuli, nocte per murum dimiserunt eum, submittentes in sporta ». Huius figura praecessit in David; primi Regum decimo nono[^p531-6]: « Porro ille abiit et aufugit et salvatus est »; submissus enim fuit a Michol per fenestram.

33. (Vers. 22.). Tertio quantum ad *iudicium divinae vindictae* subdit: *Quia dies ultionis hi sunt, ut impleantur omnia, quae*[^p531-7] *scripta sunt.* Decrevit autem Dominus destruere Ierusalem in ultionem sanguinis Iesu Christi et Prophetarum eius; unde Matthaei vigesimo tertio[^p531-8]: « Ut veniat super vos omnis sanguis iustus, qui effusus est super terram, a sanguine Abel iusti usque ad sanguinem Zachariae. Amen dico vobis: Venient haec omnia super generationem istam ». Nam tantum scelus nullo modo debuit remanere inultum; ideo Threnorum secundo: « Fecit Dominus quae cogitavit, complevit sermonem suum, quem praeceperat a diebus antiquis. Destruxit et non pepercit et laetificavit super te inimicum et exaltavit cornu hostium tuorum ».

34. *Spiritualiter* autem intelligitur per haec ultio divinae indignationis propter peccata *personarum ecclesiasticarum,* tam saecularium quam religiosarum, secundum illud Michaeae tertio[^p531-9]: « Principes eius in muneribus iudicabant, et sacerdotes eius in mercede docebant, et prophetae eius in pecunia divinabant ». « Propter hoc Sion quasi ager arabitur » etc. Et specialiter propter peccata *praelatorum;* Isaiae primo[^p531-10]: « Quomodo facta est meretrix civitas fidelis, plena iudicii! Iustitia habitavit in ea, nunc autem homicidae »; « principes tui infideles, socii furum. Omnes diligunt munera, sequuntur retributiones ». « Propter hoc ait Dominus Deus exercituum: Heu! consolabor super hostibus meis et vindicabor de inimicis meis ». Ideo Isaiae trigesimo quarto[^p531-11]: « Dies ultionis Domini, annus retributionum iudicii Sion »; et secundae ad Thessalonicenses secundo: « Mittet eis Deus operationem erroris, ut credant mendacio, ut iudicentur omnes, qui non crediderunt veritati, sed consenserunt iniquitati ». Et tunc in ultima tribulatione consummabuntur omnia, secundum illud Apocalypsis decimo[^p531-12]: « In diebus vocis septimi Angeli, cum coeperit tuba canere, consummabitur mysterium Dei, quod locutus est per servos suos Prophetas »; et decimo quinto: « Impletum est templum Dei fumo a maiestate Dei, et nemo poterat introire in templum, donec consummarentur septem plagae septem Angelorum ».

35. (Vers. 23.). *Vae autem praegnantibus et nutrientibus* etc. Postquam descripsit bellum praecedens, describit hic secundo[^p531-13] *excidium subsequens;* circa quod describendum tria insinuantur, scilicet *oppressio civitatis occupandae, destructio civitatis oppressae* et *desolatio civitatis destructae.*

Primo igitur quantum ad *oppressionem civitatis occupandae* dicit: *Vae autem praegnantibus et nutrientibus in illis diebus!* Glossa[^p531-14]: « Quarum
<!-- page 532 -->
uteri vel manus sarcina filiorum praegravatae fugae necessitatem impedient »; quia huiusmodi non poterit prodesse fuga, secundum illud primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto: « Repentinus eis superveniet interitus sicut dolor in utero habentis, et non effugient ». — Ratio autem huius est vehemens oppressio civitatis; ideo addit: *Erit enim*[^p532-1] *pressura magna super terram et ira populo huic,* secundum illud Isaiae vigesimo nono: « Vae Ariel! Vae Ariel! civitas, quam expugnavit David. Et circumvallabo Ariel, et erit tristis et moerens. Et circumvallabo ut sphaeram in circuitu tuo et iaciam contra te aggerem et munimenta ponam in obsidionem tuam ».

36. Secundum autem *mysticum intellectum* per *praegnantes* intelliguntur animae peccatrices, quae *concipiunt* per peccati concupiscentiam et *pariunt* per operationem perversam. Unde Augustinus de Verbis Domini[^p532-2]: « Quando concupiscit quis rem alienam, anima sua quasi concepisse videtur; quam rem si per aliquam nequitiam potuerit obtinere, quasi natum filium osculari et nutrire cognoscitur ». Unde Iacobi primo[^p532-3]: « Concupiscentia, cum conceperit, parit peccatum; peccatum, cum consummatum fuerit, generat mortem ». Et talibus erit *vae* in illa tribulatione; unde Sapientiae quarto: « Ex iniquis omnes filii qui nascuntur testes sunt nequitiae adversus parentes in interrogatione sua ». — Possunt autem per *praegnantes* intelligi animae, quae bonum habent propositum, sed non perveniunt usque ad partum effectus; unde Isaiae trigesimo septimo[^p532-4]: « Venerunt filii usque ad partum, et virtus non erat pariendi ». Et istis erit *vae* in illa tribulatione, quia, sicut flos vineae in frigore laeditur cito, sic propositum bonum in persecutione annihilatur; unde Iob decimo quinto[^p532-5]: « Laedetur quasi vinea in primo flore botrus eius », et hoc per mala exempla. Propter quod Amos primo: « Super tribus sceleribus filiorum Ammon et super quatuor non convertam eum, eo quod dissecuerit praegnantes Galaad ad dilatandum terminum suum ». Istis erit *vae* propter *negligentiam,* sicut aliis *vae* propter *concupiscentiam.* Et sic implebitur illud Isaiae vigesimo octavo[^p532-6]: « Erit eis verbum Domini: Manda, remanda, manda, remanda; exspecta, reexspecta, exspecta, reexspecta; modicum ibi, modicum ibi, ut vadant et cadant retrorsum et conterantur et illaqueentur et capiantur ».

37. (Vers. 24.). Secundo quantum ad *destructionem civitatis oppressae* subditur: *Et cadent in ore gladii et captivi ducentur in omnes gentes. Ad litteram* hoc impletum esse videmus in Iudaeis, de quibus miram narrat Iosephus[^p532-7] stragem, et mirabilem videmus viventium dispersionem; et sic verificatum est in eis illud Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo[^p532-8]: « Foris vastabit eos gladius et intus pavor, iuvenem simul ac virginem, lactantem cum homine sene »; et illud Threnorum primo: « Foris interficit gladius, et domi mors similis est »; et similiter illud Threnorum secundo: « Tetendit arcum suum quasi inimicus, firmavit dexteram suam quasi hostis et occidit omne, quod pulcrum erat visu in tabernaculo filiae Sion ».

38. Secundum *mysticum intellectum* per istam *occisionem* intelligitur caedes illa permaxima, quae erit in tribulatione finali, de qua Isaiae vigesimo secundo[^p532-9]: « Dies interfectionis et conculcationis et fletuum Domino Deo exercituum in valle visionis ». Et de hoc Danielis undecimo: « Et docti in populo docebunt plurimos; et ruent in gladio et in flamma et in captivitate et in rapina dierum ». « Et de eruditis ruent, ut conflentur et eligantur et dealbentur » etc. Unde dicitur Ezechielis nono[^p532-10]: « Transite per medium civitatis et percutite; non parcat oculus vester, neque misereamini senem, adolescentulum et virginem, parvulum et mulieres interficite usque ad internecionem. Et a sanctuario meo incipite » etc. Et ideo Iob decimo nono: « Fugite a facie gladii » etc.

39. Tertio quantum ad *desolationem civitatis destructae* subiungit: *Et Ierusalem calcabitur a gentibus;* Threnorum primo[^p532-11]: « Manum suam misit hostis ad omnia desiderabilia eius, quia vidit gentes ingressas sanctuarium suum, de quibus praeceperas, ne intrarent ecclesiam tuam »; et Threnorum secundo: « Praecipitavit Dominus nec pepercit ». « Praecipitavit omnia moenia eius, dissipavit munitiones eius et replevit in filia Iuda humiliatum et humiliatam »; et in Psalmo dicitur: « Deus, venerunt gentes in hereditatem tuam » etc. — Et quoniam non
<!-- page 533 -->
est ira Domini sine misericordia, ideo addit: *Donec impleantur tempora nationum;* Glossa[^p533-1]: « Id est, *donec plenitudo gentium intraverit, et sic omnis Israel salvus fieret, sicut scriptum est: Veniet ex Sion qui eripiat et avertat impietatem a Iacob* ». Et ideo dicitur Threnorum tertio[^p533-2]: « Quia non repellet in sempiternum Dominus; quia, si abiecit, et miserebitur secundum multitudinem misericordiarum suarum ».

40. Secundum *mysticum intellectum* per *tempora nationum* intelligitur tempus illud, quo filius perditionis Ecclesiam conculcabit; Apocalypsis undecimo[^p533-3]: « Atrium, quod est foris templum, ne metiaris, quoniam datum est gentibus », ad conculcandum, « et civitatem sanctam calcabunt mensibus quadraginta duobus ». Abbreviabitur autem hoc tempus propter meritum electorum; unde Matthaei vigesimo quarto[^p533-4]: « Erit tribulatio magna, qualis nunquam fuit ab initio mundi usque modo neque fiet. Et nisi abbreviati fuissent dies illi, non fieret salva omnis caro, sed propter electos breviabuntur ». Unde et Danielis nono: « Septuaginta hebdomades abbreviatae sunt super populum tuum et super urbem sanctam tuam, ut consummetur praevaricatio, et finem accipiat peccatum, et deleatur iniquitas, et adducatur iustitia sempiterna ».

## English
<!-- page 530 -->

28. *And when you shall see compassed about with an army* etc. After he has described the things that were to come generally, and also the persecutions of the Church itself; here[^p530-7] he describes *the things that are to come concerning the destruction of Jerusalem,* through which is understood the tribulation which the Church is to suffer in the time of antichrist. Now this part has two parts. In the first of which he foretells *the war going before;* but in the second, *the overthrow following,* there[^p530-8]: *But woe to them that are with child, and give suck* etc.

Now *the war* going before he describes as to three things, namely as to *the peril of the fight, the remedy of flight* and *the judgement of divine vengeance.*

29. (Vers. 20.). First, as to *the peril of the fight* he says: *And when you shall see, compassed about with an army, Jerusalem,* through the siege of the Romans, according to that of Ezechiel, the fourth chapter[^p530-9]: « Son of man, take thee a tile, and lay it before thee: and draw upon it the city of Jerusalem. And lay siege against it, and build forts, and cast up a mount, and set a camp against it, and place battering rams round about it ». — *Then know that there hath drawn nigh*[^p530-10] *the desolation thereof,* through an utter overturning; Daniel, the ninth chapter: « A people with their leader that shall come, shall destroy the city and the sanctuary: and the end thereof shall be waste, and after the end of the war the appointed desolation » etc.

30. *Spiritually,* however, by *the army compassing Jerusalem* is understood the insurrection of the perverse against the ecclesiastical peace, procured through antichrist and his ministers, according to that of the Apocalypse, the sixteenth chapter[^p530-11]: « And I saw from the mouth of the dragon, and from the mouth of the beast, and from the mouth of the false prophet, three unclean spirits go forth like frogs. For they are the spirits of devils working signs, and they go forth unto the kings of the whole earth, to gather them to battle against the great day of the Almighty God »; and the Apocalypse, the nineteenth chapter: « I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies gathered together to make war with him that sat upon the horse ». But since the wicked will be incomparably more, therefore they are said *to compass about.* In figure of which, the first of Kings, the twenty-third chapter[^p530-12]: « Saul and his men encompassed David and his men round about, to take them ». Whence Job, the nineteenth chapter: « His troops have come together, and have made themselves a way by me, and have besieged my tabernacle round about ».

31. (Vers. 21.). Second, as to *the remedy of flight* he subjoins: *Then let those who are in Judea flee to the*
<!-- page 531 -->
*mountains,* lest they be found; *and those who are in the midst thereof*[^p531-1] *depart out,* lest they be shut in; *and those who are in the countries not enter into it,* lest they be taken there. For the army of the Romans first overturned many cities throughout Judea, before it came to Jerusalem. And almost all the Jews had entered there, as Josephus says[^p531-2], on account of the solemnity and on account of security; therefore all were seized by the just judgement of God, according to that of Jeremias, the twelfth chapter: « Gather them together as sheep for a sacrifice, and prepare them for the day of slaughter ». But the Christians were set free through flight; whence the Gloss of Bede[^p531-3]: « The Ecclesiastical History relates that the Christians who were in Judea, the destruction being imminent, were warned by an Angel, and dwelt beyond the Jordan in the city of Pella until the desolation of Judea was fulfilled ».

32. *Spiritually,* however, there is intimated here the remedy which will be for the weak in that mighty persecution of antichrist, namely *of flight* and *of hiding.* For it is lawful for *the imperfect* to flee on account of dangers to be shunned; whence Zacharias, the second chapter[^p531-4]: « O, o, o! fly ye out of the land of the north, saith the Lord ». Condescending to whom, Christ hid himself from the Jews, according to that of John, the seventh chapter: « After these things Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him ». It is lawful also for *perfect* men, when they see that it is more expedient for the salvation of the people that they flee; whence the Lord said to the disciples, Matthew, the tenth chapter[^p531-5]: « And when they shall persecute you in one city, flee into another »; and of Paul it is said, the Acts, the ninth chapter, that when « the Jews watched the gates of Damascus day and night, that they might kill Paul, the disciples taking him, in the night conveyed him away by the wall, letting him down in a basket ». The figure of this went before in David; the first of Kings, the nineteenth chapter[^p531-6]: « But he went, and fled, and escaped »; for he was let down by Michol through a window.

33. (Vers. 22.). Third, as to *the judgement of divine vengeance* he subjoins: *For these are the days of vengeance, that all things may be fulfilled, that*[^p531-7] *are written.* Now the Lord decreed to destroy Jerusalem in vengeance for the blood of Jesus Christ and of his Prophets; whence Matthew, the twenty-third chapter[^p531-8]: « That upon you may come all the just blood that hath been shed upon the earth, from the blood of Abel the just, even unto the blood of Zacharias. Amen I say to you, all these things shall come upon this generation ». For so great a crime ought in no way to remain unavenged; therefore Lamentations, the second chapter: « The Lord hath done that which he purposed, he hath fulfilled his word, which he commanded in the days of old: he hath destroyed, and hath not spared, and he hath caused the enemy to rejoice over thee, and hath set up the horn of thy adversaries ».

34. *Spiritually,* however, by these things is understood the vengeance of the divine indignation on account of the sins of *ecclesiastical persons,* secular as well as religious, according to that of Micheas, the third chapter[^p531-9]: « Her princes have judged for bribes, and her priests have taught for hire, and her prophets divined for money ». « Therefore Sion shall be ploughed as a field » etc. And specially on account of the sins of *prelates;* Isaias, the first chapter[^p531-10]: « How is the faithful city, that was full of judgement, become a harlot! Justice dwelt in it, but now murderers »; « Thy princes are faithless, companions of thieves: they all love bribes, they run after rewards ». « Therefore saith the Lord the God of hosts: Ah! I will comfort myself over my adversaries: and I will be revenged of my enemies ». Therefore Isaias, the thirty-fourth chapter[^p531-11]: « The day of the vengeance of the Lord, the year of recompenses of the judgement of Sion »; and the second to the Thessalonians, the second chapter: « God shall send them the operation of error, to believe lying: that all may be judged who have not believed the truth, but have consented to iniquity ». And then in the last tribulation all things will be consummated, according to that of the Apocalypse, the tenth chapter[^p531-12]: « In the days of the voice of the seventh Angel, when he shall begin to sound the trumpet, the mystery of God shall be consummated, as he hath spoken by his servants the Prophets »; and the fifteenth chapter: « The temple of God was filled with smoke from the majesty of God, and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven Angels were consummated ».

35. (Vers. 23.). *But woe to them that are with child, and give suck* etc. After he has described the war going before, he here describes in the second place[^p531-13] *the overthrow following;* in describing which three things are intimated, namely *the oppression of the city to be seized, the destruction of the city oppressed* and *the desolation of the city destroyed.*

First, then, as to *the oppression of the city to be seized* he says: *But woe to them that are with child, and give suck, in those days!* The Gloss[^p531-14]: « Whose
<!-- page 532 -->
wombs or hands, weighed down by the burden of children, will hinder the necessity of flight »; because to such flight will not be able to profit, according to that of the first to the Thessalonians, the fifth chapter: « Sudden destruction shall come upon them, as the pains upon her that is with child, and they shall not escape ». — Now the reason of this is the vehement oppression of the city; therefore he adds: *For there shall be*[^p532-1] *great distress in the land, and wrath upon this people,* according to that of Isaias, the twenty-ninth chapter: « Woe to Ariel! Woe to Ariel! the city which David took. And I will make a trench about Ariel, and it shall be in sadness and sorrow. And I will make a circle round about thee, and will cast up a rampart against thee, and raise up bulwarks to besiege thee ».

36. Now according to *the mystical understanding,* by *them that are with child* are understood sinful souls, which *conceive* through the concupiscence of sin and *bring forth* through perverse operation. Whence Augustine on the Words of the Lord[^p532-2]: « When anyone covets another's thing, his soul seems as it were to have conceived; which thing if he shall have been able to obtain through some wickedness, he is known as it were to kiss and nourish a son born ». Whence James, the first chapter[^p532-3]: « Concupiscence, when it hath conceived, bringeth forth sin; sin, when it is completed, begetteth death ». And to such there will be *woe* in that tribulation; whence Wisdom, the fourth chapter: « All the children that are born of the wicked are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial ». — But by *them that are with child* can be understood souls which have a good purpose, but do not arrive at the bringing forth of the effect; whence Isaias, the thirty-seventh chapter[^p532-4]: « The children are come to the birth, and there was not strength to bring forth ». And to these there will be *woe* in that tribulation, because, as the flower of the vine is quickly blasted in the cold, so a good purpose is brought to nothing in persecution; whence Job, the fifteenth chapter[^p532-5]: « He shall be blasted as a vine when its grapes are in the first flower », and this through evil examples. On account of which Amos, the first chapter: « For three crimes of the children of Ammon, and for four I will not convert him: because he hath ripped up the women with child of Galaad to enlarge his border ». To these there will be *woe* on account of *negligence,* as to the others *woe* on account of *concupiscence.* And so that of Isaias, the twenty-eighth chapter[^p532-6], will be fulfilled: « The word of the Lord shall be to them: Command, command again; command, command again; expect, expect again; expect, expect again; a little there, a little there: that they may go, and fall backward, and be broken, and snared, and taken ».

37. (Vers. 24.). Second, as to *the destruction of the city oppressed* it is subjoined: *And they shall fall by the edge of the sword; and shall be led away captives into all nations. According to the letter* we see this fulfilled in the Jews, of whom Josephus[^p532-7] narrates a wonderful massacre, and we see a marvellous dispersion of the living; and so there was verified in them that of Deuteronomy, the thirty-second chapter[^p532-8]: « Without, the sword shall lay them waste, and terror within, both the young man and the virgin, the sucking child with the man in years »; and that of Lamentations, the first chapter: « Abroad the sword destroyeth, and at home there is death alike »; and likewise that of Lamentations, the second chapter: « He hath bent his bow as an enemy, he hath fixed his right hand as an adversary: and he hath killed all that was fair to behold in the tabernacle of the daughter of Sion ».

38. According to *the mystical understanding,* by this *slaughter* is understood that very great carnage which will be in the final tribulation, of which Isaias, the twenty-second chapter[^p532-9]: « A day of slaughter, and of treading down, and of weeping to the Lord the God of hosts in the valley of vision ». And of this Daniel, the eleventh chapter: « And they that are learned among the people shall teach many: and they shall fall by the sword, and by flame, and by captivity, and by spoil for many days ». « And some of the learned shall fall, to be tried, and to be chosen, and to be made white » etc. Whence it is said, Ezechiel, the ninth chapter[^p532-10]: « Pass through the midst of the city, and strike: let not your eyes spare, nor be ye moved with pity: utterly destroy old and young, maidens, children and women. And begin ye at my sanctuary » etc. And therefore Job, the nineteenth chapter: « Flee from the face of the sword » etc.

39. Third, as to *the desolation of the city destroyed* he subjoins: *And Jerusalem shall be trodden down by the Gentiles;* Lamentations, the first chapter[^p532-11]: « The enemy hath put out his hand to all her desirable things: for she hath seen the Gentiles enter into her sanctuary, of whom thou gavest commandment that they should not enter into thy church »; and Lamentations, the second chapter: « The Lord hath cast down headlong, and hath not spared ». « He hath cast down headlong all the walls thereof, he hath destroyed the strong holds thereof, and hath multiplied in the daughter of Juda the afflicted, both men and women »; and in the Psalm it is said: « O God, the heathens are come into thy inheritance » etc. — And since the wrath of the Lord is not
<!-- page 533 -->
without mercy, therefore he adds: *Till the times of the nations be fulfilled;* the Gloss[^p533-1]: « That is, *until the fulness of the Gentiles should come in, and so all Israel should be saved, as it is written: There shall come out of Sion, he that shall deliver, and shall turn away impiety from Jacob* ». And therefore it is said, Lamentations, the third chapter[^p533-2]: « For the Lord will not cast off for ever. For if he hath cast off, he will also have mercy, according to the multitude of his mercies ».

40. According to *the mystical understanding,* by *the times of the nations* is understood that time in which the son of perdition will tread under foot the Church; the Apocalypse, the eleventh chapter[^p533-3]: « The court, which is without the temple, measure it not: because it is given unto the Gentiles », to tread under foot, « and the holy city they shall tread down two and forty months ». Now this time will be shortened on account of the merit of the elect; whence Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p533-4]: « There shall be great tribulation, such as hath not been from the beginning of the world until now, neither shall be. And unless those days had been shortened, no flesh should be saved: but for the sake of the elect they shall be shortened ». Whence also Daniel, the ninth chapter: « Seventy weeks are shortened upon thy people, and upon thy holy city, that transgression may be finished, and sin may have an end, and iniquity may be abolished, and everlasting justice may be brought ».
