# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. X, Vers. 4-16
## *Nolite portare etc. Post missionem discipulorum comitantium subdit informationem euntium*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 254 -->

7. *Nolite portare* etc. Post missionem discipulorum comitantium subdit *informationem euntium.* Habet autem pars ista duas, in quarum prima praemittitur *communis informatio* respectu omnium; secundo, *specialis* respectu personarum specialium, ibi[^p254-11]: *Et in quamcumque civitatem intraveritis.*

*Generali* autem *informatione* invitat nos ad quatuor, scilicet ad *amplexandam mendicitatem,* ad
<!-- page 255 -->
*declinandam loquacitatem,* ad *exhibendam humanitatem* et *ostendendam maturitatem.*

8. (Vers. 4.). Primo igitur quantum ad *mendicitatem amplectendam* dicit: *Nolite portare sacculum*[^p255-1], scilicet ad repositionem pecuniae. Accipitur enim *sacculus* pro bursa pecuniaria, secundum illud Proverbiorum septimo: « Sacculum pecuniae secum tulit ». *Neque peram,* ad reservandos panes, secundum illud Matthaei sexto[^p255-2]: « Nolite solliciti esse in crastinum, dicentes: Quid manducabimus » etc. *Neque calceamenta,* scilicet ad cooperiendos pedes; nam Marci sexto dicitur: « Sed tantum calceatos sandaliis ». *Sandalia* enim a laesione pedem protegunt, sed non tegunt, sicut sunt soleae fratrum. Hoc autem voluit Dominus discipulis iniungere, ut non solum *essent* pauperes, verum etiam *apparerent* et ad paupertatem magis exemplis quam verbis vocarent. « Est autem paupertas, ut dicit Seneca[^p255-3], odibile bonum »; et ideo, cum appareat turpis et vilis, iunxit ei Dominus maximam *dotem,* ut per illam saltem in coniugium possit assumi, cum dixit Matthaei quinto: « Beati pauperes spiritu, quoniam ipsorum est regnum caelorum ».

9. Secundo quantum ad *loquacitatem declinandam* subdit: *Et neminem per viam salutaveritis;* super quo loco dicit Glossa[^p255-4], quod hoc dicit, « ne alicuius obvii confabulatione deflecterentur ab iniuncto officio ». Mittebat enim eos festinato, sicut Eliseus puerum suum, quarti Regum quarto: « Si occurrerit tibi vir, non salutes eum; et si salutaverit te quispiam, non respondeas illi ». Hoc autem iniunxit Dominus non ad declinandum affabilitatis indicium, cum ipse fuerit benignissimus et curialissimus; sed ad declinandum multiloquium, de quo dicitur Proverbiorum decimo[^p255-5]: « In multiloquio non deerit peccatum »; et ideo Ecclesiastici decimo nono: « Qui odit loquacitatem exstinguit malitiam ». Hoc autem
multum consuevit esse in viatoribus et frequenter est occasio contentionum; unde dicitur Marci nono de discipulis[^p255-6]: « Siquidem disputaverant in via, quis eorum esset maior ». Propter quod Genesis quadragesimo quinto dicitur, quod « Ioseph fratribus proficiscentibus ait: Ne irascamini in via ».

10. Secundum autem *spiritualem intelligentiam* prohibet optare salutem, quae est *viae,* non salutem, quae est *patriae;* quod notatur, cum dicit: *Per viam.* Omnibus enim salus est optanda, sicut dicitur primae ad Timotheum secundo[^p255-7], quod « vult Deus, omnes homines salvos fieri », et hoc, quia ipse est *salus* et salvator. Sed illa *salus* debet optari, de qua in Psalmo[^p255-8]: « Salus autem iustorum a Domino ». Hanc semper optabant Apostoli, et de hac dicitur Ecclesiastici vigesimo secundo: « Amicum salutare non confundaris »; et ad Romanos ultimo: « Salutate invicem in osculo sancto »; et in Psalmo: « Tu es ipse rex meus et Deus meus, qui mandas salutes Iacob ». — Aliter potest exponi, ut dicatur: *Neminem per viam salutaveritis,* id est, iam salvum dicatis, cum adhuc damnari possit, quamdiu est in via; nam Matthaei decimo[^p255-9]: « Qui perseveraverit usque in finem, hic salvus erit ». Ergo *salus* in fine viae, non in medio aut in capite. — Vel aliter: *Neminem per viam salutaveritis,* id est propter consortium *viae,* sed propter consortium *vitae.* Ex quo apparet, quod non est salus hominibus propter Sanctorum collocutionem, sed propter ipsorum imitationem; unde Matthaei septimo[^p255-10]: « Non omnis, qui dicit mihi: Domine, Domine, intrabit in regnum caelorum, sed qui facit voluntatem Patris mei, qui in caelis est ».

11. (Vers. 5.). Tertio quantum ad *humanitatem exhibendam* adiungit: *Et*[^p255-11] *in quamcumque domum intraveritis, primum dicite: Pax huic domui;* ut appareat, vos esse viros pacis amatores et annuntiatores, secundum illud Isaiae quinquagesimo
<!-- page 256 -->
secundo: « Quam pulcri super montes pedes annuntiantis et praedicantis pacem »! Hanc pacem Dominus *fecit,* secundum illud ad Colossenses primo[^p256-1]: « Pacificans per sanguinem crucis eius » etc.; Dominus etiam *reliquit;* Ioannis decimo quarto: « Pacem relinquo vobis, pacem meam do vobis »; Dominus *annuntiavit;* Ioannis vigesimo: « Stetit Iesus in medio et dixit eis: Pax vobis »; Dominus *mandavit* et pacem *evangelizavit,* sicut dicitur ad Ephesios secundo: « Veniens evangelizavit pacem vobis, qui longe fuistis, et pacem iis qui prope ».

12. (Vers. 6.). Et quia possent timere, ne frustra pacem offerrent; ideo subdit: *Et si fuerit ibi filius pacis; pacis* secundum aeternam praescientiam, de quibus Ioannis undecimo[^p256-2] dicitur, quod « Iesus passus est, ut filios Dei, qui erant dispersi, congregaret »; de quibus dicitur secundae ad Timotheum secundo: « Cognovit Dominus qui sunt eius ». Tales sunt filii pacis, quia dicitur Matthaei quinto: « Beati pacifici, quoniam filii Dei vocabuntur ». — In tali habet efficaciam verbum praedicationis; propter quod addit: *Requiescet super illum pax vestra,* id est a vobis annuntiata; unde Isaiae sexagesimo sexto[^p256-3] secundum aliam translationem: « Super quem requiescet spiritus meus nisi super humilem et quietum »? Requiescit utique pax Christi annuntiata super praedestinatos a Deo, secundum illud Actuum decimo tertio: « Crediderunt quotquot erant praeordinati ad vitam »; et Ioannis decimo: « Vos non creditis, quia non estis ex ovibus meis. Oves meae vocem meam audiunt ». — Utile est igitur loqui *praedestinatis* Evangelium pacis; utile est etiam loqui *praescitis;* propterea subdit: *Sin autem, revertetur ad vos,* secundum illud Psalmi[^p256-4]: « Oratio mea in sinu meo convertetur »; et hoc, quia opus pietatis et misericordiae semper est utile facienti et ad facientem revertitur, secundum illud Ecclesiastici decimo septimo: « Eleemosyna viri quasi sacculus cum ipso et gratiam hominis quasi pupillam conservabit; et postea resurget et retribuet retributionem unicuique in caput suum ».

13. (Vers. 7.). Quarto vero quantum ad *maturitatem ostendendam* subdit: *In eadem autem domo
manete,* tanquam viri maturi, ne forte notemini de instabilitate; unde Ecclesiastici vigesimo primo[^p256-5]: « Pes fatui facilis est in domum proximi ». Ideo dicit Beda: « Alienum est a praedicatore per domos cursitare et hospitium mutare ». — Et quia possent credere, hospitium esse mutandum propter cibum; ideo subdit: *Edentes et bibentes quae apud illos sunt,* sine ciborum *discretione,* sicut primae ad Corinthios decimo[^p256-6]: « Omne, quod apponitur vobis, manducate, nihil interrogantes propter conscientiam »; sine ciborum etiam *abiectione,* secundum illud primae ad Timotheum quarto: « Omnis creatura Dei bona est, et nihil reiiciendum, quod cum gratiarum actione percipitur; sanctificatur enim per verbum Dei et orationem »; sine ciborum etiam *requisitione,* secundum illud Ecclesiastici trigesimo primo: « In medio multorum sedisti, prior illis ne extendas manum tuam nec prior poscas bibere ». — Et quia possent credere, quod non liceret pasci de aliena substantia; ideo addit: *Dignus est enim operarius mercede sua,* non solum in patria ad remunerationem, sed etiam in via ad sustentationem. Unde primae ad Corinthios nono[^p256-7]: « Dominus ordinavit his qui Evangelium annuntiant, de Evangelio vivere »; et ad Galatas sexto: « Communicet autem is qui catechizatur verbo, ei qui se catechizat in omnibus bonis »; unde primae ad Corinthios nono dicitur: « Si nos vobis spiritualia seminavimus, non est magnum, si carnalia vestra metamus ». Et hoc probat ibi[^p256-8] Apostolus, quod talis operarius sit dignus, et per auctoritates et per rationes quamplures, sicut ibi patet. Quoniam ergo dignus est refectione, propter illam non oportet, eum hospitium mutare. — Ideo in fine addit: *Nolite transire de domo in domum;* ne videamini vagi, ne sitis similes illi mulieri vagae, de qua dicitur Proverbiorum septimo[^p256-9], quod « est garrula, vaga, quietis impatiens, non valens in domo consistere pedibus suis ». Tales sunt haeretici; unde secundae ad Timotheum tertio: « Ex his sunt, qui penetrant domos et captivas ducunt mulierculas oneratas peccatis ». Non tales debent esse praedicatores veritatis; unde Ambrosius[^p256-10]: « Non est de domo in domum vaga facilitate
<!-- page 257 -->
demigrandum, ut in hospitali amore servetur constantia, ne coalita amicitiae necessitudo facile dissolvatur ».

14. *Et in quamcumque civitatem intraveritis.* Post informationem discipulorum communem subiungit hic Evangelista *informationem specialem* secundum audientium diversitates[^p257-1]. Et quoniam quidam parati fuerunt ad eos *recipiendum,* ut fideles, quidam autem ad *repellendum,* ut infideles, quorum culpa crescebat ex contemptu divinae gratiae et despectu auctoritatis apostolicae; ideo pars ista habet quatuor. In quarum prima praemittitur *informatio respectu fidelium;* in secunda vero, *respectu rebellium,* ibi[^p257-2]: *In quamcumque civitatem intraveritis, et non receperint;* in tertia subditur *increpatio contemptorum,* ibi: *Vae tibi, Corozain* etc.; in quarta vero subiungitur *authenticatio praedicatorum,* ibi: *Qui vos audit* etc.

Circa informationem respectu fidelium duo introducuntur; quorum primum est *susceptio stipendii temporalis,* secundum est *communicatio beneficii spiritualis.*

15. (Vers. 8.). Primo igitur quantum ad *susceptionem temporalis stipendii* informat, cum dicit: *Et in quamcumque civitatem intraveritis,* scilicet ad praedicandum veritatem, sicut dicitur Ionae tertio[^p257-3], quod « coepit Ionas introire civitatem itinere diei unius et clamavit ». Sic et isti per divini verbi clamorem debebant in suo ingressu civitatem excitare, iuxta quod dicitur de Philippo discipulo Actuum octavo, quod « descendens in civitatem Samariae, praedicabat illis Iesum. Intendebant autem turbae his quae a Philippo dicebantur ». — Et quoniam fidelium est divinis verbis intendere, ideo subdit: *Et susceperint vos,* scilicet per fidem et dilectionem tanquam nuntios Christi; quoniam, Matthaei decimo octavo[^p257-4], « qui susceperit unum parvulum talem in nomine meo, ille me suscipit ». Ille autem suscipit praedicatorem, qui eius doctrinam acceptat cum mansuetudine et patientia, iuxta quod dicitur Iacobi primo: « In mansuetudine suscipite insitum verbum,
quod potest salvare animas vestras ». — Et quoniam a suscipientibus spiritualia documenta suscipi possunt corporalia alimenta, ideo subdit: *Manducate quae apponuntur vobis,* scilicet ad relevandam necessitatem, ut fortius laboretis, secundum illud Psalmi[^p257-5]: « Labores manuum tuarum quia manducabis, beatus es, et bene tibi erit ». Unde super illud primae ad Corinthios nono: « Dispensatio mihi credita est », Glossa: « Non debemus ideo evangelizare, ut manducemus; sed ideo manducare, ut evangelizemus, ut cibus non sit bonum, quod appetitur, sed necessarium, quod adiicitur ».

16. Et nota, quod dicit: *Quae apponuntur,* et hoc cum hilaritate; quia, Proverbiorum decimo quinto[^p257-6], « melius est vocari ad olera cum caritate quam ad vitulum saginatum cum odio ». Manducate etiam *quae apponuntur,* non quae vos ipsi paretis; quod dicit propter vitandam gulositatem, de qua Seneca[^p257-7]: « In ventrem pretiosa sic congerunt, quasi congesta servaret; quid ad rem, quid recipiat, perditurus omne, quod recipit »? Et ideo dicitur Ecclesiastici secundo[^p257-8]: « Omne, quod tibi applicitum fuerit, accipe ». Hoc autem maxime spectat ad pauperes, ut vili sint contenti refectione tanquam esurientes; quia dicitur Proverbiorum vigesimo septimo: « Anima esuriens etiam amarum pro dulci sumet »; et Iob sexto: « Quae prius nolebat tangere anima mea nunc prae angustia cibi mei sunt ».

17. (Vers. 9.). Secundo vero quantum ad *communicationem spiritualis beneficii* subditur: *Et curate infirmos, qui in illa sunt;* et hoc per virtutem vobis a Spiritu sancto collatam, de qua primae ad Corinthios duodecimo[^p257-9]: « Alii datur gratia sanitatum in uno spiritu, alii operatio virtutum ». Hanc dedit Dominus discipulis minoribus ad confirmationem doctrinae suae; unde Hieronymus: « Quia hominibus rusticanis et absque eloquii venustate nemo crederet pollicentibus regnum caelorum, dat potestatem miracula faciendi ». Huius potestatis donum prius concesserat, sicut et Apostolis supra nono[^p257-10]: « Dedit eis potestatem super omnia daemonia, et ut languores
<!-- page 258 -->
curarent »; sed hic imperat, ut dono accepto utantur, iuxta quod dicitur primae Petri quarto: « Unusquisque sicut accepit gratiam, in alterutrum illam administrantes » etc. — Et quia curatio corporalis ordinata erat ad illuminationem mentis, ideo subdit: *Et dicite illis: Appropinquabit*[^p258-1] *in vos regnum Dei;* in quo simul clauditur *veritas doctrinae, bonitas gratiae* et *sublimitas gloriae,* secundum quod sic expositum fuit[^p258-2], et *interlinearis* dicit: « Regnum Dei Christus, vel vita aeterna, vel scientia Scripturarum ». Et ita *appropinquavit,* quando inter eos erat, iuxta quod dicitur infra decimo septimo[^p258-3]: « Regnum Dei intra vos est ». Vere appropinquavit, cum ille iam esset praesens, de quo dicitur Apocalypsis decimo nono, quod « habebat in vestimento et in femore suo scriptum: Rex regum et Dominus dominantium ». Et signanter dicit: *In vos,* id est, in eos qui verbum Dei suscipiunt per fidem, de quibus Apocalypsis quinto[^p258-4]: « Fecisti nos Deo nostro regnum, et regnabimus super terram »; et per gratiam fidei, secundum illud ad Colossenses primo, « eripuit nos de potestate tenebrarum et transtulit in regnum Filii caritatis suae ». — Sic igitur patet, qualiter se debebant habere ad fideles, quia debebant recipere stipendium sustentationis et ministrare beneficium curationis et eruditionis. Qui ergo non ministrant haec beneficia spiritualia minus condigna suscipiunt stipendia corporalia. Unde Gregorius[^p258-5]: « Quid ad haec doctores dicimus, qui, Christi adventum praecurrentes, ecclesiasticum officium suscipimus et muti panem comedimus »? Et Bernardus[^p258-6]: « Venient ante tribunal iudicis, ubi erit pauperum allegatio dura, accusatio gravis, quorum vixere stipendiis nec diluere peccata ».

18. (Vers. 10.). *In quamcumque*[^p258-7] *civitatem intraveritis.* Post informationem respectu fidelium subditur *informatio respectu rebellium,* circa quam duo introducuntur: primum est *contestatio veritatis Evangelii,* secundum est *comminatio severitatis iudicii.*

Primo igitur quantum ad *contestationem veritatis Evangelii* dicitur: *In quamcumque civitatem
*intraveritis,* ad veritatis praeconium, secundum illud Actuum decimo septimo[^p258-8]: « Cum venissent Thessalonicam, ubi erat synagoga Iudaeorum, introivit ad eos Paulus et disserebat eis de Scripturis ». — *Et non receperint*[^p258-9] *vos,* per veritatis auditum, qui est praedicatoribus accommodandus, sicut dicitur tertia Canonica Ioannis: « Nos debemus suscipere huiusmodi, ut cooperatores simus veritatis. Scripsissem forsitan Ecclesiae, sed is qui amat primatum gerere in eis, Diotrephes, non recipit nos ». — *Exeuntes in platea eius,* ad veritatis testimonium, propter quod dicitur Matthaei decimo[^p258-10]: « Quod dico vobis in tenebris dicite in lumine, et quod in aure auditis praedicate super tecta ».

19. (Vers. 11.). *Dicite*[^p258-11]*: Etiam pulverem pedum, qui adhaesit nobis de civitate vestra, extergimus in vos,* propter veritatis contemptum, quod est peccatum valde detestandum, secundum illud Ioannis decimo quinto: « Si non venissem et locutus fuissem eis, peccatum non haberent ». Et ideo ad huius delicti detestationem dicit *pulverem abstergendum,* sicut Petrus Simoni Actuum octavo: « Pecunia tua tecum sit in perditionem ». — *Tamen hoc scitote, quia appropinquabit*[^p258-12] *regnum Dei,* id est veritas Evangelii, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo quarto: « Praedicabitur hoc Evangelium regni in universo orbe in testimonium omnibus gentibus », ut nullus possit se per ignorantiam excusare. Et propterea, ut hoc publice fiat, iubet *in plateis extergere pulverem,* super quo dicit Hieronymus[^p258-13], quod hoc iubet « in testimonium, quod praedicatio eorum pervenerit usque ad illam civitatem ». Et ita non habent excusationem de infidelitate.

20. Secundum *spiritualem* autem *intelligentiam pedes Apostolorum* sunt affectus praedicatorum, quibus triplex pulvis consuevit adhaerere, scilicet *inanis gloriae,* de quo in Psalmo[^p258-14]: « Gloriam meam in pulverem deducat »; *indignationis et impatientiae,* de quo Michaeae septimo: « Lingent pulverem sicut serpens »; *cupiditatis et avaritiae,* secundum illud Ecclesiastici vigesimo septimo: « In percussura
<!-- page 259 -->
cribri remanebit pulvis ». — Primus pulvis adhaeret pedibus praedicantium, cum *laudantur;* sed excutitur per *considerationem sui,* secundum illud Matthaei decimo[^p259-1]: « Vos non estis, qui loquimini »; et primae ad Corinthios duodecimo: « Nemo dicit: Dominus Iesus, nisi in Spiritu sancto »; et primae ad Corinthios quarto: « Quid habes, quod non accepisti »? Ideo Ecclesiastici decimo: « Quid superbis, terra et cinis »? — Secundus pulvis adhaeret, cum *non acceptantur;* sed hic excutitur per *rememorationem Christi,* secundum illud Ioannis decimo quinto[^p259-2]: « Mementote sermonis mei, quem ego locutus sum vobis: Non est servus maior domino suo. Si me persecuti sunt, et vos persequentur » etc. — Tertius pulvis adhaeret, quando *munera offeruntur,* quae excaecant oculos ducum, iuxta illud Baruch sexto[^p259-3]: « Oculi eorum pleni sunt pulvere a pedibus introeuntium »; sed hic excutitur per *memoriam mortis nostrae.* Hieronymus[^p259-4]: « Facile contemnit omnia qui se semper cogitat moriturum »; et in primae ad Timotheum ultimo: « Nihil intulimus in hunc mundum; haud dubium, quod nec auferre quid possumus ».

21. (Vers. 12.). Secundo vero quantum ad *comminationem severitatis iudicii* subdit: *Dico vobis, quia Sodomis in die illa remissius erit quam illi civitati;* ex quo apparet magnitudo *poenae.* Nam dicitur in Canonica Iudae[^p259-5], quod « Sodoma et Gomorrha et finitimae civitates factae sunt exemplum, ignis aeterni poenam sustinentes ». Unde et Dominus fuit illis fortissime indignatus, secundum quod dicitur Genesis decimo nono, quod « pluit Dominus super Sodomam et Gomorrham sulphur et ignem et subvertit civitates has et omnem circa regionem ». Apparet etiam ex hoc magnitudo *culpae,* quia maior culpa est contemptio[^p259-6] veritatis quam libido carnalitatis. — Ratio autem huius est, quia non fuit eis *oblata tanta gratia;* et secundum quod dicitur infra duodecimo[^p259-7], « cui multum datum est, multum quaeretur ab eo ». Unde ad Hebraeos secundo: « Si enim qui per Angelos dictus est sermo factus est firmus; quomodo nos effugiemus, si tantam neglexerimus salutem, quae, cum initium accepisset enar-
rari per Dominum, ab eis qui audierunt, in nos confirmata est »? — Alia ratio est propter *maiorem veritatis notitiam;* unde Gregorius[^p259-8]: « Ubi maius est donum scientiae, ibi transgressor maiori subiacet culpae »; quia dicitur Iacobi quarto: « Scienti bonum facere et non facienti peccatum est illi ». — Et quod contemptoribus veritatis maxima debeatur poena, patet ex hoc quod dicitur ad Romanos primo[^p259-9] de quibusdam, qui, « cum Deum cognovissent, non sicut Deum glorificaverunt », quod « tradidit illos Deus in reprobum sensum ». Et ratio huius redditur, quia « non probaverunt Deum habere in notitia ».

22. (Vers. 13.). *Vae tibi, Corozain* etc. Hic tertio loco subiungitur *increpatio contemptorum,* qui propter duo a Domino increpandi ostenduntur et tanquam damnabiles arguuntur, scilicet propter *duritiam cordis* et propter *excellentiam mentis:* duritia cordis reddebat eos *impoenitentes,* sed excellentia *arrogantes.*

Primo igitur, *increpans cordis duritiam,* dicit: *Vae tibi, Corozain! vae tibi, Bethsaida!* quae erant duae civitates Galilaeae, ubi plurima fecerat signa, et tamen adhuc permanebat in eis duritia[^p259-10]. — Propter quod subdit: *Quia, si in Tyro et Sidone factae fuissent virtutes, quae in vobis factae sunt,* id est, si Dominus eis tanta miracula ostendisset ante suam subversionem — quam comminatus est Dominus per Ezechielem vigesimo septimo[^p259-11]: « Tu fili hominis, assume super Tyrum lamentum », et in fine capituli: « Ad nihilum redacta es et non eris usque in sempiternum » — adhuc non essent subversae, et hoc, quia poenituissent. — Unde addit: *Olim in cilicio et cinere sedentes poeniterent,* sicut fecerunt Ninivitae, de quibus Ionae tertio[^p259-12], quod « praedicaverunt ieiunium et vestiti sunt saccis a minore usque ad maiorem ».

23. Et nota, quod tria dicit, in quibus exprimit poenitentiae perfectionem, scilicet *cilicium, cinerem* et *sessionem.* In poenitente enim debet esse *dolor* ex consideratione divinae offensae, et hoc in *cilicio; timor,* ex consideratione vindictae, et hoc in
<!-- page 260 -->
*cinere,* qui praefert mortis imaginem; debet esse *pudor* in consideratione sequelae sive vilitatis suae, et hoc in *sessione.* Ideo dicit Hieronymus[^p260-1], quod « cinis et cilicium arma sunt poenitentium ». Et propterea Ieremiae sexto: « Filia populi mei, accingere cilicio et aspergere cinere, luctum unigeniti fac tibi, planctum amarum ». Et propter pudorem humilitatis, qui debet comitari, dicitur Ieremiae decimo tertio[^p260-2]: « Dic regi et dominatrici: Humiliamini, sedete, quoniam descendet de capite vestro corona gloriae vestrae ». Si igitur talem fecissent poenitentiam, non haberent tantam cordis duritiam, ac per hoc nec tam duram sententiam.

24. (Vers. 14.). Et propterea subiungit: *Verumtamen Tyro et Sidoni remissius erit in iudicio quam vobis;* quia, secundum quod dicitur infra duodecimo[^p260-3], « servus sciens voluntatem domini et non faciens vapulavit multis ». Ratio autem huius est nimia duritia contra tanta beneficia; unde ad Romanos secundo dicitur: « An ignoras, quoniam Dei benignitas ad poenitentiam te adducit? Tu autem secundum duritiam tuam et impoenitens cor thesaurizas tibi iram in die irae et revelationis iusti iudicii Dei »; quia Ecclesiastici tertio dicitur: « Cor durum male habebit in novissimo »; « et peccator adiiciet ad peccandum ».

25. (Vers. 15.). Secundo vero *increpans quoad excellentiam mentis* subdit: *Et tu, Capharnaum, usque in caelum exaltata,* scilicet per arrogantiam, *usque in infernum*[^p260-4] *demergeris,* per divinam sententiam. Unde Abdiae primo: « Si exaltatus fueris ut aquila et inter sidera posueris nidum tuum, inde detraham te »; et Iob vigesimo: « Si ascenderit usque in caelum superbia eius, et caput eius nubes tetigerit; quasi sterquilinium in fine perdetur ». Dicitur autem Capharnaum *exaltata* propter plurima mira-
cula, quae facta sunt in ea, de quibus superbivit amplius, quam profecit; unde supra quarto[^p260-5] dicitur: « Quanta audivimus a te facta in Capharnaum » etc. Primo ergo exaltata est propter munera divinae gratiae, sed post demersa merito superbiae. Et hoc praedictum fuerat Isaiae nono[^p260-6]: « Primo tempore alleviata est terra Zabulon et terra Nephthalim », scilicet per praecedentia miracula, « et novissimo aggravata est via maris », id est depressa in iudicio propter sua peccata, sicut exponit ibidem Glossa. Et ideo divina dona suscipienda sunt cum timore et magna reverentia, sicut dicit Gregorius[^p260-7], quod « cum augentur dona, rationes etiam crescunt donorum ».

26. (Vers. 16.). *Qui vos audit* etc. Hic quarto loco subditur authenticatio[^p260-8] praedicatorum. Ostendit autem eos authenticos tum propter auctoritatem *Christi mediatoris,* tum etiam propter auctoritatem *summi imperatoris.*

Primo igitur authenticat eos per *auctoritatem Christi mediatoris,* cum dicit: *Qui vos audit me audit.* Nam ipsi gerebant Christi personam; unde Apostolus secundae ad Corinthios secundo[^p260-9]: « Nam et ego, si quid donavi, propter vos in persona Christi »; et decimo tertio: « An experimentum eius quaeritis, qui in me loquitur Christus »? Et ideo dicitur primae ad Thessalonicenses secundo: « Et vos, cum accepissetis a nobis verbum auditus Dei, accepistis illud non ut verbum hominum, sed, sicut est vere, verbum Dei ». In auditu ergo discipulorum Christi Christus auditur, et similiter in contemptu contemnitur. — Et propterea subdit: *Et qui vos spernit me spernit;* unde Ezechielis tertio[^p260-10]: « Domus Israel nolunt audire te, quia nolunt audire me ». Et hoc innuit Matthaei vigesimo quinto, cum dicit: « Quamdiu uni ex minimis meis non fecistis, nec
<!-- page 261 -->
mihi fecistis ». Et propterea dicebat Apostolus primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto: « Spiritum nolite exstinguere, prophetias nolite spernere », id est veras praedicationes.

27. Secundo authenticat eos propter *auctoritatem summi imperatoris,* cum subdit: *Qui*[^p261-1] *me spernit spernit eum qui me misit,* tum propter *commissam auctoritatem,* tum propter *consubstantialitatem.* Unde Ioannis quinto: « Qui non honorificat Filium non honorificat Patrem, qui misit illum ». Et ideo sequitur, quod si quis spernit Apostolos, spernit Christum; et qui Christum spernit spernit Deum: ergo qui spernit Apostolos spernit Deum; et hoc non est parvum peccatum. Propter quod Isaiae trigesimo tertio[^p261-2]: « Vae! qui spernis; nonne et ipse sperneris? Cum fatigatus desieris contemnere, contemneris ». Magna igitur est auctoritas praedicatorum, in quorum receptione Deus recipitur et in contemptu contemnitur. Ipsi enim sunt os Dei verba eius annuntiando, sicut dicitur Ieremiae decimo quinto[^p261-3]: « Si separaveris pretiosum a vili, quasi os meum eris ». Et ideo tanquam os Dei pasci debent et honorari; et ipsi etiam os praecipue custodire et ab edacitate et a loquacitate, ut vere possit dici de eis illud Apocalypsis decimo quarto: « Hi empti sunt ex hominibus, primitiae Deo et Agno, et in ore ipsorum non est inventum mendacium; sine macula enim sunt ante thronum Dei ».

7. *Do not carry,* etc. After the mission of the accompanying disciples he adds *the formation of those going.* Now this part has two, in the first of which is set forth *the common formation* with respect to all; in the second, *the special* with respect to special persons, there[^p254-11]: *And into what city soever you enter.*

By the *general formation,* then, he invites us to four things, namely to *embracing beggary,* to
<!-- page 255 -->
*shunning talkativeness,* to *showing humanity* and *displaying maturity.*

8. (Vers. 4.). First, then, as to *embracing beggary,* he says: *Carry not a purse*[^p255-1], namely for the laying up of money. For *sacculus* is taken for a money-purse, according to that of Proverbs seven: « He took with him a bag of money ». *Nor scrip,* for keeping back loaves, according to that of Matthew six[^p255-2]: « Be not solicitous for tomorrow, saying: What shall we eat », etc. *Nor shoes,* namely for covering the feet; for in Mark six it is said: « But to be shod with sandals ». For *sandals* protect the foot from hurt, but do not cover it, as are the soles of the friars. Now this the Lord willed to enjoin on the disciples, that they should not only *be* poor, but should also *appear* so, and should call to poverty by examples rather than by words. « Now poverty, as Seneca says[^p255-3], is a hateful good »; and therefore, since it appears base and vile, the Lord joined to it a very great *dowry,* so that by it at least it might be taken in marriage, when he said in Matthew five: « Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven ».

9. Secondly, as to *shunning talkativeness,* he adds: *And salute no man by the way;* on which place the Gloss says[^p255-4] that he says this « lest they be turned aside from the enjoined office by chatter with someone met on the way ». For he was sending them in haste, as Eliseus sent his servant, in the fourth of Kings, four: « If any man meet thee, salute him not; and if any man salute thee, answer him not ». Now the Lord enjoined this not for shunning a token of affability, since he himself was most kind and most courteous; but for shunning much speaking, of which it is said in Proverbs ten[^p255-5]: « In the multitude of words there shall not want sin »; and therefore in Ecclesiasticus nineteen: « He that hateth babbling extinguisheth evil ». Now this
is wont to be much among wayfarers and is frequently an occasion of contentions; whence it is said in Mark nine of the disciples[^p255-6]: « For they had disputed in the way, which of them should be the greatest ». On account of which it is said in Genesis forty-five that « Joseph said to his brethren as they set out: Be not angry in the way ».

10. But according to the *spiritual intelligence* he forbids wishing the salvation which is *of the way,* not the salvation which is *of the fatherland;* which is noted when he says: *By the way.* For salvation is to be wished to all, as is said in the first to Timothy, two[^p255-7], that « God will have all men to be saved », and this because he himself is *salvation* and saviour. But that *salvation* ought to be wished of which it is said in the Psalm[^p255-8]: « But the salvation of the just is from the Lord ». This the Apostles always wished, and of this it is said in Ecclesiasticus twenty-two: « Be not ashamed to salute a friend »; and to the Romans, the last: « Salute one another with a holy kiss »; and in the Psalm: « Thou art thyself my king and my God, who commandest the saving of Jacob ». — It can be expounded otherwise, so that it is said: *Salute no man by the way,* that is, call him already saved, since he can still be damned as long as he is on the way; for in Matthew ten[^p255-9]: « He that shall persevere unto the end, he shall be saved ». Therefore *salvation* is at the end of the way, not in the middle or at the head. — Or otherwise: *Salute no man by the way,* that is, not on account of the fellowship *of the way,* but on account of the fellowship *of life.* From which it appears that salvation is not to men on account of conversation with the Saints, but on account of the imitation of them; whence in Matthew seven[^p255-10]: « Not every one that saith to me: Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven, but he that doth the will of my Father who is in heaven ».

11. (Vers. 5.). Thirdly, as to *showing humanity,* he adjoins: *And*[^p255-11] *into whatsoever house you enter, first say: Peace be to this house;* so that it may appear that you are men who are lovers and announcers of peace, according to that of Isaias fifty-
<!-- page 256 -->
two: « How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that announceth and preacheth peace »! This peace the Lord *made,* according to that to the Colossians, one[^p256-1]: « Making peace through the blood of his cross », etc.; the Lord also *left* it; John fourteen: « Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you »; the Lord *announced* it; John twenty: « Jesus stood in the midst and said to them: Peace be to you »; the Lord *commanded* and *preached the gospel of* peace, as is said to the Ephesians, two: « Coming, he preached the gospel of peace to you that were afar off, and peace to them that were nigh ».

12. (Vers. 6.). And because they could fear lest they should offer peace in vain, therefore he adds: *And if the son of peace be there; of peace* according to the eternal foreknowledge, of whom it is said in John eleven[^p256-2] that « Jesus suffered, that he might gather together the children of God that were dispersed »; of whom it is said in the second to Timothy, two: « The Lord knoweth who are his ». Such are the sons of peace, because it is said in Matthew five: « Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called the children of God ». — In such a one the word of preaching has efficacy; on account of which he adds: *Your peace shall rest upon him,* that is, announced by you; whence Isaias sixty-six[^p256-3], according to another translation: « Upon whom shall my spirit rest but upon the humble and quiet »? The peace of Christ, when announced, rests indeed upon those predestined by God, according to that of Acts thirteen: « As many as were ordained to life believed »; and John ten: « You do not believe, because you are not of my sheep. My sheep hear my voice ». — It is useful, therefore, to speak the Gospel of peace to the *predestined;* it is useful also to speak it to the *foreknown;* therefore he adds: *But if not, it shall return to you,* according to that of the Psalm[^p256-4]: « My prayer shall be turned into my bosom »; and this because the work of piety and mercy is always useful to the doer and returns to the doer, according to that of Ecclesiasticus seventeen: « The alms of a man is as a purse with him, and shall preserve the grace of a man as the apple of the eye; and afterward he shall rise up, and shall render them their reward, to every one upon their own head ».

13. (Vers. 7.). Fourthly, as to *displaying maturity,* he adds: *And in the same house
remain,* as mature men, lest perhaps you be marked for instability; whence Ecclesiasticus twenty-one[^p256-5]: « The foot of a fool is soon in his neighbour's house ». Therefore Bede says: « It is foreign to a preacher to run about through houses and to change his lodging ». — And because they could believe that the lodging was to be changed on account of food, therefore he adds: *Eating and drinking such things as they have,* without *discrimination* of foods, as in the first to the Corinthians, ten[^p256-6]: « Eat of whatsoever is set before you, asking no question for conscience' sake »; without *rejection* of foods too, according to that of the first to Timothy, four: « Every creature of God is good, and nothing to be rejected that is received with thanksgiving; for it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer »; without *seeking out* of foods too, according to that of Ecclesiasticus thirty-one: « Thou art sitting in the midst of many; reach not thy hand out first of all, and be not first to ask to drink ». — And because they could believe that it was not lawful to be fed of another's substance, therefore he adds: *For the labourer is worthy of his hire,* not only in the fatherland for reward, but also on the way for sustenance. Whence in the first to the Corinthians, nine[^p256-7]: « The Lord ordained that they who preach the gospel should live by the gospel »; and to the Galatians, six: « And let him that is instructed in the word communicate to him that instructeth him in all good things »; whence it is said in the first to the Corinthians, nine: « If we have sown unto you spiritual things, it is no great matter if we reap your carnal things ». And there[^p256-8] the Apostle proves that such a labourer is worthy, both by authorities and by very many reasons, as is plain there. Since, then, he is worthy of refreshment, on account of it he ought not to change his lodging. — Therefore at the end he adds: *Remove not from house to house;* lest you seem wanderers, lest you be like that wandering woman of whom it is said in Proverbs seven[^p256-9] that she « is talkative and wandering, not bearing to be quiet, not able to abide still at home ». Such are heretics; whence in the second to Timothy, three: « Of these are they who creep into houses and lead captive silly women laden with sins ». Not such ought the preachers of the truth to be; whence Ambrose[^p256-10]: « One is not to move from house to house with wandering ease,
<!-- page 257 -->
so that constancy may be kept in hospitable love, lest the bond of friendship, once grown together, be easily dissolved ».

14. *And into what city soever you enter.* After the common formation of the disciples the Evangelist here subjoins *the special formation,* according to the diversities of the hearers[^p257-1]. And because some were ready for *receiving* them, as the faithful, but some for *repelling* them, as the unfaithful, whose fault grew from contempt of the divine grace and disdain of the apostolic authority; therefore this part has four. In the first of which is set forth *the formation with respect to the faithful;* in the second, *with respect to the rebellious,* there[^p257-2]: *Into whatsoever city you enter, and they receive you not;* in the third is added *the rebuke of the despisers,* there: *Woe to thee, Corozain,* etc.; in the fourth is subjoined *the authentication of the preachers,* there: *He that heareth you,* etc.

Concerning the formation with respect to the faithful two things are introduced; the first of which is *the receiving of the temporal stipend,* the second is *the communication of the spiritual benefit.*

15. (Vers. 8.). First, then, as to *the receiving of the temporal stipend* he forms them, when he says: *And into what city soever you enter,* namely for preaching the truth, as is said in Jonas three[^p257-3], that « Jonas began to enter into the city one day's journey, and he cried ». So these too, by the cry of the divine word, ought to rouse the city at their entrance, according to what is said of Philip the disciple in Acts eight, that « going down to the city of Samaria, he preached Jesus to them. And the multitudes were attentive to those things which were said by Philip ». — And because it belongs to the faithful to attend to the divine words, therefore he adds: *And they receive you,* namely by faith and dilection, as messengers of Christ; since, in Matthew eighteen[^p257-4], « he that shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me ». Now he receives the preacher who accepts his doctrine with meekness and patience, according to what is said in James one: « With meekness receive the ingrafted word, which is able to save your souls ». — And because from those who receive spiritual lessons bodily nourishment can be received, therefore he adds: *Eat such things as are set before you,* namely for relieving necessity, that you may labour more strongly, according to that of the Psalm[^p257-5]: « For thou shalt eat the labours of thy hands: blessed art thou, and it shall be well with thee ». Whence on that of the first to the Corinthians, nine: « A dispensation is committed to me », the Gloss: « We ought not therefore to preach the gospel so that we may eat; but therefore to eat so that we may preach the gospel, so that food may not be the good that is sought, but the necessary thing that is added ».

16. And note that he says: *Such things as are set before you,* and this with cheerfulness; because, in Proverbs fifteen[^p257-6], « it is better to be invited to herbs with charity than to a fatted calf with hatred ». Eat, too, *such things as are set before you,* not what you yourselves prepare; which he says for avoiding gluttony, of which Seneca[^p257-7]: « They heap precious things into the belly as if it were to keep what is heaped up; what matters it what it receives, when it will lose all that it receives »? And therefore it is said in Ecclesiasticus two[^p257-8]: « Take all that shall be applied to thee ». Now this looks chiefly to the poor, that they be content with a cheap refreshment, as hungry men; because it is said in Proverbs twenty-seven: « A soul that is hungry will take even bitter for sweet »; and in Job six: « The things which before my soul would not touch, now through anguish are my meats ».

17. (Vers. 9.). But secondly, as to *the communication of the spiritual benefit,* it is added: *And cure the sick that are therein;* and this by the power conferred on you by the Holy Spirit, of which in the first to the Corinthians, twelve[^p257-9]: « To another, the grace of healing in one Spirit; to another, the working of miracles ». This the Lord gave to the lesser disciples for the confirmation of his doctrine; whence Jerome: « Because no one would believe rustic men, and men without the comeliness of eloquence, promising the kingdom of heaven, he gives them power to work miracles ». The gift of this power he had first granted, as also to the Apostles, above in nine[^p257-10]: « He gave them power over all devils, and to cure
<!-- page 258 -->
diseases »; but here he commands that they use the gift received, according to what is said in the first of Peter, four: « As every man hath received grace, ministering the same one to another », etc. — And because bodily curing was ordained to the illumination of the mind, therefore he adds: *And say to them: The kingdom of God shall come nigh*[^p258-1] *unto you;* in which is enclosed at once *the truth of doctrine, the goodness of grace* and *the sublimity of glory,* according as it was so expounded[^p258-2], and the *interlinear* says: « The kingdom of God is Christ, or eternal life, or the knowledge of the Scriptures ». And so it *came nigh* when he was among them, according to what is said below in seventeen[^p258-3]: « The kingdom of God is within you ». Truly it came nigh, since he was already present of whom it is said in the Apocalypse nineteen that « he had on his garment and on his thigh written: King of kings and Lord of lords ». And he says pointedly: *Unto you,* that is, unto those who receive the word of God by faith, of whom in the Apocalypse five[^p258-4]: « Thou hast made us to our God a kingdom, and we shall reign on the earth »; and through the grace of faith, according to that to the Colossians, one, « he hath delivered us from the power of darkness and hath translated us into the kingdom of the Son of his charity ». — Thus, then, it is plain how they were to behave toward the faithful, since they were to receive the stipend of sustenance and to minister the benefit of curing and of erudition. Those, therefore, who do not minister these spiritual benefits receive the bodily stipends less than condignly. Whence Gregory[^p258-5]: « What do we teachers say to these things, who, running before the coming of Christ, take up the ecclesiastical office and eat bread dumb »? And Bernard[^p258-6]: « They shall come before the tribunal of the judge, where there will be the hard pleading, the grievous accusation, of the poor, on whose stipends they lived and whose sins they did not wash away ».

18. (Vers. 10.). *Into whatsoever*[^p258-7] *city you enter.* After the formation with respect to the faithful is added *the formation with respect to the rebellious,* concerning which two things are introduced: the first is *the attestation of the truth of the Gospel,* the second is *the threatening of the severity of judgement.*

First, then, as to *the attestation of the truth of the Gospel* it is said: *Into whatsoever city you
*enter,* for the proclamation of the truth, according to that of Acts seventeen[^p258-8]: « When they were come to Thessalonica, where there was a synagogue of the Jews, Paul went in unto them and reasoned with them out of the Scriptures ». — *And they receive*[^p258-9] *you not,* by the hearing of the truth, which is to be lent to preachers, as is said in the third Canonical of John: « We therefore ought to receive such, that we may be fellow helpers of the truth. I had written perhaps to the Church, but Diotrephes, who loveth to have the pre-eminence among them, doth not receive us ». — *Going forth in the street thereof,* for the testimony of the truth, on account of which it is said in Matthew ten[^p258-10]: « That which I tell you in the dark, speak ye in the light, and that which you hear in the ear, preach ye upon the housetops ».

19. (Vers. 11.). *Say*[^p258-11]*: Even the very dust of the feet that cleaveth to us from your city we wipe off against you,* on account of contempt of the truth, which is a sin greatly to be detested, according to that of John fifteen: « If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin ». And therefore, for the detestation of this offence, he speaks of *the dust to be wiped off,* as Peter to Simon in Acts eight: « Keep thy money to thyself to perish with thee ». — *Yet know this, that the kingdom of God shall come nigh*[^p258-12], that is, the truth of the Gospel, according to that of Matthew twenty-four: « This gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in the whole world for a testimony to all nations », so that no one can excuse himself by ignorance. And therefore, so that this may be done publicly, he bids them *wipe off the dust in the streets,* on which Jerome says[^p258-13] that he bids this « for a testimony that their preaching had reached even to that city ». And so they have no excuse for faithlessness.

20. But according to the *spiritual intelligence, the feet of the Apostles* are the affections of the preachers, to which a threefold dust is wont to cleave, namely *of vainglory,* of which in the Psalm[^p258-14]: « Let him bring down my glory to the dust »; *of indignation and impatience,* of which in Micheas seven: « They shall lick the dust like serpents »; *of cupidity and avarice,* according to that of Ecclesiasticus twenty-seven: « As when one sifteth with
<!-- page 259 -->
a sieve, the dust will remain ». — The first dust cleaves to the feet of those preaching when they *are praised;* but it is shaken off by *consideration of oneself,* according to that of Matthew ten[^p259-1]: « It is not you that speak »; and the first to the Corinthians, twelve: « No man can say: The Lord Jesus, but by the Holy Spirit »; and the first to the Corinthians, four: « What hast thou that thou hast not received »? Therefore Ecclesiasticus ten: « Why is earth and ashes proud »? — The second dust cleaves when they *are not accepted;* but this is shaken off by *the remembrance of Christ,* according to that of John fifteen[^p259-2]: « Remember my word that I said to you: The servant is not greater than his master. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you », etc. — The third dust cleaves when *gifts are offered,* which blind the eyes of the leaders, according to that of Baruch six[^p259-3]: « Their eyes are full of dust through the feet of them that come in »; but this is shaken off by *the memory of our death.* Jerome[^p259-4]: « He easily despises all things who always thinks that he is to die »; and in the first to Timothy, the last: « We brought nothing into this world; and certainly we can carry nothing out ».

21. (Vers. 12.). But secondly, as to *the threatening of the severity of judgement,* he adds: *I say to you, it shall be more tolerable at that day for Sodom than for that city;* from which appears the greatness of *the punishment.* For it is said in the Canonical of Jude[^p259-5] that « Sodom and Gomorrha and the neighbouring cities were made an example, suffering the punishment of eternal fire ». Whence the Lord too was most vehemently indignant with them, according to what is said in Genesis nineteen, that « the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrha brimstone and fire, and he destroyed these cities and all the country about ». There appears also from this the greatness of *the fault,* because contempt[^p259-6] of the truth is a greater fault than the lust of carnality. — Now the reason of this is that *so great a grace was not offered* to them; and, according to what is said below in twelve[^p259-7], « unto whom much is given, of him much shall be required ». Whence to the Hebrews, two: « For if the word spoken by angels became steadfast; how shall we escape if we neglect so great salvation, which, having begun to be declared
by the Lord, was confirmed unto us by them that heard him »? — Another reason is on account of *the greater knowledge of the truth;* whence Gregory[^p259-8]: « Where the gift of knowledge is greater, there the transgressor is subject to a greater fault »; because it is said in James four: « To him who knoweth to do good, and doth it not, to him it is sin ». — And that the greatest punishment is owed to the despisers of the truth is plain from what is said to the Romans, one[^p259-9], of certain men who, « when they knew God, have not glorified him as God », that « God delivered them up to a reprobate sense ». And the reason of this is given, because « they liked not to have God in their knowledge ».

22. (Vers. 13.). *Woe to thee, Corozain,* etc. Here in the third place is subjoined *the rebuke of the despisers,* who are shown to be rebuked by the Lord on account of two things, and are reproved as damnable, namely on account of *hardness of heart* and on account of *loftiness of mind:* hardness of heart made them *impenitent,* but loftiness made them *arrogant.*

First, then, *rebuking hardness of heart,* he says: *Woe to thee, Corozain! woe to thee, Bethsaida!* which were two cities of Galilee where he had done very many signs, and yet hardness still remained in them[^p259-10]. — On account of which he adds: *For if in Tyre and Sidon had been wrought the mighty works that have been wrought in you,* that is, if the Lord had shown them so great miracles before their overthrow — which the Lord threatened through Ezechiel twenty-seven[^p259-11]: « Thou, son of man, take up a lamentation for Tyre », and at the end of the chapter: « Thou art brought to nothing, and thou shalt never be any more » — they would not yet have been overthrown, and this because they would have done penance. — Whence he adds: *They would have done penance long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes,* as the Ninivites did, of whom it is said in Jonas three[^p259-12] that « they preached a fast and put on sackcloth from the least to the greatest ».

23. And note that he names three things in which he expresses the perfection of penance, namely *sackcloth, ashes* and *sitting.* For in the penitent there ought to be *sorrow* from the consideration of the divine offence, and this in *sackcloth; fear,* from the consideration of vengeance, and this in
<!-- page 260 -->
*ashes,* which bears the image of death; there ought to be *shame* in the consideration of his following, or of his own vileness, and this in *sitting.* Therefore Jerome says[^p260-1] that « ashes and sackcloth are the arms of penitents ». And therefore Jeremias six: « O daughter of my people, gird thee with sackcloth and sprinkle thee with ashes; make thee mourning as for an only son, a bitter lamentation ». And on account of the shame of humility which ought to accompany it, it is said in Jeremias thirteen[^p260-2]: « Say to the king and to the mistress: Humble yourselves, sit down, for the crown of your glory is come down from your head ». If, then, they had done such penance, they would not have had such hardness of heart, and through this not so hard a sentence.

24. (Vers. 14.). And therefore he subjoins: *But it shall be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon at the judgement than for you;* because, according to what is said below in twelve[^p260-3], « the servant who knew the will of his lord and did it not shall be beaten with many stripes ». Now the reason of this is excessive hardness against such great benefits; whence it is said to the Romans, two: « Knowest thou not that the kindness of God leadeth thee to penance? But according to thy hardness and impenitent heart thou treasurest up to thyself wrath against the day of wrath and revelation of the just judgement of God »; because it is said in Ecclesiasticus three: « A hard heart shall fare evil at the last »; « and the sinner will add sin to sin ».

25. (Vers. 15.). But secondly, *rebuking as to loftiness of mind,* he adds: *And thou, Capharnaum, exalted unto heaven,* namely through arrogance, *thou shalt be thrust down unto hell*[^p260-4], by the divine sentence. Whence Abdias one: « Though thou be exalted as an eagle, and though thou set thy nest among the stars, thence will I bring thee down »; and Job twenty: « If his pride mount up even to heaven, and his head touch the clouds; in the end he shall be destroyed like a dunghill ». Now Capharnaum is called *exalted* on account of the very many miracles
that were done in it, over which it grew proud more than it profited; whence above in four[^p260-5] it is said: « As great things as we have heard done in Capharnaum », etc. First, then, it was exalted on account of the gifts of divine grace, but afterward thrust down by the desert of pride. And this had been foretold in Isaias nine[^p260-6]: « At the first time the land of Zabulon and the land of Nephthali was lightened », namely by the preceding miracles, « and at the last the way of the sea was heavily loaded », that is, pressed down in judgement on account of its sins, as the Gloss expounds in the same place. And therefore the divine gifts are to be received with fear and great reverence, as Gregory says[^p260-7], that « when the gifts are increased, the accounts of the gifts also grow ».

26. (Vers. 16.). *He that heareth you,* etc. Here in the fourth place is added the authentication[^p260-8] of the preachers. Now he shows them to be authentic both on account of the authority *of Christ the mediator* and also on account of the authority *of the supreme commander.*

First, then, he authenticates them by *the authority of Christ the mediator,* when he says: *He that heareth you heareth me.* For they bore the person of Christ; whence the Apostle in the second to the Corinthians, two[^p260-9]: « For what I have pardoned, for your sakes have I done it in the person of Christ »; and thirteen: « Do you seek a proof of Christ that speaketh in me »? And therefore it is said in the first to the Thessalonians, two: « And you, when you had received of us the word of the hearing of God, you received it not as the word of men, but, as it is indeed, the word of God ». In the hearing, therefore, of the disciples of Christ Christ is heard, and likewise in contempt he is contemned. — And therefore he adds: *And he that despiseth you despiseth me;* whence Ezechiel three[^p260-10]: « The house of Israel will not hearken to thee, because they will not hearken to me ». And this he intimates in Matthew twenty-five, when he says: « As long as you did it not to one of these least, neither did you
<!-- page 261 -->
do it to me ». And therefore the Apostle said in the first to the Thessalonians, five: « Extinguish not the spirit, despise not prophecies », that is, true preachings.

27. Secondly, he authenticates them on account of *the authority of the supreme commander,* when he adds: *He*[^p261-1] *that despiseth me despiseth him that sent me,* both on account of *the committed authority* and on account of *the consubstantiality.* Whence John five: « He who honoureth not the Son honoureth not the Father who hath sent him ». And therefore it follows that if anyone despises the Apostles, he despises Christ; and he who despises Christ despises God: therefore he who despises the Apostles despises God; and this is no small sin. On account of which Isaias thirty-three[^p261-2]: « Woe to thee that despisest; shalt not thou thyself also be despised? When thou shalt be wearied and cease to despise, thou shalt be despised ». Great, therefore, is the authority of the preachers, in whose reception God is received and in whose contempt he is contemned. For they are the mouth of God by announcing his words, as is said in Jeremias fifteen[^p261-3]: « If thou wilt separate the precious from the vile, thou shalt be as my mouth ». And therefore as the mouth of God they ought to be fed and honoured; and they themselves also ought chiefly to guard the mouth both from greed and from talkativeness, so that it can truly be said of them, that of the Apocalypse fourteen: « These were purchased from among men, the firstfruits to God and to the Lamb, and in their mouth there was found no lie; for they are without spot before the throne of God ».
