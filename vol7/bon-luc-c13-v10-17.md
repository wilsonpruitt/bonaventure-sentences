# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XIII, Vers. 10-17
## *Confutatio, quae fit in concilio, de qua duo*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 341 -->

#### Secundo confutantur calumniantes miracula ex parte circumstantiae temporis secundum duplicem opportunitatem.

21. *Erat autem docens in synagoga* etc. Pars illa, in qua Salvator confutat dolositatem Iudaeorum calumniantium, divisa fuit in duas partes secundum duplicem modum calumniandi miracula Christi, scilicet ex parte *causae efficientis* et ex parte *circumstantiae temporis*[^p341-13]. Prima parte determinata, hic incipit secunda. Et dividitur haec pars in duas secundum duplicem temporis opportunitatem: in prima calumniantes Christi miracula, quia fiebant in Sabbato, confutat in *concilio;* in secunda vero confutat in *convivio,* ibi: *Et factum est, cum intraret,* in decimo quarto capitulo[^p341-14].

#### Confutatio, quae fit in concilio, de qua duo.

Prima pars dividitur in duas. Primo enim *confutat* eos qui dicebant, Sabbato non facienda esse miracula; secundo vero, occasione accepta, *ostendit,*
<!-- page 342 -->
quod sine intermissione facienda sunt opera bona, ibi[^p342-1]: *Dicebat: Cui simile est regnum Dei?*

Circa *confutationem* autem calumniantium miracula propter Sabbatum procedit hoc modo. Primo praemittitur *operatio curationis mirificae.* Secundo adiungitur *obiurgatio superstitionis Iudaicae,* ibi[^p342-2]: *Respondens autem archisynagogus* etc. Tertio subiungitur *confutatio obiectionis propositae,* ibi: *Respondens autem ad illum Dominus.* Quarto subinfertur *assecutio gloriosae victoriae,* ibi: *Cum haec diceret, erubescebant* etc. — Circa *operationem* autem *curationis mirificae* introducuntur tria, scilicet *solemnitas diei* et *gravitas inveterati morbi* et *virtuositas*[^p342-3] *accelerati miraculi.*

22. (Vers. 10.). Primo igitur quantum ad *solemnitatem diei sacri* dicit: *Erat autem docens in synagoga eorum Sabbatis.* — Nota, quod Dominus Sabbatis docebat sive propter *conservandam Iudaeorum consuetudinem;* Actuum decimo quinto[^p342-4]: « Moyses a temporibus antiquis habet in civitatibus singulis qui eum praedicant in synagogis, ubi per omne Sabbatum legitur ». — Sive etiam propter *doctrinae publicationem,* quia tunc tanquam in die solemni magis communiter verbo Dei erant intenti; Ioannis decimo octavo: « Ego semper docui in synagogis, ubi omnes Iudaei conveniunt ». — Sive quia Sabbatum erat *dies quietis,* et ipse venerat annuntiare pacem et quietem; unde ad Ephesios secundo[^p342-5]: « Veniens evangelizavit pacem vobis, qui fuistis longe, et pacem his qui prope ». — Sive etiam, quia praeceptum de Sabbato, quod spectabat ad caritatem, praecipue erat *figura temporis gratiae,* in quo praecipitur: « Hoc est praeceptum meum, ut diligatis invicem », Ioannis decimo quinto. — Sive etiam propter *servandum ordinem;* quia ibi debuit incipere *reparatio,* ubi terminata est *conditio;* Genesis secundo[^p342-6] dicitur, quod « complevit Deus die septimo opus suum, quod fecit ». « Et benedixit diei septimo » etc. — Sive ad *dandam informationem,* quod intentus sapientiae ab omnibus debet cessare, secundum illud
Ecclesiastici trigesimo octavo[^p342-7]: « Sapientiam scribe in tempore vacuitatis ». — Sive etiam ex septima causa, ut *ostenderet se dominum Legis et Sabbati,* faciendo miracula in Sabbato; Ioannis quinto: « Pater meus operatur usque modo, et ego operor. Propter hoc ergo quaerebant eum Iudaei interficere, quia non solum solvebat Sabbatum, sed etiam Patrem suum dicebat esse Deum, aequalem se faciens Deo ».

23. (Vers. 11.). Secundo quantum ad *gravitatem inveterati morbi* subdit: *Et ecce, mulier, quae habebat spiritum infirmitatis annis decem et octo,* quantum ad infirmitatis *diuturnitatem;* Ecclesiastici decimo[^p342-8]: « Languor prolixior gravat medicum, brevem languorem praecidit medicus ». — *Et inclinata erat,* quantum ad infirmitatis *gravitatem,* secundum quod ille lugebat: « Miser factus sum et curvatus usque in finem » etc. — *Nec omnino poterat sursum respicere,* per infirmitatis *continuitatem,* secundum illud Threnorum primo[^p342-9]: « Dedit me in manu, de qua non potero surgere ».

24. Potest autem per hanc *incurvationem longaevam* intelligi morbus *avaritiae* et cupiditatis, quae incurvat cor ad temporalia; Psalmus[^p342-10]: « Oculos suos statuerunt declinare in terram ». Et hoc quaerunt daemones; Isaiae quinquagesimo primo: « Incurvare, ut transeamus ». — Haec infirmitas curvativa durat per *decem et octo annos,* id est per senarium triplicem, hoc est per totum tempus vitae; quia, ut dicit Hieronymus[^p342-11], cum cetera vitia senescant in senibus, sola avaritia iuvenescit; et Ecclesiastae secundo dicitur de avaro: « Cuncti dies eius doloribus et aerumnis pleni sunt, nec per noctem mente requiescit »; et Osee septimo: « Comederunt alieni robur eius, et ipse nescivit; sed et cani effusi sunt in eo, et ipse ignoravit ». — Haec etiam infirmitas *non sinit respicere sursum;* unde dicitur Matthaei sexto[^p342-12]: « Ubi est thesaurus tuus, ibi et cor tuum »; et Psalmus: « Supercecidit ignis », scilicet cupiditatis, « et non viderunt solem », ne recordarentur etc.
<!-- page 343 -->

25. (Vers. 12.). Tertio quantum ad *virtuositatem accelerati miraculi* subiungit: *Quam cum vidisset*[^p343-1] *Iesus,* scilicet *oculo pietatis;* Psalmus: « Quoniam prospexit de excelso sancto suo »; « ut audiret gemitus compeditorum et solveret filios interemptorum »; et quarti Regum vigesimo: « Audivi orationem tuam, vidi lacrymas tuas ». — *Vocavit eam ad se,* per *affectum benignitatis;* Ioannis undecimo[^p343-2] dixit Martha Mariae: « Magister adest et vocat te »; et quarti Regum quarto: « Voca Sunamitidem hanc et dic ei: Quid vis, ut faciam tibi »? — *Et ait illi: Mulier, dimissa es ab infirmitate tua,* per *promissum veritatis;* unde ratione certitudinis ponit praeteritum pro futuro proximo; Numerorum vigesimo tertio[^p343-3]: « Non est Deus ut homo, ut mentiatur »; « dixit ergo, et non faciet? Locutus est, et non implebit »?

26. (Vers. 13.). *Et imposuit illi manus, et confestim erecta est et glorificabat Deum,* quantum ad *effectum virtutis;* Ecclesiastae octavo[^p343-4]: « Omne, quod voluerit, faciet, et sermo illius potestate plenus est ». Ideo autem *manus imponit,* ut ostendat, quod ipse est manus, per quam Omnipotens operatur cuncta et per quam reparat universa; Isaiae quinquagesimo nono[^p343-5]: « Ecce, non est abbreviata manus Domini, ut salvare nequeat »; et Numerorum undecimo dixit Dominus ad Moysen: « Nunquid manus Domini invalida est? Nunc videbis, utrum meus sermo opere impleatur ». Et propterea Ecclesiastici trigesimo sexto: « Innova signa et immuta mirabilia, glorifica manum et brachium dextrum ».

27. Et nota, quod hic quatuor dicit de incurvatione huius mulieris, quae Dominus exercet in *salvatione cuiuslibet animae fidelis.* Nam primo *videt* praedestinando; Sapientiae quarto[^p343-6]: « Respectus eius in electos illius ». — Secundo vero *vocat* exterius attrahendo; Matthaei nono[^p343-7]: « Non veni vocare iustos, sed peccatores »; et Proverbiorum nono: « Misit ancillas suas, ut vocarent ad arcem et ad moenia civitatis ». — Tertio *manum imponit* iustificando; Ezechielis tertio[^p343-8]: « Abii amarus in indignatione spiritus mei; manus enim Domini erat mecum confortans me »; et supra primo: « Etenim manus Domini erat cum illo ». — Quarto *erigit* glorificando; primi Regum secundo[^p343-9]: « Suscitat de pulvere egenum et de stercore elevat pauperem, ut sedeat cum principibus et solium gloriae teneat ». — De his quatuor simul dicitur ad Romanos octavo: « Quos praedestinavit, hos et vocavit; et quos vocavit, hos et iustificavit; et quos iustificavit, illos et magnificavit ».

28. (Vers. 14.). *Respondens autem archisynagogus* etc. Hic secundo subditur *obiurgatio superstitionis Iudaicae*[^p343-10]. Circa quam primo introducitur *commotio zeli inordinati in animo;* secundo, *simulatio zeli ordinati in verbo.*

Primo igitur quantum ad *commotionem zeli inordinati in animo* dicit: *Respondens autem archisynagogus indignans, quia in*[^p343-11] *Sabbato curasset Iesus,* quasi dicat, quod eius responsio ex indignatione animi originem traxit. Haec autem indignatio oriebatur ex praesumtione iustitiae; unde solum « *vera* iustitia compassionem habet, sed *falsa* iustitia indignationem[^p343-12] ». Haec autem falsa iustitia erat aemulatrix Legis quantum ad observantiam Sabbati, sed aemulatione stulta; ad Romanos decimo[^p343-13]: « Aemulationem Dei habent, sed non secundum scientiam; ignorantes enim Dei iustitiam et suam volentes statuere, iustitiae Dei non sunt subiecti ». Unde stulta praesumtio parit *aemulationem,* et aemulatio *indignationem,* et indignatio *legis divinae transgressionem,* secundum illud Proverbiorum vigesimo nono[^p343-14]: « Qui ad indignandum facilis est erit ad peccatum proclivior ». Unde stultus legis zelator est ipsius legis destructor; propter quod ad Galatas primo: « Supra modum persequebar Ecclesiam Dei et expugnabam illam »; « abundantius aemulator existens paternarum mearum traditionum » etc.

29. Secundo quantum ad *simulationem zeli ordinati in verbo* subdit: *Dicebat turbae: Sex dies sunt, in quibus oportet operari;* ecce, *allegatio veritatis;* Exodi vigesimo[^p343-15]: « Sex diebus operaberis
<!-- page 344 -->
omnia opera tua. Septimo autem die Sabbatum Domini est ». — *In his ergo venite et curamini;* ecce, *simulatio pietatis,* unde sibi competit quod dicitur secundae ad Timotheum tertio[^p344-1] de simulatoribus: « Speciem pietatis habentes, abnegantes virtutem ». — Cum dicit: *Et non in die Sabbati;* ecce, *ostentatio sanctitatis,* quia tanquam *sanctus* diem Sabbati ostendebat se velle servari ut *sanctum,* secundum illud Levitici vigesimo tertio[^p344-2]: « Sex diebus operaberis, septimus dies, quia Sabbatum requietionis est, vocabitur *sanctus* ». Unde Iudaei dicebant Ioannis nono: « Non est hic homo a Deo, qui Sabbatum non custodit »; sed diei sancti observantia in archisynagogo potius erat ad *simulationem iustitiae* quam ad *impletionem,* secundum illud Isaiae primo[^p344-3]: « Neomeniam et Sabbatum et solemnitates alias non feram ». « Calendas vestras et solemnitates vestras odivit anima mea ». Et ratio huius est, quia potius servabant ad superstitionem quam propter devotionem; unde Threnorum primo: « Viderunt eam hostes et deriserunt Sabbata eius ».

30. (Vers. 15.). *Respondens autem ad illum* etc. Hic tertio subiungitur *confutatio obiectionis propositae*[^p344-4]; circa quam primo praemittit *exemplum sensibile,* deinde elicit *argumentum rationabile.*

Primo igitur praemittens *exemplum sensibile,* dicit: *Respondens autem ad illum Dominus dixit: Hypocrita*[^p344-5]. Ut *Dominus* respondet, quia dicitur Matthaei septimo, quod « erat docens sicut potestatem habens ». Et hoc faciebat potissime, cum alios increpabat, sicut illum quem *hypocritam* vocat, quia eius occulta videbat; unde primae ad Corinthios quarto[^p344-6]: « Qui iudicat me Dominus est ». Ipse enim novit abscondita cordis, et maxime odit vitium hypocrisis. Ideo Ecclesiastici primo: « Ne fueris hypocrita in conspectu hominum »; « ne revelet Deus abscondita tua et in medio synagogae elidat te », sicut fecit huic, quem exemplo evidenti et sensibili confutat et convincit. — Ideo subdit: *Unusquisque vestrum in*[^p344-7] *Sabbato non solvit bovem suum aut asinum a praesepio et ducit adaquare?* Glossa: « Merito *hypocritas* simulatores vocat, qui, cum magistri plebium videri appetant, sanationem hominum non verentur postponere curae pecorum ». In istis impletur illud Isaiae quinquagesimo septimo[^p344-8]: « Iustus perit, et non est qui recogitet in corde suo ». Et illud etiam hodie impleri videtur, secundum illud Bernardi: « Perit anima, et non est qui curet; cadit asina, et est qui sublevet ». *Hypocritam* ergo vocat eum qui, zelum Legis simulans, magis curat de pecore vel pecunia quam de anima.

31. (Vers. 16.). Secundo, eliciens *argumentum rationale,* subiungit: *Hanc autem filiam Abrahae, quam alligavit satanas, ecce, decem et octo annis,* cui debetur misericordia, et quia mulier infirma ex sexu; primae Petri tertio[^p344-9]: « Quasi infirmiori vasculo muliebri impartientes honorem »; et quia infirma ex oppressione daemonis; unde Matthaei decimo quinto[^p344-10]: « Miserere mei, fili David, quia filia mea male a daemonio vexatur »; quia etiam erat de semine Abrahae, quem Deus sibi elegerat; unde Matthaei decimo quinto[^p344-11]: « Non sum missus nisi ad oves quae perierunt domus Israel ». — Ex his ergo causis opportunis multo fortius colligitur, quod curatio mulieris propter Sabbatum non debuit differri. — Ideo subdit: *Non oportuit solvi a vinculo isto die Sabbati?* Immo certe oportuit, quia opportunitas temporis fuit: tum quia in infinitum maior cura debet esse de salute hominis quam bruti animalis; propter quod primae ad Corinthios nono[^p344-12]: « Nunquid de bobus cura est Deo »? Tum etiam, quia Sabbatum non est introductum in praeiudicium hominis, sed potius in salutem humani generis; unde Ioannis septimo[^p344-13]: « Si circumcisionem accipit homo in Sabbato, ut non solvatur Sabbatum; mihi indignamini, quia totum hominem sanum feci in Sabbato »? Tum etiam, quia Sabbato vetantur omnia *servilia;* unde Levitici vigesimo tertio[^p344-14]: « Omne opus servile non facietis in eo », non autem opera *spiritualia;* unde Matthaei duodecimo: « Non legistis, quia Sabbatis sacerdotes in templo Sabbatum violant et sine crimine sunt »? Non vetantur etiam opera *divina et miraculosa;* unde Ioannis quinto[^p344-15]: « Pater meus usque modo operatur, et ego operor »; et nono: « Me oportet operari, donec dies est ». Non vetantur etiam opera *necessaria ad sanitatis conservationem,* ut comedere et bibere: igitur nec *ad curationem.*

32. Ex quo[^p344-16] datur intelligi, quanta homini debeat esse sollicitudo et diligentia, ut peccator de laqueis diaboli absolvatur; ut etiam, si fieri possit,
<!-- page 345 -->
nec modicum retardetur; Proverbiorum sexto[^p345-1]: « Ne dederis somnum oculis tuis, nec dormitent palpebrae tuae; discurre, festina, suscita amicum tuum ». In cuius signum dicitur de filio prodigo revertente infra decimo quinto: « Cum adhuc longe esset, vidit illum pater ipsius et misericordia motus est, et accurrens, cecidit super collum eius et osculatus est eum ». — Et nota, quod non est aliud, filiam Abrahae *solvi a vinculo satanae,* quam animam *solvi a reatu culpae;* unde Glossa[^p345-2]: « Filia Abrahae, quaeque fidelis anima ». Hanc solvit Christus ministerio sacerdotum; Matthaei decimo octavo: « Amen dico vobis, quaecumque solveritis super terram, erunt soluta et in caelo ». Sed oportet, quod homo se ipsum disponat quasi *ab interiori;* Isaiae quinquagesimo secundo[^p345-3]: « Solve vincula colli tui, captiva filia Sion ». Et tunc Deus operatur *a superiori;* Psalmus: « Dirupisti vincula mea, tibi sacrificabo hostiam laudis ». Unde in hac solutione operantur *sacerdotes* per exteriorem dispensationem Sacramenti, et *peccator* per interiorem detestationem peccati, et *Deus* per superiorem infusionem gratiae Spiritus sancti.

33. (Vers. 17.). *Et cum haec diceret* etc. Hic quarto subiungit *assecutionem gloriosae victoriae*[^p345-4] quantum ad duo, quae hic introducuntur, scilicet *confusio rebellium* et *laetificatio fidelium.*

Primo ergo quantum ad *confusionem rebellium* dicit: *Et cum haec diceret, erubescebant omnes adversarii eius;* Glossa[^p345-5]: « *Adversarii,* scribae et Pharisaei, qui nec apertae veritati contradicere possunt nec propter invidiam credere volunt ». De talibus Iob vigesimo quarto: « Ipsi rebelles fuerunt lumini et nescierunt vias eius ». Tales non instruuntur, sed confunduntur, cum veritas declaratur; Isaiae quadragesimo primo[^p345-6]: « Ecce, confundentur et erubescent omnes, qui pugnant adversum te; erunt quasi non sint, et peribunt viri, qui contradicunt tibi. Quaeres eos, et non invenies viros rebelles tuos » etc.

34. Secundo quantum ad *laetificationem fidelium* subdit: *Et omnis populus gaudebat in universis, quae gloriose fiebant ab eo;* Beda[^p345-7]: « *Populus,* id est simplices et humiles, verba veritatis et miracula amantes ». Hi gaudent de manifestatione gloriae divinae; unde Isaiae vigesimo nono[^p345-8]: « Pauperes homines in sancto Israel exsultabunt »; *exsultabunt,* inquam, *in universis, quae gloriose fiebant ab eo;* Ecclesiastici quadragesimo secundo: « Gloria Domini plenum est opus eius ».

Et nota, quod opera Christi erant *gratiosa* quantum ad *condescensionem divinae dignationis,* et de his est *regratiandum;* Tobiae duodecimo[^p345-9]: « Benedicite Deum caeli et coram omnibus viventibus confitemini ei, quia fecit vobiscum misericordiam suam ». Erant etiam *gloriosa* quantum ad *operationem virtutis* et *manifestationem maiestatis,* et de his est *gaudendum* et *exsultandum;* Psalmus[^p345-10]: « Iubilate Deo omnis terra, psalmum dicite nomini eius, date gloriam laudi eius. Dicite Deo: Quam terribilia sunt opera tua! in multitudine virtutis tuae mentientur tibi inimici tui ».

35. Possunt autem haec *allegorice* exponi de Ecclesia ex gentibus congregata[^p345-11], quae per triplicem annorum senarium fuit *recurva:* hoc est sub dictamine legis naturae, legis Moysaicae et etiam prophetiae; in quorum quolibet opera facta sunt ad sex dies spectantia, hoc est opera servilia, quae ex timore fiebant. Et haec non possunt animam sursum erigere, quia timor deprimit, et solus amor sursum erigit; unde supra primo[^p345-12]: « Erexit nobis cornu salutis in domo David »; et hoc fit per spiritum caritatis; Ezechielis secundo: « Et ingressus est in me spiritus et statuit me super pedes meos ». — Haec *solvitur die Sabbati,* sicut *bos et asinus,* qui ducuntur adaquari, quia Ecclesia ista congregata est ex populo *Iudaico* et *gentili,* qui per *bovem* et *asinum* habent intelligi, secundum illud Isaiae primo[^p345-13]: « Cognovit bos possessorem suum, et asinus praesepe domini sui »; et trigesimo secundo: « Beati, qui seminatis super omnes aquas, immittentes pedem bovis et asini ». Utrumque autem Dominus solvit, et *Iudaeos* ad Ecclesiam venientes de onere servitutis legalis et *gentiles* de vinculo servitutis idololatriae; ad Ephesios secundo[^p345-14]: « Ipse est pax nostra, qui fecit utraque unum et medium parietem maceriae solvens, inimicitias in carne sua, Legem mandatorum decretis evacuans, ut duos condat in semetipsum in unum novum hominem, faciens pacem » etc. — De hac autem solutione *confunditur* populus Iudaeorum Christo adversantium, et *exsultat* populus gentilium, secundum illud Matthaei octavo[^p345-15]: « Multi
<!-- page 346 -->
ab oriente et occidente venient et recumbent cum Abraham et Isaac et Iacob » etc. « Filii autem regni mittentur in tenebras exteriores », et hoc propter rebellionem. Unde Actuum decimo tertio: « Vobis oportebat primum loqui regnum Dei; sed quoniam repellitis illud et indignos vos iudicatis aeternae vitae, ecce, convertimur ad gentes; sic enim praecepit nobis Dominus ».

## English
<!-- page 341 -->

#### Secondly those who calumniate the miracles are confuted on the side of the circumstance of time, according to a twofold opportuneness.

21. *And he was teaching in the synagogue* etc. That part in which the Saviour confutes the guile of the calumniating Jews was divided into two parts according to the twofold manner of calumniating the miracles of Christ, namely on the side of *the efficient cause* and on the side of *the circumstance of time*[^p341-13]. The first part being determined, here the second begins. And this part is divided into two according to the twofold opportuneness of time: in the first he confutes in *the council* those who calumniate the miracles of Christ because they were done on the Sabbath; but in the second he confutes them at *the banquet,* there: *And it came to pass, when he went in,* in the fourteenth chapter[^p341-14].

#### The confutation that is made in the council, concerning which two things.

The first part is divided into two. For first he *confutes* those who said that miracles were not to be done on the Sabbath; but secondly, the occasion being taken, he *shows*
<!-- page 342 -->
that good works are to be done without intermission, there[^p342-1]: *He said: To what is the kingdom of God like?*

Now concerning *the confutation* of those who calumniate the miracles on account of the Sabbath, he proceeds in this manner. First is set forth *the operation of a wonder-working cure.* Secondly is added *the chiding of the Jewish superstition,* there[^p342-2]: *And the ruler of the synagogue answering* etc. Thirdly is subjoined *the confutation of the objection put forward,* there: *And the Lord answering him.* Fourthly is subjoined *the attainment of a glorious victory,* there: *When he said these things, they were ashamed* etc. — Now concerning *the operation of the wonder-working cure* three things are introduced, namely *the solemnity of the day* and *the gravity of an inveterate disease* and *the powerfulness*[^p342-3] *of a speeded miracle.*

22. (Vers. 10.). First, then, as to *the solemnity of the sacred day* he says: *And he was teaching in their synagogue on the sabbath days.* — Note that the Lord taught on the sabbath days either for the sake of *preserving the custom of the Jews;* Acts, the fifteenth chapter[^p342-4]: « Moses of old time hath in every city them that preach him in the synagogues, where he is read every sabbath ». — Or else for the sake of *the publishing of the doctrine,* because then, as on a solemn day, they were more commonly intent on the word of God; John, the eighteenth chapter: « I have always taught in the synagogues, whither all the Jews resort ». — Or because the Sabbath was *the day of rest,* and he had come to announce peace and rest; whence to the Ephesians, the second chapter[^p342-5]: « And coming, he preached the gospel of peace to you that were afar off, and peace to them that were nigh ». — Or else because the precept concerning the Sabbath, which looked to charity, was especially *a figure of the time of grace,* in which it is enjoined: « This is my precept, that you love one another », John, the fifteenth chapter. — Or else for the sake of *keeping order;* because the *reparation* ought to begin there where the *creation* was ended; in Genesis, the second chapter[^p342-6], it is said that « God ended on the seventh day his work which he had made ». « And he blessed the seventh day » etc. — Or for the sake of *giving formation,* that he who is intent on wisdom ought to cease from all things, according to that of
Ecclesiasticus, the thirty-eighth chapter[^p342-7]: « Write wisdom in the time of leisure ». — Or else from a seventh cause, that he might *show himself the lord of the Law and of the Sabbath,* by doing miracles on the Sabbath; John, the fifth chapter: « My Father worketh until now; and I work. Hereupon therefore the Jews sought to kill him, because he did not only break the sabbath, but also said God was his Father, making himself equal to God ».

23. (Vers. 11.). Secondly, as to *the gravity of an inveterate disease,* he subjoins: *And behold there was a woman, who had a spirit of infirmity eighteen years,* as to the *long duration* of the infirmity; Ecclesiasticus, the tenth chapter[^p342-8]: « A long sickness is troublesome to the physician; the physician cutteth off a short sickness ». — *And she was bowed together,* as to the *gravity* of the infirmity, according to what that man lamented: « I am become miserable, and am bowed down even to the end » etc. — *Neither could she look upwards at all,* by the *continuity* of the infirmity, according to that of Lamentations, the first chapter[^p342-9]: « He hath delivered me into a hand, out of which I am not able to rise ».

24. Now by this *long-lasting bowing down* can be understood the disease of *avarice* and cupidity, which bows the heart down to temporal things; the Psalm[^p342-10]: « They have set their eyes bowing down to the earth ». And this the demons seek; Isaias, the fifty-first chapter: « Bow down, that we may go over ». — This bowing infirmity lasts for *eighteen years,* that is, through a threefold six, that is, through the whole time of life; because, as Jerome says[^p342-11], while the other vices grow old in old men, avarice alone grows young; and in Ecclesiastes, the second chapter, it is said of the avaricious man: « All his days are full of sorrows and miseries, even in the night he doth not rest in mind »; and Osee, the seventh chapter: « Strangers have devoured his strength, and he knew it not; yea, grey hairs also are spread about upon him, and he is ignorant of it ». — This infirmity also *does not suffer one to look upwards;* whence it is said in Matthew, the sixth chapter[^p342-12]: « Where thy treasure is, there is thy heart also »; and the Psalm: « Fire hath fallen on them », namely of cupidity, « and they have not seen the sun », lest they should remember etc.
<!-- page 343 -->

25. (Vers. 12.). Thirdly, as to *the powerfulness of the speeded miracle,* he subjoins: *Whom when*[^p343-1] *Jesus saw,* namely *with the eye of piety*[^tr-pietas]; the Psalm: « Because he hath looked forth from his high sanctuary »; « that he might hear the groans of them that are in fetters, and release the children of the slain »; and the fourth of Kings, the twentieth chapter: « I have heard thy prayer, I have seen thy tears ». — *He called her unto him,* by *the affection of kindness;* in John, the eleventh chapter[^p343-2], Martha said to Mary: « The master is come, and calleth for thee »; and the fourth of Kings, the fourth chapter: « Call this Sunamitess, and say to her: What wilt thou have me to do for thee »? — *And said to her: Woman, thou hast been delivered from thy infirmity,* by *the promise of truth;* whence by reason of certitude he puts the past for the near future; Numbers, the twenty-third chapter[^p343-3]: « God is not as a man, that he should lie »; « hath he said then, and will he not do? Hath he spoken, and will he not fulfil »?

26. (Vers. 13.). *And he laid his hands upon her, and immediately she was made straight, and glorified God,* as to *the effect of power;* Ecclesiastes, the eighth chapter[^p343-4]: « He will do all that he will, and his word is full of power ». Now he *lays on his hands* for this reason, to show that he himself is the hand through which the Almighty works all things and through which he repairs the universe; Isaias, the fifty-ninth chapter[^p343-5]: « Behold the hand of the Lord is not shortened that it cannot save »; and in Numbers, the eleventh chapter, the Lord said to Moses: « Is the hand of the Lord unable? Now thou shalt see whether my word shall be fulfilled in deed ». And on this account Ecclesiasticus, the thirty-sixth chapter: « Renew thy signs, and work new marvels; glorify the hand and the right arm ».

27. And note that here he says four things of the bowing down of this woman, which the Lord exercises in *the saving of any faithful soul whatever.* For first he *sees,* by predestining; Wisdom, the fourth chapter[^p343-6]: « His regard is towards his chosen ». — But secondly he *calls,* by drawing outwardly; Matthew, the ninth chapter[^p343-7]: « I am not come to call the just, but sinners »; and Proverbs, the ninth chapter: « She hath sent her maids to call to the tower, and to the walls of the city ». — Thirdly he *lays on his hand,* by justifying; Ezechiel, the third chapter[^p343-8]: « I went away in bitterness in the indignation of my spirit; for the hand of the Lord was with me, strengthening me »; and above, the first chapter: « For the hand of the Lord was with him ». — Fourthly he *raises up,* by glorifying; the first of Kings, the second chapter[^p343-9]: « He raiseth up the needy from the dust, and lifteth up the poor from the dunghill, that he may sit with princes, and hold the throne of glory ». — Of these four together[^tr-corrigenda] it is said to the Romans, the eighth chapter: « Whom he predestinated, them he also called; and whom he called, them he also justified; and whom he justified, them he also magnified ».

28. (Vers. 14.). *And the ruler of the synagogue answering* etc. Here secondly is subjoined *the chiding of the Jewish superstition*[^p343-10]. Concerning which there is introduced first *the stirring of an inordinate zeal in the mind;* secondly, *the dissembling of an ordered zeal in word.*

First, then, as to *the stirring of an inordinate zeal in the mind* he says: *And the ruler of the synagogue answering, being angry that on*[^p343-11] *the sabbath Jesus had cured,* as if to say that his answer drew its origin from the indignation of his mind. Now this indignation arose from a presumption of justice; whence only « *true* justice hath compassion, but *false* justice indignation[^p343-12] ». Now this false justice was an emulator of the Law as to the observance of the Sabbath, but with a foolish emulation; to the Romans, the tenth chapter[^p343-13]: « They have an emulation of God, but not according to knowledge; for, not knowing the justice of God, and willing to establish their own, they have not submitted themselves to the justice of God ». Whence foolish presumption brings forth *emulation,* and emulation *indignation,* and indignation *the transgression of the divine law,* according to that of Proverbs, the twenty-ninth chapter[^p343-14]: « He that is easily stirred up to indignation shall be more prone to sin ». Whence the foolish zealot of the law is the destroyer of that very law; on account of which to the Galatians, the first chapter: « Beyond measure I persecuted the church of God, and wasted it »; « being more abundantly an emulator of the traditions of my fathers » etc.

29. Secondly, as to *the dissembling of an ordered zeal in word* he subjoins: *He said to the multitude: Six days there are wherein you ought to work;* behold, *the alleging of the truth;* Exodus, the twentieth chapter[^p343-15]: « Six days shalt thou labour
<!-- page 344 -->
all thy works; but on the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord ». — *In them therefore come, and be cured;* behold, *the dissembling of piety,* whence there befits him what is said in the second to Timothy, the third chapter[^p344-1], of dissemblers: « Having an appearance of piety, but denying the power ». — When he says: *And not on the sabbath day;* behold, *the ostentation of holiness,* because as one *holy* he showed that he wished the sabbath day to be kept as *holy,* according to that of Leviticus, the twenty-third chapter[^p344-2]: « Six days shalt thou work; the seventh day, because it is the rest of the sabbath, shall be called *holy* ». Whence the Jews said in John, the ninth chapter: « This man is not of God, who keepeth not the sabbath »; but the observance of the holy day in the ruler of the synagogue was rather for *the dissembling of justice* than for its *fulfilment,* according to that of Isaias, the first chapter[^p344-3]: « The new moon and the sabbath and other festivals I will not abide ». « My soul hateth your new moons, and your solemnities ». And the reason of this is that they kept them rather for superstition than on account of devotion; whence Lamentations, the first chapter: « The enemies have seen her, and have mocked at her sabbaths ».

30. (Vers. 15.). *And the Lord answering him* etc. Here thirdly is subjoined *the confutation of the objection put forward*[^p344-4]; concerning which he first sets forth *a sensible example,* then draws out *a reasonable argument.*

First, then, setting forth *a sensible example,* he says: *And the Lord answering him, said: Thou hypocrite*[^p344-5]. He answers as *Lord,* because it is said in Matthew, the seventh chapter, that « he was teaching as one having power ». And this he did most of all when he rebuked others, as that man whom he calls *a hypocrite,* because he saw his hidden things; whence the First to the Corinthians, the fourth chapter[^p344-6]: « He that judgeth me is the Lord ». For he knows the hidden things of the heart, and most of all hates the vice of hypocrisy. Therefore Ecclesiasticus, the first chapter: « Be not a hypocrite in the sight of men »; « lest God discover thy secrets, and cast thee down in the midst of the congregation », as he did to this man, whom he confutes and convinces by an evident and sensible example. — Therefore he subjoins: *Doth not every one of you on*[^p344-7] *the sabbath loose his ox or his ass from the manger, and lead it to water?* The Gloss: « Rightly does he call the dissemblers *hypocrites,* who, while they desire to be seen as masters of the peoples, do not fear to put the healing of men after the care of cattle ». In these is fulfilled that of Isaias, the fifty-seventh chapter[^p344-8]: « The just perisheth, and no man layeth it to heart ». And that also seems to be fulfilled today, according to that of Bernard: « The soul perishes, and there is none who cares; the she-ass falls, and there is one who lifts her up ». He calls *a hypocrite,* then, him who, dissembling zeal for the Law, cares more about a beast or money than about a soul.

31. (Vers. 16.). Secondly, drawing out *a rational argument,* he subjoins: *And this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years,* to whom mercy is owed, both because a woman is weak by sex; the first of Peter, the third chapter[^p344-9]: « Giving honour to the female as to the weaker vessel »; and because she was weak from the oppression of the demon; whence Matthew, the fifteenth chapter[^p344-10]: « Have mercy on me, son of David, for my daughter is grievously troubled by the devil »; because also she was of the seed of Abraham, whom God had chosen for himself; whence Matthew, the fifteenth chapter[^p344-11]: « I was not sent but to the sheep that are lost of the house of Israel ». — From these opportune causes, therefore, it is gathered much more strongly that the cure of the woman ought not to have been put off on account of the Sabbath. — Therefore he subjoins: *Ought she not to be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day?* Yea, certainly it behoved, because it was an opportuneness of time: both because care ought to be infinitely greater for the salvation of a man than of a brute animal; on account of which the First to the Corinthians, the ninth chapter[^p344-12]: « Doth God take care for oxen »? And also because the Sabbath was not brought in to the prejudice of man, but rather for the salvation of the human race; whence John, the seventh chapter[^p344-13]: « If a man receive circumcision on the sabbath day, that the sabbath may not be broken; are you angry at me because I have healed the whole man on the sabbath day »? And also because on the Sabbath all *servile* works are forbidden; whence Leviticus, the twenty-third chapter[^p344-14]: « You shall do no servile work therein », but not *spiritual* works; whence Matthew, the twelfth chapter: « Have ye not read that on the sabbath days the priests in the temple break the sabbath, and are without blame »? Nor yet are *divine and miraculous* works forbidden; whence John, the fifth chapter[^p344-15]: « My Father worketh until now, and I work »; and the ninth: « I must work, whilst it is day ». Nor yet are the works *necessary for the conservation of health* forbidden, as to eat and to drink: therefore neither those *for a cure.*

32. From which[^p344-16] it is given to be understood how great ought to be the solicitude and diligence of a man that the sinner be loosed from the snares of the devil; that also, if it could be done,
<!-- page 345 -->
he be not delayed even a little; Proverbs, the sixth chapter[^p345-1]: « Give not sleep to thy eyes, neither let thy eyelids slumber; run about, make haste, stir up thy friend ». In sign of which it is said of the prodigal son returning, below, in the fifteenth chapter: « When he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and was moved with mercy, and running to him fell upon his neck, and kissed him ». — And note that for the daughter of Abraham *to be loosed from the bond of Satan* is nothing other than for the soul *to be loosed from the guilt of fault;* whence the Gloss[^p345-2]: « The daughter of Abraham, every faithful soul ». This one Christ looses by the ministry of priests; Matthew, the eighteenth chapter: « Amen I say to you, whatsoever you shall loose upon earth, shall be loosed also in heaven ». But it is needful that a man dispose himself as it were *from within;* Isaias, the fifty-second chapter[^p345-3]: « Loose the bonds from off thy neck, O captive daughter of Sion ». And then God works *from above;* the Psalm: « Thou hast broken my bonds; I will sacrifice to thee the sacrifice of praise ». Whence in this loosing *the priests* work by the exterior dispensing of the Sacrament, and *the sinner* by the interior detestation of sin, and *God* by the superior infusion of the grace of the Holy Spirit.

33. (Vers. 17.). *And when he said these things* etc. Here fourthly he subjoins *the attainment of a glorious victory*[^p345-4] as to two things which are introduced here, namely *the confusion of the rebellious* and *the gladdening of the faithful.*

First, then, as to *the confusion of the rebellious* he says: *And when he said these things, all his adversaries were ashamed;* the Gloss[^p345-5]: « *Adversaries,* the scribes and Pharisees, who can neither contradict the open truth nor, because of envy, are willing to believe ». Of such, Job, the twenty-fourth chapter: « They have been rebellious to the light, and have not known his ways ». Such are not instructed, but confounded, when the truth is declared; Isaias, the forty-first chapter[^p345-6]: « Behold, all that fight against thee shall be confounded and ashamed; they shall be as nothing, and the men shall perish that strive against thee. Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find the men that are rebels against thee » etc.

34. Secondly, as to *the gladdening of the faithful,* he subjoins: *And all the people rejoiced in all the things that were gloriously done by him;* Bede[^p345-7]: « *The people,* that is, the simple and humble, who love the words of truth and miracles ». These rejoice at the manifestation of the divine glory; whence Isaias, the twenty-ninth chapter[^p345-8]: « The poor men shall exult in the Holy One of Israel »; *they shall exult,* I say, *in all the things that were gloriously done by him;* Ecclesiasticus, the forty-second chapter: « Full of the glory of the Lord is his work ».

And note that the works of Christ were *gracious* as to *the condescension of the divine deigning,* and for these *thanks are to be given in return;* Tobias, the twelfth chapter[^p345-9]: « Bless ye the God of heaven, give glory to him in the sight of all that live, because he hath shewn his mercy to you ». They were also *glorious* as to *the operation of power* and *the manifestation of majesty,* and in these one must *rejoice* and *exult;* the Psalm[^p345-10]: « Shout with joy to God, all the earth, sing ye a psalm to his name; give glory to his praise. Say unto God: How terrible are thy works! in the multitude of thy power thy enemies shall lie to thee ».

35. Now these things can be expounded *allegorically* of the Church gathered from the Gentiles[^p345-11], which through a threefold six of years was *bent over:* that is, under the dictate of the law of nature, of the Mosaic law, and also of prophecy; in each of which works were done that looked to the six days, that is, servile works, which were done out of fear. And these cannot raise the soul upward, because fear presses down, and love alone raises upward; whence above, in the first chapter[^p345-12]: « He hath raised up an horn of salvation to us in the house of David »; and this is done by the spirit of charity; Ezechiel, the second chapter: « And the spirit entered into me, and set me upon my feet ». — This one *is loosed on the sabbath day,* as *the ox and the ass,* which are led to be watered, because this Church is gathered from the *Jewish* and the *Gentile* people, who are to be understood by *the ox* and *the ass,* according to that of Isaias, the first chapter[^p345-13]: « The ox knoweth his owner, and the ass his master's crib »; and the thirty-second: « Blessed are ye that sow upon all waters, sending thither the foot of the ox and the ass ». Now the Lord looses both, both *the Jews* coming to the Church from the burden of the legal servitude and *the Gentiles* from the bond of the servitude of idolatry; to the Ephesians, the second chapter[^p345-14]: « He is our peace, who hath made both one, and loosing the middle wall of partition, the enmities in his flesh, making void the law of commandments contained in decrees, that he might make the two in himself into one new man, making peace » etc. — Now at this loosing the people of the Jews opposing Christ *is confounded,* and the people of the Gentiles *exults,* according to that of Matthew, the eighth chapter[^p345-15]: « Many
<!-- page 346 -->
shall come from the east and the west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob » etc. « But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into the exterior darkness », and this on account of rebellion. Whence Acts, the thirteenth chapter: « To you it behoved us first to speak the kingdom of God; but because you reject it, and judge yourselves unworthy of eternal life, behold, we turn to the Gentiles; for so the Lord hath commanded us ».
