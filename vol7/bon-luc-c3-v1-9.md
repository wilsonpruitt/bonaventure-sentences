# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. III, Vers. 1-9
## *Primo, de baptismo et praedicatione Ioannis in generali quoad tria*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 69 -->

### Capitulum III.

#### *Tertio, de nativitate extra uterum tripliciter.*

1. *Anno*[^p69-8] *quinto decimo* etc. Supra egit Evangelista de mysterio incarnationis Christi quantum ad nativitatem in utero et ex utero; hic agit de ipso quantum ad *nativitatem extra uterum,* secundum illud Ambrosii[^p69-9], quod dicit, illum renatum esse Sacramentis, scilicet quando baptizatus est. Et habet haec pars tres, in quarum prima agit de baptismo et praedicatione Ioannis *in generali respectu totius populi;* in secunda vero, *in speciali respectu specialium personarum,* ibi[^p69-10]: *Et interrogabant eum* etc.; in tertia, *in singulari respectu Christi,* ibi: *Et factum est, dum baptizaretur.* Et ordo patet, quia generale dirigit ad speciale, speciale ad singulare.

#### Primo, de baptismo et praedicatione Ioannis in generali quoad tria.

Quantum ad primum notandum, quod doctrina et praedicatio Ioannis commendatur a tribus: primo, a *temporis opportunitate;* secundo vero, ab *officii auctoritate,* ibi[^p69-11]: *Factum est verbum Domini* etc.; tertio vero, a *zeli severitate,* ibi: *Dicebat ergo ad*
<!-- page 70 -->
*turbas* etc. Primum respicit quod est *extra,* secundum, quod *supra,* tertium, quod est *intra.* Et sic per omnem modum est eius praedicatio et doctrina perfecta.

#### Praedicatio Ioannis primo commendatur quoad opportunitatem temporis tripliciter.

Quantum ad primum notandum, quod *opportunitas temporis* colligitur ex tribus, scilicet ex *unione imperii Romani,* ex *partitione regni Iudaici* et ex *divisione sacerdotii levitici;* ex quibus colligitur opportunitas temporis, quia illum annuntiabat Ioannes, qui synagogam Iudaeorum dispergebat et gentium varietates in unitatem Ecclesiae congregabat[^p70-1].

2. (Vers. 1.). Quantum ad *unionem imperii* dicitur: *Anno quintodecimo imperii Tiberii Caesaris,* in quo scilicet iam erat tempus adventus Christi, quia scilicet ante Tiberium fuit Iulius et Pompeius; et Iulius contrivit Pompeium, et post hos successit Tiberius. Et ita iam verificatum erat illud Danielis secundo[^p70-2] de quarto regno, scilicet Romanorum, quod « fuit ex parte solidum et ex parte contritum »; et ideo erat tempus verificationis illius quod sequitur ibidem: « In diebus illis suscitabit Deus caeli regnum, quod in aeternum non dissipabitur, et potestas eius alteri populo non tradetur ».

3. Quantum autem ad *partitionem regni Iudaici* subditur: *Procurante Pontio Pilato Iudaeam,* sub quo scilicet Dominus est crucifixus, secundum quod narrat historia omnium Evangelistarum; *tetrarcha autem Galilaeae Herode;* iste fuit sub quo etiam Dominus crucifixus est, infra vigesimo tertio[^p70-3], et qui interfecit Ioannem Baptistam, secundum quod dicitur Matthaei decimo quarto, quia dicebat ei: « Non licet tibi habere uxorem Philippi, fratris tui »; de quo Philippo additur: *Philippo autem, fratre eius, tetrarcha Ituraeae et Trachonitidis regionis.* Et ita iam verificatum erat illud Isaiae primo[^p70-4]: « Regionem vestram coram vobis alieni devorant »; et Proverbiorum vigesimo octavo: « Propter peccata terrae multi principes eius ». Et ideo subditur: *Et Lysania Abilinae tetrarcha,* id est alterius regionis, quae erat quarta pars regni. Tetrarchia enim dicitur quarta pars regni; dicitur enim a *tetra,* quod est quatuor, et *archos,* quod est principatus[^p70-5]. Archelao enim, filio Herodis magni, propter superbiam suam in exilium apud Lugdunum deportato, regnum in quatuor tetrarchias est divisum, ut sic minueretur superbia Iudaeorum. Unde Beda[^p70-6]: « Tres tetrarchias regebant tres filii magni Herodis, sub quo Dominus natus est. Pilatus vero procurator erat alterius tetrarchiae ». Et nota, quod fuerunt duo Lysaniae, unus filius Hircani, de quo Iosephus[^p70-7]; et alter Herodis, de quo Beda loquitur.

4. (Vers. 2.). Quantum autem ad *divisionem sacerdotii* subditur: *Sub principibus sacerdotum Anna et Caipha,* qui, ambitione moti, ut dicit Beda[^p70-8], summum sacerdotium emebant a Romanis. Unde quia iam secundum Bedam et Iosephum sacerdotes non instituebantur per unctionem, sed per terrenam potestatem; ideo cessaverat unctio sacerdotalis et unctio regalis. Iam ergo tempus advenerat, ut adveniret Christus, secundum illud Genesis penultimo[^p70-9]: « Non auferetur sceptrum de Iuda » etc. — Item iam cessaverat unctio sacerdotii levitici, et ideo tempus erat, ut veniret « sacerdos secundum ordinem Melchisedech[^p70-10] », ut impleretur illud Isaiae vigesimo
<!-- page 71 -->
secundo, quod dictum est de sacerdotio levitico et sacerdotio Christi: « Expellam te de statione tua et de ministerio tuo deponam te »; et post de Christo sub figura: « Et erit in die illa, vocabo servum meum Eliacim et induam illum tunica tua »; et infra: « Et ponam clavem David super humerum eius ». In quo ostenditur, quod iste Eliacim debet esse *rex* et *sacerdos,* quod solum in Christo est impletum, qui fuit « sacerdos secundum ordinem Melchisedech[^p71-1] », qui simul rex et sacerdos fuit, secundum illud ad Hebraeos septimo. Et quia « translato sacerdotio, necesse est, ut et Legis translatio fiat »; iam erat tempus Evangelii praedicandi; et in tali tempore a praedicatione Ioannis debuit Evangelium inchoari ac praedicari.

5. Patet igitur, quam sufficienter secundum Scripturas ostendit Evangelista opportunitatem in adventu Christi et praedicatione praecursoris, quod Iudaei caeci non viderunt. Ideo Ieremiae octavo[^p71-2]: « Milvus in caelo cognovit tempus suum, turtur et hirundo et ciconia custodierunt tempus adventus sui; populus autem meus non cognovit iudicium Domini »; et infra duodecimo: « Hypocritae, faciem caeli et terrae probare nostis, hoc autem tempus quomodo non probatis »? — Poterant autem hoc tempus cognoscere non solum per Scripturas, sed etiam per ipsas res in temporibus gestas, secundum quod dicit Gregorius[^p71-3]: « Liquet, quod ad finem Iudaea venerat, quae tot regibus divisa subiacebat ». Et cum iam subiecti essent alieno imperio, iam tempus erat, ut Dominus regnum Israeliticum relevaret, quod promiserat omni modo stabilire in sempiternum. — Patet etiam, quam rationabiliter Evangelista, licet de sacerdotio Christi intendat, tamen regum et sacerdotum tempus enuntiat, ut ostendat, *Christi* sacerdotium simul et regnum esse *coniunctum;* et per utrumque probatur tempus adventus eius; et simul regnum *Iudaeorum et gentium,* quia utrumque simul venerat copulare in unum; item hoc divisum, et illud unum, quia gentium plenitudo debebat intrare et Iudaeorum multitudo dispergi propter peccatum incredulitatis[^p71-4].

#### Praedicatio Ioannis secundo commendatur ab officii auctoritate dupliciter.

6. *Factum est verbum* etc. Hic commendatur Ioannis praedicatio ab *officii auctoritate,* quam primo commendat Evangelista ex *se ipsa;* secundo vero, ex *attestatione prophetica,* ibi[^p71-5]: *Sicut scriptum est* etc. — Quantum ad primum notandum, quod auctoritas Ioanni commissa commendatur a tribus, scilicet a *dono caelestis gratiae,* a *ministerio humilis obedientiae* et a *signo sacramentalis medicinae.*

7. Quantum ad *donum caelestis gratiae* dicitur: *Factum est verbum Domini super Ioannem, Zachariae filium, in deserto. Factum est,* scilicet per inspirationem supernam, et ideo dicitur: *Super Ioannem;* quia, secundae Petri primo[^p71-6], « Spiritu sancto inspirati, locuti sunt sancti Dei homines ». Unde *super* significat auctoritatem, secundum illud Isaiae sexagesimo primo: « Spiritus Domini super me, ad annuntiandum mansuetis misit me ». Et ad hoc erat *dignus* ratione *doni divini muneris;* ideo dicitur: *Super Ioannem,* « in quo scilicet est *gratia* », quia Sapientiae septimo[^p71-7] dicitur, quod « sapientia in animas sanctas se transfert ». — *Dignus* etiam ex *merito patris,* et ideo dicitur: *Zachariae filium;* unde poterat sibi dici illud Tobiae septimo[^p71-8]: « Benedictio sit tibi, fili mi, quia boni et optimi viri es filius ». — *Dignus* etiam ex *merito propriae virtutis;* unde dicitur: *In deserto,* in loco aspero et duro; Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo[^p71-9]: « Invenit eum in terra deserta, in loco horroris » etc.; et Osee decimo tertio: « Ego te cognovi in deserto, in terra solitudinis »; in qua terra consuevit fieri verbum Dei ad hominem, sicut ad Moysen; Exodi tertio: « Vocavit eum de medio rubi » etc., quando « minavit gregem ad interiora deserti »; Osee secundo: « Adducam eam in solitudinem, et ibi loquar ad cor eius ».

8. (Vers. 3.). Quantum ad *ministerium humilis obedientiae* subditur: *Et venit in omnem regionem Iordanis.* Unde Chrysostomus[^p71-10]: « Venit Ioannes quasi magni regis praeparator et nuntius
<!-- page 72 -->
fidelis ». Et tunc impletum est illud Malachiae tertio: « Ecce, ego mitto Angelum meum, et praeparabit viam tuam ». Et signanter dicit: *In omnem regionem,* ut significetur vera obedientia, quae nihil excipit, sed ad omnia est prompta; Ieremiae primo[^p72-1]: « Ad omnia, quae mittam te, ibis » etc.

9. Quantum autem ad *signum sacramentalis medicinae* subditur: *Praedicans baptismum poenitentiae,* sicut dicitur Actuum decimo nono[^p72-2]: « Ioannes baptizavit baptismo poenitentiae populum ». *Baptismum poenitentiae* vocat, quia ad poenitentiam informabat; Matthaei tertio: « Exibat ad eum Ierosolyma et omnis Iudaea, et baptizabantur ab eo in Iordane, confitentes peccata sua ». Sed licet ad poenitentiam informaret, tamen remissionem peccatorum non faciebat, sed ad baptismum illum praeparabat. Et ideo subditur: *In remissionem peccatorum,* id est in baptismum Christi, in quo est remissio; unde Actuum secundo[^p72-3]: « Baptizetur unusquisque vestrum in nomine Domini nostri Iesu Christi in remissionem peccatorum ». Et de hoc dicebat Zacharias decimo tertio: « Erit fons patens domui David et habitantibus Ierusalem in ablutionem peccatoris et menstruatae »; et Ezechiel trigesimo sexto: « Effundam super vos aquam mundam, et mundabimini ab omnibus inquinamentis vestris » etc.

10. (Vers. 4.). *Sicut scriptum est* etc. Hic commendatur auctoritas Ioannis ex *attestatione prophetica,* in qua Ioannes praedicans ostenditur habere officium *excitandi, commonendi* et *consolandi.* — Ostenditur, inquam, primo habere officium *excitandi per exclamationem;* et ideo dicitur: *Sicut scriptum est in libro sermonum Isaiae prophetae: Vox clamantis in deserto;* hoc enim scribitur Isaiae quadragesimo[^p72-4]. Dicitur autem Ioannes *vox,* quia, sicut vox verbum praeit, sic Ioannes Christum; et *vox clamantis,* quia excitat dormientes, ut ad lucem evigilent, secundum illud Isaiae quinquagesimo octavo[^p72-5]: « Clama, ne cesses, quasi tuba exalta vocem tuam »; et Iob trigesimo septimo: « Tonabit Dominus voce sua mirabiliter », « et non investigabitur, cum audita fuerit vox eius ». Haec est vox, de qua in Psalmo: « Vox Domini super aquas, Deus maiestatis intonuit ». Unde Ioannes, Ioannis primo, respondit quaerentibus: « Ego vox clamantis in deserto ». *Vox* igitur est signum expressivum verbi; unde prophetiae aliorum fuerunt quasi *nutus* respectu Christi, sed Ioannis fuit quasi *vox* respectu Verbi, quia ipsum visibiliter ostendit[^p72-6].

11. Ostenditur etiam secundo habere officium *commonendi per excitationem,* cum subditur: *Parate viam Domini,* per observantiam scilicet *mandatorum;* Psalmus[^p72-7]: « Viam mandatorum tuorum cucurri »; et Baruch tertio: « Si in via Dei ambulasses, habitasses utique in pace super terram ». *Rectas facite semitas eius,* per observantiam *consiliorum;* Proverbiorum quarto[^p72-8]: « Viam sapientiae monstravi tibi et duxi te per semitas aequitatis, quas cum ingressus fueris, non arctabuntur gressus tui, et currens non habebis offendiculum »; Isaiae vigesimo sexto: « Semita iusti recta, rectus callis iusti ad ambulandum » etc.; Psalmus: « Dirige me in semitam mandatorum tuorum » etc. — *Rectas facite,* scilicet *intentiones;* Iob primo[^p72-9]: « Et erat vir ille simplex et rectus ac timens Deum et recedens a malo ». — *Cogitationes;* Psalmus: « Innocentes et recti adhaeserunt mihi »; et Ecclesiastae septimo: « Hoc inveni, quod fecerit Deus hominem rectum ». — *Affectiones;* Canticorum primo: « Recti diligunt te »; et Psalmus: « Quam bonus Israel Deus his qui recto sunt corde »! — *Locutiones;* Proverbiorum octavo[^p72-10]: « Iusti sunt omnes sermones mei, recti sunt intelligentibus ». — *Actiones;* Proverbiorum vigesimo: « Ex studiis suis intelligitur puer, si munda et recta sint opera eius »; Iob octavo: « Si mundus et rectus incesseris, statim evigilabit ad te ». — *Passiones;* Iob quarto[^p72-11]: « Recordare, obsecro te, quis unquam innocens periit? Aut quando recti deleti sunt »? — *Retributiones;* Proverbiorum undecimo: « Iustitia rectorum liberabit eos ». Est enim iustitia secundum Tullium[^p72-12] « constans et perpetua voluntas, ius suum unicuique tribuens ». — Qui per has recti-
<!-- page 73 -->
tudines incedunt intelliguntur per illa animalia, de quibus Ezechielis primo: « Pedes eorum recti » etc.

12. (Vers. 5.). Ostenditur etiam nihilominus tertio habere officium *consolandi per promissionem,* cum subditur: *Omnis vallis* etc.; in quo praedicit effectum adventus Christi respectu *humilium iustorum,* respectu *peccatorum* et respectu *universorum.* — Quia *exaltabit humiles;* propter quod dicitur: *Omnis vallis implebitur,* id est humilis; Psalmus[^p73-1]: « Valles abundabunt frumento »; supra primo: « Exaltavit humiles »; infra decimo octavo: « Omnis, qui se humiliat, exaltabitur ». Huius figura, quarti Regum quarto, ubi Eliseus implevit vasa vacua. — *Humiliabit superbientes;* propter quod subditur: *Et omnis mons et collis humiliabitur;* Isaiae quadragesimo quinto[^p73-2]: « Ego ante te ibo et gloriosos terrae humiliabo »; Iob quadragesimo: « Respiciens omnem arrogantem humilia ». — *Rectificabit deviantes;* propter quod addit: *Et erunt prava in directa;* Beda[^p73-3]: Id est malorum corda per iniustitiam distorta erunt in directa, id est, ad regulam iustitiae dirigentur; Proverbiorum decimo sexto: « Revela Domino opera tua, et dirigentur cogitationes tuae ». Hoc autem est contra hypocritas, qui a Domino abscondunt consilia sua; secundae ad Thessalonicenses tertio: « Dominus autem dirigat corda vestra » etc. — *Mitigabit tumultuantes;* propter quod dicit: *Et aspera in vias planas,* id est, mentes iracundae in mansuetudinem convertentur; Isaiae undecimo[^p73-4]: « Habitabit lupus cum agno, et pardus cum haedo accubabit; vitulus et leo et ovis simul morabuntur ».

13. (Vers. 6.). *Illuminabit omnes accedentes;* propter quod adiungit: *Et videbit omnis caro salutare Dei,* id est de omni carne, ut fiat distributio pro generibus singulorum, secundum illud Psalmi[^p73-5]: « Viderunt omnes fines terrae salutare Dei nostri »; Isaiae quadragesimo post verba praemissa subditur: « Et videbit omnis caro pariter, quod os Domini locutum est ». Sed quinquagesimo secundo dicitur: « Videbunt omnes fines terrae salutare Dei nostri ». Et nota, quod idem ipse, qui est *os* vel *verbum* Patris secundum Deitatem, est *salutare* secundum humanitatem[^p73-6].

#### Praedicatio Ioannis commendatur a zeli severitate.

14. *Dicebat ergo ad turbas* etc. Hic commendatur praedicatio Ioannis a *zeli severitate.* Et quia severitati debet adiungi pietas, ideo praemittitur *increpatio;* secundo vero adiungitur *exhortatio,* ibi[^p73-7]: *Facite ergo dignos fructus poenitentiae;* tertio vero subinfertur *comminatio,* ibi: *Iam enim securis ad radicem* etc. *Increpatio* est culpae, *admonitio* poenitentiae, sed *comminatio* poenalis vindictae. — *Increpatio* autem est de duobus, scilicet de *certa malitia culpae* et de *ignorantia* sive inconsideratione *poenae.*

15. (Vers. 7.). *Increpat* igitur de *certa malitia culpae* sub metaphora viperae, cum dicitur: *Dicebat ergo ad turbas, quae exibant, ut baptizarentur ab eo: Genimina viperarum* etc. Et nota, quod viperis eos comparat propter triplicem proprietatem: « Vipera enim, cum momorderit hominem, statim currit ad aquam; sic isti, peccata mortifera confitentes, currebant ad baptismum[^p73-8] ». Quocontra Ecclesiastici trigesimo quarto: « Qui baptizatur a mortuo et iterum tangit mortuum, quid proficit lavatio illius »? Item, genimina viperarum rumpendo viscera materna nascuntur; sic isti persequendo Prophetas materna viscera violabant; Matthaei vigesimo tertio[^p73-9]: « Testes estis vobis ipsis, quia filii estis eorum qui Prophetas occiderunt ». « Serpentes, genimina viperarum, quomodo fugietis a iudicio gehen-
<!-- page 74 -->
nae »? « Item, a foris sunt speciosae et quasi pictae, sed intus veneno repletae[^p74-1] »; sic et isti; Matthaei vigesimo tertio: « Similes estis sepulcris dealbatis, quae a foris apparent hominibus speciosa, intus vero plena sunt ossibus mortuorum ».

16. Increpat etiam de *inconsideratione poenae,* cum addit: *Quis ostendit vobis fugere a ventura ira?* Gregorius[^p74-2]: « Ventura ira est animadversio ultionis extremae, quam tunc peccator fugere non valet, qui nunc ad lamenta poenitentiae non recurrit »; primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto: « Tunc repentinus eis superveniet interitus, sicut dolor in utero habentis, et non effugient ». Item, Apocalypsis sexto: « Dicent montibus: Cadite super nos, et collibus: Operite nos a facie sedentis super thronum et ab ira Agni »; Ieremiae vigesimo quinto: « Facta est terra eorum in desolationem a facie irae columbae et a facie irae furoris Domini »; propterea Iob decimo nono[^p74-3]: « Fugite a facie gladii »; Psalmus: « Quo ibo a spiritu tuo, et quo a facie tua fugiam »? Non « in Tharsis » cum Iona, Ionae primo — Tharsis enim interpretatur exploratio gaudii — sed cum Propheta in deserto poenitentiae; Psalmus: « Elongavi fugiens et mansi in solitudine »; Matthaei vigesimo quarto: « Tunc qui in Iudaea sunt fugiant ad montes »; « Orate autem, ut non fiat fuga vestra in hieme, vel Sabbato ».

17. (Vers. 8.). *Facite ergo fructus dignos poenitentiae* etc. Hic ponitur *exhortatio* de duobus, scilicet de *humilitate satisfactionis* et de *cautela praesumtionis.* — Quantum ad *humilitatem satisfactionis* dicitur: *Facite ergo fructus dignos poenitentiae,* ut sit satisfactio condigna; ad Romanos sexto[^p74-4]: « Sicut exhibuistis membra vestra servire immunditiae et iniquitati ad iniquitatem; ita nunc exhibete membra vestra servire iustitiae in sanctificationem »; quia dicitur Deuteronomii vigesimo quinto: « Pro mensura peccati erit et plagarum modus ». Unde Gregorius[^p74-5]: « Non est par fructus boni operis eius qui parum aut nihil peccavit, et eius qui graviter cecidit. Necesse est enim, ut tanto maiora sibi quis inferat lamenta per poenitentiam, quanto graviora sibi intulit damna per culpam ».

18. Quantum autem ad *cautelam praesumtionis* subditur: *Et ne coeperitis dicere: Patrem habemus Abraham,* secundum illud Ioannis octavo[^p74-6]: « Semen Abrahae sumus, et nemini servivimus unquam »; ad Romanos nono: « Non omnes, qui sunt semen Abrahae, hi sunt filii Abrahae », nisi etiam patrem imitentur; Ioannis octavo: « Si filii Abrahae estis, opera Abrahae facite »; Isaiae quinquagesimo primo: « Attendite ad Abraham, patrem vestrum, et ad Saram, quae peperit vos ».

19. Et rationem reddit, cum addit: *Dico enim vobis, quia potens est Deus de lapidibus istis suscitare filios Abrahae.* Ad litteram *potens,* quia, Sapientiae duodecimo[^p74-7], « subest tibi, cum volueris, posse ». Vel *de lapidibus,* id est de gentibus. Gregorius[^p74-8]: « Lapides recte gentes dicuntur, quae lapides colunt », iuxta illud Psalmi: « Similes illis fiant, qui faciunt ea ». Ambrosius: « Comparat gentes lapidibus, quia deformes ad usum, nudi ad ornatum, steriles ad fructum, irrationales ad profectum ». Et ex his *suscitati sunt filii Abrahae;* ad Galatas tertio[^p74-9]: « Si autem vos Christi, ergo semen Abrahae estis »; ad Romanos nono: « Non qui filii carnis, hi filii Dei; sed qui filii sunt promissionis aestimantur in semine ». Tales sunt imitantes Abraham per fidem; Ioannis primo[^p74-10]: « Dedit eis potestatem filios Dei fieri ». Et ideo propter fidem centurionis gentilis dicitur Matthaei octavo: « Multi ab oriente et occidente venient et recumbent cum Abraham et Isaac et Iacob in regno caelorum ».

20. (Vers. 9.). *Iam enim securis ad radicem arboris posita est.* Hic tertio subditur *comminatio,* quae duplex est, scilicet *de praesenti* et *de futuro.* — Quantum ad comminationem *de praesenti* dicitur: *Iam enim securis ad radicem arboris posita est.* Unde Glossa[^p74-11]: « Securis est severitas iudiciaria ad penitus exstirpandas infructuosas arbores, in quantum infructuosae sunt »; de quibus dicitur in Canonica Iudae: « Arbores autumnales, infructuosae, bis mortuae ». Et tales designati sunt per illam ficum, quam arefecit Dominus, Matthaei vigesimo primo[^p74-12], ubi dicit, quod « maledixit ficui, et continuo aruit »; et ratio huius fuit, quia non invenit in ea fructum. Et
<!-- page 75 -->
tamen dicit alius Evangelista, scilicet Marcus undecimo, quod « non erat tempus ficorum »; in quo insinuat, quod omni tempore requirit Deus fructum a nobis; ad Galatas ultimo: « Bonum autem facientes, non deficiamus; tempore enim suo » etc.

21. Quantum autem ad comminationem *de futuro* additur: *Omnis arbor non faciens fructum bonum,* id est omnis homo inutilis sine acceptione personae; Proverbiorum sexto[^p75-1]: « Zelus et furor viri non parcet in die vindictae nec acquiescet » etc. *Excidetur,* per mortem; infra decimo tertio: « Succide eam, ut quid etiam terram occupat »? Et Danielis quarto: « Succidite arborem et praescindite ramos eius, excutite folia eius et dispergite fructus eius ». Arbor ergo succiditur non solum propter defectum *fructus,* sed etiam *boni fructus.* — *Et in ignem mittetur,* scilicet per aeternam damnationem; Ioannis decimo quinto[^p75-2] dicitur de palmite infructuoso: « Colligent eum et in ignem mittent » etc.; Matthaei vigesimo quinto: « Discedite a me, maledicti, in ignem aeternum »; et Isaiae ultimo: « Vermis eorum non morietur, et ignis eorum non exstinguetur ».

### Chapter III.

#### *Thirdly, on the nativity outside the womb, in three ways.*

1. *In the fifteenth*[^p69-8] *year* etc. Above the Evangelist treated of the mystery of the incarnation of Christ as to the nativity in the womb and from the womb; here he treats of him as to the *nativity outside the womb,* according to that saying of Ambrose[^p69-9], who says that he was reborn by the Sacraments, namely when he was baptized. And this part has three, in the first of which he treats of the baptism and preaching of John *in general, with respect to the whole people;* but in the second, *in special, with respect to special persons,* there[^p69-10]: *And the multitudes asked him* etc.; in the third, *in the singular, with respect to Christ,* there: *And it came to pass, when he was baptized.* And the order is clear, because the general directs to the special, the special to the singular.

#### First, on the baptism and preaching of John in general, as to three things.

As to the first it is to be noted that the doctrine and preaching of John is commended from three things: first, from the *opportuneness of the time;* second, from the *authority of the office,* there[^p69-11]: *The word of the Lord came* etc.; but third, from the *severity of zeal,* there: *He said therefore to the*
<!-- page 70 -->
*multitudes* etc. The first regards what is *without,* the second what is *above,* the third what is *within.* And so in every way his preaching and doctrine is perfect.

#### The preaching of John is commended first as to the opportuneness of the time, in three ways.

As to the first it is to be noted that the *opportuneness of the time* is gathered from three things, namely from the *union of the Roman empire,* from the *partition of the Jewish kingdom* and from the *division of the levitical priesthood;* from which the opportuneness of the time is gathered, because John was announcing him who was scattering the synagogue of the Jews and gathering the varieties of the Gentiles into the unity of the Church[^p70-1].

2. (Vers. 1.). As to the *union of the empire* it is said: *In the fifteenth year of the empire of Tiberius Caesar,* in which, namely, it was already the time of the coming of Christ, since, namely, before Tiberius there were Julius and Pompey; and Julius crushed Pompey, and after these Tiberius succeeded. And so already that saying of Daniel, the second chapter[^p70-2], had been verified, concerning the fourth kingdom, namely that of the Romans, that it « was partly solid and partly broken »; and therefore it was the time of the verification of what follows in the same place: « In those days the God of heaven will set up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed, and its power shall not be delivered up to another people ».

3. But as to the *partition of the Jewish kingdom* it is added: *Pontius Pilate being procurator of Judea,* under whom, namely, the Lord was crucified, as the history of all the Evangelists narrates; *and Herod being tetrarch of Galilee;* this is he under whom too the Lord was crucified, below, in the twenty-third chapter[^p70-3], and who killed John the Baptist, as is said in Matthew, the fourteenth chapter, because he said to him: « It is not lawful for thee to have the wife of Philip, thy brother »; of which Philip it is added: *And Philip his brother tetrarch of Iturea and the country of Trachonitis.* And so already that saying of Isaias, the first chapter[^p70-4], had been verified: « Your country strangers devour before you »; and of Proverbs, the twenty-eighth chapter: « For the sins of the land many are the princes thereof ». And therefore it is added: *And Lysanias tetrarch of Abilina,* that is, of another region, which was the fourth part of the kingdom. For a tetrarchy is called the fourth part of a kingdom; for it is named from *tetra,* which is four, and *archos,* which is principality[^p70-5]. For when Archelaus, the son of Herod the great, had been deported into exile at Lyons on account of his pride, the kingdom was divided into four tetrarchies, that so the pride of the Jews might be diminished. Hence Bede[^p70-6]: « Three sons of the great Herod, under whom the Lord was born, ruled three tetrarchies. But Pilate was procurator of the other tetrarchy ». And note that there were two Lysaniases, one the son of Hircanus, of whom Josephus[^p70-7]; and the other Herod's, of whom Bede speaks.

4. (Vers. 2.). But as to the *division of the priesthood* it is added: *Under the high priests Annas and Caiphas,* who, moved by ambition, as Bede says[^p70-8], were buying the high priesthood from the Romans. Hence, since already, according to Bede and Josephus, priests were not instituted by anointing but by earthly power, therefore the priestly anointing and the royal anointing had ceased. Therefore the time had now come for Christ to come, according to that saying of Genesis, the next to last chapter[^p70-9]: « The sceptre shall not be taken away from Juda » etc. — Likewise the anointing of the levitical priesthood had already ceased, and therefore it was time that « a priest according to the order of Melchisedech[^p70-10] » should come, that that saying of Isaias, the twenty-
<!-- page 71 -->
second chapter, might be fulfilled, which was said of the levitical priesthood and of the priesthood of Christ: « I will drive thee out from thy station, and depose thee from thy ministry »; and afterwards, of Christ under a figure: « And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Eliacim, and I will clothe him with thy robe »; and below: « And I will lay the key of David upon his shoulder ». In which it is shown that this Eliacim must be *king* and *priest,* which has been fulfilled in Christ alone, who was « a priest according to the order of Melchisedech[^p71-1] », who was at once king and priest, according to that saying to the Hebrews, the seventh chapter. And since, « the priesthood being translated, it is necessary that a translation also be made of the Law », it was already the time of preaching the Gospel; and at such a time the Gospel had to be begun and preached from the preaching of John.

5. It is clear, therefore, how sufficiently, according to the Scriptures, the Evangelist shows the opportuneness in the coming of Christ and the preaching of the precursor, which the blind Jews did not see. Therefore Jeremias, the eighth chapter[^p71-2]: « The kite in the air hath known her time: the turtle, and the swallow, and the stork have observed the time of their coming: but my people have not known the judgment of the Lord »; and below, in the twelfth chapter: « Hypocrites, you know how to discern the face of the heaven and of the earth; but how do you not discern this time »? — But they could know this time not only through the Scriptures but also through the very things done in the times, as Gregory says[^p71-3]: « It is plain that Judea had come to its end, which lay subject, divided among so many kings ». And since they were already subject to a foreign empire, it was already time that the Lord should raise up again the kingdom of Israel, which he had promised to establish in every way for ever. — It is clear too how reasonably the Evangelist, although he intends the priesthood of Christ, yet states the time of kings and of priests, to show that the priesthood and the kingdom of *Christ* are at once *conjoined;* and by both the time of his coming is proved; and at once the kingdom *of the Jews and of the Gentiles,* because he had come to couple both together into one; likewise this one divided, and that one one, because the fulness of the Gentiles was to come in and the multitude of the Jews to be scattered on account of the sin of unbelief[^p71-4].

#### The preaching of John is commended secondly from the authority of the office, in two ways.

6. *The word came* etc. Here the preaching of John is commended from the *authority of the office,* which the Evangelist commends first from *itself;* but second, from the *prophetic attestation,* there[^p71-5]: *As it was written* etc. — As to the first it is to be noted that the authority committed to John is commended from three things, namely from the *gift of heavenly grace,* from the *ministry of humble obedience* and from the *sign of sacramental medicine.*

7. As to the *gift of heavenly grace* it is said: *The word of the Lord came upon John, the son of Zachary, in the desert. Came,* namely by inspiration from above, and therefore it is said: *Upon John;* because, the second of Peter, the first chapter[^p71-6], « inspired by the Holy Spirit, holy men of God spoke ». Hence *upon* signifies authority, according to that saying of Isaias, the sixty-first chapter: « The spirit of the Lord is upon me, he hath sent me to announce to the meek ». And for this he was *worthy* by reason of the *gift of the divine bounty;* therefore it is said: *Upon John,* « in whom, namely, is *grace* », because in Wisdom, the seventh chapter[^p71-7], it is said that « wisdom conveyeth herself into holy souls ». — *Worthy* also from the *merit of his father,* and therefore it is said: *The son of Zachary;* whence that saying of Tobias, the seventh chapter[^p71-8], could be said to him: « Blessing be to thee, my son, because thou art the son of a good and most virtuous man ». — *Worthy* also from the *merit of his own virtue;* whence it is said: *In the desert,* in a rough and hard place; Deuteronomy, the thirty-second chapter[^p71-9]: « He found him in a desert land, in a place of horror » etc.; and Osee, the thirteenth chapter: « I knew thee in the desert, in a land of wilderness »; in which land the word of God was wont to come to man, as to Moses; Exodus, the third chapter: « He called him out of the midst of the bush » etc., when « he drove the flock to the inner parts of the desert »; Osee, the second chapter: « I will lead her into the wilderness, and there I will speak to her heart ».

8. (Vers. 3.). As to the *ministry of humble obedience* it is added: *And he came into all the country about the Jordan.* Hence Chrysostom[^p71-10]: « John came as the preparer and faithful messenger
<!-- page 72 -->
of a great king ». And then that saying of Malachias, the third chapter, was fulfilled: « Behold, I send my angel, and he shall prepare thy way ». And he says pointedly: *Into all the country,* to signify true obedience, which excepts nothing but is ready for all things; Jeremias, the first chapter[^p72-1]: « To all things whereto I shall send thee, thou shalt go » etc.

9. But as to the *sign of sacramental medicine* it is added: *Preaching the baptism of penance,* as is said in Acts, the nineteenth chapter[^p72-2]: « John baptized the people with the baptism of penance ». He calls it the *baptism of penance* because it formed them to penance; Matthew, the third chapter: « Then went out to him Jerusalem and all Judea, and were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins ». But although it formed them to penance, yet it did not effect the remission of sins, but prepared for that baptism. And therefore it is added: *For the remission of sins,* that is, for the baptism of Christ, in which is remission; hence Acts, the second chapter[^p72-3]: « Be baptized every one of you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, for the remission of sins ». And of this Zacharias said, in the thirteenth chapter: « There shall be a fountain open to the house of David, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, for the washing of the sinner, and of the unclean woman »; and Ezechiel, in the thirty-sixth chapter: « I will pour upon you clean water, and you shall be cleansed from all your filthiness » etc.

10. (Vers. 4.). *As it was written* etc. Here the authority of John is commended from the *prophetic attestation,* in which John preaching is shown to have the office *of rousing, of reminding* and *of consoling.* — He is shown, I say, first to have the office *of rousing by crying out;* and therefore it is said: *As it was written in the book of the sayings of Isaias the prophet: A voice of one crying in the wilderness;* for this is written in Isaias, the fortieth chapter[^p72-4]. Now John is called a *voice* because, as the voice goes before the word, so John goes before Christ; and *the voice of one crying,* because he rouses the sleeping, that they may awaken to the light, according to that saying of Isaias, the fifty-eighth chapter[^p72-5]: « Cry, cease not, lift up thy voice like a trumpet »; and Job, the thirty-seventh chapter: « The Lord shall thunder marvellously with his voice », « and it shall not be searched out, when his voice shall be heard ». This is the voice of which it is said in the Psalm: « The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of majesty hath thundered ». Hence John, in John, the first chapter, answered those who asked: « I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness ». The *voice,* then, is a sign expressive of the word; hence the prophecies of the others were as a *nod* with respect to Christ, but John's was as a *voice* with respect to the Word, because he showed him visibly[^p72-6].

11. He is shown also, second, to have the office *of reminding by rousing,* when it is added: *Prepare ye the way of the Lord,* namely by the observance of the *commandments;* the Psalm[^p72-7]: « I have run the way of thy commandments »; and Baruch, the third chapter: « If thou hadst walked in the way of God, thou hadst surely dwelt in peace upon the earth ». *Make straight his paths,* by the observance of the *counsels;* Proverbs, the fourth chapter[^p72-8]: « I have shewn thee the way of wisdom, I have led thee by the paths of equity, which when thou shalt have entered, thy steps shall not be straitened, and when thou runnest thou shalt not have a stumblingblock »; Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter: « The path of the just is right, right is the way of the just to walk in » etc.; the Psalm: « Direct me in the path of thy commandments » etc. — *Make straight,* namely the *intentions;* Job, the first chapter[^p72-9]: « And that man was simple and upright, and fearing God, and departing from evil ». — The *thoughts;* the Psalm: « The innocent and the upright have adhered to me »; and Ecclesiastes, the seventh chapter: « This I have found, that God made man right ». — The *affections;* the Canticle, the first chapter: « The upright love thee »; and the Psalm: « How good is God to Israel, to them that are of a right heart »! — The *utterances;* Proverbs, the eighth chapter[^p72-10]: « All my words are just, they are right to them that understand ». — The *actions;* Proverbs, the twentieth chapter: « By his inclinations a child is known, if his works be clean and right »; Job, the eighth chapter: « If thou walk clean and upright, he will presently awake unto thee ». — The *sufferings;* Job, the fourth chapter[^p72-11]: « Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished being innocent? Or when were the upright destroyed »? — The *retributions;* Proverbs, the eleventh chapter: « The justice of the upright shall deliver them ». For justice, according to Tully[^p72-12], is « the constant and perpetual will, giving to each his own right ». — Those who walk by these upright-
<!-- page 73 -->
nesses are understood by those living creatures of which Ezechiel, the first chapter: « Their feet were straight » etc.

12. (Vers. 5.). He is shown also, nonetheless, third, to have the office *of consoling by promise,* when it is added: *Every valley* etc.; in which he foretells the effect of the coming of Christ with respect to the *humble just,* with respect to *sinners* and with respect to *all.* — For *he will exalt the humble;* for which reason it is said: *Every valley shall be filled,* that is, the humble; the Psalm[^p73-1]: « The vales shall abound with corn »; above, in the first chapter: « He hath exalted the humble »; below, in the eighteenth chapter: « Every one that humbleth himself shall be exalted ». The figure of this is in the fourth of Kings, the fourth chapter, where Eliseus filled the empty vessels. — *He will humble the proud;* for which reason it is added: *And every mountain and hill shall be humbled;* Isaias, the forty-fifth chapter[^p73-2]: « I will go before thee, and will humble the great ones of the earth »; Job, the fortieth chapter: « Looking upon every arrogant man, humble him ». — *He will set right those going astray;* for which reason he adds: *And the crooked shall be made straight;* Bede[^p73-3]: That is, the hearts of the wicked, twisted by injustice, shall be made straight, that is, they shall be directed to the rule of justice; Proverbs, the sixteenth chapter: « Lay open thy works to the Lord, and thy thoughts shall be directed ». But this is against the hypocrites, who hide their counsels from the Lord; the second to the Thessalonians, the third chapter: « And the Lord direct your hearts » etc. — *He will calm the turbulent;* for which reason he says: *And the rough ways plain,* that is, wrathful minds shall be turned into meekness; Isaias, the eleventh chapter[^p73-4]: « The wolf shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid; the calf and the lion and the sheep shall abide together ».

13. (Vers. 6.). *He will illumine all who approach;* for which reason he adds: *And all flesh shall see the salvation of God,* that is, from all flesh, so that it is a distribution for the kinds of individuals, according to that saying of the Psalm[^p73-5]: « All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God »; in Isaias, the fortieth chapter, after the words set down before, it is added: « And all flesh together shall see that the mouth of the Lord hath spoken ». But in the fifty-second chapter it is said: « All the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God ». And note that the same one, who is the *mouth* or *word* of the Father according to the Deity, is the *salvation* according to the humanity[^p73-6].

#### The preaching of John is commended from the severity of zeal.

14. *He said therefore to the multitudes* etc. Here the preaching of John is commended from the *severity of zeal.* And since piety ought to be joined to severity, therefore *rebuke* is set first; but second *exhortation* is joined, there[^p73-7]: *Bring forth therefore fruits worthy of penance;* but third *threat* is brought in after, there: *For now the axe is laid to the root* etc. *Rebuke* is of fault, *admonition* of penance, but *threat* of penal vengeance. — Now *rebuke* is about two things, namely about the *certain malice of fault* and about the *ignorance* or inconsiderateness *of punishment.*

15. (Vers. 7.). He *rebukes,* then, about the *certain malice of fault* under the metaphor of the viper, when it is said: *He said therefore to the multitudes that went forth to be baptized by him: Ye offspring of vipers* etc. And note that he compares them to vipers on account of a threefold property: « For the viper, when she has bitten a man, at once runs to water; so these, confessing deadly sins, ran to baptism[^p73-8] ». Against which, Ecclesiasticus, the thirty-fourth chapter: « He that is baptized after touching the dead, and toucheth the dead again, what doth his washing avail »? Likewise, the offspring of vipers are born by bursting their mother's womb; so these, by persecuting the Prophets, violated the mother's womb; Matthew, the twenty-third chapter[^p73-9]: « You are witnesses to yourselves that you are the sons of them that killed the Prophets ». « You serpents, offspring of vipers, how will you flee from the judgment of hell
<!-- page 74 -->
»? « Likewise, outwardly they are beautiful and as it were painted, but within full of poison[^p74-1] »; so these too; Matthew, the twenty-third chapter: « You are like whited sepulchres, which outwardly appear to men beautiful, but within are full of dead men's bones ».

16. He rebukes also about the *inconsiderateness of punishment,* when he adds: *Who hath shewed you to flee from the wrath to come?* Gregory[^p74-2]: « The wrath to come is the punishment of the final vengeance, which the sinner will then be unable to flee, who does not now run back to the laments of penance »; the first to the Thessalonians, the fifth chapter: « Then shall sudden destruction come upon them, as the pains upon her that is with child, and they shall not escape ». Likewise, the Apocalypse, the sixth chapter: « They shall say to the mountains: Fall upon us, and to the hills: Cover us from the face of him that sitteth upon the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb »; Jeremias, the twenty-fifth chapter: « Their land is made a desolation from the face of the wrath of the dove, and from the face of the wrath of the fury of the Lord »; therefore Job, the nineteenth chapter[^p74-3]: « Flee from the face of the sword »; the Psalm: « Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy face »? Not « into Tharsis » with Jonas, Jonas, the first chapter — for Tharsis is interpreted exploration of joy — but with the Prophet into the desert of penance; the Psalm: « Lo, I have gone far off fleeing, and I abode in the wilderness »; Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter: « Then they that are in Judea, let them flee to the mountains »; « But pray that your flight be not in the winter, or on the sabbath ».

17. (Vers. 8.). *Bring forth therefore fruits worthy of penance* etc. Here *exhortation* is set down about two things, namely about the *humility of satisfaction* and about the *caution against presumption.* — As to the *humility of satisfaction* it is said: *Bring forth therefore fruits worthy of penance,* that the satisfaction may be condign; to the Romans, the sixth chapter[^p74-4]: « As you have yielded your members to serve uncleanness and iniquity, unto iniquity; so now yield your members to serve justice, unto sanctification »; since it is said in Deuteronomy, the twenty-fifth chapter: « According to the measure of the sin shall the measure also of the stripes be ». Hence Gregory[^p74-5]: « The fruit of good work of him who has sinned little or nothing is not equal to that of him who has fallen gravely. For it is necessary that one inflict on himself laments through penance so much the greater, as he has inflicted on himself graver losses through fault ».

18. But as to the *caution against presumption* it is added: *And do not begin to say, We have Abraham for our father,* according to that saying of John, the eighth chapter[^p74-6]: « We are the seed of Abraham, and we have never served any man »; to the Romans, the ninth chapter: « Not all who are the seed of Abraham are the children of Abraham », unless they also imitate their father; John, the eighth chapter: « If you be the children of Abraham, do the works of Abraham »; Isaias, the fifty-first chapter: « Look unto Abraham your father, and to Sara that bore you ».

19. And he gives the reason, when he adds: *For I say unto you, that God is able of these stones to raise up children to Abraham.* According to the letter, *able,* because, Wisdom, the twelfth chapter[^p74-7], « thy power is at hand when thou wilt ». Or *of stones,* that is, of the Gentiles. Gregory[^p74-8]: « The Gentiles are rightly called stones, who worship stones », according to that saying of the Psalm: « Let them that make them become like unto them ». Ambrose: « He compares the Gentiles to stones, because they are misshapen for use, bare for adornment, barren for fruit, irrational for advance ». And from these *children of Abraham were raised up;* to the Galatians, the third chapter[^p74-9]: « And if you be Christ's, then are you the seed of Abraham »; to the Romans, the ninth chapter: « Not they that are the children of the flesh are the children of God; but they that are the children of the promise are accounted for the seed ». Such are those who imitate Abraham through faith; John, the first chapter[^p74-10]: « He gave them power to be made the sons of God ». And therefore on account of the faith of the Gentile centurion it is said in Matthew, the eighth chapter: « Many shall come from the east and the west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven ».

20. (Vers. 9.). *For now the axe is laid to the root of the tree.* Here third *threat* is added, which is twofold, namely *of the present* and *of the future.* — As to the threat *of the present* it is said: *For now the axe is laid to the root of the tree.* Hence the Gloss[^p74-11]: « The axe is the judicial severity for rooting out utterly the unfruitful trees, insofar as they are unfruitful »; of which it is said in the Canonical Epistle of Jude: « Trees of the autumn, unfruitful, twice dead ». And such were designated by that fig tree which the Lord withered, Matthew, the twenty-first chapter[^p74-12], where he says that « he cursed the fig tree, and immediately it withered »; and the reason of this was that he did not find fruit on it. And
<!-- page 75 -->
yet another Evangelist, namely Mark, in the eleventh chapter, says that « it was not the time for figs »; in which he intimates that at every time God requires fruit from us; to the Galatians, the last chapter: « And doing good, let us not fail; for in due time » etc.

21. But as to the threat *of the future* it is added: *Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit,* that is, every useless man, without respect of persons; Proverbs, the sixth chapter[^p75-1]: « The jealousy and rage of the man will not spare in the day of revenge, nor will he yield » etc. *Shall be cut down,* through death; below, in the thirteenth chapter: « Cut it down: why doth it even occupy the ground »? And Daniel, the fourth chapter: « Cut down the tree, and chop off the branches thereof; shake off its leaves, and scatter its fruits ». The tree, then, is cut down not only on account of the lack of *fruit,* but also of *good fruit.* — *And cast into the fire,* namely through eternal damnation; in John, the fifteenth chapter[^p75-2], it is said of the unfruitful branch: « They shall gather him up, and cast him into the fire » etc.; Matthew, the twenty-fifth chapter: « Depart from me, you cursed, into everlasting fire »; and Isaias, the last chapter: « Their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched ».
