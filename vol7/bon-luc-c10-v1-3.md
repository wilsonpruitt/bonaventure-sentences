# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. X, Vers. 1-3
## *Prima pars, quae agit de forma praedicandi, tractat de missione comitantium discipulorum, de informatione euntium, de consolatione redeuntium*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 252 -->

### Capitulum X.

#### Secundo agitur de missione septuaginta duorum discipulorum quoad tria.

1. *Post haec*[^p252-4] *designavit Dominus* etc. Expedita parte de missione Apostolorum, hic subditur pars de *missione discipulorum* ad modum *minorum praelatorum.* Habet autem haec pars tres, in quarum prima agit de *forma praedicandi;* in secunda vero, de *forma vivendi,* infra eodem[^p252-5]: *Et ecce, quidam legisperitus;* in tertia, de *forma orandi,* infra undecimo: *Et factum est, cum esset in quodam loco* etc.

#### Prima pars, quae agit de forma praedicandi, tractat de missione comitantium discipulorum, de informatione euntium, de consolatione redeuntium.

Prima pars habet tres, in quarum prima praemittitur *missio discipulorum comitantium;* in secunda vero additur *informatio euntium,* ibi[^p252-6]: *Nolite portare neque sacculum* etc.; in tertia subditur *consolatio redeuntium,* ibi: *Reversi sunt autem septuaginta duo cum gaudio* etc. *Mittuntur* autem discipuli cum auctoritate divini imperii, *informantur* ex veritate divini magisterii, *consolantur* ex familiaritate divini contubernii.

*Mittit* autem discipulos Dominus ad praedicandum hoc ordine: primo *designando* eos secundum debitum numerum; secundo *praemittendo* designatos in omnem civitatem et locum; tertio in *accelerando* praemissos ad salutem electorum; quarto in *confirmando* acceleratos contra rabiem persecutorum.

2. (Vers. 1.). Primo igitur quantum ad *discipulorum designationem* secundum debitum numerum dicit: *Post haec designavit Dominus et alios septuaginta duos* etc.; *designavit* utique, id est, signanter elegit, secundum illud Ioannis decimo quinto[^p252-7]: « Ego elegi vos et posui vos, ut eatis » etc. Unde in Glossa: « Sicut in Apostolis forma est episcoporum, sic in septuaginta duobus forma est presbyterorum secundi ordinis ». Isti debent a Deo *designari,* id est ad honorem assumi, secundum illud ad Hebraeos quinto[^p252-8]: « Nec quisquam sumit sibi honorem, sed qui vocatur a Deo, tanquam Aaron ». In cuius designationis testimonium debent esse signati signaculo ordinis et characteris sacerdotalis, signaculo tonsurae et totius disciplinae et conversationis sanctissimae, ut appareat, illos esse de numero illorum, quibus ad Ephesios quarto dicitur[^p252-9]: « Signati estis Spiritu sancto in die redemptionis nostrae ». — Designavit autem eos in *numero septuaginta,* secundum quod designati fuerant in veteri testamento, Exodi decimo quinto[^p252-10], in *septuaginta palmis,* ubi dicitur, quod « in Elim erant duodecim fontes aquarum » quoad Apostolos, « et septuaginta palmae » quoad discipulos; et Numerorum undecimo, hi signati fuerunt in *septuaginta viris,* quibus dedit Dominus Spiritum sanctum prophetandi, ubi dicitur: « Locutus est Dominus ad Moysen, auferens de spiritu, qui erat in Moyse, et dans septuaginta viris, qui prophetaverunt nec ultra cessaverunt. Remanserant autem duo viri in castris, super quos requievit spiritus. Nam et ipsi
<!-- page 253 -->
descripti fuerant »; in quo hi expresse figurati sunt. — Ratio autem, quare isti fuerunt septuaginta duo, *litteralis* fuit, ut, sicut Apostoli praedicantes duodecim tribubus duodecim erant, sic isti septuaginta duo, secundum quod in Glossa[^p253-1] dicitur: « Septuaginta duo mittuntur, qui totidem gentibus linguarum Evangelium praedicarent »; secundum *mysticum intellectum,* quia continet in se septies denarium et binarium; in quo designatur septiformis spiritus donatus eorum ministerio ad impletionem decalogi et duorum praeceptorum caritatis[^p253-2]. — Vel, quia septuaginta duo continent horas trium dierum, quia fidem Trinitatis praedicare debebant, sicut dicitur in Glossa[^p253-3], secundum triduanum circuitum solis Christi, scilicet incarnationis, passionis, resurrectionis.

3. Secundo quantum ad *designatorum praemissionem* ante suum conspectum subditur: *Et misit illos binos ante faciem suam. Misit,* inquam, auctoritate divina, quia, sicut dicitur Ioannis vigesimo[^p253-4], « sicut me misit Pater, et ego mitto vos ». *Binos* autem misit *ad litteram,* ut se mutuo custodirent et adiuvarent, secundum illud Ecclesiastae quarto: « Melius est, duos simul esse quam unum; habent enim emolumentum societatis suae »; vel secundum *spiritualem intelligentiam,* ut dicit Beda[^p253-5], « per hoc, quod binos mittit, innuit, quod nemo praedicationis officium suscipere debet, qui erga alium caritatem non habet ». In cuius rei designationem dicitur Canticorum quarto: « Dentes tui », id est praedicatores, quorum est masticare cibum parvulis, « sicut greges tonsarum, quae ascenderunt de lavacro, omnes gemellis fetibus, et sterilis non est in eis ». In cuius etiam rei *figuram,* ut dicit Glossa[^p253-6], animalia bina fuerunt in arca Noe, sicut dicitur Genesis sexto. — Et quoniam haec missio non erat nisi sicut quaedam praeparatio ad Christum, ideo addit: *In omnem civitatem et locum, quo erat ipse venturus;* unde sicut praecones praecedebant, secundum illud Isaiae quadragesimo[^p253-7]: « Parate viam Domini, rectas facite semitas eius ». Unde Glossa: « Ubi verba praedicationis praecurrunt, venit Dominus ad mentis habitaculum ».

4. (Vers. 2.). Tertio quantum ad *praemissorum accelerationem* ad salutem electorum subditur: *Et dicebat illis: Messis quidem multa,* id est turba ad conversionem parata. Unde Ioannis quarto[^p253-8], quando Samaritani crediderunt, Dominus dixit: « Videte regiones, quia albae sunt iam ad messem ». Haec messis seminata fuit in *lege naturae,* crevit autem in *lege figurae,* sed recollecta est in *tempore gratiae.* Unde dicitur Ioannis quarto: « Ego misi vos metere quod vos non seminastis; alii laboraverunt, et vos in labores eorum introistis ». Huius autem messis duplex est recollectio: *universalis* in tempore ultimo, de qua Apocalypsis decimo quarto[^p253-9] dicitur Angelo: « Mitte falcem tuam et mete, quia venit hora, ut metatur, quoniam maturavit messis terrae »; alia est recollectio sive messio *particularis* in praedicatione Evangelii. — Quia vero pauci sunt boni messores, ideo subdit: *Operarii autem pauci;* signanter dicit *operarii,* quia ad hoc, quod falx praedicationis messem recolligat, necesse est, quod *manus operis* eam teneat, iuxta quod dicit Gregorius super Ezechielem[^p253-10]: « Ad hoc, ut servetur veritas praedicandi, necessaria est altitudo vivendi ». Tales, qui faciunt quod ore dicunt, sunt *operarii* et a Domino remunerandi, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo[^p253-11]: « Voca operarios et redde illis mercedem suam ». Sed pauci sunt operarii *studiosi,* plures autem sunt *malitiosi,* secundum illud secundae ad Corinthios undecimo[^p253-12]: « Ipsi pseudoapostoli sunt, operarii subdoli, transfigurantes se in Apostolos
<!-- page 254 -->
Christi ». Plurimi autem sunt *desidiosi;* unde Matthaei vigesimo dixit Dominus operariis: « Quid hic statis tota die otiosi »? Sunt plurimi similes illis, de quibus Matthaei vigesimo tertio: « Dicunt et non faciunt. Alligant enim onera gravia et importabilia; digito autem suo nolunt ea movere ». — Et quoniam messis amittitur, quando boni operarii desunt; ideo addit: *Rogate ergo Dominum messis, ut mittat operarios in messem suam,* praedicatores scilicet bonos, qui gladium divini verbi, quo segetes metuntur, non tantum in ore, sed etiam in manu portent. Unde de bonis praedicatoribus in Psalmo[^p254-1]: « Exsultationes Dei in faucibus eorum, et gladii ancipites in manibus eorum ». Unde praedicatio est gladius, qui, nisi manu teneatur, non terret adversarium. Quod bene figuratum est Nehemiae quarto de reaedificantibus Ierusalem, et ibi dicitur, quod « unusquisque manu una faciebat opus, et alia tenebat gladium ». Tales operarii dantur divinitus, secundum quod signatum est Genesis secundo[^p254-2], quod « Deus posuit hominem in paradiso voluptatis, ut operaretur et custodiret illum »; et ideo sunt a Domino postulandi, ut eos conducat promittendo, conductos mittat praecipiendo, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo: « Qui exiit primo mane conducere operarios in vineam suam. Conventione autem facta » etc.

5. (Vers. 3.). Quarto autem quantum ad *acceleratorum confirmationem* contra rabiem persecutorum subdit: *Ite; ecce, ego*[^p254-3] *mitto vos sicut agnos inter lupos. Ite,* scilicet *expedite,* sicut animalia illa Ezechielis primo: « Animalia ibant et revertebantur in similitudinem fulguris coruscantis »; et Proverbiorum sexto: « Discurre, festina, suscita amicum tuum ». Vel *ite,* id est, pro salute ovium *periculis vos exponite,* secundum illud Ioannis decimo[^p254-4]: « Bonus pastor animam suam dat pro ovibus suis. Mercenarius autem videt lupum venientem et dimittit oves et fugit »; quasi dicat: ite ad *luctum,* ut reportetis gaudium, secundum illud Psalmi[^p254-5]: « Euntes ibant et flebant, mittentes semina sua; venientes autem venient cum exsultatione » etc.; vel ite ad *proelium,* ut reportetis triumphum, secundum illud Iosue primo[^p254-6]: « Ite armati ante fratres vestros, omnes fortes manu, et pugnate pro eis ».

6. Et quia fortissima armatura est mansuetudo et patientia, ideo dicit: *Sicut agnos inter lupos,* id est sicut pios, humiles et mansuetos inter impios, superbos et malignos, ut illos mansuetudine vincatis, sicut et Christus fecit, secundum illud Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio[^p254-7]: « Sicut agnus coram tondente se obmutuit et non aperuit os suum ». Sic Apostoli agnina mansuetudine domuerunt lupinam ferocitatem, et impletum est illud Isaiae undecimo[^p254-8]: « Lupus et agnus simul morabuntur, et leo quasi bos paleas comedet »; et sexagesimo quinto: « Lupus et agnus pascentur simul ». Unde Christus sua mansuetudine Paulum lupum convertit in agnum, et impletum est in eo illud Genesis penultimo: « Beniamin, lupus rapax, mane comedet praedam et vespere dividet spolia ». Prius enim Christum persecutus est ut lupus, postea pro eo persecutiones passus ut agnus. Tales debent esse praelati Ecclesiae, secundum illud primae Petri quinto[^p254-9]: « Seniores, pascite qui in vobis est gregem, providentes » etc.; et post: « Neque ut dominantes in clero, sed forma facti gregis ex animo »; quasi dicat: non sitis sicut lupi inter agnos, sed magis agni inter agnos et lupos, ut bonos foveatis et malos toleretis. Unde Chrysostomus[^p254-10]: « Fortior est Dei gratia quam natura; dum fuerimus oves, vincimus, etsi multi sint lupi; si autem fuerimus lupi, vincimur, et cessat a nobis summi pastoris auxilium ».

## English
<!-- page 252 -->

### Chapter X.

#### Secondly, the mission of the seventy-two disciples is treated, as to three things.

1. *After these things*[^p252-4] *the Lord designated* etc. The part concerning the mission of the Apostles being dispatched, here is subjoined the part concerning the *mission of the disciples* after the manner of *lesser prelates.* Now this part has three, in the first of which he treats of the *form of preaching;* but in the second, of the *form of living,* below in the same chapter[^p252-5]: *And behold, a certain lawyer;* in the third, of the *form of praying,* below in the eleventh chapter: *And it came to pass, as he was in a certain place* etc.

#### The first part, which treats of the form of preaching, deals with the mission of the accompanying disciples, with the formation of those going, with the consolation of those returning.[^tr-corrigenda]

The first part has three, in the first of which is put first the *mission of the accompanying disciples;* but in the second is added the *formation of those going,* at[^p252-6]: *Carry neither purse* etc.; in the third is subjoined the *consolation of those returning,* at: *And the seventy-two returned with joy* etc. Now the disciples *are sent* with the authority of the divine command, *are formed* out of the truth of the divine teaching office, *are consoled* out of the familiarity of the divine companionship.

Now the Lord *sends* the disciples to preach in this order: first by *designating* them according to the due number; secondly by *sending* the designated *before* into every city and place; thirdly in *hastening* those sent before to the salvation of the elect; fourthly in *strengthening* those hastened against the rage of the persecutors.

2. (Vers. 1.). First, then, as to the *designation of the disciples* according to the due number he says: *After these things the Lord designated also other seventy-two* etc.; *designated* indeed, that is, chose signally, according to that of John, the fifteenth chapter[^p252-7]: « I have chosen you, and have appointed you, that you should go » etc. Whence in the Gloss: « As in the Apostles is the form of bishops, so in the seventy-two is the form of the priests of the second order ». These ought to be *designated* by God, that is, taken up to the honour, according to that to the Hebrews, the fifth chapter[^p252-8]: « Neither doth any man take the honour to himself, but he that is called by God, as Aaron ». In testimony of which designation they ought to be signed with the seal of order and of the priestly character, with the seal of the tonsure and of all discipline and most holy conversation, that it may appear that they are of the number of those to whom it is said to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter[^p252-9]: « You are signed with the Holy Spirit in the day of our redemption ». — Now he designated them in the *number seventy,* according as they had been designated in the old testament, Exodus, the fifteenth chapter[^p252-10], in the *seventy palm trees,* where it is said that « in Elim there were twelve fountains of waters », as to the Apostles, « and seventy palm trees », as to the disciples; and in Numbers, the eleventh chapter, these were signified in the *seventy men,* to whom the Lord gave the Holy Spirit of prophesying, where it is said: « The Lord spoke to Moses, taking away of the spirit that was in Moses, and giving to the seventy men, who prophesied, nor did they cease afterwards. Now two men had remained in the camp, upon whom the spirit rested. For they also
<!-- page 253 -->
had been enrolled »; in which these were expressly figured. — Now the reason why these were seventy-two was *literal,* that, as the Apostles preaching to the twelve tribes were twelve, so these were seventy-two, according to what is said in the Gloss[^p253-1]: « Seventy-two are sent, who should preach the Gospel to as many nations of tongues »; according to the *mystical understanding,* because it contains in itself seven times ten and two; in which is designated the sevenfold spirit given to their ministry for the fulfilment of the decalogue and of the two precepts of charity[^p253-2]. — Or, because seventy-two contain the hours of three days, because they had to preach the faith of the Trinity, as is said in the Gloss[^p253-3], according to the three days' circuit of Christ the sun, namely of the incarnation, of the passion, of the resurrection.

3. Secondly, as to the *sending before of the designated* before his own sight, there is subjoined: *And he sent them two and two before his face. He sent,* I say, by divine authority, because, as is said in John, the twentieth chapter[^p253-4], « as the Father hath sent me, I also send you ». But he sent them *two and two according to the letter,* that they might guard and help one another, according to that of Ecclesiastes, the fourth chapter: « It is better that two should be together, than one: for they have the advantage of their society »; or according to the *spiritual intelligence,* as Bede says[^p253-5], « by this, that he sends them two and two, he intimates that no one ought to undertake the office of preaching who has not charity towards another ». In designation of which thing it is said in the Canticle of Canticles, the fourth chapter: « Thy teeth », that is, the preachers, whose part it is to chew food for the little ones, « are as flocks of sheep that are shorn, which came up from the washing, all with twins, and there is none barren among them ». In *figure* also of which thing, as the Gloss says[^p253-6], the animals were two and two in Noe's ark, as is said in Genesis, the sixth chapter. — And since this sending was nothing but a kind of preparation for Christ, therefore he adds: *Into every city and place whither he himself was to come;* whence they went before as heralds, according to that of Isaias, the fortieth chapter[^p253-7]: « Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight his paths ». Whence the Gloss: « Where the words of preaching run before, the Lord comes to the dwelling of the mind ».

4. (Vers. 2.). Thirdly, as to the *hastening of those sent before* to the salvation of the elect, there is subjoined: *And he said to them: The harvest indeed is great,* that is, a multitude ready for conversion. Whence in John, the fourth chapter[^p253-8], when the Samaritans believed, the Lord said: « See the countries, for they are white already to harvest ». This harvest was sown in the *law of nature,* but it grew in the *law of figure,* and it was gathered in the *time of grace.* Whence it is said in John, the fourth chapter: « I have sent you to reap that which you did not sow: others have laboured, and you have entered into their labours ». Now of this harvest there is a twofold gathering: a *universal* one in the last time, of which it is said to the Angel in the Apocalypse, the fourteenth chapter[^p253-9]: « Thrust in thy sickle and reap, because the hour is come that it be reaped, for the harvest of the earth is ripe »; the other is a *particular* gathering or reaping in the preaching of the Gospel. — But because good reapers are few, therefore he subjoins: *But the labourers are few;* he says pointedly *labourers,* because, for the sickle of preaching to gather the harvest, it is necessary that the *hand of work* hold it, according to what Gregory says on Ezechiel[^p253-10]: « For this, that the truth of preaching be kept, a height of living is necessary ». Such as do what they say with the mouth are *labourers* and are to be rewarded by the Lord, according to that of Matthew, the twentieth chapter[^p253-11]: « Call the labourers and pay them their hire ». But few are the *zealous* labourers, while more are the *malicious,* according to that of the second to the Corinthians, the eleventh chapter[^p253-12]: « They are false apostles, deceitful labourers, transforming themselves into the apostles
<!-- page 254 -->
of Christ ». And very many are *slothful;* whence in Matthew, the twentieth chapter, the Lord said to the labourers: « Why stand you here all the day idle »? Very many are like those of whom Matthew, the twenty-third chapter, says: « They say, and do not. For they bind heavy and insupportable burdens; but with their finger they will not move them ». — And since the harvest is lost when good labourers are wanting, therefore he adds: *Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he send labourers into his harvest,* namely good preachers, who carry the sword of the divine word, with which the crops are reaped, not only in the mouth, but also in the hand. Whence of good preachers in the Psalm[^p254-1]: « The exultations of God are in their throats, and two-edged swords in their hands ». Whence preaching is a sword which, unless it be held in the hand, does not terrify the adversary. Which was well figured in Nehemias, the fourth chapter, concerning those rebuilding Jerusalem, and there it is said that « every one with one hand did the work, and with the other held a sword ». Such labourers are given from God, according to what is signified in Genesis, the second chapter[^p254-2], that « God put man in the paradise of pleasure, that he might work it and keep it »; and therefore they are to be asked of the Lord, that he may hire them by promising and send them, once hired, by enjoining, according to that of Matthew, the twentieth chapter: « Who went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard. And having made agreement » etc.

5. (Vers. 3.). But fourthly, as to the *strengthening of those hastened* against the rage of the persecutors, he subjoins: *Go: behold I*[^p254-3] *send you as lambs among wolves. Go,* namely *speedily,* as those living creatures of Ezechiel, the first chapter: « The living creatures went and returned after the likeness of flashing lightning »; and of Proverbs, the sixth chapter: « Run about, make haste, stir up thy friend ». Or *go,* that is, for the salvation of the sheep *expose yourselves to dangers,* according to that of John, the tenth chapter[^p254-4]: « The good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep. But the hireling seeth the wolf coming, and leaveth the sheep, and flieth »; as if he should say: go to *mourning,* that you may bring back joy, according to that of the Psalm[^p254-5]: « Going they went and wept, casting their seeds; but coming they shall come with exultation » etc.; or go to *battle,* that you may bring back triumph, according to that of Josue, the first chapter[^p254-6]: « Go ye armed before your brethren, all ye strong of hand, and fight for them ».

6. And because the strongest armour is meekness and patience, therefore he says: *As lambs among wolves,* that is, as the pious, the humble and the meek among the impious, the proud and the malignant, that you may overcome them by meekness, as Christ also did, according to that of Isaias, the fifty-third chapter[^p254-7]: « As a lamb before his shearer he was dumb, and he opened not his mouth ». So the Apostles by lamblike meekness tamed wolfish ferocity, and that of Isaias, the eleventh chapter, was fulfilled[^p254-8]: « The wolf and the lamb shall abide together, and the lion as the ox shall eat straw »; and the sixty-fifth: « The wolf and the lamb shall feed together ». Whence Christ by his meekness converted Paul the wolf into a lamb, and that of Genesis, the next to last chapter, was fulfilled in him: « Benjamin, a ravenous wolf, in the morning shall eat the prey, and in the evening shall divide the spoils ». For first he persecuted Christ as a wolf, afterwards he suffered persecutions for him as a lamb. Such ought the prelates of the Church to be, according to that of the first of Peter, the fifth chapter[^p254-9]: « Ancients, feed the flock that is among you, taking care of it » etc.; and afterwards: « Neither as lording it over the clergy, but being made a pattern of the flock from the heart »; as if he should say: be not as wolves among lambs, but rather lambs among lambs and wolves, that you may cherish the good and tolerate the wicked. Whence Chrysostom[^p254-10]: « Stronger is the grace of God than nature; while we are sheep, we conquer, though the wolves be many; but if we become wolves, we are conquered, and the help of the chief shepherd ceases from us ».
