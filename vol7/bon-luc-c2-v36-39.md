# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. II, Vers. 36-39
## *Et erat Anna prophetissa etc.*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 62 -->

81. *Et erat Anna prophetissa* etc. Post testimonium sexus virilis sequitur hic testimonium sexus *muliebris.* Decebat enim, Christi adventum testimonium habere ab omni genere personarum, ut inexcusabiles sint qui non credunt Evangelio. Unde Christus habuit testimonium angelicum et humanum, et iterum quantum ad simplices et perfectos, et iterum perfectorum in utroque sexu, ut ostendatur, quod uterque sexus ad redemptionem spectabat, sicut uterque ceciderat. Unde ut nihil desit ad firmitatem testimonii, Christus natus habuit testimonium *septiforme,* scilicet a natura *caelesti,* scilicet stella; Matthaei secundo[^p62-7]: « Vidimus stellam eius in oriente ». A *supercaelesti,* scilicet angelica; supra eodem: « Facta est cum Angelo multitudo caelestis exercitus » etc. A natura *subcaelesti,* scilicet *virili simplici,* sicut pastores; supra: « Et reversi sunt pastores » etc. *Virili sapiente,* sicut Magi; Matthaei secundo: « Ecce, Magi ab Oriente venerunt » etc. *Senili in sexu masculino,* sicut Simeon; supra[^p62-8]: « Hic venit in Spiritu in templum ». *Senili in sexu femineo,* ut hic: *Et erat Anna prophetissa, filia Phanuel* etc. *Infantili,* qui « non loquendo, sed moriendo confessi sunt »; Matthaei secundo: « Mittens occidit omnes pueros » etc.; et tunc impletum est illud Psalmi: « Ex ore infantium et lactentium perfecisti laudem ». Et omnis natura, omnis sexus, omnis aetas Christo nascenti testimonium perhibuit, quia ipse debebat reparare omnia. Unde et pueris in templo clamantibus et Pharisaeis reclamantibus dictum est infra decimo nono[^p62-9]: « Si tacuerint hi, lapides clamabunt ».

Ad huius autem testimonii fidem astruendam tria introducuntur. Primum est *prophetantis auctoritas.* Secundum est *testificantis sanctitas,* ibi[^p62-10]: *Et vixerat cum viro suo annis septem* etc. Tertium est *testimonii celebritas,* ibi: *Et haec, ipsa hora superveniens* etc. — *Auctoritas* autem *prophetantis* colligitur ex duobus, scilicet ex *munere prophetali* et *origine naturali.*

82. (Vers. 36.). Ratione muneris prophetalis dicitur: *Et erat Anna prophetissa.* « *Anna* interpretatur gratia[^p62-11] », in quo ostenditur, quod recte decebat talem mulierem prophetare, quae erat habitaculum gratiae; Proverbiorum undecimo: « Mulier gratiosa inveniet gloriam »; unde ipsa poterat dicere illud primae ad Corinthios decimo quinto: « Gratia Dei sum id quod sum ». Ideo non est mirum, si
<!-- page 63 -->
Anna erat prophetissa, quia Sapientiae septimo dicitur de sapientia, quod « in animas sanctas se transfert et amicos Dei facit et Prophetas constituit ».

83. Ratione vero *originis naturalis* additur: *Filia Phanuel de tribu Aser;* et sic erat de genere *Israel,* de cuius genere erant reges, sacerdotes et Prophetae et Christus Dominus; ad Romanos nono[^p63-1] dicitur de Israelitis: « Quorum adoptio est filiorum et gloria et testamentum et legislatio et obsequium et promissio, quorum patres, et ex quibus est Christus secundum carnem ». Et specialiter erat *de tribu Aser,* cui data est benedictio in filiis; Deuteronomii trigesimo tertio: « Benedictus in filiis Aser, sit placens fratribus suis »; « Sicut dies iuventutis tuae, ita et senectutis tuae ». Et specialissime dicitur *filia Phanuel,* qui interpretatur facies Dei[^p63-2]; in quo ostenditur, quod erat de genere illorum, ad quos dicitur: « Quaerite faciem eius semper ». Nihil ergo deerat ratione originis ex parte *dignitatis parentum;* nec etiam ratione *antiquitatis dierum,* quae facit ad dignitatem et auctoritatem perplurimum; Proverbiorum decimo sexto[^p63-3]: « Corona dignitatis senectus, quae in viis iustitiae reperitur ». Et ideo subditur: *Et haec processerat in diebus multis;* Iob vigesimo nono: « In nidulo meo moriar, et sicut palma multiplicabo dies ».

84. *Et vixerat cum viro suo* etc. Hic secundo describitur *testificantis sanctitas* quantum ad perfectionem vitae *activae* et *contemplativae.* — Quantum ad vitam *activam,* quae consistit in regimine domus propriae, dicitur: *Et vixerat cum viro suo annis septem a virginitate sua;* et sic fuit *virgo,* antequam cognosceretur a marito.

85. (Vers. 37.). *Et haec* erat *vidua usque ad annos octoginta quatuor;* et ita habuit continentiam *virginalem, vidualem* et *coniugalem* pro loco et tempore. Unde laudabilis fuit in custodia corporis; propter quod ei competebat illud Iudith decimo quinto[^p63-4]: « Fecisti viriliter, et confortatum est cor tuum, eo quod castitatem amaveris et post virum tuum alterum nescieris ». Et ideo haec vidua hono-
randa, secundum illud primae ad Timotheum quinto: « Viduas honora, quae vere viduae sunt »; et iterum: « Vidua eligatur non minus sexaginta annorum, quae fuerit unius viri uxor, in operibus bonis testimonium habens ». Talis fuit Anna; in quo apparet, quod habebat perfectionem vitae *activae.*

86. Quantum ad perfectionem *contemplativae* dicitur: *Quae non discedebat de templo.* Tota vacabat contemplationi *devote.* Glossa[^p63-5]: « Exemplum datur viduis, quae sunt in Ecclesia », scilicet ut dicant cum Psalmo: « Haec requies mea in saeculum saeculi »; contra illas viduas, de quibus dicit Apostolus primae ad Timotheum quinto, quod « otiosae discunt circuire domos, et non solum otiosae, sed et verbosae et curiosae, loquentes quae non oportet ». — Vacabat etiam *officiose;* unde et additur: *Ieiuniis et obsecrationibus serviens* Deo; sicut illa bona vidua Iudith, octavo[^p63-6], dicitur quod « in superioribus domus suae fecerat cubiculum secretum, in quo cum puellis suis clausa morabatur, habens cilicium circa lumbos suos et ieiunabat omnibus diebus vitae suae »; et hoc optime, quia, sicut dicitur primae ad Timotheum quinto, « vidua, quae in deliciis est, vivens mortua est ». Et bene iungit *ieiunium* et *orationem,* quia « *ieiunio* sanantur pestes corporis, *oratione* pestes animae »; Tobiae duodecimo: « Bona est oratio cum ieiunio et eleemosyna » etc. — Vacabat etiam *continue;* unde dicitur: *Die ac nocte;* secundum formam Apostoli; primae ad Timotheum quinto[^p63-7]: « Quae vidua est et desolata speret in Domino et instet orationibus die ac nocte ».

87. (Vers. 38.). *Et haec, ipsa hora* etc. Hic tertio describitur *testimonii celebritas* tum ratione *temporis congruentis,* tum ratione *multitudinis consentientis.* — Ratione *temporis congruentis* dicitur: *Et haec, ipsa hora superveniens, confitebatur Domino;* in quo apparet, quod divino instinctu factus est iste concursus, et ideo festum Purificationis dicitur festum Ypapanti, id est obviantium[^p63-8], quia Simeon et Anna obviaverunt Ioseph et Mariae sine aliqua convocatione; et ideo in die illo universaliter
<!-- page 64 -->
fit processio. Unde sicut eodem spiritu venit, quo Simeon, ita et eodem spiritu prophetavit; et impletum est illud Ioel secundo[^p64-1]: « Et prophetabunt filii vestri et filiae, senes vestri visiones videbunt » etc. Attamen Simeonis testificatio explicite describitur et per modum praedicationis, Annae vero implicite et per modum confessionis, quia docere in Ecclesia non est mulierum, sed virorum; primae ad Timotheum secundo[^p64-2]: « Docere mulieres non permitto in ecclesia neque dominari in virum ». — Et nota, quod de Christo prophetaverant Simeon et Zacharias, virgo Maria et Elisabeth copulata coniugio; et ideo congruum est, quod prophetet et vidua, ne aliqua professio desit aut sexus.

88. Ratione vero *multitudinis consentientis* subditur: *Et loquebatur de illo omnibus, qui exspectabant redemptionem Israel.* Illi enim libenter audiebant, unde dicebant illud Isaiae vigesimo quinto[^p64-3]: « Ecce, Dominus Deus noster, exspectavimus eum, et salvabit nos ». Iam poterat decantare: « Redemptionem misit Dominus populo suo ». *Omnibus loquebatur,* quia in hoc pendebat salus omnium; Isaiae quadragesimo nono: « Et sciet omnis caro, quia ego Dominus salvans te et redemptor tuus, fortis Iacob ».

89. Et nota hic *spiritualiter,* quod in oblatione Domini quatuor personae adfuerunt, scilicet *Ioseph* et *Maria, Simeon* et *Anna,* quia anima, quae verum sacrificium vult offerre Deo, debet habere comitatum quatuor virtutum cardinalium. Per *Simeonem* namque, qui interpretatur *audiens,* intelligitur *prudentia;* Proverbiorum primo[^p64-4]: « Audiens sapiens, sapientior erit ». Per *Annam* vero, quae interpretatur *gratia,* intelligitur *temperantia;* ad Hebraeos ultimo[^p64-5]: « Optimum est gratia stabilire cor, non escis ». Per *Ioseph,* virum iustum, qui interpretatur *augmentum,* intelligitur *iustitia;*
secundae ad Corinthios nono[^p64-6]: « Multiplicabit semen vestrum et augebit incrementa frugum iustitiae vestrae ». Per *Mariam,* quae interpretatur *dominatrix,* intelligitur *fortitudo,* Proverbiorum decimo sexto[^p64-7]: « Melior est patiens viro forti ». De his quatuor virtutibus Sapientiae octavo in laudem scribitur: « Sobrietatem enim et prudentiam docet et iustitiam et virtutem ». — Et haec designantur per quatuor prophetissas mulieres, de quibus fit mentio in Scriptura; scilicet Maria, soror Moysi, Exodi decimo quinto[^p64-8]; Debbora, Iudicum quinto; Holda, quarti Regum vigesimo secundo, et Anna, sicut hic. Per Annam, sicut dictum est, intelligitur *temperantia;* per Holdam, quae interpretatur *discretio mea,* intelligitur *prudentia;* per Debboram, quae interpretatur *apis* habens aculeum, intelligitur *iustitia* quantum ad severitatem et clementiam; per Mariam, quae *dominatrix* et *mare amarum* interpretatur[^p64-9], intelligitur *fortitudo* quantum ad magnanimitatem et patientiam. — Et hae quatuor virtutes intelliguntur per quatuor filias Philippi prophetissas; Actuum vigesimo primo[^p64-10]: « Huic erant quatuor filiae, virgines prophetantes ». Per *Philippum,* qui interpretatur *os lampadis,* intelligitur *sapientia,* quae docet et generat quadruplicem virtutem praedictam.

90. (Vers. 39.). *Et ut perfecerunt* etc. Postquam descripta est Christi humiliatio secundum Legis praeceptum et clarificatio per veritatis testimonium, subiungitur hic ultima particula huius partis, in qua describitur *consummatio praedictorum,* et hoc dicitur primo respectu *legalis praecepti,* deinde respectu *prophetalis vaticinii.* — Quantum ad *legalis praecepti consummationem* dicitur: *Et ut perfecerunt omnia secundum Legem Domini;* quia, secundum quod dicitur Matthaei quinto[^p64-11], « non veni Legem solvere, sed adimplere »; et rursus: « Iota unum, aut unus apex non praeteribit a Lege, donec
<!-- page 65 -->
omnia fiant ». — Quantum autem ad *prophetalis vaticinii completionem* subditur: *Reversi sunt in Galilaeam, in civitatem suam Nazareth;* Matthaei secundo[^p65-1]: « Admonitus in somnis, secessit in partes Galilaeae. Et veniens habitavit in civitate, quae vocatur Nazareth, ut adimpleretur quod dictum est per Prophetas: Quoniam Nazaraeus vocabitur »; Isaiae nono: « Aggravata est via maris trans Iordanem Galilaeae gentium ». Sed hoc non cognoverunt scribae, qui dixerunt, Ioannis septimo: « Scrutare Scripturas, et vide, quia a Galilaea Propheta non surgit ». — Et nota, quod secundum Augustinum in libro secundo de Consensu Evangelistarum[^p65-2], puer Iesus ante perlatus est a Iudaea in Aegyptum, quam descenderet in Galilaeam, quia propter persecutionem Herodis descendit in Aegyptum, et propter timorem Archelai secessit in partes Galilaeae, rediens de Aegypto.

## English
<!-- page 62 -->

81. *And there was one Anna, a prophetess* etc. After the testimony of the male sex there follows here the testimony of the *female* sex. For it was fitting that the advent of Christ should have testimony from every kind of persons, so that they who do not believe the Gospel may be inexcusable. Whence Christ had angelic and human testimony, and again as to the simple and the perfect, and again of the perfect in each sex, so that it might be shown that each sex looked to the redemption, as each had fallen. Whence, that nothing might be wanting to the firmness of the testimony, Christ when born had a *sevenfold* testimony, namely from the *heavenly* nature, namely the star; Matthew, the second chapter[^p62-7]: « We have seen his star in the east ». From the *superheavenly,* namely the angelic; above, in the same chapter: « There was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army » etc. From the *subheavenly* nature, namely the *simple male,* as the shepherds; above: « And the shepherds returned » etc. The *wise male,* as the Magi; Matthew, the second chapter: « Behold, Magi came from the East » etc. The *aged in the masculine sex,* as Simeon; above[^p62-8]: « This man came in the Spirit into the temple ». The *aged in the feminine sex,* as here: *And there was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel* etc. The *infant,* who « not by speaking, but by dying confessed »; Matthew, the second chapter: « Sending, he killed all the men children » etc.; and then was fulfilled that of the Psalm: « Out of the mouth of infants and of sucklings thou hast perfected praise ». And every nature, every sex, every age bore testimony to Christ being born, because he was to restore all things. Whence also, when the children were crying out in the temple and the Pharisees crying against it, it was said below, in the nineteenth chapter[^p62-9]: « If these shall hold their peace, the stones will cry out ».

Now to establish faith in this testimony three things are introduced. The first is the *authority of the one prophesying.* The second is the *holiness of the one testifying,* there[^p62-10]: *And had lived with her husband seven years* etc. The third is the *renown of the testimony,* there: *And she, at the same hour coming in* etc. — Now the *authority of the one prophesying* is gathered from two things, namely from the *prophetic office* and the *natural origin.*

82. (Vers. 36.). By reason of the prophetic office it is said: *And there was one Anna, a prophetess.* « *Anna* is interpreted grace[^p62-11] », in which it is shown that it was rightly fitting that such a woman should prophesy, who was a dwelling-place of grace; Proverbs, the eleventh chapter: « A gracious woman shall find glory »; whence she could say that of the first to the Corinthians, the fifteenth chapter: « By the grace of God I am what I am ». Therefore it is no marvel if
<!-- page 63 -->
Anna was a prophetess, because in Wisdom, the seventh chapter, it is said of wisdom that she « conveyeth herself into holy souls, and maketh the friends of God, and appointeth prophets ».

83. But by reason of *natural origin* it is added: *The daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Aser;* and so she was of the race of *Israel,* of whose race were kings, priests and Prophets and Christ the Lord; in the Romans, the ninth chapter[^p63-1], it is said of the Israelites: « Whose is the adoption of children, and the glory, and the testament, and the giving of the law, and the service, and the promise; whose are the fathers, and of whom is Christ according to the flesh ». And specially she was *of the tribe of Aser,* to which was given the blessing in children; Deuteronomy, the thirty-third chapter: « Blessed in children be Aser, let him be acceptable to his brethren »; « As the days of thy youth, so also of thy old age ». And most specially she is called *the daughter of Phanuel,* which is interpreted the face of God[^p63-2]; in which it is shown that she was of the race of those to whom it is said: « Seek his face evermore ». Nothing therefore was wanting by reason of origin on the part of the *dignity of parents;* nor yet by reason of *length of days,* which makes very greatly for dignity and authority; Proverbs, the sixteenth chapter[^p63-3]: « Old age is a crown of dignity, which is found in the ways of justice ». And therefore it is subjoined: *And she had advanced in many days;* Job, the twenty-ninth chapter: « In my nest I shall die, and as a palm tree I shall multiply days ».

84. *And had lived with her husband* etc. Here secondly is described the *holiness of the one testifying* as to the perfection of the *active* and the *contemplative* life. — As to the *active* life, which consists in the ruling of one's own house, it is said: *And had lived with her husband seven years from her virginity;* and so she was a *virgin* before she was known by her husband.

85. (Vers. 37.). *And she* was *a widow until fourscore and four years;* and so she had *virginal, widowly* and *conjugal* continence, according to place and time. Whence she was praiseworthy in the keeping of the body; on account of which there befitted her that of Judith, the fifteenth chapter[^p63-4]: « Thou hast done manfully, and thy heart has been strengthened, because thou hast loved chastity, and after thy husband hast not known any other ». And therefore this widow was to be
honoured, according to that of the first to Timothy, the fifth chapter: « Honour widows, that are widows indeed »; and again: « Let a widow be chosen of no less than threescore years of age, who hath been the wife of one husband, having testimony in good works ». Such was Anna; in which it appears that she had the perfection of the *active* life.

86. As to the perfection of the *contemplative* it is said: *Who departed not from the temple.* She was wholly free for[^tr-vacare] contemplation, *devoutly.* The Gloss[^p63-5]: « An example is given to the widows who are in the Church », namely that they may say with the Psalm: « This is my rest for ever and ever »; against those widows of whom the Apostle says, the first to Timothy, the fifth chapter, that « being idle they learn to go about houses, and are not only idle, but tattlers also and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not ». — She was free for it also *dutifully;* whence it is also added: *By fastings and prayers serving* God; as it is said of that good widow Judith, the eighth chapter[^p63-6], that « in the upper part of her house she had made a private chamber, in which she abode shut up with her maids, having haircloth about her loins, and she fasted all the days of her life »; and this most excellently, because, as it is said in the first to Timothy, the fifth chapter, « a widow that is in pleasures, living, is dead ». And well does he join *fasting* and *prayer,* because « by *fasting* are healed the plagues of the body, by *prayer* the plagues of the soul »; Tobias, the twelfth chapter: « Prayer is good with fasting and alms » etc. — She was free for it also *continually;* whence it is said: *Night and day;* according to the form of the Apostle; the first to Timothy, the fifth chapter[^p63-7]: « She that is a widow and desolate, let her hope in the Lord, and continue in prayers night and day ».

87. (Vers. 38.). *And she, at the same hour* etc. Here thirdly is described the *renown of the testimony,* both by reason of the *fitting time* and by reason of the *consenting multitude.* — By reason of the *fitting time* it is said: *And she, at the same hour coming in, confessed to the Lord;* in which it appears that this coming together was done by divine instinct, and therefore the feast of the Purification is called the feast of Ypapanti, that is, of those meeting[^p63-8], because Simeon and Anna met Joseph and Mary without any calling together; and therefore on that day a procession is made
<!-- page 64 -->
universally. Whence, as she came in the same spirit as Simeon, so also she prophesied in the same spirit; and there was fulfilled that of Joel, the second chapter[^p64-1]: « And your sons and daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall see visions » etc. Yet the testification of Simeon is described explicitly and by way of preaching, but Anna's implicitly and by way of confession, because to teach in the Church belongs not to women, but to men; the first to Timothy, the second chapter[^p64-2]: « I suffer not women to teach in the church, nor to use authority over the man ». — And note that Simeon and Zachary, the virgin Mary and Elizabeth joined in wedlock had prophesied of Christ; and therefore it is fitting that a widow also should prophesy, lest any profession or sex be wanting.

88. But by reason of the *consenting multitude* it is subjoined: *And spoke of him to all that waited for the redemption of Israel.* For these were glad to hear, whence they said that of Isaias, the twenty-fifth chapter[^p64-3]: « Behold, the Lord our God, we have waited for him, and he will save us ». Now she could sing forth: « The Lord hath sent redemption to his people ». *She spoke to all,* because on this hung the salvation of all; Isaias, the forty-ninth chapter: « And all flesh shall know that I am the Lord that save thee, and thy Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob ».

89. And note here *spiritually* that at the offering of the Lord four persons were present, namely *Joseph* and *Mary, Simeon* and *Anna,* because the soul that wishes to offer a true sacrifice to God ought to have the company of the four cardinal virtues. For by *Simeon,* which is interpreted *hearing,* is understood *prudence;* Proverbs, the first chapter[^p64-4]: « The wise, hearing, shall be wiser ». But by *Anna,* which is interpreted *grace,* is understood *temperance;* to the Hebrews, the last chapter[^p64-5]: « It is best to establish the heart with grace, not with meats ». By *Joseph,* a just man, which is interpreted *increase,* is understood *justice;*
the second to the Corinthians, the ninth chapter[^p64-6]: « He will multiply your seed, and increase the growth of the fruits of your justice ». By *Mary,* which is interpreted *mistress,* is understood *fortitude,* Proverbs, the sixteenth chapter[^p64-7]: « The patient is better than the strong man ». Of these four virtues it is written in praise in Wisdom, the eighth chapter: « For she teacheth temperance, and prudence, and justice, and fortitude ». — And these are designated by the four women prophetesses of whom mention is made in Scripture; namely Mary, the sister of Moses, Exodus, the fifteenth chapter[^p64-8]; Debbora, Judges, the fifth chapter; Holda, the fourth of Kings, the twenty-second chapter; and Anna, as here. By Anna, as has been said, is understood *temperance;* by Holda, which is interpreted *my discretion,* is understood *prudence;* by Debbora, which is interpreted *a bee* having a sting, is understood *justice* as to severity and clemency; by Mary, which is interpreted *mistress* and *bitter sea*[^p64-9], is understood *fortitude* as to magnanimity and patience. — And these four virtues are understood by the four daughters of Philip, prophetesses; Acts, the twenty-first chapter[^p64-10]: « This man had four daughters, virgins, who did prophesy ». By *Philip,* which is interpreted *mouth of a lamp,* is understood *wisdom,* which teaches and begets the fourfold virtue aforesaid.

90. (Vers. 39.). *And after they had performed* etc. After the humiliation of Christ according to the precept of the Law and his glorification by the testimony of truth have been described, here is subjoined the last small part of this part, in which is described the *consummation of the things aforesaid,* and this is said first in respect of the *legal precept,* then in respect of the *prophetic foretelling.* — As to the *consummation of the legal precept* it is said: *And after they had performed all things according to the Law of the Lord;* because, according to what is said in Matthew, the fifth chapter[^p64-11], « I came not to destroy the Law, but to fulfil »; and again: « One jot, or one tittle shall not pass from the Law, till
<!-- page 65 -->
all be done ». — But as to the *fulfilment of the prophetic foretelling* it is subjoined: *They returned into Galilee, to their city Nazareth;* Matthew, the second chapter[^p65-1]: « Being warned in sleep, he retired into the quarters of Galilee. And coming he dwelt in a city called Nazareth, that it might be fulfilled which was said by the prophets: That he shall be called a Nazarene »; Isaias, the ninth chapter: « The way of the sea beyond the Jordan of the Galilee of the Gentiles was made heavy ». But this the scribes did not know, who said, John, the seventh chapter: « Search the scriptures, and see that out of Galilee a prophet riseth not ». — And note that, according to Augustine in the second book *On the Agreement of the Evangelists*[^p65-2], the child Jesus was carried from Judea into Egypt before he went down into Galilee, because on account of the persecution of Herod he went down into Egypt, and on account of the fear of Archelaus he retired into the quarters of Galilee, returning from Egypt.

[^tr-vacare]: **En.** Translator's note: the Latin *vacare* holds together several senses that English has to split: being at leisure, being free, being free *for* something and being free *from* something.
