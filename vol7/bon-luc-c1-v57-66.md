# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. I, Vers. 57-66
## *De gratiarum actione pro ortu praecursoris dicuntur tria*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 34 -->

#### De gratiarum actione pro ortu praecursoris dicuntur tria.

104. *Elisabeth impletum est tempus* etc. Post gratiarum actionem de conceptu Salvatoris sequitur hic gratiarum actio pro *ortu praecursoris.* Agitur autem laus Deo de praecursoris *ortu* magis quam de conceptu, quia conceptus fuit in peccato, sed natus fuit plenus Spiritu sancto; Christus autem tam in conceptu quam in ortu fuit sanctissimus, ideo ante ortum et post ortum ipsius laudes decantantur. — Habet autem haec pars tres partes, in quarum prima describitur *praecursoris origo;* secundo vero pro ipsa subditur *laus et gratiarum actio,* ibi[^p34-9]: *Et Zacharias, pater eius, impletus est Spiritu sancto,* ubi ponitur canticum Zachariae; tertio vero ad materiam maioris laudis subditur *praecursoris educatio,* ibi: *Puer autem crescebat* etc.
<!-- page 35 -->

#### De praecursoris origine iterum tria.

Ad describendum autem *ortum praecursoris* tria concurrunt, quae ab Angelo praenuntiata fuerunt. Primum est *partus sterilis;* secundum est *circumcisio infantis,* quae describitur ibi[^p35-1]: *Et factum est in die octavo;* tertium est *apertio oris sacerdotalis,* ibi: *Apertum est autem illico os eius.* — *Partus* autem ostenditur fuisse *opportunus in matre, perfectus in prole, iucundus in vicinitate.*

105. (Vers. 57.). *Opportunus,* inquam, fuit partus *in matre* ratione temporis; propter quod dicit: *Elisabeth impletum est tempus pariendi,* et ita opportunum erat, ut pareret, quia, Ecclesiastae tertio[^p35-2], « omnia tempus habent, et suis spatiis transeunt universa sub caelo ». *Impletum est,* inquam, secundum impletionem verbi angelici, ut dictum est supra eodem: « Ecce, eris tacens usque in diem, quo haec fient; quia non credidisti verbis meis, quae implebuntur in tempore suo ». Vere *impletum,* quia iam venerat plenitudo temporis, in quo debebat venire Christus; ad Galatas quarto: « At ubi venit plenitudo temporis, misit Deus Filium suum » etc.

106. *Perfectus* etiam fuit *in prole* ratione *sexus virilis;* propter quod dicit: *Et peperit filium,* non filiam. Talem prolem petebat Anna primi Regum primo[^p35-3]: « Domine, si recordatus mei fueris, dederisque servae tuae sexum virilem; dabo eum Domino omnibus diebus vitae eius ». — Peperit etiam *virtute perfecta,* nullo sibi praestante impedimento; quocontra dicitur Isaiae trigesimo septimo[^p35-4]: « Venerunt filii usque ad partum, et non est virtus pariendi ». — Peperit etiam filium *perfectum superna gratia,* ita ut competeret ei illud Sapientiae quarto: « Consummatus in brevi complevit tempora multa ».

107. (Vers. 58.). *Iucundus* etiam fuit *in vicinitate* ratione famae celebris et communis; propter quod dicit: *Et audierunt vicini et cognati eius, quia magnificavit Dominus misericordiam suam cum illa, et congratulabantur ei.* Et sic impletum est illud quod praedixerat Angelus[^p35-5]: « Et in nativitate eius multi gaudebunt », scilicet vicini et cognati, quia talium maxime est congratulari; infra decimo quinto: « Mulier, quae invenit drachmam perditam, convocat amicas et vicinas, dicens: Congratulamini mihi, quia inveni drachmam, quam perdideram ». *Et ei congratulabantur;* nam et ipsa pariter gaudebat, secundum illud Ioannis decimo sexto[^p35-6]: « Cum autem mulier pepererit puerum, iam non meminit pressurae propter gaudium, quia natus est homo in mundum »; et ideo congratulabantur. — *Quia magnificavit Dominus misericordiam suam cum illa,* id est, misericordiam magnam fecit; unde poterat dicere illud Psalmi[^p35-7]: « Confitebor tibi, Domine Deus meus, in toto corde meo et glorificabo nomen tuum in aeternum, quia misericordia tua magna est super me ». Magnificat enim Dominus eos quos diligit; Sapientiae ultimo: « In omnibus magnificasti populum tuum, Domine, et non despexisti ».

108. (Vers. 59.). *Et factum est in die octavo.* Post partum senis sequitur *circumcisio infantis,* in qua secundum consuetudinem facta est *impositio nominis,* primo secundum *appellationem humanam a consanguinitate;* secundo vero, secundum *revelationem propheticam a matre;* tertio, secundum *affirmationem authenticam ab ipso patre.* — Introducitur ergo circumcisio cum *appellatione humana,* cum dicitur: *Octavo die venerunt circumcidere puerum,* ut scilicet implerent Legis praeceptum; Genesis decimo septimo[^p35-8]: « Infans octo dierum circumcidetur in vobis », quia « masculus, cuius caro praeputii non fuerit circumcisa, peribit de populo eius ». — *Et vocabant eum nomine patris sui Zachariam,* secundum consuetum modum, quia filii consueverunt appellari nomine suorum patrum; Tobiae primo[^p35-9]: « Tobias genuit filium, nomen suum imponens ei », ut in hoc significaret, patrem in tam nobili filio vivere, secundum illud Ecclesiastici trigesimo: « Mortuus est pater eius et quasi non est mortuus; similem enim sibi reliquit. In vita sua vidit et laetatus est in illo ».

109. (Vers. 60.). Sed quoniam in Ioanne non habebat locum denominatio secundum appellationem humanam, ideo sequitur denominatio secundum *revelationem propheticam in Elisabeth,* cum dicitur: *Et respondens mater eius dixit: Nequaquam, sed vocabitur Ioannes.* Et hoc quidem dicebat, edocta a Spiritu sancto; unde Ambrosius[^p35-10]: « Spiritu prophetiae didicit quod non didicerat a marito ». Quia enim non in virtute naturae conceptus fuerat, sed virtute gratiae; non debebat vocari *Zacharias* a patre, sed *Ioannes* a dono gratiae, ut novo modo natus novo nomine nuncupetur; Isaiae sexagesimo secundo: « Vocabitur tibi nomen novum, quod os Domini nominavit ».

110. (Vers. 61.). Et quia cognati carnaliter sapiebant, nomen gratiae non acceptabant; ideo subdi-

tur: *Et dixerunt ad illam: Quia nemo est in cognatione tua, qui vocetur hoc nomine.* Sed eorum instantia nulla erat, quia, secundum quod dicit Ambrosius[^p36-1], « hoc nomen non est generis, sed vatis. Habent enim hoc merita Sanctorum, ut a Deo nomen accipiant, sicut Iacob Israel vocatus est, Genesis trigesimo secundo, quia vidit Deum »; sic de Salomone, secundi Regum duodecimo: « Vocavit nomen eius *amabilis Domino* ». Et quoniam contentio erat hic inter gratiam et naturam, inter appellationem humanam et revelationem propheticam; ideo requirebant opinionem paternam, ut ipse proferret assertionem authenticam.

111. (Vers. 62.). Propterea sequitur: *Innuebant autem patri eius, quem vellet vocari eum; innuebant,* inquam, surdo et muto, quia, sicut dicit Ambrosius[^p36-2], « incredulitas affatum ei surripuerat et auditum »; et tamen ab eo requirebant, quia penes ipsum residebat auctoritas nominandi, tum quia pater, tum quia sacerdos, tum quia ortum filii divina revelatione praesciverat.

112. (Vers. 63.). Et quia non poterat voce respondere, manu et litteris est locutus; et ideo subditur: *Et postulans pugillarem, scripsit dicens: Ioannes est nomen eius;* quasi dicat: nomen non impono, sed impositum declaro; supra eodem[^p36-3]: « Vocabis nomen eius Ioannem », secundum illud Isaiae quadragesimo nono: « Dominus ab utero vocavit me, de ventre matris meae recordatus est nominis mei ». Et recte dicitur *scriptum* propter firmitatem indelebilem, ut ostendatur nomen eius scriptum in libro vitae, sicut discipulorum Christi; infra decimo[^p36-4]: « Gaudete, quia nomina vestra scripta sunt in caelis ». De hac scriptura dicitur Apocalypsis tertio: « Qui vicerit, scribam super eum nomen Dei mei et nomen civitatis Dei mei novae Ierusalem »; et rursus secundo: « Dabo ei calculum candidum, et in calculo nomen scriptum, quod nemo scit, nisi qui accipit ». — Et quia causam huius denominationis nescierunt, ideo sequitur: *Et mirati sunt universi;* Glossa[^p36-5]: « Propter consonantiam inter patrem et matrem », ut iam « in ore duorum testium
staret illud verbum ». Vel etiam *mirati sunt* in puero tantam praerogativam; Ecclesiastici undecimo dicitur de viro iusto: « Deus exaltavit illum, et mirati sunt in illo multi et honoraverunt Deum ». Unde ipse poterat dicere illud Sapientiae octavo: « Facies principum mirabuntur me ». In quo docemur, quod divinorum privilegiorum praerogativae magis sunt humiliter et sobrie admirandae, quam procaciter perscrutandae.

113. *Spiritualiter* nota, quod per *Zachariam* intelligitur vetus sacerdotium, per *Ioannem* novum; et quia vetus et novum differunt sicut memoriale muneris et ipsum munus; ideo recte ille denominatur a *memoriali,* iste vero a *munere gratiae;* et hoc quidem satis recte, quia memoria Dei in nobis generat affectiones virtutis et gratiae. Nam qui vult esse *Ioannes* per gratiam, oportet, quod sit *Zacharias* prius per divinam memoriam. Zacharias enim *memor Dei* interpretatur; Psalmus[^p36-6]: « Memomoriam fecit mirabilium suorum »; Exodi vigesimo: « Altare de terra facietis mihi, et offeretis super eo holocausta et pacifica vestra, oves vestras et boves in omni loco, in quo memoria fuerit nominis mei; veniam ad te et benedicam tibi ».

114. Debet autem quilibet esse memor *sacramentorum* Dei ad *credendum* et *confitendum;* Exodi decimo tertio[^p36-7] dicitur de agno paschali: « Erit quasi signum in manu tua et quasi memoriale ante oculos tuos »; sequitur: « Custodies huiuscemodi cultum statuto tempore a diebus in dies ». — *Beneficiorum* ad *regratiandum;* Deuteronomii decimo sexto[^p36-8]: « Septem diebus comedes absque fermento afflictionis panem, quoniam in pavore egressus es de Aegypto, ut memineris diei egressionis tuae de Aegypto omnibus diebus vitae tuae »; Psalmus: « Memoriam fecit » etc. — *Iudiciorum* ad *formidandum;* Ecclesiastici decimo octavo[^p36-9]: « Memento irae in die consummationis, et tempus retributionis in conversatione faciei »; Psalmus: « Memor fui iudiciorum tuorum a saeculo, Domine ». — *Promissorum* ad *exspectandum;* Ecclesiastici undecimo: « In die malorum ne immemor sis bonorum ». — *Mandatorum* ad *faciendum;* Psal-
<!-- page 37 -->
mus[^p37-1]: « Iustitia illius in filios filiorum, his qui servant testamentum eius et memores sunt mandatorum ipsius ad faciendum ea ». — *Exemplorum* ad *imitandum;* Ioannis decimo quinto: « Mementote sermonis mei, quem dixi vobis: Non est discipulus supra magistrum ». — *Suppliciorum* ad *compatiendum;* Threnorum tertio[^p37-2]: « Recordare paupertatis et transgressionis »; et sequitur: « Memoria memor ero, et tabescet in me anima mea ». — *Solatiorum* ad *congaudendum;* Canticorum primo: « Exsultabimus et laetabimur in te, memores uberum tuorum super vinum ». — *Operum* ad *laudandum;* Psalmus[^p37-3]: « Memor fui operum Dei, quia memor ero ab initio mirabilium tuorum, et meditabor in omnibus operibus tuis et in adinventionibus tuis exercebor »; item: « Mementote mirabilium eius, quae fecit ». — *Charismatum* ad *postulandum;* Isaiae vigesimo sexto[^p37-4]: « Domine, sustinuimus te; nomen tuum et memoriale tuum in desiderio animae. Anima mea desideravit te in nocte »; et sexagesimo secundo: « Qui reminiscemini Domini, ne taceatis et ne detis silentium ei, donec stabiliat et donec ponat Ierusalem laudem in terra ».

115. *Apertum est autem illico os eius* etc. Post partum sterilis et circumcisionem et denominationem infantis sequitur *apertio oris sacerdotalis,* secundum dictum nuntii caelestis. Hoc autem miraculum erat ostensivum praerogativae et excellentis magnificentiae in Ioanne, ut merito ostendatur, ipsum vocari hoc nomine[^p37-5]. In huius autem miraculi descriptione insinuantur tria, scilicet miraculi *evidentia* et ipsius *reverentia* et eiusdem *intelligentia.* Nam apparitio miraculi parit admirationem, admiratio cognitionem sive inquisitionem.

116. (Vers. 64.). Praemittit igitur primo *miraculi evidentiam,* cum dicit: *Apertum est autem illico os Zachariae et lingua eius, et loquebatur benedicens Deum; apertum,* inquam, non virtute naturae, sed supernae potentiae; Sapientiae decimo[^p37-6]: « Sapientia aperuit os mutorum et linguas infantium fecit disertas ». Ille, inquam, fecit, de quo admirabantur turbae, dicentes: « Bene omnia fecit, et surdos fecit audire et mutos loqui », scilicet Christi Dei virtus et Dei sapientia, Marci septimo. Et ut ostendatur miraculum *verum,* dicitur *illico;* ut enim dicit Ambrosius[^p37-7]: « Nescit tarda molimina Spiritus sancti gratia ». — Ut ostendatur *plenum* et *perfectum,*
subditur: *Et loquebatur benedicens Deum,* recognoscens beneficium, sicut Tobiae decimo tertio[^p37-8]: « Aperiens autem Tobias senior os suum, benedixit Deum ». In quo instruimur, quod debemus os apertum habere ad divinam laudem; quod oramus in Psalmo: « Domine, labia mea aperies, et os meum annuntiabit laudem tuam »; et maxime post beneficium nostrae redemptionis; Isaiae trigesimo quinto: « Tunc saliet sicut cervus claudus, et aperta erit lingua mutorum ». Sed econtra dicitur de malis Iob decimo sexto[^p37-9]: « Aperuerunt super me ora sua exprobrantes »; quia, secundum quod dicitur in Psalmo, « sepulcrum patens est guttur eorum, linguis suis dolose agebant ».

117. (Vers. 65.). Secundo subdit *miraculi reverentiam,* cum dicit: *Et factus est timor super omnes vicinos eorum, et super omnia montana Iudaeae divulgabantur omnia verba haec;* Psalmus[^p37-10]: « Timebunt omnes, qui habitant terminos, a signis tuis »; unde poterant decantare illud Exodi decimo quinto: « Quis similis tui in fortibus, Domine? Quis similis tui, magnificus in sanctitate »? Et quia reverentiam miraculi consequitur magnificentia praeconii, ideo dicit: *Super omnia montana Iudaeae divulgabantur* etc. Et recte, quia, Tobiae duodecimo[^p37-11], « sacramentum regis abscondere bonum est, opera Dei revelare et confiteri honorificum est »; et ideo Psalmus: « Confitemini Domino et invocate nomen eius »; et post: « Narrate omnia mirabilia eius ».

118. (Vers. 66.). Tertio subiungit *miraculi intelligentiam,* cum dicit: *Et posuerunt omnes, qui audierunt, in corde suo, dicentes: Quis, putas, puer iste erit?* Ecclesiastici quinquagesimo[^p37-12]: « Beatus, qui in istis versatur bonis; qui ponit illa in corde suo, sapiens erit semper »; et Proverbiorum vigesimo quarto: « Per agrum hominis pigri transivi et per vineam viri stulti; et ecce, totum repleverant urticae, operuerunt superficiem eius spinae, et maceria lapidum destructa erat. Quod cum vidissem, posui in corde meo et didici disciplinam ». *Posuerunt,* scilicet per attentionem, intelligentiam et memoriam; et per magnitudinem miraculi cognoscebant magnitudinem pueri; unde dicebant: *Quis, putas, puer iste erit?* quasi dicant: magnus erit, secundum illud Angeli, supra eodem[^p37-13]: « Erit enim magnus coram Domino ». Sic dicebant de Christo; Marci quarto: « Quis, putas, iste est »? Recte de
<!-- page 38 -->
Christo dicebant *est,* tanquam de Creatore, cuius est *manere;* de Ioanne vero *erit,* tanquam de praecursore, cuius erat *pertransire.* — Et rationem reddunt: *Etenim manus Domini erat cum illo;* Ezechielis tertio[^p38-1]: « Manus Domini erat mecum confortans me »; sic et cum Ioanne erat manus Domini dextera conservans et confortans eum; Psalmus: « Fiat manus tua super virum dexterae tuae et super filium hominis, quem confirmasti tibi ». Et sic intelligebant illum, de quo scribitur Isaiae quadragesimo nono: « Posuit os meum quasi gladium acutum, sub tegumento manus suae protexit me ».

## English
<!-- page 34 -->

#### Of the thanksgiving for the birth of the precursor three things are said.

104. *Elizabeth's time was fulfilled* etc. After the thanksgiving for the conception of the Saviour there follows here the thanksgiving for *the birth of the precursor.* Now praise is given to God for the precursor's *birth* rather than for his conception, because he was conceived in sin, but was born full of the Holy Spirit; Christ, however, was most holy as much in his conception as in his birth, therefore praises are sung both before his birth and after his birth. — Now this part has three parts, in the first of which is described *the origin of the precursor;* secondly, for it is subjoined *the praise and thanksgiving,* there[^p34-9]: *And Zachary, his father, was filled with the Holy Spirit,* where the canticle of Zachary is set down; thirdly, as matter for greater praise, is subjoined *the upbringing of the precursor,* there: *And the child grew* etc.
<!-- page 35 -->

#### Of the origin of the precursor, again three things.

Now to the describing of *the birth of the precursor* three things concur, which had been foretold by the Angel. The first is *the barren woman's bringing forth;* the second is *the circumcision of the infant,* which is described there[^p35-1]: *And it came to pass on the eighth day;* the third is *the opening of the priestly mouth,* there: *And immediately his mouth was opened.* — Now *the bringing forth* is shown to have been *opportune in the mother, perfect in the offspring, joyful in the neighbourhood.*

105. (Vers. 57.). *Opportune,* I say, was the bringing forth *in the mother* by reason of the time; on account of which he says: *Elizabeth's time of bringing forth was fulfilled,* and so it was opportune that she should bring forth, because, Ecclesiastes, the third chapter[^p35-2], « all things have their season, and in their times all things pass under heaven ». *Was fulfilled,* I say, according to the fulfilment of the angelic word, as was said above, in the same chapter: « Behold, thou shalt be dumb until the day wherein these things shall come to pass, because thou hast not believed my words, which shall be fulfilled in their time ». Truly *fulfilled,* because now the fulness of the time had come, in which Christ was to come; to the Galatians, the fourth chapter: « But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent his Son » etc.

106. *Perfect* also was it *in the offspring* by reason of *the male sex;* on account of which he says: *And she brought forth a son,* not a daughter. Such offspring Anna asked for, the first of Kings, the first chapter[^p35-3]: « O Lord, if thou wilt be mindful of me, and wilt give to thy servant a man child, I will give him to the Lord all the days of his life ». — She brought forth also *with perfect strength,* no hindrance standing in her way; against which it is said in Isaias, the thirty-seventh chapter[^p35-4]: « The children are come to the birth, and there is not strength to bring forth ». — She brought forth also a son *perfect by supernal grace,* so that there befitted him that of Wisdom, the fourth chapter: « Being made perfect in a short space, he fulfilled a long time ».

107. (Vers. 58.). *Joyful* also was it *in the neighbourhood* by reason of a fame celebrated and common; on account of which he says: *And her neighbours and kinsfolks heard that the Lord had magnified his mercy with her, and they congratulated with her.* And thus was fulfilled that which the Angel had foretold[^p35-5]: « And many shall rejoice in his nativity », namely the neighbours and kinsfolk, because it belongs most of all to such to congratulate; below, the fifteenth chapter: « The woman who hath found the groat that was lost calleth together her friends and neighbours, saying: Congratulate with me, because I have found the groat which I had lost ». *And they congratulated with her;* for she too was rejoicing equally, according to that of John, the sixteenth chapter[^p35-6]: « But when the woman hath brought forth the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world »; and therefore they congratulated with her. — *That the Lord had magnified his mercy with her,* that is, had done great mercy; whence she could say that of the Psalm[^p35-7]: « I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with my whole heart, and I will glorify thy name for ever, for thy mercy is great towards me ». For the Lord magnifies those whom he loves; Wisdom, the last chapter: « In all things thou didst magnify thy people, O Lord, and didst not despise them ».

108. (Vers. 59.). *And it came to pass on the eighth day.* After the barren woman's bringing forth there follows *the circumcision of the infant,* in which, according to custom, *the imposition of the name* was made: first according to *the human appellation from kinship;* secondly, according to *the prophetic revelation from the mother;* thirdly, according to *the authentic affirmation from the father himself.* — The circumcision, then, is introduced with *the human appellation,* when it is said: *On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child,* that is, to fulfil the precept of the Law; Genesis, the seventeenth chapter[^p35-8]: « An infant of eight days old shall be circumcised among you », because « the male, whose flesh of the foreskin shall not be circumcised, shall perish out of his people ». — *And they called him by his father's name Zachary,* according to the customary mode, because sons were accustomed to be called by the name of their fathers; Tobias, the first chapter[^p35-9]: « Tobias begot a son, giving him his own name », so as to signify in this that the father lives on in so noble a son, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the thirtieth chapter: « His father is dead, and he is as if he were not dead; for he hath left one behind him that is like himself. In his life he saw and rejoiced in him ».

109. (Vers. 60.). But since in John the denomination according to the human appellation had no place, therefore there follows the denomination according to *the prophetic revelation in Elizabeth,* when it is said: *And his mother answering, said: Not so; but he shall be called John.* And this indeed she said, taught by the Holy Spirit; whence Ambrose[^p35-10]: « By the spirit of prophecy she learned what she had not learned from her husband ». For since he had been conceived not in the power of nature, but by the power of grace, he ought not to be called *Zachary* from his father, but *John* from the gift of grace, so that, born in a new way, he should be named by a new name; Isaias, the sixty-second chapter: « Thou shalt be called by a new name, which the mouth of the Lord hath named ».

110. (Vers. 61.). And because the kinsfolk savoured carnally, they did not accept the name of grace; therefore it is subjoined:
<!-- page 36 -->
*And they said to her: There is none of thy kindred that is called by this name.* But their insistence was nothing, because, according as Ambrose says[^p36-1], « this name is not of the family, but of the prophet. For the merits of the Saints have this, that they receive a name from God, as Jacob was called Israel, Genesis, the thirty-second chapter, because he saw God »; so of Solomon, the second of Kings, the twelfth chapter: « He called his name *Amiable to the Lord* ». And since there was here a contention between grace and nature, between the human appellation and the prophetic revelation, therefore they sought the father's opinion, that he himself might bring forth an authentic assertion.

111. (Vers. 62.). On this account it follows: *And they made signs to his father, how he would have him called; they made signs,* I say, to one deaf and dumb, because, as Ambrose says[^p36-2], « unbelief had stolen from him speech and hearing »; and yet they sought it from him, because with him resided the authority of naming, both because he was the father, and because he was the priest, and because he had foreknown the birth of the son by divine revelation.

112. (Vers. 63.). And because he could not answer with his voice, he spoke with his hand and with letters; and therefore it is subjoined: *And demanding a writing table, he wrote, saying: John is his name;* as if he were to say: I do not impose the name, but I declare it imposed; above, in the same chapter[^p36-3]: « Thou shalt call his name John », according to that of Isaias, the forty-ninth chapter: « The Lord hath called me from the womb, from the bowels of my mother he hath been mindful of my name ». And rightly is it said *written,* on account of its indelible firmness, so that his name may be shown to be written in the book of life, as were those of Christ's disciples; below, the tenth chapter[^p36-4]: « Rejoice, because your names are written in heaven ». Of this writing it is said in the Apocalypse, the third chapter: « He that shall overcome, I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem »; and again, the second chapter: « I will give him a white counter, and in the counter a name written, which no man knoweth, but he that receiveth it ». — And because they did not know the cause of this denomination, therefore it follows: *And they all wondered;* the Gloss[^p36-5]: « On account of the consonance between father and mother », that now « in the mouth of two witnesses that word might stand ». Or also *they wondered* at so great a prerogative in the child; in Ecclesiasticus, the eleventh chapter, it is said of the just man: « God hath exalted him, and many have wondered at him, and have honoured God ». Whence he himself could say that of Wisdom, the eighth chapter: « The faces of princes shall wonder at me ». In which we are taught that the prerogatives of divine privileges are rather to be wondered at humbly and soberly than to be searched out boldly.

113. *Spiritually* note that by *Zachary* is understood the old priesthood, by *John* the new; and since the old and the new differ as the memorial of a gift and the gift itself, therefore rightly is the one named from *the memorial,* the other from *the gift of grace;* and this indeed rightly enough, because the memory of God in us generates affections of virtue and of grace. For he who wishes to be *John* through grace must first be *Zachary* through the divine memory. For Zachary is interpreted *mindful of God;* the Psalm[^p36-6]: « He hath made a remembrance [so printed: *Memomoriam* for *Memoriam*] of his wonderful works »; Exodus, the twentieth chapter: « You shall make an altar of earth unto me, and you shall offer upon it your holocausts and peace offerings, your sheep and oxen, in every place where the memory of my name shall be; I will come to thee, and will bless thee ».

114. Now each one ought to be mindful of *the sacraments* of God for *believing* and *confessing;* in Exodus, the thirteenth chapter[^p36-7], it is said of the paschal lamb: « It shall be as a sign in thy hand, and as a memorial before thy eyes »; it follows: « Thou shalt keep this observance at the set time from days to days ». — *Of benefits* for *giving thanks in return;* Deuteronomy, the sixteenth chapter[^p36-8]: « Seven days shalt thou eat without leaven the bread of affliction, because thou camest out of Egypt in fear, that thou mayst remember the day of thy coming out of Egypt all the days of thy life »; the Psalm: « He hath made a remembrance » etc. — *Of judgements* for *dreading;* Ecclesiasticus, the eighteenth chapter[^p36-9]: « Remember the wrath in the day of consummation, and the time of retribution in the turning away of the face »; the Psalm: « I remembered thy judgements from of old, O Lord ». — *Of promises* for *awaiting;* Ecclesiasticus, the eleventh chapter: « In the day of evils be not unmindful of good things ». — *Of commandments* for *doing;* the
<!-- page 37 -->
Psalm[^p37-1]: « His justice unto children's children, to such as keep his covenant, and are mindful of his commandments to do them ». — *Of examples* for *imitating;* John, the fifteenth chapter: « Remember my word that I said to you: The disciple is not above the master ». — *Of penalties* for *suffering with;* Lamentations, the third chapter[^p37-2]: « Remember my poverty and transgression »; and it follows: « I will be mindful and remember, and my soul shall languish within me ». — *Of consolations* for *rejoicing with;* the Canticle, the first chapter: « We will be glad and rejoice in thee, remembering thy breasts more than wine ». — *Of works* for *praising;* the Psalm[^p37-3]: « I remembered the works of God; for I will be mindful of thy wonders from the beginning, and I will meditate on all thy works, and will be employed in thy inventions »; likewise: « Remember his marvellous works which he hath done ». — *Of charisms* for *asking;* Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter[^p37-4]: « O Lord, we have patiently waited for thee; thy name and thy memorial are in the desire of the soul. My soul hath desired thee in the night »; and the sixty-second chapter: « You that are mindful of the Lord, hold not your peace, and give him no silence till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth ».

115. *And immediately his mouth was opened* etc. After the barren woman's bringing forth and the circumcision and the denomination of the infant there follows *the opening of the priestly mouth,* according to the saying of the heavenly messenger. Now this miracle was ostensive of the prerogative and excellent magnificence in John, so that it might be shown that he was deservedly called by this name[^p37-5]. Now in the description of this miracle three things are insinuated, namely the *evidence* of the miracle and the *reverence* for it and the *intelligence* of the same. For the appearing of a miracle brings forth wonder, wonder brings forth cognition or inquiry.

116. (Vers. 64.). First, then, he puts forward *the evidence of the miracle,* when he says: *And immediately the mouth of Zachary was opened, and his tongue, and he spoke, blessing God; opened,* I say, not by the power of nature, but of supernal power; Wisdom, the tenth chapter[^p37-6]: « Wisdom opened the mouth of the dumb, and made the tongues of infants eloquent ». He, I say, did it, at whom the multitudes wondered, saying: « He hath done all things well; he hath made both the deaf to hear and the dumb to speak », namely Christ [so printed: *Christi* for *Christus*], the power of God and the wisdom of God, Mark, the seventh chapter. And that the miracle may be shown *true,* it is said *immediately;* for as Ambrose says[^p37-7]: « The grace of the Holy Spirit knows no slow strivings ». — That it may be shown *full* and *perfect,*
it is subjoined: *And he spoke, blessing God,* acknowledging the benefit, as in Tobias, the thirteenth chapter[^p37-8]: « And Tobias the elder, opening his mouth, blessed God ». In which we are instructed that we ought to have the mouth opened for the divine praise; which we pray for in the Psalm: « O Lord, thou wilt open my lips, and my mouth shall declare thy praise »; and most of all after the benefit of our redemption; Isaias, the thirty-fifth chapter: « Then shall the lame man leap as a hart, and the tongue of the dumb shall be opened ». But on the contrary it is said of the wicked in Job, the sixteenth chapter[^p37-9]: « They have opened their mouths upon me, reproaching »; because, as is said in the Psalm, « their throat is an open sepulchre, they dealt deceitfully with their tongues ».

117. (Vers. 65.). Secondly he subjoins *the reverence for the miracle,* when he says: *And fear came upon all their neighbours, and all these words were noised abroad over all the hill country of Judea;* the Psalm[^p37-10]: « They that dwell in the uttermost borders shall be afraid at thy signs »; whence they could sing that of Exodus, the fifteenth chapter: « Who is like to thee among the strong, O Lord? Who is like to thee, magnificent in holiness »? And because upon the reverence for the miracle there follows the magnificence of the proclamation, therefore he says: *Over all the hill country of Judea were noised abroad* etc. And rightly, because, Tobias, the twelfth chapter[^p37-11], « it is good to hide the secret of a king, but honourable to reveal and confess the works of God »; and therefore the Psalm: « Give glory to the Lord, and call upon his name »; and after: « Relate all his wondrous works ».

118. (Vers. 66.). Thirdly he subjoins *the intelligence of the miracle,* when he says: *And all they that had heard laid them up in their heart, saying: What an one, thinkest thou, shall this child be?* Ecclesiasticus, the fiftieth chapter[^p37-12]: « Blessed is he that is conversant in these good things; he that layeth them up in his heart shall be wise always »; and Proverbs, the twenty-fourth chapter: « I passed by the field of the slothful man, and by the vineyard of the foolish man; and behold, it was all filled with nettles, thorns had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall was broken down. Which when I had seen, I laid it up in my heart, and learned discipline ». *They laid them up,* namely by attention, intelligence and memory; and by the greatness of the miracle they recognized the greatness of the child; whence they said: *What an one, thinkest thou, shall this child be?* as if they were to say: he will be great, according to that of the Angel, above, in the same chapter[^p37-13]: « For he shall be great before the Lord ». So they said of Christ; Mark, the fourth chapter: « Who, thinkest thou, is this »? Rightly of
<!-- page 38 -->
Christ they said *is,* as of the Creator, to whom it belongs *to abide;* but of John *shall be,* as of the precursor, to whom it belonged *to pass through.* — And they render the reason: *For the hand of the Lord was with him;* Ezechiel, the third chapter[^p38-1]: « The hand of the Lord was with me, strengthening me »; so also with John was the right hand of the Lord, preserving and strengthening him; the Psalm: « Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, and upon the son of man whom thou hast confirmed for thyself ». And thus they understood him of whom it is written in Isaias, the forty-ninth chapter: « He hath made my mouth like a sharp sword, under the covering of his hand he hath protected me ».
