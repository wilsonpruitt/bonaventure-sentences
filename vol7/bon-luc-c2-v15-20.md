# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. II, Vers. 15-20
## *Qualiter Christi nativitas sit per pastores divulgata*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 51 -->

#### Qualiter Christi nativitas sit per pastores divulgata.

32. *Et factum est, ut discesserunt Angeli* etc. Postquam descripsit Evangelista, qualiter Christi nativitas fuit consummata et qualiter per Angelos denuntiata; hic iam tertio describit, qualiter *per pastores fuit divulgata,* quod quidem factum est diligentia pastorali. Et haec pastorum diligentia ostenditur commendabilis in tribus ab Evangelista, primo scilicet a *sollicitudine inquisitionis;* secundo, a *certitudine inventionis,* ibi[^p51-13]: *Et invenerunt Mariam* etc.; tertio vero, a *gratitudine devotionis,* ibi: *Et reversi sunt.*

*Sollicitudo* autem *inquisitionis* in pastoribus commendatur a tribus, scilicet a *vicissitudine exhortationis,* a *rectitudine intentionis* et a *promptitudine exsecutionis.*

33. (Vers. 15.). Quantum ad *vicissitudinem mutuae cohortationis* dicitur: *Et factum est, ut*
<!-- page 52 -->
*discesserunt ab eis Angeli in caelum, pastores loquebantur ad invicem,* scilicet se mutuo cohortando, secundum illud ad Hebraeos tertio[^p52-1]: « Videte, fratres, ne quando sit in aliquo vestrum cor malum incredulitatis, sed adhortamini vosmetipsos per singulos dies ». Et hoc recte, quia « ferrum ferro acuitur, et homo exacuit faciem amici sui ».

34. Quantum vero ad *rectitudinem intentionis,* subditur secundo: *Transeamus usque in Bethlehem et videamus hoc verbum, quod factum est.* Ideo volebant transire, ut viderent, sicut Moyses; Exodi tertio[^p52-2]: « Vadam et videbo visionem hanc magnam ». Et volebant videre *verbum, quod factum est,* id est rem dignam verbo, vel certe Verbum carnem factum, secundum illud Ioannis primo: « Verbum caro factum est et habitavit in nobis; et vidimus gloriam eius ». *Factum,* inquam, non ab homine, sed a Deo; unde addit: *Quod fecit Dominus*[^p52-3], id est tota Trinitas; Isaiae sexagesimo quarto: « Cum feceris mirabilia, non sustinebimus; descendisti enim, et a facie tua montes defluxerunt ». Nec tantum fecit, sed etiam *ostendit nobis.* Hoc orabat David: « Ostende nobis, Domine, misericordiam tuam, et salutare tuum da nobis »; et Dominus promisit: « Ostendam illi salutare meum ».

35. (Vers. 16.). Quantum autem ad *promptitudinem exsecutionis* subiungitur tertio: *Et venerunt festinantes.* Et hoc est quod dicitur Proverbiorum nono[^p52-4]: « Doce iustum, et festinabit accipere ». Festinantia haec signum erat promptitudinis interioris, qua divino conspectui volebant praesentari; Proverbiorum vigesimo secundo: « Vidisti virum velocem in opere suo? Coram regibus stabit ». Unde dicebant intra se illud ad Hebraeos quarto: « Festinemus ingredi in illam requiem, ut ne in id ipsum quis incidat incredulitatis exemplum ».

36. Nota, quod volenti *spiritualiter* invenire Christum haec tria sunt necessaria: scilicet *colloqui* per meditationem Scripturarum; Ecclesiastici nono[^p52-5]: « Cum sapientibus et prudentibus tracta »; « Et omnis enarratio tua sit in praeceptis Altissimi ». — *Transire* per contemplationem creaturarum; Canticorum tertio: « Surgam et circuibo civitatem, per vicos et plateas quaeram, quem diligit anima mea »; et post: « Paululum cum pertransissem eos, inveni quem diligit anima mea »; et Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto: « Transite ad me omnes, qui concupiscitis me, a generationibus meis adimplemini ». — *Festinare* per degustationem gratiarum; Canticorum secundo[^p52-6]: « Surge, propera, amica mea »; et Canticorum quinto: « Pessulum ostii mei aperui dilecto meo » etc. Et ideo supra primo de Maria dicitur, quod « abiit in montana cum festinatione ».

37. *Et invenerunt Mariam* etc. Hic secundo commendatur pastorum diligentia a *certitudine inventionis,* cuius laus ex tribus colligitur: quia fuit *certitudinaliter comprobata, celeriter*[^p52-7] *divulgata* et *memoriter conservata.*

Primo quidem fuit *certitudinaliter comprobata,* quia per probabilia signa et evidentia; propter quod dicitur: *Et invenerunt Mariam et Ioseph et infantem positum in praesepio.* Ideo *invenerunt,* quia sollicite quaesiverunt et absque fictione; unde dicitur Sapientiae primo[^p52-8]: « In simplicitate cordis quaerite illum, quoniam invenitur ab his qui non tentant illum ». — Et nota, quod invenitur cum *Maria* virgine et *Ioseph,* viro iusto, et in *praesepio,* quia eum qui vult Christum invenire, oportet habere *munditiam* respectu sui, *iustitiam* respectu proximi, *humilitatem* et reverentiam respectu Dei. Et ista sunt tria munera, quae offerunt reges Christo infanti, et ideo ab eis voluit inveniri; Matthaei secundo[^p52-9]: « Et intrantes domum, invenerunt puerum cum Maria, matre eius, et procidentes adoraverunt eum, et apertis thesauris suis, obtulerunt ei munera, aurum, thus et myrrham ». — In hoc etiam instruimur, ut, si Christum volumus invenire, prius ad Mariam debeamus accedere; de ipsa enim dicitur supra primo[^p52-10]: « Invenisti gratiam apud Dominum ». Ipsa ergo invenit gratiam et misericordiam coram Assuero « super omnes mulieres », sicut dicitur Esther secundo. Et ideo « accedamus cum fiducia ad thronum gratiae, ut misericordiam consequamur et gratiam inveniamus in auxilio opportuno ».

38. (Vers. 17.). Et quia hoc signum datum est ab Angelo, ideo subditur: *Videntes autem cognoverunt de verbo, quod dictum erat illis de puero hoc,* scilicet, quod esset verum. Unde iam poterant
<!-- page 53 -->
dicere illud Samaritanorum ipsi Angelo[^p53-1]: « Iam non propter loquelam tuam credimus, ipsi enim audivimus et scimus, quia hic est vere Salvator mundi ». Vere *cognoverunt,* quia de ovibus erant illius pastoris, qui dicit Ioannis decimo: « Cognosco meas, et cognoscunt me meae ». Hoc desiderabat Iob vigesimo tertio: « Quis mihi det, ut cognoscam et inveniam illum et veniam usque ad solium eius »?

39. (Vers. 18.). Non solum fuit certitudinaliter approbata, sed etiam *celeriter divulgata;* propter quod subditur: *Et omnes, qui audierant, mirati sunt,* scilicet de parvulo nato, *et de his quae dicta erant a pastoribus ad ipsos,* quae scilicet audierant ab Angelo. Unde de viro iusto dicitur Ecclesiastici undecimo[^p53-2]: « Admirati sunt in illo multi et honorificaverunt Deum ». Videbant enim, *verba pastorum* non contemnenda, sed admiranda; Ambrosius: « Nec contemnenda putes quasi vilia verba prophetarum; a pastoribus etiam Maria fidem colligit, a pastoribus populus ad Dei reverentiam congregatur; plebem enim Dominus simplicem requisivit, quae phalerare audita et fucare nesciret ». — *Mirati* sunt etiam, quia parvulus non videbatur eis contemptibilis, sed admirabilis. Unde Isaiae nono[^p53-3]: « Parvulus natus est nobis, et filius datus est nobis, et vocabitur nomen eius *Admirabilis* ». Ubique enim mirabilis Christus apparet, non solum *nascens,* sicut hic, sed etiam *proficiens;* infra eodem[^p53-4]: « Et stupebant super prudentia et responsis eius ». — Admirabilis etiam *docens;* infra quarto: « Mirabantur in verbis gratiae, quae procedebant de ore ipsius ». — Admirabilis *tacens;* Marci decimo quinto: « Iesus autem amplius nihil respondit, ita ut miraretur Pilatus ». — Admirabilis *in actione;* infra octavo[^p53-5]: « Quis, putas, est hic, quia et ventis et mari imperat, et obediunt ei »? — Admirabilis *in passione;* Marci decimo quinto: « Pilatus autem mirabatur, si iam obiisset » etc. — Admirabilis *in resurrectione;* Lucae ultimo: « Petrus, videns linteamina sola posita, abiit, secum mirans quod factum fuerat ». — Admirabilis *in ascensione;* Psalmus[^p53-6]: « Domine Dominus noster, quam admirabile est nomen tuum in universa terra! Quoniam elevata est magnificentia tua super caelos ». — Admirabilis *in extremo examine;* Sapientiae octavo: « Acutus inveniar in iudicio, et in conspectu prudentium admirabilis ero ».

40. (Vers. 19.). Fuit etiam tertio *memoriter conservata;* unde subditur: *Maria autem conservabat omnia verba haec, conferens in corde suo;* tanquam prudens et discreta, secundum illud Proverbiorum tertio[^p53-7]: « Misericordia et veritas te non deserant. Circumda eas gutturi tuo, et describe in tabulis cordis tui »; et Ecclesiastici quinquagesimo: « Qui ponit illa in corde sapiens erit semper ». Econtra dicitur Ecclesiastici vigesimo primo de fatuo: « Cor fatui quasi vas confractum, et omnem sapientiam non tenebit ». — Unde Virgo fuit *arca* continens divinorum eloquiorum arcana; et ideo per arcam Moysi designatur, de qua dicitur Hebraeorum nono[^p53-8], quod continebat tabulas legis divinae. Et quia non solum conservabat, sed etiam intelligebat; ideo additur: *Conferens ea in corde suo,* ita ut posset dicere illud Psalmi: « In corde meo abscondi eloquia tua ».

41. (Vers. 20.). *Et reversi sunt pastores* etc. Hic commendatur diligentia pastorum a *gratitudine devotionis.* Ad hoc autem, quod devotio sit Deo accepta, necesse est, quod simul[^p53-9] resonent et concordent *manus, mens* et *lingua: manus* operando, *lingua* laudando, *mens* recognoscendo. — Laudabilis ergo erat pastorum devotio propter *debitam operationem,* ratione cuius dicitur: *Reversi sunt pastores,* scilicet ad custodiam gregis commissi. In quo erudiuntur devoti, ut post contemplationem Salvatoris revertantur ad exercitium actionis; Ezechielis primo[^p53-10]: « Animalia ibant et revertebantur in similitudinem fulguris coruscantis »; et infra octavo: « Redi in domum tuam et narra, quanta fecit tibi Deus ». Huius figura habetur in Iosue secundo: « Reversi sunt et descenderunt exploratores de monte et venerunt ad Iosue ». In quo significatur, quod tempore tentationis recurrendum est ad orationem et postmodum redeundum est ad actionem.

42. Laudabilis etiam fuit propter *debitam gratiarum actionem,* ratione cuius subditur: *Glorificantes et laudantes Deum;* Ecclesiastici
<!-- page 54 -->
quadragesimo tertio[^p54-1]: « Glorificantes Dominum, quantumcumque potueritis; supervalebit adhuc, et admirabilis magnificentia eius. Benedicentes Dominum, exaltate eum, quantumcumque potestis; maior est omni laude ». Quocontra dicitur de sapientibus mundanis ad Romanos primo[^p54-2]: « Qui cum Deum cognovissent, non sicut Deum glorificaverunt, aut gratias egerunt, sed evanuerunt in cogitationibus suis, et obscuratum est insipiens cor eorum. Dicentes enim, se esse sapientes, stulti facti sunt ». Et ideo qui non vult excaecari necesse habet Deo de beneficiis suis regratiari; ideo dicitur ad Colossenses tertio: « Omne, quod facitis in verbo aut in opere, omnia facite in nomine Domini nostri Iesu Christi, gratias agentes Deo ».

43. Fuit etiam laudabilis propter *debitam recognitionem,* ratione cuius subiungitur: *In omnibus, quae audierant et viderant, sicut dictum est ad illos;* unde poterant dicere illud Psalmi[^p54-3]: « Sicut audivimus, sic vidimus in civitate Dei nostri ». Tunc verificatum est istud Psalmi: « Ecce, audivimus eam in Ephrata, invenimus eam in campis silvae ». Et nota, quod dicitur: *In omnibus, quae audierant et viderant;* in quo ostenditur *plenitudo gratitudinis;* ad Ephesios quinto: « Impleamini Spiritu sancto, loquentes vobismetipsis in psalmis, hymnis et canticis spiritualibus, cantantes et psallentes in cordibus vestris Deo, gratias agentes semper pro omnibus in nomine Domini nostri Iesu Christi Deo et Patri ». — Et nota *ordinem,* quo dicit: *In omnibus, quae audierant et viderant;* quia, Isaiae septimo[^p54-4], secundum aliam litteram, « nisi credideritis, non intelligetis »; sed ad Romanos decimo: « Ergo fides ex auditu », sed intelligentia ex visu, infra decimo: « Beati oculi, qui vident quae vos videtis »; Iob ultimo: « Auditu auris audivi te, nunc autem oculus meus videt te ».

## English
<!-- page 51 -->

#### How the nativity of Christ was spread abroad by the shepherds.

32. *And it came to pass, after the angels departed* etc. After the Evangelist has described how the nativity of Christ was consummated and how it was declared by the Angels, here now, thirdly, he describes how it was *spread abroad by the shepherds,* which indeed was done by pastoral diligence. And this diligence of the shepherds is shown by the Evangelist to be commendable in three things: first, namely, from the *solicitude of inquiry;* secondly, from the *certitude of finding,* there[^p51-13]: *And they found Mary* etc.; but thirdly, from the *gratitude of devotion,* there: *And they returned.*

Now the *solicitude of inquiry* in the shepherds is commended from three things, namely from the *interchange of exhortation,* from the *rectitude of intention* and from the *promptitude of execution.*

33. (Vers. 15.). As to *the interchange of mutual encouragement* it is said: *And it came to pass, after the*
<!-- page 52 -->
*angels departed from them into heaven, the shepherds spoke one to another,* namely by encouraging one another, according to that to the Hebrews, the third chapter[^p52-1]: « Take heed, brethren, lest perhaps there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, but exhort one another every day ». And this rightly, because « iron is sharpened by iron, and a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend ».

34. But as to *the rectitude of intention,* it is added, secondly: *Let us go over to Bethlehem, and let us see this word which has been made.* For this reason they wished to go over, that they might see, as Moses did; Exodus, the third chapter[^p52-2]: « I will go, and see this great sight ». And they wished to see *the word which has been made,* that is, a thing worthy of the word, or surely the Word made flesh, according to that of John, the first chapter: « The Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us; and we saw his glory ». *Made,* I say, not by man, but by God; whence he adds: *Which the Lord hath made*[^p52-3], that is, the whole Trinity; Isaias, the sixty-fourth chapter: « When thou shalt do wonderful things, we shall not bear them; for thou didst come down, and at thy presence the mountains melted away ». Nor did he only make it, but he also *hath shewed it to us.* This David prayed for: « Shew us, O Lord, thy mercy, and grant us thy salvation »; and the Lord promised: « I will shew him my salvation ».

35. (Vers. 16.). But as to *the promptitude of execution* it is subjoined, thirdly: *And they came with haste.* And this is what is said in Proverbs, the ninth chapter[^p52-4]: « Teach a just man, and he shall make haste to receive ». This haste was a sign of the interior promptitude with which they wished to be presented to the divine sight; Proverbs, the twenty-second chapter: « Hast thou seen a man swift in his work? He shall stand before kings ». Whence they said within themselves that of the Hebrews, the fourth chapter: « Let us hasten to enter into that rest, lest any man fall into the same example of unbelief ».

36. Note that for one who wishes to find Christ *spiritually* these three things are necessary: namely *to speak together* through meditation on the Scriptures; Ecclesiasticus, the ninth chapter[^p52-5]: « Treat with the wise and prudent »; « And let all thy discourse be in the precepts of the Highest ». — *To go over* through the contemplation of creatures; the Canticle, the third chapter: « I will rise, and will go about the city: in the streets and the broad ways I will seek him whom my soul loveth »; and after: « When I had a little passed by them, I found him whom my soul loveth »; and Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth chapter: « Come over to me, all ye that desire me, and be filled with my fruits ». — *To hasten* through the tasting of graces; the Canticle, the second chapter[^p52-6]: « Arise, make haste, my love »; and the Canticle, the fifth chapter: « I opened the bolt of my door to my beloved » etc. And therefore it is said of Mary above, the first chapter, that she « went into the hill country with haste ».

37. *And they found Mary* etc. Here, secondly, the diligence of the shepherds is commended from the *certitude of finding,* whose praise is gathered from three things: because it was *proven with certainty, speedily*[^p52-7] *spread abroad* and *kept in memory.*

First, indeed, it was *proven with certainty,* because through probable and evident signs; on account of which it is said: *And they found Mary and Joseph, and the infant lying in the manger.* Therefore *they found,* because they sought solicitously and without feigning; whence it is said in Wisdom, the first chapter[^p52-8]: « Seek him in simplicity of heart, for he is found by them that tempt him not ». — And note that he is found with *Mary* the virgin and *Joseph,* a just man, and in the *manger,* because he who wishes to find Christ must have *cleanness* in respect of himself, *justice* in respect of his neighbour, *humility* and reverence in respect of God. And these are the three gifts which the kings offer to the infant Christ, and therefore he willed to be found by them; Matthew, the second chapter[^p52-9]: « And entering into the house, they found the child with Mary his mother, and falling down they adored him; and opening their treasures, they offered him gifts, gold, frankincense and myrrh ». — In this we are also instructed that, if we wish to find Christ, we ought first to approach Mary; for of her it is said above, the first chapter[^p52-10]: « Thou hast found grace with the Lord ». She, then, found grace and mercy before Assuerus « above all the women », as is said in Esther, the second chapter. And therefore « let us go with confidence to the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace in seasonable aid ».

38. (Vers. 17.). And because this sign was given by the Angel, therefore it is added: *And seeing, they knew of the word that had been spoken to them concerning this child,* namely, that it was true. Whence now they could
<!-- page 53 -->
say to the Angel himself that of the Samaritans[^p53-1]: « Now we believe not for thy saying; for we ourselves have heard, and know that this is indeed the Saviour of the world ». Truly *they knew,* because they were of the sheep of that shepherd who says, John, the tenth chapter: « I know mine, and mine know me ». This Job desired, the twenty-third chapter: « Who will grant me that I might know and find him, and come even to his throne »?

39. (Vers. 18.). It was not only approved with certainty, but also *speedily spread abroad;* on account of which it is added: *And all that heard wondered,* namely at the little one born, *and at those things that were told them by the shepherds,* which, namely, they had heard from the Angel. Whence of the just man it is said in Ecclesiasticus, the eleventh chapter[^p53-2]: « Many have wondered at him, and have glorified God ». For they saw that *the words of the shepherds* were not to be despised, but to be wondered at; Ambrose: « Do not think the words of the prophets to be despised as mean; from the shepherds also Mary gathers faith, by the shepherds the people is gathered together to the reverence of God; for the Lord sought out a simple people, which would not know how to trick out and to colour what it had heard ». — *They wondered* also because the little one did not seem to them contemptible, but wonderful. Whence Isaias, the ninth chapter[^p53-3]: « A child is born to us, and a son is given to us, and his name shall be called *Wonderful* ». For everywhere Christ appears marvellous, not only *being born,* as here, but also *advancing;* below, in the same[^p53-4]: « And they were astonished at his prudence and his answers ». — Wonderful also *teaching;* below, the fourth chapter: « They wondered at the words of grace that proceeded from his mouth ». — Wonderful *keeping silence;* Mark, the fifteenth chapter: « But Jesus still answered nothing, so that Pilate wondered ». — Wonderful *in action;* below, the eighth chapter[^p53-5]: « Who, thinkest thou, is this, that he commandeth both the winds and the sea, and they obey him »? — Wonderful *in the passion;* Mark, the fifteenth chapter: « But Pilate wondered if he were already dead » etc. — Wonderful *in the resurrection;* Luke, the last chapter: « Peter, seeing the linen cloths laid by themselves, went away, wondering in himself at that which was come to pass ». — Wonderful *in the ascension;* the Psalm[^p53-6]: « O Lord our Lord, how wonderful is thy name in the whole earth! For thy magnificence is elevated above the heavens ». — Wonderful *in the last examination;* Wisdom, the eighth chapter: « I shall be found acute in judgement, and in the sight of the prudent I shall be wonderful ».

40. (Vers. 19.). It was also, thirdly, *kept in memory;* whence it is added: *But Mary kept all these words, pondering them in her heart;* as one prudent and discreet, according to that of Proverbs, the third chapter[^p53-7]: « Let not mercy and truth leave thee. Put them about thy neck, and write them in the tables of thy heart »; and Ecclesiasticus, the fiftieth chapter: « He that layeth them in his heart shall be wise always ». On the other hand it is said in Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-first chapter, of the fool: « The heart of a fool is like a broken vessel, and no wisdom at all shall it hold ». — Whence the Virgin was an *ark* containing the secrets of the divine words; and therefore she is designated by the ark of Moses, of which it is said in Hebrews, the ninth chapter[^p53-8], that it contained the tables of the divine law. And because she not only kept them, but also understood them, therefore it is added: *Pondering them in her heart,* so that she could say that of the Psalm: « In my heart I have hidden thy words ».

41. (Vers. 20.). *And the shepherds returned* etc. Here the diligence of the shepherds is commended from the *gratitude of devotion.* Now for devotion to be acceptable to God, it is necessary that *hand, mind* and *tongue* resound and accord together[^p53-9]: *the hand* by working, *the tongue* by praising, *the mind* by recognizing. — The devotion of the shepherds, then, was praiseworthy on account of *due working,* by reason of which it is said: *The shepherds returned,* namely to the keeping of the flock committed to them. In which the devout are schooled, that after the contemplation of the Saviour they return to the exercise of action; Ezechiel, the first chapter[^p53-10]: « The living creatures went and returned like flashes of lightning »; and below, the eighth chapter: « Return to thy house, and tell how great things God hath done to thee ». The figure of this is had in Josue, the second chapter: « The spies returned and came down from the mountain and came to Josue ». In which it is signified that in the time of temptation one must run back to prayer and afterwards return to action.

42. It was praiseworthy also on account of *due thanksgiving,* by reason of which it is added: *Glorifying and praising God;* Ecclesiasticus,
<!-- page 54 -->
the forty-third chapter[^p54-1]: « Glorifying the Lord as much as ever you can, he will yet far exceed, and his magnificence is wonderful. Blessing the Lord, exalt him as much as you can; he is greater than all praise ». On the contrary it is said of the wise of the world, to the Romans, the first chapter[^p54-2]: « When they knew God, they did not glorify him as God, or give thanks; but became vain in their thoughts, and their foolish heart was darkened. For saying that they were wise, they became fools ». And therefore he who does not wish to be blinded must render thanks to God for his benefits; therefore it is said to the Colossians, the third chapter: « All that you do in word or in work, do all in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, giving thanks to God ».

43. It was praiseworthy also on account of *due recognition,* by reason of which it is subjoined: *For all the things they had heard and seen, as it was told unto them;* whence they could say that of the Psalm[^p54-3]: « As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of our God ». Then was verified that of the Psalm: « Behold, we have heard of it in Ephrata, we have found it in the fields of the wood ». And note that it is said: *For all the things they had heard and seen;* in which is shown *the fulness of gratitude;* to the Ephesians, the fifth chapter: « Be ye filled with the Holy Spirit, speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual canticles, singing and making melody in your hearts to God, giving thanks always for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God and the Father ». — And note *the order* in which he says: *For all the things they had heard and seen;* because, Isaias, the seventh chapter[^p54-4], according to another text, « unless you believe, you shall not understand »; but to the Romans, the tenth chapter: « Faith then cometh by hearing », but intelligence by sight, below, the tenth chapter: « Blessed are the eyes that see the things which you see »; Job, the last chapter: « With the hearing of the ear I have heard thee, but now my eye seeth thee ».
