# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XI, Vers. 14-26
## *Confutatur fallacia blasphemantium per tria*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 288 -->
36. *Et erat Iesus*[^p288-4] *eiiciens daemonium* etc. Sicut habitum fuit in principio libri, liber iste habet quatuor partes; quarum prima est de Christi *natura;* secunda vero, de Christi *doctrina;* et haec incepit supra quarto capitulo[^p288-5], et habet partes duas secundum duo opera sapientis, « quorum primum est non mentiri; secundum, mentientem manifestare posse ». Prima igitur pars est de veritate declaranda ad discipulorum informationem; secunda, de falsitate confutanda per Iudaeorum adversantium confutationem.

#### *Secundo confutatur falsitas Iudaeorum adversantium quadrupliciter.*

Prima parte terminata, hic incipit secunda. Dividitur autem haec pars in quatuor. In quarum prima parte confutat Iudaeorum *dolositatem;* in secunda, *impietatem; Erant appropinquantes,* infra decimo quinto[^p288-6]; in tertia *curiositatem,* infra decimo septimo: *Interrogatus autem a Pharisaeis* etc.; in quarta, *incredulitatem,* infra decimo nono: *Et factum est, cum appropinquasset Iesus* etc.

#### Dolositas Iudaeorum calumniantium miracula confutatur dupliciter.

In confutando autem *dolositatem Iudaeorum* primo confutat dolositatem calumniantium miracula ex parte causae *efficientis;* in secunda vero, ex parte *temporis,* infra decimo tertio[^p288-7]: *Erat autem docens in synagogis eorum Sabbatis.*

#### Primo confutantur Iudaei calumniantes miracula ex parte causae efficientis, deinde discipuli informantur.

Prima in duas dividitur. In quarum prima *confutat Iudaeorum dolositatem et fallaciam;* in secunda *informat discipulos suos ad illam vitandam,* infra duodecimo[^p288-8]: *Multis autem turbis* etc.

#### Primo, de Iudaeorum dolositate et triplici fallacia.

Prima pars, quae continet praesens capitulum, dividitur in partes tres. In quarum prima confutat fallaciam *blasphemantium;* in secunda, fallaciam *tentantium* sive discredentium, infra[^p288-9]: *Turbis autem concurrentibus;* in tertia vero, fallaciam *simulantium,* ibi: *Nemo accendit lucernam.* Primum istorum est in *verbo,* secundum autem est in *animo,* tertium autem consistit in *quocumque signo.*

#### Confutatur fallacia blasphemantium per tria.

Ad confutandam autem *fallaciam blasphemantium* tria introducuntur. Primo enim fit *expressio fraudis Iudaicae;* secundo vero, *improbatio fraudis expressae,* ibi[^p288-10]: *Ipse autem, ut vidit cogitationes eorum* etc.; tertio vero, *commendatio veritatis ostensae,* ibi: *Factum est autem, dum haec diceret* etc. — Circa *expressionem Iudaicae fraudis* tria explicat Evangelista: primum est *materia laudis divinae,* secundum est *blasphemia fraudis humanae,* tertium est *controversia litis adiunctae.*

37. (Vers. 14.). Primo igitur quantum ad *materiam laudis divinae* dicit: *Et erat Iesus eiiciens daemonium, et illud erat mutum;* in quo simul tanguntur duo miracula, immo etiam tria. Unde Beda in Glossa[^p288-11]: « Matthaeus hunc daemoniacum etiam caecum fuisse dicit, in quo tria miracula a Domino
<!-- page 289 -->
fiunt: quia *caecus* videt, *mutus* loquitur, *possessus* a daemone liberatur ». Et in hoc erat *materia laudis divinae* tam ex parte curati quam assistentium. Propter quod dicit: *Et cum eiecisset daemonium, locutus est mutus, et admiratae sunt turbae;* et sic impletum est illud Isaiae trigesimo quinto[^p289-1]: « Tunc aperientur oculi caecorum, et aures surdorum patebunt; tunc saliet sicut cervus claudus, et aperta erit lingua mutorum ». Tanta autem miracula simul facta sunt *admirationis* et *laudis* causa, secundum illud Ecclesiastici quadragesimo tertio[^p289-2]: « Terribilis Dominus et magnus vehementer, et mirabilis potentia eius »; et post: « Glorificantes Dominum, quantumcumque potueritis, supervalebit adhuc, et admirabilis magnificentia eius ». Unde vere poterant isti dicere illud Marci septimo: « Bene omnia fecit, et surdos fecit audire et mutos loqui ».

38. Secundum autem *spiritualem intelligentiam daemonium* est peccatum, quod facit *surdum* ad veritatem audiendam, *caecum* ad videndam et *mutum* ad confitendam. Unde Chrysostomus[^p289-3]: « O nequitia daemonis! Alterutrum ingressum, per quem crediturus erat homo, oppilavit: *auditum* et *visum* », et tertium, id est *linguam,* ne confiteatur. Unde dicitur Isaiae quadragesimo primo[^p289-4]: « Non est annuntians et non est praedicans neque audiens sermones meos »; et iterum quadragesimo secundo: « *Surdi* audite, et *caeci* intuemini ad videndum. Quis caecus nisi servus meus? Et quis surdus, nisi ad quem nuntios meos misi »? Talis etiam *mutus* est ad confitendum; unde Ecclesiastici decimo quinto: « Non est speciosa laus in ore peccatoris ».

39. Et nota, quod cum ad tria sit data homini loquela, scilicet ad *laudandum Deum,* ad *aedificandum proximum* et ad *accusandum se ipsum;* haec triplex loquela aufertur per triplex daemonium. Primam aufert spiritus *luxuriae,* secundum illud Isaiae primo[^p289-5]: « Cum multiplicaveritis orationes vestras, non exaudiam. Manus enim vestrae sanguine plenae sunt ». — Secundam aufert spiritus *avaritiae,* qui facit sibi soli intendere, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo quinto: « Qui unum talentum acceperat abiens fodit in terram ». — Tertiam aufert spiritus *superbiae,* qui non permittit, quod homo accuset se, secundum illud Psalmi[^p289-6]: « Quoniam tacui, inveteraverunt ossa mea »; et Ieremiae secundo: « Ecce, ego iudicio contendam tecum, pro eo quod dixeris: Non peccavi ». — De hoc triplici daemonio dicitur Apocalypsis decimo sexto: « Vidi, de ore bestiae et de ore draconis et de ore pseudoprophetae exire tres spiritus immundos in modum ranarum ». — Primum daemonium eiicitur per *ieiunium,* secundum illud Matthaei decimo septimo[^p289-7]: « Hoc genus daemoniorum non eiicitur nisi per ieiunium ». Secundum eiicitur per *memoriam passionis dominicae,* secundum illud Tobiae sexto[^p289-8]: « De corde piscis si particulam super carbones ponas, fumus eius extricat omne genus daemoniorum » etc. Tertium per *orationem;* primi Regum decimo sexto: « Tollebat David citharam et percutiebat manu sua, et refocillabatur Saul et levius habebat; recedebat enim ab eo spiritus malus ».

40. (Vers. 15.). Secundo quantum ad *blasphemiam fraudis humanae* subditur: *Quidam autem ex eis dixerunt: In Beelzebub, principe daemoniorum, eiicit daemonia.* Et haec erat magna blasphemia, quia quod fiebat a Spiritu sancto attribuebant spiritui maligno; unde Matthaei duodecimo[^p289-9] statim post hoc dicitur: « Omne peccatum et blasphemia remittetur hominibus, spiritus autem blasphemiae non remittetur ». In hanc blasphemiam prorumpebant ex invidia; unde Chrysostomus[^p289-10]: « Non quando magna dicebat, sed quando faciebat salutes hominum, tunc institerunt Pharisaei ». « Invidia enim non quaerit, quid dicat, sed solum, ut dicat ». « Aemulatione namque non est aliqua malitia deterior. Adulter enim brevi complet peccatum suum, aemulator nunquam quiescit ». — Et quia blasphemia ista ex malitia et invidia procedebat, pro tanto hoc Evangelium legitur, quando cantatur historia Ioseph[^p289-11] in tertia Dominica Quadragesimae. Sicut enim fratres Ioseph ex invidia vocabant eum *inventorem somniorum,* sic et isti dicebant Christum *invocatorem daemonum.* Et maxime exprimunt *Beelzebub,* tum quia istud idolum erat famosum; unde
<!-- page 290 -->
quarti Regum primo[^p290-1] dicitur, quod « Ochozias misit nuntios suos dicens: Ite et consulite Beelzebub, deum Accaron »; et Beda in Glossa: « Asserebant Iudaei, in hoc simulacro principem daemoniorum habitare. Per eius potestatem dicebant Iesum daemonia eiicere »; et ideo in textu dicitur: *In principe daemoniorum;* sive quia nomen huius idoli erat ridiculosum; unde tantum sonat *Beelzebub,* quantum *vir muscarum;* quo nomine vocabant eum Iudaei irrisorie, sicut dicitur in Glossa[^p290-2], « propter sordes cruoris, qui in templo eius immolabatur ». Unde sic blasphemiam proferebant, sicut Ioannis octavo, ubi dicitur: « Nonne bene dicimus nos, quia Samaritanus es tu et daemonium habes »?

41. (Vers. 16.). Tertio vero quantum ad *controversiam litis adiunctae* subdit: *Et alii tentantes signum de caelo quaerebant ab eo.* Non quaerebant huiusmodi signum ad credendum ut fideles, sed ut rebelles ad impugnandum, secundum illud Salvatoris Matthaei vigesimo secundo[^p290-3]: « Quid me tentatis, hypocritae »? et in Psalmo: « Tentaverunt me patres vestri » etc. Et hoc malum, sicut dicitur primae ad Corinthios decimo: « Neque tentemus Christum, sicut quidam eorum tentaverunt et a serpentibus perierunt ». Petebant autem *signum de caelo,* sicut *tonitruum* tempore Samuelis, primi Regum duodecimo[^p290-4]; aut *emissionem ignis* tempore Eliae, tertii Regum decimo octavo et quarti Regum primo; aut sicut *retrocessionem solis* tempore Ezechiae, quarti Regum vigesimo. — Hoc autem erat Iudaeis proprium, sive per *assuefactionem;* unde primae ad Corinthios primo[^p290-5]: « Iudaei signa quaerunt, et Graeci sapientiam »; et ideo Petrus potens in signis fuit Apostolus ad Iudaeos, sed Paulus in sapientia, ad gentes; sive etiam propter *incredulitatem* et *duritiam,* secundum quod dixit Stephanus Actuum septimo[^p290-6]: « Dura cervice, et incircumcisis cordibus et auribus, vos semper Spiritui sancto restitistis ». Unde Chrysostomus[^p290-7]: « Semper *discere* signum est nunquam posse proficere; sic semper *testimonium quaerere* signum est nunquam velle credere ». Et hoc quidem verum est; quando enim sufficientia testimonia habebant in terris, non oportebat quaerere signa de caelis; unde Ioannis decimo[^p290-8]: « Opera, quae ego facio in nomine Patris mei, ipsa testimonia perhibent de me ».

42. *Spiritualiter* autem Dominus dedit *signa in terris,* scilicet signa *humilitatis et paupertatis,* secundum illud supra secundo[^p290-9]: « Hoc vobis signum: invenietis infantem pannis involutum ». Sed multi huic signo non credunt, ut superbi et ambitiosi, qui quaerunt Christum non in humilitate crucis, sed in appetitu honoris et laudis. Et tamen dicitur Matthaei vigesimo quarto: « Tunc apparebit signum Filii hominis in caelo » etc. Huic autem contradicunt superbi, secundum illud supra secundo[^p290-10]: « Positus est hic in signum, cui contradicetur », quia non placet eis status humilitatis, sed dignitatis. Unde in Psalmo: « Posuerunt signa sua signa », scilicet superbiae et lasciviae, secundum illud Sapientiae secundo: « Relinquamus ubique signa laetitiae nostrae », sine quibus signis nolunt sequi; unde in Psalmo: « Signa nostra non vidimus » etc.

43. *Ipse autem, ut vidit cogitationes eorum, dixit eis.* Post expressionem fraudis Iudaicae subditur hic *improbatio fraudis expressae*[^p290-11]. Habet autem pars ista tres. In quarum prima ponitur *improbatio falsitatis;* in secunda vero, *comprobatio veritatis,* ibi[^p290-12]: *Cum fortis armatus custodit* etc.; in tertia, *reprobatio dolositatis,* ibi: *Cum immundus spiritus exierit ab homine* etc.

In *improbando falsitatem* procedit hoc ordine: primo deducit ad *manifestum impossibile,* secundo perducit ad *falsum improbabile,* tertio reducit ad *verum infallibile.*

44. (Vers. 17. 18.). Primo igitur deducit ad *manifestum impossibile,* cum dicit: *Omne regnum in se ipsum divisum desolabitur;* in quo intendit tale argumentum facere: omne regnum divisum hoc ipso, quod dividitur in se, destruitur; sed si unus daemon eiicit alium, satanas in se ipsum divisus est: ergo non stat eius regnum. Sequitur igitur, quod satanas ipse destruat suum regnum et auferat sibi
<!-- page 291 -->
*posse* et dominium. Sed hoc est impossibile manifestum; et sequitur ex dicto Pharisaeorum: ergo illud fuit manifeste falsum. Huius igitur rationis *maiorem* praemittit, cum dicit: *Omne regnum in se ipsum divisum desolabitur, et domus supra domum cadet.* Et hoc quidem est per se notum et verum est, quia divisio est causa ruinae; unde causa praedicati est in subiecto. Et hoc est quod dicit Hieronymus[^p291-1]: « Concordia parvae res crescunt, discordia maximae dilabuntur ». Unitas enim est causa salvationis, et divisio interitus, secundum illud Osee decimo[^p291-2]: « Divisum est cor eorum, nunc interibunt »; et Proverbiorum vigesimo octavo: « Propter peccata terrae multi principes eius ». Exemplum huius est Genesis undecimo de aedificantibus turrem, cum essent « labii unius », ubi subditur: « Divisit eos Dominus ex eo loco in universas terras, et cessaverunt aedificare ». Unde in finali statu mundi de unitatis eius destructione dicitur infra vigesimo primo[^p291-3]: « Surget gens contra gentem, et regnum adversus regnum, et terraemotus magni » etc. — Deinde adiungit *minorem,* cum dicit: *Si autem*[^p291-4] *satanas in se ipsum divisus est,* id est, contra se ipsum pugnat; quod dicit *sub conditione,* quia hoc sequitur ex verbo praedictorum scribarum et Pharisaeorum, tamen in se non est verum; satanas enim satanae concordat in malo. Unde Iob quadragesimo primo[^p291-5]: « Corpus illius quasi scuta fusilia et compactum squamis se prementibus; una uni coniungitur, et ne spiraculum quidem incedit per eas; una alteri adhaerebit, et tenentes se nequaquam separabuntur »; unde in Psalmo: « Astiterunt reges terrae, et principes convenerunt in unum adversus Dominum » etc. — Post haec subiungit *conclusionem,* cum subdit: *Quomodo stabit regnum eius?* Quod dicit *interrogative,* tanquam impossibile sit hoc sustinere, quod satanas ipse de regno eiiciat se. Regnum enim satanae est in malis et peccatoribus; Iob quadragesimo primo[^p291-6]: « Ipse est rex super omnes filios superbiae »; et ideo dicebat Apostolus ad Romanos sexto: « Non regnet peccatum in vestro mortali corpore ». — Postremo subinfert *minoris probationem ex dicto ipsorum,* quoniam haec erat causa totius illationis[^p291-7] falsi. Et ideo addit: *Quia dicitis: In Beelzebub eiicere me daemonia.* Si enim verum est quod dicitis, ut satanas satanam eiiciat; ergo divisum est eius imperium, ac per hoc tendit ad interitum. Et hoc modo formatur argumentum Matthaei duodecimo[^p291-8]: « Si satanas satanam eiicit, adversus se ipsum divisus est; quomodo ergo stabit regnum eius »? — Sic ergo patet, quomodo ex dicto eorum deducit ad manifestum impossibile.

45. (Vers. 19.). Secundo vero perducit ad *falsum improbabile,* cum subdit: *Si autem ego in Beelzebub eiicio daemonia;* in quo intendit formare talem rationem: si ego eiicio daemonia per daemonum potentiam; et ego dedi potestatem filiis vestris eiiciendi daemonia — hanc supponit et tacet — ergo ipsi in principe daemoniorum eiiciunt daemonia. Sed hoc est falsum et improbabile[^p291-9] et secundum ipsorum opinionem et secundum ipsorum Apostolorum testificationem. Falsum est igitur quod Pharisaei sentiunt, et verum quod Apostoli testantur; igitur *ipsi erunt iudices eorum.* — In hac autem ratione praemittitur *propositio,* quam asserunt, cum dicitur: *Si autem ego in Beelzebub eiicio daemonia,* secundum vestram scilicet assertionem; supra eodem[^p291-10]: « In Beelzebub eiicit daemonia ». — Subditur *conclusio,* quam non concedunt, cum dicitur: *Filii vestri in quo eiiciunt?* quasi diceret: sequitur, quod eiiciunt in Beelzebub; quod tamen ipsi non concedunt. Unde Beda[^p291-11]: « *Filios* Iudaeorum vocat Apostolos, qui inter alia munera, quae a Domino acceperant, etiam daemonia pellebant, quam expulsionem non diabolo, sed Deo assignabant ». — Subinfertur autem *redargutio,* quam incurrunt, cum dicitur: *Ideo ipsi iudices vestri erunt;* quia quod vos blasphematis ut falsum ipsi testificantur esse verum, secundum illud Isaiae quadragesimo tertio[^p291-12]: « Vere vos testes mei, dicit Dominus ». Et non tantum *testes* propter veritatis assertionem, sed etiam *iudices* propter veritatis perfectionem, secundum illud Matthaei decimo nono: « Vos, qui reliquistis omnia et secuti estis me, sedebitis super sedes duodecim, iudicantes duodecim tribus Israel ». Ipsi namque sunt, de quibus Isaiae tertio[^p291-13]: « Dominus ad iudicium veniet cum senioribus populi sui ». *Seniores* vocat perfectiores, secundum illud Sapientiae quarto: « Senectus venerabilis est, non diuturna, neque annorum numero
<!-- page 292 -->
computata. Cani enim sunt sensus hominis et aetas senectutis, vita immaculata ».

46. (Vers. 20.). Tertio vero *reducit ad verum infallibile,* cum adiungit: *Porro, si in digito Dei eiicio daemonia. Porro,* hoc est certe; *si,* id est quia; *in digito Dei,* hoc est in Spiritu sancto; unde Matthaei duodecimo[^p292-1] dicitur: « Si in Spiritu Dei eiicio daemonia ». Spiritus sanctus namque dicitur *digitus Dei,* sicut dicitur Exodi octavo: « Digitus Dei est hic ». Ratio autem huius est, quia Filius dicitur *brachium* Patris, secundum illud Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio[^p292-2]: « Brachium Domini cui revelatum est »? Dicitur etiam *manus,* secundum illud Psalmi: « Emitte manum tuam de alto ». Et ratio huius est, quia, sicut qui operatur brachio et manu facit, sic Pater omnia facit per Filium, sicut Ioannis primo dicitur: « Omnia per ipsum facta sunt ». Spiritus autem sanctus procedit a Patre et Filio, sicut digitus a corpore et brachio, et est illi connaturalis et consubstantialis, et ideo merito dicitur *digitus* in Scripturis[^p292-3]. Vel, quia, sicut *una* est manus, et per digitos fit *distinctio,* sic per unum Spiritum fit distinctio donorum, sicut dicitur primae ad Corinthios duodecimo[^p292-4]: « Haec omnia operatur unus atque idem Spiritus, dividens singulis, prout vult ». — Quia ergo in hoc Spiritu eiicio daemonia, *profecto pervenit in vos regnum Dei,* id est potestas regia, quae protegit nos, expulso diabolo, secundum illud Ioannis duodecimo[^p292-5]: « Nunc iudicium est mundi, nunc princeps huius mundi eiicietur foras ». — Et nota, quod dicit: *pervenit in vos regnum Dei,* quia regnum Dei in nos *pervenit* per *gratiam,* secundum illud infra decimo septimo[^p292-6]: « Regnum Dei intra vos est ». Sed tunc *perveniemus* in illud per *gloriam,* quando dicetur nobis: « Venite, benedicti Patris mei, percipite regnum » etc.

47. Et nota, quod ista consequentia est necessaria; quia, si nihil est medium, quin homo sit sub potestate Dei, vel diaboli: ergo si expellitur potestas diaboli ab homine, sequitur, quod introducatur potestas divina. — Triplex autem spiritus nequam, de quo habitum est supra[^p292-7], expellitur spiritu divino dato ad tria, scilicet spiritu *sanctitatis,* de quo Sapientiae primo[^p292-8]: « Spiritus sanctus disciplinae effugiet fictum »; spiritu *paupertatis,* de quo Matthaei quinto: « Beati pauperes spiritu »; et spiritu *humilitatis,* de quo Isaiae ultimo: « Ad quem respiciam nisi ad pauperculum et contritum spiritu »? — Hunc triplicem spiritum petebat Propheta in Psalmo[^p292-9]: « Cor mundum crea in me, Deus, et spiritum *rectum* innova etc. Et Spiritum *sanctum* tuum ne auferas a me. Et spiritu *principali* confirma me »; ut tangatur hic triplex spiritus: *rectus,* id est paupertatis; *sanctus,* id est puritatis; et *principalis,* id est humilis[^p292-10]; quem spiritum qui habet habet iustitiam ad *se* et *proximum* et *Deum,* sive *intra, extra, supra* habet, ac per hoc habet pacem et gaudium, et ita intra se regnum Dei. Nam, secundum illud ad Romanos decimo quarto[^p292-11], « regnum Dei non est esca et potus, sed iustitia et pax et gaudium in Spiritu sancto ».

48. (Vers. 21.). *Cum fortis armatus custodit atrium suum.* Postquam improbavit falsitatem, hic secundo *approbat veritatem*[^p292-12]; in cuius approbatione procedit hoc ordine: primo praemittit veritatem *praeambulam,* secundo subdit veritatem *intentam,* tertio subnectit veritatem *annexam.*

Primo igitur, *praemittens veritatem praeambulam,* dicit: *Cum fortis armatus custodit atrium suum, in pace sunt omnia*[^p292-13], *quae possidet.* Hoc per se manifestum est. Quieta enim possessio venit ex fortitudine possidentis, quae non permittit, impugnari a minus potente. *Fortis* enim est iste diabolus, de quo dicitur Iob quadragesimo primo[^p292-14]: « Non est potestas super terram, quae possit ei comparari, qui factus est, ut nullum timeret »; et Habacuc primo: « Domine, in iudicium posuisti eum, et fortem, ut corriperes, fundasti eum ». Iste est *armatus,* secundum quod dicitur Iob quadragesimo primo[^p292-15]: « Corpus eius ut scuta fusilia ». Et quia tantam habet potentiam et armaturam, ideo non potest sibi ab homine infirmo fieri violentia; immo *in pace*
<!-- page 293 -->
*possidet,* quoniam non timet amittere, secundum illud Gregorii[^p293-1]: « Illos pulsare negligit, quos quieto iure possidere se sentit ». Et haec *pax peccatorum,* de qua in Psalmo: « Zelavi super iniquos, pacem peccatorum videns »; et Matthaei decimo: « Non veni pacem mittere, sed gladium ». Vult igitur in hoc dicere, quod fortis non expellitur a minus forti. Si ergo super potestatem diaboli nulla praevalet potestas terrestris, sed tantum caelestis potentia: ergo quae expellit daemones non est de terris, sed de caelis, non humana, sed divina.

49. (Vers. 22.). Secundo, adiungens *veritatem intentam* et *iam persuasam,* dicit: *Si autem fortior illo*[^p293-2] *superveniens vicerit eum. Fortior* est diabolo divina fortitudo, scilicet Christus noster; primae ad Corinthios primo: « Christum dicimus Dei virtutem et Dei sapientiam »; et rursum: « Quod infirmum est Dei fortius est hominibus »; et ideo Iob nono: « Si fortitudo quaeritur, robustissimus est ». Et propterea dicitur Isaiae nono: « Vocabitur admirabilis, consiliarius, Deus, fortis ». Iste *supervenit,* id est, de supernis venit, secundum illud Ioannis tertio[^p293-3]: « Qui de caelo venit super omnes est ». Hic autem *vicit* diabolum tanquam minus fortem, iuxta quod dicitur Ioannis decimo sexto[^p293-4]: « Confidite, ego vici mundum »; unde licet victus videatur, tamen vicit, quia moriendo revixit. Et hoc designatur Ieremiae quadragesimo sexto: « Fortis impegit in fortem, et ambo pariter corruerunt »; quia Christus corporaliter, temporaliter et visibiliter, diabolus vero sempiternaliter, totaliter et irrecuperabiliter. Unde dicitur Apocalypsis quinto[^p293-5]: « Ecce, vicit leo de tribu Iuda, radix David, aperire librum ». « Et vidi in medio throni et quatuor animalium Agnum stantem tanquam occisum »; qui ideo vicit, quia occisus est. Unde potest dici diabolo illud primae ad Corinthios decimo quinto[^p293-6]: « Ubi est mors victoria tua? Deo gratias, qui dedit nobis victoriam per Iesum Dominum nostrum ».

50. Et quia vicit ipsum in *passione,* ideo spoliavit in *resurrectione;* ideo subdit: *Universa arma eius auferet, in quibus confidebat.* Et hoc fecit, quando portas infernales confregit, secundum illud Psalmi[^p293-7]: « Quia contrivit portas aereas et vectes ferreos confregit »; et rursus in Psalmo: « Arcum conteret et confringet arma et scuta comburet igni ». Haec *arma* facit sibi diabolus de hominibus per consensum, secundum illud ad Romanos sexto[^p293-8]: « Non exhibeatis membra vestra arma iniquitatis peccato ». — Vel *arma* diabolica sunt diversa tentatorum machinamenta, quibus hominibus praevalebant. Et his *privatus* est per Christi victoriam in resurrectione, et *spoliatus* est in ascensione. — Ideo subdit: *Et spolia eius distribuet.* Et hoc quidem fecit, quando de *vasis contumeliae* fecit *vasa misericordiae*[^p293-9]; et hoc est in *ascensione* factum, secundum illud ad Ephesios quarto: « Ascendens in altum, captivam duxit captivitatem, dedit dona hominibus ». Hoc autem fecit noster Emmanuel; Isaiae octavo: « Voca nomen eius: Accelera, spolia detrahe, festina praedari ». *Praedatus* est autem fortiter diabolum, secundum illud Isaiae quadragesimo nono[^p293-10]: « Nunquid tolletur a forti praeda? Aut quod captum fuerit a robusto salvum esse poterit? Quia haec dicit Dominus: Equidem et captivitas a forti tolletur »; et Osee decimo tertio: « Diripiet Dominus thesaurum omnis vasis desiderabilis ». — Huius figura praecessit, primi Regum trigesimo[^p293-11], in David, de quo dicitur, quod regressus a caede Amalech, exspoliatis Amalechitis, spolia distribuit et « misit dona senioribus Iuda et proximis suis ». — Et nota, quod Christus dicitur *spolia diaboli distribuere,* quia, licet ipse omnes liberaverit, tamen etiam partem huius gloriae diversis praedicatoribus committit, qui et ipsi sunt *spoliatores Aegypti,* Exodi duodecimo[^p293-12]. — Ex hoc igitur apparet, quod expulsio daemonum fiebat a Christo non virtute diabolica nec humana, sed per Spiritum sanctum. Et haec est veritas intenta.

51. (Vers. 23.). Tertio vero, annectens *veritatem adiunctam,* subdit: *Qui non est mecum contra me est;* ac per hoc homines, quos diabolus possidet, Christo repugnant, et secundum illud secundae ad Corinthios sexto[^p293-13]: « Quae conventio Christi ad Belial »? — Et quia nullus fortitudini eius potest resistere, ideo subdit: *Qui*[^p293-14] *non colligit mecum dispergit;* quia, sicut dicitur Iob nono, « quis restitit ei, et pacem habuit »? Officium namque Christi est congregare, secundum illud Ioannis undecimo[^p293-15]:
<!-- page 294 -->
« Moriturus erat, ut filios Dei, qui erant dispersi, congregaret in unum »; et Isaiae undecimo: « Congregabit profugos Israel et dispersos Iuda colliget a quatuor plagis terrae »; Psalmus: « Dispersiones Israelis congregabit ». Officium autem diaboli est dispergere, secundum illud Ioannis decimo[^p294-1]: « Lupus rapit et dispergit oves ». Ideo optimum est introire in ovile Domini, qui non potest superari a fortiori, sicut dicitur Ioannis decimo: « Pater, quod dedit mihi, his omnibus plus est » etc.

52. (Vers. 24.). *Cum immundus spiritus* etc. Post improbationem falsitatis et approbationem veritatis subdit hic Evangelista *reprobationem dolositatis,* cuius origo est a spiritu maligno, secundum illud Ioannis octavo[^p294-2]: « Ipse est mendax et pater eius », id est mendacii et simulationis. Reprobat ergo Salvator dolositatem Pharisaeorum, cuius *originem, progressum* et *consummationem* ostendit in eis fieri per spiritum malignum, et hoc quantum ad maligni spiritus *egressum, regressum* et *ingressum. Egreditur* namque per detestationem immunditiae[^p294-3], *regreditur* per simulationem iustitiae, sed *ingreditur* per profundationem nequitiae.

Primo igitur quantum ad *egressum* spiritus immundi per *detestationem immunditiae* dicit: *Cum immundus spiritus exierit ab homine*[^p294-4], scilicet per veram poenitentiam, secundum illud Zachariae decimo tertio: « Pseudoprophetas et spiritum immundum auferam de terra ». Ad hoc autem necessaria est abstinentia et orationis instantia, per quae mundamur intus et extra; et tunc exit spiritus immundus, secundum illud Matthaei decimo septimo[^p294-5]: « Hoc genus daemonii non eiicitur nisi per orationem et ieiunium ». — Et quoniam diaboli desiderium semper est ad seductionem bonorum, quantumcumque ab aliis expellatur; ideo subdit: *Ambulat per loca arida et*[^p294-6] *inaquosa, quaerens requiem.* Loca *arida et inaquosa* sunt loca, in quibus non viget carnalitas nec concupiscentia, secundum illud Psalmi[^p294-7]: « In terra deserta et invia et inaquosa, sic in sancto apparui tibi », secundum quod Hieronymus exponit. Et propterea Virgo Maria, quia omnino habuit exstinctam concupiscentiam, dicitur *petra deserti;* Isaiae decimo sexto[^p294-8]: « Emitte Agnum, Domine, dominatorem terrae, de petra deserti ad montem filiae Sion ». Circa haec loca *ambulat* diabolus, *quaerens requiem,* id est, volens in illis umbram negligentiae et fontem contumeliae fabricare, ut ibi possit requiescere. In tali autem loco *requiescit,* secundum illud Iob quadragesimo[^p294-9]: « Sub umbra dormit in secreto calami in locis humentibus »; et Ezechielis vigesimo nono: « Ecce, ego ad te, draco magne, qui cubas in medio fluminum ». Non autem quiescit diabolus nisi in eis, in quibus invenit defluxus concupiscentiarum, quos quia non potest reperire in sanctis viris afflictis et maceratis, ideo in eis non requiescit, sed fugit, iuxta illud Iacobi quarto[^p294-10]: « Resistite diabolo, et fugiet a vobis ».

53. Secundo vero quantum ad *regressum* per *simulationem iustitiae* subdit: *Et non inveniens,* scilicet requiem, *dicit: Revertar in domum meam, de qua*[^p294-11] *exivi. Suam* vocat domum, quia nondum plene ab ea expulsus fuerat; et ad talem diabolus redit; in cuius figura Isaiae trigesimo septimo: « Per viam, qua venit Sennacherib », id est satanas, « per eam revertetur ». Recte autem peccatorem *domum suam* vocat, quia peccator, « qui facit peccatum, servus est peccati », sicut dicitur Ioannis octavo[^p294-12]; ac per hoc non est iuris sui, sed servus diaboli. Ideo Proverbiorum quinto: « Ne des alienis honorem tuum et annos tuos crudeli, ne forte impleantur extranei viribus tuis, et labores tui sint in domo aliena ».

54. (Vers. 25.). Ad hanc autem domum reducit diabolum expulsum simulatio bonitatis et iustitiae; et propterea addit: *Et cum venerit, invenit eam scopis mundatam et ornatam,* non *intra,* sicut dicit Propheta in Psalmo[^p294-13]: « Exercitabam et scopebam spiritum meum »; et rursus: « Omnis gloria filiae
<!-- page 295 -->
regis ab intus »; non sic invenit *scopatam et ornatam,* sed *extra,* sicut infra eodem[^p295-1]: « Nunc, vos Pharisaei, mundatis quod deforis est calicis et catini; quod autem intus est plenum est rapina et iniquitate »; et Matthaei vigesimo tertio: « Vae vobis! qui similes estis sepulcris dealbatis » etc. Talis munditia non sufficit Spiritui sancto, qui non tantum quaerit habitare in corpore mundo, sed in animo sancto; unde Sapientiae primo[^p295-2]: « In malevolam animam non introibit sapientia nec habitabit in corpore subdito peccatis ». Et ideo ad utrumque invitamur; Isaiae primo: « Lavamini, mundi estote » etc. Illa autem munditia, quae tantum est *extra,* relinquit domum vacuam a gratia Spiritus sancti, et per hoc occupabilem a praesentia diaboli. Unde hoc notans Matthaeus duodecimo[^p295-3] dicit: « Invenit eam *vacantem,* scopis mundatam et ornatam », *vacantem* scilicet a gratia et virtute. Unde Chrysostomus: « A virtute desolati daemonum actionibus facile erunt occupabiles »; et ideo Hieronymus[^p295-4]: « Semper aliquid boni facito, ut te diabolus inveniat occupatum ».

55. (Vers. 26.). Tertio vero quantum ad *ingressum* per *profundationem nequitiae* subdit: *Et*[^p295-5] *tunc vadit et assumit septem alios spiritus nequiores se, et ingressi habitant ibi.* Hi *septem spiritus* sunt septem assimulationes, quae virtutes specie esse se simulant et mentiuntur; qui ideo sunt nequiores vitiis apertis, quia maiorem habent malitiae perversitatem, sicut dicit Augustinus[^p295-6]: « Simulata aequitas non est aequitas, sed duplex iniquitas ».

56. Unde nota *tres* septenarios spirituum. Primus *bonus* est, qui continet septem dona, de quibus Isaiae undecimo[^p295-7]: « Requiescet super eum spiritus sapientiae et intellectus » etc. — Secundus est *malus* et continet septem spiritus nequitiae, id est septem vitia capitalia, septem donis opposita. Opponitur enim *sapientiae* luxuria, *intellectui* gula, *consilio* avaritia, *fortitudini* accidia, *scientiae* ira, *pietati* invidia, *timori* superbia. Et de his Marci decimo sexto[^p295-8] dicitur, quod de Maria Magdalene eiecta fuerant septem daemonia. — Tertius septenarius continet septem simulationes, quas Dominus vocat *spiritus nequiores,* quia sub specie sanctitatis decipiunt innocentes; de quibus Proverbiorum vigesimo sexto[^p295-9]: « Septem nequitiae sunt in corde eius ». Has omnes inducit hypocrisis, quae est donorum Spiritus sancti simulatrix. In cuius rei signum Dominus Matthaei vigesimo tertio[^p295-10] septem facit exclamationes ad hypocritas, comminans eis *vae;* octavo additur *vae,* nec ponitur ibi *hypocritae.* Hi ergo *spiritus nequiores* secure habitant, quia, quanto malitia minus apparet, tanto minus arguitur et tanto requiescit securius; Isaiae decimo tertio[^p295-11]: « Requiescent ibi bestiae, et replebuntur domus eorum draconibus, et habitabunt ibi struthiones, et pilosi saltabunt ibi », ubi tanguntur monstra vitiorum.

57. Et quoniam, quanto diabolus securius habitat in homine, tanto peius est homini, quia magis est servus; ideo subditur: *Et sunt*[^p295-12] *novissima hominis illius peiora prioribus,* sive propter *recidivationem,* ob quam Ioannis quinto: « Ecce, sanus factus es, iam amplius noli peccare, ne deterius tibi contingat »; et secundae Petri secundo: « Melius erat viam veritatis non cognoscere » etc.; unde Chrysostomus[^p295-13]: « Difficilior est poena posteriorum vitiorum »; sive propter *iniquitatis duplicationem,* quae fit propter simulationem, secundum illud Ecclesiastici septimo[^p295-14]: « Ne semines iniquitatem in sulcis iniustitiae et non metes ea in septuplum ». Ex quo apparet, quod Pharisaei non tantum sunt convicti de *falsitate,* sed etiam redarguti de *dolositate.*

Possunt autem *allegorice* exponi de populo Iudaico et gentili, secundum quod Beda exponit satis bene in Glossa[^p295-15]: *Exiit a Iudaeis* etc.

36. *And Jesus was casting out a devil*[^p288-4] etc. As was had at the beginning of the book, this book has four parts; of which the first is on the *nature* of Christ; the second, on the *doctrine* of Christ; and this began above, in the fourth chapter[^p288-5], and has two parts according to the two works of the wise man, « of which the first is not to lie; the second, to be able to make manifest him that lies ». The first part, then, is on the truth to be declared, for the formation of the disciples; the second, on the falsity to be confuted, through the confutation of the Jews who oppose him.

#### *Secondly the falsity of the opposing Jews is confuted in four ways.*

The first part finished, here begins the second. Now this part is divided into four. In the first part of which he confutes the *guile* of the Jews; in the second, their *impiety: Now there drew near,* below, in the fifteenth[^p288-6]; in the third, their *curiosity,* below, in the seventeenth: *And being asked by the Pharisees* etc.; in the fourth, their *unbelief,* below, in the nineteenth: *And it came to pass, when Jesus was come nigh* etc.

#### The guile of the Jews who calumniate the miracles is confuted in two ways.

Now in confuting *the guile of the Jews* he first confutes the guile of those who calumniate the miracles on the part of the *efficient* cause; in the second, on the part of the *time,* below, in the thirteenth[^p288-7]: *And he was teaching in their synagogues on the sabbaths.*

#### First the Jews who calumniate the miracles are confuted on the part of the efficient cause; then the disciples are formed.

The first is divided into two. In the first of which *he confutes the guile and deceit of the Jews;* in the second *he forms his disciples to shun it,* below, in the twelfth[^p288-8]: *And when great multitudes* etc.

#### First, on the guile of the Jews and their threefold deceit.

The first part, which contains the present chapter, is divided into three parts. In the first of which he confutes the deceit of *the blasphemers;* in the second, the deceit of *the tempters* or disbelievers, below[^p288-9]: *And the multitudes running together;* and in the third, the deceit of *the dissemblers,* at: *No man lighteth a candle.* The first of these is in *word,* the second is in *mind,* and the third consists in *any sign whatever.*

#### The deceit of the blasphemers is confuted through three things.

Now to confute *the deceit of the blasphemers* three things are introduced. For first there is made *the expression of the Jewish fraud;* secondly, *the disproof of the fraud expressed,* at[^p288-10]: *But he seeing their thoughts* etc.; and thirdly, *the commendation of the truth shown,* at: *And it came to pass, as he spoke these things* etc. — Concerning *the expression of the Jewish fraud* the Evangelist sets out three things: the first is *the matter of divine praise,* the second is *the blasphemy of human fraud,* the third is *the controversy of the dispute adjoined.*

37. (Vers. 14.). First, then, as to *the matter of divine praise* he says: *And Jesus was casting out a devil, and the same was dumb;* in which two miracles are touched on together, nay rather three. Hence Bede in the Gloss[^p288-11]: « Matthew says that this possessed man was also blind, in whom three miracles
<!-- page 289 -->
are done by the Lord: for the *blind* man sees, the *dumb* speaks, the *possessed* is freed from the demon ». And in this there was *matter of divine praise,* as much on the part of the one cured as of those standing by. Because of which he says: *And when he had cast out the devil, the dumb spoke: and the multitudes were in admiration;* and thus was fulfilled that word of Isaias, the thirty-fifth[^p289-1]: « Then shall the eyes of the blind be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped; then shall the lame man leap as a hart, and the tongue of the dumb shall be free ». Now such great miracles were done together for the sake of *admiration* and *praise,* according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the forty-third[^p289-2]: « The Lord is terrible, and exceeding great, and his power is marvellous »; and after: « Glorify the Lord as much as ever you can, for he will yet far exceed, and his magnificence is wonderful ». Hence these could truly say that of Mark, the seventh: « He hath done all things well; he hath made both the deaf to hear and the dumb to speak ».

38. Now according to *the spiritual intelligence* the *devil* is sin, which makes one *deaf* to hearing the truth, *blind* to seeing it and *dumb* to confessing it. Hence Chrysostom[^p289-3]: « O wickedness of the demon! He has stopped up both entrances by which the man was to believe: *hearing* and *sight* », and the third, that is, *the tongue,* lest he confess. Hence it is said in Isaias, the forty-first[^p289-4]: « There is none that announceth, nor that preacheth, nor that heareth my words »; and again, in the forty-second: « Hear, ye *deaf,* and ye *blind,* behold that you may see. Who is blind, but my servant? Or deaf, but he to whom I have sent my messengers »? Such a one is also *dumb* to confessing; hence Ecclesiasticus, the fifteenth: « Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner ».

39. And note that, since speech has been given to man for three things, namely for *praising God,* for *edifying the neighbour* and for *accusing oneself;* this threefold speech is taken away by a threefold devil. The first the spirit of *lust* takes away, according to that of Isaias, the first[^p289-5]: « When you multiply prayer, I will not hear. For your hands are full of blood ». — The second the spirit of *avarice* takes away, which makes one attend to oneself alone, according to that of Matthew, the twenty-fifth: « He that had received the one talent, going his way digged into the earth ». — The third the spirit of *pride* takes away, which does not permit a man to accuse himself, according to that of the Psalm[^p289-6]: « Because I was silent my bones grew old »; and Jeremias, the second: « Behold, I will contend with thee in judgment, because thou hast said: I have not sinned ». — Of this threefold devil it is said in the Apocalypse, the sixteenth: « I saw from the mouth of the beast, and from the mouth of the dragon, and from the mouth of the false prophet three unclean spirits come forth like frogs ». — The first devil is cast out by *fasting,* according to that of Matthew, the seventeenth[^p289-7]: « This kind of devils is not cast out but by fasting ». The second is cast out by *the memory of the Lord's passion,* according to that of Tobias, the sixth[^p289-8]: « If thou put a little piece of the heart of the fish upon coals, the smoke thereof driveth away all kind of devils » etc. The third by *prayer;* the first of Kings, the sixteenth: « David took his harp, and played with his hand, and Saul was refreshed, and was eased; for the evil spirit departed from him ».

40. (Vers. 15.). Secondly, as to *the blasphemy of human fraud,* there is subjoined: *But some of them said: He casteth out devils by Beelzebub, the prince of devils.* And this was a great blasphemy, because what was done by the Holy Spirit they attributed to the malign spirit; hence in Matthew, the twelfth[^p289-9], it is said straight after this: « Every sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven men, but the spirit of blasphemy shall not be forgiven ». Into this blasphemy they broke out from envy; hence Chrysostom[^p289-10]: « Not when he was saying great things, but when he was working the salvation of men, then the Pharisees set upon him ». « For envy does not ask what it may say, but only that it may say something ». « For there is no malice worse than jealousy. For the adulterer completes his sin in a short time; the jealous man never rests ». — And because this blasphemy proceeded from malice and envy, for that reason this Gospel is read when the history of Joseph[^p289-11] is sung, on the third Sunday of Lent. For as the brothers of Joseph from envy called him *the dreamer of dreams,* so these also called Christ *the invoker of demons.* And they name *Beelzebub* above all, both because that idol was famous; hence
<!-- page 290 -->
in the fourth of Kings, the first[^p290-1], it is said that « Ochozias sent messengers, saying to them: Go, consult Beelzebub, the god of Accaron »; and Bede in the Gloss: « The Jews asserted that the prince of devils dwelt in this image. By his power they said that Jesus cast out devils »; and therefore it is said in the text: *By the prince of devils;* or because the name of this idol was ridiculous; hence *Beelzebub* sounds as much as *man of flies;* by which name the Jews called him derisively, as is said in the Gloss[^p290-2], « because of the filth of the gore that was offered in sacrifice in his temple ». Hence they uttered blasphemy so, as in John, the eighth, where it is said: « Do not we say well that thou art a Samaritan and hast a devil »?

41. (Vers. 16.). Thirdly, as to *the controversy of the dispute adjoined,* he subjoins: *And others tempting asked of him a sign from heaven.* They did not ask a sign of this kind in order to believe, as the faithful, but as rebels, in order to impugn him, according to that word of the Saviour in Matthew, the twenty-second[^p290-3]: « Why do you tempt me, ye hypocrites »? and in the Psalm: « Your fathers tempted me » etc. And this is an evil, as is said in the first to the Corinthians, the tenth: « Neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them tempted and perished by the serpents ». Now they asked *a sign from heaven,* such as *the thunder* in the time of Samuel, the first of Kings, the twelfth[^p290-4]; or *the sending forth of fire* in the time of Elias, the third of Kings, the eighteenth, and the fourth of Kings, the first; or such as *the going back of the sun* in the time of Ezechias, the fourth of Kings, the twentieth. — Now this was proper to the Jews, whether through *habituation;* hence the first to the Corinthians, the first[^p290-5]: « The Jews require signs, and the Greeks seek after wisdom »; and therefore Peter, mighty in signs, was the Apostle to the Jews, but Paul, in wisdom, to the gentiles; or also because of *unbelief* and *hardness,* according to what Stephen said in Acts, the seventh[^p290-6]: « With stiff neck, and uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resisted the Holy Spirit ». Hence Chrysostom[^p290-7]: « Always *to learn* is a sign of never being able to advance; so always *to seek testimony* is a sign of never being willing to believe ». And this indeed is true; for when they had sufficient testimonies on earth, there was no need to seek signs from the heavens; hence John, the tenth[^p290-8]: « The works that I do in the name of my Father, they give testimony of me ».

42. *Spiritually,* however, the Lord gave *signs on earth,* namely signs *of humility and poverty,* according to that above, in the second[^p290-9]: « This shall be a sign unto you: you shall find the infant wrapped in swaddling clothes ». But many do not believe this sign, as the proud and the ambitious, who seek Christ not in the humility of the cross, but in the appetite for honour and praise. And yet it is said in Matthew, the twenty-fourth: « Then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven » etc. But the proud contradict this, according to that above, in the second[^p290-10]: « This one is set for a sign which shall be contradicted », because the state of humility does not please them, but that of dignity. Hence in the Psalm: « They have set up their signs for signs », namely of pride and wantonness, according to that of Wisdom, the second: « Let us everywhere leave signs of our joy », without which signs they will not follow; hence in the Psalm: « Our signs we have not seen » etc.

43. *But he seeing their thoughts, said to them.* After the expression of the Jewish fraud there is subjoined here *the disproof of the fraud expressed*[^p290-11]. Now this part has three. In the first of which is put *the disproof of the falsity;* in the second, *the confirmation of the truth,* at[^p290-12]: *When a strong man armed keepeth* etc.; in the third, *the reprobation of the guile,* at: *When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man* etc.

In *disproving the falsity* he proceeds in this order: first he leads them down to *a manifest impossible,* secondly he brings them through to *an improbable falsehood,* thirdly he leads them back to *an infallible truth.*

44. (Vers. 17. 18.). First, then, he leads them down to *a manifest impossible,* when he says: *Every kingdom divided against itself shall be brought to desolation;* in which he intends to make an argument of this kind: every kingdom divided, by this very fact that it is divided against itself, is destroyed; but if one demon casts out another, satan is divided against himself: therefore his kingdom does not stand. It follows, then, that satan himself destroys his own kingdom and takes away from himself
<!-- page 291 -->
*power* and dominion. But this is manifestly impossible; and it follows from the saying of the Pharisees: therefore that saying was manifestly false. Of this reasoning, then, he puts first the *major,* when he says: *Every kingdom divided against itself shall be brought to desolation, and house upon house shall fall.* And this indeed is known of itself and is true, because division is the cause of ruin; hence the cause of the predicate is in the subject. And this is what Jerome says[^p291-1]: « By concord small things grow, by discord the greatest fall apart ». For unity is the cause of preservation, and division of destruction, according to that of Osee, the tenth[^p291-2]: « Their heart is divided, now they shall perish »; and Proverbs, the twenty-eighth: « For the sins of the land many are the princes thereof ». An example of this is in Genesis, the eleventh, on those building the tower, when they were « of one tongue », where it is subjoined: « The Lord scattered them from that place into all lands, and they ceased to build ». Hence of the destruction of its unity in the final state of the world it is said below, in the twenty-first[^p291-3]: « Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there shall be great earthquakes » etc. — Then he adjoins *the minor,* when he says: *And if*[^p291-4] *satan also be divided against himself,* that is, fights against himself; which he says *under a condition,* because this follows from the word of the aforesaid scribes and Pharisees, yet in itself it is not true; for satan agrees with satan in evil. Hence Job, the forty-first[^p291-5]: « His body is like molten shields, shut close up with scales pressing upon one another; one is joined to another, and not so much as any air can come between them; they stick one to another, and they hold one another fast, and shall not be separated »; hence in the Psalm: « The kings of the earth stood up, and the princes met together, against the Lord » etc. — After this he subjoins *the conclusion,* when he subjoins: *How shall his kingdom stand?* Which he says *interrogatively,* as though it were impossible to maintain this, that satan himself cast himself out of his kingdom. For the kingdom of satan is in the wicked and in sinners; Job, the forty-first[^p291-6]: « He is king over all the children of pride »; and therefore the Apostle said to the Romans, the sixth: « Let not sin reign in your mortal body ». — Lastly he infers *the proof of the minor from their own saying,* since this was the cause of the whole inference[^p291-7] of the falsehood. And therefore he adds: *Because you say that through Beelzebub I cast out devils.* For if what you say is true, that satan casts out satan; then his empire is divided, and through this it tends to destruction. And in this way the argument is formed in Matthew, the twelfth[^p291-8]: « If satan cast out satan, he is divided against himself; how then shall his kingdom stand »? — So, then, it is plain how from their saying he leads them down to a manifest impossible.

45. (Vers. 19.). Secondly he brings them through to *an improbable falsehood,* when he subjoins: *Now if I cast out devils by Beelzebub;* in which he intends to form a reasoning of this kind: if I cast out devils by the power of demons; and I have given power to your children to cast out devils — this he supposes and passes over in silence — therefore they cast out devils by the prince of devils. But this is false and improbable[^p291-9], both according to their own opinion and according to the testification of the Apostles themselves. False, therefore, is what the Pharisees think, and true what the Apostles testify; therefore *they shall be their judges.* — Now in this reasoning there is put first *the proposition,* which they assert, when it is said: *Now if I cast out devils by Beelzebub,* that is, according to your assertion; above, in the same[^p291-10]: « He casteth out devils by Beelzebub ». — There is subjoined *the conclusion,* which they do not grant, when it is said: *By whom do your children cast them out?* as if he were to say: it follows that they cast them out by Beelzebub; which, however, they themselves do not grant. Hence Bede[^p291-11]: « He calls the Apostles the *children* of the Jews, who among the other gifts that they had received from the Lord also drove out devils, which expulsion they assigned not to the devil but to God ». — And there is inferred *the conviction* which they incur, when it is said: *Therefore they shall be your judges;* because what you blaspheme as false, they testify to be true, according to that of Isaias, the forty-third[^p291-12]: « You truly are my witnesses, saith the Lord ». And not only *witnesses* because of the assertion of the truth, but also *judges* because of the perfection of the truth, according to that of Matthew, the nineteenth: « You who have left all things and have followed me shall sit on twelve seats, judging the twelve tribes of Israel ». For they are those of whom Isaias, the third[^p291-13]: « The Lord will enter into judgment with the ancients of his people ». He calls the more perfect *ancients,* according to that of Wisdom, the fourth: « Venerable old age is not that of long time, nor counted by the number
<!-- page 292 -->
of years. For the understanding of a man is grey hairs, and a spotless life is old age ».

46. (Vers. 20.). Thirdly he *leads them back to an infallible truth,* when he adjoins: *But if by the finger of God I cast out devils. But* (*porro*), that is, certainly; *if,* that is, since; *by the finger of God,* that is, in the Holy Spirit; hence it is said in Matthew, the twelfth[^p292-1]: « If I by the Spirit of God cast out devils ». For the Holy Spirit is called *the finger of God,* as is said in Exodus, the eighth: « This is the finger of God ». Now the reason of this is that the Son is called the *arm* of the Father, according to that of Isaias, the fifty-third[^p292-2]: « To whom is the arm of the Lord revealed »? He is also called the *hand,* according to that of the Psalm: « Put forth thy hand from on high ». And the reason of this is that, as one who works does so with arm and hand, so the Father does all things through the Son, as is said in John, the first: « All things were made by him ». But the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son, as the finger from the body and the arm, and is connatural and consubstantial with them, and therefore is rightly called the *finger* in the Scriptures[^p292-3]. Or, because, as the hand is *one,* and through the fingers *distinction* is made, so through the one Spirit is made the distinction of gifts, as is said in the first to the Corinthians, the twelfth[^p292-4]: « All these things one and the same Spirit worketh, dividing to every one according as he will ». — Since, then, I cast out devils in this Spirit, *doubtless the kingdom of God is come upon you,* that is, the royal power, which protects us, the devil being expelled, according to that of John, the twelfth[^p292-5]: « Now is the judgment of the world; now shall the prince of this world be cast out ». — And note that he says: *the kingdom of God is come upon you,* because the kingdom of God *comes* into us through *grace,* according to that below, in the seventeenth[^p292-6]: « The kingdom of God is within you ». But then *we shall come* into it through *glory,* when it shall be said to us: « Come, ye blessed of my Father, possess you the kingdom » etc.

47. And note that this consequence is necessary; because, if there is no mean, but that a man is under the power of God or of the devil: therefore, if the power of the devil is expelled from a man, it follows that the divine power is brought in. — Now the threefold wicked spirit, of which there was treatment above[^p292-7], is expelled by the divine spirit given for three things, namely by the spirit *of holiness,* of which Wisdom, the first[^p292-8]: « The Holy Spirit of discipline will flee from the deceitful »; by the spirit *of poverty,* of which Matthew, the fifth: « Blessed are the poor in spirit »; and by the spirit *of humility,* of which Isaias, the last: « To whom shall I have respect, but to him that is poor and little, and of a contrite spirit »? — This threefold spirit the Prophet asked for in the Psalm[^p292-9]: « Create a clean heart in me, O God, and renew a *right* spirit etc. And take not thy *holy* Spirit from me. And strengthen me with a *principal* spirit »; so that a threefold spirit is touched on here: the *right,* that is, of poverty; the *holy,* that is, of purity; and the *principal,* that is, the humble[^p292-10]; and he who has this spirit has justice toward *himself* and *the neighbour* and *God,* or has it *within, without, above,* and through this has peace and joy, and so the kingdom of God within himself. For, according to that of the Romans, the fourteenth[^p292-11], « the kingdom of God is not meat and drink, but justice and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit ».

48. (Vers. 21.). *When a strong man armed keepeth his court.* After he has disproved the falsity, here secondly *he approves the truth*[^p292-12]; in the approbation of which he proceeds in this order: first he puts forward *the preambulary* truth, secondly he subjoins *the intended* truth, thirdly he joins on *the annexed* truth.

First, then, *putting forward the preambulary truth,* he says: *When a strong man armed keepeth his court, all things are in peace*[^p292-13] *which he possesseth.* This is manifest of itself. For quiet possession comes from the strength of the possessor, which does not permit that he be impugned by one less powerful. For this devil is *strong,* of whom it is said in Job, the forty-first[^p292-14]: « There is no power upon earth that can be compared with him who was made to fear no one »; and Habacuc, the first: « O Lord, thou hast appointed him for judgment, and made him strong for correction ». He is *armed,* according to what is said in Job, the forty-first[^p292-15]: « His body is like molten shields ». And because he has such power and armour, therefore violence cannot be done to him by a weak man; nay rather *in peace*
<!-- page 293 -->
*he possesses,* since he does not fear to lose, according to that word of Gregory[^p293-1]: « He neglects to assail those whom he feels that he possesses by quiet right ». And this is *the peace of sinners,* of which in the Psalm: « I had a zeal on occasion of the wicked, seeing the peace of sinners »; and Matthew, the tenth: « I came not to send peace, but the sword ». He means, then, to say in this that the strong man is not expelled by one less strong. If, then, no earthly power prevails over the power of the devil, but only heavenly might: therefore that which expels demons is not from the earth, but from the heavens, not human, but divine.

49. (Vers. 22.). Secondly, adjoining *the intended truth,* and one *already made persuasive,* he says: *But if a stronger than he*[^p293-2] *come upon him and overcome him. Stronger* than the devil is the divine strength, namely our Christ; the first to the Corinthians, the first: « We call Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God »; and again: « The weakness of God is stronger than men »; and therefore Job, the ninth: « If strength be demanded, he is most strong ». And for this reason it is said in Isaias, the ninth: « His name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, God the Mighty ». He *comes upon* him, that is, comes from on high, according to that of John, the third[^p293-3]: « He that cometh from heaven is above all ». And he *overcame* the devil as one less strong, according to what is said in John, the sixteenth[^p293-4]: « Have confidence, I have overcome the world »; hence, though he seems overcome, yet he overcame, because by dying he lived again. And this is designated in Jeremias, the forty-sixth: « The strong hath stumbled against the strong, and both are fallen together »; because Christ fell bodily, temporally and visibly, but the devil everlastingly, totally and irrecoverably. Hence it is said in the Apocalypse, the fifth[^p293-5]: « Behold, the lion of the tribe of Juda, the root of David, hath prevailed to open the book ». « And I saw in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures a Lamb standing as it were slain »; who therefore overcame because he was slain. Hence there can be said to the devil that of the first to the Corinthians, the fifteenth[^p293-6]: « O death, where is thy victory? Thanks be to God, who hath given us the victory through Jesus our Lord ».

50. And because he overcame him in *the passion,* therefore he despoiled him in *the resurrection;* therefore he subjoins: *He will take away all his arms wherein he trusted.* And this he did when he broke the gates of hell, according to that of the Psalm[^p293-7]: « Because he hath broken gates of brass, and burst iron bars »; and again in the Psalm: « He shall break the bow, and shatter the arms, and the shields he shall burn in the fire ». These *arms* the devil makes for himself out of men through consent, according to that of the Romans, the sixth[^p293-8]: « Neither yield ye your members as arms of iniquity unto sin ». — Or the diabolical *arms* are the various machinations of the tempters, by which they prevailed over men. And of these he was *deprived* through the victory of Christ in the resurrection, and *despoiled* in the ascension. — Therefore he subjoins: *And will distribute his spoils.* And this indeed he did when out of *vessels of dishonour* he made *vessels of mercy*[^p293-9]; and this was done in *the ascension,* according to that of the Ephesians, the fourth: « Ascending on high, he led captivity captive; he gave gifts to men ». And this our Emmanuel did; Isaias, the eighth: « Call his name: Hasten to take away the spoils; make haste to take away the prey ». And he *preyed upon* the devil mightily, according to that of Isaias, the forty-ninth[^p293-10]: « Shall the prey be taken from the strong? Or can that which was taken by the mighty be delivered? For thus saith the Lord: Yea verily, even the captivity shall be taken away from the strong »; and Osee, the thirteenth: « The Lord shall plunder the treasure of every desirable vessel ». — The figure of this went before, in the first of Kings, the thirtieth[^p293-11], in David, of whom it is said that, returning from the slaughter of Amalec, the Amalecites being despoiled, he distributed the spoils and « sent presents to the ancients of Juda and to his neighbours ». — And note that Christ is said *to distribute the spoils of the devil,* because, though he himself has freed all, yet he also commits a part of this glory to various preachers, who themselves too are *the despoilers of Egypt,* Exodus, the twelfth[^p293-12]. — From this, then, it appears that the expulsion of demons was done by Christ not by diabolical power nor by human, but through the Holy Spirit. And this is the intended truth.

51. (Vers. 23.). Thirdly, joining on *the adjoined truth,* he subjoins: *He that is not with me is against me;* and through this, men whom the devil possesses fight against Christ, according also to that of the second to the Corinthians, the sixth[^p293-13]: « What concord hath Christ with Belial »? — And because no one can resist his strength, therefore he subjoins: *He that*[^p293-14] *gathereth not with me scattereth;* because, as is said in Job, the ninth, « who hath resisted him, and hath had peace »? For the office of Christ is to gather together, according to that of John, the eleventh[^p293-15]:
<!-- page 294 -->
« He was to die, that he might gather together in one the children of God that were dispersed »; and Isaias, the eleventh: « He shall assemble the fugitives of Israel, and shall gather together the dispersed of Juda from the four quarters of the earth »; the Psalm: « He will gather together the dispersed of Israel ». But the office of the devil is to scatter, according to that of John, the tenth[^p294-1]: « The wolf catcheth and scattereth the sheep ». Therefore it is best to enter into the sheepfold of the Lord, who cannot be overcome by one stronger, as is said in John, the tenth: « That which my Father hath given me is greater than all » etc.

52. (Vers. 24.). *When the unclean spirit* etc. After the disproof of the falsity and the approbation of the truth, the Evangelist here subjoins *the reprobation of the guile,* whose origin is from the malign spirit, according to that of John, the eighth[^p294-2]: « He is a liar, and the father thereof », that is, of the lie and of dissembling. The Saviour, then, reproves the guile of the Pharisees, whose *origin, progress* and *consummation* he shows to come about in them through the malign spirit, and this as to the malign spirit's *going out, going back* and *going in.* For he *goes out* through the detestation of uncleanness[^p294-3], *goes back* through the dissembling of justice, but *goes in* through the deepening of wickedness.

First, then, as to the *going out* of the unclean spirit through *the detestation of uncleanness,* he says: *When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man*[^p294-4], namely through true penance, according to that of Zacharias, the thirteenth: « I will take away the false prophets and the unclean spirit out of the earth ». And for this there is need of abstinence and the instancy of prayer, by which we are cleansed within and without; and then the unclean spirit goes out, according to that of Matthew, the seventeenth[^p294-5]: « This kind of devil is not cast out but by prayer and fasting ». — And since the desire of the devil is always toward the seduction of the good, however much he is expelled by others; therefore he subjoins: *He walketh through arid and*[^p294-6] *waterless places, seeking rest.* The *arid and waterless* places are places in which carnality and concupiscence do not thrive, according to that of the Psalm[^p294-7]: « In a desert land, and where there is no way and no water, so in the sanctuary have I come before thee », according as Jerome expounds it. And for this reason the Virgin Mary, because she had concupiscence altogether extinguished, is called *the rock of the desert;* Isaias, the sixteenth[^p294-8]: « Send forth, O Lord, the lamb, the ruler of the earth, from the rock of the desert, to the mount of the daughter of Sion ». About these places the devil *walks, seeking rest,* that is, wishing to fashion in them a shade of negligence and a fountain of insult, that he may be able to rest there. And in such a place he *rests,* according to that of Job, the fortieth[^p294-9]: « He sleepeth under the shadow, in the covert of the reed, in moist places »; and Ezechiel, the twenty-ninth: « Behold, I come against thee, thou great dragon, that liest in the midst of thy rivers ». But the devil does not rest except in those in whom he finds the flowings of concupiscences, and because he cannot find these in holy men, afflicted and macerated, therefore he does not rest in them, but flees, according to that of James, the fourth[^p294-10]: « Resist the devil, and he will fly from you ».

53. Secondly, as to *the going back* through *the dissembling of justice,* he subjoins: *And not finding,* namely rest, *he saith: I will return into my house whence*[^p294-11] *I came out. His* house he calls it, because he had not yet been fully expelled from it; and to such a one the devil returns; in figure of which Isaias, the thirty-seventh: « By the way that Sennacherib came », that is, satan, « by the same shall he return ». And rightly he calls the sinner *his house,* because the sinner, « whosoever committeth sin, is the servant of sin », as is said in John, the eighth[^p294-12]; and through this he is not his own master, but the servant of the devil. Therefore Proverbs, the fifth: « Give not thy honour to strangers, and thy years to the cruel, lest strangers be filled with thy strength, and thy labours be in another man's house ».

54. (Vers. 25.). And to this house the dissembling of goodness and of justice leads back the expelled devil; and for this reason he adds: *And when he is come, he findeth it swept with a besom and adorned,* not *within,* as the Prophet says in the Psalm[^p294-13]: « I was exercised and I swept my spirit »; and again: « All the glory of the king's daughter
<!-- page 295 -->
is within »; not so does he find it *swept and adorned,* but *without,* as below, in the same[^p295-1]: « Now you Pharisees make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter; but your inside is full of rapine and iniquity »; and Matthew, the twenty-third: « Woe to you! because you are like to whited sepulchres » etc. Such cleanness does not suffice for the Holy Spirit, who seeks to dwell not only in a clean body, but in a holy mind; hence Wisdom, the first[^p295-2]: « Wisdom will not enter into a malicious soul, nor dwell in a body subject to sins ». And therefore we are invited to both; Isaias, the first: « Wash yourselves, be clean » etc. But that cleanness which is only *without* leaves the house empty of the grace of the Holy Spirit, and through this open to be occupied by the presence of the devil. Hence Matthew, noting this, says in the twelfth[^p295-3]: « He findeth it *free*[^tr-vacare], swept with a besom and adorned », *free,* namely, from grace and virtue. Hence Chrysostom: « Desolate of virtue, they will easily be open to be occupied by the actions of demons »; and therefore Jerome[^p295-4]: « Always do something good, that the devil may find thee occupied ».

55. (Vers. 26.). Thirdly, as to *the going in* through *the deepening of wickedness,* he subjoins: *And*[^p295-5] *then he goeth and taketh seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and entering in they dwell there.* These *seven spirits* are seven counterfeits, which feign and lie that they are virtues in appearance; which are therefore more wicked than open vices, because they have a greater perversity of malice, as Augustine says[^p295-6]: « Feigned equity is not equity, but double iniquity ».

56. Hence note *three* septenaries of spirits. The first is *good,* which contains the seven gifts, of which Isaias, the eleventh[^p295-7]: « The spirit of wisdom and of understanding shall rest upon him » etc. — The second is *evil* and contains seven spirits of wickedness, that is, the seven capital vices, opposed to the seven gifts. For to *wisdom* is opposed lust, to *understanding* gluttony, to *counsel* avarice, to *fortitude* sloth, to *knowledge* anger, to *piety* envy, to *fear* pride. And of these it is said in Mark, the sixteenth[^p295-8], that seven devils had been cast out of Mary Magdalen. — The third septenary contains seven dissemblings, which the Lord calls *more wicked spirits,* because under the appearance of holiness they deceive the innocent; of which Proverbs, the twenty-sixth[^p295-9]: « There are seven mischiefs in his heart ». All these hypocrisy brings in, which is the dissembler of the gifts of the Holy Spirit. In sign of which thing the Lord in Matthew, the twenty-third[^p295-10], makes seven exclamations against the hypocrites, threatening them with *woe;* at the eighth *woe* is added, and *hypocrites* is not put there. These *more wicked spirits,* then, dwell securely, because the less the malice appears, the less it is reproved and the more securely it rests; Isaias, the thirteenth[^p295-11]: « Wild beasts shall rest there, and their houses shall be filled with serpents, and ostriches shall dwell there, and the hairy ones shall dance there », where the monsters of the vices are touched on.

57. And since, the more securely the devil dwells in a man, the worse it is for the man, because he is the more a servant; therefore it is subjoined: *And the last state of that man is*[^p295-12] *worse than the first,* either because of *relapse,* on account of which John, the fifth: « Behold, thou art made whole; sin no more, lest some worse thing happen to thee »; and the second of Peter, the second: « It had been better not to have known the way of truth » etc.; hence Chrysostom[^p295-13]: « Harder is the punishment of the later vices »; or because of *the doubling of iniquity,* which comes about through dissembling, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the seventh[^p295-14]: « Sow not iniquity in the furrows of injustice, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold ». From which it appears that the Pharisees are not only convinced of *falsity,* but also convicted of *guile.*

But these things can be expounded *allegorically* of the Jewish people and the gentile, according as Bede expounds it quite well in the Gloss[^p295-15]: *He went out from the Jews* etc.
