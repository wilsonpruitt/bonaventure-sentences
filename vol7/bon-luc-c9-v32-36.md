# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. IX, Vers. 32-36
## *Petrus vero, et qui cum illo erant etc. Post demonstrationem gloriae promissae subiungit hic Evangelista contemplationem demonstratae*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 234 -->
56. *Petrus vero, et qui cum illo erant* etc. Post demonstrationem gloriae promissae subiungit hic Evangelista *contemplationem demonstratae.* Hanc autem narrat satis sufficienter, quia secundum statum quadruplicem: primo, quantum ad *initium;* secundo, quantum ad *progressum,* ibi[^p234-12]: *Factum est, cum discederent ab illo;* tertio, quoad *consummationem,*
<!-- page 235 -->
ibi: *Haec autem illo loquente;* quarto, quoad *subtractionem,* ibi: *Et dum fieret vox.*

Sciendum autem, quod ad *contemplationem* divinarum revelationum occurrunt duo quasi *in principio,* scilicet *gravamen* ex parte naturae et *iuvamen* ex parte gratiae; primum soporat, et secundum excitat.

57. (Vers. 32.). Primo igitur quantum ad *gravamen* ex parte naturae obdormientis dicitur: *Petrus vero, et qui cum illo erant gravati erant somno;* et ita omnes deficiebant. Nec mirum, quia infirmitate circumdati erant; unde Sapientiae nono[^p235-1]: « Corpus, quod corrumpitur, aggravat animam et deprimit terrena inhabitatio sensum multa cogitantem ». — Cum enim natura sensuum exteriorum impediat, ne intellectus noster figat oculum in lumen aeternum, oportet in contemplationis principio, ut homo quasi consopitus a sensibus alienetur, quasi per somnum, qui non fit sine gravamine sensuum. Unde anima contemplativa ait Canticorum quinto[^p235-2]: « Ego dormio, et cor meum vigilat »; et Iob trigesimo tertio: « Quando irruit sopor super homines, et dormiunt in lectulo; tunc aperit aures eorum et erudiens instruit eos disciplina »; et de hoc Genesis secundo dicitur: « Immisit Dominus soporem in Adam ».

58. Secundo vero quantum ad *iuvamen* ex parte gratiae excitantis subditur: *Et evigilantes viderunt maiestatem eius et duos viros, qui stabant cum illo.* Haec *vigilia* est per desiderium et amorem Spiritus sancti, secundum illud Isaiae vigesimo sexto[^p235-3]: « Domine, anima mea desideravit te in nocte, sed et spiritu meo in praecordiis meis de mane vigilabo ad te ». Haec autem potissime necessaria est in *oratione;* unde Dominus Petro et sociis Matthaei vigesimo sexto[^p235-4]: « Vigilate et orate, ut non intretis in tentationem; spiritus quidem promptus est » etc.; Marci decimo tertio: « Videte, vigilate et orate ». « Quod uni dico, omnibus dico: Vigilate ». — Ad hanc autem vigiliam praecipue nos invitat *promissum praemium,* secundum illud infra duodecimo[^p235-5]: « Beati servi illi, quos, cum venerit dominus, invenerit vigilantes; amen dico vobis » etc. Invitat etiam *praevisum periculum,* secundum illud primae Petri ultimo: « Sobrii estote et vigilate, quia adversarius vester diabolus » etc.; et Matthaei vigesimo quarto: « Amen dico vobis: Si sciret paterfamilias, qua hora fur venturus esset, vigilaret utique » etc.

59. Maxime autem *contemplationi* necessaria est *vigilia,* quia citissime subtrahitur oblata gratia; unde Canticorum quinto[^p235-6]: « Pessulum aperui dilecto meo; at ille declinaverat ac transierat ». — In hac autem *evigilatione* videtur *divina maiestas in creaturis,* secundum illud Sapientiae decimo tertio[^p235-7]: « A magnitudine speciei et creaturae » etc.; unde Isaiae sexto: « Vidi Dominum sedentem super solium excelsum, et plena » etc. — Videtur *divina humilitas in Scripturis;* propter quod dicit: *Et duos viros,* id est Legem et Prophetas, de qua visione infra ultimo[^p235-8]: « Aperuit illis sensum, ut intelligerent Scripturas »; et iterum: « Incipiens a Moyse et omnibus Prophetis, interpretabatur illis in omnibus » etc. — Propter istos duos modos contemplandi factus est duplex liber: Scripturae et creaturae, qui designantur in ascensu et descensu per *scalam Iacob,* Genesis vigesimo octavo[^p235-9]; per *egressum et ingressum per ostium,* Ioannis decimo; per *aquilam et eius pullos;* Iob trigesimo nono: « Nunquid ad praeceptum tuum elevabitur aquila »? et post: « Pulli eius lambent sanguinem ».

60. (Vers. 33.). *Et factum est, cum discederent.* Post contemplationis exordium subiungit *contemplationis progressum;* in quo duo potissime requiruntur, scilicet excedens *gaudium de dono concesso* et excedens *desiderium de continuando,* secundum illud Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto[^p235-10]: « Spiritus meus super mel dulcis ». « Qui edunt me adhuc esurient, et qui bibunt me adhuc sitient ». Per haec enim duo anima in actu contemplationis figitur, sicut comedens in cibo per solatium simul et desiderium.

Primo ergo quantum ad *gaudium de dono concesso* dicit: *Et factum est, cum discederent ab illo,* id est, discedere vellent; *ait Petrus ad Iesum: Praeceptor, bonum est nos hic esse;* in quo ostendit Petrus, miro modo sibi contemplationis gloriam esse acceptam, quasi dicat illud Prophetae in Psalmo[^p235-11]: « Mihi autem adhaerere Deo bonum est »; quia, sicut dicitur Threnorum tertio, « bonus Dominus sperantibus in eum, animae quaerenti illum ». Et propterea dicebat Petrus: *Bonum est nos hic esse;* quia *bonum* est *Christum exspectare,* secundum
<!-- page 236 -->
illud Threnorum tertio[^p236-1]: « Bonum est praestolari cum silentio salutare Dei ». — *Magis bonum* est *Christum degustare;* primae Petri secundo[^p236-2]: « Si tamen gustastis, quoniam suavis est Dominus »; unde et Sapientiae duodecimo: « O quam bonus et quam suavis est Spiritus tuus, Domine, in omnibus »! scilicet rectis corde, secundum illud Psalmi: « Quam bonus Israel Deus his qui recto sunt corde »! Unde in persona Christi et animae contemplativae dicit Elcana Annae, uxori suae, primi Regum primo: « Anna, cur fles? Et quamobrem affligitur cor tuum? Nonne ego melior sum tibi quam decem filii »? — Sed *maxime bonum cum Christo gaudere,* secundum illud ad Philippenses primo[^p236-3]: « Dissolvi et esse cum Christo multo melius est »; et Matthaei vigesimo quinto: « Euge, serve bone et fidelis, quia super pauca fuisti fidelis, super multa te constituam; intra in gaudium Domini tui ». — Primum ergo bonum, quia *meritum,* secundum magis bonum, quia *viaticum,* sed tertium maxime bonum, quia *praemium.* Omnibus his modis volebat dicere Petrus, maxime ratione gaudii: *Bonum est nos hic esse.*

61. Secundo vero quantum ad *desiderium de dono continuando* adiungit: *Et*[^p236-4] *faciamus tria tabernacula,* ad continuandum gaudium nostrum, sicut filii Israel fecerunt in figura, secundum illud Levitici vigesimo tertio[^p236-5]: « Omnis, qui de genere Israel est, manebit in tabernaculis, ut discant posteri vestri, quod in tabernaculis habitare fecerim filios Israel ». — Quia tamen non uniformiter desiderabat consortium illorum trium, ideo non uniformiter omnibus, sed cuilibet optat fieri proprium tabernaculum. Unde addit: *Unum tibi,* tanquam principali; *et unum Moysi,* tanquam legislatori; *et unum Eliae,* tanquam excellenti praedicatori, ut sic secundum diversitates dignitatum distinguantur habitacula mansionum. Sic enim erit in regno caelorum, secundum illud Ioannis decimo quarto[^p236-6]: « In domo Patris mei mansiones multae sunt ». — Hoc autem dicebat Petrus per *excessivum desiderium,* quod consuevit esse in contemplantibus; et propter hoc additur: *Nesciens, quid diceret,* scilicet prae nimietate desiderii, secundum illud Psalmi[^p236-7]: « Quia inflammatum est cor meum, et renes mei commutati sunt, et ego ad nihilum redactus sum et nescivi »; et propter *profunditatem mysterii,* secundum illud Canticorum sexto[^p236-8]: « Descendi in hortum nucum, ut viderem poma convallium et inspicerem » etc.; et paulo post: « Nescivi, anima mea conturbavit me propter quadrigas Aminadab ». Et ex hac duplici causa nesciebat Petrus, quid dicebat; unde in Glossa[^p236-9]: « Etsi Petrus ex sua infirmitate nescit, quid dicat; insiti tamen sibi dat ardoris indicium, dum eos quos videre delectabat, etiam officio sistere quaerit. Errabat, quia in terra et in hac mortali carne sibi et coapostolis suis dari cupiebat regnum, quod in caelis et carne mortali exspoliatis est promissum ».

62. Notandum autem, quod Petrus decipiebatur primo, quia *volebat manere in statu,* a quo est transeundum. Dicitur enim ad Hebraeos ultimo[^p236-10]: « Non habemus hic manentem civitatem »; Petrus autem volebat manere hic. In quo etiam reprehensibiles sunt quidam, qui, dum volunt arcem contemplationis ascendere, volunt quiescere et ad laborem actionis recusant descendere. Et hi designantur per *filios Ruben,* de quibus Numerorum trigesimo secundo[^p236-11] dicitur, quod voluerunt ultra Iordanem remanere. « Quibus ait Moyses: Nunquid fratres vestri ibunt ad pugnam, et vos hic sedebitis »? quasi dicat: non. Unde « Iacob vidit scalam in somnis », Genesis vigesimo octavo, et « Angelos », non quiescentes, sed « ascendentes et descendentes per eam ». — Secundo decipiebatur in hoc, quod *umbram credebat in hoc verificatam;* cum tamen dicat Apostolus primae ad Corinthios decimo tertio[^p236-12]: « Videmus nunc per speculum in aenigmate »; et tabernaculum gloriae credebat *materiale,* cum tamen sit *spirituale,* secundum illud Psalmi: « Ingrediar in locum tabernaculi admirabilis »; hoc est « tabernaculum, quod fixit Dominus, et non homo », sicut dicitur ad Hebraeos octavo. Et hoc potissime cavendum est contemplativis, ne decipiantur in figuris ostensis. — Tertio vero, quia *dividere volebat unita,* et quod erat unum triplicare volebat. Unum est enim in gloria Sanctorum tabernaculum, secundum illud Ioannis decimo septimo[^p236-13]: « Volo, Pater, ut, ubi ego sum, et illi sint
<!-- page 237 -->
mecum »; et Apocalypsis vigesimo primo: « Ecce, tabernaculum Dei cum hominibus, et habitabit cum eis »; et Ioannis decimo: « Fiet unum ovile et unus pastor ». Haec autem deceptio non erat per iudicium et assensum, sed magis propter mentis excessum, de quo secundae ad Corinthios quinto[^p237-1]: « Sive mente excedimus, Deo » etc.; et Psalmus: « Ibi Beniamin adolescentulus in mentis excessu ».

63. (Vers. 34.). *Haec autem illo loquente* etc. Post ingressum et progressum determinat hic Evangelista *contemplationis statum* sive consummationem[^p237-2]. Consummatur autem contemplatio in duobus: quorum primum est *ostensio maiestatis terribilis,* secundum vero, *revelatio veritatis credibilis.*

Primo ergo quantum ad *maiestatis terribilis ostensionem* dicitur: *Haec autem illo loquente, facta est nubes.* Haec erat divinae maiestatis ostensiva; unde filiis Israel terribilis in nube apparebat; unde dicitur Exodi ultimo[^p237-3]: « Postquam perfecta sunt omnia, operuit nubes tabernaculum testimonii, et gloria Domini implevit illud; nec poterat Moyses ingredi tectum foederis, nube cooperiente omnia, et maiestate Domini coruscante ». Similiter tertii Regum octavo[^p237-4] dicitur: « Factum est, cum exissent sacerdotes, nebula implevit domum Domini; et non poterant sacerdotes ministrare propter nebulam; impleverat enim gloria Domini domum Domini ». Unde nubes indicium erat supernae gloriae et maiestatis. — Et propterea subditur: *Et obumbravit eos, et timuerunt.* Sentiebant enim divinam virtutem in nube, de qua supra primo[^p237-5]: « Virtus Altissimi obumbrabit tibi »; et ideo *timuerunt* propter praesentiam summae virtutis, sicut Iob, qui dicebat trigesimo primo: « Semper quasi tumentes fluctus super me timui Dominum »; et Ieremiae decimo: « Quis non timebit te, o Rex gentium » etc.

64. Secundum *mysticum* autem sensum per *nubem* intelligi potest caro Christi, primo, quia contemperat lumen Divinitatis oculis nostris, ut possimus intueri; Exodi decimo nono[^p237-6]: « Nunc veniam ad te in caligine nubis, ut audiat me populus loquentem ad te ». — Secundo vero, quia est divinae lucis vehiculum super terram tenebrosam, secundum illud Isaiae decimo nono[^p237-7]: « Ascendet Dominus super nubem levem et ingredietur Aegyptum ». — Tertio, quia ortum traxit de amaritudine et poenalitate naturae humanae, mediante Maria Virgine; quod figuratum est tertii Regum decimo octavo[^p237-8]: « Ecce, nubecula parva quasi vestigium hominis ascendebat de mari ». — Quarto, quia aquam gratiae salutaris, quam hausit in sui origine, effundit, cum agitatur vento orationis; unde Ecclesiastici quadragesimo tertio[^p237-9]: « Medicina omnium in festinatione nebulae ». — Ex hoc erudiuntur contemplativi, ut non tantum erigant oculos ad lucem Deitatis radiantem, sed etiam ad nubem humanitatis obumbrantem. Quod figuratum est Ioannis primo[^p237-10], ubi dixit Dominus Nathanaeli: « Videbitis Angelos », id est contemplativos, « ascendentes et descendentes super Filium hominis », quia in humanitate latet divina maiestas.

65. (Vers. 35.). Secundo vero quantum ad *veritatis credibilis revelationem* adiungitur: *Intrantibus autem*[^p237-11] *illis in nubem,* scilicet excitantem ad reverentiam, iuxta quod dicitur Exodi vigesimo quarto: « Ingressus Moyses medium nebulae, ascendit in montem », ubi audivit divinas revelationes. — Unde et nunc subditur: *Vox facta est de nube dicens: Hic est Filius meus dilectus;* *discretive* ipsum demonstrat, ut ostendat, quod ipse solus est *filius per naturam;* unde in Psalmo[^p237-12]: « Ex utero ante luciferum genui te ». Et ponuntur ibi discretiones tum in hoc, quod dicit *hic,* tum in hoc, quod dicit *meus,* tum in
<!-- page 238 -->
hoc, quod dicit *dilectus;* in quo declarat, proprietatem filiationis in Christo esse *personalem* et *connaturalem* et *coaequalem.* Ostenditur *personalis* per pronomen, quod certam demonstrat personam; unde ipse dicebat Ioannis octavo[^p238-1], ad demonstrationem personae suae: « Ego principium, qui et loquor vobis ». — Ostenditur *connaturalis* per hoc, quod dicit *meus,* secundum illud Psalmi[^p238-2]: « Dixit Dominus ad me: Filius meus es tu, ego hodie genui te »; quia alium non habet patrem nisi Deum, ad cuius distinctionem Ioannis vigesimo dicitur: « Ascendo ad Patrem meum et Patrem vestrum ». — Ostenditur etiam *coaequalis* in hoc, quod addit *dilectus,* id est praecipue et summe; unde Canticorum quinto[^p238-3]: « Dilectus meus electus ex millibus »; et Ioannis quinto: « Pater diligit Filium et omnia ostendit ei ».

66. Et quia ista veritas adeo erat credibilis, ut omnia dicta Christi ostenderet esse credenda; ideo adiungit: *Ipsum audite,* non aure solum corporis, sed etiam cordis, sicut Propheta in Psalmo[^p238-4]: « Audiam, quid loquatur in me Dominus ». *Audite,* inquam, cum omni *reverentia,* secundum consilium Ecclesiastici trigesimo secundo[^p238-5]: « Audi tacens, et pro reverentia accedet tibi bona gratia ». *Ipsum audite,* cum omni *obedientia,* secundum illud Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto: « Qui audiunt me non confundentur, et qui operantur in me non peccabunt ». *Ipsum audite,* cum omni *diligentia;* unde infra decimo nono: « Omnis populus suspensus erat, audiens eum » etc. — Haec autem vox firmavit corda discipulorum, ut etiam ipsa eorum doctrina ex hoc fieret authentica, secundum illud secundae Petri primo[^p238-6]: « Non enim indoctas fabulas secuti, notam fecimus vobis Domini nostri Iesu Christi virtutem et praesentiam, sed speculatores facti illius magnitudinis. Accipiens enim a Deo Patre » etc. usque ibi: « Cum essemus cum ipso in monte sancto ».

67. (Vers. 36.). *Et dum fieret vox* etc. Hic ultimo[^p238-7] post contemplationis consummationem subiungit Evangelista *ipsius subtractionem,* circa quam duo introducit Evangelista: primum est *reditus visionis solitae,* secundum est *occultatio visionis ostensae.*

Primo igitur quantum ad *reditum visionis solitae* dicitur: *Et dum fieret vox, inventus est Iesus solus,* scilicet absque Moyse et Elia, sicut et ante cum eis erat. Et hoc est quod in Canticorum octavo[^p238-8] desiderabat sponsa: « Quis mihi det te fratrem meum sugentem ubera matris meae, ut inveniam te solum foris et deosculer te, et iam me nemo despiciat »? Et hic ideo *solus* cum eis inventus est, ut intelligant transitum Legis et prophetiae et permanentiam veritatis evangelicae, iuxta quod dicitur Matthaei vigesimo quarto[^p238-9]: « Caelum et terra transibunt, verba autem mea non praeteribunt ». Unde in Glossa Bedae: « *Solus* cernitur, quia, Legis et Prophetarum umbra discedente, verum lumen, coruscante Evangelii gratia, reperitur ». — Vel, ut ostendat, quod ipse est solus, qui adorandus est, et ei tabernaculum aedificandum, iuxta illud Deuteronomii sexto[^p238-10]: « Dominum Deum tuum timebis et illi soli servies ». — Vel, ut ostendat, quod ipse solus est, qui potest salvare; unde Actuum quarto[^p238-11]: « Non est aliud nomen sub caelo datum hominibus, in quo oporteat nos salvos fieri »; et ad Hebraeos septimo: « Iesus autem, eo quod maneat in aeternum, sempiternum habet sacerdotium; unde et salvare in perpetuum potest, semper vivens ad interpellandum pro nobis ».

68. Secundo vero quantum ad *occultationem visionis ostensae* subditur: *Et ipsi tacuerunt.* Sciebant enim, scriptum esse Tobiae duodecimo[^p238-12]: « Sacramentum regis abscondere bonum est ». Nondum enim erat tempus loquendi, sed magis occultandi, secundum illud Ecclesiastae tertio: « Tempus tacendi et tempus loquendi »; et Ecclesiastici vigesimo: « Est tacens, sciens tempus apti temporis. Homo sapiens tacebit usque ad tempus, lascivus autem et imprudens non servabit tempus ». — Et tales fuerunt Apostoli; propter quod additur: *Et nemini dixerunt in illis diebus quidquam ex his quae viderant;* non quia vellent ex avaritia occultare, sed quia ex divina obedientia reticere. Praeceperat enim hoc eis Dominus, secundum illud Matthaei decimo septimo[^p238-13]: « Nemini dixeritis visionem, donec Filius hominis resurgat a mortuis ».
<!-- page 239 -->

69. Huius autem prohibitionis triplex potest ratio assignari. Una fuit informatio *contemplativorum,* quorum est celare divinum secretum, secundum illud Isaiae vigesimo quarto[^p239-1]: « Secretum meum mihi, secretum meum mihi ». Unde Maria celavit partum virginalem annis triginta, Elisabeth conceptum mensibus quinque, sicut dicitur supra primo: « Occultabat se mensibus quinque »; Paulus raptum quatuordecim annis, secundae ad Corinthios duodecimo: « Scio hominem ante quatuordecim annos » etc. — Secunda ratio fuit informatio *praedicatorum,* ut verbum veritatis non proponant, nisi videant capacitatem auditorum ad credendum et suscipiendum verbum, secundum illud Ecclesiastici trigesimo secundo[^p239-2]: « Ubi non est auditus, non effundas sermonem ». Unde in Glossa: « Domino iubente, tacent, donec Filius hominis a mortuis resurgat, ne incredibile sit pro rei magnitudine, et post tantam gloriam sequens crux scandalum faciat »; et Hieronymus[^p239-3]: « Utique doctoris est audientium considerare personas, ne irrideant, antequam audiant ». — Tertia ratio fuit instructio *universorum,* ut nemo velit laudari, nec quis audeat laudare in hac vita, secundum illud Ecclesiastici undecimo[^p239-4]: « Ante mortem ne laudes hominem quemquam, quoniam in filiis suis agnoscitur vir »; unde Ambrosius: « Lauda post mortem, praedica post consummationem ».

## English
<!-- page 234 -->
56. *But Peter and they that were with him* etc. After the demonstration of the glory promised the Evangelist here subjoins *the contemplation of the glory demonstrated.* Now he narrates it quite sufficiently, because according to a fourfold state: first, as to *the beginning;* secondly, as to *the progress,* at[^p234-12]: *And it came to pass, as they were departing from him;* thirdly, as to *the consummation,*
<!-- page 235 -->
at: *And as he spoke these things;* fourthly, as to *the withdrawal,* at: *And whilst the voice was uttered.*

Now it is to be known that to the *contemplation* of divine revelations two things come forward as it were *in the beginning,* namely *heaviness* on the part of nature and *help* on the part of grace; the first lulls to sleep, and the second arouses.

57. (Vers. 32.). First, then, as to *the heaviness* on the part of nature falling asleep it is said: *But Peter and they that were with him were heavy with sleep;* and so they all were failing. Nor is it a wonder, because they were compassed with infirmity; whence Wisdom, the ninth chapter[^p235-1]: « The body, which is corrupted, weigheth down the soul, and the earthly habitation presseth down the sense that museth upon many things ». — For since the nature of the exterior senses hinders our understanding from fixing its eye on the eternal lumen[^tr-lumen], it behoves in the beginning of contemplation that a man be as it were lulled to sleep and estranged from the senses, as it were through sleep, which does not come about without heaviness of the senses. Whence the contemplative soul says in the Canticle, the fifth chapter[^p235-2]: « I sleep, and my heart watcheth »; and Job, the thirty-third chapter: « When deep sleep falleth upon men, and they sleep in their bed; then he openeth their ears, and teaching instructeth them in discipline »; and of this it is said in Genesis, the second chapter: « The Lord cast a deep sleep upon Adam ».

58. But secondly, as to *the help* on the part of grace arousing, it is subjoined: *And waking, they saw his majesty and the two men that stood with him.* This *vigil* is through the desire and love of the Holy Spirit, according to that of Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter[^p235-3]: « Lord, my soul hath desired thee in the night: yea, and with my spirit in my inward parts in the morning I will watch to thee ». But this is most of all necessary in *prayer;* whence the Lord to Peter and his companions, Matthew, the twenty-sixth chapter[^p235-4]: « Watch ye, and pray that ye enter not into temptation; the spirit indeed is willing » etc.; Mark, the thirteenth chapter: « Take ye heed, watch and pray ». « What I say to one, I say to all: Watch ». — But to this vigil *the reward promised* invites us above all, according to that below, the twelfth chapter[^p235-5]: « Blessed are those servants, whom the lord when he cometh shall find watching; amen I say to you » etc. *The danger foreseen* invites us also, according to that of the first of Peter, the last chapter: « Be sober and watch, because your adversary the devil » etc.; and Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter: « Amen I say to you: If the goodman of the house knew at what hour the thief would come, he would certainly watch » etc.

59. But *vigil* is most of all necessary for *contemplation,* because grace offered is very swiftly withdrawn; whence the Canticle, the fifth chapter[^p235-6]: « I opened the bolt to my beloved; but he had turned aside and was gone ». — Now in this *awakening* is seen *the divine majesty in the creatures,* according to that of Wisdom, the thirteenth chapter[^p235-7]: « For by the greatness of the beauty and of the creature » etc.; whence Isaias, the sixth chapter: « I saw the Lord sitting upon a throne high, and full » etc. — There is seen *the divine humility in the Scriptures;* on account of which he says: *And the two men,* that is, the Law and the Prophets, of which vision it is said below, in the last chapter[^p235-8]: « He opened their understanding, that they might understand the Scriptures »; and again: « Beginning at Moses and all the Prophets, he expounded to them in all things » etc. — On account of these two modes of contemplating a twofold book was made: of Scripture and of the creature, which are designated in the ascent and descent by *the ladder of Jacob,* Genesis, the twenty-eighth chapter[^p235-9]; by *the going out and the going in by the door,* John, the tenth chapter; by *the eagle and its young ones;* Job, the thirty-ninth chapter: « Will the eagle mount up at thy command »? and after: « His young ones shall suck up blood ».

60. (Vers. 33.). *And it came to pass, as they were departing.* After the opening of contemplation he subjoins *the progress of contemplation;* in which two things are most of all required, namely an exceeding *joy over the gift granted* and an exceeding *desire of continuing it,* according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p235-10]: « My spirit is sweet above honey ». « They that eat me shall yet hunger, and they that drink me shall yet thirst ». For by these two the soul is fixed in the act of contemplation, as one eating is fixed in food through solace and desire together.

First, then, as to *the joy over the gift granted* he says: *And it came to pass, as they were departing from him,* that is, were wishing to depart; *Peter said to Jesus: Master*[^tr-praeceptor]*, it is good for us to be here;* in which Peter shows that the glory of contemplation was in a wonderful manner acceptable to him, as if he were saying that of the Prophet in the Psalm[^p235-11]: « But it is good for me to adhere to God »; because, as is said in Lamentations, the third chapter, « the Lord is good to them that hope in him, to the soul that seeketh him ». And therefore Peter said: *It is good for us to be here;* because it is *good to wait for Christ,* according to
<!-- page 236 -->
that of Lamentations, the third chapter[^p236-1]: « It is good to wait with silence for the salvation of God ». — It is *better to taste Christ;* the first of Peter, the second chapter[^p236-2]: « If so be you have tasted that the Lord is sweet »; whence also Wisdom, the twelfth chapter: « O how good and sweet is thy Spirit, O Lord, in all things »! namely to the right of heart, according to that of the Psalm: « How good is God to Israel, to them that are of a right heart »! Whence in the person of Christ and of the contemplative soul Elcana says to Anna, his wife, the first of Kings, the first chapter: « Anna, why weepest thou? And why is thy heart afflicted? Am not I better to thee than ten children »? — But *best of all to rejoice with Christ,* according to that to the Philippians, the first chapter[^p236-3]: « To be dissolved and to be with Christ is by far the better »; and Matthew, the twenty-fifth chapter: « Well done, good and faithful servant, because thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will place thee over many things; enter thou into the joy of thy Lord ». — The first, then, is good, because it is *merit;* the second better, because it is *viaticum;* but the third best of all, because it is *reward.* In all these modes Peter wished to say, most of all by reason of the joy: *It is good for us to be here.*

61. But secondly, as to *the desire of continuing the gift* he adds: *And*[^p236-4] *let us make three tabernacles,* to continue our joy, as the children of Israel did in figure, according to that of Leviticus, the twenty-third chapter[^p236-5]: « Every one that is of the race of Israel shall dwell in tabernacles, that your posterity may know that I made the children of Israel to dwell in tabernacles ». — Yet because he did not desire the fellowship of those three uniformly, therefore he wishes a tabernacle to be made not uniformly for all, but proper to each one. Whence he adds: *One for thee,* as for the principal; *and one for Moses,* as for the legislator; *and one for Elias,* as for an excellent preacher, that so according to the diversities of dignities the habitations of the mansions might be distinguished. For so will it be in the kingdom of heaven, according to that of John, the fourteenth chapter[^p236-6]: « In my Father's house there are many mansions ». — But Peter said this through *an excessive desire,* which is wont to be in those who contemplate; and on account of this it is added: *Not knowing what he said,* namely for the immoderateness of desire, according to that of the Psalm[^p236-7]: « For my heart hath been inflamed, and my reins have been changed, and I am brought to nothing, and I knew not »; and on account of *the depth of the mystery,* according to that of the Canticle, the sixth chapter[^p236-8]: « I went down into the garden of nuts, to see the fruits of the valleys, and to look » etc.; and a little after: « I knew not; my soul troubled me for the chariots of Aminadab ». And from this twofold cause Peter did not know what he was saying; whence in the Gloss[^p236-9]: « Although Peter from his infirmity does not know what he says, yet he gives an indication of the ardour implanted in him, while he seeks to stay by service those whom it delighted him to see. He erred, because on earth and in this mortal flesh he desired the kingdom to be given to himself and his fellow apostles, which is promised in heaven and to those stripped of mortal flesh ».

62. But it is to be noted that Peter was deceived, first, because *he wished to abide in a state* from which one must pass over. For it is said to the Hebrews, the last chapter[^p236-10]: « For we have not here an abiding city »; but Peter wished to abide here. In which certain ones also are reprehensible, who, while they will to ascend the citadel of contemplation, will to rest and refuse to come down to the labour of action. And these are designated by *the sons of Ruben,* of whom it is said in Numbers, the thirty-second chapter[^p236-11], that they willed to remain beyond the Jordan. « To whom Moses said: Shall your brethren go to fight, and you sit here »? as if to say: no. Whence « Jacob saw a ladder in his sleep », Genesis, the twenty-eighth chapter, and « Angels », not resting, but « ascending and descending by it ». — Secondly, he was deceived in this, that *he believed the shadow verified in this;* whereas the Apostle says, the first to the Corinthians, the thirteenth chapter[^p236-12]: « We see now through a glass in a dark manner »; and he believed the tabernacle of glory *material,* whereas it is *spiritual,* according to that of the Psalm: « I shall go into the place of the wonderful tabernacle »; that is, « the tabernacle which the Lord hath pitched, and not man », as is said to the Hebrews, the eighth chapter. And this is most of all to be guarded against by contemplatives, lest they be deceived in the figures shown. — But thirdly, because *he wished to divide things united,* and what was one he wished to triple. For one is the tabernacle in the glory of the Saints, according to that of John, the seventeenth chapter[^p236-13]: « Father, I will that where I am, they also may be
<!-- page 237 -->
with me »; and the Apocalypse, the twenty-first chapter: « Behold, the tabernacle of God with men, and he will dwell with them »; and John, the tenth chapter: « There shall be one fold and one shepherd ». But this deception was not through judgement and assent, but rather on account of the transport of mind, of which it is said in the second to the Corinthians, the fifth chapter[^p237-1]: « Whether we be transported in mind, it is to God » etc.; and the Psalm: « There is Benjamin a youth, in transport of mind ».

63. (Vers. 34.). *And as he spoke these things* etc. After the ingress and the progress the Evangelist here determines *the state of contemplation,* or its consummation[^p237-2]. Now contemplation is consummated in two things: of which the first is *the showing of the terrible majesty,* but the second, *the revelation of the credible truth.*

First, then, as to *the showing of the terrible majesty* it is said: *And as he spoke these things, there came a cloud.* This was ostensive of the divine majesty; whence it appeared terrible to the children of Israel in a cloud; whence it is said in Exodus, the last chapter[^p237-3]: « After all things were perfected, the cloud covered the tabernacle of the testimony, and the glory of the Lord filled it; neither could Moses go into the tabernacle of the covenant, the cloud covering all things, and the majesty of the Lord shining ». Likewise it is said in the third of Kings, the eighth chapter[^p237-4]: « It came to pass, when the priests were come out, a cloud filled the house of the Lord; and the priests could not minister because of the cloud; for the glory of the Lord had filled the house of the Lord ». Whence the cloud was an indication of the supernal glory and majesty. — And therefore it is subjoined: *And it overshadowed them, and they were afraid.* For they felt the divine power in the cloud, of which it is said above, in the first chapter[^p237-5]: « The power of the most High shall overshadow thee »; and therefore *they were afraid* on account of the presence of the highest power, as Job, who said in the thirty-first chapter: « For I have always feared the Lord as waves swelling over me »; and Jeremias, the tenth chapter: « Who shall not fear thee, O King of nations » etc.

64. But according to the *mystical* sense by *the cloud* can be understood the flesh of Christ, first, because it tempers the lumen of the Divinity to our eyes, that we may be able to behold it; Exodus, the nineteenth chapter[^p237-6]: « Now will I come to thee in the darkness of a cloud, that the people may hear me speaking to thee ». — But secondly, because it is the vehicle of the divine light over the darkened earth, according to that of Isaias, the nineteenth chapter[^p237-7]: « The Lord will ascend upon a swift cloud, and will enter into Egypt ». — Thirdly, because it took its rise from the bitterness and penalty of human nature, by the mediation of the Virgin Mary; which was figured in the third of Kings, the eighteenth chapter[^p237-8]: « Behold, a little cloud like a man's foot ascended out of the sea ». — Fourthly, because the water of saving grace which it drew in its origin it pours out, when it is stirred by the wind of prayer; whence Ecclesiasticus, the forty-third chapter[^p237-9]: « The remedy of all is in the speedy coming of a cloud ». — From this contemplatives are taught that they should raise their eyes not only to the light of the Deity shining forth, but also to the cloud of the humanity overshadowing. Which was figured in John, the first chapter[^p237-10], where the Lord said to Nathanael: « You shall see Angels », that is, contemplatives, « ascending and descending upon the Son of man », because in the humanity the divine majesty lies hidden.

65. (Vers. 35.). But secondly, as to *the revelation of the credible truth* it is added: *But*[^p237-11] *they entering into the cloud,* namely one arousing to reverence, according to what is said in Exodus, the twenty-fourth chapter: « Moses entering into the midst of the cloud, went up into the mountain », where he heard the divine revelations. — Whence now too it is subjoined: *A voice came out of the cloud, saying: This is my beloved Son;* he points him out *discretively,* to show that he alone is *son by nature;* whence in the Psalm[^p237-12]: « From the womb before the day star I begot thee ». And discretions are set there both in this, that he says *this,* and in this, that he says *my,* and in
<!-- page 238 -->
this, that he says *beloved;* in which he declares that the property of filiation in Christ is *personal* and *connatural* and *coequal.* It is shown *personal* by the pronoun, which points out a certain person; whence he himself said, John, the eighth chapter[^p238-1], for the pointing out of his person: « The beginning, who also speak unto you ». — It is shown *connatural* by this, that he says *my,* according to that of the Psalm[^p238-2]: « The Lord hath said to me: Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee »; because he has no other father but God, for whose distinction it is said in John, the twentieth chapter: « I ascend to my Father and to your Father ». — It is shown *coequal* also in this, that he adds *beloved,* that is, chiefly and supremely; whence the Canticle, the fifth chapter[^p238-3]: « My beloved is chosen out of thousands »; and John, the fifth chapter: « The Father loveth the Son, and sheweth him all things ».

66. And because this truth was so credible that it showed all the sayings of Christ to be believed; therefore he adds: *Hear ye him,* not with the ear of the body only, but also of the heart, as the Prophet in the Psalm[^p238-4]: « I will hear what the Lord will speak in me ». *Hear ye,* I say, with all *reverence,* according to the counsel of Ecclesiasticus, the thirty-second chapter[^p238-5]: « Hear in silence, and for reverence good grace shall come to thee ». *Hear ye him,* with all *obedience,* according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth chapter: « They that hear me shall not be confounded, and they that work by me shall not sin ». *Hear ye him,* with all *diligence;* whence below, the nineteenth chapter: « All the people were very attentive, hearing him » etc. — But this voice strengthened the hearts of the disciples, so that even their very doctrine became authentic from this, according to that of the second of Peter, the first chapter[^p238-6]: « For not by following unlearned fables have we made known to you the power and presence of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we were made eyewitnesses of his greatness. For he received from God the Father » etc. as far as there: « When we were with him in the holy mount ».

67. (Vers. 36.). *And whilst the voice was uttered* etc. Here lastly[^p238-7], after the consummation of contemplation, the Evangelist subjoins *its withdrawal,* about which the Evangelist brings in two things: the first is *the return of the accustomed vision,* the second is *the hiding of the vision shown.*

First, then, as to *the return of the accustomed vision* it is said: *And whilst the voice was uttered, Jesus was found alone,* namely without Moses and Elias, as he was also before with them. And this is what the spouse desired in the Canticle, the eighth chapter[^p238-8]: « Who shall give thee to me for my brother, sucking the breasts of my mother, that I may find thee alone without, and kiss thee, and now no man may despise me »? And here he was found *alone* with them for this, that they might understand the passing of the Law and of prophecy and the permanence of the evangelical truth, according to what is said in Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p238-9]: « Heaven and earth shall pass, but my words shall not pass ». Whence in the Gloss of Bede: « He is seen *alone,* because, the shadow of the Law and the Prophets departing, the true lumen is found, the grace of the Gospel shining forth ». — Or, to show that he is the only one who is to be adored, and to whom a tabernacle is to be built, according to that of Deuteronomy, the sixth chapter[^p238-10]: « Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and shalt serve him only ». — Or, to show that he alone is the one who can save; whence Acts, the fourth chapter[^p238-11]: « There is no other name under heaven given to men, whereby we must be saved » [so printed: the Latin reads *non* for *nos*]; and to the Hebrews, the seventh chapter: « But Jesus, for that he continueth for ever, hath an everlasting priesthood; whereby he is able also to save for ever, always living to make intercession for us ».

68. But secondly, as to *the hiding of the vision shown* it is subjoined: *And they held their peace.* For they knew that it is written in Tobias, the twelfth chapter[^p238-12]: « It is good to hide the secret of a king ». For it was not yet the time of speaking, but rather of hiding, according to that of Ecclesiastes, the third chapter: « A time to keep silence, and a time to speak »; and Ecclesiasticus, the twentieth chapter: « There is one that holdeth his peace, knowing the time of the fit time. A wise man will hold his peace till the time; but the wanton and the imprudent will not keep the time ». — And such were the Apostles; on account of which it is added: *And they told no man in those days any of these things which they had seen;* not because they wished to hide it out of avarice, but because they wished to keep silence out of divine obedience. For the Lord had enjoined this on them, according to that of Matthew, the seventeenth chapter[^p238-13]: « Tell the vision to no man, till the Son of man be risen from the dead ».
<!-- page 239 -->

69. Now of this prohibition a threefold reason can be assigned. One was the formation of *contemplatives,* whose part it is to conceal the divine secret, according to that of Isaias, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p239-1]: « My secret to myself, my secret to myself ». Whence Mary concealed the virginal birth for thirty years, Elizabeth her conception for five months, as is said above, in the first chapter: « She hid herself five months »; Paul his rapture for fourteen years, the second to the Corinthians, the twelfth chapter: « I know a man fourteen years ago » etc. — The second reason was the formation of *preachers,* that they may not set forth the word of truth unless they see the capacity of the hearers for believing and receiving the word, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the thirty-second chapter[^p239-2]: « Where there is no hearing, pour not out words ». Whence in the Gloss: « At the Lord's bidding they are silent until the Son of man rise from the dead, lest it be incredible for the greatness of the thing, and lest, after so great a glory, the cross following should give scandal »; and Jerome[^p239-3]: « It is indeed the part of a teacher to consider the persons of the hearers, lest they deride before they hear ». — The third reason was the instruction of *all,* that no one should wish to be praised, nor anyone dare to praise in this life, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the eleventh chapter[^p239-4]: « Praise not any man before death, for a man is known by his children »; whence Ambrose: « Praise after death, proclaim after consummation ».
