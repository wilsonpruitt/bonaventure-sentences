# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. II, Vers. 40-52
## *Qualiter Christus factus sit sub lege morali*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 65 -->

#### Qualiter Christus factus sit sub lege morali.

91. *Puer autem crescebat* etc. Supra[^p65-3] descripsit Evangelista, qualiter Salvator factus est sub lege caeremoniali, hic describit, qualiter factus est sub *lege morali.* Sed quoniam tota morum ordinatio principaliter attenditur circa tria, scilicet circa *cultum maiestatis* et *studium veritatis* et *officium pietatis;* per primum subiicitur homo suo *Creatori,* per secundum *doctori,* per tertium *genitori:* ideo pars ista habet tres, in quarum prima ostenditur, qualiter Christus factus est sub lege morali quantum ad *Maiestatis cultum;* in secunda, qualiter factus est sub lege morali quantum ad *veritatis studium,* ibi[^p65-4]: *Et factum est, post triduum;* in tertia, qualiter factus est sub lege morali quantum ad *pietatis obsequium,* ibi: *Et dixit Mater eius ad illum* etc.

Ad perfectam descriptionem *cultus divini* duo introducuntur: primum est *excellentia virtutis propriae,* secundum est *reverentia maiestatis divinae,* ibi[^p65-5]: *Et ibant parentes eius per omnes annos* etc. — *Excellentia* autem *virtutis* in Christo ostenditur ex parte *mentis,* quamvis esset defectus aetatis ex parte *carnis;* propter quod praemittit *profectum aetatis* in carne et subiungit *perfectionem virtutis* in mente.

92. (Vers. 40.). Quantum autem ad profectum *aetatis in carne* dicitur: *Puer autem crescebat,* scilicet in corpore, sicut exponit Beda[^p65-6]: « In quantum *puer* erat, id est homo fragilis, crescere poterat, sicut ceteri homines, non in quantum *Verbum* ». — Quantum autem ad perfectionem *virtutis in mente* subditur: *Confortabatur.* Perfectio autem virtutis mentalis consistit in tribus, scilicet in *vigore potentiae* respectu irascibilis, in *splendore sapientiae* respectu rationalis et *fervore gratiae* respectu concupiscibilis. — Quantum igitur ad *vigorem potentiae* irascibilis dicitur: *Et confortabatur;* Ezechielis tertio[^p65-7]: « Manus Domini erat mecum confortans me »; ad Philippenses ultimo: « Omnia possum in eo qui me confortat ». — Quantum ad *splendorem sapientiae* in rationali subditur: *Plenus sapientia;* ad Colossenses secundo[^p65-8]: « Nam in ipso sunt omnes thesauri sapientiae et scientiae absconditi »; et Isaiae undecimo: « Requiescet super eum spiritus Domini, spiritus sapientiae et intellectus ». — Quantum vero ad *fervorem gratiae* in concupiscibili subditur: *Et gratia Dei erat in illo;* Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto[^p65-9]: « In me gratia omnis viae et veritatis, in me omnis spes vitae » etc.; et Ioannis primo: « Et vidimus gloriam eius, gloriam quasi Unigeniti a Patre, plenum gratiae et veritatis ».

93. (Vers. 41.). *Et ibant parentes eius* etc. Hic describitur *reverentia maiestatis divinae,* quae consistit in cultu religionis, et ostenditur in Christo secundum *parentum ducatum* et secundum *proprium instinctum.* — Quantum ad *parentum ducatum* dicitur: *Et ibant parentes eius per omnes annos in Ierusalem.* Nam ita mandatum erat in Lege; Exodi vigesimo tertio[^p65-10]: « Ter in anno mihi festa celebrabitis ». Et credendum est, quod etiam ducerent Iesum, quia ibidem subditur: « Ter in anno apparebit omne masculinum tuum coram Domino Deo tuo »; et hoc in Ierusalem, secundum praeceptum Deuteronomii, duodecimo[^p65-11]: « Ad locum, quem elegerit Dominus Deus vester de cunctis tribubus vestris, accedetis ». Et iste locus erat Ierusalem, secundum quod dicitur secundi Paralipomenon septimo: « Elegi et sanctificavi locum istum, ut sit ibi nomen meum in sempiternum ». Et istum cultum maxime in pascha faciebant, et ideo subditur: *In die solemni paschae;* Exodi duodecimo[^p65-12]: « Habebitis hanc diem in monumentum et celebrabitis eam solemnem Domino in generationibus vestris cultu sempiterno ».

94. (Vers. 42.). Quantum autem ad *proprium instinctum* etiam divino cultui vacabat puer Iesus; quantum ad quem subditur: *Et cum factus esset annorum duodecim;* in quo ostenditur, quod puer
<!-- page 66 -->
Iesus divino cultui vacabat sine parentum *consortio,* sine *consilio,* sine *complacito.* — Sine *consortio* quidem; licet enim cum consortio parentum ascenderit, tamen sine eis remansit; propter quod dicitur: *Ascendentibus illis Ierusalem secundum consuetudinem diei festi,* scilicet ad orandum, sicut infra decimo octavo[^p66-1]: « Duo homines ascendebant in templum, ut orarent »; et Actuum tertio: « Petrus et Ioannes ascendebant in templum » etc.

(Vers. 43.). *Consummatisque diebus, cum redirent,* scilicet ad propria, secundum illud Iudith decimo sexto[^p66-2]: Celebrata festivitate, « unusquisque rediit in sua ». Et cum omnes redirent, non tamen Iesus; unde et subditur: *Remansit puer Iesus in Ierusalem,* et ita solus, sine parentum consortio. — Huius figura praecessit in puero Samuele, de quo dicitur primi Regum secundo[^p66-3], quod « Elcana abiit », pater eius, et mater abiit « in domum suam; puer autem ministrabat coram Domino ». Unde solus remanserat, ut melius orationi vacaret; Threnorum tertio: « Sedebit solitarius et tacebit ». Et ita remansit proprio instinctu sine parentum consortio.

95. Remansit etiam sine *consilio;* unde et subditur: *Et non cognoverunt parentes eius.* Cuius figura praecessit in Samsone, « qui noluit patri et matri indicare, quod mel de ore leonis assumserat », Iudicum decimo quarto[^p66-4]. In quo etiam praebuit exemplum, quod in divino obsequio faciendo non est a parentibus carnalibus consilium requirendum; Deuteronomii trigesimo tertio: « Qui dixerit patri suo et matri: Nescio vos; et fratribus: Ignoro illos; hi custodierunt eloquium tuum et pactum tuum servaverunt ».

96. (Vers. 44.). Et ne putes, quod ista ignorantia fuerit ex negligentia, propter quam esset reprehensibilis Maria, subdit causam: *Existimantes autem, illum esse in comitatu, venerunt iter diei;* quia, cum viri irent cum viris, et mulieres cum mulieribus seorsum; probabile erat patri, quod puer esset cum matre, et probabile matri, quod esset cum patre[^p66-5]. Et ideo fuerunt decepti. Non erat de genere illorum, de quibus dicitur Iob vigesimo primo: « Semen eorum permanet coram eis, propinquorum turba et nepotum in conspectu eorum », sed erat, sicut ille, de quo in Psalmo: « Considerabam ad dexteram et videbam, et non erat qui cognosceret me ». Et sic remansit sine parentum consilio.

97. Remansit etiam sine *complacito,* quia cum dolore quaerebant, quem amiserant; unde subditur: *Et requirebant eum inter cognatos et notos;* et tamen non invenerunt, quia tales elongantur ab ipso; Iob decimo nono[^p66-6]: « Noti mei quasi alieni recesserunt a me ».

98. (Vers. 45.). Et ideo sequitur: *Et non invenientes* eum, *reversi*[^p66-7] *sunt in Ierusalem,* ut quem non poterant invenire in consortio cognationis invenirent in loco orationis, secundum illud Psalmi: « Ecce, audivimus eum in Ephrata »; et post: « Introibimus in tabernaculum eius ». *Reversi sunt,* inquam, *requirentes eum;* secundum illud Canticorum tertio[^p66-8]: « Surgam et circuibo civitatem, quaeram quem diligit anima mea »; ita dicebat virgo Maria; et iterum Canticorum quinto: « Quaesivi, et non inveni illum; vocavi, et non respondit mihi ». « Adiuro vos, filiae Ierusalem, ut, si inveneritis dilectum meum, nuntietis ei, quia amore langueo ».

99. In hoc autem admonemur, ut ab ipsa infantia divino cultui mancipemur; primi Regum secundo[^p66-9]: « Samuel ministrabat ante faciem Domini, puer accinctus ephod lineo »; et maxime ab anno duodecimo, quia tunc incipit tempus discretionis convertendae ad bonum. Unde de sancto Martino dicitur[^p66-10], quod « cum esset annorum duodecim, eremum concupivit »; similiter dicitur de beato Benedicto. Ideo Evangelista hanc aetatem exprimit. — Admonemur etiam, ut nullatenus propter amorem parentum divinum cultum intermittamus; unde Hieronymus[^p66-11]: « Per calcatum perge patrem, per calcatam perge matrem et ad vexillum crucis advola. Summum enim pietatis genus est, in his crudelem esse »; dicitur enim Matthaei decimo: « Qui amat patrem aut matrem plus quam me, non est me dignus ».

100. *Et factum est post triduum* etc. Supra ostendit Evangelista, qualiter Christus Dominus factus est sub lege morali quantum ad cultum divinae maiestatis; hic secundo ostendit, qualiter factus est sub eadem quantum ad *studium veritatis;* in quo primo describit Evangelista eius *humiliationem* in
<!-- page 67 -->
assumendo *discipuli formam;* secundo vero, eius *clarificationem* in aperiendo *sapientiae praerogativam,* ibi[^p67-1]: *Stupebant autem* etc.

101. (Vers. 46.). Formam autem *discipuli* ostendit Salvatorem assumsisse, cum dicitur: *Et factum est, post triduum invenerunt eum*[^p67-2] *in templo sedentem. Sessio* designat quietem et humilitatem; sciebat enim, quod « melius est sedere in angulo domatis quam cum muliere litigiosa et in domo communi », Proverbiorum vigesimo primo[^p67-3]. *Sedentem,* inquam, *in medio doctorum;* Ecclesiastici decimo quinto: « In medio ecclesiae aperuit os eius, et implevit eum spiritu sapientiae et intellectus ». Sciebat enim scriptum esse: « Qui ad sapientes graditur sapientior erit, amicus stultorum efficietur similis », Proverbiorum decimo tertio. — Et in hoc docet consortium studentium diligendum, non ad litigandum, sed ad conferendum; propter quod subditur: *Audientem illos et interrogantem.* Hoc enim est exercitium addiscentis, ut audiat et interroget de dubiis; Ecclesiastici trigesimo secundo[^p67-4]: « Audi tacens, et pro reverentia accedit tibi gratia bona », quia « auris bona cum omni concupiscentia audiet sapientiam », Ecclesiastici tertio; et ideo dicitur Ioannis octavo: « Qui ex Deo est verba Dei audit ». Quia non sufficit audire, ideo subditur: *Et interrogantem;* Iob octavo[^p67-5]: « Interroga generationem pristinam, et diligenter investiga patrum memoriam »; et Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo: « Interroga patrem tuum, et annuntiabit tibi » etc. Interrogabat autem, non ut doceretur, sed magis ut doceret. Hieronymus[^p67-6]: « Duodecim annos Salvator impleverat, et in templo sacerdotes interrogans, magis docet, dum prudenter interrogat ».

102. In hoc autem *spiritualiter* est notandum, quod Christus docet nos, quod ante debemus discere quam docere, secundum illud Ecclesiastici decimo octavo[^p67-7]: « Antequam loquaris, disce ». Et ideo voluit *in medio doctorum* inveniri et *in templo,* quia in Scripturarum lectione et in oratione veritas invenitur. Unde nota, quod Christus inventus est in *praesepio;* supra eodem[^p67-8]: « Hoc vobis signum »; id est in *studio sacrae Scripturae,* quia, secundum quod dicitur Proverbiorum decimo quarto, « ubi non sunt boves, praesepe vacuum est ». *Boves* sunt praedicatores; primae ad Corinthios nono: « Non alligabis os bovi trituranti » etc. — Inventus est in *domo;* Matthaei secundo[^p67-9]: « Intrantes domum »; id est in *scrutinio conscientiae quietae;* Sapientiae octavo: « Intrans domum meam, conquiescam cum illa ». — Inventus est etiam in *templo,* sicut hic, id est in *consortio sanctae Ecclesiae,* quia « Dominus in templo sancto suo », ut dicitur in Psalmo[^p67-10]. — Non autem invenitur in *monumento doctrinae erroneae;* Lucae ultimo: Mulieres « venerunt ad monumentum et non invenerunt corpus Iesu »; nec etiam in *lectulo conscientiae inquietae;* Canticorum tertio: « In lectulo meo quaesivi quem diligit anima mea, quaesivi illum et non inveni »; nec invenitur in *consortio vitae mundanae;* Iob vigesimo octavo: « Sapientia vero, ubi invenitur »? et post: « Non invenitur in terra suaviter viventium ».

103. (Vers. 47.). *Stupebant autem* etc. Hic post humiliationem subiungit *clarificationem;* cum dicitur: *Stupebant autem omnes, qui eum audiebant, super prudentia et responsis eius;* pro eo quod prudentissime respondebat, supra aestimationem. *Stupor* est enim ex insolita et magna imaginatione; infra quinto[^p67-11]: « Stupor apprehendit omnes »; Iob duodecimo: « Adducit consiliarios in stultum finem et iudices in stuporem ». Et sic impletum est illud Sapientiae octavo: « Habebo propter hanc claritatem ad turbas et honorem apud seniores iuvenis; acutus inveniar in iudicio et in conspectu potentium admirabilis ero, facies principum mirabuntur me ». Hoc in Christo impletum est nunc. — Huius *figura* praecessit tertii Regum decimo[^p67-12], ubi dicitur, quod « videns regina Saba sapientiam Salomonis, non habebat ultra spiritum », propter nimiam admirationem.

<!-- page 68 -->
104. (Vers. 48.). Et non solum illi *extranei* stupebant, sed etiam *parentes;* unde et subditur: *Et videntes admirati sunt;* Matthaei septimo[^p68-1]: « Miratae sunt turbae super doctrina eius ». Et vere mirabile, quod respondebat quod non didicerat; Ioannis septimo: « Mirabantur Iudaei dicentes: Quomodo hic litteras scit, cum non didicerit »?

105. *Et dixit Mater eius* etc. Hic tertio ostendit, qualiter factus est sub lege morali quantum ad *officium pietatis;* in quo praemittitur primo *humiliatio,* et subditur *clarificatio,* ibi[^p68-2]: *Et Mater eius conservabat omnia verba haec.*

*Humiliatio* autem ostenditur in duobus, scilicet in *subiacendo corripienti* et in *obsequendo praecipienti. Subiecit* enim se Christus *matri corripienti;* propter quod dicitur: *Et dixit Mater eius ad illum: Fili, quid fecisti nobis sic?* Ipsa fiducialius loquitur et interrogat causam dimissionis, cum sciat, se honorari debere ab eo, secundum illud Exodi vigesimo[^p68-3]: « Honora patrem tuum et matrem tuam »; et Proverbiorum sexto: « Conserva, fili, praecepta patris tui, et ne dimittas legem matris tuae ». Sed tu, inquit, conturbasti nos; unde et subdit: *Pater tuus,* scilicet putativus vel adoptivus sive educativus; *et ego dolentes quaerebamus te,* quasi insimul dicebamus illud Tobiae decimo[^p68-4]: « Heu me, fili, ut quid te misimus peregrinari, lumen oculorum nostrorum, baculum senectutis nostrae, solatium vitae nostrae! Omnia enim in te uno habentes, te non debuimus dimittere ».

106. (Vers. 49.). Et nota, quod ad quaestionem respondet humiliter et sapienter; unde et subditur: *Et ait ad illos: Quid est, quod me quaerebatis?* quasi absentem, cum ego essem vobis praesens; Ieremiae vigesimo tertio[^p68-5]: « Caelum et terram ego impleo »; et hoc ostendit, quia Filius erat aeterni Patris. Unde et subdit: *Nesciebatis, quia in his quae Patris mei sunt, oportet me esse?* Ioannis sexto: « Descendi de caelo, non ut faciam voluntatem meam, sed voluntatem eius qui misit me ». Unde non debetis mirari, si vos dimisi propter Patrem aeternum, quia sic dicitur Actuum quinto: « Obedire Deo oportet magis quam hominibus ».

107. (Vers. 50.). Et quia verbum erat occultum, subditur: *Et ipsi non intellexerunt*[^p68-6]; primae ad Corinthios secundo: « Sapientiam loquimur in mysterio absconditam »; et Tobiae duodecimo: « Sacramentum regis abscondere bonum est ». — In hoc autem, quod Christus, cum correptus est, se humiliter excusavit; et cum invitatus est a Matre, ut miraculum faceret, respondit dure; dedit nobis exemplum et formam humilitatis, ut malimus corripi quam laudari; Ecclesiastae septimo[^p68-7]: « Melius est a sapiente corripi, quam stultorum adulatione decipi ». Simul etiam cum hoc insinuavit, quod pietas ad Deum praeferenda est pietati respectu parentum.

108. (Vers. 51.). *Subiacuit* etiam Dominus *praecipienti;* propter quod subditur: *Et descendit cum eis et venit Nazareth.* Modo impletum est illud Baruch tertio[^p68-8]: « Post haec in terris visus est et cum hominibus conversatus est ». *Descendit,* inquam, quod est signum humiliationis; Canticorum sexto: « Descendi in hortum meum, ut viderem poma convallium », id est humiles. « Quia excelsus Dominus et humilia respicit », scilicet de propinquo, « et alta a longe cognoscit ». Propter quod et subditur: *Et erat subditus illis.* Bernardus[^p68-9]: « Quis? quibus? Deus hominibus. Deus, inquam, se hominibus subdit; et tu dominari gestiens, tuo te praeponis auctori? Quoties enim hominibus praeesse desidero, toties Deum meum praeire contendo, ut tale mihi aliquid Dominus respondeat: *Vade post me, satana, quia non sapis quae Dei sunt* ». Unde vere poterat ipse dicere illud primae ad Corinthios nono[^p68-10]: « Cum essem liber ex omnibus, omnium me servum feci ». Et nos omnibus amore ipsius debemus esse subiecti; primae Petri secundo: « Subiecti estote omni humanae creaturae propter Deum ».

109. *Et Mater eius* etc. Hic describitur eius *clarificatio,* et hoc per duo, scilicet per *Matris testimonium* et per *propriae virtutis indicium.* Quantum ad *Matris testimonium* dicitur: *Et Mater eius conservabat omnia verba haec,* ut pro loco et tempore testimonium perhiberet, sicut dicit Beda in Glossa[^p68-11]. Sic dictum est Danielis duodecimo: « Tu autem, Daniel, claude librum et signa sermones usque
<!-- page 69 -->
ad tempus statutum ». Et quia haec intelligebat, ideo dicitur: *Conferens*[^p69-1] *in corde suo,* secundum illud Ecclesiastae octavo: « Tempus et responsionem cor sapientis intelligit »; et Marci nono dicitur de transfiguratione Domini, quod « discipuli continuerunt verbum apud se ». Nondum enim venerant Apostoli, quibus « datum erat nosse mysterium regni Dei », sicut dicitur Matthaei decimo tertio[^p69-2], qui etiam invicem conferrent, secundum illud ad Galatas secundo: « Contuli cum illis Evangelium, quod praedico in gentibus ».

110. (Vers. 52.). Quantum autem ad *propriae virtutis indicium* subditur: *Et Iesus proficiebat aetate,* scilicet ex parte corporis, sicut Samuel; primi Regum secundo[^p69-3]: « Puer Samuel proficiebat atque crescebat et placebat tam Deo quam hominibus ». Et iste profectus erat secundum *essentiam,* sed interior profectus erat secundum *evidentiam,* de quo dicitur: *Et sapientia,* scilicet in intellectu, *et gratia,* in affectu, *apud Deum,* scilicet honorandum, *et homines,* aedificandos. Nec est contrarium ei quod dicitur supra proximo[^p69-4], quod erat « plenus sapientia »; unde Gregorius: « Proficiebat sapientia, non per accessum temporis accipiendo quod non habebat, sed dona gratiae et sapientiae, quae habebat, ceteris pandendo »; et hoc ad aedificationem aliorum; secundae ad Corinthios octavo: « Providemus bona, non solum coram Deo, sed etiam coram hominibus ». Unde ad nostram aedificationem sapientiam ad tempus *abscondit,* ut ostendat, quod nos debemus tacere usque ad tempus, secundum illud Ecclesiastici vigesimo[^p69-5]: « Est tacens, non habens sensum loquelae, et est tacens, sciens tempus apti temporis. Homo sapiens tacebit usque ad tempus ». Pro tempore etiam ad nostram salutem *ostendit,* secundum illud Ecclesiastici quarto[^p69-6]: « Ne retineas verbum in tempore salutis ». Unde, quia non quaerebat gloriam suam, sed Dei, non suum commodum, sed nostram salutem; ideo coram *Deo et hominibus gratiam* inveniebat. Primum erat *iustitiae,* secundum *misericordiae;* propter quod Proverbiorum tertio[^p69-7]: « Misericordia et veritas non te deserant, et invenies gratiam et disciplinam bonam coram Deo et hominibus ».

111. Ex praedictis igitur colligere possumus, qualiter intendere debemus *cultui divinae Maiestatis, studio veritatis* et *obsequio pietatis.* Nam *cultus divinae Maiestatis* praeoccupari debet ab infantia, praeferri debet ceteris pro reverentia divina. *Studium veritatis* debet esse cum debita *societate,* scilicet peritorum, et debito *ordine,* ut prius sit discipulus quam magister. *Obsequium pietatis* attenditur in hoc, quod *subiiciamur* patri spirituali, sive *corripienti,* sive *praecipienti.* Et si haec servamus, tunc sequitur illud quod ultimo ponitur, quod *proficimus apud Deum et homines.*

## English

#### How Christ was made under the moral law.

91. *And the child grew* etc. Above[^p65-3] the Evangelist described how the Saviour was made under the ceremonial law; here he describes how he was made under the *moral law.* But since the whole ordering of morals is principally regarded in three things, namely in the *worship of the majesty* and the *study of truth* and the *office of piety;* by the first man is subjected to his *Creator,* by the second to his *teacher,* by the third to his *parent:* therefore this part has three, in the first of which it is shown how Christ was made under the moral law as to the *worship of the Majesty;* in the second, how he was made under the moral law as to the *study of truth,* there[^p65-4]: *And it came to pass, after three days;* in the third, how he was made under the moral law as to the *obedience of piety,* there: *And his Mother said to him* etc.

For a perfect description of *divine worship* two things are introduced: the first is the *excellence of his own virtue,* the second is the *reverence of the divine majesty,* there[^p65-5]: *And his parents went every year* etc. — Now the *excellence of virtue* in Christ is shown on the part of the *mind,* although there was a defect of age on the part of the *flesh;* for which reason he sets first the *advance of age* in the flesh and adds the *perfection of virtue* in the mind.

92. (Vers. 40.). Now as to the advance of *age in the flesh* it is said: *And the child grew,* namely in body, as Bede expounds it[^p65-6]: « Inasmuch as he was a *child,* that is, a frail man, he could grow, as other men, not inasmuch as he was the *Word* ». — Now as to the perfection of *virtue in the mind* it is added: *He was strengthened.* Now the perfection of mental virtue consists in three things, namely in the *vigour of power* with respect to the irascible, in the *splendour of wisdom* with respect to the rational, and the *fervour of grace* with respect to the concupiscible. — As to the *vigour of power* of the irascible, then, it is said: *And he was strengthened;* Ezechiel, the third chapter[^p65-7]: « The hand of the Lord was with me, strengthening me »; to the Philippians, the last chapter: « I can do all things in him who strengtheneth me ». — As to the *splendour of wisdom* in the rational it is added: *Full of wisdom;* to the Colossians, the second chapter[^p65-8]: « For in him are all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge hidden »; and Isaias, the eleventh chapter: « The spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and of understanding ». — But as to the *fervour of grace* in the concupiscible it is added: *And the grace of God was in him;* Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p65-9]: « In me is all grace of the way and of the truth, in me is all hope of life » etc.; and John, the first chapter: « And we saw his glory, the glory as it were of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth ».

93. (Vers. 41.). *And his parents went* etc. Here is described the *reverence of the divine majesty,* which consists in the worship of religion, and it is shown in Christ according to the *leading of his parents* and according to *his own instinct.* — As to the *leading of his parents* it is said: *And his parents went every year to Jerusalem.* For so it had been commanded in the Law; Exodus, the twenty-third chapter[^p65-10]: « Three times in the year you shall celebrate feasts to me ». And it is to be believed that they also led Jesus, because it is added in the same place: « Three times in the year shall all thy males appear before the Lord thy God »; and this in Jerusalem, according to the precept of Deuteronomy, the twelfth chapter[^p65-11]: « You shall come to the place which the Lord your God shall choose out of all your tribes ». And this place was Jerusalem, according to what is said in the second of Paralipomenon, the seventh chapter: « I have chosen and have sanctified this place, that my name may be there for ever ». And they performed this worship most of all at the pasch, and therefore it is added: *At the solemn day of the pasch;* Exodus, the twelfth chapter[^p65-12]: « You shall have this day for a memorial, and you shall keep it a solemn feast to the Lord in your generations with an everlasting worship ».

94. (Vers. 42.). But as to *his own instinct* the child Jesus was also free for[^tr-vacare] divine worship; as to which it is added: *And when he was twelve years old;* in which it is shown that the child
<!-- page 66 -->
Jesus was free for divine worship without the *company,* without the *counsel,* without the *good pleasure* of his parents. — Without their *company,* indeed; for although he went up in the company of his parents, yet he remained without them; for which reason it is said: *They going up into Jerusalem, according to the custom of the feast,* namely to pray, as below, in the eighteenth chapter[^p66-1]: « Two men went up into the temple to pray »; and Acts, the third chapter: « Peter and John went up into the temple » etc.

(Vers. 43.). *And having fulfilled the days, when they returned,* namely to their own, according to that of Judith, the sixteenth chapter[^p66-2]: The festivity being celebrated, « every one returned to his own ». And though all returned, yet not Jesus; whence it is also added: *The child Jesus remained in Jerusalem,* and so alone, without the company of his parents. — The figure of this went before in the child Samuel, of whom it is said in the first of Kings, the second chapter[^p66-3], that « Elcana went away », his father, and his mother went away « into his house; but the child ministered before the Lord ». Whence he had remained alone, that he might the better be free for prayer; Lamentations, the third chapter: « He shall sit solitary and hold his peace ». And so he remained by his own instinct without the company of his parents.

95. He remained also without their *counsel;* whence it is also added: *And his parents knew it not.* The figure of this went before in Samson, « who would not tell his father and mother that he had taken honey from the lion's mouth », Judges, the fourteenth chapter[^p66-4]. In which he also gave an example, that in doing divine service counsel is not to be sought from carnal parents; Deuteronomy, the thirty-third chapter: « Who hath said to his father and to his mother: I do not know you; and to his brethren: I know you not; these have kept thy word and observed thy covenant ».

96. (Vers. 44.). And lest you think that this ignorance came from negligence, for which Mary would be reprehensible, he adds the cause: *And thinking that he was in the company, they came a day's journey;* because, since the men went with the men, and the women with the women apart, it was probable to the father that the child was with the mother, and probable to the mother that he was with the father[^p66-5]. And therefore they were deceived. He was not of the race of those of whom it is said in Job, the twenty-first chapter: « Their seed continueth before them, a multitude of kinsmen and of children's children in their sight », but he was, like that one of whom it is said in the Psalm: « I looked on the right hand and beheld, and there was no one that would know me ». And so he remained without the counsel of his parents.

97. He remained also without their *good pleasure,* because with sorrow they sought him whom they had lost; whence it is added: *And they sought him among their kinsfolks and acquaintance;* and yet they did not find him, because such are put far from him; Job, the nineteenth chapter[^p66-6]: « My acquaintance like strangers have departed from me ».

98. (Vers. 45.). And therefore there follows: *And not finding* him, *they returned*[^p66-7] *into Jerusalem,* that him whom they could not find in the company of kindred they might find in the place of prayer, according to that of the Psalm: « Behold, we have heard of him in Ephrata »; and after: « We will go into his tabernacle ». *They returned,* I say, *seeking him;* according to that of the Canticle, the third chapter[^p66-8]: « I will rise and will go about the city; I will seek him whom my soul loveth »; so said the virgin Mary; and again the Canticle, the fifth chapter: « I sought him, and found him not; I called, and he did not answer me ». « I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, if you find my beloved, that you tell him that I languish with love ».

99. In this, moreover, we are admonished that from very infancy we should be bound over to divine worship; the first of Kings, the second chapter[^p66-9]: « Samuel ministered before the face of the Lord, being a child, girded with a linen ephod »; and most of all from the twelfth year, because then begins the time of discretion to be turned to the good. Whence it is said of Saint Martin[^p66-10] that « when he was twelve years old, he desired the desert »; the like is said of blessed Benedict. Therefore the Evangelist expresses this age. — We are admonished also that by no means should we leave off divine worship for love of our parents; whence Jerome[^p66-11]: « Tread over your father, tread over your mother, and fly to the standard of the cross. For it is the highest kind of piety to be cruel in these things »; for it is said in Matthew, the tenth chapter: « He that loveth father or mother more than me, is not worthy of me ».

100. *And it came to pass after three days* etc. Above the Evangelist showed how Christ the Lord was made under the moral law as to the worship of the divine majesty; here, secondly, he shows how he was made under the same as to the *study of truth;* in which first the Evangelist describes his *humiliation* in
<!-- page 67 -->
taking the *form of a disciple;* but secondly, his *glorification* in opening the *prerogative of wisdom,* there[^p67-1]: *And they were astonished* etc.

101. (Vers. 46.). Now the Evangelist shows that the Saviour took the form of a *disciple* when it is said: *And it came to pass, after three days, they found him*[^p67-2] *in the temple, sitting. Sitting* signifies rest and humility; for he knew that « it is better to sit in a corner of the housetop than with a brawling woman and in a common house », Proverbs, the twenty-first chapter[^p67-3]. *Sitting,* I say, *in the midst of the doctors;* Ecclesiasticus, the fifteenth chapter: « In the midst of the church she opened his mouth, and filled him with the spirit of wisdom and understanding ». For he knew it to be written: « He that walketh to the wise shall be wiser; a friend of fools shall become like to them », Proverbs, the thirteenth chapter. — And in this he teaches that the company of those who study is to be loved, not for disputing but for conferring; for which reason it is added: *Hearing them and asking them questions.* For this is the exercise of one who is learning, that he hear and ask about things doubtful; Ecclesiasticus, the thirty-second chapter[^p67-4]: « Hear in silence, and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee », because « a good ear will hear wisdom with all desire », Ecclesiasticus, the third chapter; and therefore it is said in John, the eighth chapter: « He that is of God heareth the words of God ». Because it is not enough to hear, therefore it is added: *And asking them questions;* Job, the eighth chapter[^p67-5]: « Ask the former generation, and search diligently into the memory of the fathers »; and Deuteronomy, the thirty-second chapter: « Ask thy father, and he will declare to thee » etc. Now he asked, not that he might be taught, but rather that he might teach. Jerome[^p67-6]: « The Saviour had completed twelve years, and asking the priests in the temple, he teaches rather, while he prudently asks ».

102. In this, moreover, it is to be noted *spiritually* that Christ teaches us that we must learn before we teach, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the eighteenth chapter[^p67-7]: « Before thou speak, learn ». And therefore he willed to be found *in the midst of the doctors* and *in the temple,* because truth is found in the reading of the Scriptures and in prayer. Whence note that Christ was found in the *manger;* above, in the same chapter[^p67-8]: « This is a sign unto you »; that is, in the *study of sacred Scripture,* because, according to what is said in Proverbs, the fourteenth chapter, « where there are no oxen, the crib is empty ». The *oxen* are preachers; the first to the Corinthians, the ninth chapter: « Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn » etc. — He was found in the *house;* Matthew, the second chapter[^p67-9]: « Entering into the house »; that is, in the *searching of a quiet conscience;* Wisdom, the eighth chapter: « When I go into my house, I shall repose myself with her ». — He was found also in the *temple,* as here, that is, in the *company of holy Church,* because « the Lord is in his holy temple », as is said in the Psalm[^p67-10]. — But he is not found in the *sepulchre of erroneous doctrine;* Luke, the last chapter: The women « came to the sepulchre and found not the body of Jesus »; nor yet in the *little bed of an unquiet conscience;* the Canticle, the third chapter: « In my little bed I sought him whom my soul loveth; I sought him, and found him not »; nor is he found in the *company of worldly life;* Job, the twenty-eighth chapter: « But where is wisdom found »? and after: « It is not found in the land of them that live in delights ».

103. (Vers. 47.). *And they were astonished* etc. Here, after the humiliation, he adds the *glorification;* when it is said: *And all that heard him were astonished at his prudence and his answers;* because he answered most prudently, beyond expectation. For *astonishment* comes from an unwonted and great imagining; below, in the fifth chapter[^p67-11]: « Astonishment seized them all »; Job, the twelfth chapter: « He bringeth counsellors to a foolish end, and judges into astonishment ». And so was fulfilled that of Wisdom, the eighth chapter: « For her I shall have glory among the multitudes, and honour with the ancients, though I be young; I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgement, and in the sight of the mighty I shall be wonderful, and the faces of princes shall wonder at me ». This was fulfilled in Christ now. — The *figure* of this went before in the third of Kings, the tenth chapter[^p67-12], where it is said that « the queen of Saba, seeing the wisdom of Solomon, had no longer any spirit », because of her exceeding wonder.

<!-- page 68 -->
104. (Vers. 48.). And not only were those *strangers* astonished, but also his *parents;* whence it is also added: *And seeing, they wondered;* Matthew, the seventh chapter[^p68-1]: « The multitudes wondered at his doctrine ». And truly it is a marvel that he answered what he had not learned; John, the seventh chapter: « The Jews wondered, saying: How doth this man know letters, having never learned »?

105. *And his Mother said* etc. Here, thirdly, he shows how he was made under the moral law as to the *office of piety;* in which *humiliation* is set first, and *glorification* is added, there[^p68-2]: *And his Mother kept all these words.*

Now *humiliation* is shown in two things, namely in *submitting to one who rebukes* and in *complying with one who commands.* For Christ *submitted* himself *to his mother when she rebuked;* for which reason it is said: *And his Mother said to him: Son, why hast thou done so to us?* She speaks the more confidently and asks the cause of his leaving them, since she knows that she ought to be honoured by him, according to that of Exodus, the twentieth chapter[^p68-3]: « Honour thy father and thy mother »; and Proverbs, the sixth chapter: « My son, keep the precepts of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother ». But you, she says, have troubled us; whence she also adds: *Thy father,* namely the reputed or adoptive or fostering father; *and I have sought thee sorrowing,* as if we were saying together that of Tobias, the tenth chapter[^p68-4]: « Alas, my son, why did we send thee to go abroad, the light of our eyes, the staff of our old age, the comfort of our life! For having all things in thee alone, we ought not to have let thee go ».

106. (Vers. 49.). And note that he answers the question humbly and wisely; whence it is also added: *And he said to them: How is it that you sought me?* as one absent, when I was present to you; Jeremias, the twenty-third chapter[^p68-5]: « I fill heaven and earth »; and this he shows, because he was the Son of the eternal Father. Whence he also adds: *Did you not know that I must be about my Father's business?* John, the sixth chapter: « I came down from heaven, not to do my own will, but the will of him that sent me ». Whence you ought not to wonder if I left you for the sake of the eternal Father, because thus it is said in Acts, the fifth chapter: « We ought to obey God rather than men ».

107. (Vers. 50.). And because the word was hidden, it is added: *And they understood not*[^p68-6]; the first to the Corinthians, the second chapter: « We speak wisdom in a mystery, hidden »; and Tobias, the twelfth chapter: « It is good to hide the secret of a king ». — But in this, that Christ, when he was rebuked, humbly excused himself; and when he was invited by his Mother to work a miracle, answered harshly; he gave us an example and form of humility, that we should rather be rebuked than praised; Ecclesiastes, the seventh chapter[^p68-7]: « It is better to be rebuked by a wise man than to be deceived by the flattery of fools ». At the same time also he hinted by this that piety toward God is to be preferred to piety with respect to parents.

108. (Vers. 51.). The Lord *submitted* also *to one who commands;* for which reason it is added: *And he went down with them and came to Nazareth.* Now was fulfilled that of Baruch, the third chapter[^p68-8]: « Afterwards he was seen upon earth and conversed with men ». *He went down,* I say, which is a sign of humiliation; the Canticle, the sixth chapter: « I went down into my garden, to see the fruits of the valleys », that is, the humble. « For the Lord is high, and looketh on the low things », namely from near at hand, « and the high he knoweth afar off ». For which reason it is also added: *And was subject to them.* Bernard[^p68-9]: « Who? to whom? God to men. God, I say, subjects himself to men; and thou, eager to lord it, settest thyself before thy author? For as often as I desire to be over men, so often I strive to go before my God, so that the Lord might answer me some such thing: *Go behind me, Satan, because thou savourest not the things that are of God* ». Whence he himself could truly say that of the first to the Corinthians, the ninth chapter[^p68-10]: « Whereas I was free as to all, I made myself the servant of all ». And we for love of him ought to be subject to all; the first of Peter, the second chapter: « Be ye subject to every human creature for God's sake ».

109. *And his Mother* etc. Here is described his *glorification,* and this through two things, namely through the *testimony of his Mother* and through the *evidence of his own virtue.* As to the *testimony of his Mother* it is said: *And his Mother kept all these words,* that she might bear testimony in place and time, as Bede says in the Gloss[^p68-11]. So it was said in Daniel, the twelfth chapter: « But thou, Daniel, shut up the book and seal the words, even
<!-- page 69 -->
to the time appointed ». And because she understood these things, therefore it is said: *Conferring*[^p69-1] *in her heart,* according to that of Ecclesiastes, the eighth chapter: « The heart of a wise man understandeth time and answer »; and in Mark, the ninth chapter, it is said of the transfiguration of the Lord that « the disciples kept the word to themselves ». For the Apostles had not yet come, to whom « it had been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God », as is said in Matthew, the thirteenth chapter[^p69-2], who would also confer with one another, according to that of the Galatians, the second chapter: « I conferred with them the gospel which I preach among the Gentiles ».

110. (Vers. 52.). But as to the *evidence of his own virtue* it is added: *And Jesus advanced in age,* namely on the part of the body, as Samuel; the first of Kings, the second chapter[^p69-3]: « The child Samuel advanced and grew, and pleased both God and men ». And this advance was according to *essence,* but the interior advance was according to *evidence,* of which it is said: *And in wisdom,* namely in the intellect, *and in grace,* in the affection, *with God,* namely to be honoured, *and men,* to be edified. Nor is it contrary to what is said just above[^p69-4], that he was « full of wisdom »; whence Gregory: « He advanced in wisdom, not by receiving with the passing of time what he did not have, but by displaying to others the gifts of grace and wisdom which he had »; and this for the edification of others; the second to the Corinthians, the eighth chapter: « For we forecast what may be good not only before God, but also before men ». Whence for our edification he *hides* his wisdom for a time, to show that we ought to be silent until the time, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the twentieth chapter[^p69-5]: « There is one that is silent, not having the sense of speech, and there is one that is silent, knowing the time of the fit time. A wise man will be silent until the time ». At the due time also he *shows* it for our salvation, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the fourth chapter[^p69-6]: « Keep not back a word in the time of salvation ». Whence, because he sought not his own glory but God's, not his own advantage but our salvation, therefore he found *grace before God and men.* The first was of *justice,* the second of *mercy;* for which reason Proverbs, the third chapter[^p69-7]: « Let not mercy and truth leave thee, and thou shalt find grace and good discipline before God and men ».

111. From what has been said, then, we can gather how we ought to apply ourselves to the *worship of the divine Majesty, the study of truth* and the *obedience of piety.* For the *worship of the divine Majesty* ought to be taken up beforehand from infancy, ought to be preferred to all else for the divine reverence. The *study of truth* ought to be with due *fellowship,* namely of the learned, and in due *order,* so that one be a disciple before a master. The *obedience of piety* is regarded in this, that we be *subject* to our spiritual father, whether *rebuking* or *commanding.* And if we keep these things, then follows that which is put last, that *we advance with God and men.*
