# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XVII, Vers. 20-30
## *De reprehensione Iudaicae curiositatis tria*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 437 -->

#### Curiositas Iudaeorum confutatur dupliciter.

35. *Interrogatus autem a Pharisaeis* etc. Terminatis duabus partibus principalibus, in quibus confutata est Iudaeorum dolositas et impietas, et per oppositum simplicitas et pietas commendata; hic sequitur pars tertia, in qua *confutatur curiositas*[^p437-5]. Et quoniam curiositas non tantum consistit in perscrutatione scientiae, verum etiam in possessione pecuniae, secundum beatum Augustinum[^p437-6]; ideo ista pars dividitur in duas: in quarum prima confutat *curiositatem in perscrutando;* in secunda vero, *curiositatem in possidendo,* infra decimo octavo[^p437-7]: *Interrogavit eum quidam princeps.*

#### Primo curiositas in perscrutando confutatur per tria.

Prima pars dividitur in tres: in quarum prima introducitur *reprehensio Iudaicae curiositatis;* in secunda, *assignatio rationis,* ibi[^p437-8]: *Nam sicut fulgur coruscans* etc.; in tertia, *persuasio studiositatis,* ibi: *Dicebat autem et parabolam ad illos* etc.

#### De reprehensione Iudaicae curiositatis tria.

In *reprehensione* autem *curiositatis Pharisaeorum* tria introducuntur, scilicet *improbatio curiositatis Pharisaeorum* interrogantium, *approbatio caritatis discipulorum* desiderantium et *reprobatio contrarietatis haereticorum* decipientium.

36. (Vers. 20.). Primo igitur quantum ad *improbationem curiositatis Pharisaeorum interrogantium* dicit: *Interrogatus autem a Pharisaeis: Quando venit regnum Dei?* per curiosam scilicet inquisitionem. Haec interrogatio erat curiosa, quia de re secreta, quam Deus in suo secreto occultat, secundum illud Actuum primo[^p437-9]: « Non est vestrum nosse tempora vel momenta, quae Pater posuit in sua potestate »; et ideo Ecclesiastici tertio: « Altiora te ne quaesieris et fortiora te ne scrutatus fueris; sed quae praecepit tibi Deus, haec cogita semper, et in pluribus operibus eius ne fueris curiosus »; et Ecclesiastae septimo: « Quid necesse est homini maiora se quaerere, cum ignoret, quid conducat sibi in vita sua numero dierum peregrinationis suae et tempore, quod velut umbra praeterit? Aut quis poterit ei indicare, quid post eum futurum sit »?

37. (Vers. 20. 21.). Et ideo subdit: *Respondens eis dixit*[^p437-10]: *Non venit regnum Dei cum observatione; neque dicent: Ecce hic, aut ecce illic,* per humanam comprehensionem. Humana enim comprehensio non se extendit ad cognoscendum *iudicii*
<!-- page 438 -->
*tempus;* unde dicit: *Non venit cum observatione;* Glossa[^p438-1]: « Temporis »; Matthaei vigesimo quarto: « De die autem illa et hora nemo scit, neque Angeli caelorum, nisi solus Pater »; et primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto: « Dies Domini, sicut fur in nocte, ita veniet ». Non extendit se etiam ad sciendum *locum;* propter quod dicit: *Nec dicent: Ecce, hic* etc.; Glossa[^p438-2]: « Nec scietur determinatus locus ». — Sed contrarium videtur dici Ioelis tertio: « Congregabo omnes gentes et disceptabo cum eis in valle Iosaphat ». Sed illud intelligendum est, quod *Iosaphat* accipitur pro interpretatione; interpretatur enim *vallis iudicii.* Iudicium enim non erit, Domino existente in terra, sed potius in aëre, sicut dicit Hieronymus super hunc locum[^p438-3]. — Et quoniam curiosa inquisitio non excluditur nisi per studiosam, ideo subdit: *Ecce enim, regnum Dei intra vos est,* per divinae gratiae oblationem, quae quidem facta fuit in adventu Christi; de quo Ioannis primo[^p438-4]: « Medius vestrum stetit, quem vos nescitis ». Unde Beda: « *Regnum Dei* se ipsum dicit intra ipsos positum, id est in cordibus eorum qui crediderunt, regnantem ». Propter quod dicitur supra undecimo[^p438-5]: « Si in digito Dei eiicio daemonia, profecto pervenit in vos regnum Dei », illud scilicet, de quo dicitur ad Romanos decimo quarto: « Regnum Dei non est esca et potus » etc. Vult igitur dicere, quod adventum primum debent considerare et secundi adventus tempus desinant indagare; unde Beda[^p438-6]: « Quando veniat, neque ab hominibus neque ab Angelis potest observari, sicut dominicae incarnationis tempus certissimis et Prophetarum vaticiniis praefatum et Angelorum praeconiis est manifestatum ».

38. (Vers. 22.). Secundo quantum ad *approbationem caritatis discipulorum desiderantium* subdit: *Et ait ad discipulos suos: Venient dies, quando desideretis videre unum diem Filii hominis,* scilicet dies tribulationis, in quibus augetur desiderium aeternitatis, secundum illud Psalmi[^p438-7]: « In die tribulationis meae Deum exquisivi, manibus meis » etc. Et illa erit dies tribulationis asperrimae, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo quarto: « Erit tribulatio magna, qualis non fuit ab initio mundi usque modo ».

39. Et nota, quod pluraliter dicit *dies* propter pluralitatem tribulationum, quas Ecclesia post Christi ascensionem erat passura. Sunt autem quatuor principales: prima, *tyrannorum;* secunda, *haereticorum;* tertia, *falsorum Christianorum;* et quarta erit conflata *ex omnibus,* quae erit antichristi et suorum complicum. Et hae designatae sunt in quatuor bestiis, quas vidit Daniel, Danielis septimo[^p438-8]. Prima erat similis leoni, secunda urso, tertia pardo, quarta non describitur, quia in ea erit omnium perversitatum concursus. Et haec tribulatio parit desiderium diei gloriae, de qua Zachariae ultimo[^p438-9]: « Erit dies una, quae nota est Domino exercituum ». Et haec est *desiderabilis,* secundum illud Psalmi: « Melior est dies una in atriis tuis super millia ». Et hanc desiderant viri *iusti,* sicut Paulus; ad Philippenses primo: « Desiderium habens dissolvi et esse cum Christo, multo magis melius ». Desiderant etiam *afflicti,* sicut discipuli; unde Beda[^p438-10]: « Post ascensionem meam vos, in hoc exilio mundi ingemiscentes, desiderabitis me videre in gloria mea. Quae voluntas laudanda est, non tamen in hoc mundo implenda ». — Et ideo sequitur: *Et non videbitis.* Implebitur tamen hoc aliquando, quia, Proverbiorum decimo[^p438-11], « desiderium suum iustis dabitur ». Excitatur autem hoc desiderium ex tribulatione, tum quia mundus iste magis despicitur, tum quia spes aeternorum fortior concipitur, tum quia ignis amoris vehementius inflammatur. Propter quod secundae ad Corinthios quinto[^p438-12]: « Scimus, quia, si terrestris domus nostra huius habitationis dissolvatur, quod aedificationem ex Deo habemus, domum non manufactam, sed aeternam in caelis. Nam et in hoc ingemiscimus, habitationem nostram, quae de caelo est, superindui cupientes » etc.

40. (Vers. 23.). Tertio quantum ad *reprobationem contrarietatis haereticorum decipientium* subiungit: *Et dicent vobis: Ecce hic, et ecce illic;* Glossa[^p438-13]: « In hac persona, vel in hoc tempore est Christus ».
<!-- page 439 -->
Hoc enim haeretici aliquando ausi sunt et audebunt dicere, et maxime ille filius perditionis. Unde Beda: « Multi haeretici fuerunt et futuri sunt, qui se Christos asserunt, quorum primus Simon magus, et ultimus antichristus ». Unde etiam ille qui fuit dux sectae Manichaeorum, dixit, se esse Spiritum sanctum, sicut dicit Augustinus[^p439-1]. Unde quia haeretici discordant; unus dicit unum, alter sentit contrarium; ideo dicit, quod unus dicit: *ecce hic,* alter: *ecce illic.* Unde Matthaei vigesimo quarto[^p439-2]: « Multi venient in nomine meo, dicentes: Ego sum Christus, et multos seducent ». Seducuntur autem qui suscipiunt eorum doctrinam et subsequuntur vitam. — Ideo addit: *Nolite ire,* cum illis; Glossa[^p439-3]: « Ut credatis quae dicunt »; Isaiae vigesimo octavo: « Qui crediderit non festinet »; *nec sectemini;* Glossa[^p439-4]: « Vitam eorum »; Proverbiorum primo: « Fili mi, si te lactaverint peccatores, ne acquiescas eis ». Ideo autem tam diligenter monet, quia difficile erit illorum pseudoprophetarum et praedicatorum vitare seductiones; Matthaei vigesimo quarto[^p439-5]: « Surgent pseudochristi et pseudoprophetae et dabunt signa magna et prodigia, ita ut in errorem mittantur, si fieri potest, etiam electi ». Unde Chrysostomus[^p439-6]: « Homo circa mortem phantasias videt; sic et mundus in exitu suo multos patietur errores ».

#### De ratione huius reprehensionis tria.

41. *Nam sicut fulgur* etc. Post reprehensionem Iudaicae curiositatis subditur hic *ratio reprehensionis*[^p439-7], quae sumitur ex modo veniendi ipsius Filii Dei, qui non poterit latere, cum venerit, nec ante praevideri. Dividitur autem haec pars in tres: in quarum prima agit de modo *apparendi;* in secunda, de modo *veniendi,* ibi[^p439-8]: *Et sicut factum est in diebus Noe;* in tertia, de modo *evadendi,* ibi: *In illa die* etc.

Circa modum *apparendi* duo introducuntur, scilicet Christi *apparentis manifestatio* et *apparitionis ordo.*

42. (Vers. 24.). Primo ergo quantum ad *apparitionis manifestationem* dicit: *Nam sicut fulgur coruscans de sub caelo in ea quae sub caelo sunt, fulget,* scilicet velociter et aperte. Chrysostomus[^p439-9]: « Coruscatio non indiget praecone, sed in momento in thalamis et in domibus et per universum orbem manifestatur ». — *Ita erit Filius hominis in die sua;* Glossa[^p439-10]: « Subitus et coruscus, ut nullus in sua mente latere permittatur, quin iudicis fulgore penetretur ». Et nota, quod apparitio Domini in iudicio comparatur *coruscationi fulguris,* quia *manifesta;* Psalmus[^p439-11]: « Deus manifeste veniet, Deus noster, et non silebit »; quia *terribilis;* Ezechielis primo: « Haec erat visio discurrens, splendor ignis, et de igne fulgur egrediens »; quia *repentina;* Isaiae vigesimo nono: « Eritque repente; a Domino exercituum visitabitur in tonitruo et commotione terrae et voce magna turbinis et tempestatis et flammae ignis devorantis ». — Sicut igitur stultum est de nocte sollicitari de apparitione solis, quando apparebit, cum eius ortus nullo modo possit latere, et magis stultum est de apparitione fulguris; sic et de adventu diei illius, inquirendo certum momentum temporis, cum illud omnibus manifeste innotescat, secundum illud Apocalypsis primo[^p439-12]: « Ecce, venit cum nubibus, et videbit eum omnis oculus » etc. Et ex hoc manifeste possumus discernere Christum ab antichristo, quia antichristus in momento temporis non poterit se cunctis reddere manifestum.

43. (Vers. 25.). Secundo quantum ad *apparitionis ordinem* subdit: *Primum autem oportet, illum multa pati et reprobari a generatione hac.* Hoc autem intelligitur de Christo capite et Iudaeorum perfida generatione, a qua multa perpessus est, de qua Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo[^p439-13]: « Generatio perversa est, et infideles filii »; et Proverbiorum trigesimo: « Generatio, quae patri suo maledicit, et quae matri suae non benedicit ». Ab hac generatione Christus multa passus est opprobria, convitia, verbera et tormenta, secundum illud Isaiae quinquagesimo tertio[^p439-14]: « Nos reputavimus eum quasi leprosum et percussum a Deo et humiliatum. Ipse autem vulneratus est propter iniquitates nostras, attritus est propter scelera nostra ».

44. Haec autem passio incepit a capite et descendit in omnia Christi membra; qua consummata, tunc finis erit et tempus adventus secundi. Unde Glossa[^p439-15]: « *Oportet, reprobari a generatione hac,* non tantum
<!-- page 440 -->
Iudaeorum, sed omnium reproborum, a quibus quotidie patitur Christus in suo corpore »; propter quod et Paulus ad Colossenses primo: « Adimplebo ea quae desunt passionum Christi, in carne mea pro corpore eius, quod est Ecclesia ». — Hac autem passione in corpore Christi consummata, non restat nisi glorificatio et retributio plena; propter quod Apocalypsis sexto[^p440-1] dicitur Sanctis desiderantibus secundam stolam: « Adhuc sustinete modicum tempus, donec impleatur numerus fratrum vestrorum »; et Apocalypsis septimo: « Nolite nocere terrae et mari neque arboribus, quoadusque signemus servos Dei nostri in frontibus suis ». Hoc est signum Dei vivi, quod est signum crucis, quo omnes electos oportet consignari; Ezechielis nono[^p440-2]: « Signa thau supra frontes virorum gementium et dolentium »; et post: « Transite, inquit, per medium civitatis et occidite ». « Omnem autem, super quem videritis thau, ne occidatis ». — Et nota, quod passio iustorum praecedit reprobationem reproborum, quia excitatur iustitia ad vindictam propter summae iniquitatis perfidiam; Isaiae quinquagesimo nono[^p440-3]: « Conversum est retrorsum iudicium, et iustitia longe stetit, quia corruit in platea veritas, et aequitas non potuit ingredi. Et facta est veritas in oblivionem, et qui recessit a malo praedae patuit; et vidit Dominus, et malum apparuit in oculis eius, quia non est iudicium ». « Et indutus est iustitia ut lorica, et galea salutis in capite eius; et indutus est vestimentis ultionis et opertus est quasi pallio zeli, sicut ad vindictam » etc.

45. (Vers. 26.). *Et sicut factum est in diebus* etc. Postquam descripsit modum apparendi, hic secundo[^p440-4] describit *modum veniendi.* Describitur autem repentinus esse Christi adventus respectu peccatorum per duplex simile et exemplum. Primo quidem ostenditur per simile ex parte *diluvii inundantis;* secundo vero, ex parte *incendii consumentis,* ibi[^p440-5]: *Similiter, sicut factum est in diebus Lot.* — Circa similitudinem *diluvii inundantis* tria introducuntur, scilicet ipsius diluvii *figura, causa* et *poena.*

Primo igitur quantum ad ipsius *diluvii figuram* dicit: *Et sicut factum est in diebus Noe, ita erit et in diebus Filii hominis.* Hoc enim congruum erat, quia *Noe* figuram gerebat Christi, qui se vocat Filium hominis: *Noe* enim interpretatur requies, et in solo Christo est requies et pax. Nam, ad Ephesios secundo[^p440-6], « ipse est pax nostra, qui fecit utraque unum »; et Ioannis decimo sexto: « In mundo pressuram habebitis, in me autem pacem ». Unde Augustinus[^p440-7]: « Inquietum est cor meum, Domine, donec quiescat in te ». Ideo ipse dicit: « Tollite iugum meum super vos, et invenietis requiem animabus vestris ».

46. Non tantum *nomine* Christum significat, verum etiam *facto.* Nam sicut Noe salvavit semen hominum per lignum, sic Christus et per crucis patibulum; Sapientiae decimo quarto[^p440-8]: « Spes orbis terrarum, ad ratem confugiens, remisit saeculo semen nativitatis, quae manu tua erat gubernata. Benedictum est enim lignum, per quod fit iustitia ». Unde et praemittit: « Exiguo ligno committunt homines animas suas » etc. Et sicut Noe fabricavit arcam, sic et Christus Ecclesiam, quae ad modum arcae de lignis levigatis bitumine caritatis contextae aedificatur, habens coenacula et tristega propter diversitates officiorum, graduum et dignitatum; ad Ephesios quarto[^p440-9]: « Ipse dedit quosdam Apostolos, quosdam quidem Prophetas, alios vero Evangelistas, alios vero pastores et doctores, ad consummationem Sanctorum, ad aedificationem corporis Christi, donec occurramus omnes in virum perfectum, in mensuram aetatis plenitudinis Christi »; in qua scilicet erit longitudo, latitudo et altitudo arcae nobilissime consummata[^p440-10]. Sicut etiam qui intra arcam fuerunt salvati sunt, sic qui intra Ecclesiam sunt salvantur per aquam et baptismum; primae Petri tertio[^p440-11]: « In diebus Noe, cum fabricaretur arca, in qua pauci, id est octo animae salvae factae sunt per aquam. Quod et vos nunc similis formae salvos facit baptisma, non carnis depositio sordium, sed conscientiae bonae interrogatio in Deum, per resurrectionem Domini nostri Iesu Christi ». Et sicut qui extra arcam fuerunt deleti sunt, sic omnes, qui extra Ecclesiam sunt, per finale iudicium damnabuntur; in cuius designationem Actuum vigesimo septimo[^p440-12] « dixit Paulus: Nisi hi in navi manserint, vos salvi fieri non potestis ».
<!-- page 441 -->

47. (Vers. 27.). Secundo quantum ad *diluvii causam* subdit: *Edebant et bibebant, uxores ducebant et dabantur ad nuptias usque in diem, qua intravit Noe in arcam,* hoc est, operibus carnis totaliter intenti erant, et ideo in carne corruptibili corrumpi et deleri digni erant. Unde Glossa Ambrosii[^p441-1]: « Non connubia vel alimenta damnantur, cum in his successionis, in illis naturae sint posita subsidia, sed immoderatus licitorum usus arguitur. Quia enim his se totos dedendo Dei iudicia contemnebant, igne vel aqua perierunt ». Unde in his actibus insinuatur eorum carnalitas, propter quod digni erant ira divina; Genesis sexto[^p441-2]: « Cumque vidisset Deus, terram esse corruptam; omnis quippe caro corruperat viam suam super terram; dixit ad Noe: Finis universae carnis venit coram me; repleta est terra iniquitate, et ego disperdam eos cum terra ». Principalis autem culpa eorum venit ex carnalitate, et hoc insinuat in quatuor actibus, quos tangit: *edebant,* id est, esui erant dediti ut *gulosi,* et ideo ira digni; Psalmus[^p441-3]: « Adhuc escae eorum erant in ore ipsorum, et ira Dei ascendit super eos ». *Bibebant,* ut *ebriosi,* et ideo ira digni, secundum illud Proverbiorum vigesimo tertio[^p441-4]: « Cui vae? cuius patri vae »? « Nonne his qui commorantur in vino » etc.; Isaiae quinto: « Vae! qui potentes estis ad bibendum vinum et viri fortes ad miscendam ebrietatem »; et vigesimo secundo: « Ecce, gaudium et laetitia, comedere carnes et bibere vinum »; « et revelata est vox ista in auribus meis: Si dimitteretur iniquitas haec vobis, donec moriamini, dicit Dominus exercituum ». *Uxores ducebant,* ut *lascivi;* Genesis sexto[^p441-5]: « Cumque coepissent homines multiplicari super terram et filios procreassent, videntes filii Dei filias hominum, quod essent pulcrae; acceperunt sibi uxores ex omnibus, quas elegerant ». Et sequitur: « Dixit Dominus: Non permanebit spiritus meus in homine, quia caro est ». Et *dabantur ad nuptias,* ut *securi,* et ideo ad interitum dispositi, secundum illud primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto[^p441-6]: « Cum dixerint: Pax et securitas; tunc repentinus eis superveniet interitus ». Unde qui his vitiis est absorptus, necesse est, quod a diluvio involvatur; propter quod primae ad Thessalonicenses quinto: « Vos, fratres, non estis in tenebris, ut vos dies illa tanquam fur comprehendat ». « Igitur non dormiamus sicut et ceteri, sed vigilemus et sobrii simus ». Modus autem describitur ad Romanos decimo tertio[^p441-7]: « Nox praecessit, dies autem appropinquavit. Abiiciamus ergo » etc.; et post: « Non in comessationibus et ebrietatibus, non in cubilibus et impudicitiis ». « Sed induimini Dominum Iesum Christum ».

48. Tertio quantum ad *diluvii poenam* subdit: *Et venit diluvium et perdidit omnes,* scilicet qui erant extra arcam, statim post ingressum Noe, secundum illud Genesis septimo[^p441-8]: « In articulo diei illius ingressus est Noe in arcam ». « Factumque est diluvium quadraginta diebus et quadraginta noctibus », « consumtaque est omnis caro, quae movebatur super terram ». Unde et secundae Petri secundo: « Originali mundo non pepercit, sed octavum Noe iustitiae praeconem custodivit, diluvium mundo impiorum inducens ». Ex quo intelligitur, quod poena futuri iudicii super eos veniet, qui sunt extra Ecclesiam Christi, salvatis nihilominus raris iustis; quia Glossa[^p441-9]: « Noe arcam aedificat, cum Dominus fideles in Ecclesia congregat; quam consummatam ingreditur, cum hanc in die iudicii praesentia sua illustrat; sed dum arca aedificatur, iniqui luxuriantur; et dum intratur, aeterna damnatione plectuntur ». Dicitur autem hoc fieri *in articulo diei,* quia in exordio octavae resurrectionis; unde Psalmus[^p441-10]: « Mane astabo tibi et videbo, quoniam non Deus volens iniquitatem tu es; neque habitabit iuxta te malignus, neque permanebunt iniusti ante oculos tuos »; et secundae Petri secundo: « Novit Dominus pios de tentatione eripere, iniquos autem in diem iudicii reservare cruciandos ». — Ex hoc exemplo figurative[^p441-11] apparet, tam ex significatione allegorica quam ex similitudine expressa, quod dies iudicii super homines carnales et animales veniet *subito* et *ad ipsorum perditionem* universalem, qui sunt extra Ecclesiae unitatem; his autem, qui in arca sunt, etsi *subito* veniet, *non nocet,* quia eos invenit paratos. Unde infra vigesimo primo[^p441-12]: « Attendite vobis, ne forte graventur corda vestra crapula et ebrietate et curis huius vitae, et superveniat in vos repentina dies illa. Tanquam laqueus enim superveniet in omnes, qui sedent super faciem omnis terrae » etc.

49. (Vers. 28.). *Similiter, sicut factum est* etc. Post similitudinem sumtam ex parte diluvii inundantis introducit similitudinem ex parte *incendii consumentis*[^p441-13]; circa quam similitudinem introducuntur
<!-- page 442 -->
tria, scilicet ipsorum Sodomitarum *demeritum, supplicium* et *exemplum.*

Primum quantum ad *demeritum* dicit: *Similiter, sicut factum est in diebus Lot,* scilicet viri iusti. Et nota, quod in descriptione iniquitatis populi impudici praemittitur iustitia viri iusti, quia ex hoc magis apparet eorum impietas, quod vir iustus inter eos erat; propter quod secundae Petri secundo[^p442-1]: « Iustum Lot, oppressum a nefandorum iniuria atque luxuria eripuit. Aspectu enim et auditu iustus erat, habitans inter eos qui de die in diem animam iustam iniquis operibus cruciabant ». Unde propter iustitiam viri iusti aggravatur in impiis enormitas peccati. — Quantum ad quam subdit: *Edebant et bibebant,* scilicet Sodomitae, ut *lascivi,* sicut illi, de quibus Exodi trigesimo secundo[^p442-2]: « Sedit populus manducare et bibere, et surrexerunt ludere »; et non addit, quod uxores ducebant, sicut prius[^p442-3], quia non peccabant secundum naturam, sed contra naturam; Genesis decimo tertio: « Homines Sodomitae pessimi erant et peccatores nimis coram Domino »; et Genesis decimo nono dicitur, quod « vallaverunt domum Lot a puero usque ad senem, omnis populus simul, vocaveruntque Lot et dixerunt ei: Ubi sunt viri, qui ingressi sunt ad te? Adduc eos huc, ut cognoscamus eos ». — *Emebant et vendebant,* ut *avari;* Ecclesiastici vigesimo septimo[^p442-4]: « Qui quaerit locupletari avertit oculum suum. Sicut in medio compaginis lapidum palus figitur, sic inter medium emptionis et venditionis angustiabitur peccator ». Propter quod contra hos dicitur primae ad Corinthios septimo[^p442-5]: « Qui emunt, tanquam non possidentes, et qui utuntur hoc mundo, tanquam non utantur; praeterit enim figura huius mundi ». — *Plantabant et aedificabant,* sicut *securi,* qui deberent in domibus habitare et fructum arborum comedere, sicut ille dives supra duodecimo[^p442-6]: « Anima mea, habes multa bona reposita in annos plurimos ». « Sed Dominus dixit ei: Stulte, hac nocte » etc. Et ideo Amos quinto: « Domos aedificabitis lapide quadro, et non habitabitis in eis; vineas amantissimas plantabitis, et non bibetis vinum earum, quia cognovi multa scelera vestra ». Ideo Isaiae quinto[^p442-7]: « Vae! qui coniungitis domum ad domum, et agrum agro copulatis usque ad terminum loci ». Quia igitur Sodomitae et *lascivi* erant et *avari* et *securi,* ideo erant ira divina digni; Ezechielis decimo sexto: « Haec fuit iniquitas Sodomae, sororis tuae: superbia, saturitas panis et abundantia et otium ipsius et filiarum eius, et manum egeno et pauperi non porrigebant ».

50. (Vers. 29.). Secundum quantum ad *supplicium* subdit: *Qua die autem exiit Lot a Sodomis,* per aequitatem divinae iustitiae liberantis; Genesis decimo nono[^p442-8]: « Apprehenderunt Angeli manum Lot et manum uxoris eius et manum duarum filiarum eius, eo quod parceret Dominus eis. Deduxerunt eum et posuerunt extra civitatem ibique locuti sunt ad eum, dicentes: Salva animam tuam » etc.; et infra eodem: « Liberavit Dominus Lot a subversione urbium, in quibus habitaverat ». In quo Lot gerit typum iustorum, qui egrediuntur de medio malorum; unde Glossa[^p442-9]: « Lot, qui interpretatur *declinans,* est populus electorum, qui, dum in Sodomis, id est inter reprobos, ut advena moratur, quantum valet, scelera eorum declinat »; et hoc secundum illud secundae ad Corinthios sexto[^p442-10]: « Exite de medio eorum et separamini, dicit Dominus, et immundum ne tetigeritis, et ego recipiam vos et ero vobis in patrem, et vos eritis mihi in filios et filias ». Liberavit igitur divina iustitia Lot iustum, et e contrario perdidit populum impium. — Et ideo addit: *Pluit ignem et sulphur de caelo et omnes perdidit,* scilicet Dominus, per severitatem iustitiae ulciscentis; Genesis decimo nono[^p442-11]: « Igitur pluit Dominus super Sodomam et Gomorrham sulphur et ignem de caelo a Domino et subvertit civitates has et omnem circa regionem, universos habitatores urbium et cuncta terrae virentia ». — Et nota, quod signanter dicit, quod *pluit Dominus a Domino,* quia, licet omne iudicium debeatur Domino Filio, secundum illud Ioannis quinto[^p442-12]: « Pater non iudicat quemquam, sed omne iudicium dedit Filio », « quia Filius hominis est »; tamen huius peccati iudicium specialiter sibi debetur, quia directe est contra Verbum incarnatum et naturam, quam Filius Dei fecit caelestem, angelicam et divinam, facit viliorem, quam sit natura brutalis, subvertendo naturae leges, quas bruta animalia servant. Et ideo non tantum *igne,* sed etiam *sulphure* punitur hoc vitium propter
<!-- page 443 -->
inordinatissimae libidinis incendium et foetorem. De *incendio,* ad Romanos primo[^p443-1]: « Exarserunt in concupiscentiis suis in invicem, masculi in masculos turpitudinem operantes »; de *foetore,* Isaiae trigesimo quarto: « De cadaveribus eorum ascendet foetor, tabescent montes a sanguine eorum » etc.; et Psalmus: « Corrupti sunt et abominabiles facti sunt » etc. Propter haec merito sunt igne et sulphure concremati; unde Bernardus[^p443-2]: « Flamma gehennalis, moram non sustinens, exsecrabilem istam praevenit tollere nationem nec non etiam tellurem ipsam, utpote consciam tantae confusionis, absumsit ignis et spiritus procellarum ».

51. (Vers. 30.). Tertio quantum ad *exemplum* subdit: *Secundum haec*[^p443-3] *erit, qua die Filius hominis revelabitur.* Haec est dies iudicii, in qua fiet *revelatio* ipsius Christi ut *Salvatoris* respectu bonorum; primae Petri primo: « Custodimini per fidem, in salutem paratam revelari tempore novissimo ». Fiet etiam revelatio Christi *iudicis* respectu malorum; primae ad Corinthios quarto[^p443-4]: « Nolite ante tempus iudicare, quoadusque veniat Dominus, qui et illuminabit abscondita tenebrarum et manifestabit consilia cordium ». De utroque Isaiae quinquagesimo sexto: « Iuxta est salus mea, ut veniat, et iustitia mea, ut reveletur ». Haec dies revelationis similis est incendio Sodomorum, quia in illo ipsius praecessit exemplum; in Canonica Iudae[^p443-5]: « Sicut Sodoma et Gomorrha et finitimae civitates simili modo exfornicatae et abeuntes post carnem alteram, factae sunt exemplum, ignis aeterni poenam sustinentes »; et similiter secundae Petri secundo: « Civitates Sodomorum et Gomorrhaeorum, in cinerem redigens, eversione damnavit, exemplum eorum qui impie acturi sunt, ponens ».

52. Et ideo dicit: *Secundum haec,* ut simile sit *exemplum* quantum ad *demeritum,* quantum ad *iudicandi modum* et quantum ad *supplicium.* Quantum ad *demeritum,* quia, sicut in Sodomis multi erant mali et paucissimi boni, sic tempore finalis iudicii, secundum illud infra decimo octavo[^p443-6]: « Putasne, cum venerit Filius hominis, inveniet fidem super terram »? Unde Glossa: « Qui interim non apparens omnia videt, tunc apparens omnia iudicabit, quando cunctos iudiciorum suorum oblitos huic saeculo conspexerit mancipatos, ut merito debeant cum ipso quem inhabitant orbe deleri ». — *Secundum haec* etiam *erit* quantum ad *iudicandi modum,* quia dicitur *Dominus descendisse;* Genesis decimo octavo[^p443-7]: « Dixit Dominus Abrahae: Clamor Sodomorum et Gomorrhaeorum multiplicatus est, et peccatum eorum aggravatum est nimis. Descendam et videbo, utrum clamorem, qui venit ad me, opere compleverint ». Sic et *Dominus descendet* in die iudicii; primae ad Thessalonicenses quarto[^p443-8]: « Quoniam ipse Dominus in iussu et in voce Archangeli et in tuba Dei descendet de caelo ». Sicut etiam *liberavit* Lot iustum, damnando impios; Sapientiae decimo: « Haec iustum a pereuntibus liberavit, descendente igne in Pentapolim, quibus in testimonium nequitiae fumigabunda constat terra deserta, et incerto tempore fructus habentes arbores, et incredibilis animae memoria stans figmentum salis » etc.; sic etiam erit in finali iudicio. Unde Glossa[^p443-9]: « Exeunte Lot, Sodoma perit, quia *in consummatione saeculi exibunt Angeli et separabunt malos de medio iustorum et mittent eos in caminum ignis* », sicut dicitur Matthaei decimo tertio. Sicut etiam *subito* ira Dei impiis supervenit, sic etiam erit in iudicio finali; Isaiae trigesimo[^p443-10]: « Subito, dum non speratur, veniet contritio eius, et conteretur velut lagena » etc.; et Matthaei vigesimo quinto: « Moram autem faciente sponso, dormitaverunt omnes » etc. — *Secundum haec* etiam *erit* quantum ad *supplicium.* Illi enim puniti sunt igne et sulphure; Genesis decimo nono[^p443-11]: « Surrexit Abraham mane », « et vidit favillam ascendentem de terra sicut fumum fornacis ». Sic erit in supplicio impiorum in iudicio; Psalmus[^p443-12]: « Pluet super peccatores laqueos, ignis et sulphur et spiritus procellarum, pars calicis eorum »; et Ezechielis trigesimo octavo: « Ignem et sulphur pluam super eum et super exercitum eius et super populos multos, qui sunt cum eo »; unde et Apocalypsis decimo quarto: « Si quis adoraverit bestiam, bibet de vino irae Dei, quod mistum est mero in calice irae

#### The curiosity of the Jews is confuted in two ways.

35. *And being asked by the Pharisees* etc. The two principal parts having been finished, in which the guile and the impiety of the Jews were confuted, and by the opposite simplicity and piety commended, here follows the third part, in which *curiosity is confuted*[^p437-5]. And since curiosity consists not only in the searching out of knowledge but also in the possession of money, according to blessed Augustine[^p437-6], therefore this part is divided into two: in the first of which he confutes *curiosity in searching;* in the second, *curiosity in possessing,* below, the eighteenth chapter[^p437-7]: *A certain ruler asked him.*

#### First curiosity in searching is confuted through three things.

The first part is divided into three: in the first of which there is brought in *the reprehension of the curiosity of the Jews;* in the second, *the assigning of the reason,* there[^p437-8]: *For as the lightning that lighteneth* etc.; in the third, *the persuasion to studiousness,* there: *And he spoke also a parable to them* etc.

#### Of the reprehension of the curiosity of the Jews, three things.

Now in *the reprehension of the curiosity of the Pharisees* three things are brought in, namely *the disapproval of the curiosity of the Pharisees* who ask, *the approval of the charity of the disciples* who desire, and *the reprobation of the contrariety of the heretics* who deceive.

36. (Vers. 20.). First, then, as to *the disapproval of the curiosity of the Pharisees who ask* he says: *And being asked by the Pharisees: When cometh the kingdom of God?* by a curious inquiry, namely. This question was curious, because it was of a secret thing, which God hides in his secret, according to that of the Acts, the first chapter[^p437-9]: « It is not for you to know the times or moments, which the Father hath put in his own power »; and therefore Ecclesiasticus, the third chapter: « Seek not the things that are too high for thee, and search not into things above thy ability: but the things that God hath commanded thee, think on them always, and in many of his works be not curious »; and Ecclesiastes, the seventh chapter: « What needeth a man to seek things that are above him, whereas he knoweth not what is profitable for him in his life, in the number of the days of his pilgrimage, and the time that passeth like a shadow? Or who can tell him what shall be after him »?

37. (Vers. 20. 21.). And therefore he adds: *Answering them he said*[^p437-10]: *The kingdom of God cometh not with observation; neither shall they say: Behold here, or behold there,* by human comprehension. For human comprehension does not extend to knowing the *time* of the judgement;
<!-- page 438 -->
whence he says: *It cometh not with observation;* the Gloss[^p438-1]: « Of time »; Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter: « But of that day and hour no one knoweth, not the angels of heaven, but the Father alone »; and the first to the Thessalonians, the fifth chapter: « The day of the Lord shall so come, as a thief in the night ». Nor does it extend itself to knowing the *place;* on account of which he says: *Neither shall they say: Behold, here* etc.; the Gloss[^p438-2]: « Nor shall a determinate place be known ». — But the contrary seems to be said in Joel, the third chapter: « I will gather together all nations, and will plead with them in the valley of Josaphat ». But that is to be understood thus, that *Iosaphat* is taken for its interpretation; for it is interpreted *valley of judgement.* For the judgement will not be with the Lord standing on the earth, but rather in the air, as Jerome says on this place[^p438-3]. — And since a curious inquiry is not excluded except by a studious one, therefore he adds: *For lo, the kingdom of God is within you,* by the offering of divine grace, which indeed was made in the coming of Christ; of which John, the first chapter[^p438-4]: « There hath stood one in the midst of you, whom you know not ». Whence Bede: « He says that he himself, *the kingdom of God,* is placed within them, that is, reigning in the hearts of those who have believed ». On account of which it is said above, the eleventh chapter[^p438-5]: « If I by the finger of God cast out devils, doubtless the kingdom of God is come upon you », that kingdom, namely, of which it is said to the Romans, the fourteenth chapter: « The kingdom of God is not meat and drink » etc. He means, then, to say that they ought to consider the first coming and cease to search out the time of the second coming; whence Bede[^p438-6]: « When it is to come can be observed neither by men nor by angels, as the time of the Lord's incarnation was both foretold by the most certain oracles of the Prophets and made manifest by the proclamations of the angels ».

38. (Vers. 22.). Second, as to *the approval of the charity of the disciples who desire* he adds: *And he said to his disciples: The days will come, when you shall desire to see one day of the Son of man,* namely the days of tribulation, in which the desire of eternity is increased, according to that of the Psalm[^p438-7]: « In the day of my tribulation I sought God, with my hands » etc. And that will be a day of the bitterest tribulation, according to that of Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter: « There shall be great tribulation, such as hath not been from the beginning of the world until now ».

39. And note that he says *days* in the plural on account of the plurality of the tribulations which the Church was to suffer after Christ's ascension. And there are four principal ones: the first, *of tyrants;* the second, *of heretics;* the third, *of false Christians;* and the fourth will be blown together *out of all,* which will be that of antichrist and his accomplices. And these are designated in the four beasts which Daniel saw, Daniel, the seventh chapter[^p438-8]. The first was like a lion, the second a bear, the third a leopard; the fourth is not described, because in it there will be the concourse of all perversities. And this tribulation brings forth the desire of the day of glory, of which Zacharias, the last chapter[^p438-9]: « There shall be one day, which is known to the Lord of hosts ». And this is *desirable,* according to that of the Psalm: « Better is one day in thy courts above thousands ». And this the *just* desire, as Paul; to the Philippians, the first chapter: « Having a desire to be dissolved and to be with Christ, a thing by far the better ». The *afflicted* also desire it, as the disciples; whence Bede[^p438-10]: « After my ascension you, groaning in this exile of the world, will desire to see me in my glory. Which will is to be praised, yet is not to be fulfilled in this world ». — And therefore there follows: *And you shall not see.* Yet this will be fulfilled at some time, because, Proverbs, the tenth chapter[^p438-11], « to the just their desire shall be given ». And this desire is stirred up by tribulation, both because this world is the more despised, and because the hope of eternal things is conceived the stronger, and because the fire of love is the more vehemently inflamed. On account of which the second to the Corinthians, the fifth chapter[^p438-12]: « We know, if our earthly house of this habitation be dissolved, that we have a building of God, a house not made with hands, but eternal in heaven. For in this also we groan, desiring to be clothed upon with our habitation that is from heaven » etc.

40. (Vers. 23.). Third, as to *the reprobation of the contrariety of the heretics who deceive* he subjoins: *And they will say to you: See here, and see there;* the Gloss[^p438-13]: « In this person, or in this time is Christ ».
<!-- page 439 -->
For this the heretics have at times dared to say and will dare, and above all that son of perdition. Whence Bede: « There have been and there will be many heretics who assert themselves to be Christs, of whom the first was Simon the magician, and the last antichrist ». Whence also he who was the leader of the sect of the Manichaeans said that he was the Holy Spirit, as Augustine says[^p439-1]. Whence, because heretics disagree, one says one thing, another holds the contrary, therefore he says that one says: *see here,* another: *see there.* Whence Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p439-2]: « Many will come in my name, saying: I am Christ, and they will seduce many ». And they are seduced who receive their doctrine and follow their life. — Therefore he adds: *Go ye not,* with them; the Gloss[^p439-3]: « So as to believe what they say »; Isaias, the twenty-eighth chapter: « He that believeth, let him not hasten »; *nor follow;* the Gloss[^p439-4]: « Their life »; Proverbs, the first chapter: « My son, if sinners shall entice thee, consent not to them ». And he warns so diligently for this reason, because it will be difficult to avoid the seductions of those false prophets and preachers; Matthew, the twenty-fourth chapter[^p439-5]: « There shall arise false Christs and false prophets, and shall shew great signs and wonders, insomuch that even the elect, if it be possible, are sent into error ». Whence Chrysostom[^p439-6]: « A man about death sees phantasms; so also the world in its going out will suffer many errors ».

#### Of the reason of this reprehension, three things.

41. *For as the lightning* etc. After the reprehension of the curiosity of the Jews there is subjoined here *the reason of the reprehension*[^p439-7], which is taken from the manner of the coming of the Son of God himself, who will not be able to lie hidden when he has come, nor to be foreseen beforehand. And this part is divided into three: in the first of which he treats of the manner *of appearing;* in the second, of the manner *of coming,* there[^p439-8]: *And as it came to pass in the days of Noe;* in the third, of the manner *of escaping,* there: *In that day* etc.

Concerning the manner *of appearing* two things are brought in, namely *the manifestation of Christ appearing* and *the order of the appearing.*

42. (Vers. 24.). First, then, as to *the manifestation of the appearing* he says: *For as the lightning that lighteneth from under heaven, shineth unto the parts that are under heaven,* namely swiftly and openly. Chrysostom[^p439-9]: « The flash of lightning needs no herald, but in a moment it is made manifest in the bedchambers and in the houses and through the whole world ». — *So shall the Son of man be in his day;* the Gloss[^p439-10]: « Sudden and flashing, so that no one is permitted to lie hidden in his own mind without being pierced by the lightning of the judge ». And note that the appearing of the Lord in the judgement is compared to *the flashing of lightning,* because it is *manifest;* the Psalm[^p439-11]: « God shall come manifestly, our God, and shall not keep silence »; because it is *terrible;* Ezechiel, the first chapter: « This was the vision running to and fro, the brightness of fire, and lightning going forth from the fire »; because it is *sudden;* Isaias, the twenty-ninth chapter: « And it shall be suddenly; it shall be visited by the Lord of hosts in thunder and earthquake and with a great noise of whirlwind and tempest and of the flame of devouring fire ». — As, then, it is foolish in the night to be solicitous about the appearing of the sun, when it will appear, since its rising can in no way lie hidden, and more foolish about the appearing of the lightning; so also about the coming of that day, by inquiring the certain moment of time, since it will become known manifestly to all, according to that of the Apocalypse, the first chapter[^p439-12]: « Behold, he cometh with the clouds, and every eye shall see him » etc. And from this we can manifestly discern Christ from antichrist, because antichrist will not be able in a moment of time to make himself manifest to all.

43. (Vers. 25.). Second, as to *the order of the appearing* he adds: *But first he must suffer many things, and be rejected by this generation.* And this is understood of Christ the head and of the perfidious generation of the Jews, by which he suffered many things, of which Deuteronomy, the thirty-second chapter[^p439-13]: « It is a perverse generation, and unfaithful children »; and Proverbs, the thirtieth chapter: « A generation that curseth its father, and that doth not bless its mother ». From this generation Christ suffered many reproaches, revilings, stripes and torments, according to that of Isaias, the fifty-third chapter[^p439-14]: « We have thought him as it were a leper, and as one struck by God and afflicted. But he was wounded for our iniquities, he was bruised for our sins ».

44. And this passion began from the head and descended into all the members of Christ; which being consummated, then will be the end and the time of the second coming. Whence the Gloss[^p439-15]: « *He must be rejected by this generation,* not only
<!-- page 440 -->
of the Jews, but of all the reprobate, by whom Christ suffers daily in his body »; on account of which Paul also, to the Colossians, the first chapter: « I will fill up those things that are wanting of the sufferings of Christ, in my flesh, for his body, which is the Church ». — And this passion in the body of Christ being consummated, there remains nothing but glorification and full retribution; on account of which in the Apocalypse, the sixth chapter[^p440-1], it is said to the Saints desiring the second robe: « Wait yet a little while, until the number of your brethren be filled up »; and the Apocalypse, the seventh chapter: « Hurt not the earth and the sea nor the trees, till we sign the servants of our God in their foreheads ». This is the sign of the living God, which is the sign of the cross, with which all the elect must be signed; Ezechiel, the ninth chapter[^p440-2]: « Mark Thau upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and mourn »; and after: « Pass, he says, through the midst of the city and kill ». « But upon whomsoever you shall see Thau, kill him not ». — And note that the passion of the just precedes the reprobation of the reprobate, because justice is stirred up to vengeance on account of the perfidy of the utmost iniquity; Isaias, the fifty-ninth chapter[^p440-3]: « Judgement is turned away backward, and justice hath stood far off: because truth hath fallen down in the street, and equity could not come in. And truth hath been forgotten, and he that departed from evil lay open to be a prey: and the Lord saw, and it appeared evil in his eyes, because there is no judgement ». « And he put on justice as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation upon his head: and he put on the garments of vengeance, and was clad as with a cloak of zeal, as it were for vengeance » etc.

45. (Vers. 26.). *And as it came to pass in the days* etc. After he has described the manner of appearing, here second[^p440-4] he describes *the manner of coming.* And the coming of Christ is described as sudden in respect of sinners through a twofold likeness and example. First, indeed, it is shown through a likeness on the part of *the flood that overflows;* second, on the part of *the burning that consumes,* there[^p440-5]: *Likewise as it came to pass in the days of Lot.* — Concerning the likeness of *the flood that overflows* three things are brought in, namely the *figure,* the *cause* and the *punishment* of the flood itself.

First, then, as to *the figure* of the flood itself he says: *And as it came to pass in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.* For this was congruous, because *Noe* bore the figure of Christ, who calls himself the Son of man: for *Noe* is interpreted rest, and in Christ alone is rest and peace. For, to the Ephesians, the second chapter[^p440-6], « he is our peace, who hath made both one »; and John, the sixteenth chapter: « In the world you shall have distress, but in me peace ». Whence Augustine[^p440-7]: « My heart is restless, Lord, until it rest in thee ». Therefore he himself says: « Take up my yoke upon you, and you shall find rest to your souls ».

46. Not only by *name* does he signify Christ, but also by *deed.* For as Noe saved the seed of men through wood, so Christ also through the gibbet of the cross; Wisdom, the fourteenth chapter[^p440-8]: « The hope of the world fleeing to a vessel, sent back to the world seed of generation, which was governed by thy hand. For blessed is the wood, by which justice cometh ». Whence also he sets before it: « Men trust their lives even to a little wood » etc. And as Noe built the ark, so also Christ the Church, which, after the manner of the ark, is built of planed timbers joined together with the pitch of charity, having lower rooms and third stories on account of the diversities of offices, grades and dignities; to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter[^p440-9]: « He gave some apostles, and some prophets, and other some evangelists, and other some pastors and doctors, for the perfecting of the saints, for the edifying of the body of Christ, until we all meet into a perfect man, unto the measure of the age of the fulness of Christ »; in which, namely, the length, breadth and height of the ark will be most nobly consummated[^p440-10]. As also those who were within the ark were saved, so those who are within the Church are saved through water and baptism; the first of Peter, the third chapter[^p440-11]: « In the days of Noe, when the ark was a building, wherein a few, that is, eight souls, were saved by water. Whereunto baptism being of the like form, now saveth you also: not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the examination of a good conscience towards God, by the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ ». And as those who were outside the ark were destroyed, so all who are outside the Church will be damned by the final judgement; in designation of which, in the Acts, the twenty-seventh chapter[^p440-12], « Paul said: Except these stay in the ship, you cannot be saved ».
<!-- page 441 -->

47. (Vers. 27.). Second, as to *the cause of the flood* he adds: *They did eat and drink, they married wives, and were given in marriage, until the day in which Noe entered into the ark,* that is, they were wholly intent on the works of the flesh, and therefore were worthy to be corrupted and destroyed in corruptible flesh. Whence the Gloss of Ambrose[^p441-1]: « Not marriages or foods are condemned, since in the one are placed the aids of succession, in the other those of nature, but the immoderate use of things lawful is reproved. For because by giving themselves wholly to these they despised the judgements of God, they perished by fire or water ». Whence in these acts their carnality is intimated, on account of which they were worthy of the divine wrath; Genesis, the sixth chapter[^p441-2]: « And when God had seen that the earth was corrupted, for all flesh had corrupted its way upon the earth, he said to Noe: The end of all flesh is come before me; the earth is filled with iniquity, and I will destroy them with the earth ». And their principal fault came from carnality, and this he intimates in four acts which he touches: *they did eat,* that is, they were given to eating as *gluttons,* and therefore worthy of wrath; the Psalm[^p441-3]: « As yet their meat was in their mouth, and the wrath of God came upon them ». *They drank,* as *drunkards,* and therefore worthy of wrath, according to that of Proverbs, the twenty-third chapter[^p441-4]: « Who hath woe? whose father hath woe »? « Is it not to them that pass their time in wine » etc.; Isaias, the fifth chapter: « Woe to you that are mighty to drink wine, and stout men at drunkenness »; and the twenty-second chapter: « Behold joy and gladness, to eat flesh and to drink wine »; « and this voice was revealed in my ears: Surely this iniquity shall not be forgiven you till you die, saith the Lord of hosts ». *They married wives,* as the *lascivious;* Genesis, the sixth chapter[^p441-5]: « And after that men began to be multiplied upon the earth and had begotten children, the sons of God seeing the daughters of men, that they were fair, took to themselves wives of all which they chose ». And there follows: « The Lord said: My spirit shall not remain in man, because he is flesh ». And *they were given in marriage,* as the *secure,* and therefore disposed to destruction, according to that of the first to the Thessalonians, the fifth chapter[^p441-6]: « When they shall say: Peace and security; then shall sudden destruction come upon them ». Whence he who is swallowed up by these vices must needs be enveloped by the flood; on account of which the first to the Thessalonians, the fifth chapter: « But you, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief ». « Therefore let us not sleep, as others do, but let us watch and be sober ». And the manner is described to the Romans, the thirteenth chapter[^p441-7]: « The night is passed, and the day is at hand. Let us therefore cast off » etc.; and after: « Not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and impurities ». « But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ ».

48. Third, as to *the punishment of the flood* he adds: *And the flood came and destroyed them all,* namely those who were outside the ark, immediately after the entrance of Noe, according to that of Genesis, the seventh chapter[^p441-8]: « In the very point of that day Noe went into the ark ». « And the flood was forty days and forty nights », « and all flesh was consumed that moved upon the earth ». Whence also the second of Peter, the second chapter: « He spared not the original world, but preserved Noe, the eighth person, the preacher of justice, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly ». From which it is understood that the punishment of the future judgement will come upon those who are outside the Church of Christ, the rare just being nevertheless saved; for the Gloss[^p441-9]: « Noe builds the ark, when the Lord gathers the faithful into the Church; which when consummated he enters, when he illumines it with his presence in the day of judgement; but while the ark is being built, the wicked run riot; and while it is being entered, they are punished with eternal damnation ». And this is said to be done *in the very point of the day,* because it is in the opening of the octave of the resurrection; whence the Psalm[^p441-10]: « In the morning I will stand before thee and will see, because thou art not a God that willest iniquity; neither shall the wicked dwell near thee, nor shall the unjust abide before thy eyes »; and the second of Peter, the second chapter: « The Lord knoweth how to deliver the pious from temptation, but to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgement to be tormented ». — From this example it appears figuratively[^p441-11], as much from the allegorical signification as from the likeness expressed, that the day of judgement will come *suddenly* upon carnal and animal men, and *to their* universal *perdition,* who are outside the unity of the Church; but to those who are in the ark, although it will come *suddenly,* *it does no harm,* because it finds them prepared. Whence below, the twenty-first chapter[^p441-12]: « Take heed to yourselves, lest perhaps your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness and the cares of this life, and that day come upon you suddenly. For as a snare shall it come upon all that sit upon the face of the whole earth » etc.

49. (Vers. 28.). *Likewise as it came to pass* etc. After the likeness taken on the part of the flood that overflows he brings in the likeness on the part of *the burning that consumes*[^p441-13]; concerning which likeness three things are brought in,
<!-- page 442 -->
namely the *demerit,* the *punishment* and the *example* of the Sodomites themselves.

First, as to the *demerit* he says: *Likewise as it came to pass in the days of Lot,* a just man, namely. And note that in the description of the iniquity of the shameless people the justice of the just man is set forth first, because from this their impiety appears the more, that a just man was among them; on account of which the second of Peter, the second chapter[^p442-1]: « He delivered just Lot, oppressed by the injury and lewdness of the wicked. For in sight and hearing he was just, dwelling among them who from day to day vexed the just soul with unjust works ». Whence on account of the justice of the just man the enormity of the sin is aggravated in the impious. — As to which he adds: *They did eat and drink,* the Sodomites, namely, as the *lascivious,* like those of whom Exodus, the thirty-second chapter[^p442-2]: « The people sat down to eat and drink, and they rose up to play »; and he does not add that they married wives, as before[^p442-3], because they sinned not according to nature, but against nature; Genesis, the thirteenth chapter: « The men of Sodom were very wicked, and sinners before the Lord beyond measure »; and in Genesis, the nineteenth chapter, it is said that « they beset the house of Lot, both young and old, all the people together, and they called Lot and said to him: Where are the men that came in to thee? bring them hither, that we may know them ». — *They bought and sold,* as the *avaricious;* Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-seventh chapter[^p442-4]: « He that seeketh to be enriched turneth away his eye. As a stake sticketh fast in the midst of the joining of stones, so between the midst of buying and selling the sinner shall be straitened ». On account of which it is said against these, the first to the Corinthians, the seventh chapter[^p442-5]: « They that buy, as though they possessed not, and they that use this world, as if they used it not; for the fashion of this world passeth away ». — *They planted and built,* as the *secure,* who supposed they would dwell in the houses and eat the fruit of the trees, as that rich man above, the twelfth chapter[^p442-6]: « My soul, thou hast much goods laid up for very many years ». « But the Lord said to him: Thou fool, this night » etc. And therefore Amos, the fifth chapter: « You shall build houses with square stone, and shall not dwell in them; you shall plant most delightful vineyards, and shall not drink the wine of them, because I have known your many crimes ». Therefore Isaias, the fifth chapter[^p442-7]: « Woe to you that join house to house, and lay field to field, even to the end of the place ». Because, then, the Sodomites were both *lascivious* and *avaricious* and *secure,* therefore they were worthy of the divine wrath; Ezechiel, the sixteenth chapter: « This was the iniquity of Sodom thy sister: pride, fulness of bread, and abundance, and the idleness of her and of her daughters, and they did not put forth their hand to the needy and to the poor ».

50. (Vers. 29.). Second, as to the *punishment* he adds: *And in the day that Lot went out of Sodom,* through the equity of the divine justice that delivers; Genesis, the nineteenth chapter[^p442-8]: « The angels took the hand of Lot, and the hand of his wife, and the hand of his two daughters, because the Lord spared them. And they brought him forth and set him without the city, and there they spoke to him, saying: Save thy life » etc.; and below in the same: « The Lord delivered Lot from the overthrow of the cities in which he had dwelt ». In which Lot bears the type of the just, who go out from the midst of the wicked; whence the Gloss[^p442-9]: « Lot, who is interpreted *declining,* is the people of the elect, who, while in Sodom, that is, among the reprobate, it sojourns as a stranger, declines their crimes as much as it is able »; and this according to that of the second to the Corinthians, the sixth chapter[^p442-10]: « Go out from the midst of them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you, and I will be a Father to you, and you shall be my sons and daughters ». The divine justice, then, delivered just Lot, and on the contrary destroyed the impious people. — And therefore he adds: *He rained fire and brimstone from heaven and destroyed them all,* the Lord, namely, through the severity of the justice that avenges; Genesis, the nineteenth chapter[^p442-11]: « Therefore the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrha brimstone and fire from heaven from the Lord, and he destroyed these cities and all the country about, all the inhabitants of the cities and all things that spring from the earth ». — And note that he says pointedly that *the Lord rained from the Lord,* because, although all judgement is due to the Lord the Son, according to that of John, the fifth chapter[^p442-12]: « The Father doth not judge any man, but hath given all judgement to the Son », « because he is the Son of man »; yet the judgement of this sin is specially due to him, because it is directly against the incarnate Word and the nature which the Son of God made heavenly, angelic and divine, it makes viler than the brute nature is, by overturning the laws of nature which the brute animals keep. And therefore this vice is punished not only by *fire,* but also by *brimstone,* on account of
<!-- page 443 -->
the burning and the stench of the most inordinate lust. Of the *burning,* to the Romans, the first chapter[^p443-1]: « They burned in their lusts one towards another, men with men working that which is filthy »; of the *stench,* Isaias, the thirty-fourth chapter: « Out of their carcasses shall rise a stink, the mountains shall be melted with their blood » etc.; and the Psalm: « They are corrupt, and are become abominable » etc. On account of these they were deservedly burned up with fire and brimstone; whence Bernard[^p443-2]: « The flame of hell, brooking no delay, forestalled to take away that execrable nation, and the earth itself too, as conscious of so great a confusion, fire and the spirit of storms consumed ».

51. (Vers. 30.). Third, as to the *example* he adds: *According to these things*[^p443-3] *shall it be, in the day when the Son of man shall be revealed.* This is the day of judgement, in which there will be made the *revelation* of Christ himself as *Saviour* in respect of the good; the first of Peter, the first chapter: « You are kept by faith unto salvation, ready to be revealed in the last time ». There will be made also the revelation of Christ as *judge* in respect of the wicked; the first to the Corinthians, the fourth chapter[^p443-4]: « Judge not before the time, until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts ». Of both, Isaias, the fifty-sixth chapter: « My salvation is near to come, and my justice to be revealed ». This day of revelation is like the burning of Sodom, because in that its example went before; in the Canonical of Jude[^p443-5]: « As Sodom and Gomorrha and the neighbouring cities, in like manner having given themselves to fornication and going after other flesh, were made an example, suffering the punishment of eternal fire »; and likewise the second of Peter, the second chapter: « Reducing the cities of the Sodomites and of the Gomorrhites into ashes, he condemned them to be overthrown, making them an example to those that should act wickedly ».

52. And therefore he says: *According to these things,* that the *example* may be like as to the *demerit,* as to the *manner of judging* and as to the *punishment.* As to the *demerit,* because, as in Sodom there were many wicked and very few good, so in the time of the final judgement, according to that of below, the eighteenth chapter[^p443-6]: « Thinkest thou, when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on earth »? Whence the Gloss: « He who meanwhile, not appearing, sees all things, will then, appearing, judge all things, when he shall have beheld all, forgetful of his judgements, made over to this world, so that they deservedly ought to be destroyed together with the very world they inhabit ». — *According to these things* also *shall it be* as to the *manner of judging,* because the *Lord* is said *to have descended;* Genesis, the eighteenth chapter[^p443-7]: « The Lord said to Abraham: The cry of Sodom and Gomorrha is multiplied, and their sin is become exceedingly grievous. I will descend and see whether they have fulfilled in work the cry that is come to me ». So also *the Lord will descend* in the day of judgement; the first to the Thessalonians, the fourth chapter[^p443-8]: « For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with commandment, and with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God ». As also *he delivered* just Lot, condemning the impious; Wisdom, the tenth chapter: « She delivered the just man from them that were perishing, when the fire came down upon Pentapolis; to whom, for a testimony of their wickedness, the desert land standeth smoking, and the trees bearing fruits at an uncertain time, and a standing pillar of salt the memorial of an incredulous soul » etc.; so also will it be in the final judgement. Whence the Gloss[^p443-9]: « When Lot goes out, Sodom perishes, because *at the end of the world the angels shall go out and shall separate the wicked from among the just and shall cast them into the furnace of fire* », as is said in Matthew, the thirteenth chapter. As also the wrath of God came upon the impious *suddenly,* so also will it be in the final judgement; Isaias, the thirtieth chapter[^p443-10]: « Suddenly, when it is not looked for, the breaking thereof shall come, and it shall be broken as a flagon » etc.; and Matthew, the twenty-fifth chapter: « And the bridegroom tarrying, they all slumbered » etc. — *According to these things* also *shall it be* as to the *punishment.* For those were punished with fire and brimstone; Genesis, the nineteenth chapter[^p443-11]: « Abraham rose in the morning », « and he saw the ashes rise up from the earth as the smoke of a furnace ». So will it be in the punishment of the impious in the judgement; the Psalm[^p443-12]: « He shall rain snares upon sinners: fire and brimstone and storms of winds shall be the portion of their cup »; and Ezechiel, the thirty-eighth chapter: « I will rain fire and brimstone upon him, and upon his army, and upon many nations that are with him »; whence also the Apocalypse, the fourteenth chapter: « If any man shall adore the beast, he shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is mingled with pure wine in the cup of his wrath,
<!-- page 444 -->
and shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the sight of the holy angels ». « And the smoke of their torments shall ascend up for ever and ever ».
