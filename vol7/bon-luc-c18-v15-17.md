# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XVIII, Vers. 15-17
## *Afferebant autem ad illum infantes etc.*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 457 -->

27. (Vers. 15.). *Afferebant autem ad illum infantes*[^p457-11] etc. Postquam exemplo parabolico induxit ad humilitatem in oratione, hic consequenter exemplo expresso etiam inducit ad *humilitatem in conversatione*[^p457-12], et hoc exemplo infantium, qui Christo fuerunt oblati et acceptati ab ipso in signum approbationis humilitatis. In huius autem exempli explicatione quinque introducuntur ab Evangelista: primo *oblatio importuna* infantium; secundo, *carnalis increpatio* offerentium; tertio, *benigna susceptio* oblatorum; quarto, *iudicialis approbatio* humilium; quinto, *severa reprobatio* superborum.
<!-- page 458 -->

Primo igitur quantum ad *infantium oblationem* dicit: *Afferebant*[^p458-1] *autem ad illum infantes, ut eos tangeret.* Glossa: « Post dictam controversiam et datam sententiam humili magistro offeruntur infantes, ut ostendatur, quod innocens et simplex aetas ad gratiam pertineat »; unde in Psalmo[^p458-2]: « Ex ore infantium et lactentium perfecisti laudem ». Offerebant autem, ut eis daret benedictionem per manuum impositionem; in quo praefigurabatur et quodam modo insinuabatur Sacramentum confirmationis, in quo datur copia gratiae et benedictionis, secundum illud Actuum octavo[^p458-3]: « Imponebant manus super illos, et accipiebant Spiritum sanctum ». Hic autem Spiritus sanctus requiescit super parvulos, secundum illud Isaiae ultimo[^p458-4]: « Quae est domus, quam aedificabitis mihi? Et quis est iste locus requietionis meae »? « Ad quem autem respiciam nisi ad pauperculum et contritum spiritu et trementem sermones meos »? ubi dicit alia translatio: « Super quem requiescet Spiritus meus nisi super humilem et quietum »? Et hoc maxime verum est, quando Domino offertur; Psalmus[^p458-5]: « Quoniam pater meus et mater mea dereliquerunt me, Dominus autem assumsit me ». Et ideo in figuram huius dicitur Levitici ultimo: « Quidquid semel fuerit consecratum, sanctum sanctorum erit Domino ».

28. Secundo quantum ad *offerentium increpationem* subdit: *Quod cum viderent discipuli, increpabant illos,* scilicet offerentes, et hoc, quia carnaliter afficiebantur circa Christum. Unde Glossa[^p458-6]: « *Increpabant,* non quod nollent, infantes manu et voce Salvatoris benedici, sed quia nondum perfecti in fide putabant, eum in similitudinem hominum lassari importunitate offerentium ». Unde propter quietem Magistri impediebant opus alienae salutis, sicut et Petrus Matthaei decimo sexto[^p458-7] Domino dicenti, quod oporteret, eum pati, dicebat: « Absit a te, Domine, non erit hoc ». Vel, quia carnaliter afficiebantur circa populum suum; unde Glossa[^p458-8]: « *Increpabant,* hoc ideo fortasse, quia prius populum Iudaeorum, ex quo secundum carnem erant, volebant salvari ». Et ideo carnali misericordia movebantur, de quo Ecclesiastici decimo octavo: « Miseratio hominis circa proximum suum, misericordia autem Dei » etc. In hoc autem facto discipuli gerebant typum *carnalium sacerdotum et pontificum,* qui magis amant carnalem quietem et sanguinis proximitatem quam populorum salutem; sicut et in contentione eorum, scilicet discipulorum, quam habuerunt, figuram gerunt *ambitiosorum,* de qua infra vigesimo secundo[^p458-9]: « Facta est contentio inter discipulos, quis eorum videretur esse maior ». Et haec duo vitia sunt, quae maxime Ecclesiam Dei corrumpunt, scilicet *ambitio* dignitatum et *negligentia* salutis proximorum. Pro utroque istorum dicitur Ieremiae quadragesimo octavo[^p458-10]: « Maledictus, qui facit opus Dei fraudulenter, et maledictus, qui prohibet gladium suum a sanguine »; alia translatio habet *negligenter;* in quo simul fraudulenta ambitio et desidiosa negligentia reprobatur.

29. (Vers. 16.). Tertio quantum ad *benignam oblatorum susceptionem* additur: *Iesus autem, convocans illos, dixit: Sinite, pueros venire ad me et nolite vetare eos*[^p458-11], quos scilicet magis debetis attrahere et vocare; unde Proverbiorum nono: « Misit ancillas suas, ut vocarent ad arcem et ad moenia civitatis: Si quis est parvulus veniat ad me ». Hos ergo non debebant vetare, sed vocare; Proverbiorum tertio[^p458-12]: « Noli prohibere benefacere eum qui potest; si potes, et ipse benefac ». Unde Chrysostomus[^p458-13]: « Si sancti futuri sunt, quid vetatis, filios ad patrem venire? Si peccatores futuri sunt, quid sententiam damnationis profertis, antequam culpam videatis? Quales modo sunt, meum est; quales vero futuri sunt, ipsorum est; quod meum est honorate, quod ipsorum erit miseremini ». — Ex quo manifeste apparet, quod pueri non sunt prohibendi, ne accedant ad Christum. In cuius etiam signum, ut ipse se praeberet pueris in exemplum, dicitur de ipso supra secundo[^p458-14], quod « cum factus esset annorum duodecim, relictis parentibus, remansit in Ierusalem ». In cuius etiam signum infantium puerorum acceptavit martyrium, de quo Matthaei secundo[^p458-15]: « Mittens Herodes, interfecit omnes pueros, qui erant in
<!-- page 459 -->
Bethlehem a bimatu et infra ». In cuius etiam signum acceptavit puerorum praeconium laudativum; Matthaei vigesimo primo: « Videntes principes sacerdotum mirabilia, quae faciebat, et pueros clamantes in templo et dicentes: Hosanna filio David! indignati sunt. Et dixerunt ei: Audis, quid isti dicunt? Iesus autem dixit eis: Non legistis, quia ex ore infantium et lactentium perfecisti laudem ». Licet igitur pueri occidantur ab Herode, repellantur a Pharisaeis, suscipiendi sunt a discipulis, quia suscipiuntur a Christo et a Deo; unde Psalmus[^p459-1]: « Laudate, pueri, Dominum ».

30. Quarto quantum ad *iudicialem humilium approbationem*[^p459-2] subiungitur: *Talium est enim regnum Dei,* id est pueris similium. — Et nota, quod pueri habent aliqua in se, in quibus assimilantur eis *mali,* et aliqua, in quibus assimilantur eis *boni.* Propter quod primae ad Corinthios decimo quarto[^p459-3]: « Nolite pueri effici sensibus, sed malitia parvuli estote ». — Habent namque pueri *stoliditatem in ratione;* Proverbiorum vigesimo secundo: « Stultitia colligata est in corde pueri »; Sapientiae duodecimo: « Tanquam pueris insensatis iudicium in derisum dedisti ». — Habent *concupiscentiam in affectione;* Ecclesiastae decimo[^p459-4]: « Vae terrae! cuius rex puer est, et cuius principes mane comedunt »; et Proverbiorum vigesimo nono: « Puer, qui dimittitur voluntati suae, confundet matrem suam ». — Habent etiam *instabilitatem in operatione;* Isaiae quadragesimo[^p459-5]: « Deficient pueri et laborabunt, et iuvenes in infirmitate sua cadent »; et ad Ephesios quarto: « Non simus sicut parvuli fluctuantes » etc. Et ideo Isaiae sexagesimo quinto: « Puer centum annorum morietur, et peccator » etc.

31. Habent praeter haec pueri *puritatem;* Danielis primo[^p459-6]: « Praecepit rex praeposito eunuchorum, ut introduceret et pueros, in quibus nulla esset macula »; ideo primi Regum vigesimo primo: « Si mundi sunt pueri, maxime a mulieribus? Et David: Equidem; fuerunt vasa puerorum sancta » etc. — Habent *simplicitatem;* Proverbiorum vigesimo[^p459-7]: « Ex studiis suis intelligitur puer, si recta et munda sint opera eius »; Osee undecimo: « Puer Israel, et dilexi eum », scilicet propter simplicitatem; et primi Paralipomenon ultimo: « Scio, Domine, quod probes corda et simplicitatem diligas ». — Habent *docilitatem;* Sapientiae octavo[^p459-8]: « Puer eram ingeniosus et sortitus sum animam bonam »; Proverbiorum vigesimo tertio: « Noli subtrahere a puero disciplinam; si enim percusseris eum virga, non morietur ». — Habent *amicabilitatem;* Iob vigesimo nono[^p459-9]: « Omnipotens erat mecum, et in circuitu meo pueri mei »; et supra undecimo: « Noli mihi molestus esse, quia pueri mei mecum sunt in cubili ». — Habent *tractabilitatem;* primi Regum secundo[^p459-10]: « Samuel ministrabat ante Dominum, puer accinctus ephod lineo »; et de Ioseph Genesis trigesimo septimo: « Ioseph pascebat gregem cum fratribus suis, adhuc puer ». — Habent *veracitatem;* Danielis decimo tertio[^p459-11]: « Suscitavit Dominus spiritum sanctum pueri iunioris, et ait: Mundus ego sum a sanguine huius »; Ieremiae primo: « Nescio loqui, quia puer ego sum. Et dixit: Noli dicere, quia puer sis »; et supra primo: « Tu puer, Propheta » etc. — Habent etiam *humilitatem;* Marci septimo[^p459-12]: « Catelli comedunt sub mensa de micis puerorum »; et ratione huius maxime Deo placent, et hic introducuntur in exemplum; et Matthaei decimo octavo: « Qui susceperit unum parvulum talem in nomine meo me suscipit »; quia « excelsus Dominus et humilia respicit ». Ideo Matthaei duodecimo: « Ecce, puer meus electus, quem elegi » etc. — Talium igitur, id est habentium *puritatem* in carne, *simplicitatem* in intentione, *docilitatem* in cognitione[^p459-13], *amicabilitatem* in affectione, *tractabilitatem* in operatione, *veracitatem* in sermone et *humilitatem* in conversatione, est regnum caelorum. Et ratione huius *humilitatis* vocatur Abraham *puer* in Scriptura; Psalmus[^p459-14]: « Memor fuit testamenti sui, quod habuit ad Abraham, puerum suum »; et supra primo similiter Israel vocat puerum: « Suscepit Israel, puerum suum ». Christus autem *puer* dicitur Ecclesiastae quarto: « Melior est puer pauper et sapiens rege sene et stulto » etc. Et ratione *omnium praedictorum* et maxime ratione *humilitatis* dicitur: *Talium est regnum caelorum.* Propter quod supra primo[^p459-15]: « Deposuit potentes de sede et exaltavit humiles »; et Iob vigesimo secundo: « Qui humiliatus fuerit erit in gloria; et qui inclinaverit oculos suos, ipse salvabitur ».
<!-- page 460 -->

32. (Vers. 17.). Quinto quantum ad *severam superborum reprobationem* subinfertur: *Amen dico vobis: Quicumque non acceperit regnum Dei ut*[^p460-1] *puer, non intrabit in illud;* quasi dicat: qui recusat humiliari propter superbiam cordis sui, non introibit in illud regnum per sententiam veritatis Dei. Unde Matthaei decimo octavo[^p460-2]: « Nisi conversi fueritis et efficiamini sicut parvuli, non introibitis in regnum caelorum ». Nam oportet, eum qui vult intrare in regnum, esse sicut puerum, id est purum et parvum. *Regnum* autem *caelorum* dicitur *sacra doctrina,* ad quam qui vult venire, oportet, quod semetipsum faciat sicut puerum. Unde Glossa[^p460-3]: « *Regnum caelorum,* id est doctrinam Evangelii, sicut puer iubemur accipere. Puer in discendo non contradicit doctoribus, non rationes et verba ad resistendum componit, sed fideliter suscipit, obtemperat et quiescit ». Ideo dicitur Matthaei undecimo[^p460-4]: « Abscondisti haec a sapientibus et prudentibus et revelasti ea parvulis »; et Danielis primo: « Pueris autem his dedit Deus scientiam et disciplinam in omni libro » etc.

33. *Regnum* etiam *caelorum* dicitur *gratia,* de qua supra decimo septimo[^p460-5]: « Regnum Dei intra vos est ». Et hoc non datur nisi pueris per munditiam et humilitatem; Iacobi quarto: « Deus superbis resistit, humilibus autem dat gratiam ». — *Regnum caelorum* nihilominus dicitur *gloria aeterna;* Matthaei decimo tertio[^p460-6]: « Fulgebunt iusti sicut sol in regno Patris eorum ». Et hoc non datur nisi *parvulis,* secundum illud Matthaei quinto: « Beati pauperes spiritu, quoniam ipsorum est regnum caelorum »; et etiam *pueris,* qui sunt puri; Matthaei quinto: « Beati mundo corde, quoniam ipsi Deum videbunt ». Unde Glossa[^p460-7]: « Sicut puer non perseverat in ira, laesus non meminit, non concupiscit mulierem, non cogitat vel loquitur malum; sic vos, nisi talem innocentiam et puritatem habueritis, non intrabitis in regnum caelorum ». Qui ergo recusat parvitatem humilitatis et puritatem sanctitatis, ille despicit vitae aeternae meritum; et ideo necesse est reprobari per divinum iudicium, ut non intret in regnum caelorum, sed foras pellatur procul; Apocalypsis ultimo[^p460-8]: « Foris canes et venefici et impudici et idolis servientes ». Propter quod in Psalmo: « Non salvatur rex per multam virtutem, et gigas non salvabitur in multitudine virtutis suae »; ideo Iudith nono[^p460-9]: « Superbi non tibi placuerunt ab initio, sed humilium tibi semper et mansuetorum placuit deprecatio ». Qui igitur non vult damnari necessarium habet humiliari; ideo Ecclesiastici septimo: « Humilia valde spiritum tuum; quoniam vindicta carnis impii, ignis et vermis ».

## English
<!-- page 457 -->

27. (Vers. 15.). *And they brought unto him infants*[^p457-11] etc. After he led by a parabolic example to humility in prayer, here consequently by an express example he leads also to *humility in conduct*[^p457-12], and this by the example of infants, who were offered to Christ and accepted by him in sign of the approval of humility. And in the explication of this example five things are introduced by the Evangelist: first, *the importunate offering* of the infants; second, *the carnal rebuke* of those offering; third, *the kind reception* of those offered; fourth, *the judicial approval* of the humble; fifth, *the severe reprobation* of the proud.
<!-- page 458 -->

First, then, as to *the offering of the infants* he says: *And they brought*[^p458-1] *unto him infants, that he might touch them.* The Gloss: « After the controversy spoken and the sentence given, infants are offered to the humble master, that it may be shown that the innocent and simple age belongs to grace »; whence in the Psalm[^p458-2]: « Out of the mouth of infants and of sucklings thou hast perfected praise ». And they offered them that he might give them a blessing through the imposition of hands; in which was prefigured and in a certain manner intimated the Sacrament of confirmation, in which is given an abundance of grace and of blessing, according to that of the Acts, the eighth chapter[^p458-3]: « They laid hands upon them, and they received the Holy Spirit ». But the Holy Spirit rests upon little ones, according to that of Isaias, the last chapter[^p458-4]: « What is the house that you will build to me? And what is this place of my rest »? « But to whom shall I have respect, but to him that is poor and little, and of a contrite spirit, and that trembleth at my words »? where another translation says: « Upon whom shall my Spirit rest, but upon the humble and quiet »? And this is most of all true when one is offered to the Lord; the Psalm[^p458-5]: « For my father and my mother have left me, but the Lord hath taken me up ». And therefore in figure of this it is said in Leviticus, the last chapter: « Whatsoever is once consecrated shall be holy of holies to the Lord ».

28. Second, as to *the rebuke of those offering* he subjoins: *Which when the disciples saw, they rebuked them,* namely those offering, and this because they were carnally affected towards Christ. Whence the Gloss[^p458-6]: « *They rebuked,* not because they did not wish the infants to be blessed by the hand and voice of the Saviour, but because, not yet perfect in faith, they thought that he, after the likeness of men, was wearied by the importunity of those offering ». Whence for the sake of the Master's rest they hindered the work of another's salvation, as Peter too, in Matthew, the sixteenth chapter[^p458-7], when the Lord said that he must suffer, said: « Be it far from thee, O Lord, this shall not be ». Or, because they were carnally affected towards their own people; whence the Gloss[^p458-8]: « *They rebuked:* this perhaps for this reason, that they wished the people of the Jews, from which they were according to the flesh, to be saved first ». And therefore they were moved by a carnal mercy, of which Ecclesiasticus, the eighteenth chapter: « The compassion of man is toward his neighbour, but the mercy of God » etc. But in this deed the disciples bore the type *of carnal priests and pontiffs,* who love carnal rest and nearness of blood more than the salvation of the peoples; as also in their strife, namely of the disciples, which they had, they bear the figure *of the ambitious,* of which below, the twenty-second chapter[^p458-9]: « There was a strife among the disciples, which of them should seem to be the greater ». And these are two vices which most of all corrupt the Church of God, namely *ambition* for dignities and *negligence* of the salvation of neighbours. For each of these it is said in Jeremias, the forty-eighth chapter[^p458-10]: « Cursed be he that doth the work of God fraudulently, and cursed be he that withholdeth his sword from blood »; another translation has *negligently;* in which fraudulent ambition and slothful negligence are reprobated together.

29. (Vers. 16.). Third, as to *the kind reception of those offered* it is added: *But Jesus, calling them together, said: Suffer boys to come to me, and forbid them not*[^p458-11], whom, namely, you ought rather to draw and to call; whence Proverbs, the ninth chapter: « She hath sent her maids to call to the tower and to the walls of the city: Whosoever is a little one, let him come to me ». These, then, they ought not to forbid, but to call; Proverbs, the third chapter[^p458-12]: « Forbid not him to do good who is able; if thou art able, do good thyself also ». Whence Chrysostom[^p458-13]: « If they are to be saints, why do you forbid sons to come to their father? If they are to be sinners, why do you pronounce the sentence of damnation before you see the fault? What they are now is mine; what they are to be is theirs; honour what is mine, have mercy on what will be theirs ». — From which it manifestly appears that boys are not to be forbidden to draw near to Christ. In sign of which too, that he might offer himself to boys as an example, it is said of him above, the second chapter[^p458-14], that « when he was become twelve years old, having left his parents, he remained in Jerusalem ». In sign of which too he accepted the martyrdom of infant boys, of which Matthew, the second chapter[^p458-15]: « Herod sending killed all the boys that were in
<!-- page 459 -->
Bethlehem from two years old and under ». In sign of which too he accepted the praising proclamation of the boys; Matthew, the twenty-first chapter: « The chief priests, seeing the marvellous things that he did, and the boys crying in the temple and saying: Hosanna to the son of David! were moved with indignation. And they said to him: Hearest thou what these say? And Jesus said to them: Have you not read, that out of the mouth of infants and of sucklings thou hast perfected praise »? Although, then, boys are killed by Herod and repelled by the Pharisees, they are to be received by the disciples, because they are received by Christ and by God; whence the Psalm[^p459-1]: « Praise the Lord, ye boys ».

30. Fourth, as to *the judicial approval of the humble*[^p459-2] it is subjoined: *For of such is the kingdom of God,* that is, of those like boys. — And note that boys have some things in themselves in which *the wicked* are likened to them, and some in which *the good* are likened to them. On account of which the first to the Corinthians, the fourteenth chapter[^p459-3]: « Do not become boys in sense, but in malice be little ones ». — For boys have *dullness in reason;* Proverbs, the twenty-second chapter: « Folly is bound up in the heart of a boy »; Wisdom, the twelfth chapter: « As to senseless boys thou hast given a judgement in mockery ». — They have *concupiscence in affection;* Ecclesiastes, the tenth chapter[^p459-4]: « Woe to the land whose king is a boy, and whose princes eat in the morning »; and Proverbs, the twenty-ninth chapter: « The boy that is left to his own will shall confound his mother ». — They have also *instability in operation;* Isaias, the fortieth chapter[^p459-5]: « Boys shall faint and labour, and young men shall fall in their infirmity »; and to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter: « Let us not be as little ones tossed to and fro » etc. And therefore Isaias, the sixty-fifth chapter: « The boy of a hundred years shall die, and the sinner » etc.

31. Besides these, boys have *purity;* Daniel, the first chapter[^p459-6]: « The king commanded the master of the eunuchs that he should bring in boys also, in whom there was no blemish »; therefore the first of Kings, the twenty-first chapter: « If the boys be clean, especially from women? And David: Truly; the vessels of the boys were holy » etc. — They have *simplicity;* Proverbs, the twentieth chapter[^p459-7]: « By his pursuits a boy is known, if his works be clean and right »; Osee, the eleventh chapter: « Israel was a boy, and I loved him », namely on account of simplicity; and the first of Paralipomenon, the last chapter: « I know, O Lord, that thou provest hearts and lovest simplicity ». — They have *docility;* Wisdom, the eighth chapter[^p459-8]: « I was a witty boy, and had received a good soul »; Proverbs, the twenty-third chapter: « Withhold not correction from a boy; for if thou strike him with the rod, he shall not die ». — They have *amicability;* Job, the twenty-ninth chapter[^p459-9]: « The Almighty was with me, and my servants round about me »; and above, the eleventh chapter: « Trouble me not, for my boys are with me in bed ». — They have *tractability;* the first of Kings, the second chapter[^p459-10]: « Samuel ministered before the Lord, a boy girded with a linen ephod »; and of Joseph, Genesis, the thirty-seventh chapter: « Joseph fed the flock with his brethren, being yet a boy ». — They have *veracity;* Daniel, the thirteenth chapter[^p459-11]: « The Lord raised up the holy spirit of a younger boy, and he said: I am clean from the blood of this one »; Jeremias, the first chapter: « I know not how to speak, for I am a boy. And he said: Say not that thou art a boy »; and above, the first chapter: « Thou boy, Prophet » etc. — They have also *humility;* Mark, the seventh chapter[^p459-12]: « The whelps eat under the table of the crumbs of the boys »; and by reason of this most of all they please God, and are brought in here as an example; and Matthew, the eighteenth chapter: « He that shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me »; because « the Lord is high, and looketh on the low ». Therefore Matthew, the twelfth chapter: « Behold my chosen servant, whom I have chosen » etc. — Of such, then, that is, of those having *purity* in the flesh, *simplicity* in intention, *docility* in knowledge[^p459-13], *amicability* in affection, *tractability* in operation, *veracity* in speech and *humility* in conduct, is the kingdom of heaven. And by reason of this *humility* Abraham is called *puer* in Scripture; the Psalm[^p459-14]: « He was mindful of his testament, which he had towards Abraham his servant »; and above, the first chapter, he likewise calls Israel *puer:* « He hath received Israel his child ». But Christ is called *puer* in Ecclesiastes, the fourth chapter: « Better is a boy that is poor and wise than a king that is old and foolish » etc. And by reason of *all the things aforesaid,* and most of all by reason of *humility,* it is said: *Of such is the kingdom of heaven.* On account of which above, the first chapter[^p459-15]: « He hath put down the mighty from their seat, and hath exalted the humble »; and Job, the twenty-second chapter: « He that hath been humbled shall be in glory; and he that shall bow down his eyes, he shall be saved ».
<!-- page 460 -->

32. (Vers. 17.). Fifth, as to *the severe reprobation of the proud* it is inferred: *Amen I say to you: Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as*[^p460-1] *a boy, shall not enter into it;* as if he were to say: he who refuses to be humbled on account of the pride of his heart shall not enter into that kingdom, by the sentence of the truth of God. Whence Matthew, the eighteenth chapter[^p460-2]: « Unless you be converted and become as little children, you shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven ». For he who wills to enter into the kingdom must be as a boy, that is, pure and little. But *the kingdom of heaven* is said to be *sacred doctrine,* to which he who wills to come must make himself as a boy. Whence the Gloss[^p460-3]: « *The kingdom of heaven,* that is, the doctrine of the Gospel, we are bidden to receive as a boy. A boy in learning does not contradict his teachers, does not compose reasons and words for resisting, but faithfully receives, obeys and rests ». Therefore it is said in Matthew, the eleventh chapter[^p460-4]: « Thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them to little ones »; and Daniel, the first chapter: « And to these boys God gave knowledge and discipline in every book » etc.

33. *The kingdom of heaven* is also said to be *grace,* of which above, the seventeenth chapter[^p460-5]: « The kingdom of God is within you ». And this is not given except to boys, through cleanness and humility; James, the fourth chapter: « God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace to the humble ». — *The kingdom of heaven* is none the less said to be *eternal glory;* Matthew, the thirteenth chapter[^p460-6]: « The just shall shine as the sun in the kingdom of their Father ». And this is not given except to *little ones,* according to that of Matthew, the fifth chapter: « Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven »; and also to *boys,* who are pure; Matthew, the fifth chapter: « Blessed are the clean of heart, for they shall see God ». Whence the Gloss[^p460-7]: « As a boy does not persevere in anger, when hurt does not remember, does not lust after a woman, does not think or speak evil; so you, unless you have such innocence and purity, will not enter into the kingdom of heaven ». He therefore who refuses the littleness of humility and the purity of holiness despises the merit of eternal life; and therefore he must be reprobated by the divine judgement, so that he may not enter into the kingdom of heaven, but be driven far outside; the Apocalypse, the last chapter[^p460-8]: « Without are dogs and sorcerers and the unchaste and the servers of idols ». On account of which in the Psalm: « The king is not saved by a great army, and the giant shall not be saved by the multitude of his strength »; therefore Judith, the ninth chapter[^p460-9]: « The proud have not been acceptable to thee from the beginning, but the prayer of the humble and the meek hath always pleased thee ». He, then, who does not wish to be damned must needs be humbled; therefore Ecclesiasticus, the seventh chapter: « Humble thy spirit very much, for the vengeance on the flesh of the ungodly is fire and worms ».
