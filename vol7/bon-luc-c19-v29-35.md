# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XIX, Vers. 29-35
## *Potestatem regalem promissam se habere, ostendit per tria*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 487 -->

#### Incredulitas Iudaeorum confutatur tum per facta tum per verba et documenta.

43. *Et factum est, cum appropinquasset Iesus*[^p487-5] etc. Postquam confutavit Iudaeorum dolositatem, impietatem et curiositatem, hic sequitur quarta pars principalis, in qua *confutat incredulitatem*[^p487-6], quae in cordibus Iudaeorum fuit causa et radix omnium praedictorum. Dividitur autem haec pars in duas, in quarum prima confutat incredulitatem Iudaeorum *per facta;* in secunda vero, *per verba et documenta,* infra vigesimo[^p487-7]: *Et factum est in una dierum* etc.

#### Primo per facta confutat horum incredulitatem ostendendo, se habere quatuor dona in Lege promissa.

*Confutat* ergo *incredulitatem Iudaeorum per facta,* per quae ostendit se Christum Dominum in Lege promissum primo quoad *potestatem regalem;* secundo, quoad *pietatem pontificalem,* ibi[^p487-8]: *Et ut appropinquavit, videns civitatem* etc.; tertio quoad *aequitatem iudicialem,* ibi: *Et ingressus in templum* etc.; quarto quoad *auctoritatem magistralem,* ibi: *Et erat docens quotidie in templo* etc.

#### Potestatem regalem promissam se habere, ostendit per tria.

*Potestatem* autem *regalem* ostendit se Christus habere tripliciter: primo per *excellentiam in imperando;* secundo, per *discipulorum obedientiam in obsequendo,* ibi[^p487-9]: *Abierunt autem qui missi fuerant;* tertio, *per populorum reverentiam in laudando,* ibi: *Exeunte autem illo* etc.

*Excellentia* igitur regiae potestatis in Christo ostenditur *per imperium* ipsius *pium, providum* et *potestativum.* Tale namque imperium decet regem clementissimum, sapientissimum et potentissimum. Ostenditur autem imperium eius *pium* secundum mysterium gratiae[^p487-10] et *providum* secundum spiritum prophetiae et *potestativum* per culmen auctoritatis praecelsae.

44. (Vers. 29.). Primo igitur quantum ad *imperium pium per mysterium gratiae* dicitur: *Et factum est, cum appropinquasset Iesus ad*[^p487-11] *Bethphage et Bethaniam, ad montem qui vocatur Oliveti;* quod etsi verum fuerit *ad litteram,* hoc aliud tamen praetendebat *per figuram* et *mysticam intelligentiam.* Unde Ambrosius[^p487-12]: « Venit ad montem olivarum, ut novellas olivas in sublimi virtute plantaret ». Et nota, quod tria dicit, scilicet *Bethphage,* quae est in monte Oliveti et viculus sacerdotum, ut dicit Hieronymus[^p487-13], et interpretatur *domus buccae,* per quam confessio et poenitentia significantur; *Bethania,* domus obedientiae; et *mons Oliveti* propter
<!-- page 488 -->
unctionem olei, quae insinuat eminentiam unctionis et sapientiae. Et per haec tria Dominus *appropinquat nobis,* id est, facit appropinquare sibi, scilicet per *poenitentiam;* supra decimo quinto[^p488-1]: « Erant appropinquantes ad Iesum publicani » etc.; per *obedientiam;* Ecclesiastici ultimo: « Appropinquate ad me indocti et congregamini in domum disciplinae ». Haec est domus obedientiae seu religionis; per *sapientiam;* Deuteronomii trigesimo tertio: « Qui appropinquant pedibus eius accipient de doctrina eius ». — Pedes autem Domini venientis, ascendentis et iudicantis steterunt super *montem Olivarum,* quia qui vult vias eius videre, quae sunt misericordia et veritas[^p488-2], indiget *unctione,* per quam in eminentiam sapientiae elevetur, ut per contemplationem conscendat in caelum per tres praedictos gradus. Primus gradus est *incipientium,* secundus *proficientium,* tertius *perfectorum;* et ad hoc omnis Christi operatio et evangelica praedicatio ordinatur, scilicet ut sic tripliciter Christo appropinquemus.

45. (Vers. 29. 30.). Et ideo subdit: *Misit duos discipulos suos, dicens: Ite in castellum, quod contra vos*[^p488-3] *est.* Per *duos discipulos missos* intelligitur universitas discipulorum missorum ad convertendum mundum et maxime populum gentium. Unde Chrysostomus[^p488-4] dicit, quod isti duo discipuli fuerunt Petrus et Philippus, qui duo primo praedicaverunt gentibus: Philippus in Samaria, Actuum octavo; et Petrus in Caesarea, Actuum decimo. Ideo autem sunt *duo,* ad commendandum nexum caritatis, seu propter duplicem ordinem praedicantium, secundum dualitatem populorum convertendorum. Unde Glossa[^p488-5]: « Duos mittit propter scientiam veritatis et munditiam operis, vel geminae dilectionis sacramentum toto orbe praedicandum, vel propter duos ordines praedicatorum, unum in circumcisione et alterum in gentibus ». In cuius etiam designationem dicitur supra decimo[^p488-6]: « Misit eos binos ante faciem suam »; unde et Ecclesiastae quarto: « Melius est, duos esse simul quam unum ». — Per *castellum* illud, quod est contra Christi discipulos, intelligitur mundus, de quo Ioannis decimo quinto[^p488-7]: « Si mundus vos odit, scitote, quia me priorem vobis odio habuit »; et tamen in hunc misit discipulos ad praedicandum. Unde Glossa: « Mittit discipulos ad praedicandum in *castellum,* quod est *contra eos,* ut per eos totius contra se positi orbis munitiones penetret ». In hoc igitur praesignatur discipulorum missio ad convertendum mundum, quae facta est post passionem et resurrectionem, secundum illud Marci ultimo[^p488-8]: « Dixit eis: Euntes in mundum universum, praedicate Evangelium omni creaturae ». — Hoc igitur fuit imperium pium, et hoc designat appropinquatio ad montem Oliveti, de quo Christus ascendit in caelum.

46. Secundo quoad *mandatum providum per spiritum prophetiae* subdit: *In quod introeuntes, invenietis pullum asinae alligatum,* quod quidem dicere non poterat certitudinaliter, nisi quia habebat praescientiam futurorum; verumtamen et hoc ipsum sine mysterio esse non potest, quod Rex caeli et terrae quaerit *pullum* et non quemcumque, sed indomitum. — Unde subdit: *In quo*[^p488-9] *nemo unquam hominum sedit;* nec qualitercumque, sed *alligatum;* propter quod addit: *Solvite illum et adducite mihi.* Ex quo manifeste apparet, quod hoc sine mysterio esse non potest. Propter quod intelligendum, quod sicut per *asinam* intelligitur synagoga, ita per *pullum* gentilitas designatur. Unde Glossa[^p488-10]: « Tres Evangelistae, qui Graece scripserunt, *pullum* tantum commemorant; Matthaeus, qui Hebraice scripsit, et *asinam* solutam et adductam refert, ut Hebraicae quoque gentis, si poenituerit, non desperandam monstret esse salutem ». Unde Matthaei vigesimo primo[^p488-11]: « Invenietis asinam alligatam et pullum cum
<!-- page 489 -->
ea ». Dicitur autem populus gentilis *pullus asininus* propter stoliditatem ignorantiae; Iob undecimo[^p489-1]: « Vir vanus in superbiam erigitur et tanquam pullum onagri liberum se natum putat »; sed *alligatus* dicitur funibus peccatorum, secundum illud Proverbiorum quinto: « Funibus peccatorum quisque constringitur ». Et his funibus non tantum populus gentium ligatus erat, verum etiam synagoga; unde supra decimo tertio: « Hanc autem filiam Abrahae, quam alligavit satanas, ecce, iam decem et octo annis, nonne oportuit solvi a vinculo »? Et dicitur etiam esse *indomitus,* quia carebat omni regimine disciplinae Dei. Unde Glossa[^p489-2]: « Introeuntes in mundum, praedicatores invenerunt populum nationum perfidiae vinculis irretitum, liberum et lascivum, *cui nemo unquam hominum insederat,* id est, nullus rationalis doctor frenum correctionis imposuit ». De hoc ergo mandat discipulis, ut solvant, secundum illud Matthaei decimo octavo[^p489-3]: « Amen dico vobis: Quaecumque solveritis super terram, erunt soluta et in caelo ». Hoc autem non est virtute humana, sed divina; propter quod Iob trigesimo nono: « Quis dimisit onagrum liberum, et vincula eius quis solvit »? Et tunc qui solvitur ad Iesum adducitur; Isaiae sexagesimo[^p489-4]: « Aperientur portae tuae iugiter, ut afferatur ad te fortitudo gentium, et reges earum adducantur ».

47. (Vers. 31.). Tertio quoad *mandatum potestativum per culmen auctoritatis praecelsae* subditur: *Et si quis vos interrogaverit: Quare solvitis?* scilicet contrectantes rem alienam; *sic dicetis ei: Quia Dominus operam eius desiderat,* ut eo utatur tanquam re propria. Hic *ad litteram* ostendit, quod Christus dominus erat omnium, quia, qua ratione dominus erat asini, et universorum. Et ut dominum se significet omnium, generaliter se ipsum vocat *dominum.* Unde ipse dicit de se Ioannis decimo tertio[^p489-5]: « Vos vocatis me *magister* et *domine,* et bene dicitis; sum etenim ». Nam « ipse est, qui habet in vestimento et in femore suo scriptum: Rex regum et Dominus dominantium ». Unde et sibi dicitur illud Esther decimo tertio: « Domine Deus omnipotens, in ditione tua cuncta sunt posita, et non est qui tuae resistere possit voluntati ». « Dominus universorum tu es ». — Secundum *mysterium* autem haec debet esse responsio praedicantium et absolventium, qui non faciunt hoc ad aliud, nisi ut ad Christum trahant tanquam ad verum Dominum, qui habet desiderium animarum. Nam ipse dicit Proverbiorum octavo[^p489-6]: « Deliciae meae esse cum filiis hominum », Ipse enim desiderat principari cordi, quia ipse solus est Dominus. Unde etsi diabolus ligare permittatur per peccata, non tamen vindicat sibi super rationalem spiritum dominium verum. Et hoc est quod dicit Ambrosius[^p489-7]: « Dominatum inquit, sibi vindicare non poterat, quem *dominum* fecerat, non natura, sed culpa. Et ideo cum *dominus* dicitur, unus agnoscitur. Nam etsi *multi dii* et *multi domini,* generaliter tamen *unus Deus* et *unus Dominus* »; unde primae ad Corinthios octavo[^p489-8]: « Nam etsi sunt qui dicantur dii sive in caelo, sive in terra; siquidem sunt dii multi et domini multi; nobis tamen unus est Deus et unus Dominus Iesus ». Hic Dominus nullo indiget, secundum illud Psalmi[^p489-9]: « Dixi Domino: Deus meus es tu, quoniam bonorum meorum non eges »; et tamen desiderat operam nostrae salutis; primae ad Timotheum secundo: « Vult, omnes homines salvos fieri et ad agnitionem veritatis venire ». « Haec enim est voluntas Dei, sanctificatio vestra ».

48. (Vers. 32.). *Abierunt autem qui missi fuerant*[^p489-10] etc. Postquam manifestavit regiam potestatem in Christo per excellentiam in imperando, hic secundo[^p489-11] manifestat eam per *discipulorum obedientiam in obsequendo.* Circa quod explicandum ostenditur in discipulis perfectio obedientiae in *eundo, exsequendo* et *redeundo.*

Primo igitur quantum ad *obedientiam discipulorum per praeceptum euntium* dicitur: *Abierunt autem qui missi fuerant, et invenerunt, sicut dixit illis, stantem pullum.* In quo apparet, quod qui Christo obtemperat, decipi non potest, quia ipse sic praecipit praesentia, quod praescit futura. Unde ad Hebraeos quarto[^p489-12]: « Omnia nuda et aperta sunt oculis eius, ad quem nobis sermo ». — Secundum *mysterium* autem significationis discipuli *invenerunt*
<!-- page 490 -->
*pullum stantem,* quia invenerunt gentilem populum otiosum, secundum illud Matthaei vigesimo[^p490-1]: « Vidit alios stantes in foro otiosos et ait illis » etc. — Statum autem et modum standi expressius dicit Marcus, undecimo[^p490-2], ubi dicitur, quod « abeuntes invenerunt pullum ligatum ante ianuam foris in bivio ». Nam gentes, antequam ad Christum venirent, *foris* erant; unde primae ad Corinthios quinto: « Quid enim mihi de his qui foris sunt, iudicare »? « Nam de his qui foris sunt, Deus iudicabit ». Unde Glossa[^p490-3]: « Marcus ait: *Ante ianuam ligatum,* quia, extra Christum quicumque est, foris in via est; qui est in Christo foris non est ». Ideo Ioannis decimo quinto: « Si quis in me non manserit, mittetur foras et arescet ». Ideo autem dicitur esse *in bivio,* quia non sequebatur aliquam certam viam; unde Glossa[^p490-4]: « *In bivio ligatus* stabat qui non unam fidei vitaeque viam tenebat, sed pluribus erroribus deserviebat »; ideo ad Ephesios quarto[^p490-5]: « Testificor coram Deo, ut iam non ambuletis, sicut gentes ambulant in vanitate sensus sui, tenebris obscuratum habentes intellectum, abalienati a via Dei per ignorantiam, quae est in illis ». In hoc ergo invenerunt discipuli populum gentium, qui per diversas sectas et vias idololatrabant, secundum illud Ieremiae tertio[^p490-6]: « In viis sedebas, quasi latro exspectans eos, et polluisti terram in fornicationibus tuis ». Ideo recte designatur per Thamar, de qua dicitur Genesis trigesimo octavo: « Mutato habitu, sedit in bivio »; Iacobi primo: « Vir duplex animo inconstans est in omnibus viis suis ».

49. (Vers. 33.). Secundo quantum ad *obedientiam discipulorum praeceptum exsequentium* subditur: *Solventibus autem illis pullum, dixerunt domini eius ad illos: Quid solvitis pullum?* per contrectationem rei alienae; qui dicuntur *plures domini,* quia secundum veritatem historiae tam asina quam pullus dicitur fuisse totius illius castelli, quo utebantur pauperes ad afferendum aquam[^p490-7].

50. (Vers. 34.). *At illi dixerunt: Quia Dominus eum necessarium habet,* per dominium rei propriae. Unde ex hoc apparet *virtus divini praecepti,* quia nec amplius quaesierunt nec restiterunt. Unde Chrysostomus[^p490-8]: « Virtus Christi praeparavit eorum cor ad dandum ignotis ». Ex hoc etiam apparet, *virtus obedientiae* quantae sit efficaciae, quia ei qui obedit, nullus dominus valet contradicere; unde et Proverbiorum vigesimo primo[^p490-9]: « Vir obediens loquetur victorias ».

Et nota hic secundum *mysterium,* quod plurimi dicuntur populi gentilis *domini,* quia servus erat ille populus non unius Domini et Dei, sed multorum vitiorum et daemonum; quia, secundae Petri secundo[^p490-10], « a quo quis superatus est, huius et servus est ». Unde Glossa: « Multos habebat dominos qui non uni superstitioni deditus, sed pro libitu immundorum spirituum ad varios raptabatur errores ». Ideo Isaiae vigesimo sexto[^p490-11]: « Domine Deus noster, domini possederunt nos absque te ». Hi resistunt Apostolis volentibus solvere pullum, quia tam daemones quam peccata quam etiam diaboli membra resistunt et obstaculum ponunt gratiae, sicut Simon magus Petro, et Elymas magus Paulo, Actuum decimo tertio: « Resistebat autem illis Elymas magus, quaerens avertere proconsulem a fide ». Sed audito Christi nomine, et eius virtute per miracula patefacta, obmutescunt, quia omnes tales in Christi nomine superantur. Unde Glossa[^p490-12]: « Qui doctoribus ad salutem gentium venientibus obstiterunt suas tenebras defendunt, donec, miraculis attestantibus, veri possessoris virtus innotescat ». Unde et Petrus cum Apostolis dixit his qui prohibebant eos praedicare ad pulli solutionem, Actuum quinto[^p490-13]: « Obedire oportet Deo magis quam hominibus »; et hoc, quia ipse est solus verus Dominus et personarum nostrarum et rerum; Psalmus: « Quoniam meae sunt omnes ferae silvarum, iumenta in montibus et boves ».

<!-- page 491 -->
51. (Vers. 35.). Tertio quantum ad *discipulorum obedientiam secundum praeceptum redeuntium* subiungitur: *Et duxerunt illum ad Iesum,* ut adimplerent Christi mandatum, quibus supra[^p491-1] dixerat: « Solvite et adducite mihi ». — *Et iactantes vestimenta sua supra pullum, imposuerunt Iesum,* ut ostenderent, Christum esse regem promissum. Unde Matthaei vigesimo primo[^p491-2]: « Hoc totum factum est, ut adimpleretur quod dictum est per Prophetam dicentem: Dicite filiae Sion: Ecce, rex tuus venit tibi mansuetus, sedens super asinam et pullum filium subiugalis ». Sic enim habetur Zachariae nono: « Exsulta satis, filia Sion, iubila, filia Ierusalem: Ecce, rex tuus veniet tibi iustus et salvator: ipse pauper et ascendens super asinam et super pullum filium asinae ». Ex hoc ergo manifesto indicio et praehabito verbo[^p491-3] colligebatur, quod Christus erat verus rex Iudaeorum.

52. Sed hoc est mirabile, quod Christus, cum esset verus Deus et « Rex regum[^p491-4] », ascendere voluit super animal tam despectum. — Sed tamen hoc valde rationabiliter fecit et propter *mysterium* et propter *exemplum.* Propter *mysterium,* inquam, quia, sicut tactum est[^p491-5], per *pullum* intelligitur populus gentilis, per *asinam* Iudaicus. Et ideo, licet alter deberet sibi sufficere, tamen super utrumque ascendit, primum super pullum et deinde super asinam, ut ostenderet, se primo vocaturum populum gentium, deinde in fine mundi populum Iudaeorum. Unde et in illa nobilissima prophetia Iacob, Genesis quadragesimo nono[^p491-6], dicitur: « Non auferetur sceptrum de Iuda nec dux de femore eius, donec veniat qui mittendus est; et ipse erit exspectatio gentium, ligans ad vineam pullum suum et ad vitem, o fili mi, asinam suam. Lavabit in vino stolam suam et in sanguine uvae pallium suum »; hoc fuit in torculari crucis. Quoniam ergo hunc pullum, scilicet populum gentium, Christus ad se duci fecit et super eum ascendit per ministerium Apostolorum; ideo dicitur, quod discipuli *iactaverunt vestimenta sua,* id est exempla bona, per quae converterunt gentem illam; de quibus vestimentis, Apocalypsis decimo sexto[^p491-7]: « Beatus, qui custodit vestimenta sua, ne nudus ambulet ». In cuius figuram etiam dicitur tertii Regum decimo nono, quod « Elias misit pallium suum super Eliseum ». Quoniam igitur ista conversio facta est non solum per Apostolos, verum etiam per septuaginta discipulos, quibus deputatae sunt particulares Ecclesiae gentium conversarum; ideo in huius figuram dicitur Iudicum duodecimo[^p491-8] de iudice quodam, qui « habuit quadraginta filios et triginta ex eis nepotes, ascendentes super septuaginta pullos asinarum ». Et de hoc Glossa[^p491-9]: « *Vestimenta* Apostolorum doctrina virtutum, vel discretio Scripturarum et gratia spiritualis, quibus illi corda hominum prius nuda et frigida contegunt, ut mereantur habere sessorem Christum ». — Fecit etiam hoc propter *exemplum* ad conterendum supercilium superborum, qui delectantur in multitudine equorum et curruum; Psalmus[^p491-10]: « Hi in curribus, et hi in equis » etc.; et Isaiae trigesimo primo: « Vae! qui descendunt in Aegyptum ad auxilium, in equis sperantes et habentes fiduciam super quadrigis, quia multae sunt » etc. Quod exemplo suo Christus condemnans, non multitudinem equorum habuit, immo nec unum equum, sed super contemptibilem pullum ascendit in die praecipui honoris sui. Unde Chrysostomus[^p491-11]: « Vis cognoscere mansuetudinem venientis? Considera speciem adventus eius. Non sedet in curru aureo, purpura fulgens; non ascendit super fervidum equum, discordiae amatorem; non habuit in circuitu splendentes gladios aut cetera ornamenta terribilium armorum, sed quid? Ramos frondentes testimonia pietatis ». Et sic ostendit, verum esse quod in Psalmo[^p491-12] dicitur: « Non in fortitudine equi voluntatem habebit nec in tibiis » etc.

## English
<!-- page 487 -->

#### The unbelief of the Jews is confuted both by deeds and by words and lessons.

43. *And it came to pass, when Jesus was come nigh*[^p487-5] etc. After he confuted the guile, the impiety and the curiosity of the Jews, here follows the fourth principal part, in which *he confutes unbelief*[^p487-6], which in the hearts of the Jews was the cause and root of all the aforesaid. Now this part is divided into two, in the first of which he confutes the unbelief of the Jews *by deeds;* in the second, *by words and lessons,* below, the twentieth chapter[^p487-7]: *And it came to pass on one of the days* etc.

#### First he confutes their unbelief by deeds, showing that he has the four gifts promised in the Law.

He *confutes,* then, *the unbelief of the Jews by deeds,* by which he shows himself to be the Christ, the Lord promised in the Law, first as to *the royal power;* second, as to *the pontifical piety*[^tr-pietas], there[^p487-8]: *And when he drew near, seeing the city* etc.; third as to *the judicial equity,* there: *And entering into the temple* etc.; fourth as to *the magisterial authority,* there: *And he was teaching daily in the temple* etc.

#### That he has the royal power promised, he shows through three things.

Now Christ shows that he has the *royal power* in three ways: first through *excellence in commanding;* second, through *the obedience of the disciples in complying,* there[^p487-9]: *And they that were sent went their way;* third, *through the reverence of the peoples in praising,* there: *And as he went forth* etc.

The *excellence,* then, of the royal power in Christ is shown *through* his *command,* which is *pious, provident* and *powerful.* For such a command befits a king most clement, most wise and most powerful. Now his command is shown to be *pious* according to the mystery of grace[^p487-10], and *provident* according to the spirit of prophecy, and *powerful* through the summit of most lofty authority.

44. (Vers. 29.). First, then, as to *the pious command through the mystery of grace* it is said: *And it came to pass, when Jesus was come nigh to*[^p487-11] *Bethphage and Bethania, to the mount which is called Olivet;* which, although it was true *according to the letter,* yet held out something else *by figure* and *mystical intelligence.* Whence Ambrose[^p487-12]: « He came to the mount of olives, that he might plant young olives in sublime virtue ». And note that he says three things, namely *Bethphage,* which is on mount Olivet and a little village of priests, as Jerome says[^p487-13], and is interpreted *house of the cheek,* by which confession and penance are signified; *Bethania,* house of obedience; and *mount Olivet* because of
<!-- page 488 -->
the anointing of oil, which intimates the eminence of anointing and of wisdom. And through these three the Lord *draws near to us,* that is, makes us draw near to himself, namely through *penance;* above, the fifteenth chapter[^p488-1]: « The publicans were drawing near unto Jesus » etc.; through *obedience;* Ecclesiasticus, the last chapter: « Draw near to me, ye unlearned, and gather yourselves together into the house of discipline ». This is the house of obedience or of religion; through *wisdom;* Deuteronomy, the thirty-third chapter: « They that draw near to his feet shall receive of his doctrine ». — Now the feet of the Lord coming, ascending and judging stood upon *the mount of Olives,* because he who wills to see his ways, which are mercy and truth[^p488-2], needs *anointing,* through which he may be raised into the eminence of wisdom, so that through contemplation he may ascend into heaven by the three aforesaid steps. The first step is *of beginners,* the second *of those making progress,* the third *of the perfect;* and to this every operation of Christ and the evangelical preaching is ordained, namely that we may thus draw near to Christ in three ways.

45. (Vers. 29. 30.). And therefore he subjoins: *He sent two of his disciples, saying: Go into the town which is over against you*[^p488-3]. By *the two disciples sent* is understood the whole body of the disciples sent to convert the world, and above all the people of the Gentiles. Whence Chrysostom[^p488-4] says that these two disciples were Peter and Philip, who two first preached to the Gentiles: Philip in Samaria, Acts, the eighth chapter; and Peter in Caesarea, Acts, the tenth chapter. Now they are *two* for this reason, to commend the bond of charity, or because of the twofold order of preachers, according to the duality of the peoples to be converted. Whence the Gloss[^p488-5]: « He sends two because of the knowledge of truth and the cleanness of work, or the sacrament of the twofold dilection to be preached in the whole world, or because of the two orders of preachers, one in the circumcision and the other among the Gentiles ». In designation of which it is also said above, the tenth chapter[^p488-6]: « He sent them two and two before his face »; whence also Ecclesiastes, the fourth chapter: « It is better that two should be together than one ». — By that *town* which is over against the disciples of Christ is understood the world, of which John, the fifteenth chapter[^p488-7]: « If the world hate you, know ye, that it hath hated me before you »; and yet into it he sent the disciples to preach. Whence the Gloss: « He sends the disciples to preach into the *town* which is *over against them,* that through them he may penetrate the fortifications of the whole world set against him ». In this, then, is foreshown the sending of the disciples to convert the world, which was done after the passion and resurrection, according to that of Mark, the last chapter[^p488-8]: « He said to them: Going into the whole world, preach the gospel to every creature ». — This, then, was the pious command, and the drawing near to mount Olivet designates this, from which Christ ascended into heaven.

46. Second, as to *the provident commandment through the spirit of prophecy* he subjoins: *At your entering into which you shall find the colt of an ass tied,* which indeed he could not say with certitude, except because he had foreknowledge of things to come; yet this very thing too cannot be without mystery, that the King of heaven and earth seeks *a colt,* and not any whatever, but an untamed one. — Whence he subjoins: *On which*[^p488-9] *no man ever hath sitten;* nor in any condition whatever, but *tied;* on account of which he adds: *Loose him, and bring him to me.* From which it plainly appears that this cannot be without mystery. On account of which it is to be understood that, as by *the ass* the synagogue is understood, so by *the colt* the gentility is designated. Whence the Gloss[^p488-10]: « The three Evangelists, who wrote in Greek, mention only *the colt;* Matthew, who wrote in Hebrew, relates both *the ass* loosed and brought, that he might show that the salvation of the Hebrew nation too, if it should do penance, is not to be despaired of ». Whence Matthew, the twenty-first chapter[^p488-11]: « You shall find an ass tied, and a colt with
<!-- page 489 -->
her ». Now the gentile people is called *an ass's colt* because of the stolidity of ignorance; Job, the eleventh chapter[^p489-1]: « A vain man is lifted up into pride, and thinketh himself born free like a wild ass's colt »; but it is said to be *tied* with the ropes of sins, according to that of Proverbs, the fifth chapter: « Every one is fast bound with the ropes of sins ». And with these ropes not only the people of the Gentiles was bound, but also the synagogue; whence above, the thirteenth chapter: « And this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years, ought she not to be loosed from the bond »? And it is said also to be *untamed,* because it lacked all governance of the discipline of God. Whence the Gloss[^p489-2]: « Entering into the world, the preachers found the people of the nations ensnared in the bonds of perfidy, free and wanton, *on which no man ever had sat,* that is, no rational teacher had put on it the bridle of correction ». Of this, then, he commands the disciples that they loose it, according to that of Matthew, the eighteenth chapter[^p489-3]: « Amen I say to you: Whatsoever you shall loose upon earth, shall be loosed also in heaven ». But this is not by human virtue, but divine; on account of which Job, the thirty-ninth chapter: « Who hath sent out the wild ass free, and who hath loosed his bonds »? And then he who is loosed is brought to Jesus; Isaias, the sixtieth chapter[^p489-4]: « Thy gates shall be open continually, that the strength of the Gentiles may be brought to thee, and their kings may be brought ».

47. (Vers. 31.). Third, as to *the powerful commandment through the summit of most lofty authority* it is subjoined: *And if any man shall ask you: Why do you loose him?* namely, handling a thing that is another's; *you shall say thus unto him: Because the Lord desireth his service,* that he may use it as a thing of his own. Here *according to the letter* he shows that Christ was lord of all, because by the same reason by which he was lord of the ass, he was lord also of all things. And that he may signify that he is lord of all, he calls himself generally *the Lord.* Whence he himself says of himself in John, the thirteenth chapter[^p489-5]: « You call me *Master,* and *Lord;* and you say well, for so I am ». For « he it is who hath on his garment and on his thigh written: King of kings and Lord of lords ». Whence also that of Esther, the thirteenth chapter, is said to him: « O Lord God almighty, all things are set in thy power, and there is none that can resist thy will ». « Thou art Lord of all ». — But according to the *mystery* this ought to be the answer of those who preach and absolve, who do this for nothing else than that they may draw to Christ as to the true Lord, who has desire of souls. For he himself says in Proverbs, the eighth chapter[^p489-6]: « My delights were to be with the children of men », For he desires to rule over the heart, because he alone is Lord. Whence, although the devil be permitted to bind through sins, yet he does not claim for himself true dominion over the rational spirit. And this is what Ambrose says[^p489-7]: « He could not, he says, claim lordship for himself, whom not nature, but fault had made *lord.* And therefore when *Lord* is said, one is acknowledged. For although there be *many gods* and *many lords,* yet generally there is *one God* and *one Lord* »; whence the first to the Corinthians, the eighth chapter[^p489-8]: « For although there be that are called gods, either in heaven or on earth; for there be gods many and lords many; yet to us there is one God and one Lord Jesus ». This Lord needs nothing, according to that of the Psalm[^p489-9]: « I have said to the Lord: Thou art my God, for thou hast no need of my goods »; and yet he desires the service of our salvation; the first to Timothy, the second chapter: « He will have all men to be saved, and to come to the knowledge of the truth ». « For this is the will of God, your sanctification ».

48. (Vers. 32.). *And they that were sent went their way*[^p489-10] etc. After he made manifest the royal power in Christ through his excellence in commanding, here in the second place[^p489-11] he makes it manifest through *the obedience of the disciples in complying.* In explaining which there is shown in the disciples the perfection of obedience in *going, executing* and *returning.*

First, then, as to *the obedience of the disciples going by the precept* it is said: *And they that were sent went their way, and found, as he had said unto them, the colt standing.* In which it appears that he who obeys Christ cannot be deceived, because he so commands things present that he foreknows things to come. Whence to the Hebrews, the fourth chapter[^p489-12]: « All things are naked and open to his eyes, to whom our speech is ». — But according to the *mystery* of the signification the disciples *found*
<!-- page 490 -->
*the colt standing,* because they found the gentile people idle, according to that of Matthew, the twentieth chapter[^p490-1]: « He saw others standing in the marketplace idle, and he said to them » etc. — Now the state and manner of standing Mark tells more expressly, in the eleventh chapter[^p490-2], where it is said that « going their way, they found the colt tied before the gate without, in the meeting of two ways ». For the Gentiles, before they came to Christ, were *without;* whence the first to the Corinthians, the fifth chapter: « For what have I to do to judge them that are without »? « For them that are without, God will judge ». Whence the Gloss[^p490-3]: « Mark says: *Tied before the gate,* because whoever is outside Christ is without, in the way; he who is in Christ is not without ». Therefore John, the fifteenth chapter: « If any one abide not in me, he shall be cast forth and shall wither ». But it is said to be *in the meeting of two ways* for this reason, because it did not follow any certain way; whence the Gloss[^p490-4]: « He stood *tied in the meeting of two ways* who held not the one way of faith and life, but was in bondage to many errors »; therefore to the Ephesians, the fourth chapter[^p490-5]: « I testify before God, that henceforward you walk not as also the Gentiles walk in the vanity of their mind, having their understanding darkened, alienated from the way of God through the ignorance that is in them ». In this, then, the disciples found the people of the Gentiles, who through divers sects and ways committed idolatry, according to that of Jeremias, the third chapter[^p490-6]: « Thou didst sit in the ways, as a robber waiting for them, and thou hast polluted the land with thy fornications ». Therefore it is rightly designated by Thamar, of whom it is said in Genesis, the thirty-eighth chapter: « Having changed her habit, she sat in the meeting of two ways »; James, the first chapter: « A double minded man is inconstant in all his ways ».

49. (Vers. 33.). Second, as to *the obedience of the disciples executing the precept* it is subjoined: *And as they were loosing the colt, the lords thereof said to them: Why loose you the colt?* by the handling of a thing that is another's; who are called *many lords,* because according to the truth of the history both the ass and the colt are said to have belonged to that whole town, which the poor used for fetching water[^p490-7].

50. (Vers. 34.). *But they said: Because the Lord hath need of him,* by the dominion of a thing of his own. Whence from this there appears *the virtue of the divine precept,* because they neither asked further nor resisted. Whence Chrysostom[^p490-8]: « The virtue of Christ prepared their heart to give to strangers ». From this it appears also of how great efficacy *the virtue of obedience* is, because no lord avails to gainsay him who obeys; whence also Proverbs, the twenty-first chapter[^p490-9]: « An obedient man shall speak of victories ».

And note here, according to the *mystery,* that very many are called *lords* of the gentile people, because that people was the servant not of one Lord and God, but of many vices and demons; because, the second of Peter, the second chapter[^p490-10], « by whom a man is overcome, of the same also he is the servant ». Whence the Gloss: « He had many lords who, devoted not to one superstition, but at the pleasure of unclean spirits, was swept away to various errors ». Therefore Isaias, the twenty-sixth chapter[^p490-11]: « O Lord our God, lords have possessed us without thee ». These resist the Apostles when they will to loose the colt, because demons as well as sins, as well as the members of the devil, resist and put an obstacle to grace, as Simon the magician to Peter, and Elymas the magician to Paul, Acts, the thirteenth chapter: « But Elymas the magician withstood them, seeking to turn away the proconsul from the faith ». But when the name of Christ has been heard, and his virtue laid open through miracles, they are struck dumb, because all such are overcome in the name of Christ. Whence the Gloss[^p490-12]: « They who withstood the teachers coming for the salvation of the Gentiles defend their own darkness, until, miracles bearing witness, the virtue of the true possessor becomes known ». Whence also Peter with the Apostles said to those who forbade them to preach for the loosing of the colt, Acts, the fifth chapter[^p490-13]: « We ought to obey God rather than men »; and this, because he alone is the true Lord both of our persons and of our things; the Psalm: « For all the beasts of the woods are mine, the cattle on the hills, and the oxen ».

<!-- page 491 -->
51. (Vers. 35.). Third, as to *the obedience of the disciples returning according to the precept* it is subjoined: *And they brought him to Jesus,* that they might fulfil the commandment of Christ, who had said to them above[^p491-1]: « Loose him, and bring him to me ». — *And casting their garments upon the colt, they set Jesus thereon,* that they might show that Christ was the king promised. Whence Matthew, the twenty-first chapter[^p491-2]: « Now all this was done that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Prophet, saying: Tell ye the daughter of Sion: Behold, thy king cometh to thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of her that is used to the yoke ». For thus it is had in Zacharias, the ninth chapter: « Exult greatly, O daughter of Sion, shout for joy, O daughter of Jerusalem: Behold, thy king will come to thee, the just and saviour: he is poor, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass ». From this manifest sign, then, and from the word had beforehand[^p491-3], it was gathered that Christ was the true king of the Jews.

52. But this is marvellous, that Christ, though he was true God and « King of kings[^p491-4] », willed to mount upon so despised an animal. — But yet he did this most reasonably, both on account of the *mystery* and on account of the *example.* On account of the *mystery,* I say, because, as was touched on[^p491-5], by *the colt* is understood the gentile people, by *the ass* the Jewish. And therefore, although the one ought to have sufficed him, yet he mounted upon both, first upon the colt and then upon the ass, that he might show that he would call first the people of the Gentiles, then at the end of the world the people of the Jews. Whence also in that most noble prophecy of Jacob, Genesis, the forty-ninth chapter[^p491-6], it is said: « The sceptre shall not be taken away from Juda, nor a ruler from his thigh, till he come that is to be sent, and he shall be the expectation of the Gentiles, tying his foal to the vineyard, and his ass, O my son, to the vine. He shall wash his robe in wine, and his garment in the blood of the grape »; this was in the winepress of the cross. Since, then, Christ caused this colt, namely the people of the Gentiles, to be led to himself, and mounted upon it through the ministry of the Apostles; therefore it is said that the disciples *cast their garments,* that is, good examples, through which they converted that nation; of which garments, Apocalypse, the sixteenth chapter[^p491-7]: « Blessed is he that keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked ». In figure of which it is also said in the third of Kings, the nineteenth chapter, that « Elias cast his mantle upon Eliseus ». Since, then, this conversion was made not only through the Apostles, but also through the seventy disciples, to whom the particular Churches of the converted Gentiles were assigned; therefore in figure of this it is said in Judges, the twelfth chapter[^p491-8], of a certain judge, who « had forty sons, and of them thirty grandsons, mounting upon seventy colts of asses ». And of this the Gloss[^p491-9]: « The *garments* of the Apostles are the doctrine of the virtues, or the discretion of the Scriptures and spiritual grace, with which they cover the hearts of men, before naked and cold, that they may merit to have Christ as their rider ». — He did this also on account of the *example,* to crush the haughtiness of the proud, who delight in a multitude of horses and chariots; the Psalm[^p491-10]: « Some in chariots, and some in horses » etc.; and Isaias, the thirty-first chapter: « Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help, trusting in horses, and putting their confidence in chariots, because they are many » etc. Which Christ condemning by his example had not a multitude of horses, nay, not even one horse, but mounted upon a contemptible colt on the day of his chief honour. Whence Chrysostom[^p491-11]: « Wilt thou know the meekness of him that cometh? Consider the appearance of his coming. He sits not in a golden chariot, shining with purple; he mounts not upon a fiery horse, a lover of discord; he had not round about him shining swords or the other ornaments of terrible arms, but what? Leafy branches, the testimonies of piety ». And thus he shows that what is said in the Psalm[^p491-12] is true: « He shall not delight in the strength of the horse, nor in the legs » etc.
