# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XI, Vers. 27-32
## *Factum est autem etc. Post expressionem fraudis Iudaicae et reprobationem fraudis expressae subdit hic Evangelista commendationem veritatis apertae*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 295 -->
58. (Vers. 27.). *Factum est autem* etc. Post expressionem fraudis Iudaicae et reprobationem fraudis expressae subdit hic Evangelista *commendationem veritatis apertae*[^p295-16]. Et hoc quidem conveniens erat, ut, manifestata veritate, Veritas ipsa se ipsam manifestans laudaretur manifeste coram tota multitudine. In descriptione autem huius commendationis tria introducuntur: primum est *conditio laudantis personae,* secundum est *expressio laudis divinae,* tertium est *approbatio laudis expressae.*
<!-- page 296 -->
Primo igitur quantum ad *conditionem laudantis personae* dicit: *Factum est autem, dum*[^p296-1] *haec diceret,* scilicet ad confutationem falsitatis; *extollens vocem quaedam mulier de turba dixit illi,* ad veritatis commendationem et Pharisaeorum confutationem; in quo duplex notatur conditio in persona laudante, scilicet *virilitas* et *modicitas. Virilitas* notatur in hoc, quod *vocem extollebat,* secundum illud Isaiae quadragesimo[^p296-2]: « Exalta in fortitudine vocem tuam, exalta, noli timere »; *modicitas* in hoc, quod *quaedam mulier,* non nominata nec nobilis, sed de turba, ut impleretur illud Psalmi: « Pauper et inops laudabunt nomen tuum ». Ex quo apparet, quod laudator divini nominis non debet esse timidus, ut laudare non audeat, nec tumidus[^p296-3], ut laudare erubescat, sed virilis et humilis. Unde cum quidam *tacerent* ex *timore,* quidam *blasphemarent* ex *superbia et tumore;* haec mulier humilis et virilis nec *tacuit* cum timidis nec *vituperavit* cum blasphemis; unde in Glossa[^p296-4]: « Magna fiducia inter blasphemos confitetur Dei Filium ». Ideo poterant sibi dicere illud Matthaei decimo quinto[^p296-5]: « O mulier, magna est fides tua », quae scilicet tantum te fortificavit, cum dicatur Proverbiorum ultimo: « Mulierem fortem quis inveniet »? In te verificatum est illud Ecclesiastici vigesimo sexto: « Fundamenta aeterna supra petram solidam, et mandata Dei in corde mulieris sanctae ».

59. Secundo vero quantum ad *expressionem laudis divinae* subditur: *Beatus venter, qui te portavit;* in quo laudat Christum Dei Filium, extollens laudis eius praeconium ex beatitudine Matris; quasi dicat: beata mulier, quae portavit tam bonum filium. Et recte, quia decebat sexum femineum sic laudare, nec tantum feminas, immo etiam viros. Nam supra primo[^p296-6] dixit Virgo: « Ex hoc beatam me dicent omnes generationes ».

60. Et nota, quod beatificat Virginis ventrem, et hoc, quia portavit Dei Filium novem mensibus et sex diebus, qui pro uno mense accipiuntur; Sapientiae septimo[^p296-7]: « In ventre matris meae factus sum caro decem mensium tempore ». Unde in laude Virginis dicitur Canticorum septimo: « Venter tuus sicut acervus tritici », quia Christus erat *granum frumenti,* secundum quod dicitur Ioannis duodecimo; et hoc tritico fuit venter eius impletus, et ideo *beatus* propter *tria privilegia:* quia, ut dicit Bernardus[^p296-8], « fuit sine corruptione fecundata, sine gravedine gravida, sine dolore puerpera »; Isaiae ultimo: « Quis audivit unquam tale? Et quis vidit huic simile »? — Sive propter *tria miracula.* Primum est coniunctio infinite distantium. Deus enim ibi factus est homo; Creator, creatura; immensus, parvus; Verbum, infans; aeternus, temporalis, secundum illud Ioannis primo[^p296-9]: « Et Verbum caro factum est »; et Ieremiae trigesimo primo: « Novum faciet Dominus super terram: femina circumdabit virum ». — Secundum miraculum est, quia qui fecit ventrem factus est in ventre; unde in Psalmo[^p296-10]: « Gloriosa dicta sunt de te, civitas Dei »; et post: « Homo natus est in ea, et ipse fundavit eam Altissimus ». De hoc potest exponi illud Ecclesiastici quadragesimo tertio: « Vas admirabile opus Excelsi ». — Tertium miraculum, quia qui continet omnia continetur in ventre isto; ille ibi capitur, « quem totus non capit orbis[^p296-11] ». Unde cantat Ecclesia: « Quia quem caeli capere non poterant tuo gremio contulisti »; unde Isaiae quadragesimo quinto: « Vere tu es Deus absconditus ».

61. Nec tantum beatificat Virginem propter portationem, sed *ubera* propter lactationem, cum subdit: *Et ubera, quae suxisti,* beata scilicet; ex quo datur intelligi, quod a solis uberibus Virginis beatissimae lactatus fuit. Quod designatum fuit in Moyse, qui
<!-- page 297 -->
lactari, ut dicitur, noluit a muliere Aegyptiaca; et ideo quaesita fuit mulier Hebraea, scilicet mater propria, sicut dicitur Exodi secundo[^p297-1]. Haec autem designat Virginem Mariam, cuius ubera suxit Christus, secundum illud Canticorum octavo: « Quis mihi det te fratrem meum sugentem ubera matris meae » etc. Ideo autem iunxit ista duo, ut ostenderetur Virgo Maria fuisse mater Christi vera et perfecta, quia non solum genuit, sed etiam educavit; et sicut vere educavit, ita vere genuit; et in hoc arguitur, ut dicitur in Glossa[^p297-2], impietas Manichaei et aliorum, qui dicunt, ipsum attulisse corpus aethereum. Unde Beda: « Ex eodem fonte et lac nutriendis et semen procreandis pueris manat. Ergo de semine Virginis, secundum physicos, potuit concipi qui de lacte eius potuit nutriri ».

62. (Vers. 28.). Tertio vero quantum ad *approbationem laudis expressae* subdit: *At ille dixit: Quinimmo beati, qui audiunt verbum Dei.* Hoc non dicit adversando, sed potius superaddendo, quasi dicat: non solum *beatus* venter, qui portavit me, Verbum caro factum, verum etiam *beatior* qui suscipit verbum a me prolatum. Unde et Maria non tantum *beata* fuit, quia Christum gestavit in carne, verum etiam *beatior,* quia perfectissime gestavit in mente, secundum quod dicit Augustinus[^p297-3]: « Beatior fuit Maria concipiendo fidem Christi quam carnem Christi ». Beatus est enim omnis, qui audit et obedit, secundum illud Ioannis decimo tertio: « Si haec scitis, beati eritis, si feceritis ea ». — Et ideo subdit: *Et custodiunt illud;* et ideo Iacobi primo[^p297-4]: « Estote factores verbi et non auditores tantum ». In quo verbo Christus noluit, laudari mere cognationem *carnalem* per se; nam ita dicitur Iudaeis supra tertio: « Nolite dicere: Patrem habemus Abraham » etc.; sed *spiritualem,* quia sanctior est copula mentium quam corporum. Et ideo, cum quaererent eum mater et fratres, Matthaei duodecimo[^p297-5] dixit: « Qui facit voluntatem Patris mei, qui in caelis est, ille frater meus, soror et mater est ». Et propter hoc Virgo Maria fuit laudabilis in concipiendo, quia *fide* concepit; et ideo dixit ei Elisabeth supra primo: « Beata, quae credidisti, quoniam perficientur quae dicta sunt tibi a Domino ». — Ex quo apparet mira commendatio veritatis, quae omnes sibi adhaerentes beatos efficit, non solum adhaerentes *carnali cognatione,* ut Virgo Maria, verum etiam *spirituali dilectione,* sicut quaelibet anima sancta. Ut enim dicit Augustinus[^p297-6]: « Beatitudo est gaudium de veritate »; ad quod gaudium venient qui veritatem audiunt, diligunt et faciunt, secundum illud Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto: « Qui audiunt me non confundentur, et qui operantur in me non peccabunt, et qui elucidant me vitam aeternam habebunt ».

63. *Spiritualiter* autem hic notandum est, quod *mulier de turba* typum gerit Legis, quae *generationem* commendat *carnalem,* secundum quod facta est repromissio Abrahae, Genesis decimo quinto[^p297-7]: « Suspice caelum » etc.; et David, unde in Psalmo: « De fructu » etc.; unde ad Romanos nono: « Ex quibus est Christus secundum carnem » etc. Sed *Christus* typum gerit gratiae et spiritus, qui quidem *generationem* commendat *spiritualem,* secundum illud Matthaei duodecimo[^p297-8]: « Quae est mater mea, et qui sunt fratres mei »? — Huius generationis *spiritualis conceptio* primo fit *in fide,* quasi in unitate Ecclesiae; Ioannis septimo[^p297-9]: « Qui credit in me, sicut dicit Scriptura » etc. In cuius etiam rei signum Virgo Maria per fidem concepit; Lucae primo: « Beata, quae credidisti ». — *Partus* autem fit *per operationem;* Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto[^p297-10]: « Dixi, rigabo hortum plantationum » etc. Sed illi qui credunt et non operantur, sunt sicut illi, de quibus Isaiae trigesimo septimo: « Venerunt filii usque ad partum, et virtus non est pariendi ». — *Lactatio* fit *in dilectione et contemplatione;* Canticorum primo[^p297-11]: « Osculetur me osculo, quia meliora sunt ubera tua vino » etc.; et Proverbiorum quinto: « Cerva carissima et gratissimus hinnulus » etc. — Et sic in his tribus insinuantur *susceptio gratiae, exercitatio activae* et *consolatio contemplativae.* Et hoc totum clauditur in « fide, quae per dilectionem operatur[^p297-12] », quam soli qui habent erunt beati.

#### Confutatur fallacia tentantium sive incredulorum triplici via.

64. (Vers. 29.). *Turbis autem concurrentibus* etc. Postquam Salvator confutavit fallaciam blasphematorum sive obtrectatorum, hic secundo confutat *fallaciam incredulorum,* qui sub specie studiositatis quaerebant signa curiositatis[^p298-1]. Confutatur autem haec fallacia incredulitatis Iudaeorum, et hoc triplici via. Primo enim gens illa arguitur, quia *dubia ad credendum;* secundo, quia *tepida ad discendum;* tertio, quia *frigida ad poenitendum.*

Primo igitur arguitur gens fallax et incredula, quia *dubia ad credendum,* cum dicitur: *Turbis autem concurrentibus coepit dicere: Generatio haec generatio nequam est. Turbae* namque *concurrebant,* id est, ex diversis causis ad ipsum currebant: quidam ad audiendum documenta, quidam ad videndum miracula, quidam causa famis pellendae, quidam causa sanitatis recuperandae, quidam ad insidiandum, alii ad obsequendum, secundum diversa genera personarum, quae in Evangelio exprimuntur. Hi autem, sicut dicit Glossa[^p298-2], currebant « ad videnda mirabilia, quae ab eo fieri audiebant », quae volebant videre non ex zelo fidei, sed potius ex dolo et curiositate et fraudulentia. Et propterea dicit: *Generatio haec generatio nequam est;* et Matthaei duodecimo[^p298-3] dicitur: « Generatio mala et adultera signum quaerit »; *nequam* enim et *adultera* est propter infidelitatem et idololatriam. Unde competit ei illud Proverbiorum trigesimo: « Generatio, quae patri suo maledicit et quae matri suae non benedicit »; et illud Deuteronomii trigesimo secundo: « Generatio perversa est, et infideles filii ». Eorum enim infidelitas apparet ex quaestione curiositatis; propter quod subdit: *Signum quaerit,* scilicet de caelo; Glossa[^p298-4]: « Quasi illa quae viderant, non fuerint signa ». — Et quia huiusmodi propter incredulitatem non erant caelestes, sed terreni, non spirituales, sed potius animales; ideo denegantur eis signa *caelestia,* sed conceduntur *terrestria;* ideo dicit: *Et signum non dabitur ei nisi signum Ionae prophetae.* Hoc quidem dicit, non quia alia signa
non dederit — unde Lazarum suscitavit quatriduanum, sicut dicitur Ioannis undecimo[^p298-5]; « et vox de caelo ad eum facta est », Ioannis duodecimo; « et multa alia fecit signa in conspectu omnium », de quibus dicitur Ioannis vigesimo, quod « non sunt scripta in libro suo »; et rursus: « Sol obscuratus est » in morte, infra vigesimo tertio — sed quia omnia alia signa non valent nisi credenti, Deum passum tertia die resurrexisse; cuius figura praecessit in Iona propheta; et ideo Ioannis secundo[^p298-6] Pharisaeis quaerentibus: « Quod signum ostendis nobis, quia haec facis »? dixit: « Solvite templum hoc, et in tribus diebus excitabo illud ». « Ille autem dicebat de templo corporis sui ». — Et huius figura praecessit in *Iona,* quem tertia die evomuit piscis[^p298-7]; et ideo creditum fuit ei a Ninivitis.

65. (Vers. 30.). Propter quod subdit: *Nam, sicut fuit Ionas signum Ninivitis, sic erit*[^p298-8] *Filius hominis generationi isti.* Ionas enim fuit Ninivitis signum non tantum verbo, sed etiam miraculi facto, quia, in mare proiectus et a pisce absorptus, die tertia est ad superna regressus; per quod designavit Christum passum, mortuum et suscitatum. Unde Matthaei duodecimo[^p298-9] aptatur huiusmodi signum: « Nam sicut fuit Ionas in ventre ceti tribus diebus et tribus noctibus, sic erit Filius hominis in corde terrae ». Qui ergo huic signo non credit durior est Ninivitis, nec alia signa Domini in aliquo prosunt ei.

66. (Vers. 31.). Secundo arguitur Iudaeorum generatio, quia *tepida ad discendum,* cum subditur: *Regina austri surget in iudicio cum viris generationis huius.* Ex quo apparet, quod resurrectio erit universalis, non tantum bonorum, sed etiam malorum. Sed resurrectio erit ad iudicium, secundum illud Ioannis quinto[^p298-10]: « Omnes, qui in monumentis sunt, audient vocem Filii Dei et procedent » etc. — Sed quia mali condemnabuntur comparatione meliorum, ideo subdit: *Et condemnabit illos;* Beda[^p298-11]: « Non potestate iudicii, sed comparatione melioris facti ». — Quod factum ostendit in hoc quod ait: *Quia venit a finibus terrae audire sapientiam*
<!-- page 299 -->
*Salomonis,* secundum quod dicitur secundi Paralipomenon nono[^p299-1]: « Regina Saba, cum audisset famam Salomonis, venit, ut tentaret eum in Ierusalem »; simile dicitur tertii Regum decimo. Unde in tribus fuit *laudabilis:* et primo, quia mota fuit *fama* Salomonis; secundo vero, quia venit de *remotis partibus;* tertio, quia venit ad hominem *paris conditionis.* — Et econtra hic populus tripliciter erat *vituperabilis;* quod notat, cum subdit: *Et ecce, plus quam Salomon hic.* In hoc enim, quod dicitur *ecce,* notat se *cognitum* non tantum per auditum, sed etiam per visum, secundum illud Baruch tertio[^p299-2]: « Post haec in terris visus est » etc. In hoc quod addit *hic,* ostendit, quod non erat absens, sed *praesens,* ut possit eis merito dici illud Deuteronomii trigesimo: « Mandatum, quod ego praecipio tibi hodie, non est procul situm » etc. In hoc vero quod dicit *plus quam Salomon,* ostendit, quod non tantum credendum est ei sicut viro sapienti, sed sicut *principio et fonti totius sapientiae,* secundum illud Ioannis octavo[^p299-3]: « Ego principium, qui et loquor vobis ».

67. Maior erat namque Christus Salomone ratione *potentiae,* ratione *scientiae* et ratione *iustitiae.* Unde sibi competit praecipue Psalmus ille[^p299-4]: « Deus, iudicium tuum regi da », qui in laudem Salomonis creditur a Iudaeis factus, cum ibi dicatur: « Dominabitur a mari usque ad mare »; et iterum: « Ante solem permanet nomen eius »; et iterum: « Et benedicentur in ipso omnes tribus terrae » etc. Ille Salomon pacificus fuit *ad tempus,* sed iste *aeternaliter;* Isaiae nono[^p299-5]: « Multiplicabitur eius imperium ». Ille *sapiens,* sed iste *sapientia;* primae ad Corinthios primo: « Christum Dei virtutem et sapientiam »; et ad Colossenses secundo: « In quo sunt omnes thesauri sapientiae et scientiae absconditi ». Ille *faciens iustitiam,* sed iste *ipsa iustitia;* primae ad Corinthios primo: « Qui factus est nobis a Deo iustitia » etc.

Ideo secundum septem praeeminentias regina illa austri comparatione iudicabit[^p299-6] Iudaeos incredulos; regina: *sexus fragilis, de remotis, cum difficultate, relicto imperio, cum muneribus, ad purum hominem, sola fama cognitum* venit, sicut expresse col-
ligitur ex tertii Regum decimo[^p299-7]. Sed Iudaei: *viri, Christum praesentem, gratis oblatum, sui generis, beneficum, Deum et hominem, miraculis probatum* contemnunt et despiciunt.

68. (Vers. 32.). Tertio arguitur Iudaeorum generatio, quia *frigida ad poenitendum,* cum subdit: *Viri Ninivitae surgent in iudicio cum generatione hac et condemnabunt illam;* et hoc propter ipsorum poenitentiam et Iudaeorum impoenitentiam. Ex comparatione enim meliorum arguuntur minus boni. — Propter quod subdit: *Quia poenitentiam egerunt in praedicatione*[^p299-8] *Ionae;* Ionae tertio: « Crediderunt viri Ninivitae Domino et praedicaverunt ieiunium et vestiti sunt saccis ». Et tamen Ionas erat ignotus praedicavitque paucis diebus, erat etiam homo purus, nullum etiam fecit miraculum. — Sed maiora his ostendit Christus Iudaeis; ideo subdit: *Et ecce, plus quam Ionas hic;* quia Christus non tantum erat homo, verum etiam Deus; quod ostendebat magnitudine miraculorum; non praedicavit paucis diebus, sed etiam tribus annis. Ideo magis condemnatur duritia incredulitatis in Iudaeis; unde Chrysostomus[^p299-9]: « Ninivitae inter tres dies facti sunt populus Dei, qui tamen Prophetas non habuerunt; Iudaei intra tres dies crucifixionis facti sunt populus diaboli; illi evaserunt vindictam imminentem, isti pertulerunt atrocissimam ».

69. Sic patet, quomodo fraudulentia Iudaeorum, qui quaerebant signa, ut tentarent, per exempla convicta sit, ut in[^p299-10] infidelitatis duritia non permaneret. Sicut autem Iudaei, qui audiebant Legem et Prophetas et Christum videbant et non credebant, arguuntur comparatione reginae Saba et Ninivitarum; sic clerici et litterati argui possunt comparatione mulierum et laicorum. Nam clerici Christum in Scripturis agnoscunt et manibus contrectant in Sacramentis, et tamen minus credunt et venerantur quam mulieres et laici; unde nunc possumus dicere illud Matthaei vigesimo primo[^p299-11]: « Publicani et meretrices praecedent vos » etc.; et rursus Augustinus in libro Confessionum: « Idiotae rapiunt caelum, et nos cum litteris nostris ad inferna demergimur ». — Possunt etiam his exemplis argui omnes Christiani, qui tardi sunt ad audiendum verbum Dei et ad faciendam
<!-- page 300 -->
poenitentiam de peccatis; qui ad sui excusationem quaerunt signa miraculorum, cum tamen ante oculos habeant Christum crucifixum, ita ut possit eis dici illud ad Galatas tertio[^p300-1]: « O insensati Galatae! quis vos fascinavit non obedire veritati, ante quorum oculos Christus praescriptus est in vobis et crucifixus »? — Habentes igitur et videntes signum crucis Christi, non debemus cum Iudaeis esse nec *dubii* ad credendum nec *tepidi* ad audiendum nec *frigidi* ad poenitendum et operandum in comparatione reginae austri et Ninivitarum, ne a Domino condemnemur.

## English
<!-- page 295 -->
58. (Vers. 27.). *Now it came to pass* etc. After the expression of the Jewish fraud and the reprobation of the fraud expressed, the Evangelist subjoins here *the commendation of the truth laid open*[^p295-16]. And this indeed was fitting, that, the truth having been made manifest, the Truth himself, manifesting himself, should be praised manifestly before the whole multitude. Now in the description of this commendation three things are introduced: the first is *the condition of the person praising,* the second is *the expression of the divine praise,* the third is *the approbation of the praise expressed.*
<!-- page 296 -->
First, then, as to *the condition of the person praising,* he says: *Now it came to pass, while*[^p296-1] *he spoke these things,* namely for the confutation of falsity; *a certain woman from the crowd, lifting up her voice, said to him,* for the commendation of the truth and the confutation of the Pharisees; in which a twofold condition is noted in the person praising, namely *manliness* and *lowliness. Manliness* is noted in this, that *she lifted up her voice,* according to that of Isaias, the fortieth[^p296-2]: « Lift up thy voice with strength, lift it up, fear not »; *lowliness* in this, that she is *a certain woman,* not named nor noble, but from the crowd, that that of the Psalm might be fulfilled: « The poor and needy shall praise thy name ». From which it appears that the praiser of the divine name ought not to be timid, so as not to dare to praise, nor tumid[^p296-3], so as to blush to praise, but manly and humble. Hence, while some *were silent* from *fear,* and some *blasphemed* from *pride and swelling;* this woman, humble and manly, neither *kept silence* with the timid nor *reviled* with the blasphemers; hence in the Gloss[^p296-4]: « With great confidence among the blasphemers she confesses the Son of God ». Therefore they could say to her that of Matthew, the fifteenth[^p296-5]: « O woman, great is thy faith », which namely so strengthened thee, since it is said in Proverbs, the last: « Who shall find a valiant woman »? In thee is verified that of Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-sixth: « Everlasting foundations upon a solid rock, and the commandments of God in the heart of a holy woman ».

59. Secondly, as to *the expression of the divine praise,* there is subjoined: *Blessed is the womb that bore thee;* in which she praises Christ, the Son of God, lifting up the proclamation of his praise from the beatitude of the Mother; as if she should say: blessed the woman who bore so good a son. And rightly, because it became the female sex so to praise, and not only women, nay men also. For above, in the first[^p296-6], the Virgin said: « From henceforth all generations shall call me blessed ».

60. And note that she beatifies the Virgin's womb, and this because it bore the Son of God for nine months and six days, which are taken for one month; Wisdom, the seventh[^p296-7]: « In the womb of my mother I was made flesh in the time of ten months ». Hence in praise of the Virgin it is said in the Canticle, the seventh: « Thy belly is like a heap of wheat », because Christ was *the grain of wheat,* according to what is said in John, the twelfth; and with this wheat her womb was filled, and therefore it is *blessed* because of *three privileges:* because, as Bernard[^p296-8] says, « she was made fruitful without corruption, with child without heaviness, a mother in childbirth without pain »; Isaias, the last: « Who hath ever heard such a thing? And who hath seen the like to this »? — Or because of *three miracles.* The first is the conjunction of things infinitely distant. For God there was made man; the Creator, a creature; the immense, little; the Word, an infant; the eternal, temporal, according to that of John, the first[^p296-9]: « And the Word was made flesh »; and Jeremias, the thirty-first: « The Lord shall make a new thing upon the earth: a woman shall compass a man ». — The second miracle is that he who made the womb was made in the womb; hence in the Psalm[^p296-10]: « Glorious things are said of thee, O city of God »; and after: « Man is born in her, and the Highest himself hath founded her ». Of this can be expounded that of Ecclesiasticus, the forty-third: « A wonderful vessel, the work of the Most High ». — The third miracle, that he who contains all things is contained in this womb; he is held there « whom the whole world does not hold[^p296-11] ». Hence the Church sings: « For him whom the heavens could not hold thou hast borne in thy lap »; hence Isaias, the forty-fifth: « Verily thou art a hidden God ».

61. Nor does she beatify the Virgin only because of the bearing, but also the *paps* because of the suckling, when she subjoins: *And the paps that gave thee suck,* blessed, namely; from which it is given to be understood that he was suckled by the paps of the most blessed Virgin alone. Which was signified in Moses, who
<!-- page 297 -->
would not, as is said, be suckled by an Egyptian woman; and therefore a Hebrew woman was sought, namely his own mother, as is said in Exodus, the second[^p297-1]. Now she signifies the Virgin Mary, whose paps Christ sucked, according to that of the Canticle, the eighth: « Who shall give thee to me for my brother, sucking the breasts of my mother » etc. Now she joined these two in order that the Virgin Mary might be shown to have been the true and perfect mother of Christ, because she not only bore him but also reared him; and as she truly reared him, so she truly bore him; and in this, as is said in the Gloss[^p297-2], is reproved the impiety of Manichaeus and of others who say that he brought an ethereal body. Hence Bede: « From the same fountain flow both the milk for nourishing and the seed for procreating children. Therefore, according to the natural philosophers, he could be conceived of the seed of the Virgin who could be nourished by her milk ».

62. (Vers. 28.). Thirdly, as to *the approbation of the praise expressed,* he subjoins: *But he said: Yea rather, blessed are they who hear the word of God.* This he does not say by way of opposing, but rather of adding over, as if he should say: not only is the womb *blessed* that bore me, the Word made flesh, but *more blessed* also is he who receives the word uttered by me. Hence Mary too was not only *blessed* because she carried Christ in the flesh, but also *more blessed* because she carried him most perfectly in the mind, according to what Augustine[^p297-3] says: « Mary was more blessed in conceiving the faith of Christ than in conceiving the flesh of Christ ». For everyone is blessed who hears and obeys, according to that of John, the thirteenth: « If you know these things, you shall be blessed if you do them ». — And therefore he subjoins: *And keep it;* and therefore James, the first[^p297-4]: « Be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only ». In which word Christ would not have *carnal* kinship praised merely by itself; for so it is said to the Jews above, in the third: « Say not: We have Abraham for our father » etc.; but *spiritual,* because the bond of minds is holier than that of bodies. And therefore, when his mother and brethren sought him, he said in Matthew, the twelfth[^p297-5]: « He that doth the will of my Father, who is in heaven, he is my brother, sister and mother ». And because of this the Virgin Mary was praiseworthy in conceiving, because she conceived by *faith;* and therefore Elizabeth said to her above, in the first: « Blessed art thou that hast believed, because those things shall be accomplished that were spoken to thee by the Lord ». — From which appears the marvellous commendation of the truth, which makes all who adhere to it blessed, not only those adhering by *carnal kinship,* as the Virgin Mary, but also by *spiritual dilection,* as any holy soul. For as Augustine[^p297-6] says: « Beatitude is joy in the truth »; to which joy they will come who hear, love and do the truth, according to that of Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth: « They that hear me shall not be confounded, and they that work by me shall not sin, and they that explain me shall have life everlasting ».

63. *Spiritually,* however, it is to be noted here that *the woman from the crowd* bears the type of the Law, which commends *carnal generation,* according to what was promised to Abraham, Genesis, the fifteenth[^p297-7]: « Look up to heaven » etc.; and to David, whence in the Psalm: « Of the fruit » etc.; whence to the Romans, the ninth: « Of whom is Christ according to the flesh » etc. But *Christ* bears the type of grace and of the spirit, who indeed commends *spiritual generation,* according to that of Matthew, the twelfth[^p297-8]: « Who is my mother, and who are my brethren »? — *The conception* of this *spiritual* generation is made first *in faith,* as it were in the unity of the Church; John, the seventh[^p297-9]: « He that believeth in me, as the Scripture saith » etc. In sign of which thing too the Virgin Mary conceived through faith; Luke, the first: « Blessed art thou that hast believed ». — *The bringing forth,* however, is made *through working;* Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth[^p297-10]: « I said, I will water my garden of plants » etc. But those who believe and do not work are like those of whom Isaias, the thirty-seventh, speaks: « The children are come to the birth, and there is not strength to bring forth ». — *The suckling* is made *in dilection and contemplation;* the Canticle, the first[^p297-11]: « Let him kiss me with a kiss, for thy breasts are better than wine » etc.; and Proverbs, the fifth: « A most dear hind and a most agreeable fawn » etc. — And thus in these three are intimated *the reception of grace, the exercise of the active* and *the consolation of the contemplative.* And this whole is enclosed in « faith, which worketh by dilection[^p297-12] », which they alone who have it shall be blessed.
<!-- page 298 -->

#### The deceit of the tempters or unbelievers is confuted in a threefold way.

64. (Vers. 29.). *And the multitudes running together* etc. After the Saviour confuted the deceit of the blasphemers or detractors, here secondly he confutes *the deceit of the unbelievers,* who under the appearance of studiousness were asking for signs of curiosity[^p298-1]. Now this deceit of the unbelief of the Jews is confuted, and this in a threefold way. For first that nation is reproved because *doubtful in believing;* secondly, because *tepid in learning;* thirdly, because *cold in doing penance.*

First, then, the deceitful and unbelieving nation is reproved because *doubtful in believing,* when it is said: *And the multitudes running together, he began to say: This generation is a wicked generation.* For *the multitudes were running together,* that is, they were running to him for diverse causes: some to hear lessons, some to see miracles, some for the sake of driving away hunger, some for the sake of recovering health, some to lie in wait, others to do service, according to the diverse kinds of persons who are expressed in the Gospel. Now these, as the Gloss[^p298-2] says, were running « to see the marvels which they heard were done by him », which they wished to see not from zeal of faith, but rather from guile and curiosity and fraudulence. And because of this he says: *This generation is a wicked generation;* and in Matthew, the twelfth[^p298-3], it is said: « An evil and adulterous generation seeketh a sign »; for it is *wicked* and *adulterous* because of faithlessness and idolatry. Hence there fits it that of Proverbs, the thirtieth: « A generation that curseth its father, and that doth not bless its mother »; and that of Deuteronomy, the thirty-second: « It is a perverse generation, and unfaithful children ». For their faithlessness appears from the question of curiosity; because of which he subjoins: *It asketh a sign,* namely from heaven; the Gloss[^p298-4]: « As if those which they had seen were not signs ». — And because such men because of unbelief were not heavenly but earthly, not spiritual but rather animal; therefore *heavenly* signs are denied them, but *earthly* ones are granted; therefore he says: *And a sign shall not be given it, but the sign of Jonas the prophet.* This indeed he says, not because he did not give other signs
— whence he raised Lazarus, four days dead, as is said in John, the eleventh[^p298-5]; « and a voice came from heaven to him », John, the twelfth; « and many other signs he did in the sight of all », of which it is said in John, the twentieth, that « they are not written in his book »; and again: « The sun was darkened » at his death, below, in the twenty-third — but because all other signs avail nothing save to one who believes that God, having suffered, rose again on the third day; whose figure went before in Jonas the prophet; and therefore in John, the second[^p298-6], when the Pharisees asked: « What sign dost thou shew unto us, seeing thou dost these things »? he said: « Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up ». « But he spoke of the temple of his body ». — And the figure of this went before in *Jonas,* whom the fish vomited out on the third day[^p298-7]; and therefore he was believed by the Ninivites.

65. (Vers. 30.). Because of which he subjoins: *For as Jonas was a sign to the Ninivites, so shall*[^p298-8] *the Son of man be to this generation.* For Jonas was a sign to the Ninivites not only by word but also by the deed of a miracle, because, cast into the sea and swallowed by a fish, on the third day he returned to the upper world; by which he signified Christ suffered, dead and raised. Hence in Matthew, the twelfth[^p298-9], a sign of this kind is fitted: « For as Jonas was in the whale's belly three days and three nights, so shall the Son of man be in the heart of the earth ». He, therefore, who does not believe this sign is harder than the Ninivites, nor do the other signs of the Lord profit him in anything.

66. (Vers. 31.). Secondly the generation of the Jews is reproved because *tepid in learning,* when there is subjoined: *The queen of the south shall rise in the judgment with the men of this generation.* From which it appears that the resurrection will be universal, not of the good only, but also of the wicked. But the resurrection will be unto judgement, according to that of John, the fifth[^p298-10]: « All that are in the graves shall hear the voice of the Son of God and shall come forth » etc. — But because the wicked will be condemned by comparison with the better, therefore he subjoins: *And shall condemn them;* Bede[^p298-11]: « Not by the power of judgement, but by the comparison of a better deed ». — Which deed he shows in this, that he says: *Because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom
<!-- page 299 -->
of Solomon,* according to what is said in the second of Paralipomenon, the ninth[^p299-1]: « The queen of Saba, when she had heard the fame of Solomon, came to try him in Jerusalem »; the like is said in the third of Kings, the tenth. Hence she was *praiseworthy* in three things: and first, because she was moved by the *fame* of Solomon; secondly, because she came from *remote parts;* thirdly, because she came to a man of *equal condition.* — And on the contrary this people was *blameworthy* in a threefold way; which he notes when he subjoins: *And behold more than Solomon here.* For in this, that *behold* is said, he notes that he was *known* not only through hearing but also through sight, according to that of Baruch, the third[^p299-2]: « Afterwards he was seen upon earth » etc. In this that he adds *here,* he shows that he was not absent but *present,* so that that of Deuteronomy, the thirtieth, might deservedly be said to them: « The commandment that I command thee this day is not set far off » etc. But in this that he says *more than Solomon,* he shows that he is to be believed not only as a wise man, but as *the beginning and fount of all wisdom,* according to that of John, the eighth[^p299-3]: « I am the beginning, who also speak to you ».

67. For Christ was greater than Solomon by reason of *power,* by reason of *knowledge* and by reason of *justice.* Hence that Psalm[^p299-4] fits him especially: « Give to the king thy judgment, O God », which is believed by the Jews to have been made in praise of Solomon, since it is said there: « He shall rule from sea to sea »; and again: « Before the sun his name continueth »; and again: « And in him shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed » etc. That Solomon was peaceable *for a time,* but this one *eternally;* Isaias, the ninth[^p299-5]: « His empire shall be multiplied ». That one *wise,* but this one *wisdom;* the first to the Corinthians, the first: « Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God »; and to the Colossians, the second: « In whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge ». That one *doing justice,* but this one *justice itself;* the first to the Corinthians, the first: « Who is made unto us justice from God » etc.

Therefore according to seven pre-eminences that queen of the south will judge[^p299-6] the unbelieving Jews by comparison; the queen, *of the frail sex, from remote parts, with difficulty, having left her empire, with gifts, to a mere man, known by fame alone,* came, as is expressly gathered from the third of Kings, the tenth[^p299-7]. But the Jews: *men, Christ present, offered freely, of their own race, beneficent, God and man, proved by miracles,* they contemn and despise.

68. (Vers. 32.). Thirdly the generation of the Jews is reproved because *cold in doing penance,* when he subjoins: *The men of Ninive shall rise in the judgment with this generation and shall condemn it;* and this because of their penance and the impenitence of the Jews. For by comparison with the better the less good are reproved. — Because of which he subjoins: *Because they did penance in the preaching*[^p299-8] *of Jonas;* Jonas, the third: « The men of Ninive believed the Lord, and preached a fast, and were clothed with sackcloth ». And yet Jonas was unknown, and preached for few days, was moreover a mere man, and did no miracle either. — But Christ showed the Jews greater things than these; therefore he subjoins: *And behold more than Jonas here;* because Christ was not only man but also God; which he showed by the magnitude of the miracles; he did not preach for few days, but even for three years. Therefore the hardness of unbelief in the Jews is the more condemned; hence Chrysostom[^p299-9]: « The Ninivites within three days were made the people of God, who yet had not had the Prophets; the Jews within the three days of the crucifixion were made the people of the devil; those escaped the vengeance hanging over them, these suffered the most atrocious ».

69. Thus it is clear how the fraudulence of the Jews, who were asking for signs in order to tempt, was convinced by examples, so that it might not remain in[^p299-10] the hardness of faithlessness. And as the Jews, who heard the Law and the Prophets and saw Christ and did not believe, are reproved by comparison with the queen of Saba and the Ninivites; so clerics and lettered men can be reproved by comparison with women and laymen. For clerics recognize Christ in the Scriptures and handle him with their hands in the Sacraments, and yet believe and venerate less than women and laymen; whence we can now say that of Matthew, the twenty-first[^p299-11]: « Publicans and harlots shall go before you » etc.; and again Augustine in the book of the Confessions: « The unlearned seize heaven, and we with our letters are plunged into hell ». — By these examples too all Christians can be reproved who are slow to hear the word of God and to do
<!-- page 300 -->
penance for their sins; who for their own excuse ask for signs of miracles, although they have Christ crucified before their eyes, so that that of Galatians, the third[^p300-1], can be said to them: « O senseless Galatians, who hath bewitched you that you should not obey the truth, before whose eyes Christ hath been set forth in you, and crucified »? — Having then and seeing the sign of the cross of Christ, we ought not with the Jews to be either *doubtful* in believing, or *tepid* in hearing, or *cold* in doing penance and working, in comparison with the queen of the south and the Ninivites, lest we be condemned by the Lord.
