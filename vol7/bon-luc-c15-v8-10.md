# Commentarius in Evangelium S. Lucae — Cap. XV, Vers. 8-10
## *Parabola de drachma perdita*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 387 -->

#### Parabola de drachma perdita.

15. (Vers. 8.). *Aut quae mulier* etc. Post parabolam de pastore et ovibus suis subditur hic parabola *de muliere et drachmis*[^p387-2]; in qua quidem sub metaphora *drachmae* manifestatur *pietas Dei ad hominem,* et hoc quadrupliciter, scilicet ad hominem *conditum,* ad hominem *lapsum,* ad hominem *reparatum,* ad hominem *iustificatum.*

Primo igitur quantum ad *pietatem Dei* respectu *hominis conditi* dicit: *Aut quae mulier habens drachmas decem. Mulier* ista est divina sapientia, quae *mulier* dicitur, quia diligenda est tanquam sponsa speciosissima; Sapiens Sapientiae octavo[^p387-3] loquens de sapientia dicit: « Hanc amavi et exquisivi eam a iuventute mea et quaesivi sponsam eam mihi assumere et amator factus sum formae illius ». Dicitur autem *mulier* non ab infirmitate sexus, sed a pietate divini affectus et misericordiae, ratione cuius comparat se Deus affectui materno; Isaiae quadragesimo nono[^p387-4]: « Nunquid oblivisci potest mulier infantem suum, ut non misereatur filio uteri sui » etc. Et ideo Sapientia de se dicit Ecclesiastici vigesimo quarto: « Ego mater pulcrae dilectionis » etc. Unde cum sapientia Dei sit potentissima, sit speciosissima, sit providissima, sit etiam clementissima, datur intelligi per *mulierem fortem,* secundum quod dicitur Proverbiorum ultimo[^p387-5]: « Mulierem fortem quis inveniet », id est sapientiam, quae « attingit fortiter » etc.; per *mulierem fortem et bonam et speciosam;* Ecclesiastici vigesimo sexto: « Sicut sol oriens in mundo »; per *mulierem providam;* Proverbiorum decimo quarto: « Sapiens mulier aedificat domum », id est sapientia; per *mulierem piam,* sicut in hac parabola. Unde Glossa[^p387-6]: « Mulier, id est Dei sapientia, decem drachmas habuit, cum homines et Angelos creavit ». Drachma autem est creatura rationalis, divina imagine insignita; unde Glossa[^p387-7]: « Drachma est nummus certae quantitatis, habens imaginem regis ». Talis nummus est *homo,* de quo dicitur Genesis primo: « Faciamus hominem ad imaginem et similitudinem nostram »; unde et Ecclesiastici decimo septimo: « Deus de terra creavit hominem et secundum imaginem suam fecit illum ». Talis nummus est *angelicus spiritus;* unde Ezechielis vigesimo octavo[^p387-8]: « Tu signaculum similitudinis eius, plenus sapientia et perfectus decore, in deliciis paradisi Dei fuisti ». Igitur drachmae decem sunt novem ordines Angelorum et decima hominum ad totius universi complementum, quia intra denarium clauditur universitas numerorum; unde et in eo est quidam numerorum status. Unde Beda[^p387-9]: « Decem drachmas mulier habuit, cum novem ordinibus Angelorum homo additus est decimus, ut compleretur numerus electorum ». In hoc ergo apparet pietas Dei ad rationalem creaturam conditam sicut ad rem sua imagine insignitam.

16. Secundo quantum ad *pietatem Dei* respectu *hominis lapsi* subdit: *Et*[^p387-10] *si perdiderit drachmam unam,* scilicet in hominis praevaricatione. *Drachma* enim *ista perdita* est humana natura; unde Glossa[^p387-11]: « Mulier, id est Dei sapientia, decem drachmas habuit, cum homines et Angelos ad imaginem suam creavit; sed unam perdidit, cum homo a similitudine Creatoris recessit ». Unde quia homo decimus est post novem ordines Angelorum, recte intelligitur per *decimam drachmam.* Hinc est, quod ad liberationem hominum praefigurandam Deus praecepit, sibi offerri *decimam*[^p387-12]. Hinc etiam est, quod *decem praecepta* ei data sunt, per quae quasi per quosdam gradus usque ad Deum conscendat. Hinc est etiam, quod in praefiguratione incarnationis dicitur sol retrocessisse *decem lineis,* sicut dicitur quarti Regum vigesimo[^p387-13]; sol namque per decem lineas retrocessit, cum Deus
<!-- page 388 -->
supra Angelos usque ad hominem, qui est decimus, descendit. Hinc est etiam, quod *denarius* est pretium omnium laborantium in vinea patrisfamilias; Matthaei vigesimo[^p388-1]: « Conventione facta » etc. Recte ergo *decima perdita* designat naturam humanam lapsam.

17. Ad hanc drachmam perditam divina sapientia habet *pietatem ex misericordia;* propter quod subdit: *Nonne accendit lucernam,* in incarnatione. *Lucerna* est lux in testa, hoc est Deus in carne humana; unde Glossa[^p388-2]: « Dei sapientia lucernam ad quaerendum accendit, cum in carne apparuit, quia lucerna est lux in testa, id est Verbum in carne ». De hac lucerna dicitur in Psalmo[^p388-3]: « Illuc producam cornu David, paravi lucernam Christo meo ». Unde de accensione istius lucernae dicitur Ioannis primo: « Deus erat Verbum »; « erat lux vera, quae illuminat omnem hominem »; et post: « Verbum caro factum est et habitavit in nobis ». — Nec tantum hoc, sed *et evertit*[^p388-4] *domum,* scilicet in praedicatione; Glossa: « Domum evertit, quia conscientias hominum de reatu suo perturbavit ». Et hoc spectat ad praedicatores; unde dicitur Sophoniae primo[^p388-5]: « Scrutabor Ierusalem in lucernis et visitabo super viros defixos in faecibus suis ». — *Et quaerit diligenter, donec inveniat,* in nostra redemptione; unde Sapientiae sexto[^p388-6]: « Dignos se circuit quaerens et in viis suis ostendet se illis hilariter et cum omni providentia occurret illis ». Unde et sibi competit illud Psalmi: « Si dedero somnum oculis meis et palpebris meis dormitationem, donec inveniam locum Domino, tabernaculum Deo Iacob » etc.

18. (Vers. 9.). Tertio quantum ad *pietatem* respectu *hominis reparati* adiungit: *Et cum invenerit, convocat amicas et vicinas.* Per *amicas* et *vicinas* divinae sapientiae intelliguntur Angeli boni et animae sanctae. Unde Glossa[^p388-7]: « Amicae et vicinae sunt supernae potestates, quae tanto Deo iuxta sunt, quanto eum vicinius contemplantur ». Quae ideo dicuntur *amicae* sapientiae, quia ipsam diligunt et ab ipsa amantur, secundum illud Sapientiae septimo[^p388-8]: « Per nationes in animas sanctas se transfert et amicos Dei et Prophetas constituit. Neminem enim diligit Deus, nisi qui cum sapientia inhabitat ». *Convocare* autem
nihil aliud est quam ad unitatem et conformitatem amoris copulare; Iob vigesimo quinto[^p388-9]: « Potestas et terror apud eum est, qui facit concordiam in sublimibus suis »; et per istam concordiam mutui amoris communem materiam gaudii tribuit. — Et ideo subdit: *Dicens: Congratulamini mihi, quia inveni drachmam, quam perdideram.* Sapientia namque invitat ad *congaudendum,* quia haec est lex caritatis; primae ad Corinthios decimo tertio[^p388-10]: « Caritas non gaudet super iniquitate, congaudet autem veritati »; et Sapiens dicit Sapientiae octavo: « Haec cogitans, quia immortalitas est in cogitatione sapientiae, et in amicitia illius delectatio bona », quia vult, gaudia universorum esse *communia,* non *privata,* sicut potissime in illa Ierusalem superna. Unde Tobiae decimo tertio[^p388-11]: « Confitere Domino in bonis tuis et benedic Deum saeculorum, ut revocet ad te omnes captivos tuos, et gaudeas in omnia saecula saeculorum ». In hac enim reinventione drachmae fit reintegratio numeri electorum in illa Ierusalem; et haec est magna materia laetitiae; Isaiae sexagesimo quinto[^p388-12]: « Ecce, ego creo Ierusalem exsultationem, et populum eius gaudium », quia congratulantur divinae sapientiae et salvationi nostrae, secundum illud Isaiae ultimo: « Laetamini cum Ierusalem et exsultate in ea, omnes, qui diligitis eam; gaudete cum ea gaudio, universi, qui lugebatis super eam, ut sugatis et repleamini ab uberibus consolationis eius; ut mulgeatis et deliciis affluatis ab omnimoda gloria eius ».

19. (Vers. 10.). Quarto quantum ad *pietatem Dei* respectu *hominis iustificati* subdit: *Ita dico vobis: Gaudium est Angelis*[^p388-13] *Dei super uno peccatore poenitentiam agente. Gaudent* autem *Angeli* de poenitentia hominis peccatoris, quia destruitur *culpa,* recuperatur *iustitia,* confutatur *daemonum superbia,* efficax efficitur *Angelorum custodia,* reparatur *Ecclesia,* placatur *ira divina,* et quia restauratur *Ierusalem superna.* Et de his septem gaudent maxime Angeli beati; et ideo et de *poenitentia peccatoris,* in qua vident fieri septem praedicta, gaudium est Angelis Dei. — Primo, quia *destruitur culpa;* Actuum tertio[^p388-14]: « Poenitemini et convertimini, ut deleantur vestra peccata ». Unde dicitur
<!-- page 389 -->
Marci primo, quod erat « Ioannes praedicans baptismum poenitentiae in remissionem peccatorum » etc. — Secundo, quia per poenitentiam *recuperatur iustitia;* unde Apocalypsis secundo[^p389-1]: « Memor esto, unde excideris, et age poenitentiam et prima opera fac »; et Ecclesiastici decimo septimo: « Poenitentibus dedit Deus viam iustitiae et confirmavit deficientes ». — Tertio, quia per poenitentiam *confutatur daemonum superbia;* Psalmus[^p389-2]: « Discedite a me, omnes, qui operamini iniquitatem; quoniam exaudivit Dominus vocem fletus mei »; et post: « Erubescant et conturbentur vehementer omnes inimici mei » etc. Tunc enim daemones erubescunt de sua impudentia, quando peccator per poenitentiam erubescit de culpa; Ieremiae trigesimo primo: « Postquam convertisti me, egi poenitentiam ». « Confusus sum et erubui, quoniam sustinui opprobrium adolescentiae meae ». — Quarto, quia per poenitentiam *efficax efficitur Angelorum custodia;* unde Apocalypsis tertio[^p389-3]: « Ego quos amo arguo et castigo. Aemulare ergo et poenitentiam age. Ecce, sto ad ostium et pulso ». Hoc enim Angelus nos custodiens facit sollicite. — Quinto, quia per poenitentiam *reparatur Ecclesia;* propter quod dicitur Matthaei nono[^p389-4]: « Non veni vocare iustos, sed peccatores » ad poenitentiam. Nam Christus vocat ad unitatem ecclesiasticam, in qua fit remissio peccatorum, et datur superna gratia, secundum illud Ezechielis decimo octavo: « Si poenitentiam egerit impius ab omnibus peccatis suis, vita vivet et non morietur ». — Sexto, quia *placatur ira divina;* Ecclesiastici duodecimo[^p389-5]: « Altissimus odio habet peccatores et misertus est poenitentibus »; et Ieremiae decimo octavo: « Si poenitentiam egerit gens ista a malo suo, agam et ego poenitentiam super malo, quod cogitavi, ut facerem ei ». — Septimo, quia per poenitentiam *reintegratur Ierusalem superna.* Unde Matthaei quarto[^p389-6]: « Agite poenitentiam, appropinquabit enim regnum caelorum ». Et propterea cives caelestes exsultant, sicut dicitur hic: *Gaudium est Angelis Dei super uno peccatore poenitentiam agente.* Vident enim, quantae virtutis sit poenitentia, quae non solum recuperat perdita, sed etiam multo plura impetrat a pietate divina; unde Iob ultimo[^p389-7]: « Conversus Dominus ad poenitentiam Iob. Addidit Dominus omnia, quae fuerant Iob, duplicia ».

20. Et hac de causa omnes, qui diligunt Deum, qui diligunt bonum et qui habent pietatis affectum, cum Angelis exsultare debent de conversione et poenitentia peccatorum. Unde Apostolus secundae ad Corinthios septimo[^p389-8]: « Etsi ad horam vos contristavi, nunc gaudeo, non quia contristati estis, sed quia contristati estis ad poenitentiam. Contristati enim estis secundum Deum, ut in nullo detrimentum patiamini. Quae enim secundum Deum est tristitia poenitentiam in salutem stabilem operatur ». Et post subdit: « Operatur enim in vobis sollicitudinem; sed defensionem, sed indignationem, sed timorem, sed desiderium, sed aemulationem, sed vindictam ». Et ex his debitus honor redditur divinae maiestati, reparatur numerus caelestis, et recuperatur unitas ecclesiasticae pacis. Et ideo in conversione et poenitentia unius peccatoris merito exsultat hierarchia supercaelestis, caelestis et subcaelestis[^p389-9].

#### The parable of the lost groat.

15. (Vers. 8.). *Or what woman* etc. After the parable of the shepherd and his sheep there is subjoined here the parable *of the woman and the groats*[^p387-2]; in which, under the metaphor of *the groat,* is made manifest *the piety of God toward man*[^tr-pietas], and this in four ways, namely toward man *made,* toward man *fallen,* toward man *repaired,* toward man *justified.*

First, then, as to *the piety of God* in respect of *man made* he says: *Or what woman having ten groats. That woman* is the divine wisdom, which is called *a woman* because she is to be loved as a most beauteous spouse; the Wise man, Wisdom, the eighth chapter[^p387-3], speaking of wisdom says: « Her have I loved, and have sought her out from my youth, and have desired to take her for my spouse, and I became a lover of her beauty ». Now she is called *a woman* not from the infirmity of the sex, but from the piety of divine affection and mercy, by reason of which God compares himself to a mother's affection; Isaias, the forty-ninth chapter[^p387-4]: « Can a woman forget her infant, so as not to have pity on the son of her womb » etc. And therefore Wisdom says of herself, Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-fourth chapter: « I am the mother of fair love » etc. Whence, since the wisdom of God is most mighty, is most beauteous, is most provident, is also most clement, she is given to be understood by *the valiant woman,* according to what is said in Proverbs, the last chapter[^p387-5]: « Who shall find a valiant woman », that is, wisdom, which « reacheth mightily » etc.; by *the valiant and good and beauteous woman;* Ecclesiasticus, the twenty-sixth chapter: « As the sun when it riseth to the world »; by *the provident woman;* Proverbs, the fourteenth chapter: « A wise woman buildeth her house », that is, wisdom; by *the pious woman,* as in this parable. Whence the Gloss[^p387-6]: « The woman, that is, the wisdom of God, had ten groats when she created men and Angels ». Now the groat is the rational creature, marked with the divine image; whence the Gloss[^p387-7]: « A groat is a coin of a certain quantity, having the image of the king ». Such a coin is *man,* of whom it is said in Genesis, the first chapter: « Let us make man to our image and likeness »; whence also Ecclesiasticus, the seventeenth chapter: « God created man of the earth, and made him after his own image ». Such a coin is *the angelic spirit;* whence Ezechiel, the twenty-eighth chapter[^p387-8]: « Thou wast the seal of his likeness, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty; thou wast in the pleasures of the paradise of God ». The ten groats, then, are the nine orders of Angels and the tenth of men, for the completion of the whole universe, because within ten the universality of numbers is enclosed; whence also in it there is a certain stopping-place of numbers. Whence Bede[^p387-9]: « The woman had ten groats, since to the nine orders of Angels man was added as the tenth, that the number of the elect might be completed ». In this, then, appears the piety of God toward the rational creature made, as toward a thing marked with his image.

16. Second, as to *the piety of God* in respect of *man fallen* he subjoins: *And*[^p387-10] *if she lose one groat,* namely in man's prevarication. For *that lost groat* is human nature; whence the Gloss[^p387-11]: « The woman, that is, the wisdom of God, had ten groats when she created men and Angels to her image; but she lost one when man withdrew from the likeness of the Creator ». Whence, because man is the tenth after the nine orders of Angels, he is rightly understood by *the tenth groat.* Hence it is that, for prefiguring the deliverance of men, God commanded *the tenth* to be offered to him[^p387-12]. Hence also it is that *ten precepts* were given to him, through which, as it were through certain steps, he might ascend even to God. Hence it is also that in the prefiguration of the incarnation the sun is said to have gone back *ten lines,* as is said in the fourth of Kings, the twentieth chapter[^p387-13]; for the sun went back by ten lines when God

<!-- page 388 -->
descended above the Angels even to man, who is the tenth. Hence it is also that *the penny* is the price of all who labour in the vineyard of the householder; Matthew, the twentieth chapter[^p388-1]: « Having made an agreement » etc. Rightly, then, *the tenth lost* designates fallen human nature.

17. Toward this lost groat the divine wisdom has *piety out of mercy;* on account of which he subjoins: *Doth she not light a lamp,* in the incarnation. *A lamp* is light in an earthen vessel, that is, God in human flesh; whence the Gloss[^p388-2]: « The wisdom of God lit a lamp for seeking when she appeared in the flesh, because a lamp is light in an earthen vessel, that is, the Word in the flesh ». Of this lamp it is said in the Psalm[^p388-3]: « There will I bring forth a horn to David: I have prepared a lamp for my Christ ». Whence of the lighting of this lamp it is said in John, the first chapter: « The Word was God »; « it was the true light, which enlighteneth every man »; and after: « The Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us ». — Not only this, but *she also overturneth*[^p388-4] *the house,* namely in preaching; the Gloss: « She overturned the house, because she troubled the consciences of men concerning their guilt ». And this pertains to preachers; whence it is said in Sophonias, the first chapter[^p388-5]: « I will search Jerusalem with lamps, and will visit upon the men that are settled on their lees ». — *And seek diligently until she find it,* in our redemption; whence Wisdom, the sixth chapter[^p388-6]: « She goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her, and she will show herself to them cheerfully in the ways, and will meet them with all providence ». Whence there befits her also that of the Psalm: « If I shall give sleep to my eyes, and slumber to my eyelids, until I find out a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob » etc.

18. (Vers. 9.). Third, as to *the piety* in respect of *man repaired* he adds: *And when she hath found it, she calleth together her friends and neighbours.* By *the friends* and *neighbours* of the divine wisdom are understood the good Angels and holy souls. Whence the Gloss[^p388-7]: « The friends and neighbours are the supernal powers, who are so much the nearer to God, the more nearly they contemplate him ». Who are therefore called *friends* of wisdom, because they love her and are loved by her, according to that of Wisdom, the seventh chapter[^p388-8]: « Through nations she conveyeth herself into holy souls, and maketh the friends of God and prophets. For God loveth none but him that dwelleth with wisdom ». Now *to call together*
is nothing else than to couple unto the unity and conformity of love; Job, the twenty-fifth chapter[^p388-9]: « Power and terror are with him, who maketh concord in his high places »; and through this concord of mutual love he bestows the common matter of joy. — And therefore he subjoins: *Saying: Rejoice with me, because I have found the groat which I had lost.* For wisdom invites to *rejoicing together,* because this is the law of charity; the first to the Corinthians, the thirteenth chapter[^p388-10]: « Charity rejoiceth not in iniquity, but rejoiceth with the truth »; and the Wise man says, Wisdom, the eighth chapter: « Thinking these things, that immortality is in the thought of wisdom, and good delight in her friendship », because she wills the joys of all to be *common,* not *private,* as most of all in that supernal Jerusalem. Whence Tobias, the thirteenth chapter[^p388-11]: « Give glory to the Lord for thy good things, and bless the God of ages, that he may call back to thee all thy captives, and thou mayst rejoice for ever and ever ». For in this finding again of the groat there comes about the reintegration of the number of the elect in that Jerusalem; and this is great matter of gladness; Isaias, the sixty-fifth chapter[^p388-12]: « Behold I create Jerusalem an exultation, and the people thereof joy », because they rejoice together with the divine wisdom and with our salvation, according to that of Isaias, the last chapter: « Rejoice with Jerusalem, and exult in her, all you that love her: rejoice for joy with her, all you that mourned for her; that you may suck, and be filled with the breasts of her consolation; that you may milk out, and flow with delights, from every manner of her glory ».

19. (Vers. 10.). Fourth, as to *the piety of God* in respect of *man justified* he subjoins: *So I say to you: There is joy to the Angels*[^p388-13] *of God upon one sinner doing penance.* Now *the Angels rejoice* over the penance of a sinful man, because *fault* is destroyed, *justice* is recovered, *the pride of the demons* is confuted, *the guardianship of the Angels* is made effectual, *the Church* is repaired, *the divine wrath* is appeased, and because *the supernal Jerusalem* is restored. And over these seven the blessed Angels rejoice most; and therefore also over *the penance of a sinner,* in which they see the seven aforesaid come about, there is joy to the Angels of God. — First, because *fault is destroyed;* Acts, the third chapter[^p388-14]: « Do penance, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out ». Whence it is said

<!-- page 389 -->
in Mark, the first chapter, that there was « John preaching the baptism of penance unto the remission of sins » etc. — Second, because through penance *justice is recovered;* whence the Apocalypse, the second chapter[^p389-1]: « Be mindful from whence thou art fallen, and do penance, and do the first works »; and Ecclesiasticus, the seventeenth chapter: « To the penitent God hath given the way of justice, and he hath strengthened them that were fainting ». — Third, because through penance *the pride of the demons is confuted;* the Psalm[^p389-2]: « Depart from me, all ye that work iniquity: for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping »; and after: « Let all my enemies blush, and be very much troubled » etc. For then the demons blush at their impudence, when the sinner through penance blushes for his fault; Jeremias, the thirty-first chapter: « After thou didst convert me, I did penance ». « I am confounded and have blushed, because I have borne the reproach of my youth ». — Fourth, because through penance *the guardianship of the Angels is made effectual;* whence the Apocalypse, the third chapter[^p389-3]: « Such as I love, I rebuke and chastise. Be zealous therefore, and do penance. Behold, I stand at the gate, and knock ». For this the Angel guarding us does solicitously. — Fifth, because through penance *the Church is repaired;* on account of which it is said in Matthew, the ninth chapter[^p389-4]: « I came not to call the just, but sinners » to penance. For Christ calls to ecclesiastical unity, in which comes about the remission of sins, and supernal grace is given, according to that of Ezechiel, the eighteenth chapter: « If the wicked do penance for all his sins, living he shall live, and shall not die ». — Sixth, because *the divine wrath is appeased;* Ecclesiasticus, the twelfth chapter[^p389-5]: « The Highest hateth sinners, and hath mercy on the penitent »; and Jeremias, the eighteenth chapter: « If that nation shall do penance for their evil, I also will do penance for the evil that I have thought to do to them ». — Seventh, because through penance *the supernal Jerusalem is reintegrated.* Whence Matthew, the fourth chapter[^p389-6]: « Do penance, for the kingdom of heaven will draw near ». And on this account the heavenly citizens exult, as is said here: *There is joy to the Angels of God upon one sinner doing penance.* For they see of how great power penance is, which not only recovers things lost, but also obtains far more from the divine piety; whence Job, the last chapter[^p389-7]: « The Lord was turned at the penance of Job. And the Lord added all things that had been Job's, twofold ».

20. And for this cause all who love God, who love the good and who have the affection of piety, ought to exult with the Angels over the conversion and penance of sinners. Whence the Apostle, the second to the Corinthians, the seventh chapter[^p389-8]: « Although I made you sorrowful for a time, now I am glad: not because you were made sorrowful, but because you were made sorrowful unto penance. For you were made sorrowful according to God, that you might suffer damage in nothing. For the sorrow that is according to God worketh penance steadfast unto salvation ». And after he subjoins: « For it worketh in you carefulness; yea defence, yea indignation, yea fear, yea desire, yea zeal, yea revenge ». And from these due honour is rendered to the divine majesty, the heavenly number is repaired, and the unity of ecclesiastical peace is recovered. And therefore in the conversion and penance of one sinner the supercelestial, celestial and subcelestial hierarchy deservedly exults[^p389-9].

Chapter XV ¶ 1 divides the chapter by the one Latin root: the *impietas* of the Jews murmuring against clemency, the *manifestatio pietatis divinae* of the three parables, and the *commendatio et persuasio pietatis humanae* of Chapter XVI — "impiety … divine piety … human piety", one English root, as the Latin has one. The Latin word covers the tenderness of a parent toward a child as well as the devotion of a creature toward God; in these places Bonaventure is using the first sense. (¶ 20's *qui habent pietatis affectum*, "who have the affection of piety", is human piety: "piety", with no further note.)

## Notes

**THE EIGHTY-FOURTH CHUNK OF THE *COMMENTARIUS IN EVANGELIUM S. LUCAE*, THE SECOND OF CAPITULUM XV: THE PARABLE OF THE LOST GROAT.** ¶ 15 (Vers. 8) opens the second of the three parables named at ¶ 6 and divides it by *the piety of God toward man*, fourfold: toward man made, fallen, repaired and justified. Man made: the woman is the divine wisdom, called *woman* from the piety of divine affection (Isa. 49:15, Ecclus. 24:24), the valiant, beauteous, provident and pious woman; the ten groats are the nine orders of Angels and man the tenth, the groat a coin bearing the king's image (¶ 15). Man fallen: the lost groat is human nature; the tenth offered, the ten precepts, the sun gone back ten lines, the penny of the labourers (¶ 16). The lamp lit in the incarnation, light in an earthen vessel; the house overturned in preaching; the diligent search in our redemption (¶ 17). Man repaired: the friends and neighbours are the good Angels and holy souls; to call together is to couple into the unity of love; wisdom invites to rejoicing together, the joys of the supernal Jerusalem common, not private (¶ 18). Man justified: the joy of the Angels over one sinner doing penance, by seven reasons — fault destroyed, justice recovered, the demons' pride confuted, the Angels' guardianship made effectual, the Church repaired, the divine wrath appeased, the supernal Jerusalem reintegrated (¶ 19). The corollary: all who love God ought to exult with the Angels, and the threefold hierarchy exults (¶ 20).
