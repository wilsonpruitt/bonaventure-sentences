## *De latitudine sacrae Scripturae*

## Latin
<!-- page 202 -->

### § 1. *De latitudine sacrae Scripturae.*

Si igitur velimus *latitudinem* sacrae Scripturae speculari, prima fronte occurrit nobis Scriptura secanda in duo testamenta, scilicet in vetus et novum. — Vetus autem occurrit[^p202-9] cum multitudine librorum. Habet enim libros *legales, historiales, sapientiales* et *prophetales*, ita quod *primi* sunt quinque, *secundi* decem, *tertii* quinque et *quarti* sunt sex, ac per hoc in *universo* viginti sex[^p202-10]. — Novum testamentum similiter habet libros his correspondentes secundum quadruplicem formam. Nam *legalibus* correspondent libri evangelici, *historialibus*, Actus

<!-- page 203 -->

apostolici, *sapientialibus*, Epistolae Apostolorum et maxime Pauli, *prophetalibus* correspondet liber Apocalypsis; ut sic mira sit conformitas inter vetus et novum testamentum, non solum in continentia sensuum, verum etiam in quadriformitate partium. — In cuius figuram et consignationem vidit Ezechiel[^p203-1] rotas quatuor facierum et rotam in medio rotae, quia vetus est in novo, et e converso; et in libris legalibus et evangelicis est facies *leonis* propter excellentiam auctoritatis; in historialibus est facies *bovis* propter exempla virtutis; in sapientialibus est facies *hominis* propter prudentiam sagacem; in libris prophetalibus est facies *aquilae* propter intelligentiam perspicacem.

Recte autem sacra Scriptura dividitur in vetus et novum testamentum, et non in theoricam et practicam, sicut philosophia[^p203-2]: quia, cum Scriptura fundetur proprie super cognitionem fidei, quae virtus est et fundamentum morum et iustitiae et totius rectae vitae, non potest in ea sequestrari notitia rerum sive credendorum a notitia morum. Secus autem est de philosophia, quae non tantum de veritate morum, verum etiam agit de vero nuda speculatione considerato. Quoniam igitur Scriptura sacra est notitia movens ad bonum et revocans a malo; et hoc est per timorem et amorem: ideo dividitur in duo testamenta, quorum « brevis differentia est timor et amor[^p203-3] ».

Et quia quadrupliciter potest quis moveri ad bonum et revocari a malo, scilicet per *praecepta* potentissimae maiestatis, vel per *documenta* sapientissimae veritatis, vel per *exempla* et *beneficia* innocentissimae bonitatis, vel *ex his omnibus* in unum collectis; ideo tam in novo quam in veteri testamento sunt quadriformiter traditi libri continentes sacram Scripturam secundum correspondentiam ad quatuor praemissa. Nam libri *legales*[^p203-4] movent per praecepta maiestatis omnipotentissimae; *historiales*, per exempla bonitatis innocentissimae; *sapientiales*, per documenta veritatis providentissimae; *prophetales* movent ex omnium praedictorum aggregatione, sicut manifeste in eis apparet. Unde ipsi sunt quasi rememorativi totius legalis sapientiae et doctrinae.

Est igitur Scriptura sacra similis latissimo fluvio, qui ex concursu multarum aquarum aggregatur magis ac magis, secundum quod longius decurrit. Nam cum primo in Scriptura essent libri *legales*, postea supervenit aqua sapientiae *historialium* librorum, tertio vero superadvenit doctrina *sapientissimi Salomonis*, post haec[^p203-5] etiam doctrina *sanctorum Prophetarum* et tandem doctrina *evangelica* revelata est, per os carnis Christi prolata, per Evangelistas conscripta, per sanctos Apostolos divulgata; additis etiam documentis, quae Spiritus sanctus, super eos veniens, docuit nos per eos: ut sic omnem veritatem, per Spiritum sanctum iuxta divinum promissum edocti[^p203-6], omnis veritatis salutaris doctrinam Ecclesiae Christi darent et sacram Scripturam consummando veritatis notitiam dilatarent.

## English

### § 1. *On the breadth of sacred Scripture.*

If, then, we wish to contemplate the *breadth* of sacred Scripture, at first sight Scripture presents itself to us as to be divided into two testaments, namely the old and the new. — Now the old presents itself[^p202-9] with a multitude of books. For it has books *legal, historical, sapiential*, and *prophetic*, such that the *first* are five, the *second* ten, the *third* five, and the *fourth* are six, and through this in *all* twenty-six[^p202-10]. — The new testament likewise has books corresponding to these according to a fourfold form. For to the *legal* books correspond the books of the Gospels; to the *historical*, the Acts

of the Apostles; to the *sapiential*, the Epistles of the Apostles and especially of Paul; to the *prophetic* corresponds the book of the Apocalypse — so that there is a wondrous conformity between the old and the new testament, not only in the content of their meanings, but also in the fourfold form of their parts. — As a figure and seal of this, Ezekiel saw[^p203-1] wheels of four faces and a wheel in the midst of a wheel, because the old is in the new and conversely; and in the legal and evangelical books there is the face of the *lion* on account of the excellence of authority; in the historical, the face of the *ox* on account of the examples of virtue; in the sapiential, the face of the *man* on account of sagacious prudence; in the prophetic books, the face of the *eagle* on account of penetrating understanding.

And rightly is sacred Scripture divided into the old and the new testament, and not into theoretical and practical as philosophy is[^p203-2]: because, since Scripture is founded properly upon the cognition of faith, which is a virtue and the foundation of morals and of justice and of all right living, the knowledge of things or of what is to be believed cannot be separated in it from the knowledge of morals. But it is otherwise with philosophy, which treats not only of the truth of morals, but also of the true considered in bare speculation. Since, therefore, sacred Scripture is a knowledge that moves toward the good and calls back from evil — and this is through fear and love — therefore it is divided into two testaments, of which « the brief difference is fear and love[^p203-3] ».

And because one can be moved to good and called back from evil in four ways — namely through the *precepts* of a most powerful majesty, or through the *teachings* of a most wise truth, or through the *examples* and *benefits* of a most innocent goodness, or *from all these* gathered into one — therefore in the new testament as well as in the old the books containing sacred Scripture are handed down in a fourfold manner according to a correspondence with the four things premised. For the *legal* books[^p203-4] move through the precepts of a most omnipotent majesty; the *historical*, through the examples of a most innocent goodness; the *sapiential*, through the teachings of a most provident truth; the *prophetic* move from the gathering together of all the aforesaid, as manifestly appears in them. Hence they are, as it were, recollective of all legal wisdom and teaching.

Sacred Scripture is therefore like a very wide river, which from the confluence of many waters is gathered more and more according as it runs further. For whereas at first there were in Scripture the *legal* books, afterwards there came upon them the water of the wisdom of the *historical* books; third there came in addition the teaching of *the most wise Solomon*; after these[^p203-5] also the teaching of the *holy Prophets*; and at last the *evangelical* teaching was revealed, uttered by the mouth of Christ's flesh, written down by the Evangelists, spread abroad by the holy Apostles — with the addition also of the teachings which the Holy Spirit, coming upon them, taught us through them: so that thus, taught all truth by the Holy Spirit according to the divine promise[^p203-6], they might give to the Church of Christ the doctrine of all saving truth, and, by bringing sacred Scripture to completion, might spread abroad the knowledge of truth.
