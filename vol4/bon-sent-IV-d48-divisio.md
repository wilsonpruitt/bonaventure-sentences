## *Divisio textus et tractatio quaestionum*

## Latin

### COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XLVIII.
#### *De forma et qualitate iudicis.*

*Solet etiam quaeri, in qua forma Christus iudicabit* etc.

> **On the apparatus numbering.** Printed page 983 carries two separately numbered footer registers. The first (notes 1–3 of page 983) is headed *Notae ad librum Sententiarum* and belongs to the Lombard littera above; those notes are rendered in the littera chunk. The register rendered below is the second one, headed *Notae ad Commentarium*, which restarts at 1 and holds a single entry. Its label is qualified with a `c` so that it cannot collide with the littera register's labels for the same page.

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Supra determinavit Magister de ordine iudicii. In hac parte determinat de forma et qualitate iudicantis. Et dividitur haec pars in duas. In prima determinat de qualitate iudicantis in se. In secunda, in effectibus, in quibus ostenditur magnitudo potestatis suae, scilicet luminarium obscuratio et postmodum melioratio, ibi: *Veniente autem ad iudicium Domino* etc.

Prima pars habet tres[^p983c-1]. In prima parte determinat de forma et potestate. In secunda vero determinat de formae qualitate, in qua se ostendet, ibi: *Sed cum in humana forma constet* etc. In tertia determinat de loco, ibi: *Et putant quidam Dominum* etc. — Et ita tanguntur hic duo in universo. Primum est de forma, in qua iudex adveniet. Secundum vero est de innovatione et perfectione corporum mundi.

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam praesentis distinctionis secundum duo, quae determinat Magister, duo principaliter quaeruntur.

Primo quaeritur de iudicis apparitione.

Secundo, de corporum superiorum innovatione.

Circa primum quaeruntur quatuor.

Primo quaeritur de iudiciaria potestate.

Secundo, de forma, in qua apparebit.

Tertio, de qualitate formae.

Quarto quaeritur de hora iudicii.

## English

### COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XLVIII.
#### *On the form and quality of the judge.*

*It is also customarily asked, in what form Christ will judge* etc.

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Above the Master determined concerning the order of the judgement. In this part he determines concerning the form and quality of the one judging. And this part is divided into two. In the first he determines concerning the quality of the one judging in himself. In the second, [concerning that quality] in its effects, in which the greatness of his power is shown, namely the darkening of the luminaries and afterwards their bettering, there: *But when the Lord comes to the judgement* etc.

The first part has three [members][^p983c-1]. In the first part he determines concerning the form and the power. In the second, however, he determines concerning the quality of the form in which he will show himself, there: *But since he stands in human form* etc. In the third he determines concerning the place, there: *And some think that the Lord* etc. — And thus two things in all are touched upon here. The first is concerning the form in which the judge will come. The second is concerning the renewal and perfection of the bodies of the world.

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of the present distinction, according to the two things which the Master determines, two things are principally asked.

First it is asked concerning the appearing of the judge.

Second, concerning the renewal of the higher bodies.

Concerning the first, four things are asked.

First it is asked concerning the judiciary power.

Second, concerning the form in which he will appear.

Third, concerning the quality of the form.

Fourth it is asked concerning the hour of the judgement.

## Apparatus

[^p983c-1]: **La.** Edd. legunt: *duas. In prima determinat de forma, in qua se ostendet. In secunda de loco, ibi: Putant* etc. Cod. B hic ponit *quatuor*, et infra post *In tertia determinat* prosequitur: *in qua forma suscitabit mortuos, quia in forma servi, et hoc facit primo, cum dicit:* Et sicut dicitur Christus etc.; *secundo ponit duas rationes, quare in tali forma. Et est prima ratio, quia in ea suscepit quod est causa nostrae redemptionis, et tangitur ibi:* Sed hac ratione etc., *secunda est, quia in illa forma dabit vocem, cuius virtute resurgent mortui, et hoc ibi:* Et quia secundum eandem formam. *Tertio determinat quoddam incidens; quod enim dictum est, quod secundum formam humanam suscitabit mortuos, ne aliquis propter hoc crederet, quod secundum eandem suscitaret animas, dicit ibi, quod non, et hoc ibi:* Secundum quod Deus est etc. *In quarta determinat de loco* etc.

     **En.** The editions read: *two. In the first he determines concerning the form in which he will show himself. In the second, concerning the place, there: They think* etc. Codex B here puts *four*, and below, after *In the third he determines*, continues: *in what form he will raise the dead, namely in the form of a servant, and he does this first when he says:* And as Christ is said etc.; *secondly he sets down two reasons why in such a form. And the first reason is that in it he took up that which is the cause of our redemption, and it is touched upon there:* But for this reason etc.; *the second is that in that form he will utter the voice by whose power the dead will rise again, and this there:* And because according to the same form. *Thirdly he determines a certain incidental matter; for since it was said that he will raise the dead according to the human form, lest anyone should on this account believe that he would raise souls according to the same [form], he says there that he will not, and this there:* According as he is God etc. *In the fourth he determines concerning the place* etc.
