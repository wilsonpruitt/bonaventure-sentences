## *Commentarius in Distinctionem XI — Divisio textus et tractatio quaestionum (Pars I)*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 241 -->

### COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XI.

#### Pars I.

*De eucharistia per comparationem ad conficientem.*

> Si autem quaeritur, qualis sit illa conversio, an formalis etc.

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Supra egit Magister de Sacramento eucharistiae in se et in comparatione ad manducantem. In hac parte agit de ipso per comparationem ad conficientem[^1]. Et dividitur haec pars in tres partes. In prima parte agit de ipsa confectione. In secunda de consequentibus ad confectionem, infra distinctione duodecima, ibi: *Si autem quaeritur de accidentibus, quae remanent* etc. In tertia agit de potestate conficientis, infra distinctione decima tertia: *Solet etiam quaeri, utrum pravi sacerdotes* etc.

Prima pars habet duas partes. In prima parte agit de ipsa confectione quantum ad actum transsubstantiandi; in secunda quantum ad materiam, ibi: *Sub alia autem specie tribus de causis.* Prima pars habet duas partes. In prima determinat veritatem. In secunda reprobat falsas opiniones, ibi: *Quidam vero sic dicunt conversionem illam* etc. Prima pars habet duas. Primo enim determinat, quid sentiendum; secundo vero, quid loquendum, ibi: *Nec tamen concedunt aliqui* etc.

Similiter secunda pars habet duas. In prima ponit opiniones. In secunda reprobat, ibi: *Sed quod non sit ibi substantia nisi corpus Christi* etc.

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam huius partis sex quaeruntur.

Primo quaeritur, utrum in hoc Sacramento sit aliqua conversio panis in corpus Christi.

Secundo, dato quod sic, quaeritur, utrum totus panis convertatur, an aliquid remaneat praeter accidentia.

Tertio, dato quod convertatur totus, quaeritur, utrum talis conversio panis possit dici annihilatio.

Quarto, dato quod non, quaeritur, utrum panis convertatur in totum Christum, id est in animam et Divinitatem, sicut in carnem.

Quinto, utrum haec conversio sit subita, an successiva.

Sexto et ultimo quaeritur, quibus verbis sit exprimenda illa conversio.

### ARTICULUS UNICUS.

#### *De transsubstantiatione.*

### QUAESTIO I.

#### *Utrum in Sacramento eucharistiae sit vera conversio panis in corpus Christi.*

## English
<!-- page 241 -->

### COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XI.

#### Part I.

*On the Eucharist in relation to the one who confects it.*

> But if it is asked, of what kind that conversion is, whether formal, etc.

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Above the Master treated of the Sacrament of the Eucharist in itself and in relation to the one who eats it. In this part he treats of it in relation to the one who confects it[^1]. And this part is divided into three parts. In the first part he treats of the confecting itself. In the second, of the things consequent upon the confecting, below in the twelfth distinction, there: *But if it is asked concerning the accidents which remain,* etc. In the third he treats of the power of the one confecting, below in the thirteenth distinction: *It is also wont to be asked, whether wicked priests,* etc.

The first part has two parts. In the first part he treats of the confecting itself with respect to the act of transubstantiating; in the second, with respect to the matter, there: *Under another species, for three causes.* The first part has two parts. In the first he determines the truth. In the second he refutes false opinions, there: *But certain ones speak thus of that conversion,* etc. The first part has two. For first he determines what is to be held; second, what is to be said, there: *Nor yet do some concede,* etc.

Likewise the second part has two. In the first he sets down opinions. In the second he refutes, there: *But that there is not there a substance except the body of Christ,* etc.

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of this part six [things] are asked.

First it is asked, whether in this Sacrament there is any conversion of the bread into the body of Christ.

Second, granted that there is, it is asked, whether the whole bread is converted, or whether something remains besides the accidents.

Third, granted that the whole is converted, it is asked, whether such a conversion of the bread can be called annihilation.

Fourth, granted that it cannot, it is asked, whether the bread is converted into the whole Christ, that is, into the soul and the Divinity, as into the flesh.

Fifth, whether this conversion is sudden or successive.

Sixth and last it is asked, by what words that conversion is to be expressed.

### SINGLE ARTICLE.

#### *On transubstantiation.*

### QUESTION I.

#### *Whether in the Sacrament of the Eucharist there is a true conversion of the bread into the body of Christ.*
