## *Commentarius in Distinctionem VI, Pars II*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 148 -->

## COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM VI

### Pars II.

### *De annexis potestati baptizandi.*

*Hoc etiam sciendum est, quod licet ter immergatur etc.*[^2]

TEXTUM MAGISTRI VIDE SUPRA PAG. 133.

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Supra egit Magister de potestate baptizandi: hic agit de annexis illi potestati[^3]. Et quoniam tria sunt annexa: ex parte baptizati idoneitas, ex parte baptizantis intentio, ex parte temporis opportunitas[^5]; ideo primo determinat, quis potest baptizari. Secundo, utrum intentio sit necessaria ad baptismum, ibi: *Solet quaeri de illo qui iocans* etc. Tertio de opportunitate temporis, ibi: *Cognoscendum est etiam, in baptizandis electis* etc.

Duabus partibus remanentibus indivisis, prima dividitur in duas. In prima determinat, quis baptizari possit. Secundo, quis baptizari debeat, ibi: *Quaeri etiam solet, si corrupte verba proferantur.* Prima pars dividitur in duas. In prima determinat veritatem. In secunda solvit dubitationem, ibi: *Si vero opponitur de Ieremia* etc.

Secunda similiter dividitur in duas. In prima determinat, si ille qui baptizatus est corrupta forma, debeat rebaptizari. Secundo, utrum ille, de quo nulla est certitudo, possit vel debeat baptizari. Primum, ibi: *Solet quaeri, si corrupte* etc. Secundum facit ibi: *Praeterea sciendum est, quod illi, de quibus* etc.

*Porro cuncti ad baptismum venientes.* Supra[^4] egit Magister de Sacramento baptismi. Haec est pars in qua agit de sacramentalibus, et duo principaliter sunt, scilicet catechismus et exorcismus. Ideo haec pars habet duas partes. In prima agit de catechismo, in secundo de exorcismo, ibi: *Illa autem interrogatio et responsio* etc.[^6] Prima pars habet duas partes. In prima determinat veritatem. In secunda solvit dubitationem, ibi: *Si vero quaeritur, ex quo sensu pro parvulo* etc.

<!-- page 149 -->

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam huius partis tria principaliter quaeruntur.

Primo quaeritur de idoneitate ex parte baptizati.

Secundo de intentione ex parte baptizantis.

Tertio vero de sacramentalibus annexis baptismo.

Circa primum quaeruntur duo.

Primo quaeritur, utrum aliquis possit in utero baptizari, sicut et sanctificari.

Secundo, utrum sanctificati in utero debeant baptizari.

## English

## COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION VI

### Part II.

### *On the things annexed to the power of baptizing.*

*This too must be known, that although one be immersed three times, etc.*[^2]

FOR THE TEXT OF THE MASTER, SEE ABOVE, PAGE 133.

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Above the Master treated of the power of baptizing: here he treats of the things annexed to that power[^3]. And since three things are annexed — on the part of the one baptized, fitness; on the part of the one baptizing, intention; on the part of the time, opportuneness[^5] — therefore he first determines who can be baptized. Second, whether intention is necessary for baptism, there: *It is wont to be asked concerning him who in jest* etc. Third, concerning the opportuneness of time, there: *It must also be known, in baptizing the elect* etc.

The two [latter] parts remaining undivided, the first is divided into two. In the first he determines who can be baptized. Second, who ought to be baptized, there: *It is also wont to be asked, if the words are uttered corruptly.* The first part is divided into two. In the first he determines the truth. In the second he resolves a doubt, there: *But if an objection is raised concerning Jeremiah* etc.

The second [part] is likewise divided into two. In the first he determines whether he who has been baptized under a corrupted form ought to be rebaptized. Second, whether he of whom there is no certainty can or ought to be baptized. The first, there: *It is wont to be asked, if corruptly* etc. He treats the second there: *Furthermore it must be known that those concerning whom* etc.

*Moreover all who come to baptism.* Above[^4] the Master treated of the Sacrament of baptism. This is the part in which he treats of the sacramentals, and there are two principally, namely catechism and exorcism. Therefore this part has two parts. In the first he treats of catechism, in the second of exorcism, there: *But that interrogation and response* etc.[^6] The first part has two parts. In the first he determines the truth. In the second he resolves a doubt, there: *But if it is asked, from what sense, for the little child* etc.

<!-- page 149 -->

### THE ORDER OF QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of this part, three things are principally asked.

First it is asked concerning fitness on the part of the one baptized.

Second, concerning intention on the part of the one baptizing.

Third, indeed, concerning the sacramentals annexed to baptism.

Concerning the first, two things are asked.

First it is asked whether anyone can be baptized in the womb, just as he can also be sanctified.

Second, whether those sanctified in the womb ought to be baptized.

[^2]: **La.** Cod. *N et eius fides.* Superius pro *quia non* cod. V *quia nondum.*
    **En.** Codex *N and its witnesses.* Above, for *quia non* ("because not") codex V reads *quia nondum* ("because not yet").

[^3]: **La.** Hic in lit. Magistri, c. 2. in fine, ubi etiam in verbis Augustini tangitur factum Petri, de quo vide supra d. 3. p. II. a. 3. q. 2.
    **En.** Here in the text of the Master, c. 2, at the end, where also in the words of Augustine the deed of Peter is touched upon, concerning which see above, d. 3, p. II, a. 3, q. 2.

[^4]: **La.** Edd. in hac divisione textus multa mutaverunt et perperam, cum non attenderint, ultimam partem de sacramentalibus, quae incipit: *Porro cuncti*, supra d. 3, divisio textus (pag. 64) declaratam esse secundam principalem partem totius tractatus de baptismo. Edd. igitur hic sic prosequuntur: *Et dividitur haec pars in duas. In prima agit de annexis Sacramento baptismi. In secunda de sacramentalibus*, ibi: *Illa autem interrogatio* etc.
    **En.** The editions in this division of the text changed much, and wrongly, since they did not attend to the fact that the last part on the sacramentals, which begins *Moreover all*, was declared above at d. 3, division of the text (page 64) to be the second principal part of the whole treatise on baptism. The editions therefore here proceed thus: *And this part is divided into two. In the first he treats of the things annexed to the Sacrament of baptism. In the second, of the sacramentals*, there: *But that interrogation* etc.

[^5]: **La.** Edd., posito superius *quatuor* pro *tria* et *intentionis conformitas* pro *intentio*, hic addunt: *et ex parte levantis responsionis fidelitas*, et inferius post *electis* etc. subiungunt: *Quarto de levati responsione, ibi: Porro cuncti.*
    **En.** The editions, having set *four* above in place of *three* and *conformity of intention* in place of *intention*, here add: *and on the part of the one lifting [from the font], fidelity of response*; and below, after *the elect* etc., they subjoin: *Fourth, concerning the response of the one lifted, there: Moreover all.*

[^6]: **La.** Edd. legunt: Item, quarta pars habet duas. In prima determinat veritatem quantum ad baptizantis interrogationem, vel baptizandi, si adultus est, vel patrini, si parvulus est, responsionem. In secunda solvit dubitationem, ibi: *Sed adhuc quaeritur, ex quo sensu* etc. Similiter secunda pars, in qua agit de sacramentalibus, exorcismo scilicet et catechismo, habet duas. In prima ponit ordinem in exorcizando et catechizando parvulum, et horum nominum et aliorum quorundam notificationem. In secunda ponit exorcismi efficaciam, ibi: *Parvuli exsufflantur* etc.
    **En.** The editions read: Likewise, the fourth part has two [parts]. In the first it determines the truth as regards the interrogation of the one baptizing, or the response of the one to be baptized, if he is an adult, or of the godparent, if he is a little child. In the second it resolves a doubt, there: *But still it is asked, from what sense* etc. Likewise the second part, in which he treats of the sacramentals, namely exorcism and catechism, has two [parts]. In the first it sets the order in exorcizing and catechizing the little child, and the notification of these names and of certain others. In the second it sets the efficacy of exorcism, there: *The little children are breathed upon* etc.
