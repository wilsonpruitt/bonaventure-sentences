## Latin
<!-- page 39 -->

### QUAESTIO II. *De forma sive de integritate circumcisionis.*

Secundo quaeritur de forma sive de integritate circumcisionis. Et dicit Hugo in libro de Sacramentis[^1], quod tria sunt, quae sunt de integritate eius, scilicet *sexus masculinus, membrum praeputii et dies octavus.*

1. Sed ostenditur, quod de integritate huius Sacramenti non debet esse *sexus:* quia remedium debet respondere morbo; sed in morbo non est sexus distinctio nec determinatio, quia communiter est in omni sexu: ergo nec in remedio debet esse distinctio.

2. Item, videtur, quod *membrum praeputii* non debeat esse de substantia Sacramenti: quia Sacramenta sunt in honestatem et decorem; sed illud membrum est verecundum et inhonestum: ergo nullo modo debet fieri circa illud membrum; aut si fit, quaero, quare magis circa illud quam circa aliud?

Item, quod *octavus dies* non sit de substantia Sacramenti, ostenditur:

1. Quia nec in baptismo nec in sacramentis legis naturae est determinatio temporis: ergo nec in Sacramentis legis scriptae debet esse[^2].

2. Item, si tempus illud est de necessitate, si post fiat, Sacramentum nullius est efficaciae: ergo nihil valuit Abrahae nec aliquibus de domo eius[^3]; et iterum, nihil valuit illis quos circumcidit Iosue; et iterum, nihil valebat proselytis. Et ex hoc sequitur, quod si aliquis negligebat diem octavum, quod nullo modo posset curari ab originali, quia nec per circumcisionem nec alio modo, quia non erat aliud remedium.

3. Item, si fuit de necessitate: ergo omnes illi viri, qui fuerunt in deserto, fuerunt transgressores, quia nullus eorum fuit circumcisus.

4. Item, si erat de substantia, tunc ergo non debebat praeveniri: ergo parvulum morientem ante octavum diem necesse erat damnari, quia masculus incircumcisus damnabatur[^4]; et ante non debebat circumcidi: ergo etc.

**Sed contra:** 1. Quod sit de *necessitate,* probatur: quia circumcisio est Sacramentum necessitatis, quia cadit sub praecepto; sed omnia ista tria praecepta sunt, Genesis decimo septimo[^5][^6]: ergo omnia sunt de necessitate. Ibi enim dicitur: *Masculus,*

<!-- page 40 -->

*cuius praeputii caro circumcisa non fuerit, peribit* etc.; et iterum: *Infans octo dierum circumcidetur.*

2. Item, quod tempus sit de substantia, ostenditur: quia circumcisio significabat utramque circumcisionem, scilicet primam in anima a *culpa,* et secundam in corpore a *miseria,* quae futura erat in octava resurrectionis: ergo octavus dies erat de integritate significationis, ergo et de integritate Sacramenti[^1b].

3. Item, praeceptum de circumcisione est praeceptum *affirmativum,* quod obligat ad aliquod tempus, non ad omne, ergo determinato tempore in praecepto, obligat[^2b] ad illud: ergo cum in praecepto illo sit determinatio temporis, in quo instituitur circumcisio, tempus determinatum est de necessitate circumcisionis.

> ### CONCLUSIO.
>
> De necessitate circumcisionis simpliciter sunt sexus masculinus et membrum; tempus vero necessario observandum erat ob praeceptum.

**Respondeo:** Dicendum, quod duo ex istis, scilicet *sexus* et *membrum,* sunt de necessitate circumcisionis simpliciter; sed *tempus* uno modo est de necessitate, alio modo non. Est enim in hoc Sacramento tria considerare, scilicet *efficaciam, significantiam* et *observantiam.* Quantum ad *efficaciam* nihil facit; quantum ad *significantiam* est de congruitate, quia expressius significat; quantum ad *observantiam* est de necessitate, quia cadit sub praecepto; unde peccat qui negligit observare.

1. Ad illud ergo quod obiicitur primo, quod in morbo non est distinctio, ergo etc.: dicendum, quod circumcisio non tantum fuit in *remedium,* sed etiam in *signum distinctionis,* ut gens Iudaeorum ab aliis discerneretur, ex quo Christus debebat ab ea nasci[^3b]; et quia propagatio et genealogia attenditur penes mares: ideo illis data fuit, non mulieribus.

2. Ad illud quod obiicitur, quod membrum illud non deberet esse, quia est verecundum: dicendum, quod membrum illud maxime conveniebat *significationi* circumcisionis et *efficaciae.* Circumcisio enim data fuit in *pactum,* sicut dicitur Genesis decimo septimo[^4b], et hoc pactum est certitudo, quod Christus ex gente illa esset nasciturus. Fuit etiam data in *remedium contra originale peccatum;* unde dicitur Genesis decimo septimo: *Masculus, cuius caro praeputii circumcisa non fuerit, peribit* etc. Fuit similiter data in *meritum obedientiae;* meruit enim Abraham et posteri eius sic Domino obediendo. Fuit etiam data in *signum repressionis concupiscentiae;* unde significat, quid sit agendum, sive a quo sit cessandum. — Et quia per praeputium est *generatio,* et sic convenit *pacto* generationis; est *originalis culpae*[^5b] *transfusio,* et sic convenit *remedio circumcisionis;* et in praeputio regnat *inobedientia* et *concupiscentia,* ab oppositis per ipsius significationem et ab ipso factam ablationem et meretur obedientia et significatur continentia. Ideo rectissime convenit[^6b]. — Quod ergo dicit, quod est membrum ignobile; dicendum, quod de sui natura est nobile, sed factum est ignobile propter culpam, unde per remedium nobilitatur; unde primae ad Corinthios duodecimo[^7b] dicitur, quod *membris ignobilioribus abundantiorem honorem circumdamus;* et hoc intelligitur, dum passionibus et desideriis obviatur.

Ad illud quod quaeritur de tempore, dicendum, sicut dictum est, quod quantum ad efficaciam nihil facit; unde aeque bene curabat circumcisio proselytos, ut etiam parvulos, est tamen congruentia quantum ad significationem.

1. Quod obiicitur de baptismo, dicendum, quod *res* circumcisionis erat circumcisio a *culpa* et a *miseria*[^8b]. Circumcisio a *culpa* significabatur in actu et materia, sed circumcisio a *miseria* per actum et materiam non significabatur; et ideo congruum fuit *tempus* addere, per quod significaretur. Baptismus autem ratione immersionis et elevationis expresse significat mortem et resurrectionem; ideo non fuit congruum addere. Et sic *dies octavus* est de *bene esse* circumcisionis quantum ad *significantiam;* est tamen de necessitate quantum ad *observantiam,* quia cadit in praecepto[^9b]. — Et ratio huius fuit: quia Sacramentum illud fuit poenale; ideo oportuit observare tempus, quia, si ante octavum diem circumcideretur, propter teneritudinem posset perire; si ultra differretur, esset periculum propter mortem,

<!-- page 41 -->

et libenter differrent propter dolorem. Ideo voluit eis Dominus praefigere terminum.

3. 4. Ad illud quod obiicitur, quod si est de necessitate praecepti, non poterat differri nec praeveniri sine peccato; dicendum, quod circa hoc duplex fuit opinio: una quidem, quod praeveniri non poterat nec differri sine peccato, nisi interveniret Dei *auctoritas,* vel etiam *necessitas.* Et utraque fuit in viris, qui fuerunt in deserto incircumcisi, ut dicunt: *necessitas* propter periculum, quia ad motum nubis movebantur[^1c], et ideo circumcisionem servare non poterant; divina etiam *auctoritas* fuit dispensans, quia in deserto erant, in quo non poterant gentibus commisceri nec indigebant distingui. Rursus, Dominus voluit, ob *figuram* circumcisionem differri, ut una generalis circumcisio fieret in terra promissionis in signum illius quae futura erat in patria. Et isti dicunt, quodsi de parvulis timerent, quod tempus circumcisionis poterat et debebat praeveniri; alioquin puer damnabatur, sicut modo damnatur puer non baptizatus. — Sed haec positio, licet probabilis videatur, tamen mori repugnat Iudaeorum, quem nunc observant; et credibile est, quod a Patribus acceperunt. Nullum enim ante octavum diem circumcidunt. — Contrarietatem etiam habet in se ipsa: quia, si ob necessitatem infirmitatis poterat differri, cum parvulus erat in gravi infirmitate, non debebat secundum eos circumcidi, sed diutius exspectari; et iterum, si non poterat sine peccato differri, qualiter poterat praeveniri, ex quo Dominus illum[^2c] praefixerat?

Et ideo alia opinio est et probabilior, quod non debebant praevenire nec differre, nisi Dominus ipse dispensaret, alioquin peccabant. Et si tu obiicias, quod tunc puer damnaretur, quia scribitur: *Masculus, cuius caro* etc.; dicendum, quod secundum translationem Septuaginta habetur: *Cuius praeputii caro octava die circumcisa non fuerit;* et quamvis in nostra translatione non repetatur[^3c], tamen intelligitur repeti sub ea conditione, in qua fuit praeceptum. Et quia[^4c] non erant obligati, eodem remedio curabantur, quo mulieres. — Sed huic positioni occurrit difficultas: esto enim, quod parvulus ille mundatus illo remedio viveret usque ad octo dies[^5c], et tunc non circumcideretur, aut salvatur, aut non. Si non salvatur: ergo ex peccato patris amittit gratiam, aut eam non habuit; sed constat, quod propter peccatum patris non potest eam perdere: ergo nunquam habuit. — Si salvatur; et non est octavo die circumcisus: ergo non est verum, quod dicit Dominus: *Masculus, cuius caro* etc.

Propter hoc igitur respondendum, quod currente circumcisione, alia remedia non disponebant parvulum nisi cum hac conditione, quod ipse esset impossibilis[^6c] ad implendum mandatum de circumcisione: et ita propter impossibilitatem absolvebatur et si moriturus erat ante octavum diem, disponebant, et curabatur; sed victurus non curabatur, quousque mandatum esset totum impletum. Et hoc totum est probabile; et ideo hanc opinionem credo saniorem. — Si autem Dominus dispensaret, tunc secus esset; et ita dicunt factum esse circa viros, qui fuerunt in deserto. — Aliqui tamen dicunt, quod peccaverunt, quia ex contemptu dimiserunt[^7c]; sed tamen illud non est credibile, quod omnes ita mandatum Dei contemnerent.

Si autem obiicias similiter de parvulo, qui praeventus morte non baptizatur, utrum in fide parentum salvetur; dico, quod non, quia baptismus propter suam generalitatem et perfectionem et temporis indeterminationem alia remedia evacuavit; non sic autem circumcisio. — Huius autem positionis probabilitas habetur ex *auctoritate,* quia Proverbiorum quarto[^8c] dicit Salomon: *Unigenitus eram coram matre mea;* ubi dicitur in Glossa, quod filius Bersabee, alius parvulus, « non fuit nominatus, quia ante octavum diem est mortuus », et ita non fuit circumcisus. Habetur ex *ratione,* quia nullum animal offerebatur Domino ante octavum diem[^9c]. Habetur etiam ex *more Iudaeorum,* quem modo observant. Et ideo haec positio magis est tenenda[^10c].

## English

### QUESTION II. *On the form, or the integrity, of circumcision.*

Secondly, the question is asked concerning the form, or the integrity, of circumcision. And Hugh says in the book *On the Sacraments*[^1] that there are three things which belong to its integrity, namely *the male sex, the member of the foreskin, and the eighth day.*

1. But it is shown that *sex* ought not to belong to the integrity of this Sacrament: because the remedy ought to correspond to the disease; but in disease there is no distinction or determination of sex, since it is common to every sex: therefore neither in the remedy ought there to be any distinction.

2. Likewise, it seems that *the member of the foreskin* ought not to belong to the substance of the Sacrament: because the Sacraments are for decency and seemliness; but that member is shameful and unseemly: therefore in no way ought anything to be done about that member; or if it is done, I ask, why rather about that one than about another?

Likewise, that *the eighth day* is not of the substance of the Sacrament is shown:

1. Because neither in baptism nor in the sacraments of the law of nature is there a determination of time: therefore neither in the Sacraments of the written law ought there to be[^2].

2. Likewise, if that time is of necessity, then if it is done afterward, the Sacrament is of no efficacy: therefore it was of no avail to Abraham nor to any of his household[^3]; and again, it was of no avail to those whom Joshua circumcised; and again, it was of no avail to proselytes. And from this it follows that if anyone neglected the eighth day, he could in no way be healed from original [sin], because neither by circumcision nor by any other means, since there was no other remedy.

3. Likewise, if it was of necessity: then all those men who were in the desert were transgressors, since none of them was circumcised.

4. Likewise, if it was of the substance, then it ought not to have been anticipated: therefore a little one dying before the eighth day would necessarily have to be damned, because an uncircumcised male was condemned[^4]; and before [the eighth day] he ought not to have been circumcised: therefore etc.

**On the contrary:** 1. That it is of *necessity* is proved: because circumcision is a Sacrament of necessity, since it falls under a precept; but all those three things are commanded, in Genesis seventeen[^5][^6]: therefore all are of necessity. For there it is said: *The male,*

<!-- page 40 -->

*the flesh of whose foreskin shall not have been circumcised, shall perish,* etc.; and again: *The infant of eight days shall be circumcised.*

2. Likewise, that the time is of the substance is shown: because circumcision signified both circumcisions, namely the first in the soul from *guilt,* and the second in the body from *misery,* which was to come at the eighth [day] of the resurrection: therefore the eighth day was of the integrity of the signification, therefore also of the integrity of the Sacrament[^1b].

3. Likewise, the precept concerning circumcision is an *affirmative* precept, which obliges for some time, not for every time; therefore, the time being determined in the precept, it obliges for that [time]: therefore, since in that precept there is a determination of the time at which circumcision is instituted, the determined time is of the necessity of circumcision.

> ### CONCLUSION.
>
> Of the absolute necessity of circumcision are the male sex and the member; but the time had to be necessarily observed on account of the precept.

**I respond:** It must be said that two of these, namely *sex* and *the member,* are of the necessity of circumcision absolutely; but the *time* in one way is of necessity, in another way not. For in this Sacrament three things are to be considered, namely its *efficacy, signification,* and *observance.* As to *efficacy* it contributes nothing; as to *signification* it belongs to fittingness, because it signifies more expressly; as to *observance* it is of necessity, because it falls under a precept; hence he sins who neglects to observe it.

1. To that, then, which is objected first, that in disease there is no distinction, therefore etc.: it must be said that circumcision was not only for a *remedy,* but also for a *sign of distinction,* that the people of the Jews might be distinguished from others, from whom Christ was to be born[^3b]; and because propagation and genealogy are reckoned through the males: therefore it was given to them, not to the women.

2. To that which is objected, that that member ought not to be [the place of it], because it is shameful: it must be said that that member was most fitting for the *signification* of circumcision and for its *efficacy.* For circumcision was given as a *covenant,* as is said in Genesis seventeen[^4b], and this covenant is the assurance that Christ was to be born from that people. It was also given as a *remedy against original sin;* hence it is said in Genesis seventeen: *The male, the flesh of whose foreskin shall not have been circumcised, shall perish,* etc. It was likewise given as a *merit of obedience;* for Abraham and his posterity merited by thus obeying the Lord. It was also given as a *sign of the repression of concupiscence;* hence it signifies what is to be done, or from what one is to cease. — And because through the foreskin there is *generation,* and so it accords with the *covenant* of generation; it is the *transfusion of original guilt*[^5b], and so it accords with the *remedy of circumcision;* and in the foreskin there reign *disobedience* and *concupiscence,* and from the opposites, by its signification and by the removal made upon it, obedience is merited and continence is signified. Therefore it accords most rightly[^6b]. — As to what he says, that it is an ignoble member; it must be said that of its own nature it is noble, but it was made ignoble on account of guilt, whence through the remedy it is ennobled; hence in First Corinthians twelve[^7b] it is said that *upon the more ignoble members we bestow more abundant honor;* and this is understood [to hold] when the passions and desires are resisted.

To that which is asked concerning the time, it must be said, as has been said, that as to efficacy it contributes nothing; hence circumcision healed proselytes equally as well as little ones, yet there is a fittingness as to signification.

1. As to what is objected concerning baptism, it must be said that the *reality* of circumcision was circumcision from *guilt* and from *misery*[^8b]. Circumcision from *guilt* was signified in the act and the matter, but circumcision from *misery* was not signified through the act and the matter; and therefore it was fitting to add the *time,* by which it might be signified. But baptism, by reason of immersion and emergence, expressly signifies death and resurrection; therefore it was not fitting to add [a time]. And thus *the eighth day* belongs to the *well-being* of circumcision as to *signification;* yet it is of necessity as to *observance,* because it falls under a precept[^9b]. — And the reason for this was: that that Sacrament was penal; therefore it was necessary to observe the time, because, if [the child] were circumcised before the eighth day, on account of his tenderness he might perish; if it were deferred longer, there would be danger on account of death,

<!-- page 41 -->

and they would gladly defer it on account of the pain. Therefore the Lord willed to set a fixed term for them.

3. 4. To that which is objected, that if it is of the necessity of the precept, it could not be deferred nor anticipated without sin; it must be said that on this point there was a twofold opinion: one indeed, that it could not be anticipated nor deferred without sin, unless the *authority* of God intervened, or also *necessity.* And both were present in the men who were uncircumcised in the desert, as they say: *necessity* on account of the danger, because they were moved at the motion of the cloud[^1c], and therefore could not observe circumcision; the divine *authority* also was dispensing, because they were in the desert, where they could not mingle with the nations nor needed to be distinguished. Again, the Lord willed, for the sake of the *figure,* that circumcision be deferred, so that one general circumcision might be made in the land of promise as a sign of that which was to come in the fatherland. And these men say that if they feared concerning the little ones, the time of circumcision could and ought to be anticipated; otherwise the child was damned, just as now an unbaptized child is damned. — But this position, although it may seem probable, nevertheless is repugnant to the custom of the Jews, which they now observe; and it is credible that they received it from the Fathers. For they circumcise none before the eighth day. — It also has a contrariety within itself: because, if on account of the necessity of infirmity it could be deferred, when the little one was in grave infirmity, he ought not, according to them, to be circumcised, but to be awaited longer; and again, if it could not be deferred without sin, how could it be anticipated, since the Lord had fixed it[^2c]?

And therefore there is another and more probable opinion: that they ought neither to anticipate nor to defer, unless the Lord himself dispensed; otherwise they sinned. And if you object that then the child would be damned, because it is written: *The male, the flesh of whom* etc.; it must be said that according to the translation of the Seventy it reads: *Whose foreskin's flesh shall not have been circumcised on the eighth day;* and although in our translation it is not repeated[^3c], yet it is understood to be repeated under that condition under which it was commanded. And because[^4c] they were not obliged, they were healed by the same remedy as the women. — But a difficulty meets this position: for suppose that that little one, cleansed by that remedy, lived up to eight days[^5c], and then was not circumcised — either he is saved, or not. If he is not saved: then by the sin of the father he loses grace, or he did not have it; but it is established that on account of the sin of the father he cannot lose it: therefore he never had it. — If he is saved; and he is not circumcised on the eighth day: then it is not true what the Lord says: *The male, whose flesh* etc.

On account of this, therefore, it must be answered that, circumcision being in force, the other remedies did not dispose the little one except on this condition, that he himself was incapable[^6c] of fulfilling the mandate concerning circumcision: and so on account of the impossibility he was absolved, and if he was about to die before the eighth day, they would dispose [him], and he was healed; but one about to live was not healed, until the mandate was wholly fulfilled. And this whole [view] is probable; and therefore I believe this opinion the sounder. — But if the Lord dispensed, then it would be otherwise; and so they say it was done concerning the men who were in the desert. — Some, however, say that they sinned, because they omitted it out of contempt[^7c]; but yet that is not credible, that all should so despise the mandate of God.

But if you object likewise concerning the little one who, prevented by death, is not baptized, whether he is saved in the faith of his parents; I say no, because baptism, on account of its generality and perfection and the indeterminateness of its time, evacuated the other remedies; but not so circumcision. — Now the probability of this position is had from *authority,* because in Proverbs four[^8c] Solomon says: *I was the only-begotten before my mother;* where it is said in the Gloss that the son of Bathsheba, another little one, "was not named, because he died before the eighth day," and so he was not circumcised. It is had from *reason,* because no animal was offered to the Lord before the eighth day[^9c]. It is had also from the *custom of the Jews,* which they now observe. And therefore this position is rather to be held[^10c].
