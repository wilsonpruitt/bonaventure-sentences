# bon-sent-IV-d16-p1-divisio
## *Commentarius in Distinctionem XVI — Divisio textus et tractatio quaestionum (Pars I)*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 382 -->

### COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XVI.

#### Pars I.

*De partibus integralibus Sacramenti poenitentiae.*

> In perfectione autem poenitentiae tria observanda sunt etc.

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Supra egit Magister de poenitentia in sua generalitate. In hac parte agit de eius partibus. Et quoniam principalis pars poenitentiae, ut est Sacramentum Ecclesiae, est confessio; ideo primo assignat partes poenitentiae. Secundo vero principaliter et specialiter agit de confessione, et de his quae spectant ad eam, infra distinctione decima septima: *Hic oritur quaestio multiplex* etc.

Prima pars duas habet secundum duo genera partium: quaedam enim sunt partes integrales, quaedam subiectivae. Ideo primo agit de integralibus, ex quibus constat poenitentia perfecta. Secundo de subiectivis[^1], ibi: *Praedictis adiiciendum est, quod tribus modis agitur poenitentia.*

Prima pars habet tres. In prima assignat illas partes, ex quibus poenitentia integratur, confirmans auctoritate Chrysostomi et Augustini. In secunda tradit modum, secundum quem veram constituunt[^2] poenitentiam, ibi: *Ideo discretio necessaria est poenitenti.* In tertia determinat modum, quomodo non veram poenitentiam faciunt, sed falsam, ibi: *Et sicut sunt digni fructus poenitentiae* etc.

<!-- page 383 -->

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam huius partis quaeritur de contritione; et circa hoc principaliter quatuor quaeruntur.

Primo quaeritur de contritione quantum ad quidditatem.

Secundo, quantum ad quantitatem.

Tertio, quantum ad durationem.

Quarto, quantum ad intensionem.

Quantum ad primum quaeruntur duo.

Primo quaeritur de contritione, quid sit quantum ad suam substantiam.

Secundo, quid sit in genere moris.

### ARTICULUS I.

#### *De contritione quantum ad quidditatem.*

### QUAESTIO I.

#### *Quid sit contritio quoad suam substantiam.*

## English
<!-- page 382 -->

### COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XVI.

#### Part I.

*On the integral parts of the Sacrament of penance.*

> Now in the perfection of penance three things are to be observed, etc.

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Above the Master treated of penance in its generality. In this part he treats of its parts. And since the principal part of penance, as it is a Sacrament of the Church, is confession; therefore he first assigns the parts of penance. Second he treats principally and specially of confession, and of the things which pertain to it, below in the seventeenth distinction: *Here a manifold question arises,* etc.

The first part has two [members] according to the two kinds of parts: for some are integral parts, some subjective. Therefore he first treats of the integral ones, of which perfect penance consists. Second of the subjective ones[^1], there: *To the foregoing it must be added, that penance is carried out in three ways.*

The first part has three [members]. In the first he assigns those parts of which penance is integrated, confirming [them] by the authority of Chrysostom and Augustine. In the second he hands down the manner according to which they constitute[^2] true penance, there: *Therefore discretion is necessary for the penitent.* In the third he determines the manner in which they make not true penance, but false, there: *And as they are worthy fruits of penance,* etc.

<!-- page 383 -->

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of this part inquiry is made concerning contrition; and concerning this four things are principally asked.

First it is asked concerning contrition with respect to its quiddity.

Second, with respect to its quantity.

Third, with respect to its duration.

Fourth, with respect to its intensity.

With respect to the first, two things are asked.

First it is asked concerning contrition, what it is with respect to its substance.

Second, what it is in the genus of morals.

### ARTICLE I.

#### *On contrition with respect to its quiddity.*

### QUESTION I.

#### *What contrition is with respect to its substance.*
