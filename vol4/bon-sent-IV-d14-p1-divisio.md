## *Commentarius in Distinctionem XIV — Divisio textus et tractatio quaestionum (Pars I)*

---

## Latin
<!-- page 317 -->

### COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XIV.

#### Pars I.

*De poenitentia in generali.*

> Post haec de poenitentia agendum est.

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Terminatis tribus partibus principalibus, in quibus Magister egit de tribus Sacramentis, hic incipit quarta pars, in qua agit de Sacramento poenitentiae. Dividitur autem haec pars in duas. In prima parte agit de poenitentia in generali. In secunda agit specialiter de eius partibus, infra distinctione decima sexta: *In perfectione autem poenitentiae tria observanda sunt.*

Prima pars habet duas. In prima Magister determinat veritatem. In secunda elidit duos errores circa poenitentiam, infra eadem distinctione, ibi: *His verbis quidam vehementius* etc.

Prima pars habet tres partes. In prima determinat ordinem ipsius ad baptismum. In secunda parte determinat eius multiplicitatem, ibi: *Baptismus tantum est Sacramentum, sed poenitentia* etc. In tertia post distinctionem multiplicitatis ponit notificationes poenitentiae-virtutis, ibi: *Poenitentia dicitur a puniendo, qua quis punit illicita* etc., ubi ponit rationem a parte nominis et a parte rei.

<!-- page 318 -->

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam huius partis quaeritur de poenitentia-virtute, et circa hoc duo principaliter quaeruntur.

Primo quaeritur de ipsa absolute.

Secundo, in comparatione.

Circa primum quaeruntur tria.

Primo quaeritur, utrum poenitentia sit virtus.

Secundo, dato quod sic, quaeritur, utrum sit virtus generalis, an specialis.

Tertio quaeritur, utrum sit virtus theologica, vel cardinalis.

### ARTICULUS I.

#### *De virtute poenitentiae absolute.*

### QUAESTIO I.

#### *Utrum poenitentia sit virtus.*

## English
<!-- page 317 -->

### COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XIV.

#### Part I.

*On penance in general.*

> After these things penance must be treated.

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Having concluded the three principal parts in which the Master treated of the three Sacraments, here begins the fourth part, in which he treats of the Sacrament of penance. Now this part is divided into two. In the first part he treats of penance in general. In the second he treats specially of its parts, below in the sixteenth distinction: *But in the perfection of penance three things are to be observed.*

The first part has two [parts]. In the first the Master determines the truth. In the second he refutes two errors concerning penance, below in the same distinction, there: *By these words certain men more vehemently* etc.

The first part has three parts. In the first he determines its order to baptism. In the second part he determines its multiplicity, there: *Baptism is only a Sacrament, but penance* etc. In the third, after the distinction of the multiplicity, he sets down the notifications of penance-the-virtue, there: *Penance is so called from punishing, by which one punishes things unlawful* etc., where he sets down the account from the side of the name and from the side of the thing.

<!-- page 318 -->

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of this part inquiry is made concerning penance-the-virtue, and concerning this two [things] are principally asked.

First, inquiry is made concerning it absolutely.

Second, in comparison.

Concerning the first, three [things] are asked.

First it is asked, whether penance is a virtue.

Second, granted that it is, it is asked, whether it is a general virtue, or a special one.

Third it is asked, whether it is a theological virtue, or a cardinal one.

### ARTICLE I.

#### *On the virtue of penance absolutely.*

### QUESTION I.

#### *Whether penance is a virtue.*
