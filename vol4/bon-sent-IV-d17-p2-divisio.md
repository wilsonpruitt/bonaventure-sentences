## *Division of the text — Part II: On the necessity of sacramental confession*

---

## Latin

<!-- page 435 -->

# COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XVII.

## Pars II.

### *De necessitate confessionis sacramentalis.*

*Iam secundum quaestionis articulum inspiciamus* etc.

TEXTUM MAGISTRI VIDE SUPRA PAG. 415.

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Supra exsecutus est et determinavit Magister primam quaestionem; hic tangit et determinat secundam, scilicet utrum necesse sit confiteri homini, an sufficiat soli Deo confiteri. Et dividitur haec pars in duas. In prima adducit auctoritates ad falsam partem. In secunda adducit auctoritates ad veram, ibi: *Sed quod sacerdotibus confiteri oporteat* etc.; in quibus clarescit confessionis vocalis institutio et peccatorum obligatio; in quibus etiam consistit praesentis quaestionis difficultas et determinatio.

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam huius partis quaeritur de confessione vocali. Et circa hoc duo principaliter quaeruntur. Primo quaeritur de confessione quantum ad praeceptum instituentis. Secundo, quantum ad obligationem exsequentis.

Quantum ad primum quaeruntur tria. Primo quaeritur, utrum mandatum de confessione pertineat ad legem naturae. Secundo, utrum pertineat ad legem scriptam, vel legem gratiae. Tertio quaeritur, a quo fuerit instituta, utrum a Christo, an ab aliquo eius Apostolo.

## English

<!-- page 435 -->

# COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XVII.

## Part II.

### *On the necessity of sacramental confession.*

*Let us now examine the second article of the question,* etc.

FOR THE TEXT OF THE MASTER, SEE ABOVE, PAGE 415.

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Above, the Master carried through and determined the first question; here he touches upon and determines the second, namely, whether it is necessary to confess to a man, or whether it suffices to confess to God alone. And this part is divided into two. In the first he adduces authorities for the false side. In the second he adduces authorities for the true side, there at: *But that one ought to confess to priests,* etc.; in which the institution of vocal confession and the obligation upon sinners become clear; in which also consists the difficulty and determination of the present question.

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of this part, inquiry is made concerning vocal confession. And concerning this, two things are principally asked. First, it is asked concerning confession as to the precept of the one instituting it. Second, as to the obligation of the one carrying it out.

As to the first, three things are asked. First, it is asked whether the command concerning confession pertains to the law of nature. Second, whether it pertains to the written law, or the law of grace. Third, it is asked by whom it was instituted, whether by Christ, or by some Apostle of his.
