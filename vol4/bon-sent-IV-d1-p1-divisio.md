# IV Sent., d. 1, p. 1 — Divisio textus
## *Commentary on Distinction I, Part I — Division of the text and Treatment of the questions*

---

## Latin

<!-- page 10 -->

## COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM I.

#### Pars I.

### *De Sacramentis in generali.*

*Samaritanus enim* etc.[^1n]

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Et secundum hoc liber iste habet duas partes[^1]. In prima agit de Sacramentis secundum sanitatem, quam tribuunt. In secunda agit de gloria, ad quam disponunt, infra distinctione quadragesima tertia: *Postremo de conditione resurrectionis*[^2][^2n]. Prima pars habet duas. In prima determinat de Sacramentis in generali. In secunda in speciali, infra distinctione secunda: *Iam ad Sacramenta novae legis accedamus*. Prima pars, quae continet praesentem distinctionem, habet tres partes. In prima enim ponit partem prooemialem. In secunda principalem, ibi: *Sacramentum est sacrae rei signum* etc. In tertia incidentem, ibi: *Fuit tamen inter illa Sacramenta quoddam* etc.

In parte autem prooemiali duo facit. Primo per quandam parabolam ostendit, quae sit Sacramentorum causa institutiva. Secundo dicit, quae sint generaliter de Sacramentis determinanda, ibi: *De quibus quatuor primo consideranda sunt*.

Pars vero principalis, in qua determinat de Sacramentis in generali, habet quatuor secundum illa quatuor, quae Magister praemittit determinanda. Primo enim determinat, quid sit Sacramentum. Secundo vero, quare institutum, ibi: *Triplici autem ex causa Sacramenta instituta sunt*. Tertio vero, in quibus sit institutum, ibi: *Duo autem sunt, in quibus* etc. Quarto vero novorum[^3] et veterum assignat differentiam, ibi: *Iam videre restat differentiam* etc.[^3n]

<!-- page 11 -->

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam eorum quae in hac prima parte determinantur, quaeritur de Sacramentis in generali.

Et primo quaeritur de Sacramentorum institutione.

Secundo de significatione.

Tertio de continentia.

Quarto de efficacia.

Quinto de differentia Sacramentorum veterum et novorum.

Sexto de gratia in eis collata.

## English

<!-- page 10 -->

## COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION I.

#### Part I.

### *On the Sacraments in general.*

*For the Samaritan* etc.[^1n]

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

And accordingly this book has two parts[^1]. In the first it treats of the Sacraments according to the soundness which they confer. In the second it treats of the glory to which they dispose, below in the forty-third distinction: *Lastly, concerning the condition of the resurrection*[^2][^2n]. The first part has two [parts]. In the first it determines concerning the Sacraments in general. In the second, in particular, below in the second distinction: *Now let us approach the Sacraments of the new law*. The first part, which contains the present distinction, has three parts. For in the first it sets down the proemial part. In the second, the principal part, at: *A Sacrament is the sign of a sacred thing* etc. In the third, the incidental part, at: *There was, however, among those Sacraments a certain one* etc.

Now in the proemial part it does two things. First, by a certain parable it shows what is the instituting cause of the Sacraments. Second, it states what things are to be determined concerning the Sacraments in general, at: *Concerning which four things first occur to be considered*.

But the principal part, in which it determines concerning the Sacraments in general, has four [members] according to those four things which the Master sets forth to be determined. For first it determines what a Sacrament is. Second, why it was instituted, at: *For a threefold cause the Sacraments were instituted*. Third, in what it is instituted, at: *Now there are two things in which* etc. Fourth, it assigns the difference of the new[^3] and the old, at: *It now remains to see the difference* etc.[^3n]

<!-- page 11 -->

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of those things which are determined in this first part, inquiry is made concerning the Sacraments in general.

And first it is asked concerning the institution of the Sacraments.

Second, concerning their signification.

Third, concerning their content.

Fourth, concerning their efficacy.

Fifth, concerning the difference of the Sacraments of the old and of the new [law].

Sixth, concerning the grace conferred in them.

## Apparatus

> The numbered footnotes below correspond to markers in both the Latin body above and the English translation. They are the Quaracchi editors' notes to Bonaventure's commentary. [^1]: **La.** Hugo a S. Vict., *Sum. Sent.* tr. 4. c. 1; ex quo capitulo excerpsit Magister omnia quae in II. parte huius dist. habentur. Ipse Hugo hanc ultimam propositionem sumsit ex August. tr. 30. in Ioan. n. 5.
    **En.** Hugh of St. Victor, *Summa Sententiarum* tr. 4, c. 1; from which chapter the Master excerpted all the things which are contained in Part II of this distinction. Hugh himself took this last proposition from Augustine, *Tractate* 30 *on John*, n. 5.

[^2]: **La.** Ita codd. et ed. 1, in aliis *quia*; et mox pro *et iam* codd. AE et edd., excepta 1, *etiam*. — Hic respicitur Ps. 22, 2; cfr. ibi Glossa interlinearis.
    **En.** So the codices and the first edition read; in others *quia*; and shortly afterward, for *et iam* codices A, E and the editions, except the first, read *etiam*. — Here Psalm 22:2 is regarded; cf. the interlinear Gloss there.

[^3]: **La.** Loco supra cit. et etiam in *Comment. in Luc.* 2. Loci Scripturae sunt Ioan. 3, 5, deinde Gen. 17, 14. — Inferius pro *dudum clamat* plurimae edd. *dudum clamabat*, ed. 1 *clamavit*.
    **En.** In the place cited above and also in the *Commentary on Luke* 2. The Scripture passages are John 3:5, then Genesis 17:14. — Below, for *dudum clamat* (long ago cries out) most editions read *dudum clamabat* (was crying out), the first edition *clamavit* (cried out).

[^1n]: **La.** Coniungenda est ista propositio cum fine prooemii. Cfr. supra pag. 3, nota 5. — Edd. praeter fidem codd. legunt sic: *Postquam Magister supra in tertio libro egit de reparatione per gratiam mediatoris Dei et hominum homini praestita, consequenter in hoc quarto agit de Sacramentis, quibus fragilitatis humanae peccatorum vulnera ligantur et curantur* etc.
    **En.** This proposition is to be joined with the end of the proem. Cf. above p. 3, note 5. — The editions, against the trust of the codices, read thus: *After the Master treated above in the third book of the reparation granted to man through the grace of the mediator of God and men, consequently in this fourth book he treats of the Sacraments, by which the wounds of the sins of human frailty are bound up and healed* etc.

[^2n]: **La.** Plurimi codd. *resurgentium*.
    **En.** Very many codices read *resurgentium* (of those rising again).

[^3n]: **La.** Cod. Z supplet *Sacramentorum*. Inferius in verbis Magistri pro *differentiam* non pauci codd. *distantiam*.
    **En.** Codex Z supplies *Sacramentorum* (of the Sacraments). Below, in the words of the Master, for *differentiam* (difference) not a few codices read *distantiam* (distance).
