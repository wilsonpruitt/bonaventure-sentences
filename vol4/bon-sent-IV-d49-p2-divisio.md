## COMMENTARIUS IN DISTINCTIONEM XLIX.

**Pars II.**

### *De gloria corporis sive de stola secunda.*

*Post haec quaeri solet, si beatitudo Sanctorum etc.*

**TEXTUM MAGISTRI VIDE SUPRA PAG. 999.**

### DIVISIO TEXTUS.

Supra egit Magister de gloria spiritus, in qua consistit prima stola. Hic agit de gloria corporis, in qua consistit stola secunda. Dividitur autem haec pars in tres partes secundum tria, quae determinat. Primo enim ostendit, quod in resumtione corporum erit augmentum gloriae. Secundo vero determinat modum, per quem gloria spiritus augebitur, ibi: *Si quem movet, quid opus sit.* Tertio determinat, quale corpus reddetur animae beatae, scilicet spirituale, non animale; et hoc facit, ibi: *Proinde, cum hoc corpus iam non animale* etc.

### TRACTATIO QUAESTIONUM.

Ad intelligentiam huius partis quaeritur de gloria corporis. Et circa hoc duo principaliter quaeruntur[^p1011-4].

Primo quaeritur de gloria corporis in generali.

Secundo, in speciali.

### SECTIO I.

### *De gloria corporis in generali.*

Circa primum quaeruntur principaliter tria.

Primo quaeritur de corporis glorificatione.

Secundo, de dotium distinctione.

Tertio, de sensuum remuneratione.

Circa primum quaeruntur duo.

Primo quaeritur, utrum gloria corporis sit de substantia beatitudinis.

Secundo quaeritur de origine beatitudinis corporis sive gloriae.

## English

## COMMENTARY ON DISTINCTION XLIX.

**Part II.**

### *On the glory of the body, or on the second robe.*

*After these things it is customary to ask, whether the beatitude of the Saints etc.*

**FOR THE TEXT OF THE MASTER SEE ABOVE, PAGE 999.**

### DIVISION OF THE TEXT.

Above the Master treated of the glory of the spirit, in which the first robe consists. Here he treats of the glory of the body, in which the second robe consists. Now this part is divided into three parts according to the three things which he determines. For *first* he shows that in the resumption of bodies there will be an increase of glory. *Secondly*, he determines the manner by which the glory of the spirit will be increased, there: *If it moves anyone, what need there is.* *Thirdly*, he determines what sort of body will be restored to the beatified soul, namely a spiritual one, not an animal one; and he does this there: *Accordingly, since this body is now not animal* etc.

### TREATMENT OF THE QUESTIONS.

For the understanding of this part, inquiry is made concerning the glory of the body. And concerning this, two things are principally asked[^p1011-4].

*First*, inquiry is made concerning the glory of the body in general.

*Secondly*, in particular.

### SECTION I.

### *On the glory of the body in general.*

Concerning the first, three things are principally asked.

*First*, inquiry is made concerning the glorification of the body.

*Secondly*, concerning the distinction of the dowries.

*Thirdly*, concerning the remuneration of the senses.

Concerning the first, two things are asked.

*First*, it is asked whether the glory of the body belongs to the substance of beatitude.

*Secondly*, inquiry is made concerning the origin of the beatitude or glory of the body.

## Apparatus

[^p1011-4]: **La.** Auctor hic *duo, quae principaliter* quaeruntur, iterum distribuit, et primum membrum in tria, quae *principaliter quaeruntur*, scil. in tres articulos. Ad confusionem in citando vitandam primae divisioni nomen *sectionis* superscripsimus.

     **En.** The author here again distributes the *two things which are principally* asked, and the first member into three, which *are principally asked*, that is, into three articles. In order to avoid confusion in citation, we have superscribed upon the first division the name of *section*.
